# Kohler Numi 2.0 Smart Toilet

# The $13,000 Smart Toilet That Talks Back: Is the Kohler Numi 2.0 Worth the Price of a Used Car?

In the world of high-end bathroom technology, few products command as much attention—or as much skepticism—as the Kohler Numi 2.0 Smart Toilet. Priced at $12,999.97, this isn't just a toilet; it's a statement piece that challenges everything we thought we knew about bathroom design. But with a consensus score of 7.8/10 from 12 expert reviews and a SmartHomeExplorer proprietary rating of 6.2/10, the question remains: Does this porcelain powerhouse deliver on its promises, or is it simply a luxury gadget for the 1%?

## The Smart Home Integration That Sets It Apart

What truly distinguishes the Numi 2.0 from its competitors is its native integration with Amazon Alexa and the Kohler Konnect app. While many smart toilets rely on third-party hubs or limited app control, the Numi 2.0 offers a seamless, voice-controlled experience right out of the box. Imagine walking into your bathroom and saying, "Alexa, warm the seat," or "Alexa, play my morning playlist." The built-in Bluetooth speakers and ambient multicolor lighting transform what was once a purely functional space into a personalized sanctuary.

This level of integration is unprecedented in the toilet market. Most smart toilets, even those from premium brands like TOTO, focus primarily on hygiene features—heated seats, bidet functions, and deodorizers. The Numi 2.0, however, aims to be a full-fledged smart-home hub. It's a bold move, but one that comes with inherent risks. Firmware updates, which are essential for maintaining Alexa compatibility, can introduce bugs or require ongoing maintenance—a concern that analog designs from TOTO completely avoid.

## Expert Consensus: Strengths and Weaknesses

The expert community is largely impressed by the Numi 2.0's ambition, but they're not without reservations. On the positive side, the motion-activated lid and hands-free flush reduce touchpoints, making the toilet more hygienic in a post-pandemic world. The stainless steel self-cleaning wand with UV sanitization is another standout feature, ensuring that the bidet function remains clean without manual intervention. Additionally, the emergency backup battery flush is a thoughtful touch for power outages, ensuring you're never left in the dark—literally or figuratively.

However, the criticisms are equally pointed. The most obvious issue is the price. At nearly $13,000 before installation, the Numi 2.0 is a luxury item that justifies itself only in the context of high-end remodels. For most homeowners, this is the cost of a used car or a significant down payment on a new one. Installation is another hurdle: the heavy one-piece design may require reinforced flooring, adding to the overall expense. And if something goes wrong, repair calls must go through Kohler dealers rather than big-box plumbing channels, which could lead to longer wait times and higher service costs.

## Who Should Buy the Kohler Numi 2.0?

The Numi 2.0 is not for everyone, and that's by design. It's best suited for smart-home enthusiasts who want a fully integrated experience, buyers who prioritize native voice and app control, and those undertaking high-end remodels where cost is less of a concern. If you're building a dream home or renovating a luxury bathroom, the Numi 2.0 can be a centerpiece that wows guests and simplifies daily routines.

But if you're looking for practical, cost-effective smart toilet features—like a heated seat, bidet, and deodorizer—there are far more affordable options. The TOTO Washlet C5 Bidet Seat, for example, scores a 9.2/10 from experts and costs between $399 and $459. The Bio Bidet BB-2000 Bliss Bidet Seat is another strong contender, with an 8.8/10 consensus score and a price range of $499 to $599. These products offer many of the same hygiene benefits without the smart-home complexity or the five-figure price tag.

## The Future-Proofing Paradox

One of the most interesting aspects of the Numi 2.0's SmartHomeExplorer rating is its future-proofing score of 5/10. This is a critical metric for any smart-home device, as technology evolves rapidly. The Numi 2.0's reliance on Alexa integration and the Kohler Konnect app means that its long-term value depends on Kohler's commitment to software updates and platform compatibility. If Alexa's ecosystem changes or Kohler decides to discontinue the Konnect app, the Numi 2.0 could become a very expensive dumb toilet.

In contrast, analog smart toilets like those from TOTO have no such dependencies. They offer reliable, long-lasting performance without the need for firmware updates or cloud connectivity. This is a significant consideration for buyers who plan to keep their toilet for a decade or more.

## Final Verdict: A Luxury Experiment Worth Watching

The Kohler Numi 2.0 Smart Toilet is a fascinating product that pushes the boundaries of what a bathroom fixture can be. Its integration with Alexa and the Kohler Konnect app is genuinely innovative, and its build quality is top-notch. For the right buyer—someone with a generous budget and a passion for smart-home technology—it could be a game-changer.

However, for the vast majority of consumers, the Numi 2.0 is an expensive curiosity rather than a practical investment. Its high price, installation challenges, and future-proofing concerns make it a niche product in a market that already offers excellent alternatives at a fraction of the cost. If you're intrigued by the concept but not ready to commit, consider starting with a smart bidet seat and building your smart bathroom from there. The Numi 2.0 may be the future of toilets, but for now, it's a luxury that only a few can justify.
