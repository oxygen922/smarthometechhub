## Smart Toilet Buying Guide

### Key Features to Consider

#### 1. **Cleansing Technology**
- **Aerated wash** - Air bubbles mixed with water for gentler cleanse
- **Oscillating wash** - Wand moves back and forth for thorough cleaning
- **Pulse wash** - Alternating pressure for massage effect
- **Wide spray** - Covers larger area for rear cleansing

#### 2. **Water Heating System**
- **Tank water heaters** - Limited warm water supply
- **Tankless instant heaters** - Unlimited warm water (recommended)

#### 3. **Nozzle Material**
- **Plastic** - Common, affordable
- **Stainless steel** - More hygienic, premium (recommended)

#### 4. **Smart Features**
- Auto open/close lid
- Night light
- Motion sensor flush
- Bluetooth/music connectivity
- Smartphone app control
- User memory presets

### Installation Requirements

**Electrical:**
- GFCI outlet within 4 feet
- 110-120V standard outlet
- Minimum 15-amp circuit

**Plumbing:**
- Standard 3-inch rough-in (most US homes)
- Water supply line with T-valve
- Floor drain for overflow

**Space:**
- Minimum 27" width clearance
- Minimum 28" depth from wall
- Check door swing clearance

## Maintenance Tips

**Daily:**
- Quick wipe of seat and exterior

**Weekly:**
- Clean nozzle with soft brush
- Check and clean wand area

**Monthly:**
- Deep clean bowl with non-abrasive cleaner
- Descale if you have hard water

**Yearly:**
- Professional inspection of electrical components
- Replace water filter if equipped

**⚠️ Important:** Always use manufacturer-recommended cleaning products. Harsh chemicals can damage sensors and coatings.

## FAQ

### How much does a smart toilet cost?
Prices range from $350 for basic bidet seats to over $5,000 for fully integrated smart toilets. Budget $800-$1,500 for a quality model.

### Do smart toilets need electricity?
Yes, they require a standard 110V outlet within 4 feet. Most consume less than $5 annually in electricity.

### Are smart toilets sanitary?
Absolutely. Self-cleaning wands, UV sterilization, and EWATER+ technology make them more hygienic than traditional toilets.

### Can I install a smart toilet myself?
Bidet seats are DIY-friendly. Fully integrated smart toilets require professional installation for optimal performance and warranty coverage.

### How long do smart toilets last?
With proper maintenance, quality smart toilets can last 15-20 years. Electronic components typically last 8-12 years.

### Do smart toilets increase home value?
Yes. Bathroom upgrades typically recoup 60-70% of costs at resale, and smart features attract modern buyers.
