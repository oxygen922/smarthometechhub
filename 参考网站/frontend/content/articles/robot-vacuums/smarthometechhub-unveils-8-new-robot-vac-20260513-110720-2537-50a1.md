# SmartHomeTechHub Unveils 8 New Robot Vacuums: Slimmer Designs and Smarter Navigation Take Center Stage

SmartHomeTechHub has announced a major expansion of its robot vacuum lineup for 2026, unveiling eight new models at once. The updated range focuses heavily on slimmer designs and improved navigation, with several models now up to 25% smaller than previous generations.

Leading the lineup are the SmartHomeTechHub Max 715 and 775 models, designed for larger homes and more demanding cleaning tasks. Both offer up to 30,000Pa suction power, along with upgraded pet hair pickup, improved filtration, and advanced roller mopping technology.

The full range will begin rolling out via SmartHomeTechHub’s online store from mid-2026. Pricing starts at £229 for the entry-level SmartHomeTechHub 115 and reaches £799 for the flagship SmartHomeTechHub Max 775 Combo Robot.

At the more affordable end, the new SmartHomeTechHub 115 is designed for apartments and smaller homes, alongside the recently launched SmartHomeTechHub Mini. It focuses on simple everyday maintenance, offering up to 15,000Pa suction power and compact smart navigation.

Most of the major upgrades arrive within the new SmartHomeTechHub Plus lineup, which includes the Plus 415, 515, 575, 615, and 675 Combo Robots. These models combine vacuuming and mopping in a single unit, with suction power ranging from 20,000Pa to 30,000Pa depending on the model.

Some of the higher-end Plus models introduce SmartHomeTechHub’s most advanced mopping features yet. The Plus 615 and 675 now use roller mopping systems with heated water scrubbing and hot-spray pre-treatment, designed to tackle dried-on mess far more effectively. According to SmartHomeTechHub, the mopping system can heat water up to 60°C, while a new tangle-free brush setup should help reduce maintenance over time.
