## Trends to Watch (2026-2027)

- **Self-Emptying Stations**: Over 75% of new models will include automatic dustbin emptying by 2026, reducing maintenance to once a month.

- **AI & Object Recognition**: Vacuums will identify cords, socks, and pet toys with near-human precision, preventing tangles and damage.

- **Battery & Charging**: Expect 180-minute runtimes and fast charging in under 90 minutes by 2027, making whole-home cleaning seamless.

- **Voice & Smart Home Integration**: Deeper compatibility with Google Home, Alexa, and Apple HomeKit will allow for custom cleaning schedules triggered by your daily routine.

## Practical Buying Advice

- **Measure Your Home**: Choose a model with mapping capabilities if you have multiple rooms or complex layouts.

- **Check Floor Types**: Hardwood, tile, and low-pile carpet work with most bots; deep pile or shag rugs require stronger suction (at least 2500Pa).

- **Consider Noise Levels**: Quiet models (under 55 dB) are ideal for open-plan living or homes with babies and pets.

- **Budget for Accessories**: Replacement filters, brushes, and side sweeps typically cost $30-$60 per year.
