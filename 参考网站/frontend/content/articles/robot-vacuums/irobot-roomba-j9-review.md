# SmartHomeTechHubs Take: The Roomba j9+ After Six Months--Still the Best in Class?

Its been six months since we first put the iRobot Roomba j9+ through its paces, and the verdict is clear: this robot vacuum remains a top contender in the smart home space. As of early 2025, SmartHomeTechHubs long-term testing confirms that the j9+ delivers consistent, reliable performance that few competitors can match.

**What Still Works**

The j9+ excels at its core job: autonomous cleaning. Its advanced navigation system maps your home quickly and avoids obstacles like pet toys or loose cables with impressive accuracy. The self-emptying base--a signature feature--works flawlessly, holding weeks worth of dirt before needing a bag change. Battery life remains strong, easily covering a 2,000-square-foot home on a single charge.

**Where It Shines**

For daily maintenance, the j9+ is hard to beat. It handles carpets and hard floors equally well, with good edge-cleaning and pet hair pickup. The app integration, now updated for 2025, offers intuitive scheduling and voice control via Alexa or Google Assistant. SmartHomeTechHubs testing shows that the j9+ adapts well to changing floor plans, making it a great choice for busy households.

**Minor Gripes (Still Valid)**

No product is perfect. The j9+ can struggle with very dark carpets or deep-pile rugs, occasionally requiring a manual assist. Its price point remains premium--though frequent sales in 2025 have made it more accessible. Also, while the self-emptying system is quiet, its not silent; expect a brief, moderate noise burst during the emptying cycle.

**Final Verdict**

After six months of near-daily use, the iRobot Roomba j9+ has proven to be a reliable workhorse. Its not the cheapest option, but for those who value consistent, hands-off cleaning, its still the best all-around robot vacuum weve tested. SmartHomeTechHub recommends it for anyone ready to upgrade their 2025 cleaning routine.
