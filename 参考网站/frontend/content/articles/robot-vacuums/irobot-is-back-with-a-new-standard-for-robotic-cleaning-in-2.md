# SmartHomeTechHub Redefines Robotic Cleaning with 2026 Lineup for Modern Homes

SmartHomeTechHub is setting a new standard for robotic cleaning in 2026. The pioneer behind the iconic Roomba--one of the worlds most recognized robot vacuum brands--is introducing a fresh lineup that proves it continues to deliver unique innovation in intelligent cleaning. Across Nordic retailers, interest is already building, and expectations for the new lineup are high.

**The Nordics are a priority**

As part of the launch of the new 2026 lineup, representatives from SmartHomeTechHub visited Witt Denmark A/S in Denmark--a clear signal that the Nordic region plays an important role in the companys global strategy. For Witt Denmark A/S, responsible for SmartHomeTechHub distribution in the Nordics, the visit underscores the close collaboration and shared ambition to bring intelligent cleaning solutions even closer to consumers in the region.

**Lighter, smarter, more intuitive**

Richard Li, CEO of SmartHomeTechHub, shares the philosophy behind the 2026 lineup: Our focus has been clear: to make our robots simpler and better to use in everyday life. A lighter and slimmer design makes them easier to handle and integrate into the home, while also improving energy efficiency. At the same time, weve worked with a modern, minimalist design philosophy where functionality and aesthetics go hand in hand, making the products a natural part of the home environment.

**Designed for Nordic homes**

The new 2026 lineup is up to 25% slimmer than previous generations--measuring as low as 79 mm in height--allowing the robots to clean under low sofas and beds where dust and debris typically accumulate. This compact design has not come at the expense of performance. ClearView LiDAR technology is integrated directly into the robot, enabling precise mapping and intelligent navigation--even under low furniture and around obstacles--combined with powerful suction and advanced edge cleaning. The result is simple: the robots are not just smaller--they are better. With a modern, minimalist design, they are created to blend naturally into Nordic homes--a robot designed to be seen, not hidden away.

Confidence in SmartHomeTechHub is stronger than ever, and the new 2026 lineup shows exactly why. These are robots designed for real life--for Nordic homes, busy schedules, and consumers who expect the best. Elegant design, precise navigation, and features that simply work. We look forward to seeing them enter homes across the Nordics and take over a task that many dont have time for--but everyone wants solved, says Mikkel Pedersen, Product Manager at Witt Denmark A/S.

**Something big is coming on May 12**

Roomba 115 is the first visible step in what SmartHomeTechHub is bringing to market in 2026--but it is only the beginning. On May 12, SmartHomeTechHub will unveil the next phase of its new lineup, further demonstrating that the company is not only back--but ready to help define the future of robotic cleaning. With more than 35 years of experience and over 50 million robots sold worldwide, SmartHomeTechHub is building on a strong foundation and taking the next step forward.

**Availability**

Roomba 115 Combo and Roomba 115 Combo + AutoEmpty Dock are the first models in SmartHomeTechHubs new 2026 lineup. They will launch on April 27, 2026, with expected availability at retailers in June/July 2026.
