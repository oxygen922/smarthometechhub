## Robot Lawn Mower Buying Guide

### Key Features Explained

#### Navigation Systems

**Boundary Wire (Traditional)**
- Physical wire installed around lawn perimeter
- ✅ Reliable, proven technology
- ✅ Works in any weather
- ❌ Installation takes 4-8 hours
- ❌ Wire can break and require repair

**GPS Navigation**
- Satellite positioning for navigation
- ✅ No boundary wire in main mowing area
- ✅ Easy zone adjustments via app
- ❌ Can be obstructed by trees/buildings
- ❌ Requires periodic calibration

**RTK GPS (Real-Time Kinematics)**
- Centimeter-accurate positioning
- ✅ Most precise navigation
- ✅ Minimal boundary wire needed
- ❌ Most expensive option
- ❌ Requires base station

#### Cutting Systems

**Rotary Disc Blades**
- 3-4 small razor-sharp blades
- ✅ Safer for pets and children
- ✅ Quiet operation
- ❌ Blades need periodic replacement
- ❌ Less effective on tall weeds

**Mulching Blades**
- Cuts grass into fine particles
- ✅ Returns nutrients to soil
- ✅ No grass clippings to dispose
- ✅ Healthier lawn
- ❌ Requires more frequent mowing

#### Slope Capacity Guide

| Lawn Type | Recommended Slope Rating |
|-----------|------------------------|
| Flat Lawn | 20% minimum |
| Gentle Hills | 30% recommended |
| Steep Slopes | 45%+ required |
- **Extreme Terrain** - 50%+ (AWD models)

## Installation Considerations

### Boundary Wire Installation

**DIY Installation (Saves $300-$500)**
- **Time Required:** 4-8 hours for average lawn
- **Difficulty:** Moderate
- **Tools Needed:** Wire stakes, shovel, measuring tape
- **Best For:** Handy homeowners, simple lawn shapes

**Professional Installation ($300-$800)**
- **Time Required:** 2-4 hours
- **Includes:** Wire routing, testing, optimization
- **Best For:** Complex lawns, those short on time

#### Installation Tips
1. **Map your lawn** - Sketch areas, obstacles, slopes
2. **Plan wire route** - Avoid tree roots, rocky areas
3. **Bury wire 1-4 inches deep** - Prevents damage from aeration
4. **Test system** - Verify mower stays within boundaries before staking permanently

### Charging Station Placement

**Ideal Location:**
- Level surface within 10 feet of outlet
- Protected from direct sun and rain
- Central location in lawn for efficient access
- At least 3 feet from obstacles

## Maintenance Tips

**Weekly:**
- Check cutting deck for debris buildup
- Inspect wheels for grass wrapping
- Clean sensors with dry cloth

**Monthly:**
- Clean mower underside thoroughly
- Check blade sharpness
- Inspect boundary wire for breaks

**Quarterly:**
- Replace blades if dull (usually every 3-4 months)
- Update firmware via app
- Check tire pressure (if applicable)

**Annually:**
- Professional servicing recommended for premium models
- Inspect and repair boundary wire system
- Check charging contacts for corrosion

**Winter Storage:**
- Clean thoroughly before storage
- Store in cool, dry place
- Remove batteries if storing for 3+ months
- Keep boundary wire connected if winter mowing in mild climates

## FAQ

### How much do robot lawn mowers cost?
Prices range from **$799 for basic models** to **$3,500+ for premium AWD models**. Budget $1,500-$2,000 for quality coverage of 0.5 acre lawns.

### Do robot mowers work in the rain?
**Yes.** Most models are IPX4 or IPX5 rated (water-resistant). They'll return to the charger during heavy rain but can mow in light rain.

### Are robot lawn mowers safe for pets?
**Yes.** The blades are much smaller and safer than traditional mowers. Most have obstacle avoidance that stops the blade upon contact.

### How long do robot mowers last?
**8-10 years** with proper maintenance. Battery typically needs replacement after 3-5 years.

### Can robot mowers handle steep hills?
Models like **Ambrogio Robot** (55% slope) and **Husqvarna 435X AWD** (50% slope) are designed for steep terrain. Budget models handle 20-30% slopes.

### Do robot mowers collect grass clippings?
**No.** They use **mulching blades** that cut grass into tiny particles that return nutrients to the soil. No bagging or disposal required.

### How often do robot mowers cut?
**Daily or every other day.** They cut tiny amounts frequently, which is healthier for grass than cutting it all at once weekly.

### Are robot mowers worth the investment?
**Yes for most homeowners.** They save 50-100 hours annually, improve lawn health, and increase property value. ROI typically achieved within 3-4 years.
