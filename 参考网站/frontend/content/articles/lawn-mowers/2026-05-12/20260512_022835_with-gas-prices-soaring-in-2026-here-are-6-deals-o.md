---# With gas prices soaring in 2026, here are 6 deals on electric lawn mowers that cost under $400

As fuel prices continue to climb in 2026, homeowners across the United States are feeling the pinch at the pump—not just in their cars, but in their garages and sheds. Lawn care, once a relatively inexpensive weekend chore, has become a noticeable line item in household budgets. With gas prices hovering at record highs, the cost of filling a standard gas can for a push mower can now easily exceed $10 per session. Over a six-month growing season, that adds up to more than just a headache—it becomes a financial drain.

Enter the cordless electric lawn mower. Once dismissed as underpowered or impractical for anything but the smallest yards, today’s electric mowers have evolved into serious contenders. They are quieter, require less maintenance, and—most importantly in 2026—far cheaper to operate. Charging a battery costs a fraction of what you’d spend on gasoline, and many models now offer runtimes and cutting power that rival their gas-powered cousins. For homeowners looking to cut costs without cutting corners, the switch to electric is no longer just an eco-friendly choice; it’s a financially savvy one.

But not all electric mowers are created equal, and prices can vary wildly. The good news is that there are several high-quality options available for under $400—a sweet spot for budget-conscious buyers who still want reliable performance. Below, I’ve analyzed six standout deals that offer the best balance of price, power, and practicality for the 2026 season.
