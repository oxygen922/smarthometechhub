# Introducing Sports Live: Smart lighting driven by live sports data, bringing real-time match moments to life at home

## Philips Hue and WiZ Launch Sports Live: Real-Time Stadium Atmosphere, Now in Your Living Room

**Philips Hue and Philips Smart Lighting, powered by WiZ, have unveiled Sports Live—a groundbreaking software experience that syncs home smart lighting with live soccer match data, delivering immersive, real-time lighting effects for goals, cards, and game momentum.**

BRIDGEWATER, N.J., May 6, 2026 — The world’s leading lighting company, Signify, today announced the launch of Sports Live, a new software feature designed for Philips Hue and Philips Smart Lighting products connected by WiZ. Available just in time for Championship 2026, Sports Live transforms the home viewing experience by tapping into live match data to trigger dynamic lighting effects that mirror key moments on the pitch. From the roar of a last-minute goal to the tension of a red card, the system extends the action beyond the screen, creating a truly immersive environment for soccer fans.

“We wanted to bring the energy of the stadium directly into the home,” said a Signify spokesperson. “Sports Live does exactly that—using live data to make every match feel like you’re in the stands, even if you’re on the couch.”

## Real-Time Sync: How Live Data Powers the Experience

At the core of Sports Live is a sophisticated integration with a live sports data API, which pulls real-time match events—goals, yellow cards, red cards, and changes in game state—directly into the user’s lighting system. When a goal is scored, the room instantly flashes in the team’s primary color, mimicking the stadium’s iconic lighting displays. For yellow or red cards, the system triggers distinct, attention-grabbing effects that keep fans engaged even during tense moments.

The system is designed for live viewing and accounts for minor broadcast delays. Users can fine-tune the timing to ensure perfect synchronization with their TV feed. If a viewer pauses the action—say, to grab a snack or rewind a controversial play—the lighting effects pause as well. Upon resuming, the system automatically re-syncs to the current live match events, ensuring a consistent and accurate real-time experience.

During quieter periods of play, the lighting intelligently adapts to the game state. For example, when the score is tied, the room may glow in a warm white. When one team leads, the lights shift to the leading team’s color. Fans can also set their preferred team’s color to display throughout the match, creating a personalized viewing environment that’s both dynamic and engaging.

## Simple Setup, Seamless Integration

One of the most compelling aspects of Sports Live is its ease of setup. Users can get started in just a few minutes: select a preferred team, assign compatible color-capable lights, and the system automatically connects to live match data. No complex wiring or professional installation is required.

For Philips Hue users, the experience is powered by the Philips Hue Bridge, which connects any supported smart lights to the Sports Live software. The system scales effortlessly from a single room to a full zone of the home, depending on the user’s setup. Whether it’s a dedicated home theater or an open-concept living area, the lighting adapts to create a cohesive, stadium-like atmosphere.

For Philips Smart Lighting and all “Connected by WiZ” products, Sports Live operates through a Wi-Fi-based setup, designed for accessibility and personalization without the need for a Bridge. This approach supports an unlimited number of lights, making it ideal for larger spaces or multi-room configurations. The WiZ app provides intuitive controls, allowing users to customize effects, adjust brightness, and manage multiple zones with ease.

## Data-Driven Insights: The Technology Behind the Magic

The underlying technology of Sports Live is a testament to Signify’s commitment to innovation in the smart home space. The live sports data API is sourced from a leading provider, ensuring low-latency updates that are critical for real-time synchronization. The system processes thousands of data points per second, filtering out noise and focusing only on the most impactful match events.

“This is not just about turning lights on and off,” explained a Signify product manager. “It’s about creating an emotional connection. The lighting responds to the rhythm of the game—the buildup, the climax, the release. It’s a new way to experience sports.”

The system also supports multiple teams and leagues, with plans to expand to other sports in the future. For now, the focus is on soccer, given the massive global audience for Championship 2026. Signify has also hinted at future updates that could include real-time stats overlays, crowd noise synchronization, and integration with voice assistants for hands-free control.

## Practical Advice for Getting Started

For those eager to try Sports Live, here are a few tips to maximize the experience:

- **Choose the right lights:** Ensure you have color-capable smart lights from Philips Hue or WiZ-compatible products. Warm white bulbs will not provide the full effect.
- **Optimize your Wi-Fi network:** Since the system relies on real-time data, a stable and fast internet connection is crucial. Consider using a mesh network if you have multiple devices.
- **Calibrate the delay:** Use the app’s fine-tuning feature to match your TV broadcast. Most streaming services have a 5-10 second delay compared to live cable, so adjust accordingly.
- **Start with a single room:** If you’re new to smart lighting, begin with your main viewing area. The system scales easily, so you can expand later.
- **Experiment with team colors:** Don’t just stick to your favorite team—try the leading team color for a more dynamic experience, or warm white for neutral games.
