## FAQ

### Do smart bulbs use electricity when off?
**Yes, a tiny amount.** Vampire draw is typically 0.2-0.5 watts per bulb, costing $1-2 per year per bulb.

### Can I use smart bulbs in regular lamps?
**Yes.** Smart bulbs fit standard sockets (E26/E27 in US/EU). Just screw in and connect via app.

### Do I need a hub?
**Not always.** WiFi bulbs (LIFX, Govee, Wyze) don't require hubs. Zigbee bulbs (Philips Hue) do. Hub-based systems are more reliable and expandable.

### Are smart bulbs worth it?
**Yes, for most people.** Benefits include:
- Automation (lights on/off at sunset/sunrise)
- Energy savings
- Mood lighting
- Voice control
- Security (simulate presence when away)

### How long do smart bulbs last?
**15,000-25,000 hours** (15-20 years of typical use). Electronics may fail before LED, causing shorter lifespan than standard LEDs.

### Can smart bulbs work with switches?
**Careful here.** Smart bulbs shouldn't be controlled by smart switches (double automation is problematic). Use smart switches OR smart bulbs, not both.

### Which is better: smart bulbs or smart switches?
**It depends:**
- **Rental:** Smart bulbs (take with you when you move)
- **Many bulbs:** Smart switches (cheaper than replacing all bulbs)
- **Colors:** Smart bulbs (switches typically white-only)
- **Guest-friendly:** Smart switches (physical switch always works)
