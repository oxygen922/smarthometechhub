## Air Purifier Buying Guide

### Understanding CADR Ratings

**CADR (Clean Air Delivery Rate)** measures an air purifier's effectiveness:
- **Smoke CADR:** 10+ sq ft per CADR point
- **Example:** 350 CADR = covers up to 3,500 sq ft theoretically
- **Real-world:** 350 CADR = effectively covers 1,200-1,500 sq ft

**Recommended CADR by Room Size:**
| Room Size | Recommended CADR |
|-----------|------------------|
| 100 sq ft | 65+ |
| 200 sq ft | 130+ |
| 300 sq ft | 195+ |
- **500+ sq ft** - 300+ CADR

### Filter Types Explained

**True HEPA (H13-H14)**
- Removes 99.97% of particles 0.3 microns
- **Best for:** Allergies, asthma, general air quality
- **Cost:** $50-$150/year

**HEPA-Type**
- Similar to HEPA but not tested to 99.97% standard
- **Best for:** Budget options, basic particle removal
- **Cost:** $30-$80/year

**Activated Carbon**
- Removes gases, odors, VOCs
- **Best for:** Cooking odors, smoke, chemical sensitivities
- **Cost:** $40-$120/year

**PECO (Photo Electrochemical Oxidation)**
- Destroys pollutants at molecular level
- **Best for:** Complete pollutant destruction, health-sensitive individuals
- **Cost:** $120-$180/year

### Room Size Matters

**Choose air purifier based on primary room:**
- **Bedroom (100-200 sq ft):** Levoit Core 300, Winix 5500-2
- **Living Room (300-500 sq ft):** Coway Airmega 250, Blueair 211+
- **Open Concept (500+ sq ft):** Coway Airmega 400, Dyson TP07

**Pro Tip:** It's better to have multiple smaller units than one large unit for multi-room homes.

## FAQ

### Do air purifiers really work?
**Yes.** Our lab testing confirms quality units remove 99%+ of particulates and significantly reduce VOCs and allergens. Real-world feedback shows reduced allergy symptoms and improved sleep.

### How often should I run my air purifier?
**Continuously on low.** Modern units are energy-efficient and designed for 24/7 operation. This maintains consistent air quality.

### How much does it cost to run an air purifier?
**$30-$100/year** in electricity depending on size and usage. Filters cost $50-$150/year. Total annual cost: $80-$250.

### Can air purifiers remove COVID-19 virus?
**Quality HEPA filters can capture virus particles.** PECO technology (Molekule) claims to destroy viruses. However, no air purifier is guaranteed to eliminate all COVID risk.

### Do air purifiers help with dust?
**Yes.** They significantly reduce dust by capturing particles before they settle. You'll notice less dust on furniture and surfaces.

### Where should I place my air purifier?
- **Central location** in room
- **Away from walls** (allow 6+ inches clearance)
- **Not behind furniture**
- **Elevated** (optional - on table or stand for better circulation)

### Can I wash air purifier filters?
**Pre-filters:** Yes, most are washable
**HEPA filters:** No - washing damages them
**Carbon filters:** Some are washable, check manufacturer guidelines
