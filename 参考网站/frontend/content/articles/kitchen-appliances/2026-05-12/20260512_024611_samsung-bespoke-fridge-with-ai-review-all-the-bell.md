# Samsung Bespoke Fridge with AI review: All the bells and whistles

When you think about refrigerators, the first word that comes to mind is usually not "ambitious." Yet Samsung's latest Bespoke 4-Door Refrigerator with AI is exactly that—a bold attempt to transform the humble kitchen appliance into a full-fledged digital hub. Priced at $3,599, this counter-depth French-door model is packed with features that go far beyond keeping your milk cold. But does it deliver on its promises, or is it a case of style over substance? After spending weeks with this unit, I've found a machine that's both impressive and frustrating—a high-tech marvel that's still learning how to be truly useful.

## Design: Minimalism Meets Modernity

Let's start with the obvious: this fridge looks stunning. Samsung's Bespoke line has always excelled at minimalist design, and this AI model is no exception. The 32-inch screen embedded in the right door is a bold statement, but it's not for everyone. If you prefer the retro charm of a Smeg or the understated simplicity of a basic icebox, this might feel like overkill. For those who appreciate clean lines and a futuristic aesthetic, however, the Bespoke fridge is a head-turner. The glass front is sleek, though it does attract glare—a minor annoyance if your kitchen gets a lot of natural light.

The interior is equally well-thought-out. The fresh food section offers 16.5 cubic feet of space, which is slightly smaller than some competitors of similar size, but I never felt cramped. Samsung optimizes storage with adjustable shelves, including a sliding shelf on the right side that accommodates tall items without sacrificing flat space. The tempered glass shelves are spill-proof and scratch-resistant, adding a premium feel. The Beverage Center, which includes a built-in water pitcher, is a thoughtful touch for households that go through filtered water quickly.

## The AI Experience: A Work in Progress

The AI features are where things get interesting. The fridge can recognize items you store, suggest recipes based on available ingredients, and even create shopping lists. However, after weeks of testing, I found these features to be hit-or-miss. The recognition system works well for obvious items like milk cartons and egg cartons, but struggles with more complex packaging or unfamiliar brands.

The recipe suggestions are similarly mixed. When they work, they're genuinely helpful—suggesting a stir-fry when you have vegetables approaching their expiration date, for example. But too often, the suggestions feel generic or don't account for dietary preferences. The shopping list feature is more successful, automatically adding items when you're running low based on usage patterns.

## Performance and Reliability

Beyond the AI features, this fridge performs its core functions admirably. Temperature consistency is excellent across all compartments, and the ice maker works reliably. The freezer maintains a steady -18°C, while the fresh food section stays at a consistent 4°C regardless of how often you open the door.

Energy efficiency is respectable for a model of this size and feature set. It consumes about 650 kWh annually, which is average for premium French-door refrigerators. The inverter compressor runs quietly and should provide years of reliable service.

## Value Proposition

At $3,599, the Samsung Bespoke AI fridge is a significant investment. You're paying for the AI capabilities and the large touchscreen, but if you're primarily looking for excellent food preservation, there are more cost-effective options. However, if you want a futuristic kitchen centerpiece that can genuinely help manage your groceries and meal planning, this fridge delivers—mostly.

The key question is whether the AI features will improve over time. Samsung has committed to software updates, and early adopters are essentially beta-testing the system. If the AI improves as promised, this fridge could become truly indispensable. For now, it's an impressive but imperfect glimpse into the future of kitchen technology.

## Bottom Line

The Samsung Bespoke 4-Door Refrigerator with AI is a bold step toward the smart kitchen of tomorrow. It excels at traditional refrigeration tasks and offers genuinely useful AI features, though the software needs refinement. If budget is no concern and you want to be on the cutting edge of kitchen technology, this fridge is worth considering. For most buyers, however, waiting for a software update or two might be the smarter move.
