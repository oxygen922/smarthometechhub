## FAQ

### Are smart kitchen appliances worth it?
**Yes.** They save time, improve cooking results, reduce energy consumption, and increase home value. ROI typically 3-5 years through energy savings and convenience.

### Do smart appliances require professional installation?
**Some do.** Refrigerators, dishwashers, and ovens typically require professional installation. Countertop appliances (air fryers, multicookers) are plug-and-play.

### Can smart appliances work without WiFi?
**Mostly yes.** Basic functions work offline, but smart features require WiFi. Plan for a stable internet connection.

### How long do smart appliances last?
**10-15 years** for major appliances (fridge, dishwasher, oven). 5-10 years for small appliances. Smart electronics may need updates every 2-3 years.

### Do smart appliances increase home value?
**Yes.** Smart kitchen upgrades typically recoup 60-70% of costs at resale and make homes more attractive to modern buyers.

### What if the company stops supporting the app?
**Appliance still works.** Smart features may become limited, but core functionality remains. Choose established brands with good support track records.
