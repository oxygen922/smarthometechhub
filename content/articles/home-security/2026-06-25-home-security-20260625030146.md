## Key Features to Consider

When shopping for a smart security camera, not all features are created equal. Here’s a breakdown of the most important factors to evaluate.

### Video Resolution and Field of View
Resolution determines how much detail you can see. 1080p is the baseline, but 2K and 4K offer significantly sharper images, especially when zooming in. A wider field of view (130-160 degrees) reduces blind spots, but be aware that ultra-wide lenses can introduce fisheye distortion.

### Night Vision
Look for cameras with color night vision or infrared LEDs. Color night vision uses built-in spotlights to illuminate the scene, while infrared captures black-and-white footage. Some high-end models, like the Eufy S340, combine both for optimal clarity.

### AI Detection and Alerts
Modern cameras use AI to differentiate between motion types. The best systems let you customize alerts for people, pets, or vehicles, reducing false alarms from swaying trees or passing cars. Some models also offer package detection, which is invaluable for online shoppers.

### Storage Options
Cloud storage requires a monthly subscription, while local storage (microSD card or NVR) is free after the initial purchase. Hybrid systems, like Arlo, offer both. Consider your privacy preferences and budget when choosing.

### Smart Home Integration
If you use Alexa, Google Assistant, or Apple HomeKit, ensure the camera is compatible. Integration allows you to view feeds on smart displays, trigger automations, and arm/disarm the system with voice commands.

### Installation and Smart Home Integration

#### Installation Process

All three cameras are designed for DIY installation. The Arlo Pro 6 and Ring Stick Up Cam Pro use magnetic mounts or screw-in brackets, which can be attached to walls, ceilings, or eaves. The Eufy SoloCam S340 includes a wall plate and screws. For outdoor installation, ensure the camera is under an eave or in a weatherproof housing (all three are IP65-rated). The most time-consuming part is running power if you choose wired versions. Wireless models are simpler—just charge the battery, mount the camera, and connect via Wi-Fi.

#### Smart Home Integration

- **Arlo Pro 6:** Works with Alexa, Google Assistant, and Apple HomeKit. You can view feeds on Echo Show, Nest Hub, or Apple TV. It also supports IFTTT for advanced automations.

For best results, choose a camera that matches your existing smart home hub. If you’re building from scratch, consider a hub like Amazon Echo or Apple HomePod to unify your devices.
