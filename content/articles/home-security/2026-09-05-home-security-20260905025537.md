The system's standout feature remains its integration with Ring's extensive camera lineup. ## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Professional vs. DIY Installation

All three systems I tested support DIY installation, which saves you the $100-$200 professional installation fee charged by traditional security companies like ADT. Here's what to expect:

**DIY Installation Time:** 15-30 minutes for basic setups, 1-2 hours for comprehensive packages with multiple sensors and cameras.

**Tools Needed:** Most systems use adhesive backing or included screws. A power drill, level, and measuring tape are helpful for optimal placement.

**Professional Installation:** Some companies offer professional installation for an additional fee ($99-$199). This is recommended if you have a large home, need hardwired sensors, or simply prefer a professional touch.

### Smart Home Integration Best Practices

During testing, I found several practices that maximize the value of your smart home integration:

**Create Arrival/Departure Routines:** Set up automations that disarm your system and adjust lighting when you arrive home, then reverse everything when you leave.

**Leverage Camera Integration:** If your system supports it, configure motion sensors to trigger camera recordings. This provides visual context for alerts.

**Use Voice Control Strategically:** Voice assistants are excellent for checking system status and arming/disarming when your hands are full. However, I recommend requiring a PIN for disarming via voice to prevent unauthorized access.

**Test Automations Regularly:** Smart home integrations can break after system updates. I recommend testing your critical automations monthly.
