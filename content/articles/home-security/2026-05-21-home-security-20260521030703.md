**Performance:** The new “Instant Alarm” mode reduces response time to under 3 seconds. **Pros:**

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy Which System?

- **First-time renters or budget-conscious users:** Choose **SimpliSafe The Tower**. It’s affordable, requires no contract, and you can take it with you when you move. Self-monitoring is free, and professional monitoring is reasonably priced.
- **Tech-savvy homeowners with smart home ecosystems:** Pick **Ring Alarm Pro**. It doubles as a Wi-Fi mesh extender and integrates seamlessly with Alexa, smart lights, and locks. The $20/month plan is a steal for 24/7 monitoring plus cloud storage.
- **High-end homeowners wanting turnkey protection:** Invest in **ADT Command**. Yes, it’s expensive, but professional installation, lifetime warranty, and 24/7 monitoring with rapid dispatch are worth it for peace of mind. Ideal for large properties with complex layouts.

### Budget Considerations

- **Entry-level (under $300):** SimpliSafe or Ring base kits. Add sensors as needed (around $15–20 each). Self-monitoring is free.
- **Mid-range ($300–$600):** Ring Alarm Pro with a few extra sensors and the Protect Pro plan. Total first-year cost: ~$540.
- **Premium ($700+):** ADT Command with installation and a three-year contract. Expect to pay ~$2,200 over three years with monitoring.

Pro tip: Many insurance companies offer discounts (10–20%) for monitored alarm systems. Check with your provider—the savings can offset monthly fees.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Complexity

- **Ring Alarm Pro:** DIY, takes 15–30 minutes. Peel-and-stick sensors, plug-in hub, app-guided setup. No tools required.
- **SimpliSafe The Tower:** DIY, similar to Ring but slightly easier due to pre-paired sensors. The base station is self-contained. Total time: 20 minutes.
- **ADT Command:** Professional installation (2–3 hours). Technician mounts sensors, runs wiring if needed, and configures the system. You get a full walkthrough.

### Smart Home Integration

All three systems support the major voice assistants, but integration depth varies:

- **Ring** works natively with Alexa for arm/disarm, camera feeds on Echo Shows, and automations (“Alexa, arm the system when I leave”). It also supports Zigbee devices like Philips Hue.
- **SimpliSafe** offers basic Alexa and Google Home commands (arm/disarm, status check). No advanced automation like “turn off lights when alarm triggers.”
- **ADT Command** uses Z-Wave Plus, which means it can control smart locks, thermostats, and lights from brands like Yale, Kwikset, and Honeywell. You can create scenes like “Goodnight” that locks doors, arms the system, and dims lights simultaneously.

For serious smart home enthusiasts, ADT is the most flexible—but at a premium price. If you’re already deep in the Amazon ecosystem, Ring is the practical choice.
