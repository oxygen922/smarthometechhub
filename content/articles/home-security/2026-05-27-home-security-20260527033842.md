## Key Features to Consider

When shopping for a smart security camera in 2026, these features separate the good from the great:

### Resolution and Video Quality
- **1080p** is the minimum standard, but **4K** is becoming the norm for outdoor cameras. HDR (High Dynamic Range) is crucial for scenes with bright sunlight and deep shadows.
- **Field of View:** Wide-angle lenses (130°-160°) cover more area but can distort edges. Look for cameras with digital or optical zoom.

### AI Detection Capabilities
- **Person, vehicle, animal, and package detection** are now standard. Advanced models can recognize specific faces or license plates.
- **False alert reduction** is measured by how well the camera ignores leaves, rain, or shadows. Top-tier models achieve 95%+ accuracy.

### Storage Options
- **Local storage** (microSD or built-in memory) offers privacy and no monthly fees but risks loss if the camera is stolen.
- **Cloud storage** provides remote access and backup but requires a subscription (typically $3-$15/month per camera).
- **Hybrid systems** (like Arlo’s SmartHub) let you choose.

### Power Source
- **Battery-powered** offers flexible placement but requires periodic charging (every 2-6 months).
- **Wired (PoE or USB)** provides continuous power but limits placement near outlets.
- **Solar-powered** options (like Eufy’s) are ideal for sunny locations.

### Smart Home Integration
- **Amazon Alexa, Google Assistant, and Apple HomeKit** compatibility ensures voice control and automation.
- **IFTTT support** allows custom triggers (e.g., flash lights when motion detected).

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### DIY Installation Tips

Most modern smart cameras are designed for easy DIY setup. Here’s a quick guide:

1. **Choose location:** Mount cameras 8-10 feet high for optimal field of view and to prevent tampering.
2. **Test Wi-Fi signal:** Use a Wi-Fi analyzer app to ensure strong connectivity at the installation point. 5GHz bands offer faster speeds but shorter range; 2.4GHz is more reliable for distant cameras.
3. **Power considerations:** For battery cameras, fully charge before mounting. For wired cameras, use outdoor-rated cables and weatherproof connectors.
4. **Angle adjustment:** Most cameras allow pan/tilt adjustments after mounting. Use the live view to fine-tune the angle before securing screws.

### Smart Home Integration

- **Amazon Alexa:** All three recommended cameras work with Alexa. You can say, “Alexa, show the front door camera” on Echo Show devices or set routines like “When motion is detected, turn on porch lights.”
- **Google Assistant:** Arlo and Eufy support Google Home for voice commands and Nest Hub displays. Ring requires a workaround via IFTTT.
- **Apple HomeKit:** Eufy and Arlo support HomeKit Secure Video, which uses your iCloud storage for encrypted footage. Ring does not support HomeKit natively.
- **Automation Examples:**
  - Trigger a Ring camera to record when an Alexa smart lock is unlocked.
  - Have Eufy cameras turn on floodlights when motion is detected after sunset.
  - Use Arlo’s geofencing to arm cameras when all family members leave.
