## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy What?

**First-Time Homeowners (Budget $250-$450):** The SimpliSafe system offers the best value with no contracts and affordable environmental monitoring. Perfect for those starting their security journey.

**Tech-Savvy Families (Budget $300-$500):** Ring Alarm Pro provides excellent integration with smart home ecosystems and comprehensive environmental monitoring. Ideal for Amazon households.

**Luxury Homeowners (Budget $600-$1,000):** Vivint delivers premium features, professional installation, and the most advanced AI. Best for those who want hands-off, top-tier protection.

**Renters (Budget $200-$350):** SimpliSafe's no-drill installation and portable design make it ideal for apartments. Add environmental sensors for comprehensive protection.

### Budget Considerations

- **Initial Investment:** Entry-level systems start at $200, while premium setups can exceed $1,000

- **Long-term Value:** Systems with longer warranties and no contracts often provide better value over 3-5 years
