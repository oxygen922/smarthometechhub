## Key Features to Consider (with Comparison Table)

When shopping for a smart security camera, focus on these critical features:

- **Video Resolution:** 1080p is standard, but 2K or 4K offers better detail for identifying faces or license plates.
- **Field of View:** Wider angles (130-160 degrees) cover more area but may distort edges.
- **Night Vision:** Color night vision with a spotlight is superior to black-and-white IR.
- **AI Detection:** Look for cameras that distinguish people, pets, and vehicles to reduce false alerts.
- **Storage Options:** Cloud storage often requires a subscription; local storage (microSD or USB) saves money long-term.
- **Power Source:** Battery-powered cameras are flexible; wired cameras require installation but offer continuous power.
- **Smart Home Integration:** Ensure compatibility with your existing ecosystem (Alexa, Google, HomeKit, etc.).
- **Weather Rating:** For outdoor use, IP65 or higher is essential.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Tips

1. **Choose the Right Location:** For outdoor cameras, mount them at least 8-10 feet high to avoid tampering. Ensure a clear line of sight to entry points.
2. **Wi-Fi Strength:** Most cameras require a strong 2.4GHz or 5GHz signal. Use a Wi-Fi extender if your router is far from the camera location.
3. **Battery Management:** For battery-powered models, charge fully before mounting. Set up motion sensitivity to avoid draining the battery with false alerts.
4. **Wired vs. Wireless:** Wired cameras are more reliable but require drilling and cable management. Wireless cameras are easier but need periodic recharging.

### Smart Home Integration

- **Alexa:** All three cameras work with Alexa, but Ring offers the deepest integration. You can say, “Alexa, show the front door camera” to view on Echo Show.
- **Google Assistant:** Arlo and Eufy support Google Home for voice commands and display on Nest Hub.
- **Apple HomeKit:** Only the Arlo Pro 6 supports HomeKit Secure Video, which encrypts footage and stores it in iCloud.
- **IFTTT:** For advanced automation, Arlo and Ring support IFTTT (e.g., turn on lights when motion is detected).

**Our Recommendation:** If you have a mixed ecosystem, the Arlo Pro 6 is the most flexible. For dedicated Alexa users, Ring is unbeatable.
