## Key Features to Consider (with Comparison Table)

When shopping for a smart security camera, prioritize these features:

- **Resolution:** 1080p is standard, but 2K or 4K offers better detail for identifying faces or license plates.  
- **Field of View:** Wider angles (130°–160°) cover more area but can distort edges.  
- **Night Vision:** Color night vision is superior to black-and-white for clarity.  
- **AI Detection:** Person, vehicle, and package detection reduce false alarms.  
- **Storage Options:** Local (SD card, HomeBase) vs. cloud (subscription fees).  
- **Weather Resistance:** Look for IP65 or higher for outdoor use.  
- **Smart Home Integration:** Compatibility with Alexa, Google, or Apple HomeKit.

All three cameras are wire-free, making DIY installation straightforward. Mount them 8–10 feet high for optimal coverage, angled slightly downward. Avoid placing them near reflective surfaces or direct sunlight, which can degrade video quality. For the Eufy, ensure the solar panel faces south for maximum sun exposure.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Tips

1. **Choose the Right Spot:** Position cameras at entry points—front door, back door, garage. Avoid corners that create blind spots.  
2. **Check Wi-Fi Signal:** Use a Wi-Fi analyzer app to ensure strong connectivity (2.4 GHz is more reliable than 5 GHz for long distances).  
3. **Mounting:** Use the included screws and anchors for brick or wood. For stucco, consider masonry bits.  
4. **Sync with App:** Download the respective app (Arlo Secure, Ring, Eufy Security) and follow on-screen pairing instructions. This usually takes 5–10 minutes per camera.  
5. **Test Motion Zones:** Walk through the detection area to verify alerts. Adjust sensitivity to avoid false triggers from trees or cars.

### Smart Home Integration

- **Arlo Pro 6:** Works seamlessly with Apple HomeKit Secure Video, allowing you to view feeds on Apple TV or iPad. Alexa routines can trigger lights when motion is detected.  
- **Ring Stick Up Cam Pro:** Alexa integration is second to none—say "Alexa, show the front door" to view live feed on Echo Show. Ring also supports IFTTT for custom automations.  
- **Eufy SoloCam S340:** Compatible with Alexa and Google, but HomeKit support is absent. The Eufy app offers robust scheduling and geofencing (e.g., arm cameras when you leave home).

**Pro Tip:** For maximum security, pair your camera with a smart light (like Philips Hue) to illuminate dark areas when motion is detected. All three cameras support this via IFTTT or native routines.
