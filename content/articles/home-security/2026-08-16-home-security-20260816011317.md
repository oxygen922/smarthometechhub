## Key Features to Consider

When shopping for a smart security camera, several features can make or break your experience. Here’s what you should prioritize:

### Resolution
**1080p is the minimum** you should consider in 2026. 2K and 4K offer significantly more detail, which is crucial for identifying faces or license plates. However, higher resolution means more bandwidth and storage requirements.

### Night Vision
Look for **color night vision** (using built-in spotlights) or **high-quality IR night vision** with a range of at least 30 feet. Black-and-white IR is the norm, but color night vision provides much more useful footage.

### Field of View
A **130-160 degree field of view** is ideal. Wider angles cover more area but can introduce fisheye distortion. Narrower angles provide more detail but require more cameras to cover the same space.

### Smart Detection
Modern cameras should distinguish between **people, vehicles, and animals**. Package detection is a bonus that alerts you when deliveries arrive. Look for cameras with on-device AI processing to reduce false alerts.

### Storage
Consider your options: **cloud storage** (subscription-based, typically $5-15/month), **local storage** (microSD or hub-based), or **both**. Cloud storage is more secure (thieves can’t steal your footage) but costs recurring fees.

### Two-Way Audio
This is essential for talking to delivery drivers or deterring potential intruders. Look for **noise-cancelling microphones** and speakers that don’t echo.

### Power Source
**Battery-powered** cameras offer flexible placement but require periodic charging. **Wired cameras** are more reliable but limit placement. **Solar-powered** options offer the best of both worlds for outdoor use.

### Weatherproofing
For outdoor use, look for an **IP65 rating** or higher. IP54 is acceptable for covered areas but not for full exposure to the elements.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Tips

**Step 1: Choose Your Locations**
Before drilling any holes, walk around your property and identify the optimal camera positions. Key areas include:
- Front door (facing entry points)
- Backyard (covering patios and secondary doors)
- Garage (monitoring vehicles and tools)
- Driveway (capturing license plates)

**Step 2: Check Your Wi-Fi Signal**
Smart cameras need a stable Wi-Fi connection. Use a signal strength app to verify you have at least 2-3 bars of signal at each camera location. Consider a Wi-Fi extender or mesh system if your coverage is weak.

**Step 3: Mounting**
- **For wall mounts:** Use the included template to mark screw holes. Use a level to ensure straight placement. For drywall, use the provided anchors. For brick or stucco, use a masonry drill bit and appropriate anchors.
- **For ceiling mounts:** These are ideal for covering large areas. Ensure the mount is rated for the camera’s weight.
- **Height matters:** Mount cameras 8-10 feet high for optimal viewing angles. Too low, and faces may be obscured; too high, and you’ll lose detail.

**Step 4: Power Setup**
- **Battery cameras:** Charge fully before mounting. Set a reminder to recharge based on expected battery life.
- **Wired cameras:** Use a qualified electrician if you’re not comfortable working with wiring.
- **Solar panels:** Position them to receive maximum sunlight during the day.

### Smart Home Integration

**Google Home:**
- View live feeds on Nest Hub and Chromecast-enabled TVs
- Create routines: "When motion is detected at the front door, turn on the porch light"
- Use voice commands: "Hey Google, show the backyard camera"

**Amazon Alexa:**
- View live feeds on Echo Show devices
- Create routines: "When the doorbell camera detects a person, announce it on all speakers"
- Use voice commands: "Alexa, show the garage camera"

**Apple HomeKit:**
- (Arlo Pro 6 supports HomeKit; Nest Cam does not)
- Automate with HomeKit scenes
- Use HomeKit Secure Video for encrypted cloud storage

**IFTTT (If This Then That):**
- Create custom automations across platforms
- Connect cameras to smart locks, lights, and notifications

**Pro Tip:** When integrating multiple cameras, create a dedicated SSID (Wi-Fi network name) for your IoT devices. This improves performance and security by separating smart home devices from your primary network.
