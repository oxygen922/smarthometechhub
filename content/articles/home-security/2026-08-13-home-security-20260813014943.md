### Decoding the Specs

- **Resolution:** While 4K is becoming more common, 2K offers the best balance of detail and bandwidth. For most users, 1080p is sufficient, but higher resolution helps with identifying faces and license plates.
- **Field of View (FOV):** A wider FOV means fewer blind spots. A 130-160° range is ideal for covering large areas.
- **Night Vision:** Look for "Color Night Vision" or "Starlight Sensor" technology for better nighttime clarity. Standard IR (Infrared) produces black-and-white footage.
- **Power Source:** Battery-powered cameras offer flexibility but require recharging. Wired cameras offer constant power but limit placement. Solar panels are a great add-on for battery cameras.
- **Smart Detection:** AI-powered detection is crucial for reducing false alerts. Person, vehicle, and animal detection are the most useful. Facial recognition is a premium feature that offers the highest level of personalization.
- **Storage:** Cloud storage is convenient but requires a subscription. Local storage (microSD) is a one-time cost and offers more privacy. Some cameras offer both options.
- **Smart Home Integration:** Ensure the camera works with your existing ecosystem (Alexa, Google Home, or Apple HomeKit) for voice control and automation.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Tips

- **Location is Key:** For outdoor cameras, place them 8-10 feet off the ground, angled slightly downward. This provides a clear view of faces and packages while keeping the camera out of easy reach.
- **Use the Mobile App:** Most modern cameras have an AR (Augmented Reality) view in their setup app that shows you exactly what the camera will see before you drill any holes. Use this to fine-tune your placement.
- **Consider Wi-Fi Signal:** A weak Wi-Fi signal is the #1 cause of camera performance issues. Ensure your camera is within a good range of your router, or consider a Wi-Fi mesh system or a point-to-point extender.
- **Battery Camera Mounting:** For battery cameras like the Arlo Pro 6 and Nest Cam, the magnetic mounts are incredibly versatile. You can attach them to metal surfaces, or use the included adhesive or screw mounts for other surfaces.

### Smart Home Ecosystem Integration

- **Google Home:** If you use Google Home, the Nest Cam (Battery) is the obvious choice. You can view live feeds on Nest Hubs, create automations like "If the front door camera detects a person, turn on the porch light," and even use voice commands to check on rooms.
- **Amazon Alexa:** All three recommended cameras work with Alexa. You can use voice commands like "Alexa, show the kitchen camera on the Fire TV" and incorporate them into Alexa Routines. The Arlo Pro 6 even supports Alexa's "Hunches" feature.
- **Apple HomeKit:** If you're an Apple household, the Arlo Pro 6 is the only one of the three that supports HomeKit. This allows you to view feeds in the Apple Home app, use HomeKit Secure Video for encrypted storage, and trigger automations with other HomeKit accessories. The Wyze and Google cameras do not natively support HomeKit.
