### Resolution and Video Quality
2K is the sweet spot for most homes in 2026. 4K is worth it only if you need to identify distant objects or read license plates. Don't overpay for resolution you won't use.

### Local vs. Cloud Storage
Local microSD storage saves you money long-term and keeps footage private. Cloud storage offers off-site backup in case the camera is stolen. Many buyers use both.

### AI Detection
On-device AI (processing alerts locally rather than in the cloud) is faster and more private. Prioritize cameras that distinguish people from pets and vehicles.

### Power Source
Battery cameras offer flexible placement but require recharging. Plug-in or hardwired cameras never need charging but need an outlet nearby.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation

All three Rocco cameras are designed for DIY installation, and setup takes under 15 minutes:

1. **Download the Rocco app** and create an account.
2. **Scan the QR code** on the camera to pair it with your 2.4GHz or 5GHz Wi-Fi network (the Pro supports Wi-Fi 6).
3. **Mount the camera** using the included screw kit or adhesive mount. The Essential and Pro are outdoor-ready; the Indoor model sits on a shelf or mounts to a wall.
4. **Insert a microSD card** (optional but recommended) and format it in the app.
5. **Customize detection zones** and notification preferences.

### Smart Home Integration

Rocco cameras integrate with **Amazon Alexa** and **Google Home** for voice viewing ("Alexa, show the front door"), and all three models support **IFTTT** for advanced automations. Notably, Rocco added **Matter support** in 2026, meaning the cameras can join a unified smart home ecosystem and trigger routines across brands—for example, turning on porch lights when a person is detected.

The Rocco app is clean and responsive, with a well-organized event timeline and quick access to live view. It's available for iOS and Android, and I found the notification latency to be under two seconds in testing.
