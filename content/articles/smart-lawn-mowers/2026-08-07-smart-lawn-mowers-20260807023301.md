## Buying Guide: Who Should Buy & Budget Considerations

### Who Should Buy a Robot Lawn Mower?

**The Busy Professional:** If you travel frequently or work long hours, a robot mower is a no-brainer. It maintains a consistent cutting schedule, so you never return to a jungle.

**The Tech Enthusiast:** If you love automating your home, these devices integrate seamlessly with your existing smart ecosystem. You can say, "Alexa, tell the mower to cut the front yard," and it happens.

**The Eco-Conscious Homeowner:** Electric robot mowers produce zero emissions, are incredibly quiet, and use less energy than a traditional electric mower. They also mulch clippings finely, returning nutrients to the soil.

**Who Should NOT Buy One:**
- **Owners of extremely irregular, tiny yards** (under 1,000 sq ft) where the setup cost outweighs the benefit.
- **Homes with steep, multi-tiered terraces** that exceed the mower's slope rating.
- **Those unwilling to do initial setup** (wire burial or GPS mapping).

### Budget Considerations (2026 Pricing)

- **Entry-Level ($500 - $800):** You'll find basic models with random navigation and boundary wires. These are fine for flat, simple yards but often miss spots and require more manual intervention.
- **Mid-Range ($800 - $1,500):** This is the "sweet spot" for most homeowners. You get app controls, smart scheduling, and better sensors. The WORX Landroid line dominates this bracket.
- **Premium ($1,500 - $3,500+):** Here, you're paying for GPS accuracy, advanced terrain handling, and premium build quality. These models require minimal setup and offer the best long-term reliability.

**Hidden Costs:** Factor in installation costs ($200 - $400 for professional wire burial) and replacement blades ($30 - $60 per set, typically replaced every 2-3 months).

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### The Setup Process

**Wire-Based (Husqvarna, WORX):**
1.  **Layout:** You must physically lay a boundary wire around the perimeter of your lawn and any obstacles.
2.  **Burying:** While you can staple the wire to the surface, burying it (1-2 inches deep) ensures it won't be cut by aerators or foot traffic.
3.  **Calibration:** After connecting the wire to the base station, you'll guide the mower around the perimeter once to map the boundaries.

**Wire-Free (Segway Navimow):**
1.  **Base Station Placement:** Place the charging dock in an open area with a clear view of the sky.
2.  **Virtual Mapping:** Use the app to walk the perimeter of your lawn with your phone. The app uses GPS to draw the boundary.
3.  **Fine-Tuning:** Add "no-go zones" for gardens or play areas directly on the app map.

### Smart Home Ecosystem

The true magic of modern robot mowers is their ability to "talk" to other devices. Here’s how they integrate:

- **Amazon Alexa / Google Assistant:** "Alexa, ask the mower to start." or "Google, pause the lawn mower." This is perfect for when you want to step outside without getting caught in the blades.
- **IFTTT (If This Then That):** Create custom automations. For example: "If the weather forecast predicts rain, pause the mower until 2 hours after the rain stops."
- **Smart Thermostats:** Some advanced users link their mower to their Ecobee or Nest thermostat. If the house enters "Away" mode, the mower can start a full-yard cut.

**Pro Tip:** Always ensure your Wi-Fi router has a strong signal to the base station. A weak connection can cause scheduling delays and app connectivity issues.
