### Navigation and Mapping
This is the most crucial feature. **Boundary wire systems** are reliable but require physical installation, where you lay a wire around your lawn's perimeter. **GPS-based systems** are easier to install but require a clear view of the sky. Some advanced models, like the Husqvarna, use a hybrid of both for maximum precision.

### Connectivity and Smart Home Integration
Modern robot mowers should be app-controlled. Look for models with:
- **Wi-Fi and Bluetooth:** Essential for remote control and scheduling.
- **Voice Assistant Compatibility:** Alexa and Google Assistant support is a must for true smart home integration.
- **IFTTT or Automation Support:** Allows you to trigger mowing based on other smart home actions, like your security system being armed.

### Cutting Performance
Consider the **cutting width** (wider means fewer passes) and the **cutting height range** to ensure it can handle your grass type. The quality of the cut is also determined by the blade design; look for models with sharp, pivoting blades that are easy to replace.

### Battery Life and Charging
Most robot mowers operate for 60-90 minutes before returning to their base station to recharge. Ensure the mower's runtime is sufficient to cover your lawn in one session. A good rule of thumb is that the mower should be able to finish your entire lawn on a single charge.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Process
The installation process varies significantly between systems.

1.  **Boundary Wire Systems (Husqvarna, Worx):** This involves laying a wire around the perimeter of your lawn. You can either bury it just below the soil surface or use pegs to secure it above ground. This is the most labor-intensive part. You'll also need to create a charging base station and connect it to a power source. While it takes a few hours, most manufacturers provide clear instructions and helpful videos.

2.  **Wire-Free GPS Systems (Segway Navimow):** This is dramatically simpler. You set up the charging base and then use the app to walk the perimeter of your lawn with the mower. The mower uses its GPS to map the boundary. The entire setup can be completed in under 30 minutes.

### Smart Home Integration
The true beauty of modern robot mowers is their ability to integrate with your existing smart home setup.

- **Voice Control:** You can use simple commands like "Alexa, tell the mower to start" or "Hey Google, ask the lawn mower to go back to base."
- **Automations:** With IFTTT or the native app, you can create powerful automations. For example, you can set the mower to start when your smart thermostat is set to 'Away' mode or when your smart door lock is engaged. This ensures your lawn is cut when you're not home.
- **Geofencing:** Some apps use geofencing to automatically trigger mowing when your phone leaves a certain radius, and pause it when you return. This is a fantastic way to ensure you never mow while you're trying to enjoy your backyard.
