## Key Features to Consider

Before you buy, it helps to understand what separates a great automatic mower from a frustrating one. Here's what matters most:

### Navigation Technology
- **GPS/RTK:** Satellite-based, wire-free, excellent for open lawns. Struggles under dense tree cover.
- **Camera/AI Vision:** Uses onboard cameras to map and navigate. Great for complex yards but can be affected by low light.
- **Perimeter Wire:** Traditional, reliable, but requires installation work. Still common on budget models.

### Lawn Capacity
Match the mower's rated area to your actual lawn size — and err on the larger side. A mower rated for 0.5 acres will work harder and run more often on a 0.45-acre lawn.

### Slope Handling
Most models handle 25–35% slopes; premium units reach 70%. Measure your steepest grade before buying.

### Cutting System
Look for adjustable cutting heights (typically 1.5–4 inches), mulching capability, and edge-cutting features.

### Smart Features
App scheduling, weather adaptation, zone management, GPS theft tracking, and voice assistant integration (Alexa/Google) are increasingly standard.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy an Automatic Mower?

**You're a great candidate if:**
- Your lawn is between 0.1 and 1.25 acres
- You want to reclaim 1–3 hours per week
- Your yard is relatively open (fewer dense tree canopies)
- You travel frequently or work long hours
- You have mobility limitations that make traditional mowing difficult

**You might want to wait if:**
- Your lawn is extremely steep (over 70%) or heavily terraced
- Your yard is mostly shaded by dense trees (GPS struggles)
- Your lawn is smaller than 0.1 acres (a reel mower is cheaper and easier)
- You enjoy mowing as a hobby

### Budget Considerations

For 2026, expect these price tiers:

- **Entry-level ($700–$1,100):** Small lawns, GPS navigation, basic app control. Good for testing the waters.
- **Mid-range ($1,200–$2,000):** Camera AI navigation, better edge cutting, larger capacity. The sweet spot for most homeowners.
- **Premium ($2,500–$4,000+):** Large properties, wire-free GPS/RTK, advanced obstacle avoidance, theft tracking, dealer support.

Don't forget ongoing costs: replacement blades ($20–$40 per set, typically every 6–12 months), and occasional battery replacement every 3–5 years ($100–$250).

### Installation Considerations

Wire-free models are dramatically easier to set up — often under an hour. Perimeter-wire models require trenching or staking 100–400 feet of wire, which can take a full afternoon. If you're not handy, factor in professional installation ($200–$500).

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Setting Up Your Automatic Mower

**Wire-free models (all three reviewed here):**
1. **Choose the charging station location.** Pick a flat, shaded spot near a power outlet with good sky visibility for GPS models.
2. **Download the app and create an account.** Pairing typically takes 5–10 minutes via Wi-Fi or Bluetooth.
3. **Calibrate the mower.** Follow the app's guided walkthrough — for GPS models, this involves letting the mower map your lawn perimeter.
4. **Define zones and schedules.** Most apps let you designate no-go zones, mowing schedules, and cutting heights per zone.
5. **Run a test cycle.** Watch the first mow to confirm coverage and adjust boundaries.

**Perimeter-wire models:** Add 2–4 hours for laying wire, plus careful planning around flower beds, trees, and driveways. The wire must be staked or buried 1–2 inches deep.

### Smart Home Integration

Modern automatic mowers integrate well with the broader smart home ecosystem:

- **Amazon Alexa & Google Assistant:** Voice commands like "Alexa, ask Husqvarna to start mowing" are supported on most mid-range and premium models.
- **IFTTT & SmartThings:** Advanced users can create automations — for example, pausing mowing when a smart sprinkler runs.
- **Weather integration:** Most apps pull local forecasts and automatically skip mowing before rain.
- **Geofencing:** Some models can start or stop based on your phone's location — mowing while you're at work, pausing when you arrive home.
- **Matter compatibility:** A growing number of 2026 models support Matter, improving cross-platform integration with Apple Home and other ecosystems.

**Pro tip:** Place your charging station where it gets reliable Wi-Fi. A weak signal is the most common cause of app disconnects and missed schedules.
