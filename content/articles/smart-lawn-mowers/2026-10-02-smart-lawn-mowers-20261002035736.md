### Navigation Systems: RTK GPS vs. Boundary Wire

- **RTK GPS:** Uses satellites for precise positioning. No wires needed. Ideal for complex lawns. Requires clear sky view.
- **Boundary Wire:** Physical wire defines the mowing area. More reliable in shaded areas. Requires installation (DIY or professional).

### Cutting Capacity

Match the mower's max lawn size to your actual yard. Overworking a small mower leads to longer mowing times and more wear. Always choose a model rated for at least 20% more than your lawn size.

### Slope Handling

If your yard has hills, check the slope rating. Most models handle up to 35%, but premium models like the Husqvarna and Segway handle 45%.

### Smart Home Integration

All top models support Alexa and Google Assistant. Some also work with Apple HomeKit (rare). App quality varies—Husqvarna and Segway lead in usability.

### Battery and Charging

Runtime varies from 70 to 270 minutes. Larger lawns require longer runtime or multiple charging cycles. Look for fast-charging bases.

### Safety Features

Ultrasonic sensors, bump sensors, and camera-based systems (like VisionFence) prevent accidents with pets and children. Lift and tilt sensors stop the blades if the mower is picked up.

- Installation (if professional): $200–$500

### ROI and Long-Term Savings

A robot mower can last 5–7 years. Over that time, you save on gas, oil, and maintenance for a gas mower. Plus, your time is priceless.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Options

**DIY (Boundary Wire):**
- Requires burying or pegging wire around the perimeter.
- Takes 2–4 hours for average yard.
- Tools needed: shovel, wire staples, patience.

**DIY (RTK GPS):**
- No wire. Just set up the charging station and map the lawn via app.
- Takes 30–60 minutes.
- Requires clear sky view for GPS signal.

**Professional Installation:**
- Recommended for large or complex lawns.
- Cost: $200–$500.
- Ensures optimal placement and mapping.

### Smart Home Integration

All three recommended models work with:
- **Amazon Alexa:** "Alexa, ask Husqvarna to start mowing."
- **Google Assistant:** "Hey Google, tell Worx to mow the lawn."
- **IFTTT:** Create custom automations (e.g., start mowing when you leave home).

**App Features to Look For:**
- Remote start/stop
- Scheduling
- Rain delay
- GPS tracking
- Firmware updates
- No-go zones

### Tips for Best Performance

- Keep the charging station in a shaded area.
- Clear the lawn of debris before first use.
- Update firmware regularly.
- Clean the blades and sensors monthly.
