## Key Features to Consider

When shopping for a robot lawn mower, understanding the key features will help you make the right choice. Here's what you need to know:

### Navigation Systems
- **Boundary Wire:** Traditional systems require burying a perimeter wire. Reliable but labor-intensive to install.
- **GPS/RTK-GPS:** Uses satellite signals for navigation. No wires needed but requires clear sky view.
- **Vision-Based:** Uses cameras and AI to detect obstacles and map the lawn. Good for complex environments.
- **Hybrid Systems:** Combine multiple technologies for best results.

### Cutting Performance
- **Cutting Height:** Most mowers offer 1-4 inches of adjustment. Consider your grass type and desired length.
- **Cutting Pattern:** Random vs. systematic. Systematic patterns are more efficient but require better navigation.
- **Mulching Quality:** Look for models with sharp blades and good mulching capability for natural fertilization.

### Battery Life and Charging
- **Runtime:** Typically 60-150 minutes depending on model and lawn size.
- **Charge Time:** Most mowers take 60-120 minutes to fully charge.
- **Automatic Resume:** Mowers should return to work after charging until the job is done.

### Smart Home Integration
- **App Control:** All modern mowers offer companion apps for scheduling and monitoring.
- **Voice Assistants:** Look for Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant compatibility.
- **IFTTT Support:** Allows for advanced automation scenarios.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Process

**Boundary Wire Systems (Worx Landroid):**
1. Plan your lawn layout, marking obstacles and no-go zones
2. Dig a shallow trench (1-2 inches deep) around the perimeter
3. Lay the boundary wire and secure it with pegs
4. Connect the wire to the charging station
5. Set up the charging station on level ground near a power outlet
6. Program the mower via the app

**Wire-Free Systems (Husqvarna EPOS, Segway Navimow):**
1. Place the reference station (GPS base) in a location with clear sky view
2. Map your lawn boundaries using the app
3. Set up the charging station
4. Create virtual no-go zones
5. Let the mower learn the terrain

**Pro Tip:** For best results, install your robot mower in early spring before the grass grows tall. This gives the mower time to establish its cutting pattern.
