## Key Features to Consider

Before you buy any automatic mower, you need to understand the features that separate a great machine from a frustrating one.

### Navigation Technology

There are three main types in 2026:

- **Boundary wire:** The older method. You bury a wire around your lawn's perimeter. Cheap but labor-intensive to install and prone to breaks.
- **RTK-GPS:** Uses a fixed base station plus satellite signals for centimeter accuracy. Wire-free and reliable, but needs a clear sky view.
- **LiDAR/Vision:** Uses cameras and laser sensors to map the yard. Great for shaded or obstructed areas, but can be less precise than RTK.

Many premium mowers now combine RTK and vision for the best of both worlds.

### Slope Handling

If your yard has any incline, this is critical. Entry-level mowers handle 20–25% slopes; premium AWD models like the Mammotion Luba 2 handle up to 80%. Measure your steepest section before buying.

### Cutting Height and Width

Most automatic mowers cut between 1 and 3 inches. A wider cutting deck (typically 8–10 inches) means fewer passes and faster mowing.

### Coverage Area

Manufacturers rate mowers by maximum lawn size. Always buy one rated for *more* than your actual lawn — real-world performance drops with obstacles, slopes, and thick grass.

### App and Smart Home Integration

Look for scheduling, zone management, rain sensors, and voice control via Alexa, Google Assistant, or Apple HomeKit.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy an Automatic Mower?

An automatic mower is a smart investment if:

- **You have a lawn between 0.1 and 1.5 acres.** Smaller lawns can be handled by cheaper models; larger properties need premium units.
- **You hate mowing or travel frequently.** These machines mow daily, keeping grass consistently short.
- **You want a manicured, "golf course" look.** Daily mulching produces a healthier, greener lawn than weekly cutting.
- **Your yard has manageable obstacles.** While modern mowers handle trees and flower beds well, extremely cluttered yards remain a challenge.

You should probably **skip** an automatic mower if your lawn is smaller than about 1,000 square feet (a push mower is cheaper) or if you have a very steep, terraced landscape that even AWD models can't safely handle.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $1,000:** Basic wire-based mowers for small lawns. Expect limited features and shorter lifespans.
- **$1,000–$2,000:** The sweet spot. Wire-free navigation, decent slope handling, and solid apps. The Segway Navimow fits here.
- **$2,000–$3,500:** Premium territory. Best navigation, steep-slope capability, and top-tier build quality. The Mammotion and Husqvarna live here.

Also factor in **ongoing costs**: replacement blades ($20–$40 per year), occasional battery replacement ($150–$300 after 3–5 years), and any professional installation if you choose a wired system.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

One of the biggest changes in 2026 is how easy installation has become. With wire-free models, you no longer dig trenches around your property. Here's what to expect:

1. **Set up the base station.** For RTK mowers, place the reference station in a spot with a clear view of the sky. This takes 15–30 minutes.
2. **Map your lawn.** Drive or guide the mower around your yard's perimeter and any no-go zones. The app builds the map automatically.
3. **Configure schedules and zones.** Set cutting heights, mowing days, and rain-delay rules.

Total time: **1–2 hours** for a typical suburban yard.

### Smart Home Integration

All three mowers in this review connect to Wi-Fi and support voice assistants. Here's how they fit into a smart home ecosystem:

- **Alexa & Google Assistant:** Voice commands like "Alexa, ask the mower to start."
- **Apple HomeKit:** Supported by select Husqvarna models via a hub.
- **IFTTT:** Useful for advanced automations, such as pausing the mower when your smart doorbell detects motion in the yard.
- **Weather integration:** Mowers automatically delay or skip mowing when rain is forecast, protecting your lawn and the machine.

For the best experience, place your Wi-Fi router or a mesh node within range of the charging station. A weak signal is the number-one cause of app connectivity complaints.
