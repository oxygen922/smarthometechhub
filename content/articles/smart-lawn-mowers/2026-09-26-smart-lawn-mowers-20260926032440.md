## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy an Automatic Mower?

**Automatic mowers** are not just for the wealthy anymore. With prices starting around $800 and going up to $4,000, there is a model for almost every budget. You should consider buying an **automatic mower** if:

1.  **You Value Time:** If you spend more than an hour a week mowing, an **automatic mower** pays for itself in time saved.
2.  **You Have a Physical Limitation:** For elderly homeowners or those with back problems, a robotic mower eliminates the physical strain of pushing a heavy mower.
3.  **You Want a Healthier Lawn:** Robotic mowers cut grass frequently, producing fine clippings that act as natural fertilizer. This results in a greener, thicker lawn.
4.  **You Travel Frequently:** With app control, you can ensure your lawn is maintained even when you are away on vacation.

### Budget Considerations

- **Entry-Level ($800 - $1,200):** These mowers are best for small, simple lawns (under 0.25 acres). They often require boundary wires and lack GPS navigation. *Example: Worx Landroid M.*
- **Mid-Range ($1,200 - $2,500):** This is the sweet spot for most homeowners. You get wire-free navigation, better slope handling, and advanced app features. *Example: Segway Navimow H1500N, Worx Landroid L.*
- **High-End ($2,500+):** These are for large, complex properties. They offer the best navigation, cellular connectivity, and can handle multiple zones. *Example: Husqvarna 450X EPOS.*

**Hidden Costs:** Remember to factor in the cost of installation if you choose a wire-based model. Professional installation can cost $500-$1,000. Wire-free models save you this expense.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation

Installing an **automatic mower** has become significantly easier in 2026.

- **Wire-Free Models:** If you choose a GPS-based model like the Husqvarna EPOS or Segway Navimow, installation is as simple as placing the charging station and setting up the virtual boundaries in the app. This takes about 30-60 minutes.
- **Wire-Based Models:** For models like the Worx Landroid, you will need to lay a boundary wire around your lawn's perimeter. This can be a DIY project (4-6 hours) or a professional job. The wire is usually buried a few inches underground or pegged to the surface.

### Smart Home Integration

Modern **automatic mowers** are true smart home devices. They integrate seamlessly with:

- **Amazon Alexa & Google Assistant:** Use voice commands like "Alexa, ask Husqvarna to start mowing."
- **IFTTT:** Create applets to automate your mower based on weather conditions. For example, "If rain is forecasted, delay mowing."
- **Smart Home Hubs:** Some models can be integrated into SmartThings or HomeKit (via third-party bridges) to trigger scenes, such as turning on sprinklers after mowing.

**Pro Tip:** Ensure your Wi-Fi signal extends to your yard. A mesh Wi-Fi system is highly recommended for reliable connectivity, especially for larger properties.
