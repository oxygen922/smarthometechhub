## Key Features to Consider

Before you buy any automatic mower, here's what actually matters—and what's just marketing noise.

### Navigation Technology
This is the biggest differentiator in 2026. **GPS-RTK systems** (like Segway's EFLS and Husqvarna's EPOS) offer precise, wire-free navigation but need sky visibility. **Camera-based systems** (like Worx Vision) work well in most conditions but can struggle in low light. **Boundary-wire systems** are the old standard—reliable but require installation work.

### Coverage Area
Match the mower's rated coverage to your lawn size, and then add a 20% buffer. A mower rated for 0.25 acres will work harder and run longer on a 0.25-acre lawn than on a 0.15-acre one.

### Slope Handling
Most US lawns have some grade. Check the maximum slope rating—budget models typically handle 25–30%, while premium models reach 70%. Measure your steepest section before buying.

### Cutting Height & Quality
Look for adjustable cutting height (typically 1.2–2.4 inches) and mulching blades. Automatic mowers cut frequently in small increments, which is actually better for lawn health than weekly deep cuts.

### Smart Features
App control, scheduling, rain sensors, voice assistant support, and geofencing are now standard on mid-range and premium models. Anti-theft GPS tracking and PIN locks are worth having on higher-end units.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy an Automatic Mower?

**You'll love an automatic mower if:**
- You have a lawn between 0.1 and 1.25 acres
- You want to reclaim 1–2 hours per week
- Your lawn is relatively open (fewer than a handful of large obstacles)
- You travel or have a busy schedule and want a self-maintaining yard
- You're comfortable using a smartphone app

**You might want to wait if:**
- Your lawn is extremely steep (over 70%) or heavily terraced
- Your yard is mostly dense shade with patchy grass
- You have a very small lawn (under 0.1 acres)—a cordless push mower may be simpler
- You enjoy mowing (some people do!)

### Budget Considerations

For 2026, expect these price tiers:

- **Entry-level ($500–$1,000):** Basic navigation, smaller coverage, fewer smart features. Good for simple, flat lawns.
- **Mid-range ($1,000–$2,000):** Wire-free navigation, solid app features, decent slope handling. The sweet spot for most homeowners.
- **Premium ($2,500–$4,000+):** Large coverage, advanced GPS navigation, steep slope capability, premium build quality.

Don't forget **ongoing costs**: replacement blades ($20–$40/year), occasional battery replacement ($150–$300 every 3–5 years), and app subscriptions on some models (Husqvarna's EPOS requires a subscription for full features—budget $100–$150/year).

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation

The biggest installation story in 2026 is the shift to **wire-free setups**. With GPS or camera navigation, installation is often as simple as:

1. Placing the charging station in a level spot with power access
2. Downloading the app and pairing the mower
3. Mapping your lawn (either by driving the mower around or letting it map automatically)
4. Setting schedules and no-go zones

Expect 30–60 minutes for wire-free models. Boundary-wire models still require burying or pegging wire around your perimeter—a 2–4 hour project for most yards.

### Smart Home Integration

All three recommended mowers integrate with **Amazon Alexa** and **Google Assistant**, letting you start, stop, and check status by voice. Here's what else to know:

- **Scheduling:** Set mowing windows in the app; most mowers support multiple schedules and rain delays.
- **Geofencing:** Some models can pause mowing when you arrive home or start when you leave.
- **Weather integration:** Rain sensors and app-based forecasts prevent mowing in wet conditions (important—wet grass clogs blades).
- **Matter support:** A growing number of 2026 models support Matter, improving cross-platform compatibility with Apple Home and SmartThings.
- **Security:** GPS tracking and PIN locks deter theft; register your mower's serial number with the manufacturer.

**Pro tip:** Place your charging station within Wi-Fi range—app connectivity is essential for scheduling and firmware updates.
