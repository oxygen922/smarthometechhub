## Key Features to Consider

When shopping for an automatic mower, these features will determine your satisfaction:

- **Navigation System**: Boundary wire vs. wire-free (GPS, RTK, AI vision). Wire-free is easier to install but may cost more.
- **Lawn Size Coverage**: Match the mower’s capacity to your yard. Most models cover 0.25 to 1.5 acres.
- **Slope Handling**: If your yard has hills, check the maximum slope rating (usually 25% to 45%).
- **Cutting Width and Height**: Wider decks cut faster. Adjustable height (1.5–4 inches) is essential for different grass types.
- **Smart Home Integration**: Look for Wi-Fi, app control, voice assistant compatibility (Alexa, Google Home), and IFTTT support.
- **Weather Resistance**: IPX4 or higher rating ensures the mower can handle light rain and sprinklers.
- **Battery Life and Charging**: Longer runtime means fewer interruptions. Auto-return and resume are must-haves.
- **Security Features**: GPS theft tracking, PIN lock, and alarm systems protect your investment.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy an Automatic Mower?

- **Busy professionals**: Reclaim your weekends. Set a schedule and let the robot handle the grass.
- **Elderly or physically limited individuals**: No more pushing heavy equipment or bending to trim edges.
- **Tech enthusiasts**: Integrate mowing into your smart home routine with voice commands and automation.
- **Eco-conscious homeowners**: Electric mowers produce zero emissions and are quieter than gas models.
- **Large property owners**: Models like the Husqvarna 450X EPOS can handle acreage that would take hours manually.

### Budget Considerations

- **Entry-level ($500–$900)**: Basic models with boundary wires, smaller batteries, and limited smart features. Best for small, flat lawns.
- **Mid-range ($900–$1,500)**: Wire-free navigation, better app control, and improved battery life. Ideal for most suburban yards.
- **Premium ($1,500–$4,000+)**: Satellite or AI vision navigation, large capacity, advanced obstacle avoidance, and theft tracking. For large or complex properties.

Consider long-term costs: replacement blades ($20–$50 per set), batteries ($100–$300), and potential professional installation ($200–$500 for wire-based systems). Wire-free models save on installation but have higher upfront costs.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installing Your Automatic Mower

1. **Wire-based models (e.g., Worx Landroid)**: Lay a boundary wire around your lawn perimeter, burying it just below the soil. Install the charging station near a power outlet. This takes 2–4 hours for an average yard.
2. **Wire-free models (e.g., Husqvarna EPOS, Segway Navimow)**: Place the charging station, set up a reference station (for RTK), and perform an initial mapping walk. This takes 30–60 minutes.
3. **Common steps**: Install the charging base, connect to your Wi-Fi, download the app, and set a mowing schedule. Most mowers require an initial full charge (2–4 hours).

### Smart Home Integration

All three featured models work with major smart home platforms:
- **Voice control**: “Alexa, ask [mower] to start mowing” or “Hey Google, pause the lawn mower.”
- **Automation**: Use IFTTT or the mower’s app to trigger mowing based on weather, time of day, or even your phone’s location (start mowing when you leave for work).
- **Notifications**: Get alerts when the mower gets stuck, finishes a cycle, or needs maintenance.
- **Multi-device**: Integrate with smart sprinklers (e.g., Rachio) to avoid mowing during or after watering.
