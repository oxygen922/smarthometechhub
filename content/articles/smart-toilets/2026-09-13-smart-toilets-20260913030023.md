**Performance and Cleaning**

The self-cleaning UV sanitation cycle runs automatically after each use, neutralizing 99.9% of surface bacteria on the wand. Perhaps the most impressive feature is the integrated health monitoring system. TOTO invented the modern washlet, and the Neorest RS represents the pinnacle of their bidet engineering. While it lacks some of the health-monitoring bells and whistles of the Kohler, it delivers the most refined cleansing experience on the market.

The bidet wand offers front and rear cleansing with oscillating and pulsating modes. Water pressure is infinitely adjustable, and the wand position can be fine-tuned via the illuminated remote control. The aerated water stream feels gentler than many competitors while remaining effective.

**Comfort and Design**

The Neorest RS features TOTO's signature sleek, low-profile silhouette that fits seamlessly into modern bathroom designs. The heated seat includes an auto-energy-saving mode that learns your usage patterns and reduces power consumption during inactive hours. **Smart Features**

Connectivity is handled through the TOTO Wellness app, which supports voice control via Alexa and Google Assistant (HomeKit is notably absent). The app includes user profiles, usage history, and energy consumption tracking. The auto-open lid uses a combination of motion sensors and proximity detection that works reliably without false triggers.

**Pros:**

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

**Ideal Candidates:**
- **Homeowners remodeling bathrooms** who want to future-proof their investment
- **Individuals with limited mobility** who benefit from hands-free operation and bidet cleansing

**Entry-Level ($500–$1,500):** Bidet seat attachments with smart features that retrofit onto existing toilets. These offer heated seats, bidet function, and basic remote control without replacing your toilet.

**Mid-Range ($1,500–$2,500):** Fully integrated smart toilets like the Bio Bidet Discovery DLS. You get the complete package—integrated design, tankless heating, and app control—without premium pricing.

**Hidden Costs to Consider:**
- Electrical outlet installation: $150–$400 (if not already present)

- Extended warranty: $100–$300 (optional)
- Replacement filters/consumables: $30–$80 annually

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Requirements

**Electrical:** Every smart toilet requires a GFCI-protected electrical outlet within 3 feet of the installation. If your bathroom lacks one, hire a licensed electrician—this is not a DIY task near water sources.

**Plumbing:** Most smart toilets use a standard 12-inch rough-in, matching most US residential bathrooms. Verify your rough-in measurement before purchasing. The water supply line connects to a standard 3/8-inch compression fitting.

**Flooring and Space:** Integrated units are heavier than traditional toilets (80–120 lbs). Ensure your floor can support the weight, and confirm adequate clearance for the auto-open lid mechanism.

### Smart Home Ecosystem Integration

**Amazon Alexa:** All three recommended models support Alexa. Common commands include "Alexa, preheat the toilet seat" and "Alexa, start the bidet." Routine integration lets you trigger preheating based on morning alarms.

**Google Home:** Similar functionality to Alexa. Google's routines can incorporate toilet preheating into morning or evening sequences.

**Apple HomeKit:** Only the Kohler Numera Pro X offers native HomeKit support. If you're deep in Apple's ecosystem, this significantly narrows your options.

**IFTTT and Automation:** Advanced users can create custom automations—for example, triggering the nightlight when a motion sensor detects activity after 10 PM.

### Privacy and Data Considerations

Smart toilets with health monitoring collect sensitive biometric data. Review each manufacturer's privacy policy carefully. Key questions:

- Where is data stored (local vs. cloud)?
- Is data encrypted in transit and at rest?
- Can you export or delete your data?
- Is data shared with third parties?

Kohler and TOTO both offer data deletion options, but neither provides local-only storage. If privacy is paramount, consider models without health monitoring.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do smart toilets require special toilet paper?**
No, but many users find they use significantly less toilet paper after switching. Some models recommend septic-safe paper if you have a septic system.

**Can smart toilets work during a power outage?**
Most models include a manual flush override, but bidet functions, heated seats, and smart features won't operate without power.

**Are smart toilets worth the money?**
If you value hygiene, comfort, and smart home integration—and plan to stay in your home for years—the investment pays dividends in daily quality of life. For short-term residents, a bidet seat attachment may be more practical.

**How long do smart toilets last?**
With proper maintenance, expect 10–15 years of reliable service. Electronic components may require replacement sooner than the ceramic body.

**Do smart toilets save money on toilet paper?**
Yes—most users report reducing toilet paper usage by 70–90%, which can save $100–$200 annually for a family of four.
