# The Ultimate Smart Bidet Seat Review: Upgrade Your Bathroom in 2026

## Top 3 Product Recommendations

### 1. TOTO S550e – The Luxury Standard

**Price:** $1,099 (2026 estimate)
**Best for:** Premium smart home setups and maximum comfort

The TOTO S550e remains the gold standard in bidet seats. Its elegant, low-profile design fits most elongated toilets, and the heated seat with temperature control is genuinely luxurious. The wand extends and retracts automatically, offering both posterior and feminine wash with oscillating and pulsating modes. Water pressure and temperature are adjustable via a sleek wireless remote, and the air dryer is surprisingly effective—drying you in under 60 seconds.

What sets the S550e apart is its "EWATER+" system, which uses electrolyzed water to clean the nozzle and bowl between uses. This reduces the need for harsh chemicals and keeps maintenance minimal. The seat also features a soft-close lid and a pre-mist function that wets the bowl before use to prevent waste from sticking.

Smart home integration is excellent. The S550e works with Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant for voice control, and the TOTO app allows you to customize wash settings, track usage, and even set a "night light" that gently illuminates the bowl. The only downside is the price—it's a significant investment—but for those who want the best, this is it.

**Rating:** 9.5/10

### 2. Bio Bidet by Brondell U233 – Best Value

**Price:** $399 (2026 estimate)
**Best for:** Mid-range buyers seeking premium features without breaking the bank

The Bio Bidet U233 punches far above its price point. It offers a heated seat with four temperature settings, a warm water tank (unlimited supply), and a stainless steel self-cleaning nozzle. The remote control is intuitive, with a large LCD screen that shows water temperature, seat temperature, and dryer settings.

Water pressure is strong but adjustable, and the oscillating wash mode is particularly effective. The air dryer is decent but takes a bit longer than the TOTO—about 90 seconds for full dryness. The U233 also includes a "massage" mode that cycles pressure for a soothing effect.

Installation is straightforward, and Brondell provides clear instructions. The seat fits most elongated toilets, and the quick-release mechanism makes cleaning easy. Smart home features are limited—no voice control or app—but the remote is well-designed. For the price, the U233 is an outstanding choice that delivers 90% of the luxury experience at 40% of the cost.

**Rating:** 9.0/10

**Price:** $899 (2026 estimate)
**Best for:** Tech enthusiasts with existing Kohler smart bathroom ecosystems

Kohler's PureWash E930 is designed for seamless integration with the KOHLER Konnect smart home platform. It works with Amazon Alexa, Google Assistant, and Apple HomeKit, allowing you to control wash functions, seat heating, and even the night light with your voice or through the KOHLER app.

The heated seat is comfortable, and the dual-nozzle system provides dedicated posterior and feminine wash options. Water temperature is adjustable, and the "front wash" mode is gentle yet effective. The air dryer is powerful, and the seat includes a deodorizing fan that activates automatically.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Bidet Seat?

**Luxury buyers:** If you're remodeling a master bathroom and want the best, the TOTO S550e is unmatched. Its build quality, features, and smart home integration justify the premium price.

**Mid-range shoppers:** The Bio Bidet U233 is perfect for families who want comfort and hygiene without spending over $500. It's a reliable workhorse that will last years.

**Tech enthusiasts:** The Kohler PureWash E930 is ideal if you're building a smart home ecosystem. Its health monitoring adds real value for caregivers or those with health concerns.

**Budget buyers:** Consider the Brondell Swash 1400 (around $250) for basic heated seat and warm water wash. It lacks a dryer and smart features but delivers solid performance.

### Budget Considerations

In 2026, expect to pay:
- **Entry-level (under $300):** Basic heated seat, warm water, no dryer or smart features
- **Mid-range ($300–$600):** Heated seat, warm water, air dryer, remote control, self-cleaning nozzle
- **Premium ($600–$1,200):** All features plus smart home integration, health monitoring, instant heating

Factor in installation costs if you're not handy. A licensed electrician may charge $150–$300 to install a GFCI outlet near the toilet. Most bidet seats come with a T-valve adapter for water connection, which is DIY-friendly.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Steps

1. **Check compatibility:** Measure your toilet bowl length (elongated vs. round) and ensure you have a nearby GFCI outlet.
2. **Turn off water:** Shut off the water supply valve behind the toilet and flush to empty the tank.
3. **Remove existing seat:** Unbolt the old seat and clean the mounting area.
4. **Install mounting plate:** Attach the bidet seat's mounting bracket to the toilet using the provided hardware.
5. **Connect water line:** Use the included T-valve to split the water supply between the toilet tank and bidet seat.
6. **Plug in and test:** Connect the bidet seat to power, turn on the water, and test all functions.

Most installations take 30–60 minutes. The TOTO and Kohler models include detailed video guides accessible via QR codes.

### Smart Home Integration

- **TOTO S550e:** Works with Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant. You can say, "Alexa, tell TOTO to start the wash," or set routines like "Good morning" to activate the seat warmer.
- **Kohler PureWash E930:** Full KOHLER Konnect ecosystem support. Integrates with Apple HomeKit for Siri voice control and automations. For example, you can set the seat to warm up when your morning alarm goes off.
- **Bio Bidet U233:** No smart home features, but the remote control is excellent. This model is best for those who don't need voice control.

For the best experience, ensure your Wi-Fi network is stable near the bathroom. Some models require a 2.4GHz connection.
