# The Smart Toilet Revolution: Why Your Bathroom Needs a Tech Upgrade in 2026

The smart toilet has evolved from a luxury oddity into a legitimate home upgrade. ## Top 3 Product Recommendations for 2026

### 1. TOTO Neorest NX2 (2026 Edition) – The Gold Standard

**Price:** $6,800–$7,200 (including installation)
**Best for:** Luxury homeowners who want the absolute best in comfort and technology

The TOTO Neorest NX2 remains the benchmark for smart toilets in 2026, and the latest edition refines an already exceptional product. This unit features TOTO's signature Tornado Flush system, which uses dual nozzles to create a centrifugal cleaning action that's both powerful and whisper-quiet. The bidet function now includes variable temperature control with five preset profiles, adjustable water pressure, and oscillating spray patterns.

**What sets it apart:** The NX2's EWATER+ system electrolyzes tap water to create a natural cleaning agent that sanitizes the bowl between uses. Combined with the Premist function that coats the bowl before each use, this toilet requires significantly less manual cleaning than traditional models. The heated seat now features six temperature zones for personalized comfort, and the integrated air dryer has been upgraded to deliver 40% faster drying times.

**Smart home integration:** The NX2 connects via Wi-Fi to TOTO's Smart Toilet app, allowing you to adjust settings, monitor water usage, and receive maintenance alerts. It also works with Amazon Alexa and Google Home for voice commands like "Alexa, warm the seat."

**Performance:** The flush is remarkably efficient—just 1.0 gallon per flush (GPF) for solid waste and 0.8 GPF for liquid, yet it clears the bowl completely every time. The bowl's CEFIONTECT glaze prevents debris from sticking, reducing cleaning frequency by 50%.

**Drawbacks:** The price is prohibitive for most households, and the proprietary parts mean repairs can be expensive. Some users find the automatic lid opening sensor too sensitive.

### 2. Kohler Veil Intelligent Toilet – Best Value Premium

**Price:** $3,200–$3,800 (including installation)
**Best for:** Tech-savvy homeowners seeking premium features without the TOTO price tag

Kohler's Veil line has consistently offered excellent value, and the 2026 model introduces several upgrades that close the gap with the Neorest. The Veil features Kohler's Revolution 360 flush technology, which uses a powerful siphon jet combined with a rim wash for complete bowl cleaning. The bidet offers heated water with adjustable temperature and pressure, plus a front wash mode specifically designed for feminine hygiene.

**Standout features:** The Veil's heated seat uses Kohler's Quiet-Close technology, meaning the lid closes silently every time. The integrated nightlight offers seven color options and automatically illuminates when you approach. The self-cleaning wand retracts and sanitizes itself using UV light after each use. The touchless flush can be activated by waving your hand or using the included remote.

**Smart integration:** Kohler's Konnect app provides full control over all settings, including scheduling the seat to warm up before your morning routine. The Veil works with Apple HomeKit, Amazon Alexa, and Google Assistant, and can be integrated into Kohler's larger smart bathroom ecosystem.

**Performance:** At 1.28 GPF, the Veil is slightly less water-efficient than the TOTO but still exceeds EPA WaterSense standards. The bowl's vitreous china construction with Kohler's proprietary CleanCoat finish prevents mold and bacteria buildup.

**Drawbacks:** The remote control feels dated compared to touchscreen options on competing models. Some users report inconsistent Wi-Fi connectivity.

### 3. BioBidet BB-2000 Supreme – Budget Champion

**Price:** $899–$1,099 (seat attachment, fits most standard toilets)
**Best for:** Renters or budget-conscious buyers wanting smart features without full replacement

For those not ready to replace their entire toilet, the BioBidet BB-2000 Supreme offers an impressive array of smart features as a seat attachment. This model fits most standard two-piece toilets and includes a heated seat with three temperature settings, a warm water bidet with adjustable pressure and temperature, and an air dryer with three speed levels.

**Key features:** The BB-2000 includes a wireless remote with LCD display showing water temperature, seat temperature, and dryer settings. The self-cleaning nozzle retracts after each use and can be manually positioned for personalized cleaning. The unit includes a deodorizer fan that activates during use and continues running for 30 seconds after flushing. The seat features soft-close technology and a nightlight.

**Smart home integration:** While not as sophisticated as full toilets, the BB-2000 connects to BioBidet's SmartHome app for basic controls and usage tracking. It works with Amazon Alexa for voice commands like "Alexa, turn on the bidet."

**Performance:** The bidet provides excellent cleaning performance with oscillating and pulsing spray modes. The dryer, while functional, takes longer than integrated units—typically 90 seconds for complete drying. The unit requires a GFCI outlet and water line connection.

**Drawbacks:** The plastic construction feels less premium than ceramic units. The seat may not fit all toilet shapes perfectly, and the water heater can run out during extended use.

## Key Features to Consider

When shopping for a smart toilet in 2026, these features separate the best from the rest:

### Comparison Table: Critical Features Across Price Ranges

| Feature | Budget ($500–$1,500) | Mid-Range ($1,500–$4,000) | Premium ($4,000+) |
|---------|----------------------|---------------------------|-------------------|
| **Heated Seat** | Basic (2-3 temp settings) | Multi-zone (4-6 settings) | Full-zone (6+ settings) |
| **Bidet** | Warm water, fixed position | Heated water, adjustable | Heated water, oscillating/pulsing |
| **Dryer** | Basic, 2-3 minutes | Enhanced, 60-90 seconds | Rapid, under 45 seconds |

| **Smart Integration** | Basic app, Alexa | Full app, Alexa/Google | Multi-platform, IFTTT |

### Essential Considerations

**Flush Power vs. Water Efficiency:** Look for toilets with at least 1,000 grams of waste removal capacity (MaP score). Premium models achieve this with less than 1.0 GPF.

**Bidet Quality:** Heated water is non-negotiable for comfort. Oscillating and pulsing modes provide superior cleaning compared to fixed spray patterns.

**Seat Comfort:** Memory foam seats are becoming standard in premium models. Look for ergonomic contours and at least three temperature zones.

**Noise Levels:** High-end models use pressure-assisted or dual-flush systems that are significantly quieter than traditional gravity-flush toilets.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Considerations

**Full Toilet Installation:** Expect to pay $300–$600 for professional installation of a complete smart toilet. This includes removing your old toilet, installing a GFCI outlet if needed, and connecting the water supply. Most models require a dedicated electrical outlet within 3 feet of the toilet.

**Seat Attachments:** Installation is DIY-friendly and takes 30–60 minutes. You'll need a GFCI outlet nearby and a water supply line with a T-adapter. Most kits include all necessary hardware.

**Plumbing Requirements:** Smart toilets typically require 1/2-inch water supply lines and 3-inch waste pipes. Some premium models may require 4-inch pipes for optimal flush performance.

### Smart Home Ecosystem Compatibility

**Amazon Alexa:** Most smart toilets support basic voice commands like "Alexa, flush the toilet" or "Alexa, warm the seat." The TOTO and Kohler models offer the most comprehensive Alexa integration.

**Google Home:** Similar functionality to Alexa, with voice control for seat temperature, bidet activation, and nightlight settings.

**Apple HomeKit:** Limited to premium models like the Kohler Veil. Allows integration with HomeKit scenes (e.g., "Good Night" turns off lights and lowers seat temperature).

**IFTTT (If This Then That):** Available on TOTO and Kohler models for advanced automation. Example: "If motion sensor detects bathroom occupancy after 10 PM, turn on nightlight and warm the seat."

### Future-Proofing

Look for models with over-the-air firmware updates. Both TOTO and Kohler offer this, allowing your toilet to receive new features and security patches without hardware upgrades.
