# The Ultimate Guide to Smart Toilets: Top 3 Models for 2026 and How to Choose

## Introduction

In the ever-evolving landscape of smart home technology, few innovations have transformed daily routines quite like the smart toilet. Once considered a luxury reserved for high-end hotels and tech billionaires, smart toilets have become increasingly accessible, offering features like heated seats, bidet functions, automatic flushing, and even health monitoring. As we move through 2026, the market is flooded with options ranging from budget-friendly models under $500 to premium units exceeding $5,000. But with so many choices, how do you find the perfect smart toilet for your home?

This comprehensive review cuts through the noise, presenting three top-tier recommendations for 2026, along with a detailed buying guide, installation tips, and smart home integration advice. Whether you're a first-time buyer or upgrading from an older model, this guide will help you make an informed decision. We'll explore key features like water pressure, self-cleaning mechanisms, and energy efficiency, ensuring you get the best value for your money. Let's dive into the world of smart toilets and discover which model deserves a place in your bathroom.

## Top 3 Product Recommendations

### 1. TOTO Neorest NX2 – The Premium Powerhouse

**Price (2026):** $4,800–$5,200
**Best for:** Tech enthusiasts and luxury seekers who want the ultimate bathroom experience.

The TOTO Neorest NX2 is the gold standard in smart toilet technology. Building on decades of innovation, this model features TOTO's signature Tornado Flush system, which uses dual-nozzle technology to create a powerful, water-saving flush that cleans the bowl without leaving residue. The heated seat with temperature control is a game-changer during cold winter mornings, and the integrated bidet offers adjustable water pressure, temperature, and position for a personalized clean.

What sets the NX2 apart is its advanced health monitoring capabilities. The toilet can track urine and stool consistency, providing data that syncs with your smartphone via the TOTO app. This feature is particularly useful for individuals managing conditions like diabetes or digestive issues. The self-cleaning nozzle uses electrolyzed water to sanitize itself after every use, while the automatic open/close lid and hands-free flushing reduce contact with germs.

**Pros:**
- Exceptional build quality with a 10-year warranty
- Advanced health tracking features
- Quiet operation and efficient water use (1.0 GPF)
- Easy-to-clean glazed ceramic surface

**Cons:**
- Extremely high price point
- Requires professional installation
- App can be glitchy with firmware updates

**Performance:** The NX2 delivers a flawless user experience. The bidet spray is gentle yet effective, and the warm air dryer is surprisingly powerful. The only downside is the cost, but for those who can afford it, this toilet is a lifetime investment.

### 2. Kohler PureWash E930 – The Mid-Range Marvel

**Price (2026):** $1,200–$1,500
**Best for:** Families and homeowners seeking a balance of features and affordability.

Kohler's PureWash E930 is a bidet seat attachment that transforms your existing toilet into a smart toilet. It's compatible with most two-piece toilets and offers a comprehensive set of features without the need for a full replacement. The heated seat, adjustable water temperature, and oscillating spray patterns provide a spa-like experience. The remote control is intuitive, with a backlit display for easy night-time use.

One standout feature is the UV sanitization cycle, which automatically cleans the nozzle after each use. The E930 also includes a deodorizer that uses a carbon filter to neutralize odors, making the bathroom fresher for everyone. The installation process is straightforward, with a T-connector that taps into your existing water supply. Most DIYers can complete the setup in under an hour.

**Pros:**
- Affordable compared to full smart toilets
- Easy installation on existing toilets
- UV sanitization and deodorizer
- Multiple user presets for family members

**Cons:**
- Not as sleek as integrated models
- Requires electrical outlet near toilet
- Limited health tracking features

**Performance:** The E930 excels in daily use. The bidet is responsive, and the heated seat is a welcome luxury. The only minor complaint is that the remote can be bulky, but the functionality more than makes up for it. For the price, this is one of the best smart toilet upgrades available.

### 3. BioBidet Ultimate 770 – The Budget Champion

**Price (2026):** $450–$550
**Best for:** Budget-conscious buyers and renters who want basic smart toilet features.

Don't let the lower price fool you—the BioBidet Ultimate 770 delivers impressive performance for its cost. This bidet seat offers essential features like a heated seat, adjustable water pressure, and a front/rear wash mode. The stainless steel nozzle is self-cleaning, and the unit uses a tankless water heating system for endless warm water.

What makes the Ultimate 770 stand out is its energy-saving mode, which reduces power consumption when the seat is not in use. The slim design fits most elongated toilets, and the installation kit includes everything you need. The remote control is wired, which some users may find less convenient than wireless options, but it's reliable and easy to use.

**Pros:**
- Very affordable
- Energy-saving features
- Easy DIY installation
- Good water pressure for the price

**Cons:**
- No automatic flush or lid
- Wired remote can be limiting
- Basic design lacks premium feel

**Performance:** The Ultimate 770 is a solid entry-level smart toilet. The water pressure is consistent, and the seat heats up quickly. While it lacks advanced features like health tracking or UV sanitization, it covers the basics well. For renters or those on a tight budget, this is an excellent choice.

**Other Important Features to Consider:**

- **Water Pressure:** Look for models with adjustable pressure settings. Low water pressure can lead to ineffective cleaning, while high pressure may be uncomfortable.
- **Noise Level:** Some smart toilets produce mechanical sounds during flushing or bidet operation. Check decibel ratings if you're sensitive to noise.
- **Energy Efficiency:** Many smart toilets have energy-saving modes that reduce heating when not in use. This can lower your electricity bill by up to 20%.
- **Durability:** Ceramic quality matters. Glazed ceramic resists stains and scratches better than unglazed options.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

Smart toilets are suitable for a wide range of users, but they shine in specific scenarios:

- **Families with children:** Heated seats and bidet functions make potty training easier and more comfortable.
- **Elderly individuals:** Automatic flushing and health monitoring can improve safety and independence.
- **Tech enthusiasts:** If you love integrating smart devices, a Wi-Fi-enabled smart toilet can sync with your existing smart home ecosystem.
- **People with mobility issues:** Hands-free operation reduces the need to bend or reach.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $500:** You'll find basic bidet seats with essential features. These are great for renters or those on a tight budget.
- **$500–$1,500:** This range offers mid-range bidet seats and entry-level integrated toilets. Expect better build quality and additional features like UV sanitization.
- **$1,500–$3,000:** You can find high-quality integrated toilets with automatic functions and stylish designs.
- **Over $3,000:** Premium models like the TOTO Neorest NX2 offer luxury features, health tracking, and top-tier durability.

### Installation & Smart Home Integration

#### Installation

- **Bidet Seats:** Installation is generally DIY-friendly. You'll need a T-connector for the water supply and a nearby electrical outlet. Most kits include all necessary hardware.
- **Integrated Toilets:** These require professional installation due to plumbing and electrical connections. Expect to pay $200–$500 for installation.

#### Smart Home Integration

- **Wi-Fi Connectivity:** Models like the TOTO Neorest NX2 can connect to your home Wi-Fi, allowing you to control settings via a smartphone app. You can also integrate with voice assistants like Amazon Alexa or Google Assistant.
- **Bluetooth:** Some models use Bluetooth for remote control, but range is limited to about 30 feet.
- **Zigbee/Z-Wave:** A few high-end toilets support these protocols, enabling integration with smart home hubs like SmartThings or Hubitat.

**Pro Tip:** Ensure your bathroom has a GFCI outlet within 3 feet of the toilet. If not, hire an electrician to install one before purchasing.
