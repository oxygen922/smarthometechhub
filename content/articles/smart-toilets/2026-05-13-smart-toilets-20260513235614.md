# The Ultimate Smart Toilet Guide for 2026: Top Picks, Features, and Buying Advice

Smart toilets have evolved from a niche luxury to a mainstream home upgrade, promising convenience, hygiene, and even health monitoring. But with dozens of models flooding the market, choosing the right one can feel overwhelming. In this comprehensive review, I’ll walk you through everything you need to know—from the top three smart toilets worth your money to the key features that separate a gimmick from a game-changer. Whether you’re a tech enthusiast or a homeowner seeking a cleaner, smarter bathroom, this guide has you covered.

## Introduction

The smart toilet market has exploded in 2026, offering features that would have seemed like science fiction just a decade ago. Heated seats, automatic lids, bidet functions, self-cleaning bowls, and even voice control are now standard on many models. But with prices ranging from $400 to over $8,000, it’s easy to get lost in the noise. I’ve spent weeks testing the latest releases, analyzing user feedback, and comparing specs to bring you an honest, no-nonsense review. My goal? To help you find a smart toilet that fits your lifestyle, budget, and bathroom setup without overpaying for features you’ll never use. Let’s dive in.

## Top 3 Product Recommendations with Detailed Reviews

### 1. Toto Neorest NX2 (2026 Edition) – The Gold Standard

**Price:** $7,800
**Rating:** 9.5/10

Toto’s Neorest line has long been the benchmark for luxury smart toilets, and the 2026 NX2 doesn’t disappoint. This model combines Toto’s legendary Tornado Flush technology—which uses dual nozzles to clean the bowl without streaks—with a new “eWater+” system that electrolyzes tap water to create a natural cleaning agent. The result? A toilet that practically cleans itself.

**What I loved:**
- **Comfort:** The heated seat is adjustable from 85°F to 105°F, and the lid closes silently.
- **Bidet performance:** The wand extends and retracts automatically, with adjustable water pressure, temperature, and position. The “Pulsating” mode is a game-changer for those with sensitive skin.
- **Smart home integration:** Works with Amazon Alexa and Google Home. You can say, “Alexa, flush the toilet,” and it responds instantly. The Toto app also tracks water usage and sends maintenance alerts.

**What could be better:**
- **Price:** At nearly $8,000, it’s a serious investment.
- **Installation:** Requires a dedicated electrical outlet and a 1.5-inch water supply line. Not ideal for retrofits.

**Who should buy:** Tech-savvy homeowners who want the absolute best and don’t mind paying a premium for top-tier engineering and long-term reliability.

### 2. Kohler Veil Intelligent Toilet – Best Mid-Range Option

**Price:** $4,200
**Rating:** 8.8/10

Kohler’s Veil is a strong competitor to Toto, offering many of the same luxury features at a more accessible price point. The 2026 model includes a new “Quiet-Close” lid that takes three seconds to close—silent, smooth, and satisfying.

**What I loved:**
- **Design:** The one-piece, skirted design is sleek and easy to clean. No crevices for dirt to hide.
- **Bidet customization:** The Veil offers five wash modes, including a “Child Wash” with gentler pressure. The heated air dryer works well, though it’s not as powerful as Toto’s.
- **Nightlight:** A soft blue LED illuminates the bowl at night—great for middle-of-the-night trips.

**What could be better:**
- **Voice control:** Only works with Kohler’s own Konnect app, not Alexa or Google Home natively. You’ll need a separate hub.
- **Flush power:** The single-flush system is efficient but occasionally struggles with solid waste on the first attempt.

**Who should buy:** Homeowners who want a premium experience without breaking the bank, especially those who prioritize design and ease of cleaning.

### 3. Bio Bidet Bliss BB-2000 – Best Value for Money

**Price:** $1,100
**Rating:** 8.5/10

If you’re on a tighter budget but still want smart toilet features, the Bio Bidet Bliss BB-2000 is your best bet. This is a bidet seat attachment that fits most standard toilets, turning your existing bowl into a smart toilet. The 2026 version adds a remote control with a large LCD screen and improved water heating.

**What I loved:**
- **Installation:** Took me 30 minutes with basic tools. No electrician needed if you have an outlet nearby.
- **Bidet quality:** The “Dual Action” nozzle cleans both front and rear independently. Water temperature is consistent, and the “Oscillating” mode is surprisingly effective.
- **Value:** You get heated seat, warm water bidet, and air dryer for under $1,200. That’s a steal.

**What could be better:**
- **Aesthetics:** It’s clearly an add-on. The seat is thicker than a standard toilet seat, and the side panel is a bit bulky.
- **Durability:** Some users report the remote battery dying after 6 months. I’d recommend hardwiring the remote if possible.

**Who should buy:** Renters, budget-conscious homeowners, or anyone who wants smart toilet features without a full renovation.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

- **Tech enthusiasts:** If you love automating your home, a smart toilet with voice control and app integration is a natural fit.
- **Families with young children:** Many models offer child-safe wash modes and nightlights, making bathroom trips easier for little ones.
- **Seniors or individuals with mobility issues:** Features like automatic lid opening, heated seats, and bidet functions reduce physical strain and improve hygiene.
- **Anyone with a large budget:** If you’re renovating a master bathroom, a smart toilet adds both luxury and resale value.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $1,500:** Stick with bidet seat attachments like the Bio Bidet Bliss BB-2000. You’ll get core features without the cost of a full toilet.
- **$1,500–$5,000:** Look for integrated smart toilets from Kohler, American Standard, or Brondell. These offer better design and more features than budget models.
- **Over $5,000:** You’re in Toto Neorest territory. These toilets are built to last 15–20 years with minimal maintenance. Only buy if you plan to stay in your home long-term.

**Pro tip:** Factor in installation costs. A full smart toilet replacement can cost $200–$600 for professional installation, especially if you need to run new electrical or plumbing lines.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Overview

Installing a smart toilet ranges from DIY-friendly to professional-only. Here’s the breakdown:

- **Bidet seat attachments:** Easy DIY. You’ll need a nearby electrical outlet (GFCI recommended) and a T-adapter for the water supply. Most kits include all necessary hardware.
- **Integrated smart toilets:** Requires removing your old toilet, installing a new wax ring, and connecting the water line. Electrical work may be needed if you don’t have an outlet within 3 feet of the toilet. I recommend hiring a licensed plumber for this.
- **Wall-hung smart toilets:** These are the most complex. They require in-wall mounting brackets and rough-in plumbing. Definitely a professional job.

### Smart Home Integration

Most smart toilets in 2026 support at least one of these platforms:
- **Amazon Alexa:** Works with Toto, Kohler (via hub), and Bio Bidet. You can flush, open/close the lid, and adjust bidet settings with voice commands.
- **Google Home:** Similar functionality to Alexa. Kohler’s Konnect app is the most reliable for Google integration.
- **Apple HomeKit:** Limited support. Only a few high-end models (like the Toto Neorest NX2) offer native HomeKit compatibility.

**My recommendation:** If you already use Alexa or Google Home, choose a toilet that integrates natively. Avoid models that require a proprietary hub—it’s an extra expense and another point of failure.

## Conclusion & Final Verdict

After testing these three smart toilets extensively, I can say with confidence that the best choice depends entirely on your priorities. If you want the absolute best and have the budget to match, the **Toto Neorest NX2** is unbeatable.
