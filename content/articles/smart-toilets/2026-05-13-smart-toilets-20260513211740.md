The smart toilet has evolved from a niche luxury item to a cornerstone of the modern, connected bathroom. ## Top 3 Product Recommendations for 2026

### 1. Toto Neorest NX2 (2026 Edition) — The Gold Standard

**Price:** $8,500 – $9,200 (with installation)

**Overview:**
Toto has long been the benchmark for smart toilet technology, and the Neorest NX2 for 2026 refines the formula with meaningful upgrades. The headline feature is the new **“eWater+ System,”** which uses electrolyzed water to sanitize the bowl, wand, and floor around the toilet between uses, ensuring a hospital-grade level of cleanliness. The bidet wand now features a **Triple-Action Spray** (wide, soft, and oscillating) with temperature-controlled water, adjustable via the wireless remote or the Toto Smart Home app.

**Performance:**
The flushing power is legendary—the **Cyclone Tornado Flush** uses dual nozzles to create a centrifugal water flow that cleans the bowl completely with just 1.0 gallon per flush (GPF), well below the EPA WaterSense standard. The heated seat is ergonomically contoured and offers six temperature settings, while the built-in deodorizer activates automatically upon sitting. The 2026 model also includes **PostureSense AI**, which uses pressure sensors to analyze your sitting position and offers suggestions via the app for better bathroom ergonomics.

**Smart Home Integration:**
The NX2 works natively with Apple HomeKit, Google Home, and Amazon Alexa. You can create routines, such as “Good Morning,” which triggers a warm seat, a gentle spray, and a slow-open lid. The **TotoLink** app provides usage statistics, water consumption logs, and maintenance alerts.

**Pros:**
- Exceptional build quality and durability.
- Industry-leading flush performance.
- Advanced self-cleaning and sanitization.
- Excellent smart home ecosystem.

**Cons:**
- Very high price point.
- Requires professional installation (often included).
- App can be complex for first-time users.

**Verdict:** The best overall smart toilet for those who demand the absolute best. If your budget allows, this is the one to beat.

### 2. Kohler PureWash E930 (2026 Refresh) — Best Value Premium

**Price:** $2,800 – $3,200 (seat-only retrofits available for $1,200)

**Overview:**
Kohler’s PureWash line has been a favorite for those who want premium features without replacing their entire toilet. The 2026 E930 model is a **bidet seat** that can be retrofitted onto most standard two-piece toilets, but Kohler also offers it as a complete integrated toilet for a cleaner look. The standout feature is the **UV-C Sanitization Light** that automatically lowers to sterilize the bowl after each use.

**Performance:**
The **Precise Clean** wand extends and retracts automatically, offering front and rear wash options with adjustable water pressure, temperature, and spray position. The **Quick-Release** wand makes it easy to remove for manual cleaning. The heated seat is comfortable, and the **Warm Air Dryer** has been upgraded to a high-velocity fan that dries effectively in under 60 seconds. The **Flush Boost** technology uses a built-in pump to enhance your existing toilet’s flush power, making it suitable for older plumbing.

**Smart Home Integration:**
The E930 works with the **Kohler Konnect** app, which allows you to set user profiles, adjust spray patterns, and monitor water usage. It is compatible with Alexa and Google Assistant, though HomeKit is notably absent. Voice commands like “Alexa, start bidet” work reliably.

**Pros:**
- Affordable entry point to premium smart toilet features.
- UV-C sanitization is a game-changer for hygiene.
- Easy DIY installation for the seat version.
- Excellent app control and voice integration.

**Cons:**
- The integrated toilet version is less polished than Toto.
- No AI health monitoring features.
- Drying fan can be noisy on high speed.

**Verdict:** The best value for most homeowners. You get 90% of the luxury features at a third of the price. Highly recommended for retrofit projects.

### 3. BioBidet by Bemis 770SL — The Health & Wellness Champion

**Price:** $1,800 – $2,200 (seat retrofit)

**Overview:**
BioBidet has carved out a niche for health-conscious users, and the 770SL for 2026 takes it further. This seat-focused model integrates **Medical-Grade Health Monitoring** features, including a built-in **urine analysis strip** and a **bioimpedance sensor** that estimates body fat percentage and hydration levels. Data syncs to the **BioHealth** app, where you can track long-term trends and share reports with your doctor.

**Performance:**
The **SmartSit** sensor activates the bidet automatically, and the **DualNozzle** system provides separate front and rear wands with a self-cleaning cycle after each use. The **Turbo Dry** feature uses a quiet, high-efficiency motor. The flush assist is optional, but the seat works best with standard 1.28 GPF toilets. The heated seat has a **Energy Saver** mode that learns your schedule.

**Smart Home Integration:**
The BioBidet ecosystem is closed but robust. It works with Alexa and Google Assistant for basic commands, but the full health dashboard is only available via the app. No HomeKit support. The app’s health data is HIPAA-compliant, a first for the smart toilet category.

**Pros:**
- Unmatched health monitoring capabilities.
- Affordable price for the feature set.
- Self-cleaning nozzles are very effective.
- Energy-efficient heated seat.

**Cons:**
- Health features require consistent use for accuracy.
- No integrated toilet option—must retrofit.
- App UI is functional but not beautiful.

**Verdict:** The best choice for individuals or families interested in proactive health tracking. The data is surprisingly accurate and can be a valuable wellness tool.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

- **Luxury Seekers:** The Toto Neorest NX2 is for those who want the absolute best, with no compromises on design, performance, or integration. It’s a statement piece for a master bathroom remodel.
- **Practical Upgraders:** The Kohler PureWash E930 is ideal for homeowners who want modern bidet and hygiene features without replacing their existing toilet. It’s also perfect for renters, as the seat can be easily removed.
- **Health Enthusiasts:** The BioBidet 770SL is for individuals managing chronic conditions (like diabetes or kidney issues), athletes tracking hydration, or anyone interested in a non-invasive way to monitor key health metrics daily.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $500:** You can find basic bidet seats with heated water and seat warming, but they lack smart features. These are functional but not recommended for 2026 standards.
- **$1,000 – $2,500:** This is the sweet spot for quality retrofits. The BioBidet 770SL and Kohler PureWash seat fall here. You get excellent hygiene and some smart features.
- **$2,500 – $5,000:** Integrated toilets from brands like American Standard or Brondell offer good features and better aesthetics. Kohler’s integrated PureWash is here.
- **$5,000+:** Premium integrated units from Toto, Duravit, and Villeroy & Boch. You pay for design, durability, and the most advanced features.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Tips

- **Integrated Toilets (Toto, Kohler Integrated):** These require a dedicated 110V electrical outlet near the toilet, a water supply line, and often a drain for the deodorizer. Professional installation is strongly recommended (budget $300–$600).
- **Retrofit Seats (Kohler E930 Seat, BioBidet 770SL):** Most are DIY-friendly. You need an outlet within 3 feet. Installation involves removing your existing seat, mounting the bidet seat, and connecting the T-valve to your water supply. Takes 30–60 minutes.
- **Plumbing Compatibility:** Ensure your toilet has an elongated bowl (most do) and that the seat mounting holes are 5.5 inches apart (standard).

### Smart Home Integration for 2026

- **Voice Assistants:** All three top models work with Alexa and Google Assistant. Toto is the only one with native HomeKit support, which is a major advantage for Apple households.
- **Routines & Automation:** You can create powerful automations. For example: “When I enter the bathroom after 10 PM, turn on the night light and warm the seat to medium.” This is possible with the Toto and Kohler apps via IFTTT or native integrations.
- **Energy Management:** All three models have energy-saving modes. The BioBidet learns your schedule; the Toto uses motion sensors to reduce power when the room is empty.
- **Privacy & Security:** Health data from the BioBidet is encrypted and HIPAA-compliant. Toto and Kohler do not collect health data, but they do track usage for maintenance. Review privacy policies carefully.
