# The Ultimate Smart Toilet Review: Elevate Your Bathroom Experience in 2026

**Introduction**

The smart toilet has evolved from a futuristic novelty into a must-have centerpiece for the modern, connected home. No longer just a basic commode, today’s smart toilets combine advanced bidet functionality, heated seats, automatic lids, and even health-monitoring sensors into a single, sleek package. In 2026, the market is flooded with options ranging from budget-friendly upgrades to luxury models that can analyze your urine and sync with your fitness tracker. For US homeowners looking to enhance comfort, hygiene, and home value, choosing the right smart toilet can be overwhelming. This in-depth review cuts through the noise, presenting our top three product recommendations, a detailed buying guide, and everything you need to know about installation and smart home integration. Whether you are a tech enthusiast or simply seeking a cleaner, more comfortable bathroom experience, this guide will help you make an informed decision.

## Top 3 Product Recommendations

### 1. Toto Neorest NX2 – The Gold Standard of Luxury

The Toto Neorest NX2 remains the benchmark for premium smart toilets in 2026. Building on decades of Japanese engineering, this model offers a seamless, skirted design that is both beautiful and easy to clean. Its core feature is the eWater+ system, which uses electrolyzed water to clean the bowl and wand after every use, eliminating the need for harsh chemicals. The NX2 also includes Toto’s renowned EWATER+ PREMIST, which sprays the bowl before use to prevent waste from sticking. The heated seat is ergonomically contoured, and the remote control is intuitive, offering adjustable water temperature, pressure, and position for the bidet spray. A standout addition for 2026 is the integrated air purification system that uses a fan and deodorizing filter to keep the bathroom fresh. While the price is steep (around $6,500), the build quality and reliability are unmatched. This is a true investment piece for the discerning homeowner who wants the best.

**Pros:** Exceptional build quality, eWater+ self-cleaning, powerful bidet, quiet operation.
**Cons:** Very expensive, requires a dedicated power outlet, professional installation recommended.

### 2. Kohler Veil Intelligent Toilet – Best for Design and Customization

Kohler’s Veil Intelligent Toilet is a direct competitor to Toto, offering a more modern, minimalist aesthetic that appeals to design-conscious buyers. The Veil features a sleek, one-piece design with a concealed trapway, making it look like a piece of art. Its key innovation is the Numi 2.0 interface, which includes a touch-screen remote and voice control compatibility with Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant. You can ask Alexa to “warm the seat” or “start the bidet” without touching anything. The Veil also boasts a heated seat with multiple temperature settings, a warm-air dryer, and a self-cleaning wand. A unique feature is the customizable bidet spray patterns, including oscillating and pulsating modes. In 2026, Kohler has improved the water efficiency, using only 1.0 gallon per flush (GPF) while maintaining powerful flushing performance. Priced around $5,200, it is slightly cheaper than the Toto but offers superior smart home integration and design flexibility.

**Pros:** Excellent voice control integration, beautiful design, customizable spray patterns, efficient flush.
**Cons:** Slightly less powerful bidet than Toto, remote can be finicky, higher maintenance for the electronics.

### 3. Bio Bidet Ultimate 770 – The Best Value Smart Toilet

For homeowners who want smart toilet features without the luxury price tag, the Bio Bidet Ultimate 770 is the clear winner. This is a bidet seat attachment that can be installed on most standard two-piece toilets, making it an ideal upgrade for existing bathrooms. The Ultimate 770 offers a heated seat, warm-water bidet with adjustable temperature and pressure, a warm-air dryer, and a deodorizer. It also includes a wireless remote control and a nightlight. The standout feature is the “Spa” mode, which provides a gentle, aerated spray for a more relaxing experience. In 2026, Bio Bidet has improved the water heater to provide endless warm water, a common complaint with older models. Installation is straightforward and can be done in under an hour with basic tools. At approximately $450, it provides 80% of the luxury experience for a fraction of the cost. It is the perfect entry point for anyone curious about smart toilets.

**Pros:** Affordable, easy to install, endless warm water, good feature set.
**Cons:** Not as sleek as integrated models, requires existing toilet, can be bulky on smaller bowls.

## Key Features to Consider

When shopping for a smart toilet in 2026, focus on these critical features:

- **Bidet Functionality:** Look for adjustable water temperature, pressure, and position. Oscillating or pulsating sprays offer better cleaning.
- **Heated Seat:** A must-have for colder climates. Multiple temperature settings are ideal.
- **Self-Cleaning Wand:** Ensures hygiene by rinsing the bidet wand before and after use.
- **Flush Power:** Measured in GPF (gallons per flush). Modern toilets use 1.0-1.28 GPF. Look for dual-flush options for liquid/solid waste.
- **Smart Home Integration:** Voice control (Alexa/Google) and app connectivity add convenience.
- **Automatic Lid/Seat:** Motion-activated opening and closing is a premium feature.
- **Health Monitoring:** Some high-end models measure urine or stool composition, but this is still niche in 2026.
- **Nightlight:** A soft LED light inside the bowl helps with nighttime navigation.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy Which Model?

- **For the Luxury Seeker:** The Toto Neorest NX2 is for you if you want the absolute best in durability, hygiene, and performance. It is ideal for master bathrooms in high-end homes where budget is not a concern.
- **For the Tech Enthusiast:** The Kohler Veil is perfect if you already have a smart home ecosystem. Its voice control and sleek design make it a standout.
- **For the Practical Homeowner:** The Bio Bidet Ultimate 770 is the best choice for renters or homeowners on a budget. It offers the core smart toilet experience without a major renovation.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $500:** Stick with a bidet seat attachment like the Bio Bidet Ultimate 770.
- **$1,000 – $3,000:** You can find mid-range integrated toilets from brands like American Standard or WoodBridge, but they often lack the premium features of Toto or Kohler.
- **$4,000 – $7,000:** This is the luxury tier. You get the best materials, longest warranties, and most advanced features.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation

Installing an integrated smart toilet is not a DIY project for most people. It requires:
- A dedicated electrical outlet (GFCI protected) near the toilet.
- A water supply line with a shut-off valve.
- Often, a larger rough-in space (12 inches is standard, but some models need 14 inches).

Professional installation typically costs $200-$400. For bidet seat attachments, the process is much simpler. You remove the existing toilet seat, install a T-adapter on the water line, and mount the seat. Most users can do this in under an hour.

### Smart Home Integration

- **Kohler Veil:** Works seamlessly with Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant. You can create routines, e.g., “Good morning” triggers the seat to warm and the bidet to pre-heat.
- **Toto Neorest NX2:** Does not offer native voice control, but third-party smart plugs can automate power. It relies on its own remote, which is very intuitive.
- **Bio Bidet Ultimate 770:** No smart home integration, but the remote is wireless and works well.

For true smart home integration, ensure the toilet has Wi-Fi or Bluetooth. In 2026, Matter protocol is becoming more common, promising cross-platform compatibility, but it is not yet standard in this category.
