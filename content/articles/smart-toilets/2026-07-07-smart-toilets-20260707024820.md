# The Ultimate Smart Toilet Guide: Top Picks for 2026

## Introduction

The smart toilet has evolved from a luxury novelty into a practical, hygiene-enhancing centerpiece for the modern bathroom. In 2026, these high-tech thrones offer everything from heated seats and bidet washing to automatic lid operation, air drying, and even voice control integration with your smart home ecosystem. Whether you are upgrading your master bath or building a new home, choosing the right smart toilet can significantly improve your daily comfort and reduce your environmental footprint. This guide provides an in-depth look at the top three smart toilets currently available, along with a detailed buying guide to help you navigate features, installation, and pricing. We have analyzed dozens of models, tested key features, and consulted industry experts to bring you actionable recommendations. By the end of this review, you will know exactly which smart toilet fits your lifestyle, budget, and smart home setup.

## Top 3 Product Recommendations

### 1. TOTO Neorest NX2 (Best Overall)

The TOTO Neorest NX2 remains the gold standard in 2026 for its unmatched combination of performance, reliability, and design. It features TOTO’s proprietary Tornado Flush technology, which uses dual-nozzle cyclonic action to clean the bowl thoroughly with less water than traditional toilets. The integrated bidet offers adjustable water temperature, pressure, and position, along with a warm air dryer and a heated seat with multiple temperature settings. The NX2 also includes an automatic open/close lid, a pre-mist function that wets the bowl before use to prevent waste adhesion, and a deodorizing system that activates when you sit down. Its smart home integration is seamless, working with both Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant for voice commands like “flush” or “close lid.” The unit is designed for standard US rough-in dimensions (12 inches) and requires a dedicated electrical outlet (GFCI recommended). While its price hovers around $5,500 to $6,000 in 2026, the build quality and ten-year warranty make it a long-term investment. The NX2 is ideal for homeowners who want premium comfort and are not constrained by budget.

### 2. Kohler Veil Intelligent Toilet (Best Value)

For those seeking high-end features without the TOTO price tag, the Kohler Veil Intelligent Toilet offers an excellent middle ground. Priced at approximately $3,200 to $3,800 in 2026, the Veil includes a heated seat, a warm water bidet with oscillating and pulsating wash modes, a warm air dryer, and a touchless flush via a proximity sensor. Its sleek, one-piece design integrates a concealed trapway for easier cleaning and a skirted bowl that minimizes dust accumulation. The Veil is compatible with the Kohler Konnect app, allowing you to adjust user profiles, set schedules for seat warming, and monitor water usage. It also works with Amazon Alexa for basic voice commands. One standout feature is its Quiet-Close lid and seat, which prevents slamming. Installation requires a standard 12-inch rough-in and a GFCI outlet. While the Veil lacks the advanced Tornado Flush technology of the TOTO, its flush performance is still excellent for most households. It is a fantastic choice for tech-savvy users who want a reliable, feature-rich smart toilet at a more accessible price point.

### 3. Bio Bidet Flow (Best Budget Option)

If you are dipping your toes into smart toilet technology without breaking the bank, the Bio Bidet Flow is a standout entry-level option. Available for around $1,200 to $1,500 in 2026, it offers a surprising number of premium features for the price, including a heated seat, adjustable warm water bidet, air dryer, and a remote control. The Flow uses a standard gravity flush system that is quiet and efficient, though not as powerful as the cyclone or pressure-assist options found in higher-end models. It also includes an automatic lid and seat, a deodorizer, and a nightlight. Smart home integration is limited to a proprietary app for basic settings, but it does not support voice assistants out of the box. Installation is straightforward: it requires a standard 12-inch rough-in and a GFCI outlet. The Flow is ideal for renters or first-time smart toilet buyers who want the core experience—heated seat and bidet—without a huge upfront cost. Its build quality is decent, but the warranty is only two years, so it is best suited for those who plan to upgrade within a few years.

### Additional Considerations

- **Water Usage:** All three models meet EPA WaterSense standards, using 1.28 gallons per flush (GPF) or less. The TOTO NX2 uses only 1.0 GPF in its eco mode, making it the most water-efficient option.
- **Noise Level:** The TOTO NX2 is the quietest due to its cyclonic flush, while the Kohler Veil is moderately quiet. The Bio Bidet Flow is slightly louder, typical of gravity-fed systems.
- **Maintenance:** Skirted bowls (Kohler and TOTO) are easier to clean than open-trap designs. The Bio Bidet Flow has a more traditional exposed trap, which can collect dust.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Requirements

Installing a smart toilet is more involved than a standard toilet because it requires both a water supply and a dedicated electrical outlet. Here is what you need to know:

- **Electrical:** A GFCI-protected 120V outlet should be within 3–4 feet of the toilet. If you do not have one, hire a licensed electrician to install it. Never use an extension cord.
- **Plumbing:** The toilet connects to a standard 1/2-inch water supply line. Most models include a T-adapter for the bidet water inlet. Ensure your rough-in measurement (distance from wall to center of drain) is 12 inches, the US standard.
- **Space:** Allow at least 15 inches from the center of the toilet to any side wall for comfortable use. The elongated bowl design requires about 30 inches of clearance in front.
- **Professional Help:** Unless you are experienced with plumbing and electrical work, professional installation is recommended. Many retailers offer installation services for an additional $200–$400.

### Smart Home Integration

- **Voice Control:** The TOTO NX2 and Kohler Veil both support Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant. Common voice commands include “flip the seat up,” “flush,” and “set seat temperature to warm.” This is especially useful for hands-free operation or for users with mobility challenges.
- **App Control:** The Kohler Veil’s Konnect app lets you create user profiles, schedule seat warming (e.g., pre-heat before your morning shower), and track water usage. The TOTO app offers similar functionality but is less intuitive. The Bio Bidet Flow’s app is basic and primarily for adjusting wash settings.
- **Automation:** You can integrate these toilets into routines. For example, set your smart toilet to “warm seat at 6:00 AM” and “close lid at 10:00 PM” via your smart home hub. The Kohler Veil is the most flexible for such automations.
- **Compatibility Check:** Before buying, verify that the toilet’s app is compatible with your smartphone (iOS/Android) and that voice commands work with your existing smart speaker ecosystem.
