## Top 3 Smart Toilet Recommendations for 2026

### 1. TOTO Neorest NX2 — Best Overall Smart Toilet

**Price:** $9,800 – $11,200 (estimated 2026)

TOTO continues to dominate the premium smart toilet space, and the Neorest NX2 is their flagship masterpiece. This isn't just a toilet—it's a statement piece that belongs in a luxury spa.

**Design & Build Quality**

The NX2 features TOTO's signature Actilight self-cleaning technology, which uses UV light and a titanium dioxide-coated bowl to break down waste and bacteria after every use. The result? You'll never scrub a toilet bowl again. The sleek, skirted design hides all plumbing and wiring, creating a seamless, modern silhouette that complements any high-end bathroom.

**Performance**

The Tornado Flush system delivers a powerful, quiet, and incredibly efficient flush using just 0.8 or 1.0 gallons per flush (GPF)—well below the EPA's 1.28 GPF standard. The bidet functionality is where the NX2 truly shines: adjustable water temperature, pressure, and oscillating spray patterns are all controlled via a wireless remote or the integrated side panel.

**Smart Features**

- **EWATER+ electrolyzed water** sanitizes the wand and bowl after every use
- **Auto open/close lid** with proximity sensors
- **Heated seat** with temperature memory
- **Warm air dryer** with adjustable settings
- **Nightlight** for midnight visits
- **Premist** wets the bowl before use to prevent waste adhesion

**Verdict:** If budget is no object, the TOTO Neorest NX2 is the gold standard. It's an investment, but one that pays dividends in hygiene, comfort, and sheer luxury every single day.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

Smart toilets aren't for everyone, but they're transformative for specific households:

**Ideal Candidates:**
- **Seniors and people with mobility issues** — Hands-free operation, heated seats, and bidet functionality reduce the need for bending and reaching.
- **People with medical conditions** — Bidets are gentler and more hygienic than toilet paper, beneficial for hemorrhoids, IBS, and post-surgery recovery.
- **Eco-conscious homeowners** — Smart toilets use significantly less toilet paper, and high-efficiency models reduce water usage.
- **Tech enthusiasts** — If you love automating your home, a smart toilet is the ultimate conversation piece.
- **Luxury renovators** — A high-end smart toilet elevates any bathroom remodel.

**Who Should Skip:**
- **Renters** — Unless you have landlord approval, installing a smart toilet is a significant investment you can't take with you.
- **Tight budgets** — A bidet seat attachment is a better entry point than a full smart toilet.
- **Older homes with outdated plumbing** — Some smart toilets require electrical outlets near the toilet and may need plumbing upgrades.

### Budget Considerations

**Under $1,000:** Bidet seat attachments like the Bio Bidet BB-2000 offer smart features without replacing your toilet.

**$1,000 – $3,000:** Mid-range smart toilets from brands like Kohler and American Standard offer full functionality at accessible prices.

**$3,000 – $6,000:** Premium smart toilets with advanced features like auto-open lids and app connectivity.

**$6,000+:** Luxury flagship models like the TOTO Neorest series with all the bells and whistles.

**Hidden Costs to Consider:**
- **Electrical work:** You'll need a GFCI outlet near the toilet, which may require hiring an electrician ($150 – $500).
- **Installation:** Professional installation for a full smart toilet runs $250 – $600.
- **Water filter replacements:** Some models require periodic filter changes ($20 – $50 annually).
- **Warranty extensions:** Worth considering for high-end models.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Requirements

**For Bidet Seat Attachments:**
- Standard two-piece toilet with a compatible bowl shape
- Nearby electrical outlet (GFCI recommended)
- Basic DIY skills; 30 – 60 minutes

**For Full Smart Toilets:**
- Removal of existing toilet
- Electrical outlet within 3 feet of the toilet
- Water supply line with adequate pressure
- Floor flange in good condition
- Professional installation recommended; 2 – 4 hours

### Smart Home Integration

Most high-end smart toilets in 2026 offer some level of smart home connectivity:

- **Voice control:** TOTO and Kohler models integrate with Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant for basic commands like "open lid" or "start bidet."
- **App control:** Track usage, adjust settings, and create user profiles for multiple family members.
- **Health monitoring:** Some premium models now include urine analysis and stool monitoring, sending health insights to your smartphone—a feature that's still evolving but shows promise.
- **Automation:** Pair your smart toilet with smart lighting so the nightlight activates when you enter the bathroom after dark.

**Compatibility Notes:**
- TOTO's app works with both iOS and Android.
- Kohler's Konnect platform integrates with most major smart home ecosystems.
- Bio Bidet's remote-based system doesn't require Wi-Fi, making it more reliable but less connected.
