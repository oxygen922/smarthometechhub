# The Ultimate Smart Bidet Seat Review for 2026: Upgrade Your Bathroom with Intelligence and Comfort

In 2026, the humble toilet has undergone a remarkable transformation. Smart bidet seats have evolved from luxury hotel novelties into essential home upgrades, offering personalized hygiene, heated seating, and even voice-controlled operation. ## Top 3 Product Recommendations

### 1. TOTO S550e Washlet+ with EWATER+ (2026 Edition)
**Best Overall for Luxury and Smart Home Integration**

The TOTO S550e remains the gold standard in smart bidet seats, and the 2026 edition refines an already excellent product. This model features TOTO’s proprietary EWATER+ technology, which electrolyzes tap water to create a gentle, cleansing spray that eliminates bacteria without harsh chemicals. The heated seat now includes six temperature settings, and the warm air dryer has been upgraded to a dual-fan system for faster drying. Smart home integration is seamless: it works with Amazon Alexa, Google Assistant, and Apple HomeKit, allowing you to preheat the seat, adjust water pressure, or activate a gentle “night light” mode using voice commands. The remote control is sleek and backlit, and the deodorizing carbon filter is replaceable every six months. At an estimated $1,099, it’s expensive, but the build quality and longevity justify the price for those who want the best.

**Pros:** Superior water cleansing, excellent smart home support, durable construction, quiet operation  
**Cons:** Premium price, requires GFCI electrical outlet, longer installation time  
**Best for:** Tech enthusiasts and luxury homeowners

### 2. Bio Bidet Ultimate U990 (2026 Refresh)
**Best Value for High-End Features**

Bio Bidet has long been a favorite among smart toilet reviewers, and the U990 refresh for 2026 adds several welcome upgrades. The most notable is the new “SmartSitz” heated seat with occupancy sensors that automatically warm the seat when you approach. The bidet offers five spray modes: front, rear, oscillating, pulsating, and a “child” mode with lower pressure. Water temperature is adjustable, and the unit includes a stainless steel dual-nozzle system with self-cleaning before and after each use. Smart home integration is limited to Alexa and Google Assistant, but the companion app provides detailed usage statistics and customizable presets. The estimated price of $699 makes it a strong competitor to TOTO, offering 90% of the features at two-thirds the cost. Installation is straightforward, and the unit fits most elongated toilets.

**Pros:** Excellent value, intuitive controls, child-friendly mode, easy installation  
**Cons:** No Apple HomeKit support, somewhat bulky design, app can be laggy  
**Best for:** Families and budget-conscious buyers wanting premium features

### 3. Kohler PureWash E930
**Best for Modern Design and Hygiene**

Kohler’s PureWash E930 stands out for its minimalist aesthetic and focus on hygiene. The 2026 model features a UV-C light sanitizing cycle that sterilizes the nozzles after every use, plus a new “antibacterial” seat coating that reduces microbial growth. The bidet offers three water temperatures and five pressure levels, all adjustable via a wireless remote or the Kohler Konnect app. Smart home integration includes Alexa, Google Assistant, and IFTTT support, allowing you to create routines like “Good Morning” that preheats the seat and activates the bidet. The heated seat is comfortable and the warm air dryer is effective but slightly slower than TOTO’s. At an estimated $849, it sits between the TOTO and Bio Bidet in price, but the design and hygiene features make it an excellent choice for modern bathrooms. Installation requires a standard electrical outlet and about 45 minutes.

**Pros:** Excellent hygiene features, sleek design, strong app integration, easy cleaning  
**Cons:** Slower dryer, remote control feels cheap, no oscillating spray mode  
**Best for:** Design-conscious buyers and those prioritizing cleanliness

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Bidet Seat?

- **Tech-Savvy Homeowners:** If you already have smart lights, thermostats, and locks, adding a bidet seat with voice control is a natural next step. The TOTO S550e is your best bet.
- **Families with Children:** The Bio Bidet U990’s child mode and lower price make it perfect for households with kids who need gentler cleansing.
- **Eco-Conscious Users:** Reducing toilet paper usage is a major benefit. All three models help, but the Kohler’s UV sanitization ensures maximum hygiene with minimal waste.
- **People with Mobility Issues:** Heated seats, easy-to-reach remotes, and warm water bidets improve comfort and independence. Look for models with large, backlit remotes.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $500:** You’ll find basic bidet seats with heated seats and warm water, but smart home features are rare. Consider the Brondell Swash 1400 if you’re on a strict budget.
- **$500–$900:** The sweet spot. The Bio Bidet U990 and Kohler PureWash E930 offer excellent features without breaking the bank.
- **Over $900:** Premium options like the TOTO S550e deliver the best build quality, smart home integration, and longevity. Worth it if you plan to keep the seat for 5+ years.

### Installation Considerations

- **Electrical Requirements:** All smart bidet seats need a GFCI-protected electrical outlet near the toilet. If you don’t have one, hire an electrician (cost: $150–$300).
- **Water Supply:** You’ll need a T-valve to split water from the toilet supply line. Most units include one.
- **Toilet Shape:** Measure your toilet bowl (elongated vs. round) before purchasing. Most high-end models only fit elongated bowls.
- **DIY vs. Professional:** Installation takes 30–60 minutes for most homeowners. If you’re uncomfortable with plumbing, a plumber can do it for $100–$150.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Step-by-Step Installation Overview

1. **Turn off water supply** and flush the toilet to empty the tank.
2. **Remove the existing toilet seat** and clean the mounting area.
3. **Install the T-valve** between the water supply line and toilet tank.
4. **Mount the bidet seat** onto the toilet bowl using the included brackets.
5. **Connect the water line** from the T-valve to the bidet seat.
6. **Plug in the power cord** to a GFCI outlet.
7. **Turn on water** and check for leaks. Adjust as needed.

### Smart Home Integration Tips

- **Alexa/Google Assistant:** Use voice commands like “Alexa, turn on the bidet seat” or “Hey Google, preheat the toilet seat.” Ensure your smart speaker is in the same room or on the same network.
- **Apple HomeKit (TOTO only):** Add the TOTO S550e to the Home app for Siri control and automations. For example, set a “Good Night” scene that turns off the night light and lowers seat temperature.
- **IFTTT (Kohler only):** Create applets like “If I arrive home, preheat the seat” using your phone’s location.
- **Companion Apps:** Use the Bio Bidet or Kohler app to track water usage, adjust presets, and receive maintenance reminders (e.g., filter replacement).
