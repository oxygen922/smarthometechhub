# Smart Toilet Review 2026: The Best Intelligent Bidet Toilets Worth Buying

## Introduction

The smart toilet has quietly become one of the most transformative upgrades in the modern American bathroom. What was once a luxury reserved for high-end hotels and Japanese homes is now a mainstream smart home category, with heated seats, warm-water bidet washing, self-cleaning nozzles, and app-based controls available at nearly every price point. In 2026, the market has matured dramatically: brands like TOTO, Kohler, and Bio Bidet now compete with feature-packed newcomers, and prices have fallen while reliability has climbed.

But not every smart toilet is worth your money. Some models overpromise on "AI" features, others skimp on water heating, and a few are a nightmare to install. In this in-depth smart toilet review, I break down the three best models of 2026, explain the key features that actually matter, and help you decide whether a smart toilet is right for your home. Whether you're remodeling or simply want a warmer seat on a cold morning, this guide will save you time, money, and buyer's remorse.

## Top 3 Smart Toilet Recommendations

### 1. TOTO Neorest AS Pro — Best Overall Smart Toilet

**Price (2026 est.): $4,800–$5,600**

TOTO remains the gold standard in the smart toilet world, and the Neorest AS Pro is the company's most refined residential model to date. This is a fully integrated one-piece toilet with a tankless design, meaning it requires a strong water supply line but delivers an unlimited supply of warm wash water — no waiting between uses.

**Performance and wash quality**

The AS Pro uses TOTO's signature eWater+ system, which mists the bowl with electrolyzed water after each use to reduce bacteria and keep the bowl cleaner between scrubbings. The bidet offers front and rear washing with adjustable temperature, pressure, and nozzle position, plus a "tornado" flushing system that's both powerful and remarkably quiet.

**Comfort features**

The heated seat has five temperature settings and an auto-open/auto-close lid triggered by a proximity sensor. There's a built-in nightlight, a warm-air dryer, and a deodorizer that activates automatically. The remote control is intuitive, and the toilet integrates with TOTO's app for personalized user profiles — so every family member gets their own preferred settings.

**Smart home integration**

The AS Pro supports voice control through Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant, and it can be added to smart home routines (for example, activating the nightlight when your bedroom lights dim). It does not support Matter natively, which is a minor drawback in 2026, but the app and voice integrations cover most needs.

**Verdict:** If budget is not a concern and you want the most reliable, best-built smart toilet on the market, the Neorest AS Pro is the one to buy. It's expensive, but owners consistently report years of flawless operation.

### 2. Kohler Numera Smart Toilet — Best Value Premium Pick

**Price (2026 est.): $2,400–$2,900**

Kohler's Numera hits a sweet spot: it delivers most of the flagship experience at roughly half the price of the TOTO Neorest. It's a one-piece, tankless design with a sleek, minimalist profile that looks great in contemporary bathrooms.

**Performance and wash quality**

The Numera features a stainless steel wand with adjustable front and rear wash modes, plus a "pulsating" massage setting that users love. Water heating is instantaneous, and the bowl uses Kohler's Revolution Vortex flushing technology, which cleans the bowl thoroughly with every flush.

**Comfort features**

You get a heated seat, warm-air dryer, auto-open/close lid, and a deodorizing filter. The standout is Kohler's Konnect app, which lets you save up to five user profiles, adjust every setting, and receive maintenance alerts when the filter or descaler needs attention.

**Smart home integration**

This is where the Numera shines. It works with Alexa, Google Assistant, and — importantly — Matter, making it one of the most future-proof smart toilets available. You can trigger a "night mode" via voice or automate seat warming based on your morning routine.

**Verdict:** The Kohler Numera is the best smart toilet for most buyers who want premium quality without paying flagship prices. Its Matter support gives it a longevity edge over the TOTO.

### 3. Bio Bidet Discovery DLS — Best Mid-Range Smart Toilet

**Price (2026 est.): $1,300–$1,600**

If you want a true integrated smart toilet without crossing the $2,000 line, the Bio Bidet Discovery DLS is the standout. It's a one-piece design with a tankless heater and an impressive feature list for the money.

**Performance and wash quality**

The Discovery DLS offers front, rear, and turbo wash modes with adjustable pressure and a self-cleaning stainless nozzle. The warm-water supply is unlimited thanks to the tankless heater, and the flush is a standard but reliable siphon jet.

**Comfort features**

You get a heated seat, warm-air dryer, auto-flush, nightlight, and a wireless remote. It lacks the auto-open lid of pricier models, but that's a reasonable trade-off at this price. The deodorizer is effective, and the seat is slim and comfortable.

**Smart home integration**

The Discovery DLS does not offer native voice control, but it works with optional smart plugs for basic automation. For most users, the remote is enough. If deep smart home integration is essential, step up to the Kohler.

**Verdict:** The best budget-friendly integrated smart toilet of 2026. It's ideal for first-time smart toilet buyers who want the full bidet experience without a luxury price tag.

### The features that matter most

**Tankless vs. tank water heating.** Tankless (instantaneous) heaters provide unlimited warm water and are the modern standard. Tank models can run out of warm water during long use and are best avoided in 2026.

**Wash modes and nozzle adjustment.** At minimum, look for front and rear wash with adjustable pressure and position. Massage or pulsating modes are nice bonuses.

**Heated seat and dryer.** These are the two features owners say they can't live without. Nearly all smart toilets include a heated seat; a warm-air dryer is worth paying a little more for.

**Auto open/close and auto flush.** These hands-free features improve hygiene and convenience, but they add cost. They're most valuable in households with kids or elderly users.

**Smart home protocol.** If you're building a connected home, prioritize Matter support (Kohler) or at least Alexa/Google compatibility (TOTO). Otherwise, the remote control is sufficient.

**Water and energy efficiency.** Look for WaterSense certification and an energy-saving mode that lowers seat temperature when the toilet isn't in use.

## Buying Guide

### Who should buy a smart toilet?

A smart toilet is a worthwhile investment if you:

- Want improved hygiene and comfort, especially for elderly family members or anyone with limited mobility.
- Are remodeling a bathroom and want a modern, integrated look.
- Live in a cold climate where a heated seat is a daily luxury.
- Have a household with multiple users who would benefit from personalized settings.

You may want to skip it if you're on a tight budget, renting (installation is semi-permanent), or uncomfortable with electronic components in a wet environment. In those cases, a smart toilet seat (bidet seat) attached to your existing toilet is a smarter, cheaper alternative.

### Budget considerations

- **Under $500:** Smart toilet seats (bidet attachments) — excellent value, less integrated.
- **$1,000–$1,800:** Entry-level integrated smart toilets like the Bio Bidet Discovery DLS.
- **$2,000–$3,000:** Mid-to-premium models like the Kohler Numera — the sweet spot.
- **$4,000+:** Flagship models like the TOTO Neorest AS Pro.

Don't forget installation costs, which can run $200–$600 for a plumber, plus the cost of an electrical outlet near the toilet if one doesn't exist (an electrician may charge $150–$400).

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation basics

Integrated smart toilets require three connections: a standard water supply, a standard drain, and — critically — a GFCI-protected electrical outlet within about three feet of the toilet. If your bathroom lacks an outlet, factor in the cost of an electrician.

Most homeowners hire a licensed plumber for installation, especially for tankless models that require a higher water flow rate. DIY installation is possible for confident homeowners, but the electrical work should always be done by a professional.

### Smart home integration tips

- **Pair with voice assistants** to control night mode, seat warming, or to trigger a "clean" routine.
- **Use routines** to automate the deodorizer or nightlight based on time of day.
- **Prioritize Matter** if you want your smart toilet to work with future hubs and platforms.
- **Keep firmware updated** through the manufacturer's app for security and performance improvements.

A smart toilet works best as part of a broader smart bathroom — pair it with a smart mirror, motion-sensing lights, and a smart scale for a fully connected morning routine.
