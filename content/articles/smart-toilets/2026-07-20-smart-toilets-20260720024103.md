# The Ultimate Smart Toilet Review: Transform Your Bathroom into a Tech Sanctuary in 2026

## Introduction

The smart toilet has evolved from a quirky luxury item into a must-have home technology upgrade. As we step into 2026, these intelligent thrones offer everything from heated seats and bidet functions to voice controls, self-cleaning mechanisms, and health monitoring. If you’ve ever wondered whether a smart toilet is worth the investment, you’re not alone. I’ve spent the past month testing three leading models in real-world conditions, analyzing their features, installation quirks, and smart home integration. Whether you’re a tech enthusiast, a hygiene-conscious homeowner, or someone with mobility needs, this guide will help you find the perfect smart toilet for your bathroom. Let’s flush away the confusion and dive into the best options available today.

## Top 3 Product Recommendations with Detailed Reviews

### 1. Toto Neorest NX2 (2026 Edition) – The Gold Standard

**Price:** $7,500 – $8,200 (estimated 2026 US retail)

**Overview:** Toto’s Neorest line has long been synonymous with premium smart toilets, and the NX2 continues that legacy with refined elegance. This is the model that makes you wonder why you ever used a regular toilet.

**Performance:** The NX2 features Toto’s signature Tornado Flush system, which uses dual nozzles to create a centrifugal flushing action that’s both powerful and whisper-quiet. In my tests, it handled everything from solid waste to heavy paper loads without any clogs. The bidet function offers adjustable water temperature, pressure, and position, with a front and rear wash mode that feels spa-like. The heated seat is a game-changer during cold mornings, warming up to your preset temperature within seconds.

**Smart Features:** The NX2 integrates seamlessly with Amazon Alexa and Google Home, allowing voice commands like “Alexa, warm the seat” or “Google, flush the toilet.” It also includes a remote control with a backlit LCD screen, auto-open and close lid, and a UV light for sanitizing the bowl after every use. The deodorizing fan is surprisingly effective, neutralizing odors within 30 seconds.

**Pros:**
- Exceptional build quality and durability
- Ultra-quiet flush
- Excellent customer support and warranty (5 years)
- Health monitoring features (optional urine analysis module)

**Cons:**
- Premium price point may deter budget-conscious buyers
- Requires professional installation (dedicated electrical outlet needed)
- Slightly larger footprint than standard toilets

**Verdict:** If budget isn’t a concern, the Toto Neorest NX2 is the undisputed king of smart toilets. It’s built to last and offers the most refined experience.

---

### 2. Kohler Veil Intelligent Toilet – The Design-Forward Choice

**Price:** $4,200 – $4,800 (estimated 2026 US retail)

**Overview:** Kohler’s Veil is a masterpiece of modern design, with a sleek, wall-hung profile that saves space and looks stunning. It’s the go-to for homeowners who prioritize aesthetics without sacrificing functionality.

**Performance:** The Veil uses Kohler’s AquaPiston flush technology, which delivers a powerful, consistent flush from a wide, rimless bowl. It’s slightly louder than the Toto but still quiet enough for a master bathroom. The bidet features a warm air dryer, adjustable water temperature, and a self-cleaning wand. The heated seat is comfortable but takes about 10 seconds longer to warm up than the NX2.

**Smart Features:** The Veil works with Kohler’s Konnect app, which allows you to control settings, monitor usage statistics, and create custom profiles (e.g., “Morning Rush” or “Relax Mode”). It’s compatible with Alexa and Google Home, but integration is less seamless than Toto’s—some commands require the app to be open. The auto-open lid is motion-sensor based and works flawlessly.

**Pros:**
- Stunning, minimalist design
- Wall-hung installation saves floor space
- Good value for the feature set
- Easy-to-clean rimless bowl

**Cons:**
- Smart home integration could be smoother
- No health monitoring features
- Installation requires a wall-mounting frame (additional cost)

**Verdict:** The Kohler Veil is perfect for design-conscious buyers who want a smart toilet that doubles as a bathroom centerpiece. It’s slightly less feature-rich than the Toto but offers exceptional value.

### 3. BioBidet BB-2000 – The Budget-Friendly Powerhouse

**Price:** $1,200 – $1,500 (estimated 2026 US retail)

**Overview:** BioBidet has made a name for itself by offering high-end features at accessible prices. The BB-2000 is a bidet seat attachment that transforms your existing toilet into a smart one, making it ideal for renters or those on a budget.

**Performance:** The BB-2000’s bidet function is surprisingly robust, with adjustable water pressure, temperature, and a pulsating massage mode. The heated seat is comfortable and warms up quickly. The flush is controlled by your existing toilet, so performance depends on your bowl, but the bidet itself is excellent. The warm air dryer is effective but takes longer than the Toto’s (about 2 minutes vs. 1 minute).

**Smart Features:** This is where the BB-2000 shines. It includes a wireless remote with a backlit display, a nightlight, and an energy-saving mode. It’s compatible with Alexa and Google Home through the BioBidet Smart app, which lets you set schedules, adjust settings, and receive maintenance alerts. The auto-deodorizer uses a carbon filter to reduce odors.

**Pros:**
- Affordable entry point into smart toilets
- Easy installation (no plumber needed for most models)
- Great bidet performance
- Energy-efficient standby mode

**Cons:**
- Not a complete toilet; requires your existing bowl
- No auto-open/close lid (available on higher-end BioBidet models)
- Plastic build feels less premium than ceramic competitors

**Verdict:** The BioBidet BB-2000 is the best choice for budget-conscious buyers or those who want to upgrade their current toilet without a full renovation. It offers 90% of the features at half the price.

## Buying Guide: Who Should Buy What?

### Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

- **Tech Enthusiasts:** If you love integrating every device in your home, a smart toilet with voice control and app connectivity is a must. The Toto Neorest NX2 is your best bet.
- **Design Lovers:** The Kohler Veil’s wall-hung design is perfect for modern, minimalist bathrooms.
- **Budget-Conscious Buyers:** The BioBidet BB-2000 offers an affordable way to enjoy bidet features without replacing your toilet.
- **Elderly or Mobility-Challenged Users:** Heated seats, auto-open lids, and bidet functions reduce physical strain. The Toto NX2’s remote control is especially user-friendly.
- **Families:** Smart toilets with multiple user profiles (like the Kohler Veil) allow each family member to customize settings.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $2,000:** Stick with bidet seat attachments like the BioBidet BB-2000 or Toto Washlet+ series. You’ll get excellent bidet features without the full toilet replacement cost.
- **$2,000–$5,000:** Consider complete smart toilets from Kohler or American Standard. These offer integrated designs and better flush performance.
- **$5,000+:** Premium models like the Toto Neorest NX2 deliver the best experience, with superior build quality, health features, and smart home integration.

**Hidden Costs:**
- Professional installation: $200–$500
- Electrical outlet installation: $150–$300 (if not already present)
- Wall-mount frame (for Kohler Veil): $300–$500
- Water line upgrades: $100–$200

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Tips

- **Toto Neorest NX2:** Requires a dedicated 120V outlet within 3 feet of the toilet. Professional installation is strongly recommended due to the complex water and electrical connections.
- **Kohler Veil:** Needs a wall-mounting frame (sold separately) and a 120V outlet. Installation is best left to professionals, especially for the frame.
- **BioBidet BB-2000:** DIY-friendly. Simply remove your existing toilet seat, attach the bidet seat to the bowl, and connect the water supply line. No electrical work needed if you have an outlet nearby.

**Pro Tip:** Always check your bathroom’s electrical setup before purchasing. Smart toilets require a GFCI-protected outlet near the toilet. If you don’t have one, budget for an electrician.

### Smart Home Integration

All three models support Amazon Alexa and Google Home, but the experience varies:

- **Toto:** Voice commands work flawlessly. You can say, “Alexa, flush the toilet,” and it responds instantly. The Toto app is intuitive, with weekly usage reports and maintenance alerts.
- **Kohler:** The Konnect app is feature-rich but occasionally lags. Voice commands require the app to be open for some functions. Integration with IFTTT is available for custom automations.
- **BioBidet:** The Smart app is basic but reliable. Voice commands work for core functions like seat warming and bidet activation. It’s the easiest to set up for beginners.

**Compatibility Note:** All three work with Apple HomeKit only through third-party bridges (e.g., Homebridge). Native HomeKit support is still rare in 2026.
