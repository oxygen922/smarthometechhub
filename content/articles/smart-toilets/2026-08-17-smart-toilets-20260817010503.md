# The Ultimate Smart Toilet Review: Are High-Tech Thrones Worth the Investment in 2026?

The bathroom is no longer just a utilitarian space—it's becoming the final frontier of smart home technology. ## Top 3 Smart Toilet Recommendations for 2026

The warm air dryer is surprisingly powerful, and the heated seat features multiple temperature settings. The automatic flush, deodorizing system, and pre-mist function round out a feature set that feels complete.

Where the NX2 truly excels is its integration capabilities. It works with Alexa and Google Assistant, allowing you to control settings via voice commands. The TOTO app provides usage analytics and allows for granular customization of all functions. It even has a night light that activates based on ambient lighting conditions.

**Verdict**

The Neorest NX2 is expensive, there's no denying that. But if you want the absolute best smart toilet experience with proven reliability (TOTO has been perfecting this technology for decades), this is the gold standard. The build quality is exceptional, and the feature set is unmatched.

### Bidet Functionality

The bidet is the heart of any smart toilet. Look for adjustable water temperature, pressure, and spray position. Oscillating and pulsating modes provide a more thorough clean. Some models offer a "feminine wash" setting with a wider spray pattern for women.

### Heated Seat

This is often the first feature people fall in love with. Look for multiple temperature settings and a seat that heats up quickly. Some premium models offer a "sensor" mode that pre-heats the seat when it detects your approach.

### Drying System

A good dryer should be powerful enough to actually eliminate the need for toilet paper (though most users still use some). Look for adjustable temperature and airflow settings.

### Odor Control

Activated carbon filters are effective at neutralizing odors. Ozone-based systems are more powerful but require additional maintenance. Some models automatically activate the deodorizer when the seat is occupied.

### Water Efficiency

Smart toilets should be more efficient than their traditional counterparts. Look for models with dual-flush capabilities and low GPF (gallons per flush) ratings. The most efficient models use less than 1.0 GPF for liquid waste.

### Smart Features

Consider which smart features are essential for you. Voice control is convenient but not necessary. Usage tracking can be useful for health monitoring. Automatic flushing and lid operation add convenience but also add to the cost.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

**Ideal for:**
- **Tech enthusiasts** who want to complete their smart home ecosystem
- **Individuals with mobility issues** who benefit from automatic features and bidet functionality
- **Families** who can benefit from user presets and personalized settings
- **Eco-conscious consumers** who want to reduce water usage
- **Anyone who values hygiene** and wants to reduce toilet paper usage

**Not ideal for:**
- **Budget-conscious buyers** who can't justify the premium over traditional toilets
- **Renters** who can't modify their bathroom fixtures (though bidet seats are an option)
- **Those who prefer simple, traditional fixtures**
- **Homes with outdated plumbing** that may not support the additional electrical and water connections

### Budget Considerations

**Entry Level ($500 - $1,500):** At this price point, you're looking at bidet seat attachments or basic integrated smart toilets. You'll get heated seats, basic bidet functions, and perhaps a dryer, but you'll miss out on automatic flush and smart home integration.

**Mid-Range ($1,500 - $5,000):** This is the sweet spot for most buyers. You'll find integrated smart toilets with comprehensive features, including automatic flush, deodorizers, and some smart home integration. Brands like TOTO, Kohler, and American Standard offer excellent options in this range.

**Premium ($5,000+):** At this level, you're getting the best of everything—exceptional build quality, advanced features, and full smart home integration. The TOTO Neorest and Kohler Numi are the benchmarks here.

**Hidden Costs:** Don't forget to factor in installation costs ($200-$500 for professional installation), potential electrical outlet installation ($200-$400), and water line modifications ($100-$300). Extended warranties may also be worth considering for peace of mind.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Considerations

**Electrical Requirements:** Smart toilets require a nearby electrical outlet (GFCI-protected). If your bathroom doesn't have one near the toilet, you'll need to hire an electrician. This is often the most significant installation hurdle.

**Water Supply:** Most smart toilets require a dedicated water supply line. Some models need both cold and hot water connections, while others have internal water heaters and only need cold water.

**Professional vs. DIY:** Bidet seats can typically be installed by a competent DIYer in under an hour. Integrated smart toilets are heavier and more complex—professional installation is strongly recommended. Expect installation costs between $200-$500.

**Space Requirements:** Smart toilets are generally larger than traditional models. Ensure you have adequate clearance on all sides. Some models require more space behind the toilet for the control unit.

### Smart Home Integration Options

**Voice Assistants:** Most premium smart toilets work with Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant. Voice commands can control seat temperature, bidet settings, and flushing. This is particularly useful for individuals with mobility issues.

**Mobile Apps:** Manufacturer apps provide granular control over all settings. You can create user profiles, track usage statistics, and receive maintenance reminders. Some apps offer health monitoring features that track weight, hydration, and other metrics.

**Automation:** Smart toilets can be integrated into broader home automation routines. For example, you can set the seat to warm up at specific times of day or have the toilet flush when you leave the bathroom.

**Compatibility:** Before purchasing, verify that the smart toilet is compatible with your existing smart home ecosystem. Most major brands support both Alexa and Google Assistant, but Apple HomeKit compatibility is less common.
