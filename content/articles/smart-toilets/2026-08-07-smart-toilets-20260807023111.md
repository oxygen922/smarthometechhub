## Top 3 Product Recommendations for 2026

### 1. TOTO Washlet S7 (The Premium Powerhouse)

TOTO continues to dominate the premium segment with the Washlet S7, a masterpiece of Japanese engineering. This isn't just a bidet; it's a complete hygiene system. **Performance & Comfort:** The S7 boasts TOTO's signature **PREMIST** and a powerful yet gentle **AEROWASH** spray. The oscillating and pulsating modes are surprisingly effective and customizable via the included remote. The heated seat is exceptionally comfortable, with temperature control that responds quickly. The warm-air dryer is one of the best we've tested—strong, warm, and effective at reducing the need for toilet paper.

**Smart Features:** This is where the S7 shines. It connects to the TOTO app via Wi-Fi, allowing you to customize profiles, track usage, and receive maintenance alerts. It also features a motion-activated lid and a deodorizing system that works automatically.

**Verdict:** If you want the absolute best in comfort, hygiene, and smart features, the S7 is worth the premium price. It's built like a tank and offers a truly luxurious experience.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation: DIY or Professional?

Most bidet seats are designed for DIY installation, and the models we reviewed are no exception. The process typically involves:

1.  **Removing the existing toilet seat:** This is usually a simple process with a wrench.
2.  **Installing the mounting bracket:** The bracket attaches to the toilet bowl's mounting holes.
3.  **Connecting the water supply:** You'll need to install a T-adapter to split the water supply between the toilet tank and the bidet.
4.  **Connecting the power:** All three models require a standard 3-prong electrical outlet. **This is the most critical requirement.** If you don't have an outlet near your toilet, you'll need to hire an electrician, which can add $150-$300 to the cost.

**Time Estimate:** For a handy homeowner, expect about 1-2 hours for installation. The instructions are generally clear, and YouTube is full of helpful tutorials.

### Smart Home Integration: The Future is Connected

The integration landscape is evolving rapidly. In 2026, we're seeing a clear split between "dumb" and "smart" bidets.

- **The Kohlo Neo** represents the future, acting as a true IoT device. You can create "Scenes" in Google Home or Alexa, such as "Good Night" which turns on the night light and lowers the seat.
- **The TOTO S7** offers excellent app-based control and customization, but it doesn't yet support voice assistants. Its strength lies in deep personalization and usage tracking.
- **The Bio Bidet Flow 500** is a traditional, non-connected device. Its "smart" features are limited to sensors for the seat and lid.

**Our Take:** For a truly integrated smart home experience, look for a model with native Alexa/Google support. However, for many, the app-based control of a model like the TOTO is more than sufficient. The key is to decide how much "smart" you actually need in your life.
