## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Overview
Installing a smart bidet seat is a DIY-friendly project for most homeowners. Here’s what you need to know:

1. **Check Compatibility:** Most bidet seats fit standard two-piece toilets with elongated or round bowls. Measure your toilet’s mounting hole spacing (typically 5.5 inches) to ensure a proper fit.
2. **Electrical Requirements:** Electric models need a GFCI-protected outlet within 3 feet of the toilet. If you don’t have one, hire a licensed electrician.
3. **Water Supply:** You’ll need a T-valve (included with most kits) to split water flow between the bidet and the toilet tank.
4. **Step-by-Step:** Turn off water supply, remove existing seat, install mounting plate, connect water line, plug in the unit, and test.

**Time Estimate:** 30–60 minutes for most models. The SmartBidet SB-1000 can be installed in 15 minutes.

### Smart Home Integration
Currently, **no major bidet seat offers native Wi-Fi or voice control** (like Alexa or Google Assistant). However, you can still integrate them into your smart home:

- **Smart Plugs:** Use a Wi-Fi smart plug to control power to the bidet. This allows you to schedule heating cycles, turn on the bidet before you enter the bathroom, or monitor energy usage.
- **Presence Sensors:** Pair with a motion sensor to automatically power on the bidet when someone enters the bathroom.
- **Future Outlook:** TOTO has hinted at a Wi-Fi-enabled model for 2027, but for now, smart home integration is limited.

**Workaround:** For a fully automated bathroom, consider a smart toilet (not just a seat) like the TOTO Neorest, which includes integrated bidet functions and smart features.
