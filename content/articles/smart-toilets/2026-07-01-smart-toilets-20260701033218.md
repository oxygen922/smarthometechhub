# The Ultimate Guide to Smart Toilets in 2026: Top Picks, Features, and Installation Tips

The smart toilet has evolved from a luxury novelty into a cornerstone of the modern connected bathroom. In 2026, these intelligent fixtures offer far more than heated seats and bidet functions—they integrate seamlessly with your home's ecosystem, track health metrics, and even learn your preferences over time. As a smart home technology reviewer, I've tested dozens of models across various price points to help you navigate this rapidly maturing market. Whether you're building a new home, renovating an existing bathroom, or simply curious about upgrading your daily routine, this comprehensive guide will walk you through the top recommendations, critical features, and practical considerations. ## Top 3 Smart Toilet Recommendations for 2026

### 1. TOTO Neorest NX2 – The Gold Standard

**Price:** $7,500 (estimated 2026)

The TOTO Neorest NX2 remains the benchmark for luxury smart toilets. Its signature Tornado Flush system uses dual-nozzle technology to create a powerful, silent swirl that cleans the bowl without splashing. The heated seat warms to your preferred temperature within seconds, and the integrated bidet offers adjustable water pressure, temperature, and oscillation patterns. What sets the NX2 apart is its "Actilight" UV light that sanitizes the bowl after each use, reducing bacterial growth by 99.9%. In testing, the NX2's self-cleaning wand retracted and sanitized itself after every cycle, eliminating cross-contamination concerns. The only downsides are its premium price and the need for a dedicated 120V outlet near the toilet.

**Pros:** Exceptional build quality, superior hygiene features, whisper-quiet operation  
**Cons:** High upfront cost, requires professional installation, limited color options

### 2. Kohler PureWash E930 – Best Value with Advanced Features

**Price:** $1,800 (estimated 2026)

Kohler's PureWash E930 strikes an impressive balance between affordability and premium functionality. This bidet seat attachment retrofits onto most standard toilets, making it an excellent choice for renters or those not ready for a full replacement. Its heated seat offers five temperature settings, while the remote control provides intuitive access to adjustable water pressure, spray position, and a warm air dryer. The E930 includes a UV sanitizing function that cleans the nozzle after each use, and its "Comfort Clean" feature automatically oscillates the spray for thorough cleaning. In my tests, the installation took under 30 minutes using basic tools, and the unit integrated with Kohler's Konnect app for voice control via Amazon Alexa or Google Assistant. The app allows you to create user profiles with personalized settings, making it easy for families to share the same toilet without constant adjustments.

**Pros:** Easy DIY installation, excellent app integration, strong water pressure  
**Cons:** No automatic lid, limited to standard toilet shapes, dryer could be stronger

**Price:** $2,400 (estimated 2026)

For those interested in health monitoring, the BioBidet Ultimate 770 offers a unique value proposition. This smart toilet includes built-in sensors that track urine flow rate, frequency, and color analysis, providing insights into hydration levels and potential urinary tract issues. The data syncs to a companion app that generates weekly wellness reports, which you can share with your healthcare provider. Beyond health tracking, the Ultimate 770 features a heated seat, adjustable bidet with five spray patterns, and a powerful deodorizer that neutralizes odors within seconds. **Pros:** Innovative health monitoring, robust deodorizer, eco-friendly sanitation

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

**Ideal candidates include:**
- **Tech enthusiasts** who want a fully integrated smart home experience
- **Individuals with mobility issues** who benefit from hands-free operation
- **Families** looking to reduce toilet paper usage and improve hygiene
- **Health-conscious users** interested in monitoring wellness metrics
- **Luxury homeowners** seeking a premium bathroom upgrade

### Budget Considerations

Your budget should account for more than just the toilet itself. Installation costs range from $200 for a basic bidet seat to $1,500+ for a full smart toilet requiring electrical and plumbing work. Water efficiency is another factor—look for models with a WaterSense label, which can save up to 4,000 gallons per year compared to older toilets. In 2026, expect entry-level smart seats to start around $800, while full smart toilets with advanced features begin at $2,000. Premium models like the TOTO Neorest NX2 can exceed $7,000, but they often include longer warranties (5-10 years) and superior customer support.

**Pro tip:** Many manufacturers offer financing options, and some utility companies provide rebates for water-efficient models. Check your local programs before purchasing.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Complexity

Installing a smart toilet is more involved than replacing a standard unit. Most full smart toilets require a 120V electrical outlet within 3 feet of the toilet, as well as a standard water supply line. If you're replacing an existing toilet, you'll need to ensure the rough-in distance (distance from wall to drain center) matches—typically 10 to 14 inches. Bidets seats are simpler, attaching to your existing toilet bowl, but still require a nearby outlet. For best results, hire a licensed plumber for full toilet installations, especially if electrical work is needed.

### Smart Home Integration

The smart toilet market has embraced Matter and Thread protocols in 2026, ensuring compatibility with major ecosystems. Most models now support:
- **Amazon Alexa** – Voice commands for flush, seat warming, and bidet activation
- **Google Assistant** – Integration with routines (e.g., "Good morning" triggers pre-heated seat)
- **Apple HomeKit** – Secure access and automation via Home app
- **IFTTT** – Custom applets like "Turn on bathroom fan when toilet is used"

For example, with the Kohler PureWash E930, you can set a routine that warms the seat 15 minutes before your typical wake-up time, then automatically flushes after use. The TOTO Neorest NX2's integration is more limited but still supports basic voice commands. The BioBidet Ultimate 770's health data syncs only with its proprietary app, though it can export data to Apple Health.

**Important:** Always verify compatibility with your existing smart home hub before purchasing. Some older models may require a separate bridge or hub for full functionality.
