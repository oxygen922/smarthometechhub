# The Ultimate Smart Toilet Review: Top Picks, Features, and Buying Guide for 2026

## Introduction

The smart toilet has evolved from a luxury novelty into a cornerstone of the modern, connected bathroom. In 2026, these high-tech thrones offer far more than heated seats and a bidet spray—they integrate with your smart home ecosystem, track health metrics, and even learn your bathroom habits to optimize water and energy usage. Whether you’re remodeling your master bath or simply seeking a more hygienic and comfortable experience, the right smart toilet can transform your daily routine. From self-cleaning wands to voice-activated flushing, the market is brimming with innovation. However, with prices ranging from $800 to over $8,000, choosing the perfect model requires careful consideration. This in-depth review covers the top three smart toilets of 2026, breaks down essential features, and provides a comprehensive buying guide to help you make an informed decision. Let’s dive into the future of bathroom technology.

## Top 3 Product Recommendations

### 1. TOTO Neorest NX2 (2026 Edition) – Best Overall

The TOTO Neorest NX2 remains the gold standard in smart toilets, and the 2026 edition refines an already stellar design. This integrated toilet-bidet combo features TOTO’s legendary Tornado Flush system, which uses dual-nozzle technology to create a powerful, clog-resistant flush while using only 1.0 gallon per flush (GPF). The heated seat now includes a "Smart Warm" mode that adapts to your body temperature and room humidity, ensuring optimal comfort without wasting energy. The bidet wand offers adjustable water pressure, temperature, and position, with an oscillating pulse for a spa-like cleanse. A new addition for 2026 is the "Health Sense" seat, which tracks basic wellness metrics like seat time and ambient air quality, syncing with a companion app. The auto-open lid, motion-activated flush, and built-in deodorizer are standard. The NX2 also features a nightlight with customizable colors and a hands-free UV sanitization cycle for the bowl. While it lacks a wall-hung option, its sleek, skirted design is easy to clean. At a 2026 estimated price of $6,500, it’s a significant investment, but for unmatched quality, durability, and smart home integration, the NX2 is the definitive choice.

### 2. Kohler PureWash E930 – Best Value

For homeowners seeking premium features without the premium price tag, the Kohler PureWash E930 is a standout. This is a bidet seat that retrofits onto most standard two-piece toilets, making it an excellent upgrade option. The E930 offers a heated seat with adjustable temperature, a warm air dryer, and a stainless steel wand with oscillating and pulsating wash modes. Its standout feature is the "Smart Clean" technology, which automatically cleans the wand before and after each use. The seat includes a remote control with a backlit display and intuitive icons, plus a dedicated "Night Light" mode. In 2026, Kohler has added voice control compatibility with Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant, allowing you to activate the bidet or flush hands-free. Installation is straightforward for a DIYer, taking about 30 minutes. The E930 is also WaterSense certified, using an average of 0.8 GPF. Estimated at $1,200, it offers a compelling blend of comfort, hygiene, and smart features. The only trade-off is that the seat design may not match every toilet perfectly, and the dryer is less powerful than integrated models. Nevertheless, for budget-conscious buyers, the PureWash E930 delivers exceptional value.

### 3. BioBidet Ultimate 770 – Best for Health Monitoring

The BioBidet Ultimate 770 is the top choice for those who prioritize health tracking. This integrated smart toilet features a built-in urine analysis system that measures hydration levels, pH balance, and specific gravity, offering insights into your overall wellness via a smartphone app. The seat also includes a pulse sensor and a scale that estimates body fat percentage, making it a truly connected health device. Beyond its medical-grade sensors, the Ultimate 770 offers all the standard luxuries: a heated seat, adjustable bidet with front and rear wash, warm air dryer, and a deodorizer. The flush is powerful yet quiet, using 1.28 GPF. A unique feature is the "Auto-Clean" cycle that uses electrolyzed water to sanitize the bowl after every flush. The 2026 model adds a larger, color touchscreen remote and improved voice control. However, the health data is for informational purposes only and not FDA-approved for diagnosis. The toilet requires a power outlet and a water line, and installation is best left to a professional. Estimated at $4,200, it’s a niche product but perfect for tech-savvy health enthusiasts. The main downside is that the health features may feel gimmicky to some, and the app interface could be more polished.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

A smart toilet is an excellent upgrade for anyone who values comfort, hygiene, and convenience. It’s particularly beneficial for:
- **Elderly or mobility-impaired individuals:** Heated seats, auto-open lids, and bidet functions reduce physical strain.
- **Families with young children:** Many models have child-safe settings and lower water pressure.
- **Tech enthusiasts:** Smart home integration and health tracking appeal to gadget lovers.
- **People with chronic conditions:** Bidet use can improve hygiene for those with hemorrhoids, IBS, or post-surgery recovery.
- **Homeowners remodeling a master bath:** A smart toilet adds a luxurious, modern touch.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $1,000:** Focus on bidet seats. You’ll get heated seats, basic bidet functions, and a dryer. Brands like Brondell and Kohler offer quality options.
- **$1,000 – $3,000:** Integrated toilets with better flush systems, auto-open lids, and more advanced bidet features. TOTO’s entry-level models and BioBidet’s mid-range fall here.
- **$3,000 – $5,000:** Premium integrated toilets with health monitoring, advanced sanitization, and superior build quality. The BioBidet Ultimate 770 is a prime example.
- **$5,000+:** Top-tier models like the TOTO Neorest NX2. Expect the best materials, most advanced features, and professional installation included.

Remember to account for installation costs ($200-$500 for integrated models) and potential electrical work ($150-$400). A smart toilet is an investment that pays off in daily comfort and reduced toilet paper usage.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation

Installing a smart toilet can range from simple to complex. **Bidet seats** (like the Kohler PureWash E930) are the easiest: they attach to your existing toilet in about 30 minutes using basic tools. You’ll need a T-adapter for the water line and a nearby power outlet. **Integrated smart toilets** (like the TOTO Neorest NX2 and BioBidet Ultimate 770) require removing your old toilet, potentially modifying the plumbing, and ensuring a secure electrical connection. Most manufacturers recommend professional installation to avoid leaks and electrical issues. Always check your local building codes; some areas require a licensed electrician for GFCI outlet installation. A pro installation typically takes 1-2 hours and ensures your warranty remains valid.

### Smart Home Integration

In 2026, smart toilet integration is more seamless than ever. All three of our top picks work with Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant, allowing voice commands like “Alexa, flush the toilet” or “Hey Google, warm the seat.” The TOTO Neorest NX2 goes a step further with Apple HomeKit support, enabling automation—for example, setting the seat to warm up when your morning alarm goes off. The BioBidet Ultimate 770’s health data syncs with Apple Health and Google Fit, giving you a holistic view of your wellness. Most models also have dedicated apps for adjusting settings, viewing usage statistics, and receiving maintenance alerts. Some advanced toilets even support IFTTT, letting you create custom routines (e.g., “If the toilet flushes after 10 PM, turn on the nightlight”). For the best experience, ensure your Wi-Fi network is strong and secure near the bathroom.
