# The Best Bidet Seats of 2026: Smart Toilet Upgrades That Actually Deliver

## Introduction

If you've been putting off upgrading your bathroom because full smart toilets cost as much as a used car, you're not alone. In 2026, the smart toilet market has matured significantly—but the real sweet spot for most American households isn't the $8,000 all-in-one unit. It's the humble bidet seat: a smart toilet seat that bolts onto your existing commode and delivers heated water, warm air drying, and app connectivity for a fraction of the cost.

Bidet seats have quietly become one of the fastest-growing smart home categories in the US, driven by rising awareness of hygiene benefits, water savings, and the simple fact that once you try one, going back feels primitive. But the market is crowded, specs are confusing, and prices range from $80 to over $1,200.

In this review, I break down the three best bidet seats of 2026, walk through the features that actually matter, and help you decide which smart toilet seat fits your bathroom—and your budget.

---

## Top 3 Product Recommendations

### 1. TOTO Washlet S7A — Best Overall

**Estimated 2026 Price: $1,150–$1,300**

TOTO essentially invented the modern bidet seat, and the Washlet S7A is the company's flagship for 2026. If you want the closest thing to a luxury smart toilet without replacing your fixture, this is it.

The S7A uses TOTO's signature **eWater+ system**, which electrolyzes tap water to create a mildly acidic cleaning solution that sanitizes the wand before and after each use. In practice, this addresses the biggest concern skeptics have about bidet seats: wand hygiene. The self-cleaning cycle is automatic and genuinely reassuring.

Comfort is where the S7A shines. The **premisting feature** sprays a fine water mist on the bowl before use, reducing sticking and making cleaning easier. The seat itself is heated with adjustable temperature, and the **tornado flush assist** works with your existing toilet's water pressure. The remote is wall-mountable, backlit, and intuitive—no app required, though there is a companion app for firmware updates and user profiles.

The **dual-action spray** offers rear cleansing, front cleansing, and a oscillating massage mode. Water pressure is adjustable across five levels, and the **warm air dryer** is one of the few on the market that actually dries effectively rather than just blowing lukewarm air around. The **auto-open/auto-close lid** and **night light** round out a premium package.

**Pros:** Best-in-class hygiene tech, excellent dryer, reliable brand support, no subscription fees
**Cons:** Premium price, requires a GFCI outlet near the toilet, remote is required for most functions

**Verdict:** The S7A is the benchmark. If your budget allows, this is the bidet seat to beat in 2026.

---

### 2. Bio Bidet Discovery DLS — Best Value Smart Seat

**Estimated 2026 Price: $550–$650**

Bio Bidet has built a loyal following by packing premium features into mid-range prices, and the Discovery DLS is their strongest offering yet. It's the seat I recommend most often to first-time buyers who want smart functionality without flagship pricing.

The DLS delivers **heated water with a hybrid heating system**—a tankless design that provides unlimited warm water without the bulk or temperature fluctuations of traditional tanks. The **stainless steel nozzle** has three positions and a self-cleaning cycle, and the **enema wash** and **turbo wash** modes give it surprising versatility for the price.

Where the DLS really punches above its weight is in smart features. The **wireless remote** is slim and magnetic, docking to a wall mount. The **night light** is motion-activated and adjustable. The **soft-close lid** is standard, but the **slim profile** means it doesn't look bulky on a standard toilet—a common complaint with cheaper bidet seats.

The **warm air dryer** is adequate but not as powerful as the TOTO's. Water pressure maxes out at a solid but not exceptional level. And while there's no app connectivity, the remote covers everything you'd want in daily use.

**Pros:** Excellent value, unlimited warm water, slim design, strong warranty (3 years)
**Cons:** Dryer is mediocre, no app integration, installation requires basic DIY confidence

**Verdict:** The best bidet seat under $700, full stop. Ideal for households upgrading from a basic non-electric seat.

---

### 3. Kohler PureWash E930 — Best for Smart Home Integration

**Estimated 2026 Price: $900–$1,050**

Kohler's PureWash E930 is the bidet seat for people who've already built a smart home ecosystem and want their bathroom to keep up. It's the only seat in this roundup with native **Amazon Alexa and Google Home voice control**, and it integrates with the **Kohler Konnect app** for remote operation, usage tracking, and personalized presets.

The E930 offers **heated water with a continuous-flow heater**, a **stainless steel wand with UV sanitization**, and a **heated seat with five temperature settings**. The **warm air dryer** is adjustable across three levels, and the **deodorizer** uses a carbon filter to neutralize odors—a feature that sounds gimmicky until you live with it.

Voice control is genuinely useful here: "Alexa, ask Kohler to start the rear wash" works reliably, and you can set up user profiles so each household member gets their preferred pressure and temperature. The **motion-activated lid** opens and closes automatically, and the **night light** can be dimmed via app.

The trade-off is price and complexity. At roughly $1,000, the E930 sits between the Bio Bidet and the TOTO, but its cleaning performance doesn't quite match the S7A's eWater+ system. The app is also more cluttered than it needs to be.

**Pros:** Best smart home integration, UV sanitization, strong deodorizer, voice control that works
**Cons:** Pricier than comparable non-smart seats, app can be buggy, requires 2.4GHz Wi-Fi

**Verdict:** The clear choice for Alexa/Google households that want a connected bathroom.

## Key Features to Consider

Before you buy, here's what separates a great bidet seat from a frustrating one.

| Feature | Why It Matters | Budget Tier | Mid Tier | Premium Tier |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Water Heating** | Tankless = unlimited warm water; tank = limited but cheaper | Tank (limited) | Hybrid tankless | Continuous tankless |
| **Nozzle Material** | Stainless steel resists buildup better than plastic | Plastic | Stainless | Stainless + UV sanitization |
| **Warm Air Dryer** | Reduces toilet paper use; quality varies wildly | Basic/absent | Adequate | Powerful, adjustable |
| **Seat Heating** | Essential for winter comfort | On/off | Adjustable | Adjustable + memory |
| **Remote Control** | Wall-mount vs. side panel vs. app | Side panel | Wireless remote | Remote + app + voice |
| **Deodorizer** | Carbon filter neutralizes odors | Rare | Optional | Standard |
| **Auto Lid** | Convenience and hygiene | No | No | Yes |
| **Night Light** | Safety and usability at night | Rare | Motion-activated | Dimmable, app-controlled |
| **Warranty** | Electronics fail; coverage matters | 1 year | 2–3 years | 3+ years |
| **Power Requirement** | Most need a GFCI outlet near toilet | GFCI outlet | GFCI outlet | GFCI outlet |

**The features that matter most:** tankless heating (no cold surprises mid-use), a stainless steel nozzle (hygiene and durability), and a genuinely functional dryer (the single most common disappointment in budget seats). Everything else—voice control, auto lids, deodorizers—is nice-to-have.

**The feature people overlook:** your electrical setup. Nearly every electric bidet seat requires a GFCI-protected outlet within about three feet of the toilet. If your bathroom doesn't have one, budget $150–$400 for an electrician before you buy.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Bidet Seat?

**You'll love a bidet seat if:**
- You have mobility limitations and want easier hygiene
- You're focused on reducing toilet paper use and waste
- You want luxury bathroom features without a full remodel
- You have a standard two-piece toilet with a compatible bowl shape

**You should skip it if:**
- You have a one-piece toilet with an integrated seat (check compatibility first)
- Your bathroom lacks a nearby outlet and you can't add one
- You rent and can't modify electrical fixtures

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $300:** Basic electric seats with tank heating and limited features. Fine for testing the waters, but expect short warranties and weak dryers.
- **$500–$700:** The value sweet spot. Hybrid tankless heating, stainless nozzles, wireless remotes, and 3-year warranties. This is where most buyers should shop.
- **$900–$1,300:** Flagship territory. Premium hygiene tech, powerful dryers, auto lids, and smart home integration.

**Hidden costs to plan for:** GFCI outlet installation ($150–$400), a compatible toilet (most seats fit standard elongated bowls; round bowls need specific models), and replacement filters or descaling kits ($20–$40 annually).

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

**Installation difficulty:** Most bidet seats install in 30–60 minutes with basic tools. You'll remove your existing seat, mount a bracket, connect the water supply via a T-adapter, and plug in. The TOTO and Kohler units include clear instructions and video guides; the Bio Bidet is similarly straightforward.

**The one real hurdle is power.** If you don't have a GFCI outlet near the toilet, hire an electrician—don't run an extension cord across a bathroom floor.

**Smart home integration in 2026:**
- **Kohler E930:** Native Alexa and Google Home support, Kohler Konnect app, user profiles
- **TOTO S7A:** TOTO app for firmware and profiles; no voice control (a deliberate design choice)
- **Bio Bidet DLS:** No app or voice control; remote-only operation

If voice control and routines (e.g., "Good morning" triggering seat heating) matter to you, Kohler is the only real option in this tier. If you value reliability over connectivity, TOTO's approach is arguably smarter—fewer failure points.

The bidet seat market in 2026 offers something for nearly every budget, and the gap between mid-range and flagship has narrowed considerably. Here's my final ranking:

- **Best overall:** **TOTO Washlet S7A** — unmatched hygiene tech and drying performance for those who want the best
- **Best value:** **Bio Bidet Discovery DLS** — 80% of the flagship experience at half the price
- **Best for smart homes:** **Kohler PureWash E930** — the only seat that genuinely integrates with your connected home

**My recommendation for most US households:** Start with the **Bio Bidet Discovery DLS**. It delivers the features that matter—unlimited warm water, stainless nozzle, heated seat—without the premium markup. If you fall in love with bidet seats (and you will), the TOTO S7A is the natural upgrade when it's time to replace.

**Final verdict:** A bidet seat is one of the highest-satisfaction smart home upgrades you can make in 2026. Buy once, buy well, and check your outlet situation before you click "add to cart."
