# The Ultimate Smart Bidet Seat Review: Transform Your Bathroom in 2026

## Introduction

In the ever-evolving world of smart home technology, few upgrades deliver as much daily impact as a high-quality bidet seat. Once considered a luxury reserved for high-end hotels or overseas travelers, bidet seats have become an essential fixture in modern American bathrooms. They offer superior hygiene, reduce toilet paper usage, and provide comfort features that make your morning routine feel like a spa experience. With 2026 pricing now reflecting advanced features like heated seats, customizable wash patterns, and smart home integration, there’s never been a better time to upgrade. Whether you’re looking to save money on paper products, reduce your environmental footprint, or simply enjoy a cleaner, more comfortable bathroom experience, a smart bidet seat is a worthwhile investment. In this comprehensive review, we’ll explore the top three bidet seats on the market, break down key features, and help you choose the perfect model for your home.

## Top 3 Product Recommendations with Detailed Reviews

### 1. TOTO S550e Washlet+ – The Premium Powerhouse

**Price (2026): $1,099 – $1,249**

The TOTO S550e Washlet+ remains the gold standard in the bidet seat category, and for good reason. TOTO has been perfecting bidet technology for decades, and the S550e represents the pinnacle of their engineering. This model features TOTO’s proprietary EWATER+ technology, which uses electrolyzed water to clean the nozzle and bowl, effectively reducing bacteria by 99.9% without harsh chemicals. The heated seat is adjustable across multiple temperature settings, and the air dryer is surprisingly powerful—drying you in under 60 seconds.

The remote control is sleek, wireless, and intuitive, with a backlit display that’s easy to read even in dim lighting. The oscillating and pulsating wash patterns provide a thorough clean, and the “pre-mist” function wets the bowl before use to prevent waste from sticking. Installation is straightforward for a DIY enthusiast, though professional installation is recommended for those unfamiliar with plumbing.

**Pros:** Exceptional build quality, advanced hygiene features, powerful drying, intuitive remote.
**Cons:** High price point, requires a nearby electrical outlet, somewhat bulky design.

**Best For:** Homeowners who want the absolute best and are willing to pay for it.

What sets the BB-2000 apart is its customizable user presets. You can save up to four individual profiles with preferred settings for water temperature, spray position, and seat temperature. This makes it ideal for families where multiple people use the same bathroom. **Cons:** Plastic construction feels less premium than TOTO, deodorizer could be stronger, remote requires batteries.

**Best For:** Families and budget-conscious buyers who don’t want to sacrifice features.

### 3. Kohler Novita BH-90 – The Smart Home Champion

**Price (2026): $799 – $899**

Kohler’s Novita BH-90 is designed with smart home integration in mind. It works seamlessly with Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant, allowing you to control the bidet with voice commands. Beyond its smart capabilities, the BH-90 offers a heated seat with six temperature settings, a warm water wash with adjustable pressure, and a powerful air dryer. ## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Overview

Installing a bidet seat is a manageable DIY project for most homeowners. Here’s a general outline:

1. **Turn off the water supply** to your toilet and flush to empty the tank.
2. **Remove the existing toilet seat** by unscrewing the bolts.
3. **Install the bidet mounting bracket** onto the toilet bowl using the provided hardware.
4. **Attach the bidet seat** to the bracket and secure it.
5. **Connect the water supply** using the included T-adapter (one side goes to the bidet, the other to the toilet tank).
6. **Plug in the power cord** (most models require a standard 120V outlet near the toilet).
7. **Test all functions** before finalizing the installation.

**Pro Tip:** If your bathroom lacks an outlet near the toilet, consider hiring a licensed electrician. Alternatively, some models offer battery-powered options, though they lack heated features.

### Smart Home Integration

For the Kohler Novita BH-90, smart home integration is a standout feature. Pairing with Alexa or Google Assistant is straightforward via the KOHLER Konnect app. You can create routines like “Good Morning” that automatically heats the seat and activates your preferred wash settings. Voice commands are particularly useful for those with mobility issues or when your hands are full.

The TOTO and Bio Bidet models lack native smart home support, but you can integrate them using a smart plug (e.g., TP-Link Kasa or Amazon Smart Plug) to schedule power-on times. This allows you to have the seat preheated before you wake up, though you won’t be able to control wash functions remotely.
