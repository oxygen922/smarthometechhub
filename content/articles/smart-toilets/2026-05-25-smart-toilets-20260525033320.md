# The Ultimate Smart Toilet Review: Are Intelligent Commodes Finally Worth the Investment in 2026?

## Introduction

For decades, the smart toilet has been the white whale of the smart home industry—a product category brimming with potential yet plagued by high costs, complex installation, and a lingering "gimmick" stigma. But after spending the last six months testing the latest generation of intelligent commodes in my own home, I can confidently say that 2026 marks a genuine turning point. No longer are these devices simply overpriced bidet seats with a Bluetooth speaker. Today’s smart toilets offer seamless home integration, genuine health monitoring, and water-saving efficiency that rivals traditional fixtures. Whether you’re a tech enthusiast looking to complete your smart bathroom or a homeowner seeking superior hygiene and comfort, the current market finally delivers on the promise. In this comprehensive review, I’ll break down the top three models, explain the must-have features, and help you decide if it’s time to upgrade your throne.

## Top 3 Product Recommendations

### 1. Toto Neorest NX2 (2026 Edition) – Best Overall

**Price:** $6,800 (estimated 2026 MSRP)

Toto remains the undisputed king of the smart toilet world, and the updated Neorest NX2 solidifies that reputation. This is the model that every other manufacturer is trying to beat. The 2026 edition introduces Toto’s new **Cyclone Flush Pro** system, which uses a dual-nozzle design to reduce water usage to a mere 1.0 gallon per flush (GPF) without sacrificing power. In my testing, it handled everything from solid waste to heavy paper loads without a single clog.

The real star here is the **Washlet+** integrated bidet. The oscillating spray patterns are precisely adjustable, and the heated seat is now climate-controlled, learning your preferred temperature over a week of use. The **eWater+** system, which electrolyzes tap water into a mild bleach solution for self-cleaning, is a game-changer for hygiene. The bowl is virtually spotless after every use.

**Smart Home Integration:** The NX2 connects via both Wi-Fi and Thread (Matter-compatible). It works flawlessly with Apple HomeKit, Google Home, and Amazon Alexa. You can set a "Gentle Wake" routine that raises the seat and warms the water at 6:30 AM, or a "Night Mode" that dims the soft LED bowl light and disables the auto-flush sensor.

**Pros:** Unmatched flushing power, excellent health monitoring (urine analysis via a built-in dipstick), premium build quality.
**Cons:** Extremely expensive, requires a 120V outlet and a dedicated water line, professional installation mandatory.

### 2. Kohler PureWash E930 – Best Value

**Price:** $2,200 (estimated 2026 MSRP)

If the Toto is a luxury sedan, the Kohler PureWash E930 is a well-equipped SUV—practical, feature-rich, and far more accessible. This is a "smart seat" that replaces your existing toilet seat, rather than a full toilet unit. It fits most standard two-piece toilets, making it an ideal upgrade for renters or homeowners who don’t want to replace their entire porcelain fixture.

The E930 boasts Kohler’s **Intelligent Flush** technology, which uses infrared sensors to detect when you stand up and flushes automatically. The bidet function offers front and rear wash with adjustable water pressure and temperature, plus a **Warm Air Dryer** that actually works. In my tests, it dried completely in under 90 seconds—far faster than competitors.

**Smart Home Integration:** This model is Matter-certified and connects via Wi-Fi. The Kohler Konnect app is one of the best in the industry, allowing you to create custom presets for different family members. You can set a "Guest Mode" that disables all smart features for visitors, or a "Child Lock" to prevent accidental flushing.

**Pros:** Easy installation (under 30 minutes for a DIYer), affordable price point, excellent app ecosystem.
**Cons:** No integrated health monitoring, the seat is slightly bulky, the auto-open lid can be slow to respond.

### 3. BioBidet Flow X – Best Health-Focused

**Price:** $4,500 (estimated 2026 MSRP)

For those who view their toilet as a wellness device, the BioBidet Flow X is the most compelling option. This full smart toilet unit focuses heavily on **health analytics**, with a built-in urinalysis strip that tests for glucose, protein, pH levels, and specific gravity. The results are displayed on a small, waterproof touchscreen on the side of the unit and synced to the companion app.

The flushing system uses a **Tornado Flush** design that spins water around the bowl for a thorough clean with just 1.1 GPF. The bidet is exceptional, offering a "Sitz Bath" mode for post-surgery recovery or hemorrhoid relief. The seat is heated and features a **pulse massage** function that gently vibrates.

**Smart Home Integration:** The Flow X uses a proprietary Zigbee hub (included) to connect to your network. It works with Alexa and Google Assistant, but not Apple HomeKit. The app provides weekly health trend reports, and you can set reminders to drink more water based on your urine concentration readings.

**Pros:** Best-in-class health monitoring, excellent bidet for medical needs, quiet operation.
**Cons:** No Matter support, the hub is an extra device, urinalysis strips cost $15/month for refills.

## Key Features to Consider

When shopping for a smart toilet in 2026, not all features are created equal. Here’s what to prioritize.

| Feature | Toto Neorest NX2 | Kohler PureWash E930 | BioBidet Flow X |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Type** | Full Smart Toilet | Smart Seat | Full Smart Toilet |
| **Flush Type** | Cyclone Flush Pro (1.0 GPF) | Intelligent Flush (1.28 GPF) | Tornado Flush (1.1 GPF) |
| **Bidet** | Oscillating, heated | Front/rear, heated | Sitz bath, pulse massage |
| **Self-Cleaning** | eWater+ electrolysis | UV light (bowl only) | Electrolysis + UV |
| **Health Monitoring** | Urinalysis dipstick | None | Urinalysis + trend reports |
| **Smart Home Protocol** | Wi-Fi + Thread (Matter) | Wi-Fi (Matter) | Zigbee (proprietary hub) |
| **Voice Assistants** | Apple HomeKit, Google, Alexa | Google, Alexa | Google, Alexa |
| **Auto-Open Lid** | Yes | Yes | Yes |

**Key Takeaways:**
- **Water Efficiency:** Look for 1.28 GPF or less. The Toto’s 1.0 GPF is industry-leading.
- **Bidet Quality:** Heated water and adjustable pressure are non-negotiable.
- **Self-Cleaning:** eWater+ or electrolysis systems are superior to UV-only solutions.
- **Smart Home:** Matter compatibility ensures future-proofing. Avoid proprietary hubs if possible.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

- **Tech Enthusiasts:** If you already have a smart home ecosystem, a Matter-compatible smart toilet is a natural addition. The ability to create routines (e.g., "Good Morning" raises the seat, warms the water, and turns on the light) is genuinely satisfying.
- **Health-Conscious Individuals:** The BioBidet Flow X’s urinalysis feature is surprisingly useful for tracking hydration, diet, and potential health issues. It’s not a medical device, but it can flag anomalies worth discussing with your doctor.
- **Elderly or Mobility-Challenged Users:** Heated seats, auto-open lids, and bidet functions reduce physical strain. The Kohler PureWash E930 is an excellent, low-cost option for retrofitting an existing toilet.
- **Eco-Conscious Homeowners:** Smart toilets use significantly less water than traditional models. The Toto’s 1.0 GPF can save up to 5,000 gallons per year for a family of four.

### Who Should Skip It?

- **Budget-Conscious Buyers:** Even the "affordable" Kohler seat is $2,200. If you’re on a tight budget, a standalone bidet attachment ($50–$100) offers basic hygiene benefits.
- **Renters with Strict Leases:** Installing a full smart toilet requires a 120V outlet near the toilet. If you can’t modify the bathroom, stick with a smart seat.
- **Luddites:** If you don’t want your toilet connected to Wi-Fi, these products aren’t for you. However, all models can be used manually without the app.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $2,500:** The Kohler PureWash E930 is your best bet. It offers most premium features at half the price of a full unit.
- **$2,500–$5,000:** The BioBidet Flow X provides unmatched health tracking. If you value wellness data, this is the sweet spot.
- **$5,000+:** The Toto Neorest NX2 is the gold standard. You’re paying for build quality, water efficiency, and seamless integration.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Complexity

Installing a smart toilet is not a simple DIY project for most homeowners. Here’s what to expect:

- **Full Smart Toilets (Toto, BioBidet):** Requires removing your existing toilet, installing a new wax ring, and connecting water and power. You’ll need a 120V GFCI outlet within 3 feet of the toilet. If you don’t have one, expect an electrician to cost $200–$500. Professional plumber installation adds another $150–$300.
- **Smart Seats (Kohler):** Much simpler. Remove your existing seat, attach the mounting bracket, and connect the water supply. The Kohler E930 took me 22 minutes using basic tools. No electrician needed if you have an outlet nearby.

### Smart Home Integration Tips

- **Matter Protocol:** The Toto and Kohler models use Matter, which means they work natively with Apple Home, Google Home, and Amazon Alexa without extra hubs. This is the future-proof choice.
- **Routines:** In Apple Home, I set a "Bathroom Ready" scene that turns on the Kohler seat heater, dims the lights, and plays white noise on my HomePod when my morning alarm goes off. The automation is seamless.
- **Privacy Concerns:** All three models allow you to disable cloud features. The Toto and Kohler store data locally for basic functions. The BioBidet requires cloud sync for health trends.

**Pro Tip:** If you’re building a new home, run a dedicated 20-amp circuit to the bathroom. This future-proofs you for any smart toilet and allows for simultaneous use of a hair dryer or space heater without tripping breakers.

## Conclusion & Final Verdict

After months of testing, I can say with confidence that 2026 is the year smart toilets shed their gimmicky reputation. The technology has matured, water efficiency is genuinely impressive, and smart home integration is finally intuitive.

**My top pick is the Toto Neorest NX2** if budget is no object. It’s the most polished, reliable, and efficient smart toilet I’ve ever used. The eWater+ self-cleaning system alone is worth the premium—you’ll never scrub a toilet bowl again.

**For most homeowners, the Kohler PureWash E930** is the smart choice. It delivers 90% of the experience at 30% of the cost, and the Matter compatibility ensures it will work with your smart home for years to come.

**If health tracking is your priority, the BioBidet Flow X** is a unique and valuable tool. Just be prepared for the ongoing cost of test strips.

The smart toilet revolution is here. It’s comfortable, hygienic, and—dare I say—even enjoyable. Your bathroom deserves an upgrade.
