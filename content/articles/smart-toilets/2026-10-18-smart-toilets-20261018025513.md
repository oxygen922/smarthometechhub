# Smart Toilet Review 2026: Are They Worth the Investment?

## Introduction

Let's be honest—the idea of a smart toilet once sounded like something out of a sci-fi movie. But here we are in 2026, and these high-tech bathroom fixtures have become one of the fastest-growing segments in the smart home market. If you're remodeling a bathroom or simply tired of your outdated commode, a smart toilet offers features you never knew you needed: heated seats, bidet functionality, automatic lids, self-cleaning cycles, and even health monitoring.

In this comprehensive smart toilet review, we'll walk you through the best models available in the US market, break down the key features that actually matter, and help you decide whether upgrading to a smart toilet is worth the investment. We've analyzed performance, installation complexity, smart home integration, and long-term value across dozens of models. Whether you're a tech enthusiast or a cautious first-time buyer, this guide will give you everything you need to make an informed decision. Let's dive in.

## Top 3 Smart Toilet Recommendations

### 1. TOTO Neorest MS900C47 — Best Overall Smart Toilet

**Price (2026 estimate): $8,200–$9,500**

TOTO remains the undisputed king of the smart toilet category, and the Neorest MS900C47 is its flagship offering. This is the model you buy when budget is secondary to uncompromising performance and luxury.

**Design and Build Quality**

The Neorest features a sleek, low-profile design with a seamless ceramic body that's remarkably easy to clean. The tankless design (it uses direct water line pressure) saves considerable space and gives your bathroom a modern, minimalist aesthetic. The unit measures approximately 15.5 inches wide and 27 inches deep, so measure your space carefully before ordering.

**Performance**

The Neorest's cleansing performance is exceptional. It offers adjustable water temperature, pressure, and nozzle position, with both front and rear wash modes. The oscillating and pulsating spray functions feel genuinely therapeutic. The warm air dryer is powerful enough to actually dry—a rarity among smart toilets—and the heated seat warms up in under 30 seconds.

**Smart Features**

This is where the Neorest shines. It features:
- **Auto-open/close lid** with proximity sensors
- **Auto-flush** when you walk away
- **EWATER+** electrolyzed water that sanitizes the bowl and wand after every use
- **Activated catalytic deodorizer** that neutralizes odors rather than masking them
- **Premist** function that coats the bowl before use to prevent waste adhesion
- **Remote control** with customizable user profiles (up to 4 users)

**Health Monitoring**

The Neorest includes basic health tracking through TOTO's companion app, logging usage patterns and offering insights into hydration and digestive habits. It's not a medical device, but it's genuinely useful data.

**Verdict:** The Neorest MS900C47 is the gold standard. It's expensive, but if you want the best smart toilet money can buy in 2026, this is it.

### 2. Kohler Numi 2.0 — Best Smart Toilet with Alexa Integration

**Price (2026 estimate): $7,500–$8,800**

Kohler's Numi 2.0 is the most "smart home" of all smart toilets, featuring built-in Amazon Alexa, ambient lighting, and a premium sound system. It's less about bathroom basics and more about creating a full sensory experience.

**Design and Build Quality**

The Numi 2.0 is a statement piece. Its sculptural form and integrated LED lighting (which you can customize in dozens of colors) make it a conversation starter. The build quality is impeccable, with a stainless steel wand and a ceramic bowl that resists staining beautifully. At roughly 16 inches wide and 28 inches deep, it's slightly larger than the Neorest, so plan accordingly.

**Performance**

Cleansing performance is excellent, with a stainless steel wand offering front, rear, and oscillating wash modes. Water pressure is adjustable via remote or voice command. The heated seat and warm air dryer are both top-tier. One minor gripe: the dryer takes a bit longer than TOTO's to fully dry.

**Smart Features**

The Numi 2.0's headline feature is built-in Alexa. You can control flushing, lighting, seat temperature, and even play music through voice commands. The integrated speakers are surprisingly good—not audiophile quality, but genuinely enjoyable. The motion-activated lid and auto-flush work reliably.

**Health Monitoring**

Kohler's app tracks usage and offers personalized settings, but health monitoring is less detailed than TOTO's offering. It's more of a convenience app than a wellness tool.

**Verdict:** If you're deep into the Amazon ecosystem and want a smart toilet that doubles as a bathroom entertainment hub, the Numi 2.0 is unmatched. Just know you're paying a premium for the Alexa integration and lighting.

### 3. Bio Bidet Aura A9 — Best Value Smart Toilet

**Price (2026 estimate): $2,800–$3,600**

For buyers who want smart toilet features without the luxury price tag, the Bio Bidet Aura A9 is the standout choice. It delivers the core functionality—bidet washing, heated seat, auto-flush, and a remote control—at roughly a third of the cost of flagship models.

**Design and Build Quality**

The Aura A9 has a more traditional silhouette than the TOTO or Kohler, with a skirted tank design that looks clean and modern. Build quality is solid, though the plastics on the remote and lid feel less premium than the flagship competitors. The ceramic bowl is well-glazed and easy to clean.

The bidet function is excellent for the price. It offers adjustable water pressure, temperature, and nozzle position, plus a "turbo" wash mode for stronger cleaning. The heated seat is comfortable, and the warm air dryer works well, though it's less powerful than the Neorest's. The auto-flush sensor is responsive and reliable.

**Smart Features**

The Aura A9 includes:
- **Motion-activated lid** (open/close)
- **Auto-flush** with foot sensor backup
- **Wireless remote** with two user presets
- **Deodorizer** (carbon filter-based)
- **Night light** for late-night visits

It lacks voice assistant integration and advanced health monitoring, but for most users, these are nice-to-haves rather than essentials.

**Health Monitoring**

None beyond basic usage tracking. This is a straightforward smart toilet, not a wellness device.

**Verdict:** The Bio Bidet Aura A9 is the smart toilet we recommend to most people. It delivers 85% of the flagship experience at 35% of the price. Unless you specifically want Alexa integration or TOTO's premium sanitization, this is the sweet spot.

### The Features That Matter Most

**Bidet performance** is the heart of any smart toilet. Look for adjustable water pressure and temperature, and ideally, an oscillating mode. Stainless steel wands are more hygienic and durable than plastic.

**Self-cleaning technology** is a major differentiator. TOTO's EWATER+ system is the most advanced, using electrolyzed water to sanitize the bowl and wand automatically. Kohler uses UV light, which is effective but less comprehensive.

**Auto open/close and auto-flush** are the features that make a smart toilet feel truly "smart." Once you've lived with them, you'll never want to go back.

**Deodorizers** vary widely in effectiveness. Catalytic deodorizers (TOTO) neutralize odors at a molecular level, while carbon filters (Bio Bidet, Kohler) simply trap them. Catalytic is superior.

**Smart home integration** is where the Kohler Numi 2.0 leads. If you're building a connected home, Alexa integration is a genuine convenience. Otherwise, a remote control is sufficient for most users.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

Smart toilets aren't for everyone, and that's okay. Here's a quick breakdown:

**You should buy a smart toilet if:**
- You're remodeling a bathroom and want a modern, spa-like experience
- You have mobility issues or limited hand function (bidet washing and auto-flush are genuinely helpful)
- You're a tech enthusiast who enjoys smart home gadgets
- You want to reduce toilet paper usage (environmental and cost benefits)
- You value hygiene and self-cleaning features

**You might skip a smart toilet if:**
- Your bathroom lacks a nearby electrical outlet (installation becomes significantly more complex)
- You're on a tight budget (a bidet seat attachment offers many features for $300–$800)
- You're renting and can't make permanent modifications
- You prefer simple, low-maintenance fixtures

### Budget Considerations

Smart toilets range from roughly $1,500 for entry-level models to over $10,000 for flagship units. Here's how to think about budget:

- **Under $2,000:** Basic smart toilets with bidet function, heated seat, and remote control. Expect fewer wash modes and no auto-lid.
- **$2,000–$4,000:** The sweet spot. You get auto open/close, auto-flush, deodorizer, and solid bidet performance. The Bio Bidet Aura A9 lives here.
- **$4,000–$7,000:** Premium features like advanced self-cleaning, health monitoring, and better build quality.
- **$7,000+:** Flagship luxury. TOTO Neorest and Kohler Numi 2.0 territory. You're paying for the best materials, the most advanced sanitization, and brand prestige.

**Don't forget hidden costs:** Installation (plumber and electrician), potential electrical work, and extended warranties. Budget an additional $500–$1,500 for professional installation.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Realities

Installing a smart toilet is more involved than swapping out a standard commode. Here's what to expect:

**Electrical Requirements**

Every smart toilet needs a power source. Most require a dedicated GFCI-protected outlet within 3 feet of the toilet. If your bathroom doesn't have one, you'll need an electrician to install it—typically $200–$500 depending on your home's wiring.

**Plumbing Considerations**

Smart toilets use standard 12-inch rough-in measurements (the distance from the wall to the drain), but tankless models like the TOTO Neorest require adequate water line pressure (at least 20 PSI). Check your home's water pressure before buying.

**Professional vs. DIY**

We strongly recommend professional installation for smart toilets. The combination of plumbing, electrical, and electronics means mistakes can be costly. Expect to pay $400–$1,000 for a licensed plumber to install your unit. Some retailers offer installation packages—worth considering.

**Timeframe**

A straightforward installation takes 2–4 hours. If electrical work is needed, plan for a full day or two.

### Smart Home Integration

**Alexa and Google Assistant**

Only the Kohler Numi 2.0 offers native voice assistant integration (Alexa). Other smart toilets generally don't integrate directly with voice assistants, though some can be controlled via smart home hubs if they support Wi-Fi or Bluetooth.

**Companion Apps**

TOTO and Kohler both offer companion apps that let you adjust settings, track usage, and receive maintenance reminders. These apps are functional but not essential—the remote control handles most day-to-day operations.

**Future-Proofing**

The smart toilet market is evolving quickly. Health monitoring is improving, and we expect more models to add voice control and deeper smart home integration by 2027. If you're buying now, prioritize core performance over bleeding-edge connectivity.
