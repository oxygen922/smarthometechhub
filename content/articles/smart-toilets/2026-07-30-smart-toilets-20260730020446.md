# The Ultimate Smart Toilet Review: Top 3 Models for 2026

## Introduction

The bathroom has undergone a quiet revolution. What was once a purely functional space is now becoming a hub of smart home technology, and at the center of this transformation is the smart toilet. These high-tech thrones offer far more than just a flush—they bring heated seats, bidet functions, air dryers, automatic lids, and even health monitoring into your daily routine. As we move into 2026, the smart toilet market has matured significantly, with brands competing on features, design, and integration with the broader smart home ecosystem. Whether you’re looking to upgrade your primary bathroom, add luxury to a guest suite, or embrace the future of personal hygiene, choosing the right smart toilet can be overwhelming. This in-depth review will guide you through the top three models available in 2026, break down the key features you need to consider, and help you decide which smart toilet best fits your home and lifestyle. From budget-friendly options to premium, AI-enhanced models, we’ve tested them all to bring you the definitive guide.

## Top 3 Product Recommendations

### 1. **Toto Neorest NX2 (2026 Edition)** – The Gold Standard

**Overview:** Toto has long been synonymous with premium smart toilets, and the Neorest NX2 continues that legacy with refinements that make it the benchmark for 2026. This model combines Toto’s legendary Tornado Flush technology with a redesigned, sleeker bowl that reduces cleaning time. The heated seat now offers five temperature settings, and the bidet wand includes a new oscillating massage pattern for enhanced comfort.

**Performance:** The NX2’s flush is incredibly quiet yet powerful, handling waste with ease. The bidet function is precise, with adjustable water pressure, temperature, and position. The air dryer is effective but still takes about 90 seconds for a full dry, which is average for this class. The automatic lid opens as you approach and closes after you leave, with a customizable delay.

**Smart Home Integration:** This is where the NX2 shines. It works natively with Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant, allowing you to control seat temperature, bidet settings, and even trigger a flush via voice. The Toto Connect App is intuitive, offering usage statistics, maintenance reminders, and the ability to create user profiles (up to four). It also integrates with IFTTT for advanced automation, such as turning on the bathroom lights when the lid opens.

**Pros:**
- Exceptional build quality and reliability
- Powerful, quiet flush
- Excellent voice assistant support
- Heated seat with multiple zones

**Cons:**
- Very expensive
- Requires professional installation
- Air dryer could be faster

**Price (2026):** $5,800 - $6,200

### 2. **Kohler Veil Intelligent Toilet (K-5401)** – Best Balance of Features and Value

**Overview:** Kohler’s Veil series has always been a strong competitor, and the 2026 model strikes an excellent balance between luxury and practicality. It features a one-piece design with a skirted trapway for easy cleaning, a heated seat with three temperature settings, and a customizable bidet with front and rear wash. The Veil also includes an integrated nightlight and a slow-close lid.

**Performance:** The flush is a dual-flush system (1.1/1.6 GPF) that is both efficient and effective. The bidet spray is gentle yet thorough, with a dedicated feminine wash mode. The air dryer is slightly faster than the Toto, taking about 75 seconds. The seat is comfortable, and the lid opens automatically when you enter the room (motion sensor). The remote control is sleek and easy to use, with a backlit display.

**Smart Home Integration:** Kohler’s Konnect app is well-designed and supports both Alexa and Google Assistant. You can create custom presets (e.g., “Morning” with a warm seat and gentle wash) and schedule them. The Veil also connects to Kohler’s Home Generator system, allowing it to function during power outages (with a backup battery). It lacks IFTTT support, which limits some advanced automation.

**Pros:**
- Excellent value for the features offered
- Easy-to-use remote and app
- Dual-flush for water efficiency
- Good voice assistant integration

**Cons:**
- No IFTTT support
- Motion sensor can be overly sensitive
- Plastic seat feels less premium than Toto’s

**Price (2026):** $3,200 - $3,600

### 3. **BioBidet Flow E200** – Best Budget Smart Toilet

**Overview:** BioBidet has carved a niche for itself by offering smart toilet features at a fraction of the price of premium brands. The Flow E200 is a bidet seat that fits most standard toilets, but BioBidet also offers a complete smart toilet version (the Flow E200 Toilet) for those who want a seamless look. The 2026 model adds a new self-cleaning nozzle and an improved heated seat with memory foam padding.

**Performance:** The flush is standard (1.28 GPF single flush) and works well with most waste. The bidet is surprisingly good, with adjustable pressure and temperature, plus a rear wash with oscillating and pulsating modes. The air dryer is the weakest point—it’s slow (about 2 minutes) and not very powerful. The seat is very comfortable, and the lid closes quietly.

**Smart Home Integration:** The Flow E200 works with Alexa and Google Assistant, but the integration is basic. You can turn the seat heat on/off and activate the bidet, but you can’t adjust temperature or pressure via voice. The BioBidet app is functional but lacks the polish of Toto’s or Kohler’s. It does support IFTTT, which is a nice bonus for tinkerers.

**Pros:**
- Affordable entry point into smart toilets
- Comfortable memory foam seat
- Self-cleaning nozzle
- IFTTT support

**Cons:**
- Weak air dryer
- Basic voice control
- App is less intuitive
- No automatic lid (on some models)

**Price (2026):** $1,200 - $1,500 (seat only); $1,800 - $2,200 (complete toilet)

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

- **Tech Enthusiasts:** If you love integrating every aspect of your home with smart technology, a smart toilet with voice control and app support is a natural addition. The Toto Neorest NX2 is the ultimate choice, but the Kohler Veil offers a more balanced experience.
- **Luxury Seekers:** For those who want the best bathroom experience money can buy, the Toto Neorest NX2 is unmatched. Its build quality, comfort, and features justify the high price.
- **Budget-Conscious Buyers:** If you want smart toilet features without breaking the bank, the BioBidet Flow E200 (complete toilet version) is a solid entry point. You’ll sacrifice some luxury but still get a heated seat, bidet, and basic smart home integration.
- **Eco-Conscious Users:** Smart toilets with dual-flush (like the Kohler Veil) or efficient single-flush (like the Toto) can save water compared to older toilets. Look for models with WaterSense certification.
- **Elderly or Mobility-Impaired Users:** The automatic lid, heated seat, and bidet functions can greatly improve comfort and independence. The Kohler Veil’s simple remote and voice control make it a good choice.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $2,000:** The BioBidet Flow E200 is your best bet. You can get a complete smart toilet for around $2,000, but expect compromises on dryer speed and app quality.
- **$3,000 - $4,000:** The Kohler Veil offers the best value in this range. You get premium features, good smart home integration, and reliable performance.
- **Over $5,000:** The Toto Neorest NX2 is the only option in this price class. It’s a significant investment, but it delivers the best overall experience.

### Installation & Smart Home Integration

**Installation:** Smart toilets are more complex to install than standard toilets. Most require a dedicated electrical outlet (GFCI-protected) near the toilet. The Toto Neorest NX2 and Kohler Veil are best installed by a professional plumber, especially if you’re replacing a standard toilet. The BioBidet Flow E200 can be installed as a seat on an existing toilet, making it a DIY-friendly option, but the complete toilet version still requires plumbing work.

**Smart Home Integration:** All three models support Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant, but the level of integration varies:
- **Toto:** Full control via voice, including seat temperature, bidet settings, and flush. The app allows for user profiles and usage analytics.
- **Kohler:** Good voice control, but limited to basic commands (seat heat on/off, bidet on/off). The app is better for scheduling and presets.
- **BioBidet:** Basic voice control only (seat heat, bidet activation). IFTTT support adds flexibility but requires more setup.

For seamless integration, consider your existing smart home ecosystem. If you use Alexa heavily, the Toto or Kohler will feel more natural. For advanced automations, the Toto’s IFTTT support is a plus.

## Conclusion & Final Verdict

The smart toilet market in 2026 offers something for every budget and lifestyle. The **Toto Neorest NX2** remains the undisputed king, with unmatched build quality, features, and smart home integration. It’s a significant investment, but for those who want the best, it’s worth every penny. The **Kohler Veil** is the smart choice for most buyers, offering a near-premium experience at a more accessible price. It balances luxury, performance, and value better than any other model. Finally, the **BioBidet Flow E200** is the budget champion, proving that you don’t need to spend a fortune to enjoy the benefits of a smart toilet.

**Final Verdict:** If you can afford it, go with the Toto Neorest NX2. If you want the best value, choose the Kohler Veil. If you’re on a tight budget, the BioBidet Flow E200 will serve you well.

Your bathroom is about to become the smartest room in your house. Choose wisely, and happy flushing.
