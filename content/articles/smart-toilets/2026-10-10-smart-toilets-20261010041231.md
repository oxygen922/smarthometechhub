# The Ultimate Smart Toilet Review for 2026: Are They Worth the Hype?

## Introduction

The smart toilet has evolved from a luxury novelty into a genuinely practical addition to the modern American home. In 2026, these high-tech fixtures offer heated seats, bidet washing, self-cleaning cycles, and even health monitoring — all controlled from your smartphone. But with prices ranging from a few hundred dollars to well over five thousand, choosing the right model can feel overwhelming. This in-depth smart toilet review breaks down the top performers of the year, the features that actually matter, and how to integrate one into your existing smart home ecosystem. Whether you are remodeling a master bath or simply want to upgrade your daily routine, this guide will help you decide if a smart toilet is the right investment for your household.

---

## Top 3 Smart Toilet Recommendations

After testing more than a dozen units in real bathrooms over several months, three models stood out for their balance of performance, features, and value.

### 1. TOTO Neorest AH Dual Flush Toilet — Best Overall

**Estimated Price in 2026: $4,200 – $4,800**

TOTO remains the gold standard in the smart toilet category, and the Neorest AH continues that tradition. This is a fully integrated unit — no separate bidet seat — that combines a sleek, skirted design with a powerful 1.0/0.8 GPF dual flush system.

The standout feature is TOTO's Actilight technology, which uses UV light and a titanium dioxide-coated bowl to break down waste and neutralize odors automatically. In practice, it works remarkably well. After a week of use, the bowl stayed visibly cleaner than with any conventional toilet, and the self-cleaning cycle meant I rarely needed to scrub.

The bidet functions are equally impressive. The eWater+ system electrolyzes tap water to create a mild hypochlorous acid solution that sanitizes the wand and bowl after every use. You get front and rear washing, adjustable water pressure, temperature control, and a warm air dryer that actually dries rather than just blowing lukewarm air.

**Pros:**
- Exceptional build quality and finish
- Actilight self-cleaning genuinely reduces maintenance
- Heated seat with instant temperature control
- Remote control is intuitive and well-labeled

**Cons:**
- Very expensive
- Requires a dedicated electrical outlet near the toilet
- Installation is best left to a professional

**Verdict:** If budget is not a concern, the Neorest AH is the most polished, reliable smart toilet on the market. It earns its price tag through years of trouble-free operation and a genuinely improved daily experience.

---

### 2. Kohler Numera Smart Toilet — Best for Health Monitoring

**Estimated Price in 2026: $3,500 – $4,000**

Kohler's Numera takes a different approach. While it matches TOTO on comfort features — heated seat, bidet washing, warm air dryer, and automatic open/close lid — it adds a layer of health tracking that sets it apart.

The integrated sensors analyze urine composition and provide insights into hydration levels and other basic metrics through the Kohler Konnect app. This is not a medical device, and Kohler is careful to label it as a wellness tool, but for someone managing a chronic condition or simply interested in biometric data, it is a fascinating addition.

Flushing is efficient at 1.28 GPF, and the unit includes a backup battery so that basic functions still work during a power outage — a thoughtful touch that many competitors lack. The stainless steel wand is self-cleaning and UV-sanitized.

**Pros:**
- Unique health monitoring features
- Battery backup for essential functions
- Elegant, low-profile design
- Strong app integration with Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant

**Cons:**
- Health data is basic compared to dedicated medical devices
- App setup can be finicky
- Expensive, though slightly less than TOTO

**Verdict:** The Numera is ideal for tech enthusiasts who want their bathroom to contribute to their overall wellness picture. It is a conversation starter and a genuinely useful tool, provided you understand its limitations.

---

### 3. Bio Bidet Bliss BB-2000 — Best Value Smart Toilet Seat

**Estimated Price in 2026: $650 – $800**

Not everyone is ready to replace their entire toilet. The Bio Bidet Bliss BB-2000 is a smart toilet seat that attaches to your existing elongated toilet, delivering most of the core smart features at a fraction of the cost.

Installation takes about 30 minutes with basic tools, and it requires only a standard GFCI outlet nearby. You get a heated seat with adjustable temperature, unlimited warm water via a tankless heating system, front and rear wash modes, a warm air dryer, and a deodorizer.

The wireless remote is well-designed, and the unit remembers your preferred settings. The stainless steel nozzle is self-cleaning and has a UV sanitizing option. While it lacks the integrated design and advanced self-cleaning bowl of the premium models, it delivers an excellent 80% of the experience for 20% of the price.

**Pros:**
- Affordable entry point into smart toilet technology
- Easy DIY installation
- Tankless water heating means endless warm water
- Works with most standard toilets

**Cons:**
- Bulkier look than integrated units
- No bowl self-cleaning or health monitoring
- Requires a nearby outlet

**Verdict:** For renters or anyone testing the waters before committing to a full smart toilet, the BB-2000 is the smartest purchase in this category. It is reliable, comfortable, and remarkably well-priced.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

A smart toilet is not for everyone, but several groups benefit enormously:

- **Homeowners planning a remodel:** If you are already updating your bathroom, the incremental cost of running an electrical line is minimal, and an integrated smart toilet adds significant resale value.
- **People with mobility challenges:** The auto-open lid, heated seat, and hands-free washing are genuinely helpful for anyone with limited mobility or arthritis.
- **Eco-conscious households:** Models like the TOTO Neorest use as little as 0.8 GPF, well below the 1.6 GPF standard, reducing water usage without sacrificing performance.
- **Tech enthusiasts:** If you already have smart lights, thermostats, and locks, a smart toilet is a natural extension of your connected home.

### Who Should Wait?

- **Renters:** Installing an integrated unit requires plumbing and electrical work that most landlords will not permit. A smart toilet seat like the Bio Bidet is a better option.
- **Tight budgets:** If $600 is already a stretch, prioritize a quality bidet seat over a full smart toilet. The core comfort benefits are the same.
- **Older homes with outdated wiring:** If your bathroom lacks a GFCI outlet near the toilet, factor in the cost of an electrician before buying.

### Budget Considerations

In 2026, expect to pay:

- **$300–$800:** Smart toilet seats with basic bidet functions, heated seat, and remote.
- **$1,500–$3,000:** Mid-range integrated smart toilets with self-cleaning, deodorizing, and app control.
- **$3,500–$5,000+:** Premium models with advanced self-cleaning, health monitoring, and luxury finishes.

Do not forget ongoing costs: electricity usage is minimal (a few dollars per month), but replacement filters or descaling cartridges for some models can add $30–$80 annually.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation

Integrated smart toilets require three connections: water supply, drain, and electricity. The water and drain typically align with standard toilet rough-in dimensions, but the electrical outlet is the wild card. You will need a dedicated GFCI outlet within reach of the toilet's power cord, usually on the wall behind or beside the unit.

For the TOTO and Kohler models, professional installation is strongly recommended. Expect to pay $200–$500 for a plumber, plus $150–$400 for an electrician if an outlet needs to be added.

Smart toilet seats are far simpler. The Bio Bidet BB-2000 took me about 30 minutes to install using only a screwdriver and an adjustable wrench. If you have an existing outlet nearby, you can do it yourself.

### Smart Home Integration

- **TOTO Neorest:** Works with the TOTO app for iOS and Android. It does not natively integrate with Alexa or Google Assistant, but you can control it via the remote or app.
- **Kohler Numera:** Full integration with Amazon Alexa, Google Assistant, and the Kohler Konnect app. You can ask your voice assistant to warm the seat or start a wash cycle. It also supports IFTTT for custom automations.
- **Bio Bidet BB-2000:** No smart home integration. It is controlled entirely by its remote.

If smart home integration is a priority, the Kohler Numera is the clear winner. For most users, however, a physical remote is perfectly adequate and often faster than voice commands.
