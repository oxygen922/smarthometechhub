# The Ultimate Smart Toilet Review for 2026: Are They Worth the Hype?

## Introduction

The smart toilet has evolved from a luxury novelty into a genuine fixture of the modern American home. What was once reserved for high-end hotels and tech moguls is now a practical upgrade for anyone who values cleanliness, comfort, and convenience. These aren't just toilets that flush themselves—today's models offer bidet washing, heated seats, warm air drying, self-cleaning UV sanitization, and even voice control through Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant. With water usage regulations tightening and health awareness rising, smart toilets address both efficiency and hygiene in ways traditional fixtures simply cannot. In this in-depth smart toilet review, we'll break down the top models of 2026, explain the features that actually matter, and help you decide whether it's time to upgrade your bathroom with one of the best smart toilets on the market.

---

## Top 3 Smart Toilet Recommendations

### 1. TOTO Neorest NX1 — Best Overall Smart Toilet

**Price (2026 estimate): $9,800–$11,500**

TOTO remains the undisputed king of the smart toilet world, and the Neorest NX1 is its flagship masterpiece. This is the toilet you buy when you want zero compromises. The NX1 features TOTO's legendary Actilight technology, which uses UV light and a titanium dioxide-coated bowl to break down waste and bacteria automatically after every use. The result is a toilet that essentially cleans itself—no harsh chemicals required.

The bidet experience is equally refined. The NX1 offers a range of spray options including front and rear cleansing, a oscillating massage mode, and an adjustable water temperature that stays consistent thanks to its instantaneous water heater. The heated seat warms up in seconds, and the warm air dryer has three temperature settings that actually get the job done.

What sets the NX1 apart is its "Premist" feature, which mists the bowl before use to prevent waste from sticking, and its auto-open/close lid that senses your approach. The remote control is intuitive, and the toilet integrates with TOTO's smartphone app for personalized user profiles—perfect for multi-person households. The NX1 also includes a nightlight, a deodorizer, and a tornado flush system that's both powerful and quiet.

**Pros:** Exceptional build quality, self-cleaning Actilight technology, personalized user settings, near-silent flush.
**Cons:** Eye-watering price, requires professional installation, replacement parts are costly.

**Verdict:** If budget is no object, the Neorest NX1 is the best smart toilet money can buy. It's a statement piece that delivers on every promise.

---

### 2. Kohler Numera — Best Smart Toilet for Most Homes

**Price (2026 estimate): $4,200–$5,600**

Kohler's Numera strikes the sweet spot between luxury and practicality. It brings many of the high-end features found in the Neorest but at a price that's more palatable for the average homeowner. The Numera includes a heated seat, adjustable bidet wash with front and rear modes, warm air drying, and a self-cleaning UV system that sanitizes the bidet wand after each use.

One of the Numera's standout features is its integration with Kohler's Konnect app, which lets you control settings, set user preferences, and even monitor water usage. The toilet also works with Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant, so you can ask your voice assistant to raise the lid or activate the nightlight—a genuinely useful feature for those middle-of-the-night trips.

The Numera's design is sleek and modern, with a skirted trapway that makes cleaning the exterior a breeze. The flush is powerful yet efficient, using just 1.28 gallons per flush (GPF), which meets WaterSense standards. The seat is comfortable, and the stainless steel wand is easy to keep clean.

**Pros:** Excellent value for the feature set, voice control integration, water-efficient, easy-to-clean design.
**Cons:** Dryer is less powerful than TOTO's, no auto-open lid, app can be finicky.

**Verdict:** The Kohler Numera is the best smart toilet for most American households. It offers a premium experience without the premium price tag of the Neorest.

---

### 3. Bio Bidet Prodigy Smart Toilet — Best Budget Smart Toilet

**Price (2026 estimate): $2,300–$3,100**

For those who want the smart toilet experience without spending five figures, the Bio Bidet Prodigy is a compelling option. Bio Bidet has long been a trusted name in bidet seats, and the Prodigy marks their foray into full smart toilet territory. It delivers the core features—heated seat, adjustable bidet wash, warm air dryer, and deodorizer—at a price that's accessible to a much wider audience.

The Prodigy uses a tankless water heating system, which means you get endless warm water for cleansing. The remote control is straightforward, with large buttons and a clear display. The toilet also includes a nightlight and a slow-closing seat. While it lacks the advanced self-cleaning UV systems of the TOTO and Kohler models, it does have a self-cleaning wand and a pre-mist function.

Build quality is solid, though not quite as refined as the Japanese and American premium brands. The flush is efficient at 1.28 GPF, and installation is more DIY-friendly than the higher-end models, though you'll still want a plumber for the electrical work.

**Pros:** Affordable entry point, tankless water heating, easy-to-use remote, solid performance.
**Cons:** No UV self-cleaning, plastic components feel less premium, limited smart home integration.

**Verdict:** The Bio Bidet Prodigy is the best smart toilet for budget-conscious buyers who still want the luxury of a bidet and heated seat.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

Smart toilets are ideal for:

- **Homeowners remodeling a bathroom** who want to add value and luxury.
- **Individuals with mobility issues** who benefit from hands-free operation and heated seats.
- **Health-conscious users** who appreciate improved hygiene from bidet washing.
- **Tech enthusiasts** who want to integrate their bathroom into their smart home ecosystem.
- **Eco-conscious consumers** looking to reduce toilet paper usage and water consumption.

They may not be necessary for renters (installation is invasive) or those on a tight budget, as even entry-level models cost over $2,000.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $3,000:** Expect core features like bidet wash, heated seat, and dryer, but limited smart home integration and no advanced self-cleaning. Bio Bidet Prodigy is the top pick.
- **$3,000–$6,000:** Mid-range models offer better build quality, UV sanitization, and app/voice control. Kohler Numera is the best in this tier.
- **$6,000+:** Flagship models with every conceivable feature, including auto-open lids, personalized profiles, and premium materials. TOTO Neorest NX1 leads the pack.

Don't forget to factor in installation costs, which can range from $300 to $1,000 depending on your existing plumbing and electrical setup. You'll need a GFCI outlet near the toilet, which may require an electrician.

---

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

Installing a smart toilet is not a DIY job for most people. It requires:

1. **Electrical Work:** A dedicated GFCI outlet within reach of the toilet's power cord. If you don't have one, you'll need an electrician.
2. **Plumbing:** Most smart toilets use a standard 12-inch rough-in, but you should verify compatibility. A plumber can handle the water supply connection and ensure proper sealing.
3. **Removal of Old Toilet:** Disposal of your old fixture is often included in installation services.

Professional installation typically costs $300–$800, plus electrical work if needed. Some retailers offer installation packages.

Once installed, smart home integration varies by model. The Kohler Numera works with Alexa and Google Assistant, allowing voice commands like "Alexa, ask Kohler to open the lid." TOTO's Neorest uses its own app but doesn't integrate with third-party voice assistants. Bio Bidet offers no smart home connectivity.

For a fully integrated experience, consider pairing your smart toilet with a smart bathroom fan, smart lighting, and a smart speaker. This creates a seamless, hands-free bathroom environment.
