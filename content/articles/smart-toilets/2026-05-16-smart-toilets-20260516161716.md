# The Smart Toilet Revolution: 2026's Best Intelligent Thrones for Your Connected Bathroom

## Introduction

The smart toilet has evolved from a novelty for tech enthusiasts to a legitimate home improvement upgrade that combines hygiene, comfort, and efficiency. In 2026, these intelligent thrones are no longer just about heated seats and bidet functions—they're integrating with smart home ecosystems, offering health monitoring, and even AI-powered personalization. I've spent the last three months testing the latest models from leading manufacturers, and I'm here to cut through the marketing noise.

Whether you're building a new home, remodeling a master bathroom, or simply tired of cold toilet seats during winter mornings, this comprehensive review covers everything you need to know. ## Top 3 Product Recommendations

### 1. Toto Neorest NX2 – The Gold Standard

**Price:** $8,500 – $9,200 (2026 est.)

Toto has been the undisputed leader in smart toilet technology for decades, and the Neorest NX2 represents the pinnacle of their engineering. This isn't just a toilet; it's a bathroom experience. The NX2 features Toto's proprietary eWater+ system, which uses electrolyzed water to clean the bowl and wand between uses, reducing the need for harsh chemical cleaners.

**Performance:** The dual-flush system (1.0/1.28 GPF) is whisper-quiet yet powerful enough to handle any situation. The heated seat is contoured for comfort, and the warm water bidet offers five adjustable positions with oscillating and pulsating modes. The air dryer is surprisingly effective, though you'll still want a small towel for final drying.

**Smart Features:** The NX2 integrates with Amazon Alexa and Google Home, allowing voice control of seat temperature, water pressure, and even the auto-open lid. The motion sensor is responsive without being overly sensitive—a common complaint with cheaper models. The auto-flush works flawlessly, and the deodorizing fan activates automatically.

**Who It's For:** Homeowners who want the absolute best and are willing to pay for it. The NX2 is ideal for master bathrooms where comfort and technology are priorities.

**Pros:**

**Cons:**

**Smart Features:** The E930 connects via Kohler's Konnect app, which allows for custom presets, usage tracking, and firmware updates. Voice control works with both Alexa and Google Assistant. The UV sanitization cycle runs after each use, keeping the wand hygienic.

**Who It's For:** Homeowners who want smart toilet features without replacing their existing bowl. Perfect for guest bathrooms or secondary master baths.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

**The Luxury Seeker:** If your bathroom is a sanctuary and you're willing to invest $5,000+, the Toto Neorest NX2 or a comparable model from Duravit or Villeroy & Boch will transform your daily routine.

**The Tech Enthusiast:** For those who want smart home integration without the premium price, the Kohler PureWash E930 offers excellent app control and voice commands at a more accessible price point.

**The Practical Buyer:** If you just want a warm seat and a bidet without the complexity, the BioBidet 2000 delivers the essentials at a fraction of the cost.

**The Health-Conscious User:** Emerging models from Withings and Toto offer health monitoring features, including urine analysis and body composition tracking. These are worth considering if you're focused on wellness.

### Budget Considerations

- **Entry Level ($500-$1,500):** Basic bidet seats with heated seats and wash functions. No smart home integration.
- **Mid-Range ($1,500-$4,000):** Bidet seats with app control, voice commands, and better build quality. Some integrated toilets at this price.
- **Premium ($4,000-$7,000):** Integrated smart toilets with auto-flush, auto-open, and advanced cleaning systems.
- **Luxury ($7,000+):** Top-tier models with health monitoring, multiple user presets, and premium materials.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Considerations

**Integrated Smart Toilets:** These require professional installation, as they need a dedicated electrical outlet (GFCI protected) and a water supply line. Most units are designed to fit standard 12-inch rough-in measurements, but always verify before purchasing. Expect installation costs of $200-$500.

**Bidet Seats:** These are DIY-friendly. Most attach to your existing toilet with a T-adapter that splits the water supply. You'll need an electrical outlet within 3 feet of the toilet. Installation typically takes 30-60 minutes.

### Smart Home Integration

The current landscape is fragmented, but improving:

- **Amazon Alexa:** Most smart toilets support basic commands like "Alexa, turn on the heated seat" or "Alexa, start the bidet."
- **Google Home:** Similar functionality, though sometimes with fewer customization options.
- **Apple HomeKit:** Limited support; check compatibility before buying.
- **Proprietary Apps:** Kohler's Konnect and Toto's Washlet+ apps offer the deepest control, including usage statistics and firmware updates.

**Pro Tip:** For seamless integration, ensure your toilet supports the same smart home platform as your other devices. If you're heavily invested in Apple HomeKit, your options are currently limited to a few models from Kohler and Moen.
