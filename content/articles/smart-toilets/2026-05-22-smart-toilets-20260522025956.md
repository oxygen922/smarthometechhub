## Key Features to Consider

When shopping for a smart toilet in 2026, these features separate good models from great ones:

### 1. Bidet Functionality
Look for adjustable water temperature (warm or cold), pressure (at least three levels), and nozzle position. Oscillating and pulsating modes improve cleaning effectiveness. Self-cleaning nozzles are essential for hygiene.

### 2. Heated Seat
Most smart toilets offer heated seats with multiple temperature settings. Premium models heat up quickly (under 15 seconds) and maintain consistent warmth. Some have "Eco Mode" to save energy.

### 3. Flush Technology
Dual flush systems (1.0/1.6 GPF) save water. Look for "tornado" or "cyclone" flush designs that clean the bowl thoroughly. Noise level is also a factor—premium toilets are quieter.

### 4. Smart Home Integration
Alexa, Google Assistant, and Apple HomeKit compatibility allow voice control and routines. Some brands offer proprietary apps for detailed customization. Ensure the model works with your existing ecosystem.

### 5. Self-Cleaning Features
Electrolyzed water systems, UV light sanitization, and automatic wand cleaning reduce manual maintenance. Some models have "pre-mist" functions that wet the bowl before use to prevent waste from sticking.

### 6. Additional Comfort Features
- **Air Dryer:** Speed and effectiveness vary. Premium models dry in 25–40 seconds.
- **Nightlight:** Soft LED lights for nighttime use.
- **Motion Sensors:** Automatic lid opening and closing, and hands-free flushing.
- **Remote Control:** Wireless, backlit, and easy-to-use is ideal.

### 7. Installation Requirements
Full smart toilets require a nearby electrical outlet (GFCI protected) and a water supply line. Bidets seats are easier to install but may need an outlet. Professional installation is recommended for premium models.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Tips

- **Electrical Outlet:** A GFCI-protected outlet within 3 feet of the toilet is required for most smart toilets. If you don’t have one, hire a licensed electrician.
- **Water Supply:** Most models use a standard 3/8-inch compression fitting. Check your existing supply line compatibility. Some premium toilets require a dedicated water line.
- **Professional vs. DIY:** Bidets seats are DIY-friendly (30–60 minutes). Full smart toilets are heavy and complex—professional installation is strongly recommended to avoid damage or leaks.
- **Floor Reinforcement:** Some luxury toilets are heavy (100+ lbs). Ensure your bathroom floor can support the weight.

### Smart Home Integration

- **Voice Control:** Use Alexa or Google Assistant to adjust seat temperature, activate bidet, or flush. Example: “Alexa, warm up the toilet seat.”
- **Routines:** Create morning routines that pre-heat the seat and turn on the nightlight. Kohler Konnect offers the most robust routine options.
- **App Control:** TOTO and Kohler apps allow detailed customization of spray patterns, drying time, and energy-saving modes. Bio Bidet’s app is basic but functional.
- **IFTTT:** For advanced users, IFTTT applets can link your smart toilet to other devices (e.g., turn off the heated seat when you leave home).

**Compatibility Check:** Ensure your smart toilet works with your existing smart home hub. Most support Wi-Fi (2.4 GHz), but some require a bridge for connectivity.
