# The Ultimate Smart Bidet Seat Review: Transform Your Bathroom in 2026

## Introduction

The humble toilet has undergone a remarkable transformation in the past decade, and nowhere is this more evident than in the rise of the smart bidet seat. Once considered a luxury reserved for high-end hotels or Japanese households, smart bidet seats have become an essential upgrade for the modern American home. In 2026, these devices are no longer just about hygiene—they integrate seamlessly with your smart home ecosystem, offer personalized comfort settings, and even provide health monitoring features. Whether you’re looking to reduce toilet paper usage, improve personal cleanliness, or simply enjoy the convenience of a heated seat on a cold morning, a smart bidet seat is a worthwhile investment. This review will guide you through the top three products on the market, key features to consider, and everything you need to know before making a purchase. Let’s dive into the world of smart bidet seats and find the perfect fit for your bathroom.

## Top 3 Product Recommendations

### 1. Toto Washlet S7A: The Premium Performer

**Price:** $899 (2026 estimate)

The Toto Washlet S7A represents the gold standard in smart bidet seats. Building on Toto’s decades of innovation, this model features their patented eWater+ technology, which uses electrolyzed water to clean the nozzle and bowl, ensuring maximum hygiene. The S7A offers a sleek, low-profile design that fits most standard toilets, and its heated seat is adjustable across five temperature settings. The bidet functions include front and rear wash, oscillating and pulsating modes, and a soft-air dryer that rivals a hair dryer in efficiency. What sets the S7A apart is its smart home integration: it works with Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant, allowing you to activate the wash cycle or adjust settings with voice commands. The remote control is wireless and backlit, making it easy to use in low light. Users rave about the “pre-mist” function that sprays the bowl before use to prevent waste from sticking. With a 1.2 gallon-per-minute flow rate and a warm water tank that provides up to 60 seconds of continuous spray, the S7A is ideal for households with multiple users. The only downside is the price, but for those seeking the best, it’s a worthy investment.

### 2. Bio Bidet Ultimate 770: The Best Value

**Price:** $549 (2026 estimate)

For those who want premium features without breaking the bank, the Bio Bidet Ultimate 770 is a standout. This seat offers a heated seat, adjustable water temperature and pressure, and a dual-nozzle system for front and rear cleaning. The Ultimate 770 includes a warm air dryer, deodorizer, and a self-cleaning nozzle that retracts after each use. What makes it particularly appealing is its user-friendly side panel with large, tactile buttons and a LED nightlight that illuminates the bowl. The installation is straightforward, requiring only basic tools and about 30 minutes of your time. In terms of smart home integration, the Ultimate 770 is compatible with the Bio Bidet app, which allows you to create custom wash profiles for up to four users. The app also tracks water usage and provides maintenance reminders. While it lacks voice assistant support, the app functionality is robust enough for most users. The water heater is tankless, meaning you get endless warm water, and the seat occupancy sensor ensures the bidet only activates when someone is seated. At this price point, the Ultimate 770 offers exceptional value, making it a top choice for budget-conscious buyers who don’t want to compromise on features.

### 3. Brondell Swash 1400: The Smart Home Champion

**Price:** $699 (2026 estimate)

The Brondell Swash 1400 is designed for the tech-savvy homeowner who wants full smart home integration. This seat works seamlessly with Amazon Alexa, Google Assistant, and Apple HomeKit, allowing you to control everything from the wash cycle to the seat temperature using voice commands or your smartphone. The Swash 1400 features a sleek, contemporary design with a heated seat, adjustable water temperature and pressure, and a dual-nozzle system with oscillating and pulsating modes. Its standout feature is the “Smart Seat” technology that learns your preferences over time, automatically adjusting settings based on your usage patterns. The remote control is intuitive and includes a backlight for nighttime use. The Swash 1400 also includes a warm air dryer, deodorizer, and a slow-close lid that prevents slamming. Installation is similar to other models, but the included quick-connect T-valve simplifies the process. Users appreciate the “Eco Mode” that reduces energy consumption when the seat is not in use. The only potential drawback is that the nozzle cleaning cycle is slightly louder than competitors, but this is a minor issue. For those building a fully integrated smart home, the Swash 1400 is the clear winner.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Bidet Seat?

Smart bidet seats are suitable for a wide range of users, but they particularly benefit:
- **Families with multiple users:** Models with user profiles allow everyone to save their preferred settings.
- **Elderly or mobility-challenged individuals:** The hands-free operation and heated seat enhance comfort and independence.
- **Eco-conscious homeowners:** Reducing toilet paper usage can save trees and lower household waste.
- **Tech enthusiasts:** Integration with smart home systems adds convenience and automation.
- **Anyone with sensitive skin:** Gentle cleaning reduces irritation compared to dry toilet paper.

### Budget Considerations

- **Entry-level ($200–$400):** Basic models with heated seats and simple wash functions. Suitable for small bathrooms or guest bathrooms.
- **Mid-range ($400–$700):** Most popular category, offering tankless water heaters, multiple wash modes, and app control. The Bio Bidet Ultimate 770 and Brondell Swash 1400 fall here.
- **Premium ($700–$1,000+):** Top-tier features like voice control, advanced hygiene systems, and premium materials. The Toto Washlet S7A is a prime example.

Consider your long-term needs: a higher upfront cost often translates to better durability and more features. Also, factor in potential savings on toilet paper (the average family saves $50–$100 per year).

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Process

Installing a smart bidet seat is a DIY-friendly project for most homeowners. Here’s a general overview:
1. **Turn off the water supply** and flush the toilet to empty the tank.
2. **Remove the existing toilet seat** by unscrewing the bolts.
3. **Install the bidet seat** using the included mounting bracket and bolts. Ensure it’s level and secure.
4. **Connect the water supply:** Use the included T-valve to split the water line between the toilet tank and the bidet. Most models use standard 1/2-inch connections.
5. **Plug in the power cord** (models require a nearby GFCI outlet). Some units have a battery backup option.
6. **Test the functions** and adjust settings as needed.

Most installations take 30–45 minutes. If you’re not comfortable with plumbing, hire a handyman for around $100–$150.

### Smart Home Integration

- **Voice Assistants:** Models like the Toto Washlet S7A and Brondell Swash 1400 work with Alexa and Google Assistant. Common voice commands include “Alexa, turn on the bidet” or “Hey Google, set seat temperature to warm.”
- **App Control:** The Bio Bidet app and Brondell app allow you to create schedules, monitor water usage, and receive maintenance alerts.
- **Automation:** Integrate with smart home hubs like SmartThings or HomeKit to trigger actions (e.g., turn on the bidet when the bathroom light turns on).
- **Energy Management:** Use smart plugs or built-in Eco modes to reduce standby power.
