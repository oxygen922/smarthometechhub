# Smart Toilet Review 2026: Are AI-Powered Bidets Worth the Investment?

## Introduction

The smart toilet has evolved from a luxury novelty into one of the most transformative additions to the modern American bathroom. In 2026, these intelligent fixtures do far more than warm your seat—they analyze, sanitize, self-clean, and integrate seamlessly with your broader smart home ecosystem. If you've been researching smart toilet reviews, you've likely noticed a flood of options ranging from $300 bidet seats to $10,000 integrated intelligent toilets, each promising spa-level comfort and hands-free hygiene.

But here's the honest truth: not every smart toilet justifies its price tag. Some excel at the fundamentals—heated seats, adjustable bidet spray, and reliable self-cleaning—while others pile on gimmicky features like voice assistants and app controls you'll rarely use. In this in-depth smart toilet review, I'll break down the top three models worth buying in 2026, the key features that actually matter, and how to choose the right intelligent toilet for your home, budget, and plumbing setup.

Whether you're upgrading a master bathroom or dipping your toes into bidet technology for the first time, this guide will help you make a confident, informed decision.

## Top 3 Smart Toilet Recommendations for 2026

### 1. TOTO Neorest NX2 — Best Overall Smart Toilet

**Estimated Price (2026): $9,200–$10,500**

TOTO remains the undisputed king of the smart toilet category, and the Neorest NX2 is its flagship masterpiece. This integrated intelligent toilet combines TOTO's legendary Cyclone flushing system with the most refined bidet experience on the market. The Actilight self-cleaning technology uses UV light and a titanium dioxide-coated bowl to break down waste and neutralize odors automatically—no scrubbing required.

The bidet functions are where the NX2 truly shines. The eWater+ system electrolyzes tap water into a mild hypochlorous acid solution that sanitizes both the wand and the bowl after every use. The heated seat, warm air dryer, and adjustable spray patterns (front, rear, and oscillating) are all controlled via a sleek wireless remote or the accompanying TOTO app. The Premist feature mists the bowl before use, preventing waste from sticking—a small detail that makes a big difference in cleanliness.

**Pros:**
- Industry-leading self-cleaning and odor elimination
- Luxurious, precise bidet controls
- Whisper-quiet, powerful flushing
- Sleek, low-profile design that complements modern bathrooms

**Cons:**
- Extremely expensive
- Requires a dedicated electrical outlet near the toilet
- Overkill for casual users

**Verdict:** If budget is no object and you want the absolute best smart toilet money can buy, the TOTO Neorest NX2 is the gold standard. It's a long-term investment in hygiene, comfort, and bathroom luxury.

### 2. Kohler Numera — Best Smart Toilet for Design-Conscious Homes

**Estimated Price (2026): $4,800–$5,600**

Kohler's Numera smart toilet strikes an impressive balance between premium features and accessible pricing. Unlike the TOTO Neorest, the Numera is designed to fit standard 12-inch rough-in plumbing, making it a far easier retrofit for most American homes. The one-piece design is available in multiple finishes, including Kohler's signature Vibrant Brushed Nickel and Matte Black, so it doubles as a design statement.

Functionally, the Numera delivers a heated seat with adjustable temperature, a stainless steel bidet wand with front and rear wash modes, and a warm air dryer. The self-cleaning UV system sanitizes the bowl interior, and the integrated nightlight provides gentle illumination for late-night visits. The touchscreen remote is intuitive, and Kohler's Konnect app lets you customize user profiles—handy for households with multiple family members who prefer different spray intensities and temperatures.

**Pros:**
- Beautiful, customizable design
- Easier installation than many competitors
- Reliable Kohler build quality and warranty
- User profiles for multiple family members

**Cons:**
- Bidet spray pressure is slightly less adjustable than TOTO
- App connectivity can occasionally lag
- Still a significant investment

**Verdict:** The Kohler Numera is the best smart toilet for homeowners who want premium performance without the TOTO price premium. Its design flexibility and easier installation make it a practical choice for most US bathrooms.

### 3. Bio Bidet Discovery DLS — Best Value Smart Toilet

**Estimated Price (2026): $1,800–$2,300**

For shoppers who want integrated smart toilet features without spending five figures, the Bio Bidet Discovery DLS is the standout value pick. This one-piece intelligent toilet offers a heated seat, adjustable warm water bidet with front and rear wash, a warm air dryer, and a deodorizer—all the essentials that matter most in daily use.

The Discovery DLS uses a tankless design with a powerful 1.28 GPF flush, and the wireless remote is straightforward and responsive. While it lacks the UV self-cleaning and advanced odor-neutralizing tech of the TOTO and Kohler models, it compensates with a remarkably low price for an integrated unit. The stainless steel nozzle is self-cleaning, and the seat features a slow-close mechanism.

**Pros:**
- Exceptional value for an integrated smart toilet
- Strong flush performance
- Simple, reliable remote control
- Affordable entry into integrated smart toilets

**Cons:**
- No UV or electrolyzed water self-cleaning
- Less premium materials than luxury competitors
- Limited smart home integration

**Verdict:** The Bio Bidet Discovery DLS is the smart toilet I recommend most often to first-time buyers. It delivers 80% of the luxury experience at 20% of the price.

## Buying Guide: Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

### Who Should Buy

- **Comfort seekers:** Anyone who dreads cold toilet seats or wants a spa-like bathroom experience.
- **Households reducing toilet paper:** A bidet-equipped smart toilet dramatically cuts paper usage—many users report savings of $100+ per year.
- **People with mobility limitations:** Heated seats, remote controls, and self-cleaning reduce physical effort.
- **Smart home enthusiasts:** Integrated models pair well with Google Home, Amazon Alexa, and Apple Home ecosystems.
- **Home renovators:** If you're remodeling a bathroom, installing an integrated smart toilet is far easier than retrofitting later.

### Who Should Skip

- **Renters:** Installation requires plumbing and electrical work you may not be permitted to do.
- **Tight budgets:** A quality bidet seat attachment ($300–$600) delivers most hygiene benefits for far less.
- **Those without a nearby outlet:** Every smart toilet needs a GFCI-protected electrical outlet within reach.

### Budget Considerations

In 2026, expect to spend:

- **$300–$800:** Bidet seat attachments (not full toilets) with heated seats and basic wash.
- **$1,500–$3,000:** Entry-level integrated smart toilets like the Bio Bidet Discovery DLS.
- **$4,000–$6,000:** Mid-to-premium models like the Kohler Numera.
- **$8,000–$11,000:** Flagship luxury toilets like the TOTO Neorest NX2.

Don't forget installation costs, which can run $300–$1,000 depending on whether you need new electrical work or plumbing modifications.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Requirements

Installing a smart toilet is more involved than swapping a standard fixture. You'll need:

1. **A standard 12-inch rough-in** (most US homes qualify, but verify before buying).
2. **A GFCI-protected electrical outlet** within about 3 feet of the toilet. If you don't have one, hire a licensed electrician—this typically costs $200–$500.
3. **A water supply line** with adequate pressure. Most integrated smart toilets use a tankless flush requiring decent water pressure.
4. **Professional installation recommended** for integrated units. While bidet seats are DIY-friendly, full smart toilets involve electrical and plumbing connections best handled by a pro.

### Smart Home Integration

Premium models like the TOTO Neorest and Kohler Numera integrate with major smart home platforms. You can:

- Control bidet functions and seat temperature via **Google Home** or **Amazon Alexa**.
- Create **user profiles** that remember individual preferences.
- Receive **maintenance alerts** and filter replacement reminders through companion apps.
- Sync with **smart lighting** so your bathroom nightlight activates automatically.

Budget models often lack these integrations, so if smart home connectivity matters to you, prioritize mid-range and premium options.
