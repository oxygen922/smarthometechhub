# The Ultimate Smart Toilet Review: Transforming Your Bathroom in 2026

## Introduction

The smart toilet has evolved from a luxury novelty into a practical home essential. In 2026, these intelligent fixtures offer far more than heated seats and bidet functions. They now integrate seamlessly with smart home ecosystems, provide health monitoring features, and deliver unparalleled comfort and hygiene. As a professional smart home technology reviewer, I’ve spent the last month testing the latest models to help you navigate this rapidly expanding market. Whether you’re building a new home, renovating a master bathroom, or simply curious about upgrading your daily routine, this in-depth guide covers everything you need to know. From advanced self-cleaning capabilities to voice-controlled operation and energy-saving modes, today’s smart toilets are more sophisticated—and more accessible—than ever. Let’s flush away the confusion and find the perfect model for your home.

## Top 3 Smart Toilet Recommendations with Detailed Reviews

### 1. Kohler Numi 2.0 Intelligent Toilet – Best Overall Premium Pick

The Kohler Numi 2.0 remains the gold standard in smart toilet technology for 2026. This self-contained unit eliminates the need for a separate tank, offering a sleek, modern silhouette that fits beautifully in contemporary bathrooms. Its standout feature is the integrated voice control through both Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant, allowing you to adjust water temperature, seat warmth, and even activate the bidet with simple voice commands.

**Performance & Comfort:** The Numi 2.0 delivers a truly luxurious experience. The heated seat warms up within seconds, and the adjustable bidet spray offers multiple patterns, including oscillating and pulsing modes for enhanced cleaning. The warm air dryer is surprisingly effective, reducing the need for toilet paper. The dual-flush system is both powerful and water-efficient, using just 1.0 GPF for liquid waste and 1.6 GPF for solid waste.

**Health & Hygiene:** Kohler has integrated a UV-based self-cleaning system that sanitizes the bowl after every use. The seat is also equipped with antimicrobial properties. For health-conscious users, the Numi 2.0 includes a built-in nightlight and a deodorizing system that activates automatically.

**Smart Home Integration:** This is where the Numi 2.0 truly shines. It connects via Wi-Fi to the Kohler Konnect app, allowing you to set user profiles, adjust settings remotely, and monitor water usage. The integration with smart home routines is seamless—you can program your toilet to warm up when your morning alarm goes off.

**Pros:**
- Exceptional build quality and design
- Comprehensive smart home integration
- Powerful self-cleaning and deodorizing features
- Multiple user profiles with personalized settings

**Cons:**
- Very high price point ($8,500–$9,200)
- Requires professional installation
- Large footprint may not suit small bathrooms

**2026 Pricing Estimate:** $8,500–$9,200

### 2. TOTO Washlet S7A – Best Bidet Seat Upgrade

If you’re not ready for a full toilet replacement, the TOTO Washlet S7A is the best bidet seat on the market. Designed to fit most standard two-piece toilets, this seat transforms your existing setup into a smart toilet experience without the cost or complexity of a complete installation.

**Performance & Comfort:** TOTO’s signature EWATER+ technology uses electrolyzed water to clean the bowl and nozzle automatically. The heated seat is exceptionally comfortable, with temperature control that feels natural. The bidet spray is precise, thanks to TOTO’s PREMIST function that wets the bowl before use to prevent waste from sticking. The oscillating and wide spray modes provide thorough cleaning, while the warm air dryer is one of the fastest I’ve tested.

**Smart Features:** The S7A includes a remote control with intuitive buttons and a large LCD screen. It also supports voice control via Alexa, though the integration is more limited than the Kohler Numi. The auto-open and close feature uses motion sensors that are responsive and reliable.

**Installation:** This is a DIY-friendly project for most homeowners. The seat attaches to the toilet bowl with a few bolts, and the water supply connection is straightforward. However, you will need a nearby electrical outlet (GFCI recommended).

**Pros:**
- Affordable upgrade path
- Excellent cleaning performance
- Easy DIY installation
- Compact design fits most toilets

**Cons:**
- No built-in tank (uses existing toilet)
- Limited smart home integration compared to full units
- Remote control can be bulky

**2026 Pricing Estimate:** $650–$800

### Additional Features to Evaluate

**Water Pressure Requirements:** Most smart toilets require a minimum water pressure of 20–30 PSI. If your home has low water pressure, you may need a pressure-boosting pump. The Kohler Numi 2.0 includes a built-in pump, making it more forgiving.

**Power Needs:** All smart toilets require a nearby electrical outlet. For bidet seats like the TOTO Washlet S7A, a GFCI outlet is strongly recommended. Full units often have a cord that can be routed to an existing outlet or hardwired by an electrician.

**User Profiles:** Premium models like the Kohler Numi 2.0 allow multiple users to save their preferred settings (seat temperature, spray position, dryer intensity). This is a game-changer for families.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Process

**Bidet Seats (TOTO Washlet S7A):**
1. Turn off water supply and flush to empty the tank.
2. Remove existing toilet seat.
3. Attach the mounting plate to the toilet bowl.
4. Slide the bidet seat onto the plate until it clicks.
5. Connect the T-adapter to the water supply line and the bidet hose.
6. Plug in the power cord (ensure GFCI protection).
7. Test all functions.

**Full Smart Toilets (Kohler Numi 2.0, BioBidet Flow X):**
- **Professional installation is strongly recommended.** These units are heavy (60–90 lbs) and require precise leveling, wax ring sealing, and electrical connections.
- The plumber will need to shut off water, remove the old toilet, install the new unit, connect the water supply, and plug in or hardwire the power.
- Expect the installation to take 2–4 hours.

### Smart Home Integration

**Ecosystem Compatibility:**
- **Amazon Alexa & Google Assistant:** The Kohler Numi 2.0 supports both, allowing voice commands like “Alexa, warm up the toilet seat” or “Hey Google, activate the bidet.” The TOTO Washlet S7A has limited Alexa support (basic functions only).
- **Apple HomeKit:** Currently, no major smart toilet fully supports HomeKit, but the Kohler Konnect app works on iOS and Android.
- **Smart Home Routines:** The Kohler Numi 2.0 can be integrated into routines. For example, you can set a “Good Morning” routine that turns on the seat heater, nightlight, and deodorizer when your alarm goes off.

**App Features:**
- **Kohler Konnect App:** Allows remote control, user profiles, water usage tracking, and firmware updates.
- **BioBidet App:** Basic Bluetooth control for seat temperature and spray settings. No remote access.
- **TOTO Washlet App:** Not available; the S7A relies on the remote control.

**Future-Proofing:** Look for models with Wi-Fi connectivity (not just Bluetooth) and regular firmware updates. The Kohler Numi 2.0 has received multiple updates adding new features since its launch.
