# The Ultimate Smart Bidet Seat Review: Transform Your Bathroom in 2026

## Introduction

The humble toilet has undergone a remarkable transformation. What was once a simple porcelain throne is now a hub of smart home technology, and at the center of this revolution is the smart bidet seat. In 2026, these devices have evolved far beyond their basic predecessors, offering heated seats, customizable wash programs, air dryers, and even voice control integration. For the modern American homeowner, a smart bidet seat isn't just a luxury—it's an investment in hygiene, comfort, and sustainability. With the average American household using 300 rolls of toilet paper per year, a bidet seat can drastically reduce waste while providing a superior clean. However, navigating the crowded market can be daunting. This review will guide you through the top products, essential features, and installation considerations, ensuring you find the perfect smart bidet seat for your home in 2026.

## Top 3 Product Recommendations

### 1. Toto S7A Washlet+ – The Premium Choice

**Price:** $1,299 (estimated 2026 MSRP)

The Toto S7A Washlet+ is the gold standard in smart bidet seats. Building on decades of Japanese engineering, this model introduces the "eWater+" system, which uses electrolyzed water for self-cleaning and nozzle sterilization. The heated seat features six temperature settings, and the air dryer now includes a "gentle wave" mode that alternates temperature for a spa-like experience.

**Performance:** The oscillating, pulsating, and wide-angle spray patterns are genuinely adjustable to the millimeter. The "Rear Comfort Clean" mode uses a wider spray at lower pressure, ideal for sensitive users. The front wash for women is equally refined, with a soft, aerated stream.

**Smart Home Integration:** The S7A connects via Wi-Fi to Toto's app, allowing you to save up to five user profiles (including seat temperature, water temperature, and spray position). It works with Amazon Alexa and Google Home for voice commands like "Alexa, start rear wash." The app also tracks usage for water conservation insights.

**Pros:** Unmatched build quality, self-cleaning nozzle, excellent app support, true hands-free operation.
**Cons:** Premium price, requires a nearby electrical outlet, complex installation for DIY enthusiasts.

### 2. Bio Bidet BB-2000 Supreme – The Best Value

**Price:** $599 (estimated 2026 MSRP)

For those seeking high-end features without breaking the bank, the Bio Bidet BB-2000 Supreme is a standout. This model offers a heated seat, dual stainless steel nozzles, and a powerful 5-position warm air dryer. The unique "Smart Seat Sensor" automatically activates the bidet when you sit down and deactivates when you stand, preventing accidental sprays.

**Performance:** The water pressure and temperature controls are intuitive, with a side-mounted remote that includes a backlit LCD screen. The "Turbowash" mode delivers a concentrated stream for stubborn messes, while the "Soft Wash" is gentle enough for daily use. The nozzle self-cleans before and after each use.

**Smart Home Integration:** While it lacks Wi-Fi, the BB-2000 Supreme includes a "Night Light" feature with motion sensor activation. It also has a "Energy Saver" mode that learns your usage patterns and adjusts heating accordingly. For true smart home integration, you can pair it with a smart plug (sold separately) for voice control via Alexa or Google Assistant.

**Pros:** Excellent value, powerful drying, easy-to-use remote, reliable build.
**Cons:** No native Wi-Fi, remote is not backlit, plastic seat feels less premium than Toto.

### 3. Kohler Novita BH90 – The Budget Smart Option

**Price:** $349 (estimated 2026 MSRP)

Kohler's Novita line brings smart bidet technology to the budget-conscious consumer. The BH90 offers a heated seat, warm water wash, and a front-and-rear cleansing wand, all in an ultra-slim profile. The "Quick-Release" mechanism makes cleaning a breeze, and the seat is designed to fit most standard toilets.

**Performance:** The water pressure is adjustable across 5 levels, and the temperature has 3 settings. The wash is effective but not as refined as the Toto or Bio Bidet models. The air dryer is functional but takes longer to achieve full dryness. The self-cleaning nozzle uses ultraviolet light, a nice touch at this price point.

**Smart Home Integration:** The BH90 includes a simple remote with a digital display, but no Wi-Fi or voice control. However, it does feature a "Sit-and-Sense" sensor that pre-heats the seat when you enter the bathroom (if paired with a motion sensor). This is a clever, low-cost way to add a smart element.

**Pros:** Affordable, easy installation, slim design, UV sanitization.
**Cons:** No app or voice control, slower drying, limited spray customization.

### Additional Considerations

- **Water Pressure:** Look for adjustable pressure (at least 5 levels) to accommodate sensitive users.
- **Spray Patterns:** Oscillating and pulsating modes provide a more thorough clean.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Basics

Installing a smart bidet seat is a DIY-friendly project for most homeowners, but it requires careful attention to detail. Here's a step-by-step overview:

1. **Turn off the water supply** to the toilet.
2. **Remove the existing toilet seat** by unscrewing the mounting bolts.
3. **Install the bidet seat** using the provided mounting plate and bolts.
4. **Connect the water supply** using the included T-valve adapter. This splits the water line between the toilet tank and the bidet.
5. **Plug in the bidet** to a GFCI-protected outlet. If you don't have one, call an electrician.
6. **Test the unit** for leaks and functionality.

**Time Estimate:** 30–60 minutes for a standard toilet. For older toilets with non-standard bolt spacing, you may need an adapter kit.

### Smart Home Integration

- **Wi-Fi Models (Toto S7A):** Connect to your home network via the Toto app. Create user profiles, set schedules (e.g., seat pre-heating at 7 AM), and receive maintenance alerts. Voice commands via Alexa or Google Assistant let you start washes, adjust temperature, and activate the night light.
- **Non-Wi-Fi Models (Bio Bidet, Kohler):** Use a smart plug (like the Kasa KP125) to enable basic voice control. For example, "Alexa, turn on the bidet" will power the unit, but you'll still need the remote for wash functions.
- **Future-Proofing:** Consider a smart bathroom hub like the Moen Flo for water leak detection. Pairing a bidet seat with a smart leak sensor adds an extra layer of safety.
