# The Ultimate Smart Toilet Review: 2026’s Best Intelligent Thrones for a Smarter Bathroom

## Introduction

The smart toilet has evolved from a futuristic luxury into a practical, health-conscious upgrade for the modern American home. In 2026, these intelligent thrones are no longer just about heated seats and bidet functions—they now integrate seamlessly with smart home ecosystems, offer advanced health monitoring, and even self-clean. As a professional smart home technology reviewer, I’ve tested dozens of models over the past year, analyzing everything from water pressure to Wi-Fi connectivity. This comprehensive guide will help you navigate the crowded market, whether you’re a tech enthusiast seeking voice control or a family looking for hygiene-first features. By the end, you’ll know exactly which smart toilet deserves a spot in your bathroom renovation.

## Top 3 Product Recommendations with Detailed Reviews

### 1. **Kohler Numi 2.0 Intelligent Toilet** – Best Overall Premium Smart Toilet

**Rating: 9.5/10**  
**Price: $7,500 – $8,200 (2026 estimate)**

The Kohler Numi 2.0 remains the gold standard for smart toilets. This fully integrated unit features a sleek, one-piece design with virtually no seams, making cleaning effortless. The heated seat is adjustable across five temperature zones, and the bidet offers customizable water pressure, temperature, and spray patterns.

**Standout Features:**
- **Voice Control:** Compatible with Amazon Alexa, Google Assistant, and Apple HomeKit. You can say, “Alexa, warm the seat,” and it responds instantly.
- **Health Monitoring:** The Numi 2.0 includes a built-in urine analysis system that tracks hydration levels, glucose, and pH balance. Data syncs to the Kohler Konnect app.
- **Self-Cleaning:** After each use, the bowl is sanitized with UV light and electrolyzed water, eliminating 99.9% of bacteria.
- **Night Light:** Ambient LED lighting with customizable colors.

**Performance:** The dual-flush system (1.1/1.6 GPF) is powerful yet quiet. The bidet spray is precise, with a warm air dryer that actually works in under 30 seconds. The only downside is the price—this is a luxury investment.

**Verdict:** If budget isn’t a concern, the Numi 2.0 is the ultimate smart toilet for 2026. Perfect for high-end smart homes.

### 2. **TOTO Washlet G5** – Best Mid-Range Smart Toilet Seat

**Rating: 8.8/10**  
**Price: $1,200 – $1,500 (2026 estimate)**

TOTO’s Washlet G5 is a smart toilet seat that retrofits onto most standard two-piece toilets. It’s the perfect entry point for those who want smart features without replacing their entire toilet.

**Standout Features:**
- **EWATER+ System:** Electrolyzed water cleans the nozzle and bowl, reducing the need for harsh chemicals.
- **PREMIST:** Pre-mists the bowl before use to prevent waste from sticking.
- **ActiClean:** A self-cleaning system that uses a combination of water and UV light.
- **Remote Control:** A sleek wireless remote with intuitive buttons.

**Performance:** The bidet offers oscillating and pulsating spray modes for a more thorough clean. The seat is heated, and the air dryer is effective but slightly slower than the Numi. Installation is straightforward for DIYers.

**Verdict:** The Washlet G5 is the best value for money in 2026. It’s reliable, hygienic, and integrates with Alexa and Google Home via the TOTO app.

## Buying Guide: Who Should Buy and Budget Considerations

### Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

1. **Tech Enthusiasts:** If you love smart home gadgets, a voice-controlled smart toilet is a natural addition. The Kohler Numi 2.0 or TOTO Washlet G5 will integrate beautifully with your existing ecosystem.

2. **Families with Young Children or Seniors:** Heated seats, automatic lids, and bidet functions reduce mess and improve hygiene. The self-cleaning features are a game-changer for busy parents.

3. **People with Mobility Issues:** Touchless flushing, automatic opening/closing lids, and warm air dryers make daily routines easier. Look for models with remote controls and high seats.

4. **Eco-Conscious Consumers:** Smart toilets use less water per flush (1.1–1.6 GPF vs. 1.6–3.5 GPF for older models). The self-cleaning systems also reduce chemical use.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $700:** Bio Bidet BB-2000 or similar seat attachments. You’ll get core features but no smart home integration.
- **$1,000 – $2,000:** TOTO Washlet G5 or comparable. Excellent hygiene features with basic app control.
- **$5,000+:** Integrated units like Kohler Numi 2.0 or TOTO Neorest. These offer the best design, health monitoring, and smart home compatibility.

**Pro Tip:** Factor in installation costs ($200–$500 for integrated units) and electrical work if you don’t have a GFCI outlet near the toilet.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Overview

- **Toilet Seat Attachments (TOTO Washlet G5, Bio Bidet BB-2000):** These are DIY-friendly. You’ll need a T-adapter for the water supply and a GFCI outlet within 3 feet. Most models include clear instructions, and installation takes 30–60 minutes.

- **Integrated Toilets (Kohler Numi 2.0):** Professional installation is strongly recommended. These units require precise leveling, plumbing connections, and often a dedicated electrical circuit. Expect to pay $300–$500 for installation.

### Smart Home Integration

- **Amazon Alexa & Google Assistant:** The Kohler Numi 2.0 and TOTO Washlet G5 support voice commands for seat heating, bidet activation, and flushing. You can create routines like, “Goodnight,” which closes the lid and turns off the night light.

- **Apple HomeKit:** Only the Numi 2.0 offers full HomeKit support, allowing Siri control and automation via the Home app.

- **Proprietary Apps:** Both Kohler and TOTO have apps for firmware updates, usage tracking, and health data (Numi 2.0 only). These apps are well-designed and regularly updated.

**Compatibility Note:** Ensure your Wi-Fi router is within range. Some smart toilets require a 2.4 GHz network, so check specifications before purchase.
