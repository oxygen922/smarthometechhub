# The Best Bidet Seats of 2026: Smart Toilet Upgrades Worth Every Penny

## Introduction

The humble bidet seat has quietly become one of the most transformative smart home upgrades you can make in 2026. Gone are the days when a bidet meant a separate porcelain fixture requiring dedicated plumbing and floor space. Today's bidet seats retrofit onto your existing toilet in under an hour, adding heated water, warm air drying, and app-based controls that rival the smartest devices in your home. Whether you're motivated by hygiene, sustainability, or simply the luxury of a warmed seat on a cold winter morning, the modern bidet seat delivers. In this review, we'll break down the top three bidet seats on the market, explain the features that actually matter, and help you decide which model fits your bathroom—and your budget. If you've been on the fence about upgrading your throne, 2026 is the year to do it.

## Top 3 Bidet Seat Recommendations

### 1. TOTO Washlet S7A — The Luxury Benchmark

**Estimated Price (2026): $1,899–$2,100**

If there's a gold standard in the bidet seat world, it's the TOTO Washlet S7A. TOTO practically invented the modern electronic bidet seat, and the S7A represents decades of refinement. This is the model you buy when you want the best and don't want to think about compromises.

The S7A's headline feature is its **actuated lid and seat**, which open and close automatically as you approach and leave—a genuinely delightful touch that never gets old. The **ewater+ system** electrolyzes tap water to create a natural cleaning agent that sanitizes the wand and bowl after every use, reducing the need for harsh chemicals. The **premist** function sprays a fine mist of water on the bowl before use, which helps prevent waste from adhering to the porcelain.

Performance-wise, the S7A offers **adjustable water temperature, pressure, and oscillating or pulsating spray patterns**. The warm air dryer is powerful enough to actually dry you off, not just blow lukewarm air for three minutes. The heated seat has multiple temperature settings, and the **remote control** is intuitive, with a wall-mountable dock that keeps it within easy reach.

Installation requires a GFCI outlet near the toilet and a flexible water supply line, both of which TOTO includes. The unit supports **auto-flush integration** with compatible TOTO toilets, and the **nightlight** is a subtle but welcome addition for late-night visits.

**Pros:** Best-in-class build quality, auto open/close lid, ewater+ sanitation, powerful dryer
**Cons:** Premium price, requires nearby outlet, best paired with a TOTO toilet for full features

**Verdict:** The S7A is the bidet seat to beat. If your budget allows, this is the one.

The Bio Bidet BB-2000 has been a perennial favorite, and for good reason: it packs an absurd number of features into a mid-range price. This is the model we recommend most often to friends and family who want a "real" bidet experience without a four-figure investment.

The BB-2000 uses a **tankless, instant water heating system**, which means unlimited warm water—no waiting for a reservoir to refill and no cold surprise halfway through. It offers **three spray modes** (posterior, feminine, and turbo), each with adjustable pressure and a **wide, oscillating spray** that covers more surface area. The **heated seat** and **warm air dryer** are both adjustable, and the **deodorizer** uses a carbon filter to neutralize odors effectively.

One standout feature is the **massage mode**, which pulses water pressure to stimulate circulation—a surprisingly pleasant addition. Build quality is solid, though not quite TOTO-level. The plastic feels slightly less premium, and the lid isn't as sturdy. But at roughly a quarter of the S7A's price, the BB-2000 is an extraordinary value.

**Pros:** Tankless heating, three spray modes, massage function, excellent price-to-feature ratio

**Verdict:** The best all-around bidet seat for most households. Hard to beat at this price.

### Water Heating: Tankless vs. Reservoir

**Tankless (instant) heating** is the feature to prioritize. Reservoir-based seats store a small amount of heated water, which runs out quickly and can leave you with a cold spray. All three models above use tankless heating, which provides unlimited warm water on demand. This is non-negotiable in 2026.

### Spray Modes and Adjustability

At minimum, look for **posterior and feminine wash modes**, plus adjustable water pressure and temperature. Higher-end models add **oscillating** (moving back and forth) and **pulsating** (massaging) patterns, which improve coverage and comfort.

### Heated Seat and Dryer

A **heated seat** is one of the most appreciated features, especially in colder climates. A **warm air dryer** is convenient but rarely as effective as a few squares of toilet paper—still, a good dryer reduces paper use and adds a touch of luxury.

### Deodorizer and Sanitization

A **carbon filter deodorizer** neutralizes odors effectively. **Self-cleaning wands** and **UV or electrolyzed water sanitization** (like TOTO's ewater+) keep the unit hygienic with minimal effort.

### Smart Home Integration

Only some bidet seats offer **voice control or app connectivity**. If you want your toilet to respond to Alexa or Google, the Kohler E930 is your best bet. Otherwise, a physical remote is perfectly adequate.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Bidet Seat?

- **Hygiene-focused households:** Bidets provide a more thorough clean than toilet paper alone, and they're gentler on sensitive skin.
- **Eco-conscious users:** A bidet seat dramatically reduces toilet paper consumption, saving trees and money over time.
- **People with mobility issues:** Heated seats, remote controls, and auto-open lids make bathroom visits easier and more dignified.
- **Cold-climate residents:** A heated seat is a small daily luxury that pays off all winter.
- **Smart home enthusiasts:** If you already control your lights and thermostat by voice, a connected bidet seat fits right in.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $400:** Basic bidet seats with cold water only or small reservoirs. Fine for mild climates, but limited.
- **$400–$700:** The sweet spot. You get tankless heating, heated seat, dryer, and deodorizer. The **Bio Bidet BB-2000** dominates here.
- **$700–$1,300:** Premium features like UV sanitization, sleeker designs, and smart home integration. The **Kohler E930** is the standout.
- **$1,300+:** Luxury tier with auto open/close lids, advanced sanitation, and the best build quality. The **TOTO S7A** leads.

### Additional Costs to Budget For

- **GFCI outlet installation:** $150–$350 if you don't already have one near the toilet.
- **Water supply line:** Usually included, but verify.
- **Extended warranty:** Optional; most units come with a 3-year warranty.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Overview

Installing a bidet seat is a DIY-friendly project that typically takes **30–60 minutes**. Here's the general process:

1. **Turn off the water supply** and flush the toilet to empty the tank.
2. **Remove the existing toilet seat** by unscrewing the mounting bolts.
3. **Install the mounting bracket** for the bidet seat, following the manufacturer's template.
4. **Connect the T-adapter** to the toilet's water supply line, then attach the bidet's flexible hose.
5. **Plug the unit into a GFCI outlet.** If you don't have one nearby, hire a licensed electrician.
6. **Attach the bidet seat** to the bracket, connect the water line, and secure the seat.
7. **Turn the water back on**, check for leaks, and power on the unit.

Most manufacturers, including TOTO, Bio Bidet, and Kohler, provide clear instructions and all necessary hardware. If you're not comfortable with basic plumbing, a handyman can install it for $100–$200.

### Smart Home Integration

- **Kohler PureWash E930:** Works with **Amazon Alexa and Google Home**. You can create routines, such as lowering the seat or activating the nightlight with a voice command. It also integrates with Kohler's **Konnect app** for firmware updates and settings.
- **TOTO Washlet S7A:** Offers a **remote control app** via Bluetooth for basic adjustments, but no voice assistant support. It pairs best with TOTO's **Neorest** toilets for auto-flush.
- **Bio Bidet BB-2000:** No smart home integration. It relies on its physical remote, which is fine for most users.

If voice control is a priority, the Kohler E930 is the clear winner. Otherwise, a physical remote is more than sufficient for daily use.
