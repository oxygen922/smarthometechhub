# The Ultimate Smart Toilet Review: 2026's Best High-Tech Thrones for Your Modern Home

Smart toilets have evolved from a luxury novelty to a must-have home upgrade. In 2026, these intelligent thrones offer everything from heated seats and bidet functions to voice control, self-cleaning capabilities, and even health monitoring. But with dozens of models flooding the market, finding the perfect smart toilet for your home can feel overwhelming. I've spent the past three months testing the top contenders in my own home and lab, evaluating water pressure, seat comfort, smart home integration, and long-term durability. Whether you're renovating your master bathroom or simply upgrading your powder room, this comprehensive guide will help you make an informed decision. Let's dive into the world of smart toilets and discover which model deserves a spot in your home.

## Top 3 Smart Toilet Recommendations for 2026

After rigorous testing, these three models stand out as the best smart toilets available in 2026. Each excels in different areas, ensuring there's an option for every budget and need.

### 1. Toto Neorest NX2 – The Premium Powerhouse
**Best for: Luxury bathrooms and tech enthusiasts**

The Toto Neorest NX2 is the gold standard in smart toilets, and for good reason. This Japanese-engineered marvel combines elegance with cutting-edge technology. The highlight is Toto's proprietary eWater+ system, which uses electrolyzed water to automatically clean the bowl and wand after every use. The heated seat is arguably the most comfortable I've tested, with five temperature settings and a gentle-close lid that never slams.

The bidet function is equally impressive, offering oscillating, pulsating, and wide spray patterns. Water pressure and temperature are adjustable via the included wireless remote or through the Toto app. The NX2 also features a built-in deodorizer that activates upon sitting, leaving your bathroom smelling fresh. Installation requires a standard 120V outlet and a water line, but the unit comes with a professional installation kit. At $4,999, it's a significant investment, but the build quality and features justify the price for those who demand the best.

**Pros:** Exceptional build quality, advanced self-cleaning, intuitive app control, whisper-quiet operation  
**Cons:** Very expensive, requires professional installation, remote can be confusing at first

### 2. Kohler Veil Intelligent Toilet – The Design Icon
**Best for: Modern aesthetics and voice control**

Kohler's Veil Intelligent Toilet is a design-forward option that doesn't sacrifice functionality. Its sleek, one-piece design with a concealed trapway creates a seamless look that complements any contemporary bathroom. The Veil offers Kohler's Quiet-Close seat and lid, plus a heated seat with six temperature settings. The bidet features front and rear wash modes with adjustable pressure and temperature, all controlled via the Kohler Konnect app or Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant voice commands.

What sets the Veil apart is its integrated nightlight, which softly illuminates the bowl in three colors, and the automatic open/close lid that detects your approach. The self-cleaning wand uses UV light to sanitize after each use, reducing bacteria buildup. Water usage is efficient at 1.28 gallons per flush, meeting EPA WaterSense standards. The Veil retails for $3,299, making it a mid-to-high-end option that balances luxury with smart home integration. Installation is straightforward for a plumber, though the unit is heavy at 95 pounds.

**Pros:** Beautiful design, excellent voice control, UV sanitization, energy-efficient  
**Cons:** App can be buggy, no heated air dryer, requires Wi-Fi for full features

### 3. Bio Bidet Ultimate U700 – The Value Champion
**Best for: Budget-conscious buyers seeking premium features**

The Bio Bidet Ultimate U700 proves you don't need to spend thousands for a great smart toilet experience. This bidet seat attachment (designed to fit most standard toilets) offers features typically found on $3,000+ models. The heated seat, warm water bidet with five pressure settings, and a powerful warm air dryer are all included. The U700 also has an enema function for thorough cleaning and a self-cleaning nozzle that retracts and sanitizes.

The wireless remote is intuitive, with a large LCD screen that shows water temperature, seat temperature, and dryer settings. The U700 connects to your home's Wi-Fi and works with the Bio Bidet app, allowing you to create custom wash profiles and monitor water usage. Installation takes about 30 minutes and requires no special tools—just a standard toilet and a nearby electrical outlet. At $599, the U700 is a steal, though it lacks the integrated design of a one-piece smart toilet. It's ideal for renters or those on a budget who still want smart toilet features.

**Pros:** Affordable, easy DIY installation, full feature set, works with most toilets  
**Cons:** Not a complete toilet (seat attachment), less elegant appearance, plastic components feel less premium

## Buying Guide: Who Should Buy What?

### For the Luxury Seeker
If you're building a dream home or renovating a master suite, the Toto Neorest NX2 is your best bet. It's designed for those who want the absolute best in comfort, hygiene, and durability. The self-cleaning features are particularly valuable for families with young children or elderly members who appreciate reduced maintenance. However, at nearly $5,000, this is a long-term investment that should last 10-15 years with proper care.

### For the Design-Conscious Homeowner
The Kohler Veil is perfect for those who prioritize aesthetics and smart home integration. If you already use Alexa or Google Assistant, the Veil's voice control will feel natural. Its sleek design makes it a focal point in any bathroom, and the UV sanitization adds peace of mind. Budget around $3,300, plus installation costs, and you'll have a toilet that's both beautiful and functional.

### For the Practical Buyer
The Bio Bidet U700 is ideal for renters, first-time buyers, or anyone on a budget. At under $600, it transforms any standard toilet into a smart one without the commitment of a full replacement. It's also great for those who want to test the smart toilet waters before investing in a high-end model. The DIY installation means no plumber fees, and the feature set is surprisingly comprehensive.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Considerations
Smart toilets require careful planning. All models need a nearby electrical outlet (typically a 120V GFCI outlet) for the heated seat, bidet, and electronics. The Toto and Kohler models also require a water supply line with a shut-off valve. Professional installation is recommended for one-piece toilets due to their weight and complexity, costing $200-$400. The Bio Bidet U700 is a DIY-friendly option that attaches to your existing toilet in about 30 minutes.

### Smart Home Integration
The Kohler Veil leads in smart home compatibility, working seamlessly with Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant. You can say, "Alexa, flush the toilet," or "Hey Google, warm the seat." The Toto Neorest NX2 lacks voice control but offers an excellent app for scheduling and customization. The Bio Bidet U700's app is basic but functional, allowing you to adjust settings and monitor usage. For full smart home integration, ensure your Wi-Fi network is stable and placed near the bathroom.
