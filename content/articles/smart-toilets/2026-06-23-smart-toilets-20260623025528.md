# The Ultimate Smart Toilet Review for 2026: Are High-Tech Thrones Worth the Splash?

## Introduction

The smart toilet has evolved from a quirky luxury item into a legitimate cornerstone of the modern smart home. In 2026, these intelligent thrones are no longer just about heated seats and bidet washes; they integrate seamlessly with home automation systems, offer advanced health monitoring, and deliver unprecedented water efficiency. As data centers grapple with cooling challenges (ironically, Nvidia’s latest designs aim to reduce water usage in AI facilities), the smart toilet industry is quietly solving its own water conservation puzzle—using less water per flush than ever before while providing a superior clean.

Whether you’re a tech enthusiast looking to complete your smart bathroom ecosystem or a homeowner seeking to reduce your environmental footprint, the right smart toilet can transform your daily routine. This in-depth review covers the top three models of 2026, breaks down essential features, and helps you decide which high-tech throne deserves a spot in your home. We’ll explore everything from installation quirks to budget considerations, ensuring you make an informed purchase.

---

## Top 3 Product Recommendations with Detailed Reviews

### 1. **TOTO Neorest NX2 (2026 Edition) – The Gold Standard**

**Price Estimate:** $5,800 – $6,500 (including installation kit)

TOTO’s Neorest line has long been the benchmark for luxury smart toilets, and the 2026 NX2 iteration solidifies that reputation. This unit features TOTO’s proprietary **eWater+** system, which uses electrolyzed water to clean the bowl and wand between uses, eliminating the need for harsh chemicals. The **Cyclone Flush** technology has been refined to use just 1.0 gallon per flush (GPF) while outperforming many 1.6 GPF toilets.

**Health & Wellness:** The NX2 now includes a **biometric seat sensor** that tracks heart rate variability and body composition via the seat’s integrated electrodes. Data syncs wirelessly to the TOTO Wellness app (iOS/Android), providing daily trends. While not a medical device, it’s a compelling addition for health-conscious users.

**Smart Home Integration:** Works natively with **Apple HomeKit**, **Amazon Alexa**, and **Google Home**. You can create routines like “Good Morning” that triggers the seat warmer, opens the lid, and pre-mists the bowl with eWater. The unit also supports **Matter** protocol, ensuring future compatibility.

**Pros:**
- Exceptional build quality and ceramic glaze
- Ultra-quiet operation (under 45 dB during flush)
- Comprehensive health tracking suite
- Elegant, minimalist design

**Cons:**
- Extremely high price point
- Requires a dedicated 120V outlet near the toilet
- Health data accuracy varies by user

**Verdict:** The NX2 is for those who want the absolute best. If budget is no object, this is the smart toilet to beat.

---

### 2. **Kohler PureWash E930 – The Sweet Spot of Value and Performance**

**Price Estimate:** $2,200 – $2,800

Kohler’s PureWash E930 is a **bidet seat** that transforms your existing toilet into a smart unit, but it comes with an integrated bowl design that makes it feel like a one-piece system. The 2026 version introduces **Kohler Konnect 3.0**, which adds voice control and advanced scheduling.

**Water Efficiency:** The E930 uses a **dual-flush mechanism** (0.8 GPF for liquid, 1.28 GPF for solid) and features an **automatic bowl cleaning cycle** that uses UV light and electrolyzed water to reduce bacteria buildup. It’s WaterSense certified and saves an estimated 3,000 gallons per year compared to a standard 1.6 GPF toilet.

**User Experience:** The heated seat is adjustable from 80°F to 105°F, and the **oscillating front/rear wash** offers five pressure settings. The **warm air dryer** is noticeably faster than previous generations, drying in under 60 seconds.

**Smart Home Integration:** Supports **Alexa** and **Google Home** for basic commands (flush, seat warm, light). The Kohler Konnect app allows you to set user profiles, so each family member gets their preferred wash temperature and seat warmth. No **HomeKit** support, but a **Matter** bridge is promised for Q3 2026.

**Pros:**
- Excellent value for the feature set
- Easy retrofit installation (fits most standard toilets)
- Great water savings
- User profile memory

**Cons:**
- Not a true one-piece design (seat attaches to existing bowl)
- No health monitoring features
- App can be sluggish

**Verdict:** The E930 is the best choice for most households. It offers 90% of the luxury features at half the price of the TOTO Neorest.

---

### 3. **BioBidet Flow X2 – The Budget-Friendly Powerhouse**

**Price Estimate:** $899 – $1,200

BioBidet (a brand under the LIXIL umbrella) has carved a niche for affordable yet feature-rich smart toilets. The Flow X2 is a complete one-piece unit with a sleek, skirted design that’s easy to clean. The 2026 model adds **voice-activated flush** via a built-in microphone and **smart leak detection**.

**Water & Energy Efficiency:** The Flow X2 uses a **1.28 GPF single-flush** system with a **power-assist** mechanism that ensures a strong flush even with low water pressure. It also features an **energy-saving mode** that reduces seat temperature and water heater usage during off-peak hours.

**Key Features:** Includes a **self-cleaning wand** with UV sterilization, a **nightlight** with motion sensor, and a **deodorizer** that activates after each use. The **remote control** is wireless and wall-mountable.

**Smart Home Integration:** Works with **Alexa** and **Google Home** via the BioBidet Smart app. The app allows you to set schedules, monitor water usage, and receive leak alerts. No **HomeKit** or **Matter** support.

**Pros:**
- Most affordable one-piece smart toilet on the market
- Great water efficiency
- Leak detection is a standout safety feature
- Easy DIY installation

**Cons:**
- Build quality not as premium as TOTO or Kohler
- Seat is slightly less comfortable for larger users
- No health tracking or user profiles

**Verdict:** If you’re on a tight budget but want a true smart toilet experience, the Flow X2 delivers impressive value without major compromises.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

- **Tech Enthusiasts:** If you already have smart lights, thermostats, and locks, a smart toilet completes the ecosystem. The TOTO Neorest NX2 offers the deepest integration with HomeKit and Matter.
- **Eco-Conscious Homeowners:** Water efficiency is a primary benefit. The Kohler PureWash E930 and BioBidet Flow X2 both save thousands of gallons annually.
- **Health-Conscious Individuals:** The Neorest NX2’s biometric tracking is unique, but remember it’s not a substitute for medical advice.
- **Families with Small Children or Elderly:** Features like heated seats, nightlights, and automatic cleaning reduce accidents and improve safety.
- **Renters:** A bidet seat like the Kohler PureWash E930 is a better choice than a full toilet replacement.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $1,000:** The BioBidet Flow X2 is your best bet. You get a complete one-piece toilet with essential smart features.
- **$2,000 – $3,000:** The Kohler PureWash E930 offers the best balance of luxury and cost. This is the sweet spot for most homeowners.
- **$5,000+:** The TOTO Neorest NX2 is for those who want the absolute best, with health tracking and premium materials.

**Hidden Costs:** Factor in installation ($150-$500 for a plumber), electrical work (if no outlet exists near the toilet), and potential need for a pressure-assist pump if your home has low water pressure.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Overview

**DIY Friendly?** The BioBidet Flow X2 is the easiest to install—it comes with a wax ring, bolts, and clear instructions. Most handy homeowners can complete it in 2-3 hours. The Kohler PureWash E930 is a seat replacement that takes about 30 minutes.

**Professional Recommended:** The TOTO Neorest NX2 requires a licensed plumber due to its complex water and electrical connections. Expect to pay $300-$500 for installation.

**Electrical Requirements:** All three units need a grounded 120V outlet within 3 feet of the toilet. If you don’t have one, budget $200-$400 for an electrician to run a new line.

### Smart Home Ecosystem Compatibility

- **Apple HomeKit:** Only the TOTO Neorest NX2 works natively. This is a dealbreaker for Apple-only smart homes.
- **Amazon Alexa & Google Home:** All three models support basic commands. You can say “Alexa, flush the toilet” or “Hey Google, warm the seat.”
- **Matter Protocol:** The TOTO Neorest NX2 is Matter-certified, which means it will work with any future smart home system. The Kohler PureWash E930 promises Matter support later in 2026.
- **Routines & Automation:** The TOTO and Kohler apps allow you to create routines like “Good Night” (seat cools, lid closes, nightlight activates). The BioBidet app is more basic but still useful for scheduling.

**Pro Tip:** If you’re building a new home or renovating, install a dedicated 20-amp circuit for your smart toilet. This prevents power issues when the bidet heater and seat warmer run simultaneously.

## Conclusion & Final Verdict

The smart toilet market in 2026 offers something for every budget and lifestyle. The **TOTO Neorest NX2** remains the undisputed king of luxury, with unparalleled build quality, health tracking, and smart home integration. It’s the choice for those who want the best and are willing to pay for it.

The **Kohler PureWash E930** is the intelligent choice for the majority of homeowners. It delivers nearly all the premium features—heated seat, warm dryer, self-cleaning—at a price that won’t break the bank. Its water efficiency and user profiles make it perfect for families.

The **BioBidet Flow X2** proves that smart toilets don’t have to cost a fortune. It’s an excellent entry point for those curious about the category, with leak detection and voice control that punch above its weight class.

**Final Recommendation:** If you can stretch your budget to the $2,200 range, the Kohler PureWash E930 is the best overall value. For a no-compromise experience, the TOTO Neorest NX2 is worth every penny. And if you’re on a tight budget, the BioBidet Flow X2 won’t disappoint.
