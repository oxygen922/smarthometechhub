### Water Heating: Tankless vs. Tank

One of the most important decisions you will make is whether to choose a tankless or tank water heating system. Tankless models, like the AquaSense Pro 5000, heat water on demand, providing endless warm water. Tank models, like the PureFlow Elite, store a small amount of heated water and can run out during extended use. If you prioritize unlimited warm water, tankless is the way to go.

### Heated Seat

A heated seat is one of the most appreciated features of a smart bidet seat, especially during colder months. Most models offer adjustable temperature settings, so you can find your perfect level of warmth.

### Warm Air Dryer

A warm air dryer eliminates the need for toilet paper, making your bathroom experience more hygienic and environmentally friendly. The effectiveness of dryers varies by model, so look for one with adjustable temperature and fan speed.

### Deodorizer

Built-in deodorizers use carbon filters or catalytic technology to neutralize odors. This is a particularly useful feature if your bathroom is shared or lacks ventilation.

### Remote Control vs. Side Panel

Remote controls offer convenience and ease of use, especially for users with limited mobility. Side panels are more traditional and do not require batteries, but they can be harder to reach.

### Smart Home Integration

If you are building a smart home, look for a bidet seat that integrates with your existing ecosystem. Voice control via Alexa, Google Assistant, or Apple HomeKit adds a layer of convenience that many users find indispensable.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation

Installing a smart bidet seat is a DIY-friendly project that typically takes 30–60 minutes. Here is a general overview:

1. **Turn off the water supply** to your toilet and flush to empty the tank.
2. **Remove the existing toilet seat** by unscrewing the mounting bolts.
3. **Install the T-adapter** to the water supply line to divert water to the bidet seat.
4. **Mount the bidet seat** onto the toilet bowl and secure it with the provided hardware.
5. **Connect the water hose** from the T-adapter to the bidet seat.
6. **Plug in the power cord** to a nearby GFCI outlet.
7. **Test the system** and adjust settings as needed.

Most models come with detailed instructions and all necessary hardware. If you are not comfortable working with plumbing, consider hiring a professional.

### Smart Home Integration

For models with smart home capabilities, integration is typically straightforward:

- **Alexa:** Enable the skill in the Alexa app and link your bidet account.
- **Google Assistant:** Use the Google Home app to add the device and link your account.
- **Apple HomeKit:** Scan the HomeKit code on the device or in the app to add it to your home.

Once integrated, you can use voice commands to perform actions like "Alexa, turn on the bidet" or "Hey Google, set bidet temperature to 98 degrees." Some models also support routines, allowing you to trigger bidet settings based on time of day or other smart home events.
