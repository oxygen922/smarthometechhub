# The Ultimate Bidet Seat Review: Smart Toilet Upgrades for 2026

## Introduction

In the rapidly evolving world of smart home technology, few upgrades deliver the same blend of luxury, hygiene, and practicality as a high-quality bidet seat. Once considered a niche Japanese import, bidet seats have become a mainstream staple in American bathrooms, offering features ranging from heated seats and warm water washes to air dryers and even self-cleaning nozzles. As we move through 2026, the market is flooded with options that promise to transform your daily routine while saving on toilet paper costs and reducing environmental impact. But with so many models vying for your attention—from budget-friendly basics to Wi-Fi-enabled smart seats—choosing the right one can be overwhelming. This in-depth review will guide you through the top contenders, key features to consider, and everything you need to know to make an informed purchase. Whether you're a tech enthusiast seeking full smart home integration or a practical homeowner looking for comfort, we've got you covered.

## Top 3 Product Recommendations with Detailed Reviews

### 1. Toto Washlet S7A – The Premium Smart Bidet Seat

**Price Estimate (2026):** $899–$1,099

**Overview:**
Toto remains the gold standard in bidet technology, and the Washlet S7A exemplifies why. This top-tier model builds on years of Japanese engineering excellence, offering a sleek, low-profile design that fits most standard elongated toilets. The S7A features Toto's patented "EWATER+" system, which uses electrolyzed water to clean the nozzle and bowl, ensuring maximum hygiene without harsh chemicals. The heated seat is contoured for comfort, and the remote control offers intuitive access to all functions.

**Performance:**
The wash experience is where the S7A truly shines. It provides adjustable water pressure, temperature, and spray position, with a "Pulsating" mode that alternates between concentrated and wide sprays for a gentle yet thorough clean. The "Oscillating" feature moves the nozzle back and forth, covering more area with less effort. The air dryer is surprisingly powerful, drying in under two minutes on the highest setting. One standout feature is the "Premist" function, which sprays the bowl before use to prevent waste from sticking—a subtle but effective touch.

**Smart Home Integration:**
The S7A supports Wi-Fi connectivity via the Toto Washlet app (iOS/Android), allowing you to adjust settings, schedule cleaning cycles, and even monitor water usage. It also integrates with Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant for voice control, letting you start a wash or adjust the seat temperature hands-free. However, the app can be clunky, and setup requires a stable 2.4GHz network.

**Pros:**
- Exceptional build quality and longevity
- Advanced hygiene features (EWATER+, Premist)
- Powerful, quiet operation
- Heated seat with multiple temperature settings

**Cons:**
- Premium price point
- App interface could be more user-friendly
- Requires a GFCI outlet near the toilet

**Verdict:** The Toto Washlet S7A is the ultimate choice for those who demand the best. It's an investment, but one that pays off in daily comfort and reliability.

### 2. Bio Bidet 6800U – The Best Value Smart Bidet Seat

**Price Estimate (2026):** $499–$599

**Overview:**
Bio Bidet has carved a niche as the "smart but affordable" brand, and the 6800U is their flagship model. It offers many of the premium features found in the Toto but at roughly half the price. The design is slightly bulkier, but it fits both round and elongated bowls (with included adapters) and comes in white or biscuit. The 6800U includes a wireless remote with a digital display, making it easy to adjust settings without twisting around.

**Performance:**
The 6800U delivers a robust wash with three spray modes: Front Wash, Rear Wash, and a "Pulse" mode that oscillates for a massaging effect. Water pressure is adjustable across five levels, and the temperature can be set to warm, warmest, or off. The heated seat offers three temperature settings, and the air dryer is effective but slightly slower than the Toto—expect around three minutes for full drying. One unique feature is the "Night Light" that illuminates the bowl, perfect for late-night trips.

**Smart Home Integration:**
This model lacks Wi-Fi or voice control, which may disappoint tech enthusiasts. However, the remote is backlit and intuitive, and the seat has a "Energy Saver" mode that automatically lowers temperatures during periods of inactivity. For smart home integration, you'd need to pair it with a smart plug, but that's a workaround rather than a native feature.

**Pros:**
- Excellent value for the feature set
- Works with both round and elongated toilets
- Heated seat and warm water wash
- Easy DIY installation

**Cons:**
- No Wi-Fi or voice control
- Slightly bulkier design
- Dryer could be more powerful

**Verdict:** The Bio Bidet 6800U is the sweet spot for most households—offering premium comfort without breaking the bank. It's our top recommendation for value seekers.

### 3. Kohler PureWash E930 – The Best Smart Home Integration

**Price Estimate (2026):** $799–$949

**Overview:**
Kohler's PureWash E930 is designed for the connected home. It features a slim, modern profile that complements Kohler's own toilet lines but also fits most standard elongated bowls. The E930 includes a touchscreen remote and supports the Kohler Konnect ecosystem, which allows integration with other Kohler smart products like faucets and showers. The seat is heated, and the wash system offers five spray patterns, including a "Wide" option for a broader coverage.

**Performance:**
The wash experience is refined, with water temperature and pressure adjustable in fine increments. The "Comfort Clean" mode uses a gentle, aerated spray that feels less invasive than some competitors. The air dryer is one of the best in class, drying in under 90 seconds. The seat also features a "Slow-Close" lid and a "Quick Release" mechanism for easy cleaning. However, the nozzle is self-cleaning but lacks the electrolyzed water system found in the Toto.

**Smart Home Integration:**
This is where the E930 excels. It connects via Wi-Fi to the Kohler Konnect app, which offers full control, usage tracking, and even a "Vacation Mode" that shuts down all functions to save energy. It works natively with Amazon Alexa, Google Assistant, and Apple HomeKit, enabling voice commands like "Alexa, start the bidet" or "Hey Siri, warm the seat." The app also supports geofencing, so the seat can preheat when you arrive home.

**Pros:**
- Best-in-class smart home integration
- Excellent air dryer performance
- Sleek, low-profile design
- Touchscreen remote with clear display

**Cons:**
- Premium pricing
- Limited to elongated bowls
- Some users report app connectivity issues

**Verdict:** The Kohler PureWash E930 is the go-to choice for smart home enthusiasts who want seamless integration with their existing ecosystem. It's a close second to the Toto for overall quality.

## Buying Guide: Who Should Buy and Budget Considerations

### Who Should Buy a Bidet Seat?

- **Tech Enthusiasts:** If you love voice control and app integration, the Kohler PureWash E930 is your best bet. It's designed for the smart home ecosystem.
- **Comfort Seekers:** Those who prioritize a luxurious, spa-like experience should invest in the Toto Washlet S7A. It's the most comfortable and feature-rich option.
- **Budget-Conscious Shoppers:** The Bio Bidet 6800U offers 90% of the premium experience at half the price. It's ideal for first-time bidet users.
- **Eco-Conscious Homeowners:** Bidet seats drastically reduce toilet paper usage, saving money and trees. Any model will do, but the Toto's EWATER+ system minimizes chemical use.
- **Seniors or Individuals with Mobility Issues:** Heated seats and warm water washes can be a game-changer for those with arthritis or limited mobility. Look for models with easy-to-use remotes.

### Budget Considerations

- **Entry-Level ($200–$400):** Basic bidet seats with cold water only or limited features. Brands like Brondell and Luxe offer decent options, but you'll miss out on heated seats and warm water.
- **Mid-Range ($400–$700):** The sweet spot. The Bio Bidet 6800U fits here, along with models like the Brondell Swash 1400. You get heated seats, warm water, and air dryers.
- **Premium ($700–$1,200):** Top-tier models from Toto, Kohler, and American Standard. Expect Wi-Fi, voice control, and advanced hygiene features.
- **Luxury ($1,200+):** Integrated smart toilets (not just seats) like the Toto Neorest or Kohler Numi. These replace your entire toilet but offer built-in bidet functions, automatic flushing, and hands-free operation.

**Pro Tip:** Don't forget to factor in installation costs if you're not handy. DIY installation is straightforward for most seats, but you'll need a GFCI outlet. If you don't have one, hiring an electrician can add $150–$300 to your budget.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Overview

Installing a bidet seat is a DIY-friendly project that typically takes 30–60 minutes. Here's the general process:

1. **Turn off the water supply** to your toilet and flush to empty the tank.
2. **Remove the existing toilet seat** and set it aside.
3. **Install the T-adapter** onto the toilet's fill valve, then connect the water supply line to the adapter.
4. **Mount the bidet seat** onto the toilet bowl using the included brackets and bolts.
5. **Connect the water line** from the seat to the T-adapter.
6. **Plug in the power cord** to a GFCI outlet. If you don't have one, you'll need an electrician.
7. **Test for leaks** and adjust the seat position as needed.

**Important:** Most bidet seats require a GFCI outlet within 3–4 feet of the toilet. If your bathroom lacks one, installation becomes a more involved (and expensive) project.

### Smart Home Integration

- **Toto Washlet S7A:** Connects via Wi-Fi to the Toto Washlet app. Works with Alexa and Google Assistant for basic commands like "Start wash" or "Set seat temperature to high." The app also allows scheduling and usage monitoring.
- **Kohler PureWash E930:** The best for smart home integration. Supports Alexa, Google Assistant, and Apple HomeKit. The Kohler Konnect app offers geofencing, so the seat can preheat when you arrive home. You can also create routines, like having the bidet start automatically when you wake up.
- **Bio Bidet 6800U:** No native smart features. However, you can plug it into a smart plug (like a Kasa or Wemo) to control power on/off remotely—though this only turns the seat on or off, not the wash functions.

**Smart Home Tip:** For full automation, pair a motion sensor with a smart plug (for the Bio Bidet) or use a smart home hub to trigger the seat based on time of day or occupancy.
