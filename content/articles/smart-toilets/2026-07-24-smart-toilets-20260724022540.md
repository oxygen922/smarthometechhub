## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

- **Tech Enthusiasts:** If you already have smart lights, thermostats, and locks, a smart toilet is a natural addition. Voice-controlled flushing and seat warming are surprisingly satisfying.
- **Families with Children or Elderly:** Heated seats and bidet functions reduce bathroom discomfort. Automatic lids improve hygiene by minimizing contact.
- **Eco-Conscious Homeowners:** Smart toilets use significantly less water than traditional models. Some even offer leak detection.
- **Anyone with Mobility Issues:** Motion-activated lids and bidet assistance can make bathroom visits easier and safer.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $1,000:** You’ll find good value models like the BioBidet 770 or Tushy Ace. Expect basic smart features and a separate seat attachment rather than a full toilet.
- **$1,000–$3,000:** Mid-range options from Kohler (C3 series) or American Standard offer better build quality and more advanced bidet settings. Smart home integration is often included.
- **$3,000–$6,000+:** Premium units like TOTO Neorest or Kohler Veil deliver luxury comfort, robust app control, and health monitoring. Professional installation is recommended.

**Pro Tip:** Don’t forget to factor in installation costs ($150–$400 for professional setup) and potential electrical work (if your bathroom lacks an outlet near the toilet).

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Considerations

Installing a smart toilet can range from simple to complex. Here’s what to expect:

- **Power Source:** Most smart toilets need a GFCI-protected electrical outlet within 3 feet of the toilet. If you don’t have one, hire an electrician (cost: $150–$300).
- **Water Line:** Some models require a dedicated water line for the bidet. Check if your existing supply line is compatible.
- **Toilet Type:** Full smart toilets (one-piece or two-piece) replace your entire toilet. Smart seats attach to your existing bowl.
- **DIY vs. Pro:** Smart seats are often DIY-friendly (30–60 minutes). Full toilets, especially wall-hung models, are best left to professionals.

### Smart Home Integration

Connecting your smart toilet to your home ecosystem unlocks automation possibilities:

- **Voice Commands:** “Alexa, flush the toilet” or “Hey Google, warm the seat.” Ensure your model supports your preferred assistant.
- **Routines:** Create morning routines that warm the seat and turn on the bidet at a set time. Use IFTTT for advanced triggers (e.g., flush when you leave the room).
- **Health Monitoring:** Some high-end models (TOTO Neorest) offer optional urine analysis or weight tracking. Data can sync to health apps.
- **Security:** Always use strong passwords and keep firmware updated to prevent unauthorized access.

**Compatibility Note:** As of 2026, Matter protocol support is still rare in smart toilets. Stick with Alexa or Google-compatible models for the widest integration.
