# The Ultimate Smart Toilet Review: Elevating Your Bathroom Experience in 2026

## Introduction

The modern bathroom has evolved far beyond mere functionality, and at the forefront of this revolution is the smart toilet. Once considered a luxury reserved for high-end hotels, smart toilets have become increasingly accessible and feature-rich, transforming daily routines into personalized wellness experiences. In 2026, these intelligent fixtures offer everything from heated seats and bidet functions to voice-activated controls and health monitoring. Whether you're a tech enthusiast seeking the latest innovations or a homeowner looking to upgrade comfort and hygiene, choosing the right smart toilet can be daunting. This comprehensive review dives deep into the top models available today, breaking down key features, installation considerations, and smart home integration. By the end, you'll have a clear understanding of which smart toilet best fits your lifestyle, budget, and bathroom design. Let's explore the intersection of technology and sanitation, where convenience meets sustainability.

## Top 3 Product Recommendations with Detailed Reviews

### 1. Toto Neorest NX2 – The Premium All-in-One Powerhouse

**Price (2026 estimate):** $8,500 – $9,200

Toto has long been synonymous with smart toilet innovation, and the Neorest NX2 continues that legacy with unparalleled sophistication. This model features Toto's signature Tornado Flush system, which uses dual-nozzle technology to create a powerful, water-saving vortex that cleans the bowl with minimal water usage. The heated seat is ergonomically contoured and offers multiple temperature settings, while the integrated bidet provides adjustable water pressure, temperature, and spray patterns.

**Key highlights:**
- **EWater+ System:** Electrolyzed water automatically cleans the bowl and wand, reducing the need for harsh chemicals.
- **Premist Technology:** A fine mist coats the bowl before use to prevent waste from sticking.
- **Wireless Remote:** A sleek, wall-mounted remote controls all functions, including a customizable user profile for up to two people.
- **Health Monitoring:** Optional sensor kit tracks urine and stool data, syncing with a smartphone app for wellness insights.

**Performance:** The NX2's flush is exceptionally quiet and efficient, using only 1.0 gallon per flush (GPF) for solid waste and 0.9 GPF for liquid. The bidet experience is luxurious, with a warm air dryer that rivals a gentle breeze. The seat occupancy sensor ensures the toilet only activates when in use, conserving energy.

**Pros:** Unmatched build quality, extensive feature set, quiet operation, excellent water efficiency.
**Cons:** Very high price point, requires professional installation, bulky design may not suit small bathrooms.

**Verdict:** The Toto Neorest NX2 is the gold standard for those who demand the best and have the budget to match. It's an investment in daily comfort and long-term reliability.

### 2. Kohler PureWash E930 – The Smart Seat Upgrade Champion

**Price (2026 estimate):** $1,200 – $1,500 (seat only, compatible with most two-piece toilets)

For homeowners who want smart features without replacing their entire toilet, the Kohler PureWash E930 is an excellent electric bidet seat. It fits most standard two-piece toilets and offers a comprehensive suite of functions at a fraction of the cost of a full smart toilet.

**Key highlights:**
- **Instant Heating:** The water heater provides endless warm water for the bidet, with adjustable temperature and pressure.
- **UV-C Sanitization:** The wand self-cleans with UV light after each use, ensuring hygiene.
- **Heated Seat:** Three temperature settings keep the seat comfortable in any season.
- **Remote Control:** A wireless remote with an intuitive LCD screen displays settings and battery status.
- **Night Light:** A soft, blue LED illuminates the bowl for nighttime use.

**Performance:** Installation is straightforward for DIY enthusiasts, taking about 30 minutes with basic tools. The bidet spray is precise and adjustable, with a feminine wash option for added comfort. The heated seat is responsive and maintains temperature well. The UV sanitization is a standout feature, keeping the wand clean without manual scrubbing.

**Pros:** Affordable entry point, easy installation, universal fit, excellent hygiene features.
**Cons:** Not as integrated as full smart toilets, limited to seat functions, requires an electrical outlet near the toilet.

**Verdict:** The Kohler PureWash E930 is the best value for those seeking a smart upgrade. It delivers core features like a heated seat and bidet without the high cost of a complete replacement.

### 3. Bio Bidet Flow 800 – The Tech-Savvy Mid-Range Marvel

**Price (2026 estimate):** $2,800 – $3,200

Bio Bidet has carved a niche by offering feature-rich smart toilets at competitive prices. The Flow 800 is a one-piece, elongated toilet that combines sleek design with impressive technology. It's ideal for tech enthusiasts who want voice control and smart home integration without breaking the bank.

**Key highlights:**
- **Voice Control:** Compatible with Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant for hands-free operation (e.g., "Alexa, flush the toilet").
- **Smart Home Integration:** Works with IFTTT and SmartThings for automated routines (e.g., seat warming when you enter the bathroom).
- **Dual Flush:** 1.28 GPF for full flush, 0.9 GPF for half flush, reducing water usage.
- **Self-Cleaning Wand:** The wand extends and retracts automatically, with a pre-mist and post-use rinse.
- **User Profiles:** Stores settings for up to four users, including seat temperature, water pressure, and dryer strength.

**Performance:** The Flow 800's flush is powerful and quiet, with a smooth, glazed trapway that resists clogs. The bidet offers oscillating and pulsating modes for enhanced cleaning. The voice control works reliably, though it requires a stable Wi-Fi connection. The seat is comfortable and heated, with a soft-close lid.

**Pros:** Excellent smart home integration, good value for features, multiple user profiles, energy-efficient.
**Cons:** Voice control can be laggy at times, plastic components feel less premium than Toto, customer service can be slow.

**Verdict:** The Bio Bidet Flow 800 is perfect for smart home enthusiasts who want a connected toilet. It offers a balance of features, performance, and price that's hard to beat.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Overview

Installing a smart toilet can be a DIY project for handy homeowners, but professional installation is recommended for full models. Here's what to expect:

1. **Remove Old Toilet:** Disconnect water supply, remove bolts, and lift off the old toilet. Clean the flange and floor.
2. **Install New Toilet:** Place the smart toilet on the flange, secure with bolts, and connect the water supply line.
3. **Electrical Connection:** Plug the toilet into a nearby GFCI outlet. If none exists, hire an electrician.
4. **Set Up Remote:** Mount the wireless remote on the wall (if included) and pair it with the toilet.
5. **Test Functions:** Run a flush cycle, test the bidet, and adjust settings.

**For bidet seats like the Kohler PureWash E930:** Installation is simpler—remove the existing seat, attach the bidet seat, connect the water supply, and plug it in.

### Smart Home Integration

The level of integration varies by model:
- **Toto Neorest NX2:** Limited to its proprietary remote. No direct smart home compatibility, but the remote is intuitive.
- **Kohler PureWash E930:** No smart home features—it's a standalone seat.
- **Bio Bidet Flow 800:** Full integration with Amazon Alexa, Google Assistant, IFTTT, and SmartThings. You can create routines like "Good Morning" that warm the seat and turn on the night light.

**Pro tip:** For seamless integration, ensure your Wi-Fi network is stable near the bathroom. Use a mesh network if the bathroom is far from the router.
