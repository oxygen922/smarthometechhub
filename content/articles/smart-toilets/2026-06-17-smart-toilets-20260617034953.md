## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Bidet Seat?

- **Health-conscious individuals:** Bidets provide superior hygiene compared to toilet paper, reducing the risk of irritation and infections. They're especially beneficial for those with sensitive skin, hemorrhoids, or post-surgical conditions.
- **Eco-friendly consumers:** Using a bidet significantly reduces toilet paper consumption, saving trees and reducing waste. A typical household can cut paper usage by 75% or more.
- **Tech enthusiasts:** If you love integrating smart devices into your home, a bidet seat with wireless controls, user presets, and auto-open features is a natural addition.
- **Seniors or those with mobility issues:** Heated seats, intuitive controls, and automatic cleaning functions make bidet seats easier and safer to use than traditional toilets.
- **Families:** Many models support multiple user presets, allowing each family member to save their preferred settings.

### Budget Considerations

- **Entry-Level ($150–$350):** These models offer basic features like cold water wash (or limited warm water), a non-heated seat, and manual nozzle adjustment. They're functional but lack the comfort and convenience of premium models. Examples include the TOTO Washlet C2 or Brondell FreshSpa.
- **Mid-Range ($400–$700):** This is the sweet spot for most buyers. Models like the Brondell Swash 1400 offer tankless water heating, heated seats, warm air dryers, and multiple spray patterns. You get excellent performance without breaking the bank.
- **Premium ($800–$1,200+):** High-end models like the TOTO Washlet S7A or Bio Bidet Bliss BB-2000 add advanced hygiene features, wireless remotes, user presets, and optional smart home integration. These are best for those who want the absolute best experience.

**Recommendation:** If you're new to bidets, start with a mid-range model like the Brondell Swash 1400. It offers the best balance of features and cost, and you can always upgrade later if desired.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Overview

Installing a bidet seat is generally a DIY-friendly project, but it does require some basic tools and comfort with plumbing. Here's a step-by-step outline:

1. **Turn off the water supply** to your toilet.
2. **Flush the toilet** to empty the bowl and tank.
3. **Disconnect the water supply line** from the toilet tank.
4. **Remove the existing toilet seat** by unscrewing the bolts.
5. **Attach the bidet seat's mounting bracket** to the toilet bowl using the provided hardware.
6. **Connect the bidet's water supply line** to the toilet's water shut-off valve using the included T-adapter.
7. **Plug the bidet seat into a nearby electrical outlet.** If you don't have an outlet near your toilet, you'll need to hire an electrician for installation.
8. **Secure the bidet seat onto the mounting bracket** and connect the water line.
9. **Turn the water supply back on** and check for leaks.
10. **Test all functions** using the remote control.

**Time Required:** 30–60 minutes for most models.
**Skill Level:** Intermediate. If you're uncomfortable with plumbing or electrical work, hire a professional.

### Smart Home Integration

In 2026, smart bidet seats are increasingly compatible with home automation systems. Here's what to look for:

- **Wi-Fi Connectivity:** Some premium models (like the TOTO Washlet S7A with an optional module) can connect to your home network, allowing you to control settings via a smartphone app.
- **Voice Assistant Integration:** Models with Wi-Fi can often be controlled through Amazon Alexa or Google Assistant. You can say, "Alexa, turn on the heated seat" or "Hey Google, start the rear wash."
- **Automation Rules:** Using platforms like SmartThings or HomeKit, you can create routines. For example, the bidet seat can automatically turn on the night light when motion is detected in the bathroom, or pre-heat the seat when you wake up.
- **Energy Monitoring:** Some models provide energy usage data, helping you optimize settings for efficiency.

**Note:** Smart home features are still emerging in this category. The TOTO and Bio Bidet models offer the best integration options, while the Brondell Swash 1400 is more limited.
