## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Overview

Installing a smart bidet seat is generally straightforward, but there are critical considerations:

1. **Electrical Requirements:** Most smart bidet seats require a nearby electrical outlet (GFCI protected). If you don't have one, you'll need an electrician. Some models offer battery-powered options, but they're less common.

2. **Plumbing Connections:** You'll need access to the toilet's water supply line. Most kits include a T-adapter that connects to the existing shut-off valve. Ensure you have a standard 1/2-inch supply line.

3. **Toilet Compatibility:** Measure your toilet bowl shape (round or elongated) and dimensions. Most bidet seats fit standard toilets, but some may not work with skirted or one-piece designs.

4. **Tools Required:** Adjustable wrench, screwdriver, and possibly a drill for mounting brackets. Most units include all necessary hardware.

**Installation Time:** 30–60 minutes for DIY; 1–2 hours for professional installation.

### Smart Home Integration

Modern bidet seats offer varying levels of smart home connectivity:

- **App Control:** Allows you to adjust settings, create user profiles, and schedule cleaning cycles from your smartphone. TOTO and Kohler offer robust apps.
- **Voice Assistant Integration:** Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant support enables hands-free operation. This is especially useful for elderly users or those with mobility issues.
- **Automation:** Some models can integrate with smart home hubs like Samsung SmartThings or Apple HomeKit for advanced automation (e.g., automatically warming the seat when you enter the bathroom).

**Pro Tip:** If smart home integration is important, check for native compatibility with your preferred ecosystem. Some models require a separate hub or bridge.
