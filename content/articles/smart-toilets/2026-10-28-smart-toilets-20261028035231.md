# Smart Toilet Review 2026: Are High-Tech Bidet Toilets Worth the Investment?

**Meta Description:** Looking for the best smart toilet in 2026? Our in-depth review covers top models, key features, pricing, and smart home integration to help you choose the perfect bidet toilet.

## Introduction

The bathroom has finally caught up with the rest of your connected home. Smart toilets—once a luxury reserved for high-end hotels and Japanese hospitality—have exploded into the mainstream US market, and in 2026 they're more capable, more affordable, and more practical than ever. These aren't just toilets with heated seats (though they have those too). We're talking about bidet washing with adjustable temperature and pressure, automatic lid opening, self-cleaning nozzles, built-in deodorizers, night lights, and app-based controls that sync with your smart home ecosystem.

But with dozens of models flooding the market—ranging from $300 bidet seats to $10,000 integrated smart toilets—how do you know which one deserves a spot in your bathroom? We spent weeks researching specs, reading owner feedback, and comparing real-world performance to bring you this comprehensive guide. Whether you're a first-time bidet buyer or upgrading an aging fixture, this review will help you cut through the marketing hype and find the right smart toilet for your home and budget.

## Top 3 Smart Toilet Recommendations for 2026

### 1. TOTO Neorest AS — Best Overall Smart Toilet

**Estimated Price (2026): $5,800–$6,500**

If there's a gold standard in the smart toilet world, it's the TOTO Neorest line—and the AS model is the sweet spot for most US households. TOTO invented the washlet category decades ago, and that engineering heritage shows in every detail.

The Neorest AS combines a fully integrated toilet and bidet system with TOTO's signature Tornado Flush, which uses a powerful centrifugal water jet plus a secondary rim wash to clean the bowl with remarkable efficiency. In practice, this means fewer clogs, less water waste (as low as 1.0 gallons per flush), and a bowl that stays cleaner between scrubbings.

The bidet experience is where the Neorest shines. You get front and rear washing with adjustable pressure, temperature, and nozzle position, plus a warm air dryer that's genuinely usable rather than an afterthought. The premist function sprays the bowl before use to prevent waste from sticking—a small feature that makes a big difference in maintenance. The seat is heated with adjustable temperature, and the lid opens and closes automatically via proximity sensor, which feels futuristic every single time.

Smart features include a wireless remote, user presets for multiple family members, automatic flushing, and a built-in deodorizer with catalytic air filtration. The night light is soft and unobtrusive, ideal for late-night trips. Installation requires a dedicated electrical outlet near the toilet and a standard 12-inch rough-in, so plan accordingly.

**Pros:**
- Exceptional build quality and reliability
- Tornado Flush is powerful and water-efficient
- Full-featured bidet with warm air dryer
- Auto open/close lid and auto flush
- Energy-saving timer modes

**Cons:**
- Premium price
- Professional installation strongly recommended
- No native app control (remote only)

**Verdict:** The Neorest AS is the smart toilet we'd buy with our own money if budget allows. It's the most polished, dependable all-in-one experience on the market.

### 2. Kohler Numi 2.0 — Best Premium Smart Toilet with Alexa

**Estimated Price (2026): $8,500–$9,500**

The Kohler Numi 2.0 is less a toilet and more a bathroom entertainment system that happens to flush. It's the only mainstream smart toilet with built-in Amazon Alexa, meaning you can issue voice commands, play music through its surround sound speakers, and control ambient lighting—all while answering nature's call.

Beyond the novelty, the Numi 2.0 is a seriously capable fixture. The bidet offers adjustable spray shape, position, temperature, and pressure, with a heated seat and warm air dryer. The hands-free opening and closing works via motion detection, and the built-in night light can shift colors. The flush is quiet and efficient, and the skirted design hides the trapway for a sleek, modern look.

The Alexa integration is genuinely useful for hands-free control—"Alexa, open the lid" or "Alexa, start the rear wash" work as expected—but we'd be lying if we said the speakers and light show weren't the main draw for some buyers. If you want the most feature-packed, conversation-starting smart toilet available, this is it.

**Pros:**
- Built-in Alexa with voice control
- Premium surround sound speakers
- Customizable ambient lighting
- Excellent bidet performance
- Striking design

**Cons:**
- Very expensive
- Requires reliable Wi-Fi for full functionality
- Overkill for buyers who just want a bidet

**Verdict:** The Numi 2.0 is the enthusiast's choice—a luxury statement piece with genuinely useful smart features.

### 3. Bio Bidet Aura Smart Toilet — Best Value Smart Toilet

**Estimated Price (2026): $2,200–$2,800**

For buyers who want the integrated smart toilet experience without the five-figure price tag, the Bio Bidet Aura is the standout value pick. Bio Bidet has built a strong reputation in the bidet seat market, and the Aura brings that expertise to a complete toilet package.

You still get the essentials: heated seat, adjustable bidet washing (front and rear), warm air dryer, auto open/close lid, auto flush, night light, and a deodorizer. The remote control is intuitive, and the toilet includes user presets so each family member can save their preferred settings. The tankless design saves space and delivers consistent water pressure.

Where the Aura cuts corners is in fit and finish—the plastics feel a step below TOTO and Kohler, and the dryer is less powerful—but the core functionality is solid. Installation is more DIY-friendly than the premium models, and it works with a standard 12-inch rough-in and nearby outlet.

**Pros:**
- Excellent price-to-feature ratio
- Tankless, space-saving design
- Full bidet, dryer, and auto functions
- Easier installation than premium models

**Cons:**
- Build quality not on par with TOTO/Kohler
- Weaker dryer
- Limited smart home integration

**Verdict:** The Aura is the smart toilet we recommend to most first-time buyers. It delivers 80% of the premium experience at 40% of the price.

### What to prioritize

- **Bidet performance:** Adjustable pressure, temperature, and nozzle position are non-negotiable. A warm air dryer is a nice-to-have but rarely replaces toilet paper entirely.
- **Auto open/close and auto flush:** These are the features that make a smart toilet feel truly "smart" day-to-day.
- **Deodorizer:** A built-in carbon or catalytic filter makes a real difference in small bathrooms.
- **Water efficiency:** Look for 1.0–1.28 gallons per flush (GPF). Most smart toilets are WaterSense-certified.
- **Electrical requirements:** Every integrated smart toilet needs a GFCI-protected outlet within roughly 3 feet. If your bathroom lacks one, budget $150–$400 for an electrician.
- **Smart home integration:** Only a handful of models (like the Numi 2.0) offer app or voice control. For most buyers, a wireless remote is perfectly sufficient.

## Buying Guide: Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

### Who should buy

- **Bidet newcomers:** If you've never used a bidet, a smart toilet is the most comfortable introduction—warm water, heated seat, and easy controls remove all the intimidation.
- **People with mobility limitations:** Auto open/close lids, auto flush, and remote controls make smart toilets genuinely helpful for seniors or anyone with limited reach.
- **Remodelers:** If you're already renovating a bathroom, running a dedicated outlet and installing an integrated unit is far easier than retrofitting later.
- **Eco-conscious households:** Bidet washing dramatically reduces toilet paper use, and efficient flush systems cut water consumption.

### Who should wait

- **Renters:** Installation is semi-permanent and requires electrical work. A bidet seat attachment (starting around $250) is a smarter rental-friendly option.
- **Tight budgets:** A quality bidet seat on your existing toilet delivers 70% of the experience for a fraction of the cost.
- **Bathrooms without outlets:** Unless you're willing to hire an electrician, an integrated smart toilet isn't feasible.

### Budget considerations

Plan for the total cost, not just the sticker price:

- **Entry-level integrated smart toilets:** $1,500–$2,500
- **Mid-range:** $2,500–$4,500
- **Premium:** $5,000–$10,000+
- **Electrical work (if needed):** $150–$400
- **Professional installation:** $250–$600
- **Bidet seat alternative:** $250–$900

A realistic all-in budget for a mid-range smart toilet with installation is roughly $3,000–$5,000. Most manufacturers offer 3-year limited warranties; extended coverage is rarely worth it.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation realities

Integrated smart toilets are not a drop-in replacement for a standard toilet. Expect the following:

1. **Electrical:** A dedicated GFCI outlet within about 3 feet of the toilet is required. If you don't have one, hire a licensed electrician—this is not a DIY job.
2. **Rough-in measurement:** Most models use a standard 12-inch rough-in, but verify before ordering. Incorrect rough-in means a return and re-installation.
3. **Water supply:** A standard supply line works, but some models include a T-valve for the bidet feed—follow the manufacturer's diagram precisely.
4. **Weight:** Integrated units can weigh 80–120 pounds. Two people (or a pro) are recommended for lifting and setting.
5. **Time:** A straightforward swap takes 2–4 hours; new electrical work adds a day.

Professional installation typically runs $250–$600 and is worth it for premium models, both for warranty compliance and to avoid costly mistakes.

### Smart home integration

Here's the honest truth: most smart toilets in 2026 are not deeply integrated into smart home ecosystems. The Kohler Numi 2.0 is the clear leader with built-in Alexa, allowing voice commands and even routines (e.g., dimming lights and playing music). TOTO and Bio Bidet rely on dedicated remotes rather than apps.

If smart home control matters to you, prioritize the Numi 2.0—or pair a remote-controlled toilet with a smart bathroom setup (motion-sensor lights, smart exhaust fans, and a smart speaker for voice control of the room). For most households, the remote is all you'll ever need.
