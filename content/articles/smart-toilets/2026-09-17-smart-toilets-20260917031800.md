## Top 3 Bidet Seat Recommendations for 2026

### 1. TOTO Washlet S7A – Best Overall

**Price Estimate: $1,450–$1,600**

TOTO has long been the gold standard in the bidet world, and the Washlet S7A continues that legacy with a refined design and a feature set that borders on luxurious. If you want the best bidet seat money can buy, this is it.

The S7A features TOTO's signature eWater+ system, which electrolyzes tap water to create a gentle, bacteria-inhibiting mist that keeps the wand and bowl cleaner between uses. The heated seat offers three temperature settings, and the warm air dryer is genuinely effective—not the weak puff of air you get from cheaper models.

What truly sets the S7A apart is its "Activated Cleansing" modes. You get front and rear washes, a oscillating option for broader coverage, and a pulsating mode that alternates water pressure for a massage-like experience. Water pressure and temperature are both adjustable across five levels, and the wand position can be fine-tuned with a remote that's intuitive enough for guests to figure out without a manual.

Installation is straightforward if you have a nearby GFCI outlet, though the S7A requires a dedicated power source. The seat itself is elongated and fits most standard American toilets, but TOTO recommends checking your bowl's dimensions before ordering.

**Pros:**
- Industry-leading build quality
- eWater+ self-cleaning system
- Exceptional dryer performance
- Intuitive remote control

**Cons:**
- Premium price tag
- Requires electrical outlet near toilet
- Remote mounting hardware sold separately

**Verdict:** The TOTO Washlet S7A is the bidet seat to beat. If your budget allows, this is the one to buy.

### 2. Kohler Numera – Best Smart Home Integration

**Price Estimate: $1,100–$1,250**

Kohler's Numera bidet seat is a strong contender for anyone building a connected home ecosystem. It works seamlessly with the Kohler Konnect app, allowing you to create personalized presets for different users, schedule warm-up times, and even monitor usage statistics.

The Numera offers a full suite of cleansing features: front and rear wash, adjustable water temperature and pressure, and a heated seat with an auto mode that activates when you sit down. Where the Numera shines is its app integration—you can adjust settings from your phone, and the seat remembers your preferences the next time you sit down.

The design is sleek and modern, with a low-profile remote that mounts easily on the wall. Kohler also includes a nightlight that can be customized to different colors, which is a nice touch for late-night bathroom trips.

Installation requires a GFCI outlet and a standard water supply line, both of which are common in most modern homes. The Numera fits most elongated toilets, and Kohler provides a template to ensure compatibility.

**Pros:**
- Excellent app integration
- Personalized user presets
- Attractive, low-profile design
- Customizable nightlight

**Cons:**

- App can be finicky with older Wi-Fi routers

**Verdict:** The Kohler Numera is ideal for smart home enthusiasts who want their bidet seat to be part of a larger connected ecosystem.

### Water Heating: Tankless vs. Tank

Tankless water heating is the gold standard. It provides unlimited warm water on demand, whereas tank-style heaters can run out after a few minutes. All three of our top picks use tankless systems, which is why they're worth the investment.

### Heated Seat

A heated seat is one of those features you never knew you needed until you try it. Most models offer adjustable temperature settings, and some—like the Kohler Numera—have auto modes that activate when you sit down.

### Warm Air Dryer

Dryers vary significantly in performance. TOTO's is the most powerful, while budget models often provide a gentle breeze that requires a follow-up with toilet paper. If a dryer is important to you, prioritize a model with multiple heat and fan settings.

### Remote Control vs. Side Panel

Remote controls are more convenient, especially for users with limited mobility. Side panels are simpler but require you to reach behind you. All three of our top picks include wireless remotes.

### Smart Home Integration

If you're building a smart home, look for bidet seats that work with apps like Kohler Konnect or platforms like Alexa and Google Home. Currently, the Kohler Numera is the leader in this category.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Bidet Seat?

Bidet seats are for anyone who wants a cleaner, more comfortable bathroom experience. They're especially beneficial for:

- **People with limited mobility** who find reaching for toilet paper difficult
- **Individuals with sensitive skin** who want to avoid irritation from dry paper
- **Eco-conscious consumers** looking to reduce toilet paper waste
- **Smart home enthusiasts** who want to add another connected device to their ecosystem
- **Anyone who appreciates a little luxury** in their daily routine

### Budget Considerations

Bidet seats range from around $200 for basic models to over $1,500 for premium options. Here's how to think about your budget:

- **Under $400:** Basic features, often tank-style heating, limited adjustability
- **$400–$700:** Tankless heating, heated seat, dryer, remote control (best value)
- **$700–$1,200:** Premium features, better build quality, app integration
- **$1,200+:** Top-tier performance, advanced self-cleaning, superior dryers

For most buyers, the $500–$700 range offers the best balance of features and price. The Bio Bidet BB-2000 is a standout in this category.

### Compatibility Checklist

Before you buy, make sure:

1. **Your toilet shape:** Most bidet seats fit elongated bowls, but round models exist. Measure your bowl before ordering.
2. **Power source:** You'll need a GFCI outlet within reach of the toilet. If you don't have one, factor in the cost of hiring an electrician.
3. **Water supply:** Most bidet seats connect to your existing toilet water line. Check that your shut-off valve is in good condition.
4. **Space:** Some seats add a few inches to the front of the toilet. Ensure there's enough clearance.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### DIY Installation

Installing a bidet seat is a moderate DIY project. Here's what's involved:

1. **Turn off the water supply** and flush the toilet to empty the tank.
2. **Remove the existing seat** by unscrewing the mounting bolts.
3. **Install the T-adapter** to the water supply line. This diverts water to the bidet seat.
4. **Mount the bidet seat** and secure it with the provided hardware.
5. **Connect the water hose** from the T-adapter to the seat.
6. **Plug in the power cord** to a GFCI outlet.
7. **Test the system** and adjust settings as needed.

Most installations take 30–60 minutes. If you're not comfortable working with plumbing, a handyman can handle it for $100–$200.

### Smart Home Integration

The Kohler Numera is the clear winner for smart home integration. It works with the Kohler Konnect app, which allows you to:

- Create personalized presets for multiple users
- Schedule warm-up times
- Monitor usage and filter status
- Control the nightlight color

The Numera also works with Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant, so you can use voice commands to adjust settings. For example, you can say, "Alexa, tell Kohler to start the rear wash."

TOTO and Bio Bidet don't offer app integration, but their remotes are robust enough that you likely won't miss it unless you're deep into smart home automation.

### Maintenance Tips

- **Clean the nozzle regularly** using the self-cleaning function and a soft brush.
- **Replace the deodorizer filter** every 6–12 months, depending on use.
- **Descale the water heater** annually if you live in a hard water area.
- **Check the water supply line** for leaks every few months.
