**Cons:** Premium price, requires a nearby electrical outlet, app setup can be finicky.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Bidet Seat?

- **Families:** Multiple user presets and a heated seat make bidet seats ideal for busy households. The Bio Bidet BB-2000 offers excellent value for families on a budget.
- **Seniors or Individuals with Mobility Issues:** The Toto S7A’s easy-to-use remote and warm water features can greatly improve comfort and independence.
- **Eco-Conscious Consumers:** Reducing toilet paper usage is a major environmental benefit. All three models offer significant paper savings.
- **Smart Home Enthusiasts:** The Kohler Novita BH-90 is the clear winner for those who want voice control and app integration.

### Budget Considerations

- **Entry-Level ($200–$400):** Basic models with heated seats and front/rear wash. These lack air dryers and smart features. Example: Brondell Swash 1400.
- **Mid-Range ($400–$700):** The sweet spot. The Bio Bidet BB-2000 and Kohler Novita BH-90 fall here, offering excellent features without the luxury price.
- **Premium ($700+):** The Toto S7A represents the pinnacle. You pay for superior build quality, advanced self-cleaning, and best-in-class comfort.

### Installation Tips

- Most bidet seats are DIY-friendly and can be installed in 30–60 minutes.
- Ensure your toilet is a standard elongated or round shape. Some models are shape-specific.
- You will need a GFCI outlet within 3–4 feet of the toilet. If you don’t have one, hire an electrician (cost: $150–$300).
- Use a T-connector (usually included) to split the water supply line.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Process

Installing a smart bidet seat is simpler than you might think. Here’s a step-by-step overview:

1. **Turn off water supply** and flush the toilet to empty the tank.
2. **Remove the existing toilet seat** by unscrewing the bolts.
3. **Install the bidet seat** using the included mounting plate and bolts. Most seats use a quick-release mechanism for easy removal.
4. **Connect the water line** using the T-connector. Attach one end to the toilet tank, the other to the bidet seat.
5. **Plug in the power cord** to a GFCI outlet.
6. **Test all functions** before finalizing the seat position.

### Smart Home Integration

- **Toto S7A:** Works with Alexa and Google Assistant. You can say, “Alexa, warm up the toilet seat.” The Toto app allows scheduling and remote control.
- **Bio Bidet BB-2000:** No native smart features, but you can use a smart plug (e.g., TP-Link Kasa) to control power and schedule heating times.
- **Kohler Novita BH-90:** The best integration. Supports Alexa, Google Assistant, and Apple HomeKit. You can create scenes like “Good Morning” that pre-heat the seat and turn on the deodorizer.

**Pro Tip:** For advanced automation, consider a smart water leak sensor near the bidet. If a leak is detected, the sensor can shut off the water supply via a smart valve, preventing damage.
