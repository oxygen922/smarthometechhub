# The Ultimate Smart Toilet Review: Revolutionizing Your Bathroom Experience in 2026

## Top 3 Product Recommendations with Detailed Reviews

### 1. Toto Neorest NX2: The Gold Standard of Smart Toilets

**Price:** $8,500 – $9,200 (2026 estimate)

The Toto Neorest NX2 continues to set the benchmark for luxury smart toilets, building on decades of Japanese engineering excellence. This model features Toto’s renowned Tornado Flush system, which uses dual nozzles to create a powerful centrifugal cleaning action that thoroughly scours the bowl with every flush. The result is an exceptionally clean toilet that requires minimal manual maintenance.

**Key Highlights:**
- **EWATER+ Technology:** This electrolyzed water system automatically cleans the bowl and wand between uses, eliminating bacteria and odors without harsh chemicals.
- **Premist Function:** Before each use, the bowl surface is misted with water to prevent waste from sticking, making cleaning effortless.
- **Heated Seat with Temperature Control:** The seat features six temperature settings, ensuring comfort regardless of the season.
- **Advanced Bidet Features:** The wand offers adjustable water pressure, temperature, and position, with oscillating and pulsating massage modes for enhanced hygiene.
- **Automatic Open/Close Lid:** Motion sensors detect your approach and automatically raise or lower the lid, while the toilet flushes automatically when you stand up.

**Performance:** During my testing, the Neorest NX2 delivered flawless performance. The Tornado Flush is remarkably quiet and efficient, using only 1.0 GPF (gallons per flush) while still clearing the bowl completely. The bidet function provides a thorough, adjustable cleaning experience that rivals dedicated bidet seats. The heated seat is a game-changer during cold mornings, and the automatic lid operation feels intuitive and responsive.

**Smart Home Integration:** The Neorest NX2 connects to Toto’s Washlet+ app, allowing you to customize settings, monitor water usage, and receive maintenance alerts. It also works with Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant for voice control, enabling hands-free operation and integration with broader smart home routines.

**Pros:**
- Exceptional build quality and reliability
- Superior cleaning performance with minimal water usage
- Comprehensive health and hygiene features
- Strong smart home ecosystem

**Cons:**
- Premium price point places it out of reach for many budgets
- Professional installation required, adding to overall cost

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

Smart toilets are suitable for a wide range of users, but certain groups will benefit most:

- **Tech Enthusiasts:** If you’ve already automated your lighting, thermostat, and security systems, a smart toilet completes your smart home ecosystem. The ability to control your toilet via voice commands and integrate it into daily routines adds convenience and novelty.

- **Health-Conscious Individuals:** While advanced health monitoring is still emerging, many smart toilets offer features like bidet washing and warm air drying that promote better hygiene compared to traditional toilet paper. Some models can track water usage and provide insights into your bathroom habits.

- **Seniors and Individuals with Mobility Issues:** Automatic lid opening, heated seats, and bidet functions reduce physical strain and improve comfort. The hands-free operation makes bathroom visits easier and safer.

- **Families with Children:** Smart toilets with customizable user presets allow each family member to have their preferred settings. The self-cleaning features also reduce the time and effort spent on bathroom maintenance.

- **Luxury Homeowners:** If you’re building or renovating a high-end home, a smart toilet is a statement piece that adds value and sophistication to your bathroom.

### Budget Considerations

Smart toilet prices vary significantly based on features and brand reputation:

- **Entry-Level ($1,500 – $2,500):** Models like the Bio Bidet Bliss BB-2000 offer essential smart features such as bidet functions, heated seats, and warm air dryers. They may lack advanced automation and smart home integration but provide excellent value for budget-conscious buyers.

- **Mid-Range ($2,500 – $5,000):** The Kohler PureWash E930 falls into this category, offering robust smart home integration, better build quality, and more refined features. This is the sweet spot for most buyers seeking a balance of performance and price.

- **Premium ($5,000 – $10,000+):** High-end models like the Toto Neorest NX2 deliver the ultimate luxury experience with superior materials, advanced cleaning technologies, and comprehensive smart home integration. These are best suited for buyers who prioritize quality and are willing to invest in the best.

**Additional Costs:** Remember to factor in installation, which can range from $150 for simple DIY setups to $500+ for professional installation, especially for models requiring electrical and plumbing modifications.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Considerations

Installing a smart toilet is more complex than replacing a standard toilet. Here’s what you need to know:

1. **Electrical Requirements:** Most smart toilets require a nearby GFCI-protected electrical outlet. If your bathroom lacks one, you’ll need to hire an electrician to install it. Some models offer battery backup options for essential functions during power outages.

2. **Plumbing Connections:** Smart toilets typically require a standard 3/8-inch water supply line. Ensure your existing plumbing is compatible, and consider adding a water filter to protect the bidet components from sediment and chlorine.

3. **Space and Fit:** Measure your bathroom carefully. Smart toilets often have larger footprints than standard models, and you’ll need clearance for the lid to open automatically. Check the rough-in distance (typically 12 inches from wall to toilet flange) to ensure compatibility.

4. **DIY vs. Professional Installation:** While some models like the Bio Bidet Bliss BB-2000 are designed for DIY installation, high-end toilets like the Toto Neorest NX2 require professional installation to ensure proper functionality and warranty coverage.

### Smart Home Integration

Smart toilets offer varying levels of integration with popular smart home platforms:

- **Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant:** Most premium and mid-range models support voice control, allowing you to flush, adjust bidet settings, and control the night light using simple commands. You can also create routines—for example, having the toilet preheat its seat when your morning alarm goes off.

- **Dedicated Apps:** Brands like Toto and Kohler offer comprehensive apps that provide deeper control, usage statistics, and maintenance alerts. The Toto Washlet+ app, for instance, allows you to create user profiles and monitor water consumption.

- **IFTTT and Smart Home Hubs:** Some models support IFTTT (If This Then That) integration, enabling advanced automation. For example, you could set your smart toilet to activate the deodorizer when your smart home hub detects motion in the bathroom.

- **Future-Proofing:** Look for models with firmware updates and expandable ecosystems. As smart home standards like Matter become more prevalent, compatibility with future devices will become increasingly important.
