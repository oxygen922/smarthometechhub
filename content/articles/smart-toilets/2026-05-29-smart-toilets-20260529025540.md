# The Ultimate Smart Toilet Review: Elevating Your Bathroom Experience in 2026

## Introduction

The smart toilet has evolved from a quirky luxury into a cornerstone of the modern smart home. In 2026, these high-tech thrones offer far more than heated seats and automated flushes—they integrate seamlessly with your home automation system, provide health monitoring features, and even reduce water consumption. As a smart home technology reviewer who has tested dozens of models across various price points, I can confidently say that the right smart toilet transforms your daily routine from mundane to magnificent. Whether you're a tech enthusiast looking to upgrade your master bath or a homeowner seeking water efficiency and comfort, this comprehensive guide will help you navigate the latest innovations. From bidet functions to voice control, we'll explore the top contenders and help you find the perfect match for your home. Let's dive into the world of smart toilets and discover which model deserves a place in your bathroom.

## Top 3 Product Recommendations

### 1. Kohler Numi 2.0 Intelligent Toilet

The Kohler Numi 2.0 remains the gold standard for luxury smart toilets in 2026. Priced at approximately $7,500, this model is not for the faint of budget, but it delivers an unparalleled experience. The Numi 2.0 features a sleek, one-piece design with a heated seat, customizable bidet settings (including oscillating and pulsating sprays), and a warm air dryer. The real standout is its integration with Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant—you can ask your toilet to preheat the seat or adjust water temperature before you even enter the room. The built-in speaker plays your favorite tunes, and the ambient nightlight creates a spa-like atmosphere. The self-cleaning function uses UV light to sanitize the bowl after each use, while the touchless flush keeps things hygienic. Installation requires a dedicated electrical outlet and water line, but the result is a bathroom centerpiece that impresses guests every time.

**Pros:** Exceptional smart home integration, premium build quality, advanced hygiene features  
**Cons:** Very expensive, requires professional installation, large footprint

### 2. TOTO Neorest NX2

TOTO's Neorest line has long been synonymous with innovation, and the NX2 model, priced around $5,200, continues that tradition. This toilet features TOTO's signature Tornado Flush system, which uses dual nozzles to create a powerful, water-efficient flush that cleans the bowl without streaks. The heated seat, warm water bidet, and air dryer are standard, but the NX2 adds a new "eWater+" system that uses electrolyzed water to sanitize the toilet surface between uses. The integrated motion sensor automatically opens and closes the lid, and the remote control offers intuitive adjustments for water pressure, temperature, and nozzle position. For smart home enthusiasts, the NX2 works with the TOTO app for remote control and usage tracking. The sleek, elongated design fits most standard bathrooms, and the installation is straightforward for a professional plumber.

**Pros:** Best-in-class flush performance, excellent hygiene technology, reasonable price for features  
**Cons:** Limited third-party smart home integration, remote can be confusing for new users

### 3. Bio Bidet Ultimate 770

For those seeking a premium experience without breaking the bank, the Bio Bidet Ultimate 770, priced at $1,800, offers exceptional value. This smart toilet seat (which can be added to most standard toilets) includes heated seat, warm water bidet with adjustable pressure, and an energy-saving mode. The wireless remote control is intuitive, and the self-cleaning nozzle retracts after each use. What sets the Ultimate 770 apart is its compatibility with smart home systems—it works with both Alexa and Google Assistant for voice commands, and the Bio Bidet app allows for scheduling and usage monitoring. The installation is DIY-friendly, requiring only a standard electrical outlet and a T-adapter for the water supply. While it lacks the integrated design of the Kohler and TOTO models, the Ultimate 770 delivers 90% of the functionality at a fraction of the cost.

**Pros:** Affordable, easy DIY installation, strong smart home compatibility  
**Cons:** Not an integrated toilet, less premium aesthetic, limited health monitoring features

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

- **Luxury Homeowners:** If you're building or renovating a high-end home and want the ultimate bathroom experience, the Kohler Numi 2.0 is your best bet. It's a statement piece that integrates fully with your smart home ecosystem.
- **Tech Enthusiasts:** For those who love gadgets and want a smart toilet that offers the best balance of features and value, the TOTO Neorest NX2 is ideal. Its hygiene-focused technology and reliable performance make it a favorite among early adopters.
- **Budget-Conscious Upgraders:** The Bio Bidet Ultimate 770 is perfect for homeowners who want smart toilet features without replacing their existing toilet. It's also great for renters who can take it with them when they move.
- **Eco-Conscious Users:** If water conservation is a priority, look for models with dual-flush options or low GPF (gallons per flush) ratings. The TOTO Neorest NX2's 1.0 GPF is exceptional.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $2,000:** Smart toilet seats like the Bio Bidet Ultimate 770 offer the best value. You get heated seats, bidet functions, and smart home integration without a major investment.
- **$2,000 - $5,000:** This mid-range bracket includes integrated toilets from brands like American Standard and Duravit, as well as the TOTO Neorest NX2. You'll get better design, quieter operation, and more advanced hygiene features.
- **Over $5,000:** The luxury tier includes the Kohler Numi 2.0 and high-end models from Japanese manufacturers like Lixil. These offer the best materials, most comprehensive smart home integration, and often include health monitoring features.

**Pro Tip:** Don't forget to factor in installation costs. Professional installation for an integrated toilet typically runs $300-$600, while a smart seat can be installed yourself in under an hour.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Considerations

**Integrated Smart Toilets (Kohler Numi 2.0, TOTO Neorest NX2):**
- Requires a dedicated 120V electrical outlet within 3 feet of the toilet (GFCI protected recommended)
- Needs a standard 1/2-inch water supply line with shut-off valve
- Professional plumber recommended for proper leveling and sealing
- Floor must be level and able to support the weight (typically 80-100 lbs)
- Some models require a specific rough-in distance (usually 12 inches from wall to waste pipe center)

**Smart Toilet Seats (Bio Bidet Ultimate 770):**
- Compatible with most standard two-piece toilets (elongated or round, depending on model)
- Requires a standard electrical outlet nearby (extension cords are not recommended)
- Includes T-adapter for water supply connection
- DIY installation typically takes 30-60 minutes with basic tools (wrench, screwdriver)
- Not suitable for toilets with unusual shapes or integrated bidet seats

### Smart Home Integration

**Voice Assistants:** The Kohler Numi 2.0 and Bio Bidet Ultimate 770 both support Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant. You can create routines like "Good Morning" that preheats the seat and turns on the nightlight. The TOTO Neorest NX2 is limited to its own app, which offers scheduling and usage tracking but no voice control.

**Automation Platforms:** For advanced users, the Kohler Numi 2.0 works with IFTTT (If This Then That), allowing triggers like "if motion sensor detects entry, turn on bathroom lights and preheat toilet seat." The Bio Bidet app offers basic scheduling, but doesn't integrate with platforms like HomeKit or SmartThings.

**Future-Proofing:** Look for models that support over-the-air firmware updates, as new features and integrations are added regularly. The Kohler Numi 2.0 has received updates adding new bidet patterns and improved voice command support since its launch.
