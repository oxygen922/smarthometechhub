# The Ultimate Smart Toilet Guide: 2026's Best Intelligent Thrones Reviewed

## Introduction

The bathroom is undergoing a silent revolution, and at the epicenter is the smart toilet. No longer a novelty reserved for luxury hotels or tech billionaires, the intelligent toilet has evolved into a sophisticated wellness device that combines hygiene, comfort, and connectivity. As we move through 2026, these fixtures offer heated seats, automated bidets, warm-air dryers, and even health monitoring that can track biomarkers and sync data to your smartphone.

But with prices ranging from a modest $500 to a jaw-dropping $10,000, navigating this market can feel overwhelming. This comprehensive guide cuts through the marketing noise to bring you the top three smart toilets of 2026, a detailed breakdown of essential features, and practical buying advice. Whether you're a tech enthusiast looking for the latest in health tracking or a homeowner seeking the ultimate in bathroom luxury, this review will help you make an informed decision for your smart home ecosystem.

## Top 3 Product Recommendations

### 1. TOTO Neorest NX2: The Reigning Champion of Comfort

**Price Estimate: $8,500 - $9,800**

When it comes to premium smart toilets, TOTO remains the undisputed pioneer, and the Neorest NX2 is their flagship masterpiece. This isn't just a toilet; it's a full-body experience designed around the Japanese philosophy of *omotenashi* (selfless hospitality).

**Design & Build Quality:** The NX2 features a sleek, one-piece design with a smooth, glazed ceramic surface that is incredibly easy to clean. The lid opens and closes automatically as you approach, and the seat is heated to your preferred temperature. The build quality is exceptional, with a robust mechanism that feels like it will last decades.

**Performance & Features:** The star of the show is TOTO's ACTIVEWASH technology, which offers a variety of spray modes, including a gentle "soft" wash for sensitive skin and a powerful "oscillating" mode for thorough cleaning. The warm-air dryer is surprisingly effective, and the pre-mist function wets the bowl before use to prevent waste from sticking.

What truly sets the NX2 apart, however, is its **EWATER+ technology**. This electrolyzed water system uses tap water and salt to create a cleaning solution that eliminates 99.9% of bacteria on the bowl, effectively self-cleaning the toilet. The integrated air purifier also helps keep the bathroom smelling fresh.

**Smart Features:** The NX2 connects to the TOTO app, allowing you to customize wash settings, seat temperature, and even track your usage patterns. While it lacks the advanced biometric health monitoring of some competitors, its reliability and comfort features are second to none.

**Verdict:** The NX2 is the gold standard for those who want a flawless, luxurious experience with the best self-cleaning technology on the market.

**Price Estimate: $9,000 - $11,500**

Its angular, modern design is a departure from traditional curves, and it comes with customizable LED lighting that can cycle through 16 million colors to set the mood. The seat is heated, and the unit features integrated speakers that allow you to play music or listen to the news via Bluetooth.

**Performance & Features:** Kohler's bidet system is robust, offering a range of spray patterns and temperatures. The "Charged" and "Precision" sprays provide a targeted clean, while the "Comfort" spray is softer. The dryer is high-performance, and the unit includes a hands-free flushing system activated by a touchless sensor.

**Smart Features:** This is where the Numi 2.0 shines. It supports **Alexa and Google Assistant**, allowing you to control the toilet with voice commands. You can say, "Alexa, flush the toilet," or "Hey Google, turn on the bidet." It also integrates with the Kohler Konnect app, which offers a digital dashboard for all settings. The health monitoring feature tracks your weight and can even analyze urine samples for key biomarkers, sending reports to your phone.

**Verdict:** The Numi 2.0 is the most feature-rich smart toilet available, perfect for those who want full smart home integration, voice control, and a futuristic aesthetic.

### In-Depth Look at Key Features

- **Bidet Functionality:** The core of any smart toilet. Look for adjustable water pressure, temperature, and spray patterns. Oscillating and pulsating modes offer a more thorough clean.
- **Heated Seat:** A non-negotiable luxury. Ensure the temperature is adjustable and ideally has a "wake-up" mode that warms the seat before you wake.
- **Drying:** A good dryer should have multiple heat settings and be powerful enough to dry you effectively without requiring toilet paper.
- **Self-Cleaning:** This is a major convenience factor. Look for features like electrolyzed water (TOTO), UV light (Kohler), or automatic nozzle cleaning (Bio Bidet).
- **Odor Control:** An internal carbon filter or air purifier is essential for keeping the bathroom fresh.
- **Smart Connectivity:** Do you want app control, voice commands, or both? Consider which ecosystem (Alexa vs. Google) you already use at home.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

- **The Tech Enthusiast:** If you love having the latest gadgets and integrating everything into your smart home, a model like the Kohler Numi 2.0 is a must-have.
- **The Luxury Seeker:** If you value comfort and a premium experience above all else, the TOTO Neorest NX2 offers unrivaled build quality and comfort.
- **The Practical Homeowner:** For those who want the benefits of a smart toilet—hygiene, comfort, and convenience—without the astronomical price, the Bio Bidet Ultimate 7500 is the perfect entry point.
- **Individuals with Mobility Issues:** The hands-free operation, heated seat, and bidet functions are incredibly helpful for elderly individuals or those with limited mobility.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $1,000:** You're in the range of smart toilet seats (like a TOTO Washlet) rather than full smart toilets. These attach to your existing toilet and offer many of the same features.
- **$2,000 - $4,000:** This is the "sweet spot" for full smart toilets. Models like the Bio Bidet Ultimate 7500 offer excellent features and performance.
- **$5,000 - $8,000:** This range gets you into premium territory with better design, more advanced cleaning systems, and improved smart features.
- **$8,000+:** This is the flagship tier, where you get the absolute best in technology, design, and health monitoring from brands like TOTO and Kohler.

**Pro Tip:** Always factor in installation costs, which can range from $300 to $1,000 depending on your plumbing setup.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Considerations

Installing a smart toilet is more complex than a standard toilet. It requires:

1.  **Electrical Outlet:** Most smart toilets need a standard 120V outlet near the toilet. If you don't have one, an electrician will need to install it.
2.  **Water Supply:** A standard water supply line is usually sufficient, but some models may require a dedicated line.
3.  **Plumbing:** The toilet needs to be connected to the water supply and the drain. This is often best left to a professional plumber.

**Installation Time:** Expect a professional installation to take 2-4 hours. Always check the manufacturer's requirements before purchasing.

### Smart Home Integration

The true magic of a smart toilet is its ability to integrate into your daily routine.

- **Voice Control:** Models like the Kohler Numi 2.0 allow you to use Alexa or Google Assistant to control the toilet. You can create routines like, "Good morning," which triggers the seat warmer and turns on the night light.
- **App Control:** Use the manufacturer's app to fine-tune settings, track usage, and receive health reports. You can also update the firmware to get new features.
- **Automation:** Some systems can be integrated with other smart home devices. For example, you can set a scene where the bathroom lights dim and your favorite playlist starts playing when you sit down.
