# Smart Toilet Review 2026: Are They Worth the Splash?

## Introduction

Smart toilets have quietly become one of the most talked-about upgrades in modern American bathrooms. Once a luxury reserved for high-end hotels in Japan and Europe, smart toilets are now available at nearly every price point, from budget-friendly bidet seats to fully integrated intelligent fixtures. These high-tech thrones combine hands-free flushing, heated seats, warm-water cleansing, air dryers, and even health-monitoring sensors that analyze your waste for early signs of illness. According to industry analysts, the U.S. smart toilet market is projected to grow by double digits annually through 2028, fueled by aging-in-place trends, hygiene awareness, and the broader smart home boom. But with prices ranging from $200 to over $10,000, choosing the right model can feel overwhelming. In this in-depth smart toilet review, we break down the best options of 2026, explain the features that actually matter, and help you decide whether it's finally time to upgrade your bathroom.

---

## Top 3 Smart Toilet Recommendations for 2026

### 1. TOTO Neorest RS — Best Overall Smart Toilet

**Estimated Price: $7,200–$8,500 (2026)**

TOTO remains the gold standard in the smart toilet category, and the Neorest RS is its most refined flagship yet. This fully integrated unit pairs TOTO's legendary Actilight self-cleaning technology with a redesigned remote that feels more like a smartphone than a bathroom accessory.

**Performance and Cleansing**
The Neorest RS uses TOTO's eWater+ system, which electrolyzes tap water to create a mildly acidic solution that keeps the bowl cleaner between flushes. The warm-water bidet offers front and rear cleansing, oscillating and pulsating spray modes, and an adjustable water temperature range that goes from 86°F to 104°F. The heated seat warms up in under 30 seconds, and the built-in air dryer has three adjustable temperature settings.

**Design and Build**
The skirted, one-piece design hides the trapway for a sleek, easy-to-clean silhouette. The tankless configuration requires a dedicated electrical outlet and a water supply line, but TOTO's installation network makes professional setup straightforward in most U.S. metro areas.

**Smart Features**
The RS adds auto open/close lid, auto flush, nightlight, and a wireless remote with user presets. There's no app integration, which some buyers may find limiting, but TOTO argues the remote is faster and more hygienic than pulling out a phone.

**Pros:**
- Exceptional build quality and reliability
- Superior self-cleaning system
- Five-year limited warranty
- Quiet operation (under 55 dB)

**Cons:**
- Premium price
- No smartphone app
- Requires professional installation

**Verdict:** If budget is not a concern and you want the most polished, reliable smart toilet on the market, the Neorest RS is the one to beat.

### 2. Kohler Numera — Best Smart Toilet for Health Monitoring

**Estimated Price: $5,800–$6,900 (2026)**

Kohler's Numera takes the smart toilet concept in a more futuristic direction by integrating health-sensing technology into the seat. Using a combination of biometric sensors, the Numera can track hydration levels, electrolyte balance, and even flag potential urinary tract issues through a companion app.

**Cleansing and Comfort**
The bidet functionality is excellent, with adjustable spray position, temperature, and pressure. Kohler's stainless steel wand self-cleans before and after each use. The heated seat and warm air dryer are comparable to TOTO's, though slightly less refined in terms of drying speed.

**Health Monitoring**
This is where the Numera shines. The seat's sensors analyze urine flow and composition, then sync results to the Kohler Health app. For users managing chronic conditions like diabetes or kidney disease, this data can be genuinely useful between doctor visits. Note that the Numera is not a medical device, and Kohler is careful to frame its readings as wellness insights rather than diagnoses.

**Smart Home Integration**
The Numera supports Wi-Fi, works with Amazon Alexa and Google Home for voice commands (flush, open lid), and integrates with Kohler's Konnect platform. Firmware updates arrive over the air.

**Pros:**
- Unique health-tracking features
- Strong smart home integration
- Attractive, modern design
- Excellent customer support

**Cons:**
- Health data requires subscription after first year
- Sensors need periodic calibration
- Higher maintenance than simpler models

**Verdict:** The Numera is ideal for tech-forward households and anyone interested in passive health tracking. It's the most "smart home" of the three.

### 3. Bio Bidet Discovery DLS — Best Value Smart Toilet

**Estimated Price: $1,600–$2,100 (2026)**

If you want most of the flagship experience at a fraction of the price, the Bio Bidet Discovery DLS is the value champion. This one-piece integrated smart toilet delivers heated seat, warm-water bidet, air dryer, and auto flush without the four-figure flagship markup.

**Cleansing Performance**
The DLS offers posterior and feminine washes, adjustable water pressure, and a self-cleaning stainless nozzle. Water temperature is adjustable across three levels, and the tankless instant heater means no waiting between uses. The heated seat has five temperature settings, and the dryer is effective, if a bit slower than TOTO's.

**Design and Features**
The skirted design looks more expensive than it is. You get a nightlight, remote control, auto open/close lid, and a foot-sensor flush. There's no app or health monitoring, but for most households, that's a fair trade for the price.

**Pros:**
- Outstanding value
- Easy DIY-friendly installation relative to flagships
- Reliable instant water heater
- Three-year warranty

**Cons:**
- No smart home integration
- Plastic remote feels basic
- Dryer performance is average

**Verdict:** The Discovery DLS is the smart toilet we recommend to most first-time buyers. It delivers 85% of the flagship experience for 25% of the price.

### Feature Deep Dive

**Bidet Cleansing**
All three models offer front and rear washing with adjustable temperature and pressure. Flagship models add oscillating and pulsating modes that many users find more thorough. Look for stainless steel wands with self-cleaning cycles.

**Heated Seat and Dryer**
A heated seat is arguably the most appreciated feature in cold climates. Air dryers vary widely in effectiveness; premium models dry faster and at higher temperatures.

Auto open/close lids and auto flush are convenience features that become habit quickly. Foot-sensor flushing is a nice hygienic touch on the Bio Bidet.

Only the Kohler Numera offers true smart home connectivity. If voice control and app data matter to you, that narrows the field considerably.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

**Seniors and mobility-limited users:** Smart toilets with auto open/close and remote-controlled cleansing reduce bending and reaching, making them excellent for aging-in-place renovations.

**Health-conscious households:** If you're interested in passive health tracking, the Kohler Numera's sensors offer insights you won't get elsewhere.

**Anyone with a cold bathroom:** Heated seats alone justify the upgrade for many buyers in northern states.

**Tech enthusiasts:** If your home already runs on Alexa or Google Home, an integrated smart toilet is a natural extension.

**Who should skip it:** Renters who can't modify plumbing, households on tight budgets (a $300 bidet seat gets you 70% of the benefit), and anyone unwilling to handle occasional electronic maintenance.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $500:** Bidet seats (not full toilets) — great entry point.
- **$1,500–$2,500:** Entry-level integrated smart toilets like the Bio Bidet Discovery DLS.
- **$3,000–$6,000:** Mid-range flagships with more features and better build quality.
- **$7,000+:** Premium flagships like the TOTO Neorest RS.

Don't forget installation costs, which can run $300–$800 for a licensed plumber, plus the cost of adding an electrical outlet near the toilet if one doesn't exist (typically $200–$500).

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### What Installation Involves

Smart toilets require three connections: a standard water supply, a standard drain, and — critically — a nearby GFCI electrical outlet. Most homes don't have an outlet behind the toilet, so plan for an electrician visit. Professional installation is strongly recommended for integrated units because of the weight (often 80–120 lbs) and the electrical work involved.

### Smart Home Integration

Only a handful of smart toilets integrate with smart home platforms. The Kohler Numera leads here, supporting Alexa and Google Home voice commands and app-based health tracking. TOTO's flagship relies on its own remote, while budget models like the Bio Bidet typically skip connectivity entirely. If smart home integration is a priority, verify platform support before buying, and check whether any features require a subscription.
