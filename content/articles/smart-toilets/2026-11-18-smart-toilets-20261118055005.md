# Smart Toilet Review 2026: Are These High-Tech Thrones Worth the Investment?

## Introduction

The bathroom has quietly become the most surprising battleground in the smart home revolution. While we've spent the last decade automating our lights, thermostats, and doorbells, the humble toilet has undergone a transformation that would have seemed absurd just a few years ago. Enter the smart toilet—a fixture that now packs bidet functionality, heated seats, motion sensors, self-cleaning mechanisms, and even AI-powered health monitoring into a single porcelain package.

If you're shopping for a smart toilet in 2026, you're in luck. Prices have finally started to stabilize, features have matured, and the market offers genuine options for nearly every budget. But with that choice comes complexity. Which features actually matter? Do you need app connectivity for your toilet? And is a $3,000 smart toilet really three times better than a $1,000 model?

In this comprehensive smart toilet review, we'll break down our top recommendations, explain the key features you should prioritize, and help you decide whether it's time to upgrade your bathroom. Let's dive in.

---

## Top 3 Smart Toilet Recommendations

### 1. TOTO Neorest NX2 — Best Overall Smart Toilet

**Estimated Price (2026): $2,800–$3,200**

TOTO remains the undisputed king of the smart toilet category, and the Neorest NX2 represents the brand's sweet spot between flagship luxury and practical ownership. This is the model we recommend to most buyers who want the full smart toilet experience without stepping into ultra-premium territory.

The NX2 delivers TOTO's signature **Washlet bidet technology** with multiple spray settings, a heated seat with adjustable temperature, and the brand's celebrated **Tornado Flush system**, which uses a powerful centrifugal water motion to clean the bowl more effectively than traditional flushing. In real-world use, this translates to noticeably fewer cleaning sessions—the bowl simply stays cleaner longer.

What sets the NX2 apart is its **Activated Katalyst technology**, which uses electrolyzed water to break down waste and reduce odors at the source. Combined with the built-in deodorizer, this is one of the few smart toilets where you genuinely notice the difference in bathroom freshness.

Smart features include a motion-activated lid, auto-flush, and a wireless remote. TOTO's app connectivity is functional rather than flashy—you can adjust settings and monitor usage, but don't expect Alexa voice commands to flush for you. The NX2 also includes a night light and a soft-close seat, small touches that elevate daily use.

**Pros:**
- Industry-leading bidet and flush performance
- Excellent odor control
- Reliable, mature technology with strong service network

**Cons:**
- Premium price
- Requires a dedicated power outlet and water line
- App is basic compared to competitors

**Verdict:** The TOTO Neorest NX2 is the smart toilet we recommend to anyone who wants proven reliability and best-in-class cleaning performance. It's not the flashiest option, but it's the one you'll likely be happiest owning long-term.

### 2. Kohler Innate Intelligent Toilet — Best Smart Home Integration

**Estimated Price (2026): $1,900–$2,300**

Kohler's Innate Intelligent Toilet is the best choice for buyers who are already invested in a smart home ecosystem. With native **Amazon Alexa and Google Home support**, the Innate lets you control flushing, bidet functions, and seat heating with voice commands—a genuinely useful feature when your hands are, shall we say, occupied.

Beyond connectivity, the Innate impresses with its **dual-flush technology**, heated seat, warm air dryer, and a motion-activated lid that opens and closes automatically. The bidet offers adjustable water temperature, pressure, and position, with a self-cleaning wand that sanitizes after each use.

Kohler's app is more polished than TOTO's, offering detailed usage statistics and customization profiles for multiple household members. The design is sleek and contemporary, with a skirted trapway that makes cleaning the exterior far easier than traditional toilets.

One notable feature is the **emergency flush button**, which allows manual flushing during a power outage—a practical consideration many smart toilet buyers overlook.

**Pros:**
- Best-in-class voice control and app experience
- Skirted design simplifies cleaning
- Manual flush backup during outages

**Cons:**
- Bidet performance slightly behind TOTO
- Voice commands can occasionally misfire

**Verdict:** If smart home integration is your priority, the Kohler Innate is the smart toilet to beat. It's a joy to use and plays nicely with the rest of your connected home.

### 3. Bio Bidet Prodigy Smart Toilet — Best Value Smart Toilet

**Estimated Price (2026): $1,100–$1,400**

For buyers who want smart toilet features without the luxury price tag, the Bio Bidet Prodigy is the standout value pick. This model proves you don't need to spend $3,000 to get a heated seat, bidet, and auto-flush.

The Prodigy includes a **heated seat with five temperature settings**, a warm air dryer, and a bidet with adjustable pressure and nozzle position. The **auto-flush and motion-activated lid** work reliably, and the included remote is intuitive. It also features a night light and a self-cleaning nozzle—features often reserved for pricier models.

Where the Prodigy cuts corners is in build quality and advanced features. The plastic components feel less premium than TOTO or Kohler offerings, and there's no app connectivity or voice control. Odor control is basic compared to the activated carbon systems on premium models. But for the price, the core smart toilet experience is remarkably complete.

**Pros:**
- Excellent value for the feature set
- Reliable core functions
- Easy installation relative to premium models

**Cons:**
- No app or voice control
- Build quality feels less premium
- Basic odor management

**Verdict:** The Bio Bidet Prodigy is the smart toilet we recommend to first-time buyers and budget-conscious households. It delivers 80% of the premium experience at roughly half the price.

### Feature Deep Dive

**Bidet Performance:** This is the heart of any smart toilet. Look for adjustable water temperature, pressure, and nozzle position. TOTO leads here, but Kohler and Bio Bidet are perfectly serviceable.

**Heated Seat:** A non-negotiable for most buyers. All three picks deliver, though premium models offer more granular temperature control.

**Auto Open/Close and Auto Flush:** These "magic" features define the smart toilet experience. Motion sensors detect approach and departure, handling the lid and flush automatically.

**Odor Control:** Often overlooked but genuinely important. Electrolyzed water and activated carbon systems (TOTO, Kohler) outperform basic filters.

**Smart Home Integration:** Voice control and app connectivity are nice-to-haves, not must-haves. If you're deep into Alexa or Google Home, Kohler is the clear winner.

**Power and Plumbing Requirements:** Every smart toilet needs a nearby GFCI outlet and a standard water supply line. Confirm your bathroom can accommodate this before purchasing.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

**Buy a smart toilet if:**
- You have mobility limitations or appreciate the comfort of a heated seat and bidet
- You're remodeling a bathroom and want modern amenities
- You value cleanliness and reduced toilet paper usage
- You're a smart home enthusiast who wants full bathroom integration

**Skip a smart toilet if:**
- Your bathroom lacks a nearby power outlet (retrofitting is possible but adds cost)
- You're renting and can't modify plumbing
- You prefer simple, low-maintenance fixtures
- Budget is tight—a bidet seat attachment ($200–$500) offers many benefits at a fraction of the cost

### Budget Considerations

Smart toilets range from roughly **$800 to $6,000+** in 2026. Here's how to think about spending:

- **$800–$1,500:** Entry-level smart toilets like the Bio Bidet Prodigy. Core features, less premium materials.
- **$1,500–$2,500:** Mid-range models like the Kohler Innate. Better build quality, smart home integration.
- **$2,500–$4,000:** Premium models like the TOTO Neorest NX2. Best performance, reliability, and service.
- **$4,000+:** Ultra-premium flagship models with advanced health monitoring and luxury finishes.

Don't forget installation costs, which typically run **$300–$800** for professional installation, plus any electrical work needed to add an outlet.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Considerations

Installing a smart toilet is more involved than swapping a traditional fixture. You'll need:

1. **A dedicated GFCI electrical outlet** within reach of the toilet (typically within 3 feet). If you don't have one, expect to pay an electrician $200–$500.
2. **A standard water supply line**, which most bathrooms already have.
3. **Proper floor flange and wax ring** compatibility—most smart toilets use standard rough-in dimensions, but verify before purchase.

Professional installation is strongly recommended. While confident DIYers can handle the plumbing, the electrical work and weight of these fixtures (many exceed 100 pounds) make professional help worthwhile.

### Smart Home Integration

Integration varies significantly by brand:

- **Kohler** offers the most robust ecosystem, with Alexa and Google Home voice control, plus a polished app for settings and usage tracking.
- **TOTO** provides app connectivity for settings and diagnostics but lacks voice control.
- **Bio Bidet** offers no smart home integration—it's a smart toilet in function, not connectivity.

For most buyers, app and voice control are conveniences rather than necessities. The core value of a smart toilet lies in its bidet, heated seat, and automated functions—not its ability to respond to voice commands.
