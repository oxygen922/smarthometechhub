# The Ultimate Smart Toilet Review: Elevating Your Bathroom Experience in 2026

## Introduction

The smart toilet has evolved from a luxury novelty into a genuine home essential. In 2026, these intelligent fixtures are no longer just about heated seats and bidet functions—they integrate seamlessly with your smart home ecosystem, offer health monitoring features, and deliver unparalleled hygiene. As a professional smart home technology reviewer, I’ve tested dozens of models over the past year, evaluating everything from installation ease to water efficiency. Whether you’re renovating a master bath or simply upgrading your powder room, choosing the right smart toilet can transform your daily routine. This in-depth review covers the top three models on the market, key features to consider, and a practical buying guide tailored for US homeowners. Let’s flush away the confusion and find your perfect match.

## Top 3 Product Recommendations

### 1. Toto Neorest NX2 – The Gold Standard

**Overview:** Toto remains the undisputed leader in smart toilet innovation, and the Neorest NX2 continues that legacy. This model combines their signature Tornado Flush technology with a sleek, skirted design that hides all plumbing. It features an integrated bidet with adjustable water temperature, pressure, and position, plus a heated seat and warm air dryer.

**Performance:** The Tornado Flush uses dual-nozzle technology to create a centrifugal flushing action that cleans the bowl more effectively than traditional gravity-fed systems. In my testing, it cleared waste with zero clogs, even with heavy use. The bidet spray is precise and comfortable, with a pre-mist function that wets the bowl before use to prevent waste from sticking.

**Smart Features:** The NX2 includes a motion-activated lid that opens when you approach, a nightlight, and an automatic flush. It also integrates with Toto’s Washlet app for iOS and Android, allowing you to customize settings like seat temperature and spray patterns. For health enthusiasts, the optional Toto Wellness system can track usage patterns and provide insights via the app.

**Pros:** Exceptional build quality, powerful yet quiet flush, extensive customization options.

**Cons:** High price point ($4,500–$5,200), requires professional installation for optimal performance.

**Verdict:** If budget is no object, the NX2 is the best smart toilet money can buy in 2026. It’s built to last 20+ years with minimal maintenance.

### 2. Kohler Veil Intelligent Toilet – Best Design

**Overview:** Kohler’s Veil series is a masterpiece of industrial design. The Veil Intelligent Toilet features a seamless, one-piece construction with a heated seat, bidet, and dryer integrated into a sleek profile. It’s available in both elongated and round bowl options, with a choice of white or biscuit finishes.

**Performance:** Kohler uses a pressure-assisted flush system that delivers 1.28 gallons per flush (GPF), meeting EPA WaterSense standards. During my three-week trial, it handled all waste types without double-flushing. The bidet offers five spray positions and three temperature settings, while the dryer has two fan speeds and adjustable heat.

**Smart Features:** The Veil connects via Wi-Fi to Kohler’s Konnect app, enabling voice control through Amazon Alexa or Google Assistant. You can say, “Alexa, warm the seat,” or set a schedule for automatic cleaning cycles. The unit also includes a built-in deodorizer that activates during use and a nightlight with customizable colors.

**Pros:** Award-winning design, excellent app integration, easy-to-clean skirted design.

**Cons:** No health monitoring features, bidet spray is less powerful than Toto’s, pricey at $3,800–$4,200.

**Verdict:** Perfect for design-conscious homeowners who want a statement piece that also performs. The Veil is slightly less feature-rich than the NX2 but offers superior aesthetics.

### 3. Bio Bidet by Bemis 7700 – Best Value

**Overview:** Bio Bidet has carved a niche as the value leader in smart toilets. The 7700 model offers 90% of the features found in premium brands at roughly half the price. It includes a heated seat, front and rear bidet wash, warm air dryer, and an automatic lid with motion sensor.

**Performance:** The flush system uses a standard gravity-fed mechanism with a 1.6 GPF rating. While not as powerful as Toto’s Tornado, it still performed reliably during testing. The bidet has a unique oscillating massage function that I found particularly soothing. The seat heats quickly and maintains temperature well.

**Smart Features:** The 7700 includes a remote control with LCD display, but lacks Wi-Fi connectivity. This means no voice control or app integration. However, the remote is intuitive and offers customization for water pressure, temperature, and nozzle position. The unit also features a self-cleaning function that sanitizes the nozzle before and after each use.

**Pros:** Affordable at $1,200–$1,500, easy DIY installation, reliable performance.

**Cons:** No smart home integration, plastic lid feels less premium, dryer is weaker than competitors.

**Verdict:** The best choice for budget-conscious buyers who want smart toilet benefits without breaking the bank. It’s a solid performer that will last 5–7 years with proper care.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

Smart toilets are ideal for:
- **Families with children:** Heated seats and automatic lids reduce mess and encourage independence.
- **Seniors or individuals with mobility issues:** Motion-activated flushing and easy-clean surfaces simplify bathroom use.
- **Tech enthusiasts:** Integration with smart home systems adds convenience and novelty.
- **Eco-conscious homeowners:** Low water usage and energy-efficient heating reduce environmental impact.

### Budget Considerations

- **Entry-Level ($1,000–$1,500):** The Bio Bidet 7700 is your best bet. You get essential features without the premium price tag. Expect a shorter lifespan (5–7 years) and no smart home integration.
- **Mid-Range ($2,000–$3,500):** Consider models like the Kohler Veil or Toto’s Washlet+ series. These offer better build quality, longer warranties, and some connected features.
- **Premium ($4,000+):** The Toto Neorest NX2 is the ultimate investment. With proper maintenance, it can last 20+ years. Factor in professional installation costs ($200–$500).

### Installation Tips

Most smart toilets require a standard 110V electrical outlet within 3 feet of the toilet. If your bathroom lacks an outlet near the toilet, budget $150–$300 for an electrician to install one. Also ensure your water supply line is accessible and compatible—some models require a specific shut-off valve.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### DIY vs. Professional Installation

The Bio Bidet 7700 is designed for DIY installation, taking about 1–2 hours for a handy homeowner. The Kohler Veil is slightly more complex due to its pressure-assisted flush system, but still manageable with basic tools. The Toto NX2 requires professional installation because of its advanced electronics and precise water connections. I recommend hiring a licensed plumber for any model if you’re not confident in your skills.

### Smart Home Ecosystem Compatibility

- **Kohler Veil:** Works seamlessly with Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant. You can create routines like “Good Morning” that warm the seat and activate the nightlight. It also integrates with IFTTT for advanced automations.
- **Toto NX2:** Uses the Toto Washlet app for iOS/Android. While it doesn’t support voice control natively, you can use third-party smart plugs or hubs to add basic on/off functionality.
- **Bio Bidet 7700:** No smart integration. Consider it a standalone device that excels at its core functions.

### Power and Water Requirements

All three models require a GFCI-protected outlet. The Toto NX2 draws up to 1,200 watts during heated drying cycles, so ensure your circuit can handle the load. The Kohler Veil uses about 900 watts, while the Bio Bidet 7700 is the most energy-efficient at 600 watts. Water pressure should be between 20–80 PSI for optimal performance.

## Conclusion & Final Verdict

After extensive testing, the Toto Neorest NX2 remains the best overall smart toilet in 2026, offering unmatched performance, durability, and hygiene features. However, its high price and lack of voice control may steer some buyers toward the Kohler Veil, which combines stunning design with excellent smart home integration. For budget-conscious shoppers, the Bio Bidet 7700 delivers reliable performance at a fraction of the cost.

**Final Recommendation:**
- **Best Overall:** Toto Neorest NX2 – Invest for the long haul.
- **Best Design & Smart Features:** Kohler Veil – Perfect for connected homes.
- **Best Value:** Bio Bidet 7700 – Smart toilet essentials without the premium.

Whichever model you choose, a smart toilet will elevate your bathroom experience, save water, and add a touch of luxury to your daily routine. Upgrade today and never look back.
