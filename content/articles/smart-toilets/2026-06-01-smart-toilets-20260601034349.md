# The Ultimate Smart Toilet Review: Why 2026 Is the Year to Upgrade Your Bathroom

## Introduction

The smart toilet has evolved from a luxury novelty into a must-have home technology. In 2026, as the smart home ecosystem matures and sustainability becomes a priority, the humble toilet is receiving a radical upgrade. Gone are the days of simple bidet seats; today’s smart toilets integrate heated seats, automatic flushing, self-cleaning nozzles, and even health monitoring sensors. With the global smart toilet market projected to exceed $12 billion by 2027, manufacturers are competing fiercely to deliver features that blend convenience, hygiene, and connectivity.

But with so many options—from budget-friendly models to high-end luxury units—choosing the right smart toilet can be overwhelming. This review cuts through the noise, analyzing three top-tier products available in 2026. We’ll explore key features, installation considerations, and who should invest in this transformative home upgrade. Whether you’re a tech enthusiast, a homeowner seeking water savings, or someone prioritizing accessibility, this guide will help you make an informed decision.

## Top 3 Product Recommendations

### 1. Toto Neorest NX2 – The Gold Standard

**Price:** $5,999 (estimated 2026 MSRP)
**Best for:** Luxury seekers and those wanting the most advanced features.

The Toto Neorest NX2 remains the benchmark for smart toilets. Building on decades of innovation, the NX2 features Toto’s eWater+ system, which electrolyzes tap water to create a cleansing agent that keeps the bowl and wand clean between uses. The toilet also includes a pre-mist function that wets the bowl before use to prevent waste from sticking.

The heated seat is ergonomically contoured, and the remote control is intuitive with a large LCD screen. The NX2 offers adjustable water temperature, pressure, and spray position. Its Tornado Flush system uses dual nozzles to create a powerful, quiet flush that uses only 1.0 gallon per flush (GPF)—significantly less than standard toilets.

**Pros:**
- Exceptional build quality and durability
- Advanced self-cleaning technology
- Quiet, powerful flush
- Intuitive remote with memory settings for multiple users

**Cons:**
- Very high price point
- Requires professional installation
- Large footprint may not fit small bathrooms

### 2. Kohler Veil Intelligent Toilet – The Design Icon

**Price:** $4,299 (estimated 2026 MSRP)
**Best for:** Design-conscious homeowners who want seamless integration.

Kohler’s Veil Intelligent Toilet combines minimalist design with robust functionality. Its one-piece, wall-hung design saves floor space and makes cleaning effortless. The Veil features Kohler’s Quiet-Close seat and lid, plus a heated seat with five temperature settings.

The bidet function offers front and rear wash with oscillating and pulsating modes. The Veil also includes a warm air dryer, deodorizer, and a nightlight. The touchless flush is activated by a wave of your hand, while the remote control is sleek and waterproof.

**Pros:**
- Stunning, modern design
- Wall-hung saves space
- Excellent water efficiency (1.28 GPF)
- Easy-to-clean skirted design

**Cons:**
- No health monitoring features
- Remote can be confusing for first-time users
- Limited color options (only white)

### 3. Bio Bidet Flow X3 – The Value Champion

**Price:** $1,499 (estimated 2026 MSRP)
**Best for:** Budget-conscious buyers who don’t want to compromise on core features.

Bio Bidet’s Flow X3 proves you don’t need to spend thousands for a great smart toilet. This two-piece unit includes a heated seat, adjustable bidet spray, warm air dryer, and a deodorizer. The Flow X3 uses a standard gravity flush system with 1.28 GPF, making it both efficient and reliable.

The remote control is simple and includes presets for up to two users. The Flow X3 also features a slow-close seat and lid, plus a child-safe lock for the bidet functions. While it lacks the premium materials of Toto or Kohler, it delivers reliable performance at a fraction of the cost.

**Pros:**
- Affordable entry point to smart toilets
- Easy DIY installation (fits standard rough-in)
- Good water efficiency
- User-friendly remote

**Cons:**
- Plastic components feel less premium
- No self-cleaning nozzle
- Limited spray pattern options

### What to Prioritize

- **Flush Efficiency:** Look for 1.28 GPF or less. Toto’s 1.0 GPF is industry-leading.
- **Bidet Quality:** Adjustable water pressure and temperature are non-negotiable for comfort.
- **Self-Cleaning:** Models with automatic nozzle cleaning reduce maintenance.
- **Smart Home Integration:** Check for compatibility with Alexa, Google Home, or Apple HomeKit.
- **Health Features:** Some high-end models (not reviewed here) offer urine analysis or heart rate monitoring.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

- **Tech Enthusiasts:** If your home is already smart, a Wi-Fi-enabled toilet with voice control is a natural addition.
- **Elderly or Mobility-Challenged Users:** Heated seats, bidet functions, and automatic flushing reduce physical strain.
- **Eco-Conscious Homeowners:** Smart toilets use significantly less water per flush than traditional models.
- **Families with Children:** Child-safe features and easy-clean designs make life easier.
- **Luxury Homeowners:** High-end models like the Toto Neorest NX2 add value and comfort.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $1,500:** The Bio Bidet Flow X3 offers excellent value. Expect basic bidet functions and a heated seat.
- **$1,500–$4,000:** Mid-range models from Kohler, American Standard, and Brondell add design and better flush systems.
- **$4,000 and Above:** Luxury units like Toto and Duravit include advanced self-cleaning, health monitoring, and premium materials.

**Hidden Costs:** Don’t forget installation fees ($200–$500 for professional), electrical work if an outlet is needed ($150–$300), and potential plumbing modifications.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Overview

Most smart toilets require:
- A standard 12-inch rough-in (distance from wall to drain center).
- A nearby electrical outlet (GFCI protected).
- A water supply line (some models require a separate line for the bidet).

**DIY vs. Professional:**
- **Easy:** Two-piece models like the Bio Bidet Flow X3 can be installed by a handy homeowner in 1–2 hours.
- **Moderate:** One-piece units like the Kohler Veil may require lifting heavy ceramic (100+ lbs).
- **Hard:** Wall-hung toilets and those with complex electronics (Toto NX2) need a plumber and electrician.

### Smart Home Integration

All three reviewed toilets offer basic smart features, but integration varies:

- **Toto Neorest NX2:** Works with Toto’s Washlet+ app for remote control and usage tracking. No direct Alexa/Google Home integration.
- **Kohler Veil:** Compatible with Kohler Konnect, which integrates with Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant. You can voice-activate the flush or bidet.
- **Bio Bidet Flow X3:** No smart home integration. The remote is the only control.

**Future-Proofing Tip:** If you want voice control, choose a model with native Wi-Fi and IFTTT support. Otherwise, you can add a smart plug for basic on/off functionality.

Smart toilets are no longer a gimmick—they’re a practical upgrade that improves hygiene, saves water, and enhances daily comfort. In 2026, the market offers options for every budget, but not all models are created equal.

**Best Design:** The **Kohler Veil** is perfect for those who prioritize aesthetics. Its wall-hung design and smart home integration make it a standout in modern bathrooms.

**Final Verdict:** If you can afford it, go for the Toto Neorest NX2. If you’re budget-conscious, the Bio Bidet Flow X3 is a solid choice. The Kohler Veil sits in between, offering design flair but lacking the advanced self-cleaning of Toto.

Upgrade your bathroom, reduce your water bill, and enjoy a cleaner, more comfortable experience. The smart toilet revolution is here—and it’s worth flushing your old habits.
