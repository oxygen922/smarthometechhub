# The Best Bidet Seats of 2026: Smart Toilet Upgrades Worth Every Penny

## Introduction

The humble bidet seat has quietly become one of the most sought-after smart home upgrades of 2026. Once a niche luxury found only in high-end hotels, bidet seats—also known as electronic bidet toilet seats or washlets—are now mainstream fixtures in American bathrooms. And for good reason: they offer superior hygiene, reduce toilet paper consumption, and deliver spa-like comfort at the touch of a button. Modern bidet seats now pack heated seating, warm air drying, deodorizers, night lights, and even app-based controls that integrate with your broader smart home ecosystem. Whether you're remodeling a primary bathroom or simply want a more civilized bathroom experience, a quality bidet seat is a practical, eco-friendly investment. In this in-depth review, we'll walk you through the top three bidet seats for 2026, break down the key features to consider, and help you decide which smart toilet upgrade fits your home and budget. Let's dive in.

## Top 3 Product Recommendations

### 1. TOTO Washlet S7A – Best Overall Bidet Seat

**Estimated 2026 Price: $1,299–$1,499**

If there's a gold standard in the bidet seat world, it's TOTO's Washlet lineup—and the S7A represents the pinnacle of that engineering. TOTO practically invented the modern washlet, and the S7A showcases decades of refinement in a single, elegant package.

**Performance and Cleaning**
The S7A features TOTO's signature eWater+ system, which electrolyzes tap water to create a mildly alkaline mist that helps keep the bowl cleaner between flushes. The wand offers multiple spray modes—front, rear, soft, and oscillating—with adjustable temperature, pressure, and position. The warm water is instant and unlimited thanks to a tankless heating system, so you never run cold mid-clean.

**Comfort Features**
The heated seat is adjustable across five temperature levels, and the lid opens automatically via a motion sensor—a genuinely delightful touch for late-night visits. The built-in air deodorizer activates the moment you sit down, and the warm air dryer is powerful enough to actually be useful, not just a token feature.

**Smart Controls**
The S7A includes a backlit remote control that mounts to the wall, plus a slim side panel for quick access. It doesn't offer Wi-Fi or app connectivity, which is a notable omission at this price point—but TOTO argues (convincingly) that a dedicated remote is more reliable and hygienic than reaching for your phone.

**Verdict**
The S7A is the most polished, reliable bidet seat on the market. It's expensive, but if you want the best and plan to stay in your home for years, it's hard to beat.

**Pros:** Superior build quality, instant unlimited warm water, auto lid, excellent dryer
**Cons:** Premium price, no app connectivity, requires a GFCI outlet near the toilet

**Estimated 2026 Price: $899–$1,099**

Bio Bidet has carved out a reputation for feature-dense bidet seats at competitive prices, and the Discovery DLS is their most connected model yet. For smart home enthusiasts, this is the seat to beat.

**Comfort Features**
You get a heated seat, warm air dryer, and a deodorizer, all adjustable via the included remote or the companion app. The seat also includes a soft-close lid and a night light that can be customized in color and brightness.

**Smart Controls**
Here's where the Discovery DLS shines: it connects via Wi-Fi and works with Amazon Alexa and Google Home. You can create user profiles (so each family member gets their preferred settings), schedule energy-saving modes, and even receive maintenance reminders. The app is well-designed and responsive, and firmware updates have steadily improved reliability since launch.

**Verdict**
If you want a bidet seat that plays nicely with your smart home and don't want to pay TOTO prices, the Discovery DLS is the sweet spot. It's packed with features, and the app integration is genuinely useful rather than gimmicky.

**Pros:** Excellent smart home integration, UV nozzle sterilization, user profiles, strong value

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Bidet Seat?

Bidet seats benefit nearly everyone, but they're especially valuable for:

- **People with mobility limitations** – No reaching or twisting required.
- **Anyone with sensitive skin or medical conditions** – Gentler than paper, and often recommended by healthcare providers.
- **Eco-conscious households** – A bidet seat can dramatically reduce toilet paper use, saving money and trees.
- **Families** – User profiles and adjustable settings accommodate different preferences.
- **Smart home enthusiasts** – Models like the Bio Bidet integrate with your existing ecosystem.

### Budget Considerations

Bidet seats range from about $200 for basic models to well over $1,500 for flagship units. Here's how to think about budget:

- **Under $400:** Basic features—rear wash, adjustable pressure, maybe a heated seat. Good entry point.
- **$400–$800:** Adds warm water, dryer, deodorizer, and remote controls. The sweet spot for most buyers.
- **$800–$1,200:** Premium features like UV sterilization, app control, and superior build quality.
- **$1,200+:** Flagship models with auto lids, unlimited instant hot water, and the best materials.

Also factor in **installation costs** ($0 if you DIY, $100–$250 for a plumber) and **long-term energy use**—most seats cost just a few dollars per year to run, especially with energy-saving modes.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation: Easier Than You Think

Most bidet seats install in 30–60 minutes with basic tools. The process generally involves:

1. **Removing your existing toilet seat** (two bolts).
2. **Installing the mounting bracket** included with your bidet seat.
3. **Connecting the water supply** using the included T-adapter—no plumbing changes needed.
4. **Plugging into a GFCI outlet.** This is the one catch: you need a grounded outlet near the toilet. If you don't have one, an electrician will need to install it ($150–$400).

All three seats in this review include clear instructions and all necessary hardware. The Kohler is the most beginner-friendly; the TOTO requires slightly more care due to its auto-lid mechanism.

### Smart Home Integration

Only the **Bio Bidet Discovery DLS** offers full smart home integration out of the box, with Wi-Fi, Alexa, and Google Home support. You can use voice commands to adjust settings, and the app lets you create profiles and schedules. The **Kohler E930** offers limited Bluetooth connectivity for diagnostics, while the **TOTO S7A** is intentionally offline—a design choice focused on reliability and privacy. If you're building a connected bathroom, the Bio Bidet is the clear winner; otherwise, all three deliver excellent day-to-day performance without requiring an app.
