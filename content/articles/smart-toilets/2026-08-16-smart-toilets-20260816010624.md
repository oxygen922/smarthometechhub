# The Ultimate Smart Toilet Buyer's Guide for 2026: Top Picks, Features, and Installation Insights

## Introduction

The modern bathroom has undergone a remarkable transformation, and at the epicenter of this revolution is the smart toilet. No longer a niche luxury reserved for high-end hotels in Tokyo, smart toilets have become the new frontier of home automation, offering features that blend hygiene, comfort, and technology in ways that were unimaginable just a decade ago. From heated seats and warm-water bidets to self-cleaning wands and health-monitoring sensors, these intelligent fixtures are redefining the daily ritual of bathroom visits. For the American homeowner looking to upgrade their primary or guest bathroom, the decision can be overwhelming. With prices ranging from budget-friendly models under $1,000 to ultra-premium units exceeding $8,000, and a dizzying array of features to consider, this comprehensive guide will break down the best options on the market, what to look for, and how to seamlessly integrate a smart toilet into your existing smart home ecosystem.

## Top 3 Product Recommendations

### 1. TOTO Neorest NX2 (MS990CUG) - The Gold Standard
**Price Estimate (2026):** $7,500 - $8,200

When discussing smart toilets, TOTO is the undisputed pioneer, and the Neorest NX2 represents the pinnacle of their engineering. This is not just a toilet; it's a complete sanitation system. The NX2 features TOTO's signature Tornado Flush technology, which uses dual nozzles to create a powerful centrifugal rinse that cleans the bowl more effectively than conventional gravity-fed systems, using significantly less water (a mere 1.0 gallon per flush). The ACTIVEWater cleaning system automatically sanitizes the bowl with electrolyzed water, ensuring that the ceramic surface remains free of bacteria and stains without harsh chemicals.

**Comfort and Hygiene:** The heated seat is temperature-adjustable, and the integrated bidet offers adjustable water temperature, pressure, and position. The oscillating and pulsating spray modes provide a gentle yet thorough clean. The front and rear soft spray functions are meticulously designed for anatomical correctness. The warm air dryer is surprisingly powerful, reducing the need for toilet paper for many users. The automatic lid opens and closes with a proximity sensor, and the unit features a hands-free flush via a wave sensor or the included wireless remote.

**Smart Home Integration:** This is where the Neorest NX2 truly shines. It is compatible with the TOTO WASHLET+ app, allowing you to control all features from your smartphone. More importantly, for the smart home enthusiast, it integrates seamlessly with Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant. You can program routines such as "Good Morning," which triggers the seat to warm up and the light to turn on at 6:30 AM. The unit also features an ambient night light and a pre-mist function that wets the bowl surface before use to prevent waste from sticking.

**Pros:**
- Unmatched build quality and longevity
- Superior Tornado Flush system
- Excellent app and voice assistant integration
- Automatic bowl cleaning (ACTIVEWater)

**Cons:**
- Extremely high price point
- Requires a dedicated 120V GFCI outlet near the toilet
- Professional installation is highly recommended

**Verdict:** The Neorest NX2 is the ultimate luxury purchase for those who want the absolute best. It is an investment in daily comfort and long-term hygiene.

### 2. Kohler PureWash E930 - The High-Tech Value Champion
**Price Estimate (2026):** $2,800 - $3,400

Kohler has carved out a niche for itself by offering near-premium features at a more accessible price point. The PureWash E930 is a one-piece, skirted smart toilet that offers incredible value. It features a sleek, minimalist design that will complement any modern bathroom aesthetic. The flush is powerful and quiet, utilizing Kohler's AquaPiston technology to deliver a 1.1-gallon flush that is both efficient and thorough.

**Comfort and Hygiene:** The E930 includes a heated seat, adjustable warm water bidet with front and rear wash, and a warm air dryer. One of its standout features is the UV-C light that automatically sanitizes the wand after each use, ensuring the nozzle remains hygienic. The seat is ergonomically designed, and the lid closes slowly to prevent slamming. The unit is equipped with an intuitive side panel and a wireless remote control with a backlit LCD screen, making operation simple for all ages.

**Smart Home Integration:** The Kohler Konnect app is the hub for this toilet. Through the app, you can customize user presets (e.g., "User 1" and "User 2"), adjust water temperature, and monitor water usage. Voice control is available via Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant, allowing for hands-free operation. You can say, "Alexa, tell Kohler to flush," or "Hey Google, turn on the bidet." The integration is stable and user-friendly, though it lacks the advanced automation routines of the TOTO.

**Pros:**
- Excellent value for the feature set
- UV-C self-cleaning wand
- Sleek, modern design
- Reliable app and voice integration

**Cons:**
- Flush power is slightly less robust than TOTO's Tornado
- Limited health monitoring features
- The remote control feels slightly plastic

**Verdict:** The PureWash E930 is the sweet spot for most homeowners. It provides 90% of the luxury experience of the TOTO at less than half the price.

**Price Estimate (2026):** $1,200 - $1,600

For those looking to enter the smart toilet arena without breaking the bank, the Bio Bidet Flow 7000 is an incredible option. This is a "smart bidet toilet" that combines a standard one-piece toilet base with a high-end bidet seat. This design allows for easier installation and maintenance, as the seat can be removed for cleaning or replacement. The Flow 7000 features a dual-flush system (1.1/1.6 GPF) that is both eco-friendly and powerful.

**Comfort and Hygiene:** The seat offers a heated seat, warm water wash, and a warm air dryer. The bidet features a unique "Tornado" wash mode that uses a powerful oscillating spray for a deep clean. The seat is equipped with an auto-flush function triggered by a sensor when the user stands up. The lid and seat are soft-closing, and the unit includes an activated carbon deodorizer that filters air inside the bowl. The control panel is conveniently mounted on the side of the seat, with a wireless remote included for added convenience.

However, it does offer a "Night Light" feature and can be paired with a smart plug to enable basic voice control (e.g., turning the seat warmer on/off via Alexa). For full smart functionality, you would need to use a universal smart home hub like SmartThings or Home Assistant to create automations based on its power draw.

**Pros:**

- Easy installation (no special wiring beyond standard outlet)
- Powerful wash modes
- Excellent for rental properties or DIY installs

**Cons:**
- No native app or voice assistant integration
- The seat is not as sleek as integrated models
- Build quality is good but not premium

**Verdict:** The Flow 7000 is the best entry-level smart toilet. It offers the core features that matter—heated seat, bidet, dryer—without the premium price tag.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

- **The Luxury Seeker:** If you value daily comfort and have the budget, a premium model like the TOTO Neorest is a lifestyle upgrade akin to a high-end espresso machine.
- **The Tech Enthusiast:** If you love automating your home, a model with robust app and voice integration (TOTO or Kohler) will be the centerpiece of your smart bathroom.
- **The Eco-Conscious Homeowner:** Smart toilets use significantly less water per flush than standard toilets. The 1.0-1.1 GPF models can save thousands of gallons of water per year.
- **Individuals with Mobility Issues:** The hands-free operation, heated seats, and automated cleaning features make smart toilets incredibly beneficial for the elderly or those with physical limitations.
- **Families:** For households with multiple people, the ability to set individual user profiles for seat temperature and wash settings is a game-changer.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $1,500:** You can find excellent bidet-seat combos (like the Bio Bidet) or basic integrated units from brands like HomeCraft. You'll get the core features (heated seat, wash, dryer) but sacrifice smart home integration.
- **$2,000 - $4,000:** This is the "sweet spot." You'll get a fully integrated, sleek unit with app and voice control (Kohler E930, TOTO Washlet+ models).
- **$5,000+:** This is the luxury tier. You'll get advanced cleaning systems, superior materials, and the most reliable long-term performance (TOTO Neorest, Kohler Numi).

**Hidden Costs:** Don't forget to budget for installation. Most smart toilets require a GFCI electrical outlet near the toilet, which may require an electrician if you don't have one. Plumbing modifications may also be necessary if your existing rough-in is not standard (typically 12 inches from the wall). Professional installation can range from $300 to $800.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Process

Installing a smart toilet is more complex than a standard toilet due to the electrical requirements.

1.  **Electrical:** You **must** have a grounded 120V GFCI outlet within reach of the toilet. If you don't have one, hire a licensed electrician. This is a safety requirement, not a suggestion.
2.  **Plumbing:** The water supply line needs to be a standard 1/2-inch supply. The toilet will come with a T-adapter for the bidet function. Ensure your water pressure is adequate (usually 20-80 PSI).
3.  **Physical Installation:** For integrated units (TOTO, Kohler), the toilet is heavy and bulky. You will need a second person to lift it into place. The bowl must be seated correctly on the wax ring, and the unit must be level. For bidet-seat models (Bio Bidet), installation is much simpler—you just mount the seat on a standard bowl.

**Pro Tip:** Always read the manufacturer's installation manual. Some units require specific clearance from the wall or side walls for the lid to open properly.

### Smart Home Integration

- **Amazon Alexa & Google Assistant:** Both TOTO and Kohler support these natively. You can create voice commands like "Alexa, turn on the bidet" or "Hey Google, flush the toilet." More importantly, you can integrate the toilet into routines. For example, a "Goodnight" routine could dim the bathroom lights, close the toilet lid, and activate the deodorizer.
- **Apple HomeKit:** As of 2026, most smart toilets do not natively support HomeKit. However, you can bridge them using a hub like Homebridge or Hubitat to control basic functions (like the seat warmer) through HomeKit.
- **Matter Protocol:** The smart home industry is moving towards the Matter standard. While no major toilet manufacturer has announced Matter support yet, it's likely to arrive in the next generation of products. This would allow for unified control across all ecosystems without needing separate apps.
