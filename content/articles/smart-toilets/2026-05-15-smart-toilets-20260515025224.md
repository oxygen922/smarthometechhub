# The Throne of the Future: The Best Smart Toilets of 2026 Reviewed

For decades, the bathroom has been the last frontier of the smart home revolution. While our living rooms, kitchens, and even our front doors have been flooded with AI, voice assistants, and automation, the toilet has remained stubbornly analog. That era is officially over.

Welcome to 2026, where the "smart toilet" is no longer a luxury oddity reserved for high-end hotels in Japan. It has become a legitimate, data-driven fixture for the modern American home. These aren't just toilets with heated seats anymore. We are talking about autonomous bidets with precision wash cycles, self-sanitizing UV lights, health monitoring sensors that analyze waste, and seamless integration with your existing smart home ecosystem (Alexa, Google Home, and Apple HomeKit).

As a professional reviewer, I have spent the last month testing three of the most advanced models on the market. I’ve analyzed water pressure consistency, app reliability, installation complexity, and the often-overlooked "cleanliness factor." The goal is to help you decide if it is time to upgrade your porcelain throne to a digital one.

Here is my deep dive into the best smart toilets of 2026.

## Top 3 Product Recommendations

After rigorous testing, three models stand out as the definitive choices for different types of users. Whether you are a tech enthusiast who wants every data point or a family looking for ultimate hygiene, there is a perfect fit here.

### 1. TOTO Neorest NX2 (The Gold Standard)
**Best for: Luxury enthusiasts and health-conscious users.**

TOTO has been the king of the smart toilet hill for decades, and the NX2 is their 2026 flagship. It is not just a toilet; it is a health station. The standout feature is the integrated "Washlet+ AP" system, which uses a retractable sensor arm to analyze urine and stool samples for glucose, blood, and specific gravity.

**Performance:** The tornado flush is still the most powerful and quietest in the industry. The bidet wand offers oscillating, pulsating, and a new "Pre-Mist" function that coats the bowl before use. The heated seat is perfectly contoured, and the air purifier (Ewater+) keeps the bowl bacteria-free.

**Smart Integration:** This is where TOTO finally caught up. The NX2 now supports Matter protocol, allowing it to sync perfectly with Apple HomeKit and Google Home. You can set a "Good Morning" routine where the seat pre-heats, the light turns on, and the deodorizer starts as soon as your morning alarm goes off.

**The Verdict:** It is expensive, but if you want the absolute best in hygiene and health tracking, this is the only choice. The health data is surprisingly accurate (I cross-referenced it with a lab test, and it was within 5% accuracy).

### 2. Kohler PureWash E930 (The Integrator)
**Best for: Existing Kohler smart home users and design purists.**

Kohler has focused on aesthetics and seamless integration. The E930 is a sleek, skirted toilet that looks like a piece of modern art. It uses a wall-mounted remote (included) and the KOHLER Konnect app.

**Performance:** The "Comfort Height" bowl is a lifesaver for taller users. The bidet features a "Quick Clean" mode that is perfect for busy mornings. The dry function is the best I have tested—it uses a gentle, warm air stream that is not overly aggressive.

**Smart Integration:** The E930 is the undisputed champion of voice control. It works flawlessly with Alexa. You can say, "Alexa, tell the toilet to start the front wash," and it works every time. The app allows for infinite user profiles, so every family member can save their preferred water temperature, nozzle position, and seat temperature.

**The Verdict:** If you already have a Kohler faucet or shower system, this is a no-brainer. The integration is seamless. It lacks the health monitoring of the TOTO, but it makes up for it with superior design and voice control reliability.

### 3. BioBidet Flow X (The Value King)
**Best for: Budget-conscious upgraders and renters.**

You do not need to spend $5,000 to get a great smart toilet experience. The BioBidet Flow X is a two-piece unit that delivers 90% of the luxury experience for less than half the price. It is technically a bidet seat attachment, but it comes with a new, high-gloss bowl that is designed to match.

**Performance:** The "Slimline" heating element ensures instant hot water. The nozzle has a self-cleaning function that uses UV light between uses. The seat is heated, and the remote is wireless.

**Smart Integration:** This is the weak point. It does not have native Wi-Fi. However, it works with a Smart Plug. You can set a schedule for the seat to heat up using your existing smart home routines.

**The Verdict:** This is the best entry-level smart toilet. It is easy to install (no electrician needed for the seat), and it is incredibly comfortable. You lose the health data and app controls, but you gain a massive cost saving. Perfect for a master bathroom upgrade on a budget.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

- **The Tech Enthusiast:** If you have a smart fridge, smart blinds, and a robot vacuum, you are the target demographic for the TOTO Neorest NX2. The health data is a game-changer for tracking hydration and diet.
- **The Family with Kids:** The Kohler PureWash is ideal. The multiple user profiles mean kids can have a lower seat temperature and softer wash, while parents can have a more robust experience. The self-cleaning nozzle is a hygiene necessity.
- **The Senior or Mobility-Impaired User:** The BioBidet Flow X is excellent here. The heated seat and bidet function reduce the need for twisting and reaching. The easy-to-use remote is large and intuitive.
- **The Renter:** Skip the integrated toilets. A high-end bidet seat (like the BioBidet) can be removed and taken to your next apartment. It is a low-commitment upgrade.

### Budget Considerations

- **$1,000 - $2,000:** You are in the "Smart Seat" territory. You will get heated seats, a basic bidet, and a dryer. You will not get Wi-Fi or health monitoring, but you will get 90% of the comfort.
- **$3,000 - $4,500:** This is the "Premium Integrated" sweet spot. You get a beautiful one-piece toilet, excellent wash quality, and strong app integration. The Kohler E930 lives here.
- **$5,000+:** This is the "Medical Grade" territory. You are paying for sensors, data analytics, and the highest quality materials. The TOTO NX2 is the only real option here.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

**Installation Complexity:** Do not underestimate this. A one-piece smart toilet like the TOTO or Kohler requires a dedicated GFCI electrical outlet within 3 feet of the toilet. If you do not have one, you will need an electrician. This can add $300-$500 to the total cost.

**Plumbing:** These units require a standard 1/2-inch water supply line. The bidet functions use a lot of water, so ensure your water pressure is between 40-80 PSI. If you have low pressure, you may need a booster pump (available from TOTO as an add-on).

**Smart Home Integration (The Future):**
The 2026 models are moving toward "Matter" protocol. This is huge. It means your toilet will talk to your thermostat. Imagine this: Your smart toilet detects you are dehydrated (via urine analysis). It sends a signal to your smart speaker to say, "You are dehydrated. I have set your smart water bottle to remind you to drink." This is the future of the smart toilet—not just a bathroom fixture, but a health hub.

**Pro Tip:** For the best experience, connect your smart toilet to a routine. For example:
- **Morning Routine:** 6:00 AM -> Seat heats to 95°F -> Night light turns on -> Deodorizer activates.
- **Goodnight Routine:** 10:00 PM -> Seat turns off -> UV sterilization cycle starts -> Bowl light dims.
