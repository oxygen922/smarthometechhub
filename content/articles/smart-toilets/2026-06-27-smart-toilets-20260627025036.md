# The Ultimate Smart Toilet Buyer’s Guide for 2026: Top Picks, Features, and Expert Insights

Imagine a toilet that knows your bathroom habits better than you do—a seat that warms itself before you sit, opens its lid as you approach, and even analyzes your waste for health insights. This isn’t science fiction; it’s the reality of the modern smart toilet market in 2026. As a smart home technology reviewer who has tested over a dozen models in the past year, I can confidently say that the leap from standard commodes to intelligent lavatories is one of the most transformative upgrades you can make to your home. These devices are no longer just about luxury—they’re about hygiene, water conservation, and integrating seamlessly with your existing smart home ecosystem.

The smart toilet industry has exploded, with prices ranging from budget-friendly $500 units to flagship models exceeding $5,000. Whether you’re a tech enthusiast wanting voice-controlled bidets or a family looking for the most hygienic solution for a busy household, there’s a perfect option. In this comprehensive 2026 review, I’ll break down the top three smart toilets on the market, explain the key features that actually matter, and guide you through installation, integration, and budgeting. By the end, you’ll know exactly which smart toilet deserves a permanent spot in your bathroom.

## Top 3 Smart Toilet Recommendations for 2026

### 1. **Toto Neorest NX2** – The Gold Standard for Luxury and Performance

**Price:** $4,800 – $5,200 (2026 estimated)
**Best for:** High-end homeowners who want the best of everything

The Toto Neorest NX2 is the culmination of decades of Japanese engineering excellence. This isn’t just a toilet; it’s a complete bathroom experience. The moment you approach, its motion sensor triggers the automatic lid and seat to rise, welcoming you with a soft, warm seat that’s preheated to your preferred temperature (adjustable from 85°F to 104°F). The bidet function is nothing short of revolutionary, offering a pulsating, oscillating spray that’s both gentle and thorough. It features Toto’s signature eWater+ system, which electrolyzes tap water into a mild disinfectant that cleans the bowl and wand after every use, ensuring zero bacterial buildup.

The flushing system is where the NX2 truly shines. Toto’s Tornado Flush uses dual nozzles to create a centrifugal force that cleans the bowl with just 1.0 gallon per flush (GPF)—a 20% improvement over standard 1.28 GPF toilets. This translates to significant water savings over a year. The integrated Deodorizing system automatically activates when you sit down, using a carbon filter to neutralize odors before they escape. For health tracking, the NX2 includes a built-in bio-impedance sensor that can measure body fat percentage and heart rate (data syncs to the Toto Wellness app). While this sounds gimmicky, it’s surprisingly accurate for daily trend tracking.

**Pros:**
- Unmatched build quality and reliability (Toto’s 10-year warranty on ceramics)
- Ultra-quiet flush (only 38 dB – quieter than a library)
- Heated seat with five temperature levels
- Self-cleaning wand and bowl

**Cons:**
- High price point (not for budget-conscious buyers)
- Requires a dedicated 120V outlet near the toilet
- Large footprint (28.5 inches deep) – may not fit small bathrooms

### 2. **Kohler Veil Intelligent Toilet** – Best Smart Home Integration

**Price:** $3,200 – $3,800 (2026 estimated)
**Best for:** Tech-savvy users with existing smart home ecosystems

If you live in a Google Home or Amazon Alexa household, the Kohler Veil is your dream toilet. This model is the most deeply integrated smart toilet I’ve tested, with native support for voice commands like “Hey Google, flush the toilet” or “Alexa, warm the seat to 95 degrees.” The Veil also works with Kohler’s own Konnect app, which lets you create custom presets for different users. For example, you can program a “Morning” profile that sets the seat temperature to 100°F, enables the front wash, and turns on the nightlight—all triggered by a simple tap on your smartphone.

The Veil’s bidet system is equally impressive, featuring a dual-nozzle design with a dedicated feminine wash. The water pressure is adjustable across 10 levels, and the water temperature is thermostatically controlled to within 1°F of your set point. The toilet uses a 1.28 GPF flush with a powerful siphon jet that clears waste in under 3 seconds. One standout feature is the integrated UV sanitation light that automatically sterilizes the bowl after every flush, killing 99.9% of bacteria. The seat is heated, but unlike the Toto, it only has three temperature settings, which some users may find limiting.

**Pros:**
- Best-in-class smart home compatibility (Google, Alexa, IFTTT)
- User-specific profiles for up to 8 people
- UV self-cleaning function
- Sleek, modern design with no visible plumbing

**Cons:**
- App setup can be finicky (took me 45 minutes)
- No health tracking features
- Slightly louder flush (42 dB)

### 3. **BioBidet Flow 700** – Best Value Smart Toilet Under $1,500

**Price:** $1,200 – $1,500 (2026 estimated)
**Best for:** Budget-conscious families wanting essential smart features

Don’t let the lower price fool you—the BioBidet Flow 700 punches well above its weight class. This is the smart toilet I recommend to friends who want a bidet, heated seat, and automatic flush without breaking the bank. The Flow 700 uses a standard 1.28 GPF gravity-fed flush, which is reliable if not as dramatic as the Toto’s tornado. The bidet features a single nozzle that offers both posterior and feminine wash modes, with adjustable water pressure (5 levels) and temperature (3 levels). The seat is heated with three settings, and there’s a built-in deodorizer that activates automatically.

Where the Flow 700 truly excels is its simplicity. There’s no complex app or voice control—everything is managed via a side-mounted remote control with large, tactile buttons. This is ideal for elderly users or those who prefer not to fiddle with smartphones in the bathroom. The toilet includes a motion sensor for automatic flush when you stand up, and the lid closes automatically after 60 seconds. The ceramic bowl is coated with BioBidet’s CleanBowl technology, which prevents waste from sticking. While it lacks the premium feel of the Toto or Kohler, the Flow 700 delivers 90% of the functionality at less than half the price.

**Pros:**
- Exceptional value for money
- Simple, intuitive remote control
- Easy DIY installation (most homes have existing outlets)
- Reliable performance with minimal maintenance

**Cons:**
- No smart home integration
- Single nozzle (less hygienic than dual-nozzle designs)
- Flush is slightly less powerful than competitors

## Buying Guide: Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

### **You’re a Luxury Seeker**
If you have a generous budget (over $4,000) and want the absolute best, the Toto Neorest NX2 is non-negotiable. It’s built to last 20+ years, offers unmatched comfort, and includes health tracking that can genuinely improve your wellbeing. This is for homeowners who view their bathroom as a sanctuary, not just a utility room.

### **You’re a Smart Home Enthusiast**
The Kohler Veil is your match if you already have smart lights, thermostats, and speakers. Being able to say “Alexa, flush” while you’re washing your hands is genuinely convenient. The user profiles are a game-changer for families—no more fighting over seat temperature. Just be prepared for a slightly longer setup process.

### **You’re a Practical Family**
For most households, the BioBidet Flow 700 offers the best balance of features and value. It’s affordable enough that you can buy two for a master and guest bathroom. The lack of smart home integration is a minor trade-off for the simplicity and reliability. This is the smart toilet I’d recommend to my own parents.

### **Budget Considerations for 2026**
- **Entry-level (under $1,000):** You’ll find basic bidet seats (not full toilets) that attach to existing bowls. These lack automatic flush and heated seats but still offer bidet functions.
- **Mid-range ($1,000 – $2,500):** Full smart toilets with heated seats, bidets, and auto flush. Expect 1.28 GPF flush and basic remote controls.
- **Premium ($2,500 – $5,000+):** Top-tier models with tornado flushes, UV cleaning, health tracking, and full smart home integration. These are long-term investments.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### **Installation Requirements**
All smart toilets require a standard 120V electrical outlet within 3 feet of the toilet—this is the most common installation hurdle. If your bathroom lacks an outlet near the toilet, you’ll need to hire an electrician (budget $200–$400 for installation). Additionally, you need a standard 12-inch rough-in (distance from wall to drain center). Most models accommodate 10- to 14-inch rough-ins with included adapters.

**DIY vs. Professional:** The BioBidet Flow 700 is genuinely DIY-friendly—I installed mine in under 2 hours with basic tools. The Toto and Kohler models are heavier (over 100 lbs) and require precise leveling, so I recommend professional installation ($150–$300). All models include wax rings and mounting bolts, but you may need to purchase a new water supply line (usually included).

### **Smart Home Integration**
- **Toto Neorest NX2:** Works with Toto’s own app (iOS/Android) for settings adjustment and health data. No native voice control, but you can use IFTTT applets to trigger actions (e.g., “If I arrive home, warm the seat”).
- **Kohler Veil:** Native Google Assistant and Amazon Alexa support. Also works with Kohler Konnect app and IFTTT. This is the only model that truly feels like part of a smart home ecosystem.
- **BioBidet Flow 700:** No smart home integration. It’s a standalone smart toilet that relies on its remote control. For some, this is a feature, not a bug—fewer points of failure.

**Pro Tip:** If you want voice control but prefer the Toto, consider adding a smart plug (like a Kasa or Wemo) to control the power—though this won’t give you bidet or flush control.
