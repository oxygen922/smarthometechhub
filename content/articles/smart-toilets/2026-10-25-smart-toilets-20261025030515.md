# Smart Toilet Review 2026: Are These High-Tech Thrones Worth the Investment?

## Introduction

Let's be honest—the idea of a smart toilet once sounded like a gadget gimmick reserved for luxury hotels and tech-obsessed millionaires. But here we are in 2026, and smart toilets have quietly become one of the fastest-growing segments of the smart home market. And unlike the flashy AI features that seem to be creeping into everything these days—often making products feel *too* polished and artificial—smart toilets offer genuinely practical, tangible benefits: heated seats, bidet washing, self-cleaning functions, and even health monitoring.

In this in-depth smart toilet review, we'll cut through the marketing hype and look at what actually matters. We've tested the leading models on the market, compared their features, and evaluated whether these high-tech bathroom fixtures deliver real value for American households. Whether you're remodeling a master bath or simply curious about upgrading your bathroom experience, this guide will help you make an informed decision.

Let's dive in.

## Top 3 Smart Toilet Recommendations

### 1. TOTO Neorest AS Pro — Best Overall Smart Toilet

**Estimated Price (2026): $2,800 – $3,400**

TOTO remains the undisputed king of the smart toilet category, and the Neorest AS Pro is arguably its most balanced offering yet. This integrated one-piece toilet combines TOTO's legendary build quality with a comprehensive suite of smart features that justify its premium price tag.

**Design & Build Quality**

The Neorest AS Pro features a sleek, skirted design that hides the trapway for a cleaner, more modern look. The ceramic construction is dense and durable, and the fit and finish are impeccable—something we've come to expect from TOTO. It's available in both cotton white and a matte finish that resists fingerprints and water spots.

**Performance**

TOTO's Tornado Flush system uses a combination of centrifugal force and a rimless bowl design to deliver a powerful, quiet, and efficient flush. In our testing, it handled solid waste with a single flush every time—no double-flushing required. The 1.0/0.8 GPF (gallons per flush) dual-flush system is also impressively water-efficient.

**Smart Features**

- **Washlet bidet seat** with front and rear cleansing, adjustable temperature and pressure
- **Heated seat** with five temperature settings
- **Auto open/close lid** with proximity sensor
- **EWATER+ electrolyzed water** for bowl and wand cleaning
- **Premist** technology that coats the bowl before use to prevent waste from sticking
- **Night light** for late-night visits
- **Remote control** with a wall-mountable dock

**Health Monitoring**

One of the standout features of the AS Pro is its optional health monitoring capability. Using sensors built into the bowl, it can track usage patterns and provide insights through the companion app. While not a medical device, it's a fascinating glimpse into the future of preventive health.

**Verdict**

The TOTO Neorest AS Pro is the smart toilet to beat. It's expensive, yes, but it delivers on every front: performance, reliability, and smart features that actually enhance daily life. If you're looking for the best smart toilet money can buy, this is it.

### 2. Kohler Innate Intelligent Toilet — Best Design & Value

**Estimated Price (2026): $1,800 – $2,200**

Kohler's Innate Intelligent Toilet offers a compelling alternative to TOTO's dominance, blending elegant design with a strong feature set at a more accessible price point. It's an excellent choice for homeowners who want smart functionality without the flagship price tag.

**Design & Build Quality**

The Innate features a clean, minimalist silhouette that fits seamlessly into modern bathroom aesthetics. The skirted design and compact footprint make it a great option for smaller bathrooms where space is at a premium. Kohler's vitreous china construction is solid and easy to clean.

**Performance**

The Innate uses Kohler's Revolution 360 swirl flushing technology, which we found to be reliable and efficient. The 1.28 GPF single-flush system is slightly less water-efficient than TOTO's dual-flush setup, but performance was consistently strong during our testing.

**Smart Features**

- **Bidet seat** with adjustable water temperature, pressure, and nozzle position
- **Heated seat** with three temperature settings
- **Auto open/close lid** with motion sensor
- **UV cleaning** for the stainless steel wand
- **Deodorizer** with replaceable carbon filter
- **Night light** with adjustable brightness
- **Remote control** with magnetic wall mount

**App Integration**

The Kohler Konnect app allows you to customize settings, create user profiles, and even set up personalized presets. The app is intuitive and responsive, though it lacks the health-tracking capabilities of the TOTO model.

**Verdict**

The Kohler Innate is a beautifully designed smart toilet that offers excellent value. It's not quite as feature-rich as the TOTO Neorest, but it delivers a premium experience at a price that's easier to justify. For design-conscious buyers who want smart functionality without breaking the bank, it's a top pick.

**Estimated Price (2026): $1,200 – $1,500**

Bio Bidet has built a reputation for affordable bidet products, and the Aura Smart Toilet brings that value proposition to the integrated smart toilet category. It's the most affordable model on our list, but it doesn't feel cheap.

**Smart Features**

- **Bidet seat** with rear and front wash, adjustable pressure and temperature
- **Heated seat** with three temperature settings
- **Auto open/close lid** with motion sensor
- **Self-cleaning wand** with UV sterilization
- **Deodorizer** with activated carbon filter
- **Night light**
- **Remote control** with wall mount

**What's Missing**

The Aura lacks some of the advanced features found on higher-end models, such as electrolyzed water cleaning, premist technology, and app integration. However, for the price, the feature set is impressive.

**Verdict**

The Bio Bidet Aura is the smart toilet to consider if you're curious about the category but not ready to commit to a flagship price. It offers the core smart toilet experience—heated seat, bidet washing, auto lid—at a fraction of the cost. It's an excellent entry point.

## Buying Guide: Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

### Who Should Buy?

**1. Homeowners Remodeling a Bathroom**
If you're already in the process of remodeling, adding a smart toilet is a natural upgrade. The installation process is easier when the bathroom is already torn up, and you can factor the cost into your overall renovation budget.

**2. People with Mobility Issues**
Smart toilets are particularly beneficial for individuals with limited mobility. Features like auto open/close lids, remote controls, and bidet washing reduce the need for bending and reaching, making the bathroom safer and more accessible.

**3. Tech Enthusiasts**
If you love smart home technology, a smart toilet is a natural addition to your connected ecosystem. The ability to customize settings, track usage, and control features via app or voice assistant is appealing for those who enjoy a tech-forward lifestyle.

**4. Families with Young Children**
The self-cleaning and deodorizing features of smart toilets make them a hygienic choice for families. The heated seat is also a nice comfort feature for kids during potty training.

### Who Should Wait?

**1. Renters**
Smart toilets are a significant investment, and installing one in a rental property may not be practical. Unless you have a landlord willing to split the cost, it's probably best to wait until you own your home.

**2. Budget-Conscious Buyers**
If you're on a tight budget, a smart toilet may not be the best use of your funds. A high-quality bidet seat attachment can provide many of the same hygiene benefits at a fraction of the cost.

**3. People with Older Plumbing**
Smart toilets require a standard 12-inch rough-in and a nearby electrical outlet. If your bathroom doesn't have these, you may need to invest in plumbing and electrical work, which can significantly increase the total cost.

### Budget Considerations

Smart toilets range from around $1,000 for entry-level models to over $5,000 for flagship models with advanced features. Here's a rough breakdown:

- **Entry-Level ($1,000–$1,500):** Core features like bidet washing, heated seat, and auto lid. Good for first-time buyers.
- **Mid-Range ($1,500–$2,500):** Added features like UV cleaning, deodorizers, and app integration. Best value for most households.
- **Premium ($2,500–$5,000+):** Top-tier performance, advanced cleaning technologies, health monitoring, and premium design.

Don't forget to factor in installation costs, which can range from $200 to $500 depending on your local labor rates and whether electrical work is needed.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Overview

Installing a smart toilet is more involved than installing a traditional toilet. Here's what to expect:

1. **Electrical Outlet:** Smart toilets require a dedicated 120V GFCI outlet within reach of the toilet's power cord (typically 3–4 feet). If you don't have one, you'll need to hire an electrician.

2. **Water Supply:** Most smart toilets connect to a standard water supply line, but some models require a dedicated line. Check the manufacturer's specifications.

3. **Rough-In:** Smart toilets typically require a 12-inch rough-in, which is standard in most US homes. If your rough-in is different, you may need an adapter or a different model.

4. **Professional Installation:** While some homeowners may be comfortable installing a smart toilet themselves, we recommend hiring a licensed plumber for the job. The combination of water and electricity makes this a job best left to the professionals.

### Smart Home Integration

One of the most exciting aspects of smart toilets is their integration with the broader smart home ecosystem. Here's what to expect:

- **App Control:** Most smart toilets offer companion apps that allow you to customize settings, create user profiles, and monitor usage. The TOTO and Kohler apps are particularly polished.

- **Voice Assistants:** Some models support integration with Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant, allowing you to control basic functions with voice commands. This feature is still somewhat limited but improving.

- **Health Monitoring:** Premium models like the TOTO Neorest AS Pro offer basic health monitoring capabilities, tracking usage patterns and providing insights. While not a substitute for medical advice, it's a fascinating glimpse into the future of preventive health.

- **Firmware Updates:** Smart toilets receive firmware updates that can add new features or improve performance over time. Check the manufacturer's track record for ongoing support.
