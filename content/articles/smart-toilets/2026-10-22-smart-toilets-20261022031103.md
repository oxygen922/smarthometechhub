## Key Features to Consider

Before you buy, it helps to understand what separates a great bidet seat from a mediocre one. Here's what to look for:

### Cleansing Performance

The core function of any bidet seat is, well, cleaning. Look for adjustable water pressure, multiple spray modes (front/rear/feminine), and—ideally—oscillating or pulsating options. Nozzle position adjustment is also valuable, especially for taller or shorter users.

### Heated Seat

A heated seat is one of those features you didn't know you needed until you try it. Most mid-range and premium models offer adjustable temperature settings; budget models sometimes skip this entirely.

### Warm Air Dryer

Dryers vary wildly in effectiveness. Premium models (like the TOTO S7A) offer genuinely useful drying; budget models often provide a weak breeze that requires backup toilet paper. If drying matters to you, prioritize this feature.

### Smart Home Integration

App connectivity and voice control (Alexa, Google Assistant) are increasingly common in 2026. These features are convenient but not essential—decide whether they're worth the premium for your household.

### Deodorizer

An automatic deodorizer neutralizes odors during and after use. It's a nice quality-of-life feature, especially in smaller bathrooms.

### Night Light

A soft LED night light illuminates the bowl for nighttime visits. Small feature, big difference.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Bidet Seat?

Bidet seats appeal to a surprisingly broad range of users:

- **First-time bidet users** should start with a value model like the Kohler Novita BH90. It's affordable, easy to install, and lets you test whether bidet life is for you without a major investment.
- **Smart home enthusiasts** will love the Bio Bidet Discovery DLS, with its voice control and app-based personalization.
- **Comfort-focused buyers** who want the best possible experience should spring for the TOTO Washlet S7A. The difference in build quality and drying performance is noticeable.
- **Households with multiple users** benefit from models with user profiles (like the DLS) or at least easily adjustable settings.
- **People with mobility challenges** often find bidet seats life-changing, as they reduce the need for reaching and wiping. Look for models with remote controls for easier operation.

### Budget Considerations

Bidet seats range from budget-friendly to luxury:

- **Under $300:** Basic models with cold water only or limited features. Fine for testing the waters, but you'll likely want to upgrade.
- **$300–$600:** The sweet spot for most buyers. You'll get heated seats, warm water, adjustable pressure, and often a dryer.
- **$600–$1,000:** Mid-to-premium territory with smart features, better build quality, and more advanced cleansing modes.
- **$1,000+:** Flagship models with the best performance, materials, and longevity.

Don't forget to factor in potential electrical work. If you don't have a GFCI outlet near your toilet, you'll need to install one—typically $150–$300 depending on your setup.

### Sizing and Fit

Bidet seats come in two shapes: elongated and round. Measure your existing toilet bowl before ordering—most manufacturers list compatible dimensions. A poorly fitting seat is uncomfortable and looks awkward. Also consider the seat's profile; some models protrude significantly, which matters in smaller bathrooms.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation: Easier Than You Think

One of the best things about bidet seats is that they're a DIY-friendly upgrade. Most models install in 30–60 minutes with basic tools—no plumber required.

The typical process involves:

1. **Shut off the water supply** and flush the toilet to empty the tank.
2. **Remove your existing seat** by unscrewing the mounting bolts.
3. **Install the mounting bracket** for your new bidet seat.
4. **Connect the water supply** using the included T-adapter, which splits water between the toilet tank and the bidet seat.
5. **Plug in the power cord** to a GFCI outlet (this is the one step that may require an electrician if you don't have a nearby outlet).
6. **Mount the seat** and test all functions.

Most manufacturers include detailed instructions and even video guides. If you're handy at all, you can handle this in an afternoon.

### Smart Home Integration

For smart home users, integration options vary by model:

- **Voice control:** The Bio Bidet Discovery DLS works with Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant, letting you adjust settings hands-free. This is especially useful for users with mobility limitations.
- **App control:** Companion apps let you save profiles, adjust settings, and sometimes monitor usage. App quality varies—Bio Bidet's is among the better ones we've tested.
- **No integration:** Many premium models, including TOTO's Washlet line, skip smart features entirely, focusing instead on build quality and performance. If smart home integration is a priority, check compatibility before buying.

**Pro tip:** If you're building a smart bathroom, consider pairing your bidet seat with a smart mirror, motion-sensing lighting, and a smart exhaust fan for a fully connected experience.
