# The Best Smart Toilets of 2026: A Complete Buyer's Guide and Review

## Introduction

The bathroom has quietly become the most upgraded room in the American smart home. In 2026, smart toilets have moved from luxury novelty to mainstream fixture, with features like heated seats, bidet washing, auto-open lids, and app-based control now available at nearly every price point. Whether you're remodeling a master bath or simply want to ditch toilet paper for good, today's smart toilets deliver real comfort, better hygiene, and genuine water savings. In this guide, I'll walk you through the three best smart toilets you can buy right now, break down the features that actually matter, and help you decide which model fits your home and budget. Let's dig in.

## Top 3 Smart Toilet Recommendations

### 1. TOTO Neorest AH Dual Flush Toilet — Best Overall

TOTO has been the gold standard in bidet technology for decades, and the Neorest AH remains the benchmark smart toilet in 2026. This is a fully integrated unit, meaning the tank, bidet, and electronic controls are all built into one sleek, skirted design that looks like it belongs in a five-star hotel.

**Performance and cleaning:** The Neorest AH uses TOTO's signature Tornado Flush system, which leverages cyclonic rinsing to scour the bowl with remarkable efficiency. It's a dual-flush design (1.0 GPF full, 0.8 GPF light), so it uses a fraction of the water of a standard toilet. The integrated bidet offers front and rear warm-water washing with adjustable temperature, pressure, and nozzle position, plus a oscillating and pulsating massage mode that genuinely feels spa-like.

**Comfort features:** The seat is heated with five temperature settings, and the lid opens and closes automatically via a proximity sensor. A built-in air deodorizer neutralizes odors at the source, and the warm-air dryer means you can realistically skip paper entirely. The remote control is intuitive, and there's also an automatic nightlight.

**Smart home integration:** The Neorest AH works with TOTO's companion app over Bluetooth, and it supports voice control through Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant. You can set user profiles, so each family member gets their preferred seat temperature, spray settings, and dryer intensity.

**The trade-offs:** This is a premium product with a premium price — expect to pay around **$2,800–$3,400** in 2026. It also requires a dedicated GFCI outlet near the toilet and a professional install is strongly recommended. But if you want the best and you're remodeling anyway, this is the one.

**Verdict:** The most refined, reliable, and feature-complete smart toilet on the market. Best overall for buyers who want zero compromises.

### 2. Kohler Numera Smart Toilet — Best Mid-Range Value

Kohler's Numera is the sweet spot for most American households. It delivers the majority of the Neorest's comfort features at roughly half the price, and it's backed by Kohler's excellent US-based customer support and parts network.

**Performance and cleaning:** The Numera uses Kohler's Revolution 360 flushing technology with a 1.28 GPF single flush — not quite as water-efficient as the TOTO, but still well within EPA WaterSense guidelines. The bidet offers a stainless steel wand with warm-water washing, adjustable pressure, and a self-cleaning function that sanitizes the wand before and after each use.

**Comfort features:** You get a heated seat, warm-air dryer, and an in-line deodorizer. The lid is auto-opening, and the unit includes a motion-activated nightlight. Kohler's touchscreen remote is one of the best in the business — large, backlit, and easy to navigate even in a dim bathroom.

**Smart home integration:** The Numera supports Kohler's Konnect app, plus Alexa and Google Assistant voice commands. It also integrates with Kohler's broader smart-home ecosystem, including their smart shower and faucet lines, if you're building out a fully connected bathroom.

**The trade-offs:** The flush is slightly less powerful than TOTO's Tornado system, and the unit is a bit bulkier, which can matter in tight spaces. Pricing in 2026 sits around **$1,600–$2,100** depending on retailer and finish.

**Verdict:** The best balance of features, brand reliability, and price. Ideal for most homeowners upgrading a primary bathroom.

### 3. Bio Bidet Prodigy Smart Toilet — Best Budget Pick

If you want the core smart toilet experience without the four-figure commitment, the Bio Bidet Prodigy is the most compelling entry point in 2026. It's a one-piece integrated design that punches well above its price class.

**Performance and cleaning:** The Prodigy uses a 1.28 GPF siphon jet flush that's dependable and quiet. The bidet features a stainless steel nozzle with adjustable warm-water wash, a feminine wash setting, and a turbo wash mode for stronger pressure. Water temperature and pressure are both adjustable across multiple levels.

**Comfort features:** Heated seat, warm-air dryer, and deodorizer are all included — features you'd normally expect to pay much more for. The lid is not auto-opening on the base model, but the upgraded Prodigy X adds that along with a foot-sensor flush.

**Smart home integration:** The Prodigy line offers app control via Bluetooth and works with Alexa and Google Assistant on the higher trims. The base model relies on a wireless remote, which is honestly fine for most users.

**The trade-offs:** Build quality isn't quite at TOTO or Kohler level, and the plastic components feel less premium. Long-term durability reviews are generally positive but not as bulletproof as the big brands. Pricing runs **$900–$1,400** in 2026.

**Verdict:** The best smart toilet for first-time buyers and budget-conscious remodelers. You get 80% of the luxury experience for about a third of the price.

### What to prioritize

- **Warm water and heated seat:** These are the two features people say they can never live without after trying. Make sure both are included.

## Buying Guide: Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

### Who is this for?

Smart toilets make the most sense for:

- **Homeowners remodeling a bathroom** who are already running new electrical and plumbing. Adding a dedicated outlet now is far cheaper than retrofitting later.
- **Anyone with mobility issues** — the auto-open lid, heated seat, and hands-free washing are genuinely helpful for seniors or people with limited reach.
- **Households that want to reduce toilet paper use** — a bidet with a dryer can cut paper consumption dramatically.
- **Tech-forward homeowners** building a connected home who want voice control and app integration.

### Who should skip it?

- **Renters** who can't modify plumbing or add outlets.
- **Budget-first buyers** — a quality bidet seat attachment (like a TOTO Washlet) on your existing toilet costs $300–$700 and delivers much of the same experience without replacing the whole toilet.
- **Anyone in a home with unreliable power** — these units need electricity to function.

### Budget considerations

Set your budget by tier:

- **Under $500:** Bidet seat attachments only. Great value, but not a "smart toilet."
- **$900–$1,400:** Entry-level integrated smart toilets like the Bio Bidet Prodigy. Best bang for the buck.
- **$1,600–$2,100:** Mid-range sweet spot. Kohler Numera and similar.
- **$2,800+:** Flagship luxury. TOTO Neorest and comparable premium models.

Don't forget to budget for **installation** ($200–$600 for a pro) and **a GFCI outlet** ($150–$400 if one doesn't already exist near the toilet).

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation basics

Replacing a standard toilet with a smart model is similar to a normal toilet swap, with two added requirements:

1. **A nearby electrical outlet.** This is the biggest hurdle. Most smart toilets need a standard 120V GFCI outlet within about three feet. If you don't have one, you'll need an electrician — factor that into your total cost.
2. **A water supply line.** Most units include a flexible supply hose, but check that your shut-off valve is in good shape.
3. **Proper measurements.** Smart toilets are larger than standard models. Measure your rough-in (the distance from the wall to the floor drain), typically 12 inches, and confirm clearance on both sides.

For DIYers, a basic swap takes 1–2 hours. If you're adding an outlet, hire a licensed electrician.

### Smart home integration

All three of our picks support **Amazon Alexa** and **Google Assistant**, so you can issue voice commands like "Alexa, turn on the bidet" or "Hey Google, set the seat to warm." App control lets you save personal presets, schedule the nightlight, and monitor water usage on some models.

For a fully connected bathroom, look for brands that play well with others. Kohler's ecosystem is the most developed, linking toilets, showers, faucets, and even mirrors under one app. TOTO's app is more focused but rock-solid. Bio Bidet is the most basic but still covers the essentials.

**Pro tip:** Put your smart toilet on a smart plug or dedicated circuit so you can reboot it remotely if the electronics ever glitch. It sounds silly, but it saves service calls.
