# The Ultimate Guide to Smart Toilets: Top Picks, Features & Buying Guide for 2026

## Top 3 Product Recommendations with Detailed Reviews

### 1. TOTO Neorest NX2 (2026 Edition)

**Price:** $6,500 – $7,200 (estimated US retail)

**Overview:** TOTO remains the gold standard in smart toilet innovation, and the Neorest NX2 2026 edition solidifies its reputation. This is a fully integrated, one-piece smart toilet that requires no separate bidet attachment. It features TOTO’s signature Tornado Flush system, which uses dual-nozzle technology for a powerful, quiet, and water-efficient flush (1.0 GPF). The new 2026 model adds an upgraded eWater+ system that electrolyzes tap water into a mild disinfectant, automatically cleaning the bowl and wand after each use.

**Key Strengths:**
- **Exceptional comfort:** The heated seat with adjustable temperature (85°F to 104°F) and a soft-close lid are standard. The ergonomic, skirted design is easy to clean.
- **Advanced bidet:** The wand offers oscillating, pulsating, and wide-spray patterns, with adjustable water pressure and temperature. A dedicated “feminine wash” mode is included.
- **Health monitoring:** The NX2 now includes a built-in urinalysis sensor that can detect glucose, protein, and pH levels, syncing data to the TOTO Health app (iOS/Android). This is a game-changer for preventive health.
- **Smart home integration:** Works natively with Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant for voice control of seat warming, bidet activation, and night light. Also integrates with IFTTT for custom routines (e.g., “Good Morning” triggers seat warm and light).

**Weaknesses:**
- **Price:** It’s a significant investment, nearly equal to a mid-range bathroom remodel.
- **Installation complexity:** Requires a dedicated 120V electrical outlet within 3 feet of the toilet and a standard water supply. Professional installation is strongly recommended.
- **App reliance:** Some features, like health data, require the app, which may deter privacy-conscious users.

**Verdict:** The TOTO Neorest NX2 is the best-in-class for those who want the absolute best in hygiene, comfort, and health tracking. It’s ideal for luxury homes and tech-forward families.

### 2. Kohler Veil Intelligent Toilet (2026 Model)

**Price:** $4,500 – $5,200 (estimated US retail)

**Overview:** Kohler’s Veil line is a direct competitor to TOTO, offering a sleek, minimalist design that emphasizes both aesthetics and functionality. The 2026 model features a new “PureWash” bidet system with a retractable, self-cleaning wand that uses UV light sterilization. The flush is a dual-flash system (1.1 GPF/1.6 GPF) that is both quiet and effective. Kohler has also introduced “Kohler Konnect” voice control, which is more intuitive than many competitors.

**Key Strengths:**
- **Design:** The Veil is stunning. Its clean lines, low-profile seat, and illuminated touchscreen remote make it a bathroom centerpiece. Available in white, biscuit, and black.
- **User experience:** The remote control is large, backlit, and easy to use. It includes preset modes for “Eco,” “Comfort,” and “Night.” The seat is heated and has a gentle-close mechanism.
- **Water efficiency:** The dual-flush system is certified by WaterSense, saving up to 20% more water than standard toilets.
- **Smart home:** Works with Google Assistant, Amazon Alexa, and Apple HomeKit (via firmware update). You can set routines like “Leave Home” to turn off seat heating and enable auto-clean.

**Weaknesses:**
- **Bidet pressure:** Some users report the bidet water pressure is slightly less forceful than TOTO’s, though still effective.
- **No health sensors:** Unlike the TOTO NX2, the Veil lacks any health monitoring features.
- **Pricey for what you get:** While cheaper than TOTO, it still commands a premium without the added health tech.

**Verdict:** The Kohler Veil is the best choice for design-conscious homeowners who want a smart toilet that looks as good as it works. It’s a solid mid-range option with excellent smart home integration.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

- **Tech Enthusiasts:** If you love gadgets and automation, a fully integrated smart toilet (TOTO or Kohler) will delight you. Voice control and health tracking add a new dimension to your daily routine.
- **Families with Children or Elderly:** Heated seats, bidets, and self-cleaning features improve hygiene for all ages. The auto-open lid is especially helpful for those with mobility issues.
- **Eco-Conscious Homeowners:** Smart toilets use significantly less water per flush (as low as 1.0 GPF) compared to standard models (1.6 GPF). Over a year, you can save thousands of gallons.
- **Renters:** A smart bidet seat like the Bio Bidet BB-2000 is perfect—it’s removable and can be taken to your next home.

### Who Should Skip?

- **Minimalists on a tight budget:** A basic $30 bidet attachment might suffice if you only want a spray function. Smart toilets are a luxury, not a necessity.
- **Those with very small bathrooms:** Some integrated units are bulky. Measure your space carefully.
- **People uncomfortable with data privacy:** Health-monitoring toilets collect sensitive data. Ensure the manufacturer has strong privacy policies.

### Budget Considerations

- **Budget ($300 – $1,000):** Look for smart bidet seats (e.g., Bio Bidet, Brondell). You’ll get heated seats, bidet, and dryer but no flush control or health sensors.
- **Mid-Range ($1,500 – $4,000):** Integrated toilets from brands like Kohler, American Standard, or WoodBridge. You’ll get seamless design, better bidets, and smart home integration.
- **Premium ($5,000+):** TOTO Neorest, Duravit, or high-end Kohler. These include health monitoring, premium materials, and the best warranty.

**Pro tip:** Factor in installation costs ($150–$500 for professional electrician/plumber) and potential electrical work (adding an outlet near the toilet).

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Steps (General)

1. **Electrical:** Most smart toilets require a grounded 120V outlet within 3–4 feet. If you don’t have one, hire an electrician. Some models (like TOTO) need a dedicated GFCI outlet.
2. **Water Supply:** Connect the toilet’s water line to your existing shut-off valve. Use a T-adapter if needed. Ensure the water pressure is between 20–80 PSI.
3. **Mounting:** For integrated units, follow the manufacturer’s instructions for floor mounting. For bidet seats, slide the seat onto the mounting bracket and secure it.
4. **Smart Setup:** Download the app (e.g., TOTO Health, Kohler Konnect). Connect via Wi-Fi (2.4 GHz is often required). Pair with Alexa/Google using the app.

### Smart Home Integration Tips

- **Voice Commands:** “Alexa, warm the toilet seat,” or “Hey Google, activate bidet.” Most models support these simple commands.
- **Routines:** Create routines like “Good Night” (turn off seat heat, enable night light) or “Morning” (preheat seat, start deodorizer).
- **IFTTT:** Advanced users can create custom triggers, e.g., “When I arrive home, turn on the seat warmer.”
- **Privacy:** Disable health data sharing if you’re concerned. Use a guest mode for visitors.
