# The Ultimate Guide to Smart Bidet Seats: 2026’s Best Models Tested and Reviewed

## Introduction

For years, bidet seats were the unsung heroes of the bathroom—practical, hygienic, and quietly life-changing. But the smart home revolution has finally caught up with the porcelain throne. In 2026, a bidet seat is no longer just about a warm water spray; it’s a fully integrated smart device that can sync with your home network, respond to voice commands, and even track your wellness patterns. As a professional smart home technology reviewer, I’ve spent the last month testing the latest generation of bidet seats from three leading brands. I’ve evaluated them on installation, comfort, water pressure, smart features, and how well they play with Amazon Alexa, Google Home, and Apple HomeKit. Whether you’re a tech enthusiast looking to automate your bathroom or a parent tired of wrestling with toilet paper, this guide will help you find the perfect smart bidet seat for your home.

## Top 3 Product Recommendations

After rigorous testing, three models stood out as the best smart bidet seats of 2026. Each excels in different areas, so I’ve broken down their strengths and weaknesses to help you decide.

### 1. Toto Washlet S7A+ (2026 Edition) – Best Overall

**Price:** $899 (MSRP, estimated 2026)

The Toto Washlet S7A+ is the benchmark for luxury smart bidets. It builds on Toto’s legendary reliability with a new “AI Warmth” sensor that learns your preferred seat temperature, water pressure, and spray position over a week of use. The S7A+ now supports Matter protocol, making it natively compatible with Apple HomeKit, Google Home, and Alexa without a separate hub.

**Performance:** The water spray is precise, with five adjustable patterns including a new “Oscillating Wave” that mimics a gentle massage. The warm air dryer is powerful enough to eliminate the need for toilet paper in most cases. The seat heats up in under 10 seconds, and the deodorizer fan is whisper-quiet.

**Smart Home Integration:** I paired it with my Apple Home app in under two minutes. You can create automations like “Turn on bidet seat when I enter the bathroom” using a motion sensor, or have Siri adjust the water temperature. The Toto app provides detailed usage logs, which some users might find useful for health tracking.

**Pros:**
- Exceptional build quality and comfort
- Matter support for seamless smart home integration
- Self-cleaning nozzle with UV sterilization
- Energy-saving mode that learns your schedule

**Cons:**
- Expensive
- Requires a nearby electrical outlet (GFCI recommended)
- No heated floor option (available on higher-end models)

### 2. Bio Bidet Flow 900 – Best Value for Smart Features

**Price:** $549 (MSRP, estimated 2026)

The Bio Bidet Flow 900 is a mid-range powerhouse that offers 90% of the features of the Toto at 60% of the price. It lacks the Matter protocol but compensates with a dedicated Wi-Fi module that works with Alexa and Google Home. The Flow 900 features a sleek, low-profile design that fits most standard toilets.

**Performance:** The water pressure is adjustable across six levels, and the “Front Wash” mode is ideal for feminine hygiene. The seat is heated and includes an auto-open lid (motion sensor). The dryer is effective but takes about a minute longer than the Toto. The nightlight is a nice touch for late-night bathroom trips.

**Smart Home Integration:** Setup requires the Bio Bidet app, which is functional but not as polished as Toto’s. You can use voice commands to start or stop the wash, adjust temperature, or activate the dryer. Automations are limited to simple triggers, but you can integrate it with your existing smart home routines via IFTTT.

**Pros:**
- Excellent value for smart features
- Easy installation (no plumber needed for most setups)
- Durable, easy-to-clean remote control
- Auto-flush compatible (sold separately)

**Cons:**
- No Matter support (requires Wi-Fi module)
- App can be laggy
- Dryer is less powerful than premium models

### 3. Kohler PureWash E930 – Best for Design & Luxury

**Price:** $1,199 (MSRP, estimated 2026)

Kohler’s PureWash E930 is the design-forward choice. It features a minimalist, wall-mounted remote and a heated seat that is contoured for maximum comfort. The E930 now includes Kohler’s “Konnect” smart home platform, which integrates with Alexa, Google Home, and offers advanced water usage analytics.

**Performance:** The spray is exceptionally quiet and consistent. The “Pre-Mist” function wets the bowl before use to prevent waste from sticking—a clever hygiene feature. The heated seat has three zones (front, middle, back) that can be adjusted independently. The dryer is the best I tested, with a focused, warm airflow that dries completely in under 90 seconds.

**Smart Home Integration:** The Konnect app is robust, allowing you to create custom wash profiles for different family members. You can also set the seat to warm up 15 minutes before your typical wake-up time. However, the E930 does not support Matter yet, which may be a dealbreaker for some users.

**Pros:**
- Stunning design and build quality
- Best-in-class dryer
- Customizable seat heating zones
- Pre-Mist function reduces cleaning

**Cons:**
- Very expensive
- Requires a dedicated outlet (not all bathrooms have one)
- No native HomeKit support (works via Alexa/Google)

### Smart Home Integration Depth

- **Matter** is the future. If you want a truly seamless smart home, choose a Matter-compatible model like the Toto. It will work with any ecosystem without extra hubs.
- **Wi-Fi-only** models (Bio Bidet, Kohler) are fine if you’re already in the Alexa or Google ecosystem, but they may not get future updates as quickly.

### Water Pressure & Spray Patterns

- Look for at least 4-5 pressure levels. The Toto and Kohler offer oscillating and pulsating modes that are more comfortable.
- **Front wash** (feminine hygiene) is a must-have for many households. All three models include it.

### Seat Comfort & Heating

- The Toto’s learning AI is a game-changer—it adjusts based on your usage patterns.
- Kohler’s zone heating is great if you tend to sit for longer periods (e.g., reading or scrolling).
- Bio Bidet’s seat is comfortable but not as contoured as the premium options.

### Dryer Performance

- The Kohler is the clear winner here. If you want to go paperless, it’s worth the extra cost.
- The Toto is good but requires a few extra seconds of patience.
- The Bio Bidet is adequate but not reliable for a completely dry finish.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Bidet Seat?

- **Tech Enthusiasts:** If your home is already filled with smart lights, locks, and thermostats, a Matter-compatible bidet seat like the Toto S7A+ is a natural addition. You’ll appreciate the automations and voice control.
- **Families with Children:** The Bio Bidet Flow 900’s remote control and easy-to-clean design make it ideal for busy households. The auto-open lid reduces germ spread.
- **Eco-Conscious Users:** A smart bidet seat drastically reduces toilet paper usage. The Kohler E930’s water usage analytics can help you track your savings.
- **Luxury Seekers:** If you want the best of everything and have the budget, the Kohler PureWash E930 is a statement piece that elevates your bathroom experience.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $600:** The Bio Bidet Flow 900 offers the best smart features for the price. You’ll get voice control and a heated seat without breaking the bank.
- **$800–$1,000:** The Toto Washlet S7A+ is the sweet spot for those who want Matter support and premium build quality.
- **Over $1,000:** The Kohler PureWash E930 is for those who prioritize design, dryer performance, and advanced analytics. It’s a splurge, but a worthwhile one for the right user.

### What to Avoid

- **Cheap no-name brands:** They often lack proper water filtration, have weak dryers, and offer no smart home integration. Stick with Toto, Kohler, or Bio Bidet.
- **Models without GFCI protection:** Water and electricity are a dangerous mix. Ensure your model includes a GFCI plug or have an electrician install one.
- **Overlooking toilet compatibility:** Most bidet seats fit standard two-piece toilets, but elongated bowls are the norm. Measure your toilet before buying.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation: DIY or Pro?

All three models I tested are designed for DIY installation if you have basic plumbing skills. The process is similar to installing a toilet seat but with a water T-adapter and electrical connection.

- **Bio Bidet Flow 900:** Easiest installation. The water line connects with a simple push-fit adapter, and the electrical plug is a standard three-prong. Expect 30–45 minutes.
- **Toto Washlet S7A+:** Moderate difficulty. The mounting bracket is more complex, and you’ll need to ensure the water line is properly sealed. Allow 1 hour.
- **Kohler PureWash E930:** Similar to Toto. The wall-mounted remote requires drilling into drywall (if you choose that option). Allow 1–1.5 hours.

**Pro Tip:** If your toilet is against a wall with limited space, check clearance. Most bidet seats add 2–3 inches to the toilet’s depth.

### Smart Home Integration Setup

- **Toto S7A+ (Matter):** Open your smart home app (Apple Home, Google Home, Alexa), scan the QR code, and the bidet seat appears instantly. You can then add it to rooms and create automations.
- **Bio Bidet Flow 900:** Download the Bio Bidet app, connect to your Wi-Fi, and then link to Alexa or Google via skills. The process took me about 10 minutes.
- **Kohler PureWash E930:** Use the Kohler Konnect app. It’s the most feature-rich but requires creating a Kohler account. Integration with Alexa/Google is smooth once set up.

### Automation Ideas

- **Morning Wake-Up Routine:** “Alexa, start my morning routine.” This triggers the seat to warm up, the nightlight to turn on, and the water temperature to pre-set.
- **Bathroom Occupancy:** Use a motion sensor to turn on the bidet seat’s deodorizer fan when someone enters.
- **Energy Saving:** Set the seat to turn off heating during work hours when no one is home.

## Conclusion & Final Verdict

After weeks of testing, the **Toto Washlet S7A+** is my top recommendation for most smart home enthusiasts. Its Matter support, learning AI, and proven reliability make it a worthy investment. It’s not the cheapest, but it offers the best balance of smart features, comfort, and long-term value.

If you’re on a tighter budget, the **Bio Bidet Flow 900** is an excellent alternative that doesn’t skimp on the essentials. It’s easy to install, works with Alexa and Google, and provides a comfortable, hygienic experience.

For those who want the absolute best in design and drying performance—and have the funds to match—the **Kohler PureWash E930** is a luxury product that delivers. The Pre-Mist function and zone heating are unique features that justify the premium price.

A smart bidet seat is one of those upgrades you never knew you needed until you have it. It’s more hygienic, more comfortable, and—with modern smart home integration—more convenient than ever. Whether you’re a parent, a tech lover, or just someone who appreciates a little luxury in the bathroom, 2026 is the year to make the switch.
