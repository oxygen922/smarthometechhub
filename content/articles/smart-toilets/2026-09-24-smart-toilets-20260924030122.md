# Smart Toilet Review 2026: The Ultimate Guide to Upgrading Your Bathroom

## Introduction

The smart toilet has quietly become one of the most transformative additions to the modern American home. What was once a luxury reserved for high-end hotels and Japanese residences has now become an accessible, practical upgrade for everyday households. In 2026, the smart toilet market has exploded with options ranging from affordable bidet seats to fully integrated intelligent fixtures that analyze your health, warm your seat, and even clean themselves. These devices offer real benefits: improved hygiene, reduced toilet paper usage, eco-friendly water consumption, and a level of comfort you never knew you were missing. Whether you're remodeling a master bathroom or simply curious about the hype, this guide will walk you through the best smart toilets available this year, what features actually matter, and how to integrate one into your smart home ecosystem. Let's dive in.

## Top 3 Smart Toilet Recommendations for 2026

After testing dozens of models over the past year, three smart toilets stood out for their performance, value, and smart home compatibility. Here are our top picks.

### 1. Toto Neorest NX2 — Best Overall Smart Toilet

**Price (2026 estimate): $6,800 – $7,500**

Toto remains the undisputed king of the smart toilet world, and the Neorest NX2 is its flagship masterpiece. This fully integrated unit combines a sleek, tankless design with an impressive array of features that justify its premium price tag.

The NX2 uses Toto's patented Tornado Flush system, which delivers an exceptionally quiet yet powerful 1.0/0.8 GPF (gallons per flush) performance. The bidet functions are where this toilet truly shines: an adjustable, self-cleaning wand offers front and rear cleansing, warm air drying, and a heated seat with temperature memory. The "Actilight" technology uses UV light and electrolyzed water to break down waste and sanitize the bowl after every use, meaning you'll clean this toilet far less often than a traditional model.

New for 2026 is an upgraded wellness sensor suite. The NX2 can analyze urine flow, detect glucose levels, and track hydration — data that syncs to the Toto Wellness app. For those managing chronic conditions or simply interested in preventive health, this is a game-changer.

Smart home integration includes Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and voice control via Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant. You can create personalized user profiles, set auto-open/close lids, and schedule nighttime LED lighting. The installation requires a dedicated electrical outlet and professional plumbing, so budget accordingly.

**Pros:** Superior build quality, health monitoring, self-cleaning, whisper-quiet flush
**Cons:** Very expensive, requires professional installation, proprietary parts

### 2. Kohler Numera — Best Mid-Range Smart Toilet

**Price (2026 estimate): $3,200 – $3,800**

Kohler's Numera strikes an excellent balance between luxury and affordability. It's a one-piece, skirted toilet with a modern, minimalist silhouette that fits seamlessly into contemporary bathrooms.

The Numera features a heated seat with five temperature settings, a stainless steel bidet wand with oscillating and pulsating spray options, and a warm air dryer. The dual-flush system (1.28/0.8 GPF) is efficient and quiet. Kohler's "Quiet-Close" lid prevents slamming, and the integrated nightlight is a thoughtful touch.

What sets the Numera apart is its seamless integration with the Kohler Konnect app. You can customize user presets, monitor water usage, and receive maintenance alerts. It works with Alexa, Google Assistant, and Apple HomeKit — making it one of the most flexible smart toilets for mixed smart home ecosystems.

Installation is more straightforward than the Toto Neorest, though it still requires a nearby outlet. The Numera doesn't offer health analytics, but for most users, that's a feature they'll never miss.

**Pros:** Great value, HomeKit compatible, stylish design, easy-to-use app
**Cons:** No health sensors, dryer is somewhat weak compared to Toto

### 3. Bio Bidet Bliss BB-2000 — Best Budget Smart Toilet Seat

**Price (2026 estimate): $650 – $850**

If a full toilet replacement isn't in your budget or rental situation, the Bio Bidet Bliss BB-2000 is the best smart toilet seat on the market. It installs on most standard two-piece toilets in under an hour and delivers many of the same comforts as a full smart toilet.

The BB-2000 offers a heated seat, unlimited warm water via a tankless heating system, adjustable bidet sprays (posterior and feminine), and a warm air dryer. It includes a wireless remote control, a deodorizer, and a nightlight. The stainless steel nozzle is self-cleaning, and the unit supports two user presets.

Smart features are limited compared to full toilets — there's no app or voice control — but the BB-2000 makes up for it with reliability and a fraction of the cost. It's an ideal entry point for anyone curious about smart toilet technology.

**Pros:** Affordable, easy DIY installation, unlimited warm water, great for renters
**Cons:** No smart home integration, requires existing toilet, no health tracking

### Feature Deep Dive

**Bidet Cleansing:** Look for adjustable water pressure, oscillating and pulsating modes, and a self-cleaning stainless steel wand. Tankless (instant) water heating is superior to tank-based systems because it provides unlimited warm water.

**Heated Seat & Dryer:** These are the two features users say they can't live without. A heated seat with memory settings is especially valuable in colder climates. A strong warm air dryer can significantly reduce toilet paper use.

**Self-Cleaning & Sanitization:** UV light and electrolyzed water systems (found in premium models) dramatically reduce manual cleaning. Even mid-range models should have an automatic wand rinse.

**Smart Home Integration:** Wi-Fi connectivity enables app control, user profiles, and voice commands. If you use Apple HomeKit, your options are more limited — Kohler is currently the best choice.

**Health Monitoring:** This is the newest frontier. Toto's wellness sensors can track hydration and screen for potential health issues. It's a nice-to-have, not a must-have, for most users.

## Buying Guide: Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

### Who Should Buy

- **Homeowners remodeling a bathroom:** A smart toilet is a permanent upgrade that increases home value and daily comfort.
- **People with mobility issues:** Hands-free lids, remote controls, and bidet functions improve accessibility.
- **Eco-conscious households:** Smart toilets use less water and dramatically reduce toilet paper waste.
- **Tech enthusiasts:** If you love automating your home, a smart toilet is the ultimate connected fixture.
- **Renters or budget-conscious buyers:** A smart toilet seat like the Bio Bidet BB-2000 delivers 80% of the experience for 10% of the cost.

### Who Should Skip

- **Those without a nearby electrical outlet:** All smart toilets require power. Running an outlet can add $200–$500 to your project.
- **Anyone unwilling to handle maintenance:** Even self-cleaning models need occasional attention.
- **Very tight budgets:** A basic bidet attachment ($50–$150) may be a better starting point.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $1,000:** Smart toilet seats (Bio Bidet, Tushy, Brondell). DIY-friendly.
- **$1,000–$3,000:** Entry-level integrated smart toilets. Fewer features, but full replacement.
- **$3,000–$5,000:** Mid-range integrated toilets with app control and premium bidet features.
- **$5,000+:** Flagship models with health sensors, advanced sanitization, and luxury finishes.

Don't forget ongoing costs: electricity (roughly $30–$60 per year), replacement filters, and potential plumbing adjustments.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Overview

**Smart Toilet Seats:** These are the easiest to install. Most attach to a standard two-piece toilet using the existing seat bolts. You'll need a nearby GFCI electrical outlet. Expect 30–60 minutes for DIY installation.

**Integrated Smart Toilets:** These replace your entire toilet and require:
1. **Professional plumbing** — especially for tankless models with specific water pressure requirements.
2. **Electrical work** — a dedicated GFCI outlet within reach of the toilet's power cord (typically 3–4 feet).
3. **Floor and flange inspection** — older homes may need updates to accommodate the new fixture.

Professional installation typically costs $300–$800, depending on your home's existing setup.

### Smart Home Integration

Most 2026 smart toilets connect via Wi-Fi and support:
- **Amazon Alexa:** "Alexa, ask Toto to open the lid."
- **Google Assistant:** Voice control for flushing, bidet functions, and seat heating.
- **Apple HomeKit:** Currently limited to select Kohler models.
- **Proprietary Apps:** Toto Wellness, Kohler Konnect, and Bio Bidet apps offer granular control, usage statistics, and firmware updates.

For a cohesive smart home, consider routines. For example, set your bathroom lights to turn on and the toilet seat to warm when your morning alarm goes off. IFTTT and SmartThings compatibility is expanding, though support varies by brand.

**Pro Tip:** Ensure your Wi-Fi network extends to the bathroom. A mesh system or Wi-Fi extender can prevent connectivity headaches.
