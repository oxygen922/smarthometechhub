## Top 3 Product Recommendations with Detailed Reviews

### 1. Toto Washlet S7+ (2026 Edition)
**Price: $1,299 (estimated 2026 MSRP)**

Toto remains the gold standard in bidet technology, and the Washlet S7+ is their flagship model for 2026. This unit builds on the legendary S7 series with enhanced water heating, a redesigned ergonomic seat, and deeper smart home integration.

**Performance and Comfort**  
The S7+ features Toto’s patented Premist technology, which coats the bowl with a fine water mist before use to prevent waste from sticking. The heated seat is now more responsive, with six temperature settings and an occupancy sensor that preheats the seat when you approach. The water spray offers five adjustable positions and three pressure levels, including a new “Oscillating Pulse” mode that mimics a gentle massage. **Pros and Cons**  
- **Pros:** Exceptional build quality, advanced water heating (instantaneous), quiet operation, excellent smart home integration.
- **Cons:** High price point, requires a nearby GFCI electrical outlet, complex installation for DIYers.

**Verdict:** If budget is no object and you want the best-in-class experience, the Toto Washlet S7+ is unmatched. It’s ideal for luxury smart homes where every detail matters.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Bidet Seat?

- **Tech Enthusiasts:** If you already have a smart home hub (Alexa, Google Home, or Apple HomeKit), a smart bidet seat is a natural addition. Look for models with robust app support and voice control.
- **Eco-Conscious Consumers:** Bidet seats drastically reduce toilet paper usage. The average household saves 20-30 rolls per year, which is both cost-effective and environmentally friendly.
- **Individuals with Mobility Issues:** Heated seats, adjustable spray positions, and remote controls make bidet seats accessible for seniors or people with arthritis. Some models even offer a “night light” for easier use in the dark.
- **Families with Children:** User profiles allow each family member to save their preferred settings. The low-temperature safety lock prevents accidental burns.

### Budget Considerations

- **Entry-Level ($300-$600):** These models offer basic heated seats and wash functions but lack smart home integration. Brands like Brondell and Luxe Neo fit this category.
- **Mid-Range ($600-$1,000):** The Bio Bidet Ultimate 770 is the sweet spot. You get smart features, multiple wash modes, and solid build quality.
- **Premium ($1,000+):** Toto and Kohler dominate this segment. You’re paying for superior materials, advanced features, and deeper ecosystem integration. The Toto S7+ is the clear winner for luxury.

**Pro Tip:** Check for utility rebates. Some states and local utilities offer rebates for water-saving fixtures, including bidet seats. In 2026, California and New York have active programs.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Basics

Installing a smart bidet seat is a DIY-friendly project for most homeowners, but there are a few prerequisites:

1. **Electrical Outlet:** You need a GFCI-protected outlet within 3 feet of the toilet. If you don’t have one, hire an electrician. Cost: $150-$300.
2. **Water Supply:** The bidet connects to your existing toilet water supply line via a T-adapter. Most models include the necessary hardware.
3. **Toilet Compatibility:** Measure your toilet bowl. Elongated seats require elongated toilets, and round seats fit round bowls. Most bidet seats are universal, but check dimensions.

**Step-by-Step Installation (Average time: 45 minutes):**
- Turn off water supply and flush to empty the tank.
- Disconnect the water line and attach the T-adapter.
- Connect the bidet seat to the T-adapter and toilet.
- Plug in the power cord and mount the seat.
- Test for leaks and configure settings via the app.

### Smart Home Integration

All three reviewed models support Wi-Fi and app control, but the level of integration varies:

- **Toto Washlet S7+:** Best for Alexa/Google Home users. You can create routines like “Good Morning” to warm the seat and activate a gentle wash. The app is intuitive but lacks IFTTT support.
- **Bio Bidet Ultimate 770:** Similar to Toto but with a more basic app. Voice commands are reliable, but you can’t create complex automations.
- **Kohler PureWash E930:** The most integrated option if you own other Kohler products. The KOHLER Konnect app lets you build scenes that link your bidet, shower, and lighting. Siri Shortcuts are a nice bonus for Apple users.

**Pro Tip:** If you’re a heavy IFTTT user, consider the Kohler E930. It supports triggers like “When the sun sets, activate night light on bidet.”
