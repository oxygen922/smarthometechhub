# The Ultimate Smart Toilet Review: Upgrade Your Bathroom in 2026

## Introduction

Imagine stepping into your bathroom, and before you even sit down, the toilet has already adjusted the seat temperature, activated a gentle bidet spray, and turned on a soft night light. Welcome to the world of smart toilets in 2026—where hygiene, comfort, and technology converge in a single, elegant fixture. No longer a luxury reserved for tech billionaires, smart toilets are now accessible to the modern American homeowner seeking convenience, water efficiency, and a touch of futuristic living. With features like self-cleaning nozzles, heated seats, built-in deodorizers, and even voice control, these intelligent thrones are transforming daily routines. In this in-depth review, we’ll explore the top three smart toilet models of 2026, break down the key features you need to consider, and help you decide which one fits your lifestyle and budget. Whether you’re a tech enthusiast or just tired of cold toilet seats, this guide has you covered.

## Top 3 Product Recommendations with Detailed Reviews

### 1. TOTO Neorest NX2 (2026 Edition)

**Rating:** 9.5/10  
**Price:** $4,999 (estimated 2026 MSRP)  
**Best for:** Luxury home owners and tech enthusiasts

The TOTO Neorest NX2 remains the gold standard in smart toilets, and the 2026 edition refines an already stellar package. This one-piece, wall-hung toilet features TOTO’s patented Tornado Flush technology, which uses dual-nozzle jets to create a powerful, silent, and water-saving flush (just 1.0 gallons per flush). The NX2 now includes an upgraded eWater+ system that electrolyzes tap water to create a natural cleaning agent, reducing the need for harsh chemicals. The bidet function offers adjustable water temperature, pressure, and position, with a new "Oscillating Pulse" mode for enhanced comfort. The heated seat now features a "Smart Warm" sensor that learns your preferred temperature over time. Voice control via Amazon Alexa or Google Assistant is standard, and the included wireless remote is backlit for nighttime use.

**Pros:**  
- Exceptional build quality and durability  
- Ultra-quiet flush (under 40 decibels)  
- Self-cleaning wand with UV light sterilization  
- Energy-efficient standby mode  

**Cons:**  
- High price point may deter budget buyers  
- Requires professional installation for wall-hung models  
- Limited color options (only white and bone)  

**Verdict:** If you’re willing to invest in the best, the TOTO Neorest NX2 delivers unrivaled performance and luxury. Perfect for master bathrooms where every detail matters.

### 2. Kohler Veil Intelligent Toilet (2026 Edition)

**Rating:** 9.0/10  
**Price:** $3,299 (estimated 2026 MSRP)  
**Best for:** Design-conscious homeowners seeking modern aesthetics

Kohler’s Veil Intelligent Toilet has been a design icon for years, and the 2026 update focuses on smarter integration and sustainability. This one-piece, floor-mounted toilet features a sleek, skirted design that hides all plumbing for a minimalist look. The Veil now boasts a new "PureWash" bidet with a heated seat that reaches up to 104°F, a warm air dryer, and a deodorizer that uses activated carbon filters. The flush system uses Kohler’s AquaPiston technology, delivering a powerful 1.28 GPF flush that’s both efficient and quiet. The 2026 model includes a built-in "Kohler Konnect" hub, allowing you to control the toilet via the Kohler Konnect app, voice commands, or a touchscreen remote. The app provides usage analytics, leak detection alerts, and customizable presets for multiple users.

**Pros:**  
- Stunning design with hidden plumbing  
- Excellent water efficiency (1.28 GPF)  
- User-friendly app with real-time monitoring  
- Heated seat and bidet are responsive and comfortable  

**Cons:**  
- Bidet water pressure could be stronger for some users  
- No UV self-cleaning on the wand (requires manual cleaning)  
- Remote can be confusing for first-time users  

**Verdict:** The Kohler Veil is a beautiful, reliable smart toilet that balances style with substance. Ideal for modern bathrooms where aesthetics are as important as function.

### 3. Bio Bidet Ultimate U700 (2026 Edition)

**Rating:** 8.5/10  
**Price:** $1,799 (estimated 2026 MSRP)  
**Best for:** Value-conscious buyers who want premium features without the premium price

Bio Bidet has long been a leader in the bidet seat market, and the Ultimate U700 is their most advanced integrated smart toilet. This two-piece, floor-mounted model offers an incredible value proposition: it includes a heated seat, a front-and-rear bidet with adjustable settings, a warm air dryer, a deodorizer, and a self-cleaning wand with stainless steel nozzles. The flush system uses a dual-flush mechanism (0.8/1.28 GPF) for maximum water savings. The 2026 edition adds a "Night Glow" LED light that illuminates the bowl in a soft blue hue, plus a "Smart Seat" sensor that automatically opens and closes the lid when you approach or leave. Voice control is available via Alexa and Google Assistant, and the included remote is intuitive with a large LCD screen. The U700 also features a "Spa Mode" that cycles through water temperature and pressure settings for a relaxing experience.

**Pros:**  
- Exceptional value for the feature set  
- Dual-flush system saves water  
- Easy DIY installation (fits standard toilets)  
- Quiet operation  

**Cons:**  
- Build quality is slightly less premium than TOTO or Kohler  
- No UV sterilization on the wand  
- App connectivity can be finicky  

**Verdict:** The Bio Bidet Ultimate U700 is the best smart toilet for most people. It offers 90% of the luxury features at half the price, making it a smart choice for guest bathrooms or budget-conscious upgrades.

## Buying Guide: Who Should Buy and Budget Considerations

### Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

- **Tech Enthusiasts:** If you love automating your home, a smart toilet with voice control and app integration is a must-have. The TOTO Neorest NX2 is your dream toilet.
- **Eco-Conscious Homeowners:** Smart toilets use significantly less water than traditional models (0.8–1.28 GPF vs. 1.6 GPF). The Bio Bidet U700 is the most water-efficient option.
- **Families with Multiple Users:** Features like auto lid opening, heated seats, and customizable presets make smart toilets ideal for busy households. The Kohler Veil’s app allows up to 10 user profiles.
- **Elderly or Mobility-Challenged Individuals:** Heated seats, bidet functions, and touchless operation enhance safety and comfort. All three models are excellent choices.
- **Luxury Home Builders:** If you’re designing a high-end bathroom, the TOTO Neorest NX2 or Kohler Veil will impress guests and add resale value.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $2,000:** The Bio Bidet U700 offers the best value. You get nearly all premium features (bidet, heated seat, dryer, deodorizer) without breaking the bank. DIY installation saves on labor costs.
- **$2,000–$4,000:** The Kohler Veil is your sweet spot. You get superior design, reliable performance, and a robust app ecosystem. Professional installation may add $200–$500.
- **Over $4,000:** The TOTO Neorest NX2 is the pinnacle. If budget isn’t a concern, this toilet will last 20+ years with minimal maintenance. Expect professional installation costs of $300–$600.

**Hidden Costs:** Don’t forget electrical requirements (most smart toilets need a nearby outlet), potential plumbing modifications, and the cost of a bidet seat adapter if you’re retrofitting an existing toilet.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Considerations

- **Power Requirements:** All smart toilets need a standard 120V electrical outlet within 3–4 feet of the toilet. If you don’t have one, hire an electrician ($150–$300). Some models (like the Bio Bidet U700) can use a battery backup for the remote, but the toilet itself requires AC power.
- **Plumbing:** Smart toilets require a standard 1/2-inch water supply line. If you’re replacing a traditional toilet, you may need a shut-off valve upgrade. TOTO and Kohler recommend professional installation for wall-hung models to ensure proper mounting and leak prevention.
- **Venting:** Most smart toilets use a built-in deodorizer fan, so no additional venting is needed. However, ensure your bathroom has adequate ventilation to prevent moisture buildup.

### Smart Home Integration

- **Voice Control:** All three models work with Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant. Common commands include: "Alexa, activate bidet," "Hey Google, warm the seat," or "Alexa, flush the toilet." Setup takes about 10 minutes via the respective app.
- **App Features:** The Kohler Konnect app is the most comprehensive, offering leak detection, usage history, and remote troubleshooting. TOTO’s app is simpler but reliable. Bio Bidet’s app is functional but occasionally lags.
- **Automation Scenarios:** Pair your smart toilet with a motion sensor to automatically open the lid when you enter the bathroom. Use IFTTT (If This Then That) to turn on the bidet when your morning alarm goes off. For advanced users, integrate with Apple HomeKit via a hub (Kohler supports HomeKit, TOTO and Bio Bidet do not).
- **Future-Proofing:** Look for models with Over-the-Air (OTA) updates. TOTO and Kohler both offer OTA firmware updates, ensuring your toilet stays current with new features and security patches.
