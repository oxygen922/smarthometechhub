# Smart Toilet Review 2026: Are They Worth It? Top Picks, Features & Buying Guide

**Category:** Smart Toilets | **Topic:** Smart Toilet Reviews & Buying Guide

## Introduction

Smart toilets have quietly become one of the most sought-after upgrades in American homes. Once a luxury reserved for high-end hotels and Japanese residences, bidet-style smart toilets are now mainstream, with major brands like Kohler, TOTO, and Bio Bidet competing for space in your bathroom. In 2026, these fixtures do far more than wash—they heat seats, dry with warm air, deodorize, self-clean, and even sync with your smart home ecosystem.

But with prices ranging from $400 to well over $10,000, choosing the right smart toilet can feel overwhelming. In this in-depth smart toilet review, we break down the top three models worth buying this year, explain the key features that actually matter, and help you decide whether a smart toilet is the right investment for your home. Whether you're remodeling or simply curious about the bidet boom, this guide covers everything you need to know before you buy.

## Top 3 Smart Toilet Recommendations

### 1. TOTO Neorest NX1 — Best Overall Smart Toilet

**Estimated Price (2026): $9,800–$12,500**

If money is no object and you want the absolute pinnacle of smart toilet technology, the TOTO Neorest NX1 is the benchmark. This flagship model combines TOTO's legendary Washlet bidet technology with a sculpted, wall-hugging design that looks like it belongs in a modern art museum.

The NX1 features TOTO's **Actilight** self-cleaning technology, which uses UV light and a titanium dioxide-coated bowl to break down waste and neutralize bacteria after every use. The **ewater+** system mists the wand and bowl with electrolyzed water, keeping things hygienic without harsh chemicals. The heated seat, warm air dryer, and adjustable bidet settings (front, rear, and oscillating) are all controlled via a sleek remote or a smartphone app.

**What we love:** The automatic lid opens as you approach thanks to a proximity sensor, the flush is virtually silent, and the tornado flush system is remarkably effective while using less water than a standard toilet. The NX1 also integrates with smart home platforms, and its nightlight and heated seat make those 3 a.m. trips surprisingly pleasant.

**Where it falls short:** The price. At nearly five figures, this is a statement purchase. Installation also requires a dedicated electrical outlet near the toilet and, ideally, a professional plumber. For most households, the NX1 is overkill—but for luxury buyers, it's unmatched.

**Verdict:** The best smart toilet money can buy in 2026. A true luxury fixture with impeccable engineering.

### 2. Kohler Numera Smart Toilet — Best Mid-Range Smart Toilet

**Estimated Price (2026): $2,800–$3,600**

The Kohler Numera hits the sweet spot between luxury and practicality. It delivers most of the premium features of the Neorest at roughly a third of the price, making it the best smart toilet for most American households.

The Numera offers a **heated seat with adjustable temperature**, a **warm air dryer**, and a **bidet with customizable water pressure and temperature**. Kohler's **Revive** self-cleaning system uses UV light to sanitize the stainless steel wand after each use. The tankless design provides a powerful, consistent flush and gives the toilet a clean, contemporary silhouette.

**What we love:** The touchscreen remote is intuitive, and the motion-activated lid opens and closes automatically. The Numera also supports **Kohler Konnect**, allowing voice control via Amazon Alexa or Google Assistant—you can literally ask your toilet to raise the lid or activate the dryer. The nightlight is a thoughtful touch, and the seat is surprisingly comfortable for extended sitting.

**Where it falls short:** The dryer is effective but slower than the TOTO's, and the app occasionally lags. Installation is still a professional job due to the electrical and plumbing requirements.

**Verdict:** The best value smart toilet for buyers who want premium features without the five-figure price tag.

### 3. Bio Bidet BB-2000 Bliss — Best Budget Smart Toilet (Bidet Seat)

**Estimated Price (2026): $550–$700**

Strictly speaking, the Bio Bidet BB-2000 is a **smart bidet seat** rather than a full smart toilet—but for budget-conscious buyers, it's the smartest way to upgrade an existing toilet. It installs on most standard two-piece toilets in under an hour and delivers a surprising number of premium features.

The BB-2000 includes a **heated seat**, **warm air dryer**, **adjustable bidet with front and rear wash**, and a **wireless remote**. It also features a **deodorizer** and a **nightlight**. The stainless steel nozzle is self-cleaning, and the unit includes a **massage mode** and **oscillating wash** for a spa-like experience.

**What we love:** The price-to-feature ratio is outstanding. You get heated water, a heated seat, and a dryer for under $700—features that cost thousands in full smart toilets. Installation is DIY-friendly for most homeowners, requiring only a standard outlet and a water supply connection.

**Where it falls short:** It's not a full toilet, so you keep your existing bowl. The dryer is weaker than premium models, and the unit adds bulk to your toilet's profile. It also lacks smart home integration.

**Verdict:** The best entry point into smart toilet technology. Perfect for renters or anyone testing the waters before committing to a full smart toilet.

## Buying Guide: Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

### Who Should Buy

- **Home remodelers:** If you're renovating a bathroom, a smart toilet is a future-proof upgrade that adds resale value and modern appeal.
- **Comfort seekers:** Anyone who appreciates a heated seat, warm wash, and dryer will wonder how they lived without one.
- **Eco-conscious households:** Smart toilets use less water than traditional models and dramatically reduce toilet paper consumption.
- **Mobility-limited individuals:** Bidet functions offer independence and improved hygiene for people with limited mobility.
- **Tech enthusiasts:** If your home is already smart, a connected toilet fits right into your ecosystem.

### Who Should Skip

- **Renters in short-term leases:** Installation requires electrical work; not ideal if you're moving soon.
- **Tight budgets:** A $30 bidet attachment gets you 50% of the experience for a fraction of the cost.
- **Households without an outlet near the toilet:** You'll need to hire an electrician, adding $200–$500 to the project.

**Pro tip:** Factor in installation costs. A full smart toilet typically requires a licensed plumber ($150–$400) and possibly an electrician ($200–$500) if no outlet exists near the toilet.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Requirements

Full smart toilets require three connections: **water supply**, **drainage**, and **electrical power**. Most American bathrooms lack an outlet near the toilet, so budget for an electrician unless you're lucky. The installation process typically takes 2–4 hours for a professional.

Bidet seats are far simpler. Most attach to an existing toilet in 30–60 minutes using basic tools. You'll still need a nearby GFCI outlet—extension cords are not recommended in bathrooms for safety reasons.

### Smart Home Integration

In 2026, smart home integration is a major selling point. Here's what leading brands offer:

- **Kohler Konnect:** Works with Amazon Alexa, Google Assistant, and Apple HomeKit. Voice commands can raise the lid, activate the bidet, or start the dryer.
- **TOTO:** The Neorest line supports app control and select smart home platforms, though integration is less open than Kohler's.
- **Bio Bidet:** Limited smart home support; primarily remote-controlled.

**Practical integration ideas:**
- Set a morning routine that warms the seat before you wake up.
- Use motion sensors to trigger the nightlight automatically.
- Monitor water usage through the app to track conservation.

**A word of caution:** Smart toilets are not without controversy. In 2025, security researchers demonstrated that some connected bidet models had unencrypted Bluetooth connections, raising privacy concerns. When buying, choose brands with a track record of firmware updates and secure app design. Always change default passwords and keep firmware current.
