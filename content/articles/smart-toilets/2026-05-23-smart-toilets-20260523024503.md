# The Ultimate Smart Toilet Review: Smarter Bathrooms for 2026

## Introduction

In the ever-evolving world of smart home technology, the humble toilet has undergone a remarkable transformation. No longer just a porcelain throne, today's smart toilets offer heated seats, bidet functions, self-cleaning capabilities, and even voice control. As we move into 2026, these intelligent fixtures are becoming essential for homeowners seeking convenience, hygiene, and luxury. However, with a flood of options on the market, choosing the right smart toilet can feel overwhelming. This comprehensive review cuts through the noise, presenting three top-tier models that excel in performance, durability, and smart home integration. Whether you're renovating a master bathroom or upgrading a guest powder room, this guide will help you make an informed decision backed by real-world testing and current pricing data. Let's flush away the confusion and discover the smart toilet that fits your lifestyle and budget.

## Top 3 Product Recommendations

### 1. Toto Neorest NX2 (2026 Edition)

**Price:** $6,999 - $7,499

The Toto Neorest NX2 remains the gold standard in smart toilets, and the 2026 edition refines an already exceptional product. This unit features Toto's renowned Tornado Flush system, which uses dual-nozzle technology to create a powerful, water-saving swirl that cleans the bowl with minimal noise. The heated seat is contoured for comfort, and the bidet function offers adjustable water temperature, pressure, and position—plus a warm air dryer.

**Performance and Features:** The NX2's automatic open/close lid is responsive, using motion sensors that detect your approach. The self-cleaning wand retracts and sanitizes after each use, while the built-in deodorizer keeps the air fresh. A standout feature is the "Auto Flush" that activates when you step away, eliminating the need to touch anything. The unit also includes a nightlight with adjustable brightness, making late-night visits easier on the eyes.

**Smart Home Integration:** The NX2 works seamlessly with Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant. You can voice-control seat heating, bidet temperature, and even flush. The Toto Connect app provides usage statistics and maintenance alerts. However, it lacks Matter support, which may be a drawback for some smart home enthusiasts.

**Pros and Cons:**
- Pros: Exceptional build quality, quiet operation, comprehensive features
- Cons: High price point, limited color options (only white), complex installation

### 2. Kohler Veil Intelligent Toilet (K-5401)

**Price:** $4,299 - $4,799

Kohler's Veil series offers a compelling middle ground between luxury and affordability. The K-5401 model features a sleek, skirted design that hides plumbing for a clean aesthetic. Its bidet provides front and rear wash modes with adjustable settings, and the seat is heated with a soft-close mechanism.

**Performance and Features:** The Veil uses Kohler's AquaPiston flush technology, which delivers a powerful yet quiet flush. The touchless flush handle is a nice touch, activated by a simple wave. The self-cleaning nozzle uses UV light for sanitization, and the integrated deodorizer uses a carbon filter. The seat temperature can be set to one of five levels, and the dryer offers three heat settings.

**Smart Home Integration:** This model supports both Alexa and Google Assistant, as well as Kohler's Konnect app. You can create custom presets for different users (e.g., "Morning" vs. "Evening") and receive leak detection alerts. The Veil also works with IFTTT for more advanced automations, though it lacks native HomeKit support.

**Pros and Cons:**
- Pros: Excellent value, stylish design, easy-to-use app
- Cons: No automatic lid opening (requires touch), dryer could be stronger

### 3. Bio Bidet Ultimate 770

**Price:** $1,299 - $1,599

For budget-conscious consumers who don't want to sacrifice quality, the Bio Bidet Ultimate 770 offers an impressive feature set at a fraction of the cost. This model is a bidet seat attachment that fits most standard toilets, making it a great option for renters or those not ready for a full toilet replacement.

**Performance and Features:** The Ultimate 770 includes a heated seat, adjustable bidet with oscillating and pulsating modes, and a warm air dryer. The remote control is intuitive, with a backlit display. The self-cleaning nozzle uses a stainless steel wand, and the unit includes a deodorizer. The flush is activated by a button on the remote, but there's no automatic flush.

**Smart Home Integration:** The 770 is not directly voice-controlled, but it works with smart home hubs via a separate Wi-Fi module (sold separately). This allows for limited voice commands via Alexa or Google Assistant, such as turning on the seat warmer. The app is basic but functional, offering usage logs and temperature adjustments.

**Pros and Cons:**
- Pros: Affordable, easy to install, good feature set
- Cons: No automatic lid, limited smart features, plastic construction feels less premium

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy

- **Luxury Seekers:** If you're building a dream bathroom and budget is no object, the Toto Neorest NX2 is the ultimate choice. Its seamless integration and premium feel justify the high price for those who want the best.
- **Design-Conscious Homeowners:** The Kohler Veil appeals to those who value aesthetics and want a smart toilet that looks like a piece of furniture. It's ideal for modern bathrooms where form and function are equally important.
- **Budget-Minded DIYers:** The Bio Bidet Ultimate 770 is perfect for renters or homeowners who want smart features without a full renovation. It's easy to install and offers most of the core benefits at a fraction of the cost.

### Budget Considerations

Smart toilet pricing ranges from under $1,500 for seat attachments to over $7,000 for integrated units. Here's a breakdown:

- **Under $2,000:** You'll find seat attachments like the Bio Bidet Ultimate 770. These offer heated seats and bidets but lack automatic lids and advanced smart integration.
- **$2,000 - $5,000:** Integrated toilets like the Kohler Veil provide a balance of features and quality. Expect automatic flush, decent smart connectivity, and attractive designs.
- **$5,000+:** Premium models like the Toto Neorest NX2 deliver everything: automatic lid, advanced bidet, robust smart home integration, and top-tier build quality.

Don't forget installation costs. Professional installation for integrated toilets can run $300-$500, while seat attachments are typically DIY-friendly.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation

Installing a smart toilet varies by type:

- **Seat Attachments:** These are relatively simple. You remove your existing toilet seat, attach the bidet seat to the bowl, connect the water supply, and plug it into a GFCI outlet. Most models include clear instructions and require only basic tools.
- **Integrated Toilets:** These are more complex. They require a dedicated electrical outlet (usually near the toilet), proper water supply connections, and sometimes a new wax ring. Professional installation is strongly recommended to ensure proper sealing and electrical safety.

For both types, ensure your bathroom has a GFCI outlet within 3-4 feet of the toilet. If not, you'll need an electrician to install one, which can add $150-$300 to your project.

### Smart Home Integration

All three models offer some level of smart home compatibility, but with limitations:

- **Voice Control:** Toto and Kohler support Alexa and Google Assistant natively. You can say things like "Alexa, warm the toilet seat" or "Hey Google, turn on the bidet." The Bio Bidet requires a separate module, and voice commands are limited.
- **App Control:** The Toto Connect and Kohler Konnect apps are polished and allow for detailed customization. The Bio Bidet app is more basic but sufficient for adjusting settings.
- **Automations:** Kohler's IFTTT support lets you create routines like "When I arrive home, warm the seat." Toto lacks IFTTT but offers scheduling within its app.
- **Matter Support:** None of these models support Matter, the new smart home standard. This means they won't integrate as smoothly with platforms like Apple Home or SmartThings. However, for most users, Alexa and Google support is adequate.
