# The Ultimate Guide to Smart Toilets: Top Picks, Features, and Buying Advice for 2026

**Introduction**

In the rapidly evolving landscape of smart home technology, few innovations blend luxury, hygiene, and convenience as seamlessly as the modern smart toilet. No longer a futuristic fantasy reserved for tech billionaires, these intelligent thrones have become accessible, feature-rich essentials for homeowners seeking to elevate their bathroom experience. From heated seats and bidet functions to self-cleaning capabilities and voice control, smart toilets are redefining personal comfort while promoting better hygiene and water efficiency. As we move through 2026, the market is saturated with options ranging from budget-friendly models to high-end units with integrated air purifiers and health monitoring. This comprehensive review will guide you through the top three smart toilets currently available, break down essential features, and help you decide which model best fits your lifestyle, budget, and smart home ecosystem. Whether you're a tech enthusiast or simply tired of cold toilet seats, this guide is your roadmap to a smarter, cleaner bathroom.

## Top 3 Product Recommendations

### 1. Toto Neorest NX2 (2026 Edition)
**Best Overall for Luxury and Performance**

Toto continues to dominate the premium smart toilet segment with the Neorest NX2, a masterpiece of Japanese engineering that sets the benchmark for 2026. This unit combines Toto's legendary Tornado Flush technology—which uses dual-nozzle cyclonic action to clean the bowl without brushes—with an upgraded eWater+ system that electrolyzes tap water into a mild disinfectant for automatic bowl cleaning between uses.

The heated seat features multi-zone temperature control, allowing you to set different warmth levels for the seat and lid. The bidet function offers oscillating, pulsating, and wide-spray patterns, all adjustable for water pressure and temperature. A new addition for 2026 is the "Gentle Wave" pre-mist, which coats the bowl before use to prevent waste adhesion.

**Smart Integration:** The NX2 connects via Wi-Fi to the Toto Smart Home app, enabling remote seat warming scheduling, usage tracking, and firmware updates. It also integrates with Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant for voice commands like "Alexa, warm the seat" or "Hey Google, activate the bidet."

**Pros:** Exceptional build quality, silent operation, self-cleaning, health monitoring (urine analysis optional), and a sleek, skirted design.
**Cons:** Very high price point ($6,500–$7,200), requires professional installation, and the app can be slow to connect.

**Price:** $6,800 (estimated 2026 MSRP)

### 2. Kohler PureWash E930
**Best Smart Toilet Seat for Existing Toilets**

For homeowners who want smart features without replacing their entire toilet, the Kohler PureWash E930 is the gold standard in bidet seats. This retrofit unit fits most two-piece toilets and transforms them into a fully featured smart toilet in under an hour. The E930 boasts a sleek, low-profile design with a heated seat, warm water bidet, and a powerful air dryer.

The standout feature is Kohler's "Quick-Release" system, which allows the seat to be removed instantly for cleaning—a godsend for maintaining hygiene. The bidet nozzle is self-cleaning with a UV sanitizer that activates after each use. Users can customize wash modes including rear, front, and oscillating, all controlled via a wireless remote with an intuitive LCD display.

**Smart Integration:** The PureWash E930 works with the Kohler Konnect app, offering scheduling, usage statistics, and integration with other Kohler smart products. It supports IFTTT for custom automations, such as turning on the bidet when a smart sensor detects motion.

**Pros:** Affordable entry point ($1,200), easy DIY installation, universal fit for most toilets, excellent warranty (3 years parts/labor), and strong water pressure.
**Cons:** Not as aesthetically seamless as an integrated toilet, the dryer can be slow, and the remote is not backlit, making night use tricky.

**Price:** $1,199 (estimated 2026 MSRP)

### 3. BioBidet Bliss BB-2000
**Best Value for Features and Performance**

BioBidet, a brand known for democratizing smart toilet technology, offers the Bliss BB-2000 as a compelling mid-range option that punches well above its weight. This integrated smart toilet (not a seat) includes a one-piece, skirted design with a powerful direct-drive pump that ensures consistent water pressure even in low-flow homes.

The BB-2000 features a heated seat with five temperature settings, a warm air dryer with adjustable speed and temperature, and a deodorizer that uses a carbon filter to neutralize odors instantly. The bidet wand offers three spray modes (rear, feminine, and pulsating) with adjustable position and pressure. A unique feature is the "Spa" mode, which delivers a gentle, aerated stream for sensitive users.

**Smart Integration:** The BB-2000 connects via Bluetooth to the BioBidet app for remote control and customization. However, it lacks Wi-Fi connectivity, meaning no voice assistant integration or remote scheduling. This is a trade-off for the lower price.

**Pros:** Excellent value ($2,800), powerful flush (1.28 GPF), easy-to-use side panel controls, slow-close seat and lid, and a sleek modern design.
**Cons:** No Wi-Fi or voice control, the app is basic, and the seat is slightly smaller than standard (may not suit larger users).

**Price:** $2,799 (estimated 2026 MSRP)

- **Water Efficiency:** Look for toilets with 1.28 GPF or less. Toto's 1.0 GPF is exceptional.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

- **Tech Enthusiasts:** If you love integrating your home with smart devices, a Wi-Fi-enabled model like the Toto Neorest NX2 is a must-have for voice control and automation.
- **Eco-Conscious Homeowners:** Smart toilets use significantly less water per flush than traditional models (1.0–1.28 GPF vs. 1.6 GPF), saving thousands of gallons annually.
- **Families with Children or Elderly:** Heated seats, bidet functions, and automatic flush features improve comfort and hygiene for all ages. The Kohler PureWash E930 is an excellent retrofit for existing bathrooms.
- **Renters or Apartment Dwellers:** A bidet seat like the Kohler E930 is ideal because it can be removed and taken with you when you move.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $1,500:** You're limited to bidet seats, not integrated toilets. The Kohler PureWash E930 is the best in this range, offering most smart features except Wi-Fi.
- **$2,000–$3,500:** This sweet spot includes integrated toilets like the BioBidet Bliss BB-2000, which offer a complete solution with good features but limited smart home integration.
- **$5,000+:** Premium models like the Toto Neorest NX2 deliver the best performance, health monitoring, and full smart home compatibility. Consider this an investment in long-term comfort and home value.

**Hidden Costs:** Professional installation for integrated toilets can cost $200–$500, depending on electrical and plumbing requirements. Factor this into your budget.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Overview

- **Bidet Seats (e.g., Kohler PureWash E930):** These are designed for DIY installation. You'll need a standard toilet with a power outlet nearby (GFCI recommended). The process involves removing the existing seat, attaching a mounting bracket, connecting a T-valve to the water supply, and plugging in the unit. Most users complete this in 30–60 minutes with basic tools (wrench, screwdriver).
- **Integrated Toilets (e.g., Toto Neorest NX2, BioBidet BB-2000):** Professional installation is strongly advised. These units require a dedicated electrical connection (often 120V/15A), a water supply line, and precise leveling. Improper installation can lead to leaks, electrical hazards, or voided warranties. Expect a plumber or electrician to take 2–4 hours.

### Smart Home Integration

- **Wi-Fi Models (Toto Neorest NX2):** Connect to your home network via the Toto Smart Home app. Once paired, you can create routines like "Good Morning" that pre-warms the seat and activates the deodorizer. Voice commands work seamlessly with Alexa and Google Assistant.
- **Bluetooth Models (BioBidet BB-2000):** Limited to proximity control via the app. No remote scheduling or voice commands. This is fine for basic customization but not true smart home integration.
- **IFTTT Support (Kohler PureWash E930):** Enables conditional automations, such as activating the bidet when a motion sensor detects movement after 10 PM. This adds a layer of convenience but requires some technical know-how.

**Pro Tip:** For full integration, ensure your smart toilet is compatible with your existing smart home hub (e.g., Samsung SmartThings, Apple HomeKit via third-party bridges). Most Wi-Fi models work with Alexa and Google Assistant natively.
