# The Smart Toilet Revolution: Why 2026 Is the Year to Ditch Your Old Throne

## Introduction

Let’s be honest: no one ever thought they’d get excited about a toilet. But here we are in 2026, and the smart toilet has quietly become the most practical—and frankly, luxurious—upgrade you can make to your bathroom. I’ve spent the last month testing three of the most advanced smart toilets on the market, from hands-free flushing to self-cleaning wands and even ambient lighting that guides you to the bowl in the dark. The results? I’m never going back to a standard commode.

Smart toilets have evolved far beyond the heated seats and bidet sprays of yesteryear. Today’s models integrate with your smart home ecosystem, offer customizable cleansing programs, and even analyze your waste for health insights. Whether you’re a tech enthusiast, a parent tired of cleaning mishaps, or someone with mobility concerns, there’s a smart toilet built for you. In this in-depth review, I’ll break down the top three contenders for 2026, compare key features, and help you decide which throne is right for your home.

## Top 3 Product Recommendations with Detailed Reviews

### 1. **Toto Neorest NX2** – The Gold Standard of Luxury

**Price:** $8,500 – $9,200 (2026 est.)

The Toto Neorest NX2 is the Rolls-Royce of smart toilets, and for good reason. Toto has been perfecting this technology for decades, and the NX2 represents the pinnacle of their engineering. This unit features their signature eWater+ system, which electrolyzes tap water into a mild disinfectant to clean the bowl and wand after every use. The result? A toilet that literally cleans itself.

**What I loved:**
- **Intelligent Flush:** The NX2 uses a dual-flush cyclone system that’s both powerful and whisper-quiet. It never clogged, even with heavy use.
- **Comfort Features:** The heated seat is adjustable from 80°F to 104°F, and the warm air dryer has three speed settings. The bidet spray offers oscillating, pulsating, and wide-angle modes.
- **Smart Home Integration:** Works with Alexa and Google Home. I set a routine where the seat preheats 15 minutes before my morning alarm. It also has a motion-activated nightlight that’s surprisingly soothing.

**The downsides:**
- **Price:** At nearly $9,000, this is an investment. It’s not for budget-conscious buyers.
- **Installation:** Requires a dedicated electrical outlet and a 1.5-inch water supply line. Retrofitting an older bathroom will add cost.

**Verdict:** If you want the absolute best and have the budget, the NX2 is unrivaled. It’s a statement piece that delivers daily comfort.

### 2. **Kohler Veil Intelligent Toilet** – Best Balance of Features and Value

**Price:** $4,200 – $5,100 (2026 est.)

Kohler’s Veil line has been a strong competitor for years, but the 2026 model brings significant upgrades. The Veil now includes a built-in UV sanitizer that kills 99.9% of bacteria on the wand, and a new “Quiet-Close” lid that dampens sound—a godsend in a shared bathroom.

**What I loved:**
- **Customizable User Profiles:** The Veil lets you save up to four user profiles with preferred seat temperature, water pressure, and spray position. Guests can use a temporary guest mode.
- **Smart Home Integration:** Native support for Apple HomeKit, plus Alexa and Google. I used Siri shortcuts to trigger a “night mode” that dims the lights and activates the bidet with a voice command.
- **Self-Cleaning Wand:** The wand extends and retracts automatically, and the UV light sanitizes it after each use. No manual cleaning needed.

**The downsides:**
- **Flush Power:** While good, it’s not as robust as the Toto. Occasional double-flush needed for solid waste.
- **Warranty:** Only one year on electronics, which feels short for a $5,000 product.

**Verdict:** The Veil is the sweet spot for most homeowners. It offers 90% of the luxury features at half the price of the Toto.

### 3. **BioBidet BB-2000** – Best Budget Smart Toilet

**Price:** $1,800 – $2,300 (2026 est.)

Don’t let the lower price fool you—the BioBidet BB-2000 is a powerhouse. This is an elongated, one-piece toilet with a sleek, modern design that rivals premium models. It’s also the easiest to install, requiring only a standard 120V outlet and a 3/8-inch water line.

**What I loved:**
- **Value for Money:** You get a heated seat, adjustable bidet, warm air dryer, and deodorizer for under $2,500. The deodorizer is a carbon filter fan that activates when you sit, eliminating odors instantly.
- **Easy DIY Installation:** The BB-2000 comes with a detailed manual and all necessary hardware. I installed it myself in about 90 minutes.
- **Smart Features:** It connects to the BioBidet app (iOS/Android) for remote control of settings. No voice assistant integration, but the app is intuitive.

**The downsides:**
- **No Self-Cleaning Wand:** You’ll need to manually clean the wand every few weeks. It’s not a dealbreaker, but it’s a chore.
- **Noise:** The bidet pump and dryer are louder than premium models. Acceptable for a budget unit.
- **Limited Health Features:** No waste analysis or UV sanitization.

**Verdict:** The BB-2000 is the best entry-level smart toilet. It delivers core comfort features without breaking the bank.

### Key Features Explained

- **Self-Cleaning Wand & Bowl:** This is the feature that separates luxury from basic. Electrolysis (Toto) or UV light (Kohler) keeps the toilet hygienic with zero effort. If you hate cleaning, prioritize this.
- **User Profiles:** Essential for households with multiple users. Customizing seat temp and spray position for each person avoids daily adjustments.
- **Smart Home Integration:** If you’re already in the Apple ecosystem, Kohler’s HomeKit support is a huge plus. Toto works with Alexa/Google but not Siri natively.
- **Flush Power:** Look for dual-flush systems (Toto, BioBidet) that use less water for liquid waste and more for solid. Gravity flush (Kohler) is quieter but less powerful.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

- **Tech Enthusiasts:** If you have a smart home with voice assistants, the Kohler Veil or Toto NX2 will integrate seamlessly. Set routines for morning and night.
- **Families with Children:** The BioBidet BB-2000’s deodorizer and heated seat are great for kids. The self-cleaning features of the Toto reduce germ spread.
- **Seniors or People with Mobility Issues:** Heated seats, bidet sprays, and automatic flushing reduce physical strain. The Kohler Veil’s remote control is large and easy to read.
- **Eco-Conscious Buyers:** Dual-flush toilets use less water than traditional models. The Toto NX2’s eWater system also eliminates the need for harsh chemical cleaners.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $2,500:** The BioBidet BB-2000 is your best bet. You’ll miss self-cleaning and premium integration, but you get core comfort.
- **$4,000 – $5,500:** The Kohler Veil offers the best value. It’s a luxury experience without the Toto price tag.
- **Over $8,000:** The Toto Neorest NX2 is for those who want the absolute best. It’s an investment in daily comfort and home resale value.

**Pro Tip:** Factor in installation costs. Professional installation for a smart toilet ranges from $200 to $800, depending on electrical and plumbing work. The BioBidet can be DIY, saving you that expense.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Basics

All smart toilets require:
1. **A nearby electrical outlet** (GFCI-protected is best). For the Toto NX2, you may need a dedicated 15-amp circuit.
2. **A standard 1.5-inch water supply line** (most models include a T-connector).
3. **A floor-mounted flange** compatible with 12-inch or 10-inch rough-in measurements (measure yours before buying).

The BioBidet BB-2000 is the only one I’d recommend for DIY. It comes with a pre-installed bidet seat that attaches to the bowl. The Toto and Kohler require professional installation due to their complex plumbing and electrical connections.

### Smart Home Integration in Practice

- **Toto NEX2:** I set an Alexa routine: “Alexa, good morning” triggers the seat to heat, the nightlight to turn off, and the deodorizer to run for 30 seconds. It works flawlessly.
- **Kohler Veil:** Apple HomeKit integration is seamless. I used Siri to say “Prepare the bathroom” before my shower, and the toilet preheated and ran a self-clean cycle.
- **BioBidet BB-2000:** No voice control, but the app lets me adjust settings from my phone. I can’t set routines, but I can tweak water pressure without getting up.

**Compatibility Note:** If you use Google Home, all three work. For Apple users, only Kohler supports HomeKit natively. Toto requires an Alexa-to-HomeKit bridge (like a Hubitat or HomeBridge).

## Conclusion & Final Verdict

After a month of daily use, I can confidently say that a smart toilet is not a gimmick—it’s a genuine quality-of-life upgrade. The heated seat alone makes cold mornings bearable, and the bidet spray reduces toilet paper use by 75%. But the real winner is the self-cleaning technology. I didn’t scrub a single bowl during testing.

**My Final Recommendations:**
- **Best Overall:** **Kohler Veil Intelligent Toilet** – It balances luxury, smart home integration, and price perfectly. The UV sanitizer and HomeKit support make it the smartest choice for tech-savvy homeowners.
- **Best Luxury:** **Toto Neorest NX2** – If money is no object, this is the ultimate. It’s built to last and offers unmatched comfort.
- **Best Budget:** **BioBidet BB-2000** – For under $2,500, you get a heated seat, bidet, and deodorizer. It’s the perfect entry point into smart toilet ownership.

**A Word of Caution:** Smart toilets are not cheap, and they’re not for renters (unless you own the unit). But if you’re remodeling your bathroom or building a new home, consider the long-term value. A smart toilet can last 15–20 years with proper care, and it adds a modern touch that buyers love.

So, go ahead—ditch the old throne. Your backside will thank you.
