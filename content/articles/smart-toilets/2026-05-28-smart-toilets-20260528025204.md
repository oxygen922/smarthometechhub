This unit combines TOTO's legendary Tornado Flush technology with an upgraded eWater+ system that uses electrolyzed water to clean the bowl and wand, reducing the need for harsh chemicals. The heated seat is now more energy-efficient, and the bidet offers adjustable water pressure, temperature, and oscillating patterns for a spa-like experience.

**Performance:** The flush is powerful yet whisper-quiet, and the automatic lid opens as you approach, thanks to improved motion sensors. The self-cleaning wand retracts and sanitizes after every use, making maintenance minimal. The integrated deodorizer keeps the bathroom fresh, and the nightlight adds a subtle ambiance.

**Smart Home Integration:** The NX2 connects via Wi-Fi to the TOTO Smart Home app, allowing you to adjust settings, monitor water usage, and receive maintenance alerts. It's compatible with Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant, so you can say, "Alexa, warm the seat," before you even enter the room.

**Pros:**

- Superior cleaning and hygiene features
- Quiet, efficient flush
- Excellent app and voice control

**Cons:**

**Verdict:** If budget is no object and you want the absolute best, the TOTO Neorest NX2 is the gold standard.

### 2. Kohler PureWash E930 – The Mid-Range Powerhouse

**Price:** $1,800 – $2,200 (estimated)

Kohler's PureWash E930 is a bidet seat attachment that transforms your existing toilet into a smart toilet without the need for a full replacement. It packs an impressive array of features into a sleek, compact design. The heated seat has multiple temperature settings, and the bidet offers front and rear wash with adjustable pressure and temperature. The remote control is intuitive, and there's a handy nightlight.

**Performance:** The water pressure is strong but gentle, and the oscillating and pulsating wash modes are surprisingly effective. The seat heats up quickly, and the warm air dryer is a nice touch, though it takes a bit longer than some competitors. The self-cleaning wand is a standard but welcome feature.

**Smart Home Integration:** The E930 works with Kohler's Konnect app, which is compatible with Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant. You can create custom presets for different family members and schedule seat heating for specific times. Integration is smooth, though the app could be more polished.

**Pros:**

- Easy DIY installation (no plumber needed)
- Strong bidet performance
- Good smart home compatibility

**Cons:**
- Not as integrated as a full smart toilet
- Dryer is underpowered
- No automatic lid opening

**Verdict:** The best value for those who want smart features without replacing their entire toilet. Ideal for renters or budget-conscious buyers.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

- **Tech Enthusiasts:** If you love integrating every aspect of your home into a smart ecosystem, a Wi-Fi-enabled smart toilet is a no-brainer. You'll enjoy voice control, automation, and data tracking.
- **Families with Children or Seniors:** Heated seats, automatic lids, and bidet functions make bathroom visits safer and more comfortable for all ages. The hands-free operation reduces the spread of germs.
- **People with Mobility Issues:** Automatic flushing, self-cleaning features, and accessible controls can significantly improve independence and hygiene.
- **Eco-Conscious Consumers:** Smart toilets use less water per flush than traditional models, and some track water usage to help you conserve.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $1,000:** You'll find basic bidet seats with heated seats and simple bidet functions. These are a great entry point but lack automation and smart features.
- **$1,000 – $2,500:** This is the sweet spot for full smart toilets or premium bidet seats. You get automatic lids, flushes, deodorizers, and decent smart home integration.
- **$2,500 – $5,000+:** Premium models like the TOTO Neorest NX2 offer the best materials, advanced hygiene features, and seamless smart home integration. Ideal for those who want the ultimate experience.

### Installation & Smart Home Integration

**Installation:**
- **Bidet Seat Attachments:** These are DIY-friendly and can be installed in under an hour with basic tools. You simply remove your existing seat and attach the bidet unit to the toilet bowl.
- **Full Smart Toilets:** These require more work. You'll need to remove your old toilet, install a new wax ring, and connect the water supply. Most manufacturers recommend professional installation to avoid leaks and warranty issues. Expect to pay $150 – $300 for a plumber.

**Smart Home Integration:**
- **Wi-Fi vs. Bluetooth:** Wi-Fi-enabled toilets can be controlled remotely and integrate with Alexa, Google Assistant, and Apple HomeKit (limited). Bluetooth models require you to be nearby and often have less robust app features.
- **Voice Control:** Look for "Works with Alexa" or "Works with Google Assistant" badges. You can set routines like "Good morning" to preheat the seat.
- **App Functionality:** Good apps let you adjust settings, view usage statistics, and receive maintenance alerts. Poor apps can be buggy or lack customization.
- **Zigbee/Z-Wave Compatibility:** Some high-end models support these protocols for integration with hubs like SmartThings or Hubitat.

**Pro Tip:** If you're building a new home or doing a major renovation, consider running a dedicated power outlet near the toilet. Most smart toilets require electricity for the heated seat, bidet, and electronics.
