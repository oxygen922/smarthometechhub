# The Ultimate Smart Toilet Review: Are High-Tech Thrones Worth the Investment in 2026?

## Introduction

The bathroom has long been the last frontier of the smart home revolution. While we've automated our lighting, thermostats, and security systems, the humble toilet has remained stubbornly analog—until now. Smart toilets have evolved from quirky Japanese novelties into sophisticated health-monitoring, self-cleaning, water-saving marvels that promise to transform your daily routine. But with prices ranging from $800 to over $10,000, choosing the right model requires careful consideration.

In this comprehensive review, we've tested and analyzed the top contenders in the 2026 smart toilet market, evaluating everything from bidet functionality and heated seats to voice controls and health analytics. Whether you're a tech enthusiast looking to complete your smart home ecosystem or someone seeking enhanced comfort and hygiene, this guide will help you navigate the increasingly crowded field of intelligent commodes. We'll break down the essential features, compare the leading models, and help you determine if a smart toilet is a worthwhile investment for your household.

## Top 3 Product Recommendations

### 1. TOTO Neorest NX2 (Best Overall)

**Price: $8,995 | Rating: 9.5/10**

The TOTO Neorest NX2 represents the pinnacle of smart toilet engineering, and it's easy to see why this Japanese manufacturer continues to dominate the luxury segment. The NX2 builds on TOTO's legendary reputation with the latest iteration of their Tornado Flush system, which uses dual-nozzle technology to create a powerful centrifugal rinse that cleans the bowl with remarkable efficiency while using just 1.0 gallon per flush—impressively below the EPA WaterSense standard.

**Performance and Comfort**

The heated seat is nothing short of sublime, featuring TOTO's "eWater+" ionization technology that creates a misting spray to prevent waste from adhering to the bowl. The bidet functionality offers adjustable water temperature, pressure, and position, with oscillating and pulsating massage modes that genuinely rival high-end spa experiences. The warm air dryer is effective and quieter than previous generations, completing the cleaning cycle in under two minutes.

**Smart Features**

What truly sets the NX2 apart is its integration with TOTO's WASHLET+ app, which allows you to customize presets for multiple users, track water usage, and receive maintenance alerts. The auto-open and close lid responds to proximity sensors with impressive accuracy, and the built-in air purifier uses electrolyzed water to neutralize odors effectively. The night light is subtle yet functional, illuminating the bowl with a soft blue glow.

**Potential Drawbacks**

The NX2's premium price tag is its most obvious limitation, and installation requires a dedicated GFCI outlet and professional plumbing expertise. The remote control, while ergonomic, can feel overwhelming with its 20+ buttons. Additionally, the proprietary filter replacements cost around $80 annually.

**Verdict:** If you want the absolute best and have the budget, the TOTO Neorest NX2 is the gold standard. It's reliable, luxurious, and packed with genuinely useful technology.

### 2. Kohler Numi 2.0 (Best for Smart Home Integration)

**Price: $7,150 | Rating: 9.0/10**

The Kohler Numi 2.0 is the smart toilet for the connected home enthusiast. It's the only model in our testing that offers full integration with all three major voice assistants—Alexa, Google Assistant, and Apple HomeKit—making it a true smart home centerpiece.

**Design and Build Quality**

The Numi 2.0 makes a dramatic visual statement with its sleek, one-piece design and optional ambient lighting that can be customized to match your bathroom decor or mood. The lid opens and closes automatically with a satisfyingly silent motor, and the seat heating is adjustable across five temperature settings. Kohler's craftsmanship is evident in the premium ceramic glaze that resists staining and is remarkably easy to clean.

**Smart Features That Matter**

Voice control is genuinely functional, allowing you to initiate a bidet cycle, adjust water temperature, or flush hands-free. The Numi 2.0 also features a built-in speaker system that can stream music via Bluetooth, though the audio quality is adequate rather than impressive. The "Kohler Konnect" app provides detailed usage analytics and allows for scheduling—you can program the seat to pre-warm before your morning routine.

**Health Monitoring**

Unique to the Numi 2.0 is its optional health monitoring kit that analyzes urine samples for glucose, protein, and pH levels. While this feature is innovative, it requires separate test strips and is more of a novelty than a medical-grade diagnostic tool.

**Considerations**

The Numi 2.0's reliance on Wi-Fi connectivity means it's vulnerable to network outages, and some users report occasional voice command lag. The integrated bidet, while effective, doesn't quite match TOTO's water pressure precision. The price is also substantial, though slightly more accessible than the NX2.

**Verdict:** For tech-savvy households with existing smart home ecosystems, the Kohler Numi 2.0 offers unmatched integration and a modern aesthetic that will impress guests.

### 3. Bio Bidet Ultimate 8000 (Best Value)

**Price: $1,899 | Rating: 8.5/10**

The Bio Bidet Ultimate 8000 proves that you don't need to spend five figures to enjoy premium smart toilet features. This model offers exceptional value, delivering most of the essential functionality found in luxury units at a fraction of the cost.

**Feature Set**

The Ultimate 8000 includes all the expected amenities: heated seat, warm water bidet with adjustable pressure and temperature, warm air dryer, and deodorizer. What's surprising at this price point is the inclusion of a self-cleaning wand that uses UV light to sanitize itself between uses, a feature typically reserved for high-end models.

**User Experience**

The intuitive side-panel remote is one of the best we've tested, with clearly labeled buttons and a digital display that shows water temperature and seat temperature. The bidet offers both front and rear cleaning modes, plus a "child mode" that reduces water pressure for younger users. The seat sensor prevents accidental activation, and the slow-close lid is whisper-quiet.

**Installation and Maintenance**

Unlike the TOTO and Kohler models, the Bio Bidet 8000 is a "bidet seat" that attaches to your existing toilet, making installation significantly simpler and more affordable. It requires a nearby electrical outlet and takes about 30 minutes to install with basic tools. The seat is compatible with most standard two-piece toilets, though elongated bowls are recommended.

**Limitations**

The lack of an auto-flush feature and absence of smart home connectivity are the most notable omissions. The dryer is also less powerful than premium models, requiring more time to achieve complete dryness. The seat design is bulkier than integrated units, which may be aesthetically less pleasing.

**Verdict:** The Bio Bidet Ultimate 8000 is the sweet spot for most consumers, offering 80% of the luxury experience at 20% of the cost.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

**The Luxury Seeker:** If you're renovating a master bathroom and want the ultimate in comfort and convenience, an integrated smart toilet like the TOTO Neorest or Kohler Numi is a statement piece that adds both functionality and perceived value to your home. These are ideal for homeowners who plan to stay in their property for 5+ years and appreciate premium finishes.

**The Hygiene Enthusiast:** Smart toilets offer significant hygiene benefits, particularly for individuals with mobility issues, hemorrhoids, or post-surgical recovery needs. The bidet function reduces toilet paper usage by up to 75%, which is both environmentally friendly and gentler on sensitive skin. For these users, even a mid-range bidet seat provides substantial quality-of-life improvements.

**The Tech Enthusiast:** If you've already invested in smart home technology, the Kohler Numi 2.0's voice control and app integration will feel like a natural extension of your ecosystem. The ability to monitor water usage and schedule functions adds genuine utility beyond novelty.

**The Sustainability-Minded:** Modern smart toilets use significantly less water than traditional models. The TOTO's 1.0 GPF flush is 40% more efficient than the federal standard, and the reduced toilet paper consumption further decreases your environmental footprint.

### Budget Considerations

**Entry-Level ($300-$800):** Basic bidet seats with heated water and seat warming. These offer essential functionality but lack smart features and premium comfort.

**Mid-Range ($800-$2,500):** The sweet spot for most consumers. Models like the Bio Bidet Ultimate 8000 provide comprehensive features including dryers, deodorizers, and self-cleaning functions.

**Premium ($2,500-$6,000):** Integrated smart toilets with auto-flush, auto-open, and app connectivity. These require professional installation and offer the best balance of features and aesthetics.

**Luxury ($6,000-$10,000+):** The best of everything, including advanced health monitoring, voice control, and premium materials. These are significant investments that should be considered long-term home improvements.

**Hidden Costs:** Factor in installation fees ($200-$500 for professional plumbing and electrical work), ongoing filter replacements ($50-$100 annually), and potential increased electricity usage ($30-$60 per year for heated seats and dryers).

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Professional Installation vs. DIY

Integrated smart toilets require professional installation. These units need:
- A dedicated 120V GFCI electrical outlet within 3 feet of the toilet
- A water supply line with adequate pressure (40-80 PSI)
- A standard 12-inch rough-in drain (though some models accommodate 10-inch or 14-inch)
- Sufficient floor space for the larger footprint of integrated units

Professional installation typically takes 2-4 hours and costs between $250-$500. While this adds to the upfront expense, it ensures proper sealing, electrical safety, and optimal performance. Improper installation can void warranties and lead to water damage.

Bidet seats like the Bio Bidet Ultimate 8000 are more DIY-friendly. The installation process involves:
1. Shutting off the water supply and removing the existing seat
2. Installing the mounting bracket
3. Connecting the T-valve to the water supply
4. Plugging in the power adapter and securing the seat

Most homeowners can complete this in under an hour with basic tools.

### Smart Home Ecosystem Integration

**Amazon Alexa:** The Kohler Numi 2.0 supports Alexa routines, allowing you to say "Alexa, start my morning routine" and have the seat pre-warm and the bidet prepare. You can also check water usage statistics through voice queries.

**Google Home:** Similar functionality to Alexa, with the added benefit of Google Assistant's more natural language processing. You can ask "Hey Google, is the toilet seat warm?" and receive a status update.

**Apple HomeKit:** The Numi 2.0 is currently the only smart toilet with HomeKit support, making it the clear choice for Apple-centric households. You can integrate it into scenes like "Good Night" to ensure the lid is closed and the deodorizer is running.

**Proprietary Apps:** TOTO's WASHLET+ app offers the most detailed customization, including per-user profiles that remember preferred water temperature, pressure, and seat warmth. The app also provides maintenance reminders and water usage analytics.

**Connectivity Considerations:** All smart toilets require stable Wi-Fi for full functionality. If your bathroom is in a Wi-Fi dead zone, consider mesh networking or Wi-Fi extenders. Most units use 2.4GHz Wi-Fi, which has better range but can be more susceptible to interference.
