# Smart Toilet Review 2026: The Ultimate Guide to Upgrading Your Bathroom

## Introduction

Smart toilets have officially moved from luxury novelty to mainstream home upgrade. In 2026, these intelligent fixtures are no longer reserved for high-end custom homes—they're increasingly common in everyday American bathrooms, and for good reason. A modern smart toilet combines a bidet, heated seat, warm-air dryer, automatic lid, and self-cleaning technology into a single streamlined unit, while also connecting to your smart home ecosystem through apps like Alexa, Google Home, and Apple HomeKit.

If you've been curious about making the switch, you're in the right place. In this in-depth smart toilet review, we'll break down what makes a great model, highlight our top three recommendations for 2026, explain the key features you should compare, and walk you through installation and smart home integration. Whether you're remodeling a master bath or simply want to upgrade your daily routine, this guide will help you choose the right smart toilet for your home and budget.

## Top 3 Smart Toilet Recommendations for 2026

After evaluating dozens of models across price points, here are the three smart toilets we recommend most highly this year.

### 1. TOTO Neorest NX1 — Best Overall Smart Toilet

**Estimated Price (2026): $8,500–$9,800**

TOTO remains the undisputed name in smart toilets, and the Neorest NX1 is the company's flagship model. It delivers an almost spa-like experience with a level of polish that few competitors can match.

**Performance and Cleaning**
The NX1 uses TOTO's signature Tornado Flush system, which creates a powerful cyclonic rinse that cleans the bowl thoroughly while using just 0.8 to 1.0 gallons per flush. The bidet function offers adjustable water temperature, pressure, and position, plus a "premist" feature that coats the bowl before use to prevent waste from sticking.

**Comfort Features**
The heated seat has multiple temperature settings, and the warm-air dryer is genuinely effective rather than an afterthought. The lid opens automatically as you approach thanks to a proximity sensor, and the unit includes a nightlight, deodorizer, and self-cleaning electrolyzed water system that sanitizes the bowl and wand after every use.

**Smart Home Integration**
The NX1 connects via the TOTO app and works with Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant. You can set user profiles, adjust settings by voice, and receive maintenance reminders. It's one of the most robust smart integrations in the category.

**The Verdict**
If budget is not a concern and you want the best smart toilet money can buy, the Neorest NX1 is the clear winner. It's expensive, but it's built to last and delivers an unmatched combination of hygiene, comfort, and technology.

### 2. Kohler Numera — Best Mid-Range Smart Toilet

**Estimated Price (2026): $3,200–$4,100**

Kohler's Numera smart toilet hits a sweet spot between premium features and a more accessible price. It's a strong choice for homeowners who want the full smart toilet experience without the flagship price tag.

**Performance and Cleaning**
The Numera uses Kohler's Revolution 360 flushing technology, which swirls water around the bowl for a thorough clean. It features a stainless steel wand with adjustable spray patterns, including a front wash for feminine hygiene, and a self-cleaning function that sanitizes the wand after each use.

**Comfort Features**
You get a heated seat, warm-air dryer, and automatic open/close lid. The seat is ergonomically contoured, and the remote control makes it easy to adjust settings without fumbling for buttons on the side of the unit.

**Smart Home Integration**
The Numera works with the Kohler Konnect app and supports Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant voice commands. You can create personalized presets for different family members, which is a nice touch for shared bathrooms.

**The Verdict**
The Kohler Numera offers about 85% of the flagship experience for roughly half the price. It's our top pick for most households that want a premium smart toilet without going over budget.

### Feature Breakdown

**Flushing Technology**
Integrated smart toilets use advanced flushing systems that are both powerful and water-efficient. Look for models that use 1.0 GPF or less. TOTO's Tornado Flush and Kohler's Revolution 360 are two of the best in the business.

**Bidet Functions**
The core of any smart toilet is its bidet. Key variables include water temperature control, pressure adjustment, nozzle position, and spray patterns. Higher-end models offer oscillating and pulsating sprays, plus a dedicated feminine wash.

**Heated Seat and Dryer**
A heated seat is one of the most appreciated features, especially in colder climates. The warm-air dryer should have adjustable temperature and be powerful enough to actually dry you off—some budget models fall short here.

**Self-Cleaning and Hygiene**
Look for self-cleaning wands, UV sterilization, and electrolyzed water systems. These reduce the need for manual cleaning and improve overall hygiene.

**Smart Home Connectivity**
If you want voice control and app-based settings, confirm that the model supports Alexa, Google Assistant, or Apple HomeKit. Not all smart toilets are equally connected.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

Smart toilets are ideal for:

- **Homeowners remodeling a bathroom** who want to add long-term value and luxury.
- **People with limited mobility** who benefit from automatic lids, heated seats, and hands-free operation.
- **Families** who want better hygiene and reduced toilet paper use.
- **Tech enthusiasts** who enjoy integrating every part of their home into a smart ecosystem.

If you're a renter or not ready for a full remodel, a bidet seat like the Bio Bidet Bliss BB-2000 is a smart alternative.

### Budget Considerations

Smart toilets range from around $1,000 for a high-end bidet seat to nearly $10,000 for a flagship integrated unit. Here's how to think about your budget:

- **Under $1,500:** Bidet seats with heated seats, warm water, and dryers. No full smart home integration.
- **$3,000–$5,000:** Mid-range integrated smart toilets with app control and voice assistant support.
- **$8,000+:** Flagship models with the best flushing, cleaning, and smart features.

Don't forget to factor in installation costs, which can range from $200 to $800 depending on whether you need electrical and plumbing work.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Basics

Installing an integrated smart toilet is more involved than swapping out a standard toilet. You'll need:

1. **A dedicated electrical outlet** near the toilet (most models require 120V).
2. **A standard water supply line**, which most homes already have.
3. **Proper clearance**—smart toilets are often slightly larger than standard models.

For integrated units, we strongly recommend hiring a licensed plumber and electrician. Bidet seats are much easier and can often be installed DIY in under an hour.

### Smart Home Integration

If smart home control matters to you, check compatibility before buying:

- **Amazon Alexa:** Works with TOTO and Kohler models for voice control.
- **Google Assistant:** Also supported by TOTO and Kohler.
- **Apple HomeKit:** Less common; verify before purchase.
- **Apps:** TOTO and Kohler offer robust apps for profiles, settings, and maintenance alerts.

Integration typically lets you adjust seat temperature, activate the bidet, and receive maintenance reminders by voice or app.
