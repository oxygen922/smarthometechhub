# The Ultimate Smart Toilet Guide for 2026: Luxury, Hygiene, and Home Automation

## Introduction

In the ever-evolving landscape of smart home technology, few innovations have transformed daily routines as profoundly as the modern smart toilet. Once considered a futuristic luxury reserved for tech billionaires and high-end hotels, smart toilets have become accessible, practical, and increasingly essential for homeowners seeking superior hygiene, water efficiency, and seamless home automation. As we move through 2026, these intelligent fixtures offer features ranging from heated seats and bidet washing to automatic lid operation, self-cleaning functions, and voice control integration with platforms like Amazon Alexa and Google Home.

The global smart toilet market has exploded, driven by growing awareness of personal hygiene, aging populations seeking bathroom accessibility, and tech-savvy homeowners who refuse to compromise on comfort. Today's smart toilets are not just about luxury—they represent a convergence of plumbing engineering, IoT connectivity, and user-centric design. Whether you're renovating your master bathroom, building a new smart home, or simply curious about upgrading your daily experience, this comprehensive guide will walk you through the top products, essential features, and buying considerations for 2026.

## Top 3 Product Recommendations

### 1. TOTO Neorest NX2 (2026 Edition)

**Price:** $7,500–$8,200 (estimated retail)

TOTO continues to dominate the premium smart toilet segment with the Neorest NX2, a masterpiece of Japanese engineering that redefines bathroom luxury. This 2026 edition builds on the legendary Neorest line with enhanced water-saving technology and improved connectivity.

**Performance:** The NX2 features TOTO's patented Tornado Flush system, which uses dual-nozzle technology to create a powerful centrifugal flush that cleans the bowl thoroughly using just 1.0 gallon per flush—significantly less than standard toilets. The heated seat warms to your preferred temperature within seconds, and the integrated bidet offers adjustable water pressure, temperature, and position. What sets this model apart is the new "Eco Wash" mode that uses 30% less water during bidet cycles while maintaining optimal cleaning.

**Smart Features:** The NX2 includes built-in Wi-Fi and Bluetooth connectivity, allowing you to control all functions via the TOTO Smart App. You can preset user profiles (up to 10), schedule automatic cleaning cycles, and receive maintenance alerts. Voice control works seamlessly with both Alexa and Google Assistant. The automatic lid opens when you approach, thanks to a motion sensor with a 5-foot range, and closes after use. The self-cleaning wand sanitizes itself before and after each use using electrolyzed water.

**Build Quality:** Ceramic construction with a Cefiontect glaze that prevents debris from adhering, making cleaning effortless. The seat is ergonomically contoured and heated, with a soft-close mechanism. Warranty: 3 years on electronics, lifetime on ceramic.

**Pros:** Exceptional water efficiency, quiet operation (under 45 dB), intuitive app interface, outstanding durability.
**Cons:** High price point, requires 120V electrical outlet near toilet, professional installation mandatory.

### 2. Kohler PureWash E930 (2026 Model)

**Price:** $3,200–$3,800 (estimated retail)

Kohler's PureWash E930 strikes an excellent balance between advanced features and reasonable cost, making it the best mid-range option for 2026. This bidet toilet seat can be retrofitted onto most standard two-piece toilets, offering flexibility for homeowners who want smart functionality without replacing their existing bowl.

**Performance:** The E930 delivers a powerful yet gentle bidet experience with five spray modes: rear wash, front wash, oscillating, pulse massage, and a new "child mode" with reduced water pressure. Water heating is instantaneous via a tankless system, ensuring consistent temperature throughout the cycle. The heated seat has six temperature settings, and the warm air dryer uses a new ceramic heating element that reaches full temperature in under three seconds.

**Smart Features:** Kohler's Konnect app allows you to create user profiles, adjust settings remotely, and monitor water usage. The toilet supports voice commands through Alexa, Google Assistant, and Apple HomeKit. A built-in night light with adjustable brightness and color temperature (warm white to cool blue) adds ambiance. The automatic open/close feature uses a radar sensor that can distinguish between a person and a pet, preventing false triggers.

**Build Quality:** The seat is made from reinforced thermoset plastic with a UV-resistant finish. The wand is stainless steel with a self-cleaning cycle that uses UV light and electrolyzed water. Warranty: 2 years on electronics, 5 years on seat components.

**Pros:** Easy retrofit installation, excellent app integration, multiple user profiles, reasonable price for feature set.
**Cons:** No built-in toilet bowl (seat only), plastic seat feels less premium than ceramic, requires GFCI outlet.

### 3. BioBidet BB-2000 Ultimate (2026 Version)

**Price:** $1,800–$2,400 (estimated retail)

BioBidet has carved a niche as the value leader in smart toilets, and the BB-2000 Ultimate continues that tradition with impressive features at a price point that won't break the bank. This model is ideal for budget-conscious buyers who still want premium functionality.

**Performance:** The BB-2000 offers a robust bidet system with adjustable water pressure (10 levels), temperature (5 levels), and nozzle position. The warm air dryer has three speed settings and uses a low-wattage ceramic heater for energy efficiency.

**Smart Features:** While not as connected as the TOTO or Kohler, the BB-2000 includes a wireless remote control with a backlit LCD screen showing water temperature, seat temperature, and dryer settings. It does not have Wi-Fi or voice control, but the remote is intuitive and can be wall-mounted. The automatic deodorizer uses a carbon filter to neutralize odors, and the self-cleaning wand uses a UV light cycle.

**Build Quality:** The seat is made from injection-molded ABS plastic with a soft-touch coating. The bowl is standard ceramic (sold separately). Warranty: 3 years on electronics, 1 year on mechanical parts.

**Pros:** Excellent value, powerful bidet performance, easy to install, reliable mechanical components.
**Cons:** No Wi-Fi or voice control, plastic seat may feel cheap, limited user profiles (only 2).

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

**Luxury Seekers:** If you're building a high-end master bathroom and budget is not a primary concern, the TOTO Neorest NX2 is the gold standard. It offers the most comprehensive feature set, best build quality, and superior water efficiency. Ideal for homeowners who want a fully integrated, seamless experience.

**Tech Enthusiasts:** The Kohler PureWash E930 is perfect for those who want extensive smart home integration. With support for Alexa, Google Assistant, and Apple HomeKit, plus a robust app, you can create automated routines—like having the seat warm up when your morning alarm goes off.

**Value-Conscious Buyers:** The BioBidet BB-2000 Ultimate is an excellent entry point. If you're new to smart toilets or have a limited budget, this model provides core features like heated seat, bidet washing, and warm air drying without the premium price tag. It's also great for renters or those who may move soon, as it can be easily removed.

**Seniors or People with Mobility Issues:** Smart toilets with automatic open/close, heated seats, and bidet functions can significantly improve bathroom accessibility. The TOTO and Kohler models are particularly well-suited due to their motion sensors and intuitive controls.

### Budget Considerations

- **Entry Level ($800–$2,000):** Basic bidet seats with heated seats and limited controls. The BioBidet BB-2000 fits here.
- **Mid-Range ($2,000–$5,000):** Feature-rich bidet seats with app control and voice integration. The Kohler PureWash E930 is a prime example.
- **Premium ($5,000–$10,000):** Fully integrated smart toilets with advanced flushing, multiple user profiles, and luxury materials. The TOTO Neorest NX2 dominates this segment.

Remember to factor in installation costs ($200–$500 for professional installation) and potential electrical work ($300–$800 if you need to add a GFCI outlet near the toilet).

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Requirements

All smart toilets and bidet seats require access to a standard 120V electrical outlet within 3–4 feet of the toilet. For integrated toilets like the TOTO Neorest NX2, a dedicated 15-amp circuit is recommended. Bidet seats like the Kohler and BioBidet can plug into existing bathroom outlets, provided they are GFCI-protected.

**Plumbing:** Most models use standard 3/8-inch compression fittings for the water supply. The TOTO Neorest NX2 requires a 1/2-inch supply line due to its higher flow rate. Always check your existing plumbing before purchasing.

**Space Considerations:** Bidet seats add about 2–3 inches to the overall height of the toilet. Integrated toilets typically have a standard height (16–17 inches to seat). Ensure your bathroom has adequate clearance (at least 30 inches in front of the toilet).

### Smart Home Integration

For those building a connected home, integration is a key differentiator:

- **TOTO Neorest NX2:** Works with Alexa and Google Assistant. You can create routines like "Alexa, start the morning wash" or have the toilet adjust settings based on time of day. The TOTO app also integrates with IFTTT for advanced automation.
- **Kohler PureWash E930:** The most versatile option, supporting Alexa, Google Assistant, and Apple HomeKit. You can use Siri to control the bidet or set up HomeKit scenes that include lighting, music, and toilet functions.
- **BioBidet BB-2000:** No smart home integration, but the wireless remote is intuitive and can be programmed for two users.

**Pro Tip:** For maximum smart home synergy, pair your smart toilet with a smart humidity sensor and exhaust fan. When the toilet detects use, the fan can automatically turn on to manage moisture and odors.
