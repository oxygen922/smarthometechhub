# The Ultimate Smart Toilet Review: Are High-Tech Thrones Worth the Investment in 2026?

## Introduction

The smart toilet has evolved from a futuristic novelty to a legitimate home upgrade, blending hygiene, comfort, and cutting-edge technology. In 2026, the market is flooded with options ranging from heated seats to self-cleaning wands, voice controls, and even health monitoring sensors. But with prices spanning from $500 to over $8,000, choosing the right smart toilet can feel overwhelming. Whether you’re building a new master bathroom or retrofitting an existing space, this in-depth review will help you navigate the landscape. We’ve tested the top contenders, analyzed key features, and considered real-world usability for the average American homeowner. From bidet functionality to smart home integration, we break down what matters most. Let’s flush away the confusion and find the perfect smart toilet for your home.

## Top 3 Smart Toilet Recommendations

### 1. TOTO Neorest NX2 – The Gold Standard

**Price Estimate (2026):** $6,500 – $7,200

**Review:** TOTO continues to dominate the luxury smart toilet segment with the Neorest NX2. This model builds on decades of Japanese engineering, offering a sleek, one-piece design with a powerful Tornado Flush system that uses dual nozzles for a thorough, quiet clean. The heated seat is contoured for comfort, and the integrated bidet features adjustable water temperature, pressure, and position. What sets the NX2 apart is its **eWater+** system, which electrolyzes tap water to create a mild disinfectant that cleans the bowl and wand between uses. The automatic open/close lid, hands-free flushing, and nightlight make it incredibly convenient. However, the lack of a remote control (it uses a side panel) can be a minor inconvenience for some users. The unit requires a dedicated electrical outlet and a water supply line, so professional installation is recommended.

**Pros:** Exceptional build quality, quiet operation, advanced self-cleaning, excellent water efficiency.
**Cons:** Very expensive, no wireless remote, limited color options (mostly white).

### 2. Kohler PureWash E930 – Best for Bidet Enthusiasts

**Price Estimate (2026):** $1,200 – $1,500 (seat only) or $2,800 – $3,200 (complete toilet)

**Review:** The Kohler PureWash E930 is a standalone bidet seat that can be added to most standard two-piece toilets, offering a more affordable path to smart toilet features. It boasts a sleek, low-profile design with a heated seat, warm air dryer, and a UV-light sanitizing wand. The intuitive remote control allows precise adjustment of water temperature, spray pattern, and oscillating massage. Kohler’s **Quick-Release** mechanism makes cleaning easy, and the seat is compatible with Kohler’s **Kohler Konnect** smart home ecosystem, allowing voice control via Amazon Alexa or Google Assistant. The E930 also includes a soft-close lid and a motion-activated nightlight. While the seat is excellent, the complete toilet version (with a matching bowl) offers a more seamless look. The unit’s installation is straightforward for DIYers, but electrical proximity is a must.

**Pros:** Excellent remote control, strong smart home integration, easy to clean, good value for features.
**Cons:** Seat-only model requires existing toilet, UV bulb replacement needed every few years, dryer can be slow.

### 3. Bio Bidet Bliss BB-2000 – Best Value for Features

**Price Estimate (2026):** $650 – $800 (seat only)

**Review:** The Bio Bidet Bliss BB-2000 is a powerhouse of features at a mid-range price point. This elongated bidet seat offers a heated seat, warm air dryer, and a stainless steel self-cleaning wand with five spray modes, including a powerful rear wash and a gentle feminine wash. The wireless remote is backlit and easy to use, even in the dark. The BB-2000 also includes an **enema** function for deeper cleaning and a **sitz bath** mode for soothing relief. Its heated seat has an energy-saving mode that learns your usage patterns. While the build quality isn’t as premium as TOTO or Kohler, it’s durable and reliable. The unit is easy to install with basic tools, but you’ll need a nearby GFCI outlet. The main downside is the lack of smart home integration—no Alexa or Google Assistant support.

**Pros:** Feature-packed for the price, powerful bidet, energy-saving mode, easy DIY installation.
**Cons:** No smart home voice control, plastic remote feels less premium, seat can be slightly less comfortable than TOTO.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

- **Luxury homeowners:** If you’re renovating a master bath and want the ultimate in comfort and hygiene, the TOTO Neorest NX2 is a statement piece that will impress guests and provide daily luxury.
- **Tech enthusiasts:** The Kohler PureWash E930, especially with its smart home integration, is perfect for those who want voice control and a connected bathroom ecosystem.
- **Budget-conscious upgraders:** The Bio Bidet Bliss BB-2000 is ideal for anyone wanting a heated seat and bidet without breaking the bank. It’s a fantastic entry point into smart toilets.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $1,000:** Focus on bidet seats (like the Bio Bidet or basic Kohler models). You get heated seats, bidet, and dryer, but often lack auto-flush or smart home features.
- **$1,000 – $3,000:** You can find complete smart toilets or premium bidet seats with remote controls, better dryers, and some smart home compatibility.
- **Over $5,000:** This is the luxury tier. Expect complete toilets with integrated design, advanced self-cleaning, premium materials, and quiet, powerful flushes.

**Don’t forget installation costs:** Professional installation for a complete toilet can add $200–$500, especially if you need electrical work.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Basics

Most smart toilets require:
- **A standard electrical outlet** (GFCI-protected) within 3–4 feet of the toilet. If you don’t have one, an electrician will need to run a new circuit.
- **A standard water supply** (1/2-inch or 3/8-inch compression fitting). Some models include a T-adapter for the bidet seat.
- **Clearance:** Bidet seats add about 1–2 inches to the seat height. Ensure your toilet bowl is compatible (elongated vs. round).

**DIY vs. Pro:** Bidet seats are generally DIY-friendly (30–60 minutes). Complete smart toilets are heavier and require precise leveling, plumbing, and electrical connections—hire a plumber.

### Smart Home Integration

- **Kohler Konnect:** Works with Alexa and Google Assistant. You can say, “Alexa, turn on the bidet” or set schedules for seat warming. It also integrates with Kohler’s DTV+ shower system.
- **TOTO:** No native smart home integration. The NX2 relies on its side panel. Some users add a third-party smart plug to control the seat heater, but it’s clunky.
- **Bio Bidet:** No voice control. The remote is infrared, not Bluetooth or Wi-Fi.

**Future-proofing:** If you want smart home integration, Kohler is the clear winner. For those who prefer simplicity, the TOTO is a standalone masterpiece.

The smart toilet market in 2026 offers something for every budget and lifestyle. The **TOTO Neorest NX2** remains the king of luxury, with unmatched build quality and self-cleaning technology.
