# The Best Smart Toilets of 2026: A Complete Buyer's Guide and Review

## Introduction

Smart toilets have quietly become one of the most transformative upgrades in the modern American bathroom. What was once a luxury reserved for high-end hotels and Japanese homes is now a mainstream smart home category, with bidet seats, integrated tankless models, and app-connected fixtures competing for space in bathrooms across the country. In 2026, the market has matured significantly: prices have dropped, installation has gotten easier, and features like heated seats, warm-air dryers, UV sterilization, and voice control are no longer exotic extras.

But with that maturity comes confusion. Do you need a full smart toilet, or is a smart bidet seat enough? Are the app features genuinely useful, or just marketing fluff? And how much should you realistically spend? In this guide, we break down the top smart toilets of 2026, explain the features that actually matter, and help you decide which model fits your home, your plumbing, and your budget.

## Top 3 Smart Toilet Recommendations for 2026

### 1. TOTO Neorest NX2 — Best Overall Smart Toilet

**Estimated Price (2026): $8,500–$9,800**

If there's a gold standard in the smart toilet world, it's still TOTO's Neorest lineup. The NX2 is the company's flagship integrated toilet, and after spending time with it, it's easy to see why it consistently tops professional rankings.

The NX2 combines a tankless, tornado-flush system with a full suite of smart features. The heated seat is genuinely comfortable, with adjustable temperature settings that respond quickly. The bidet functions—front, rear, and a oscillating "ewater+" stream—are precise and well-aimed, with pressure and position controls that feel intuitive rather than fiddly. The warm-air dryer is one of the few on the market that actually works well enough to reduce toilet paper use meaningfully.

What sets the NX2 apart is its sensor intelligence. The lid opens automatically as you approach, the flush triggers when you walk away, and the nightlight activates in low light. The included remote is well-designed, but the companion app adds scheduling, usage tracking, and energy-saving modes. TOTO's ewater+ system electrolyzes water to sanitize the bowl and wand after each use, which is a meaningful hygiene feature rather than a gimmick.

**Pros:**
- Exceptional build quality and finish
- Reliable, powerful tornado flushing
- Excellent bidet and dryer performance
- Automatic lid, flush, and nightlight
- Strong app and remote controls

**Cons:**
- Very expensive
- Requires a dedicated GFCI outlet near the toilet
- Professional installation strongly recommended

**Who it's for:** Buyers who want the best possible smart toilet and are willing to pay for it. Ideal for primary bathrooms in higher-end homes.

### 2. Kohler Numera — Best Mid-Range Integrated Smart Toilet

**Estimated Price (2026): $3,200–$3,900**

Kohler's Numera sits in the sweet spot for buyers who want a fully integrated smart toilet without TOTO's flagship pricing. It's a one-piece, tankless design with a clean, modern silhouette that fits a wide range of bathroom styles.

The Numera offers a heated seat, adjustable warm-water cleansing, and a warm-air dryer. The bidet wand self-cleans and includes UV sanitation, and the seat has a sensor that prevents accidental flushing when no one is present. The flush itself is strong and quiet, and Kohler's "Revolution" flush technology handles waste efficiently with less water than many traditional toilets.

Where the Numera stands out is its integration with the broader Kohler Konnect ecosystem. You can control settings via the Kohler Konnect app, set personalized profiles for different household members, and even connect with voice assistants for basic commands. It's not as refined as TOTO's app experience, but it's close—and at roughly half the price, that's a compelling trade-off.

**Pros:**
- Fully integrated, tankless design
- Strong value for an integrated smart toilet
- UV sanitation and self-cleaning wand
- Works with Kohler Konnect and voice assistants
- Quieter flush than many competitors

**Cons:**
- Dryer is less powerful than TOTO's
- App can be slow to sync occasionally
- Still requires professional installation

**Who it's for:** Homeowners who want a premium integrated smart toilet without flagship pricing, especially those already using Kohler smart home products.

### 3. Bio Bidet Bliss BB-2000 — Best Smart Bidet Seat (Budget-Friendly Entry)

**Estimated Price (2026): $600–$800**

Not everyone wants to replace their entire toilet—and you don't have to. The Bio Bidet Bliss BB-2000 is a smart bidet seat that attaches to your existing toilet, delivering most of the comfort features of an integrated model at a fraction of the cost.

The BB-2000 includes a heated seat, unlimited warm water, adjustable bidet and posterior washes, a warm-air dryer, and a deodorizer. The wireless remote is easy to use, and the seat includes a nightlight and a slow-close lid. Installation is straightforward for most DIYers: it typically involves removing your existing seat, mounting the bidet seat, and connecting a T-adapter to your toilet's water supply. You will still need a nearby outlet.

It's not as sleek as an integrated toilet, and the dryer is less effective than premium models. But for the price, the BB-2000 delivers an enormous upgrade in comfort and hygiene. It's the model we recommend most often to first-time smart toilet buyers.

**Pros:**
- Affordable entry into smart toilet features
- Heated seat, warm water, dryer, deodorizer
- DIY-friendly installation
- Wireless remote included

**Cons:**
- Requires an existing toilet and nearby outlet
- Bulkier look than integrated models
- Dryer performance is modest

**Who it's for:** Renters (where allowed), budget-conscious buyers, and anyone curious about smart toilets before committing to a full replacement.

## Buying Guide: Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

### Who Should Buy

**Comfort seekers:** If you deal with cold bathroom floors, cold seats, or simply want a more comfortable bathroom experience, a smart toilet or bidet seat is one of the highest-impact upgrades you can make. The heated seat alone changes the morning routine.

**People with mobility or hygiene needs:** Warm-water cleansing and hands-free flushing are genuinely helpful for older adults, people with limited mobility, or anyone managing conditions that make toilet paper uncomfortable.

**Eco-conscious households:** Bidet functions reduce toilet paper consumption significantly. Over time, that adds up—both in cost and environmental impact.

**Smart home enthusiasts:** If you already use smart speakers, smart lighting, and connected appliances, an app-connected toilet fits naturally into your ecosystem.

### Who Should Wait

**Renters with strict leases:** Integrated smart toilets require replacing the fixture, which most landlords won't allow. A bidet seat is a better compromise.

**Bathrooms without a nearby outlet:** Every smart toilet and bidet seat needs power. If there's no GFCI outlet within reach, you'll need an electrician, which adds cost and complexity.

**Tight budgets:** A $600 bidet seat is a great value. A $9,000 integrated toilet is not a budget purchase. Be honest about which category you're in.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $1,000:** Bidet seats like the Bio Bidet BB-2000 deliver most comfort features without replacing your toilet.
- **$1,000–$3,000:** Mid-range integrated smart toilets and high-end bidet seats. Good balance of features and price.
- **$3,000–$6,000:** Premium integrated models like the Kohler Numera, with full smart home integration.
- **$6,000+:** Flagship TOTO Neorest models. Best-in-class everything, at a price.

Don't forget installation costs. Professional installation for an integrated smart toilet typically runs $300–$800, plus any electrical work needed for a new outlet.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Basics

**Bidet seats:** Most DIYers can install a bidet seat in 30–60 minutes with basic tools. You'll remove your existing seat, attach a T-adapter to the toilet's water supply, mount the bidet seat, and plug it in. The main hurdle is the outlet.

**Integrated smart toilets:** These replace your entire toilet, which means removing the old fixture, setting the new one, connecting the water supply, and ensuring a GFCI outlet is within cord reach. Because of the weight and complexity, professional installation is strongly recommended. Expect a plumber to handle the fixture and an electrician if an outlet needs to be added.

### Smart Home Integration

In 2026, smart toilet integration is still evolving. TOTO's app is polished but largely self-contained. Kohler's Konnect platform is more open, with voice assistant support for basic commands and better cross-device compatibility. If you're building a cohesive smart home, Kohler currently offers the smoother experience.

That said, don't expect your toilet to become the centerpiece of your smart home. The most useful "smart" features are the ones you interact with directly—heated seat, auto flush, and personalized profiles—rather than voice commands or automation routines. Treat app and voice control as nice extras, not deciding factors.
