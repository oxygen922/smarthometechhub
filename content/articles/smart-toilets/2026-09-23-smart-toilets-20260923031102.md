### Bidet Cleansing System

The core function of any smart toilet is its bidet. Entry-level models offer basic rear wash, while premium models provide front cleansing, oscillating spray patterns, pulsating massage modes, and adjustable water pressure. Tankless water heating is essential for unlimited warm water—tank-based systems run out quickly.

### Self-Cleaning Technology

Premium smart toilets like the TOTO Neorest use UV light and photocatalytic coatings to break down bacteria and waste automatically. This reduces cleaning frequency and eliminates the need for harsh chemicals. Mid-range models typically offer automated wand cleaning, which is a good middle ground.

### Smart Home Connectivity

If you're building a connected home, look for models with Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and compatibility with Amazon Alexa or Google Assistant. Kohler's Konnect platform and TOTO's remote systems offer the most robust integration options in 2026.

### Energy Efficiency

Smart toilets consume electricity for heating water, warming seats, and running dryers. Look for Energy Star certification and energy-saving modes that reduce power consumption when the toilet isn't in use. Tankless water heaters are generally more efficient than tank-based systems.

## Installation and Smart Home Integration

### Installation Requirements

Most full smart toilets require:

1. **Dedicated electrical outlet:** A GFCI-protected outlet within 3 feet of the toilet is essential. If your bathroom lacks one, expect to pay $150–$400 for an electrician to install it.

2. **Standard plumbing connection:** Most smart toilets use a standard 12-inch rough-in, but always verify measurements before purchasing.

3. **Water supply line:** A flexible supply line is typically included, but check your existing setup.

4. **Professional installation:** While some DIYers can handle bidet seat installation, full smart toilets should be installed by a licensed plumber to ensure proper sealing and electrical safety.

### Smart Home Integration Tips

- **Voice control:** Connect your smart toilet to Alexa or Google Assistant for hands-free operation.
- **Automation routines:** Create scenes where your bathroom lights, fan, and toilet activate together when you enter.
- **Health monitoring:** Some 2026 models include sensors that track usage patterns and can alert you to potential health issues—a feature that's particularly valuable for aging-in-place applications.
- **Firmware updates:** Keep your toilet's software updated for optimal performance and security.
