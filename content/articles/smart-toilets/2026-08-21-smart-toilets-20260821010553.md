# The Ultimate Smart Toilet Review: Is 2026 the Year You Ditch Toilet Paper?

**Introduction**

For decades, the porcelain throne has remained stubbornly analog. But as we barrel deeper into the decade of the "connected home," the smart toilet has evolved from a quirky luxury item found in Tokyo hotels to a legitimate, feature-rich appliance for the modern American bathroom. In 2026, these devices are no longer just about heated seats; they are about health analytics, hands-free operation, and seamless integration with your existing smart home ecosystem.

However, with prices ranging from a modest $600 to a jaw-dropping $10,000, navigating this market can be overwhelming. Is the premium worth it? Do the health metrics actually work? And can you install one without ripping up your entire bathroom floor? In this in-depth review, we break down the current state of smart toilets, offer our top three recommendations for 2026, and provide a comprehensive buying guide to ensure you don't flush your money down the drain.

---

## Top 3 Product Recommendations for 2026

After rigorous testing of water pressure, seat ergonomics, and app reliability, we have narrowed the field to three distinct winners that cater to different budgets and needs.

### 1. The Best Overall: Kohler Numi 2.0
**Price Estimate: $9,500 - $10,500**

The Kohler Numi 2.0 remains the undisputed king of the throne. It is not just a toilet; it is a bathroom centerpiece. The 2026 iteration features a sleek, skirted design with a backlit touchscreen remote that feels like a smartphone. The standout feature is the **ambient intelligence**—it learns your usage patterns and pre-warms the seat and bidet nozzle to your exact preferences at the time of day you usually visit.

**Performance:** The bidet offers a "precision spray" that oscillates and pulses with adjustable temperature and pressure. The integrated air dryer is powerful enough to eliminate the need for toilet paper in most cases, and the "SonicAir" deodorizer neutralizes odors instantly rather than just masking them. The Numi 2.0 also includes a voice assistant that understands natural language commands like "Hey Kohler, warm the seat."

**Health Features:** It includes a built-in urinalysis system that tracks hydration levels and glucose, sending data to the Kohler Konnect app. While not a medical diagnostic tool, it provides excellent trend data for fitness enthusiasts.

**The Verdict:** If you want the absolute best, the Numi 2.0 delivers. The build quality is exceptional, with a vitreous china bowl that resists staining. However, the price is prohibitive for most, and the sheer size requires a spacious bathroom.

### 2. The Best Value: TOTO Washlet S7A
**Price Estimate: $1,200 - $1,400**

While technically a "washlet" (a seat replacement rather than a full toilet), the TOTO S7A is the smartest upgrade you can make without a full remodel. TOTO has been perfecting this technology for decades, and the S7A is the culmination of that expertise, offering features found on toilets three times the price.

**Performance:** The ACTI-LIGHT technology uses an automatic sensor to open and close the lid, and the "Premist" function sprays the bowl before use to prevent waste from sticking. The bidet spray is TOTO's signature "EWATER+" which uses electrolyzed water to break down grime. The seat is heated, and the air dryer is efficient, though slightly louder than the Kohler.

**Integration:** The S7A connects via Bluetooth to the TOTO app, allowing you to set up user profiles. This is a massive feature for households with multiple people—everyone gets their own preferred water temperature and pressure settings. However, it lacks Wi-Fi connectivity for remote control, which is a minor miss in 2026.

**The Verdict:** The S7A offers 90% of the luxury experience at 15% of the cost of the Numi. It is the sweet spot for most families looking to upgrade their existing bathroom. It requires a standard electrical outlet near the toilet, but the installation is significantly easier than a full smart toilet.

### 3. The Best Budget/Practical: Bio Bidet Ultimate U900
**Price Estimate: $800 - $950**

For those dipping their toes into the smart toilet world, the Bio Bidet Ultimate U900 offers a fully-featured experience that won't break the bank. This is a "smart toilet seat" that fits on most standard two-piece toilets, making it a versatile option for renters and homeowners alike.

**Performance:** The U900 features a "Warm Water Vortex" rinse that is surprisingly effective for the price point. It has a heated seat, a warm air dryer, and an integrated deodorizer. The remote control is intuitive, with a large LCD screen. While the spray pressure isn't as refined as the TOTO, it is more than adequate for daily use.

**Health & Smart Features:** The U900 includes a "Night Glow" feature that makes late-night trips easier on the eyes. It connects to a basic app that tracks usage frequency but lacks the advanced health metrics of the premium models.

**The Verdict:** The U900 is the best "gateway" device. It proves the comfort of a bidet without the risk of a massive installation project. It is also the only unit in our top three that is compatible with non-TOTO bowls, giving you flexibility in design.

## Key Features to Consider

When shopping for a smart toilet in 2026, you are essentially buying a computer for your bathroom. Here is what matters most.

| Feature | Why It Matters | Budget ($500-$1,500) | Mid-Range ($1,500-$5,000) | Premium ($5,000+) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Bidet Type** | The core function. Look for oscillating vs. pulsating spray. | Fixed nozzle | Retractable, multi-position | Self-cleaning, UV sterilized |
| **Heated Seat** | Non-negotiable for comfort. Look for temperature ranges. | Basic (Low/Med/High) | Dual-zone heating | Ambient intelligence (auto-adjust) |
| **Drying** | The power of the air dryer determines if you skip TP. | Weak, takes 2+ mins | Adequate | High-velocity, warm air |
| **Deodorizer** | Catches odors before they linger. | Carbon filter | Fan-based, active | SonicAir (instant neutralization) |
| **Health Monitoring** | Tracks weight, urine, or stool analysis. | None | Basic usage tracking | Urinalysis, body composition |
| **Voice Control** | Integration with Alexa/Google Home. | None | Limited (on/off) | Full command suite |
| **App Connectivity** | Allows for profiles and remote control. | Bluetooth only | Wi-Fi, basic app | Full cloud integration |
| **Water Heating** | Inline heaters provide endless hot water vs. storage tanks. | Storage tank | Inline heater | Inline heater + temperature boost |

### The "Non-Negotiables"
- **Instant Water Heating:** Avoid any model with a tank. They run out of hot water quickly and breed bacteria.
- **Soft-Close Lid:** This is a basic expectation. A slamming toilet seat is a recipe for divorce.
- **Nozzle Hygiene:** Ensure the nozzle is self-cleaning (usually with UV light or electrolyzed water) to prevent cross-contamination.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

- **The Tech Enthusiast:** If you have a smart thermostat, smart locks, and a robot vacuum, a smart toilet is the natural final frontier. The integration with Google Home and Alexa allows you to create routines like "Good Night," which lowers the seat temperature and turns on the night light.
- **The Mobility Impaired or Elderly:** The hands-free operation and heated bidet are game-changers for those with arthritis or limited mobility. The auto-open/close lids reduce bending and reaching.
- **The Hygiene-Conscious:** The bidet provides a more thorough clean than toilet paper. For those with hemorrhoids or IBS, the gentle washing is not just a luxury; it is a medical relief tool.
- **The "Idealist":** If you want to reduce your environmental footprint, eliminating toilet paper saves trees and water (it takes 37 gallons of water to make one roll of TP).

### Who Should NOT Buy?

- **The Minimalist:** If you hate charging devices and updating firmware, the complexity of these toilets will annoy you.
- **Small Bathroom Owners:** Full smart toilets (like the Numi) are bulky. If your bathroom is under 30 square feet, stick to a washlet seat.
- **Budget-Conscious Renters:** Unless you own the property, modifying the plumbing and electrical for a full unit is a bad investment. Opt for a plug-in seat instead.

### Budget Considerations for 2026

- **Under $1,000:** You are buying a seat, not a toilet. Stick to brands like Bio Bidet or Brondell. Expect a good bidet and a heated seat, but skip the health tracking.
- **$1,000 - $3,000:** This is the "sweet spot." You can get a TOTO Washlet or a budget integrated toilet (like the Woodbridge T-0019). These offer excellent cleaning performance and app connectivity.
- **$3,000+:** You are paying for design and health data. The Kohler Numi and the Duravit SensoWash are in this range. You are buying a statement piece as much as a toilet.
- **Hidden Costs:** Remember to budget for installation ($200-$500) and potentially an electrician ($150-$300) to install a GFCI outlet near the bowl.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation: The DIY Reality Check

**Full Smart Toilets:** This is not a DIY project for most. These units require a dedicated 120V electrical line and a specific rough-in distance (usually 12 inches from the wall to the drain center). If you are replacing a standard toilet, you may need to move the plumbing. We strongly recommend hiring a licensed plumber. A botched installation voids the warranty and risks water damage to your subfloor.

**Washlet Seats:** This is a manageable Saturday afternoon project. You remove the existing seat, attach a mounting bracket, and snap the washlet into place. You then connect a T-valve to your water supply line. The only tricky part is ensuring you have an electrical outlet within 3 feet of the toilet. If you don't, you will need an extension cord (not ideal) or an electrician.

### The Smart Home Ecosystem

In 2026, the best smart toilets are not just "connected"; they are "ambient."

- **Amazon Alexa & Google Home:** Use voice commands to flush, open the lid, and activate the bidet. *Note: Be careful with voice commands if you have guests—saying "Alexa, flush the toilet" while someone is mid-use can be a shocking experience.*
- **Home Assistant/Apple HomeKit:** The Kohler Numi 2.0 supports HomeKit, allowing you to integrate the toilet into "Scenes." For example, when you unlock your front door at night, the toilet seat can pre-warm.
- **Routines:** The best feature is "Away Mode." When you leave the house, the toilet automatically lowers the lid and activates a UV sanitization cycle.

**The "Health Data" Caveat:** While the 2026 models offer urinalysis, this data is stored locally on the app. However, privacy concerns remain. Ensure you read the privacy policy—some brands anonymize and sell non-identifiable data to health research firms. If you are uncomfortable with that, disable the cloud sync.
