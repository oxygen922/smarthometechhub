# The Ultimate Smart Bidet Seat Review: Transform Your Bathroom in 2026

## Introduction

In the rapidly evolving world of smart home technology, few upgrades deliver as much daily impact as a high-quality bidet seat. While smart toilets have dominated headlines with their integrated designs and eye-watering price tags, bidet seats offer a more accessible entry point into bathroom luxury—without requiring a full toilet replacement. As we navigate 2026, the bidet seat market has matured significantly, with manufacturers now offering features that were once reserved for flagship smart toilets. From heated seats and customizable wash patterns to smart home integration and energy-efficient operation, today’s bidet seats are smarter, more comfortable, and more affordable than ever. Whether you’re a tech enthusiast looking to complete your smart bathroom ecosystem or a homeowner seeking enhanced hygiene and comfort, this comprehensive review will guide you through the best options available. We’ve tested the top contenders, analyzed key features, and considered real-world usability to help you make an informed decision. Let’s dive into the world of smart bidet seats and discover which model deserves a place in your home.

## Top 3 Product Recommendations with Detailed Reviews

### 1. Toto S550e Washlet+ – The Premium Powerhouse

**Price: $899 (2026 MSRP)**

The Toto S550e Washlet+ remains the gold standard in bidet seats, and for good reason. This is not your average bathroom accessory—it’s a fully integrated smart device that redefines what a bidet seat can do. The S550e features Toto’s patented EWATER+ system, which uses electrolyzed water to clean the nozzle and bowl before and after each use, ensuring exceptional hygiene without harsh chemicals.

**Performance and Comfort**: The heated seat is genuinely luxurious, with temperature settings that adjust seamlessly to your preference. The oscillating and pulsating wash modes provide a thorough yet gentle cleaning experience, while the warm air dryer rivals many standalone units. The remote control is intuitive, with a backlit display that’s easy to read even in low light.

**Smart Home Integration**: This is where the S550e truly shines. It connects via Wi-Fi to the Toto Smart App, allowing you to customize wash patterns, set schedules, and monitor water usage. It’s compatible with Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant, so you can start a wash cycle with a simple voice command. The “Night Light” feature with motion sensor activation is a thoughtful touch for midnight visits.

**Pros**: Exceptional build quality, superior hygiene features, comprehensive smart home integration, excellent warranty support.

**Cons**: High price point, requires a nearby power outlet, and the app can be occasionally finicky with firmware updates.

**Verdict**: If budget is no object, the Toto S550e is the undisputed king of bidet seats. It’s a long-term investment in bathroom luxury that will pay dividends in comfort and hygiene.

### 2. Bio Bidet 770 – The Best Value Smart Bidet Seat

**Price: $449 (2026 MSRP)**

Bio Bidet has carved a niche for itself by offering premium features at mid-range prices, and the 770 model is their current flagship. It strikes an excellent balance between functionality and affordability, making it the ideal choice for most households.

**Performance and Comfort**: The 770 offers a heated seat with five temperature levels, a front and rear wash with adjustable pressure and temperature, and a warm air dryer that actually works well. The “Smart Seat” sensor automatically activates the wash when you sit down, and the deodorizer effectively neutralizes odors. The remote control is simple and straightforward, perfect for users who prefer not to fiddle with apps.

**Smart Home Integration**: While not as deeply integrated as the Toto, the 770 does offer basic Wi-Fi connectivity through the Bio Bidet app. You can adjust settings, monitor usage, and receive maintenance alerts. It lacks voice assistant support, which may be a dealbreaker for some, but the physical remote is so well-designed that most users won’t miss it.

**Pros**: Excellent value for money, reliable performance, easy installation, good customer support.

**Cons**: No voice control, plastic build feels slightly less premium than Toto, app is basic.

**Verdict**: The Bio Bidet 770 is the smart choice for buyers who want genuine smart features without breaking the bank. It’s reliable, comfortable, and represents the sweet spot in the current market.

### 3. Kohler Novita BH-90E – The Sleek Minimalist

**Price: $699 (2026 MSRP)**

Kohler’s Novita line has always emphasized design, and the BH-90E is no exception. With its ultra-slim profile and minimalist aesthetic, this bidet seat looks like it belongs in a modern spa rather than a standard bathroom. But don’t let its good looks fool you—it’s packed with smart technology.

**Performance and Comfort**: The BH-90E features a unique “Turbowash” system that delivers a powerful yet precise stream. The heated seat is exceptionally comfortable, with memory foam-like padding. The warm air dryer is among the fastest we’ve tested, often drying completely in under two minutes. The remote is sleek and magnetic, attaching to the wall or a metal plate included in the box.

**Smart Home Integration**: Kohler’s Konnect app is one of the best in the industry. It supports Amazon Alexa, Google Assistant, and even Apple HomeKit (a rarity in this category). You can create custom profiles for different family members, schedule automatic cleaning cycles, and receive real-time water usage data. The BH-90E also features a built-in occupancy sensor that can trigger other smart home actions, like turning on bathroom lights or exhaust fans.

**Pros**: Stunning design, excellent app and smart home integration, fast dryer, comfortable seat.

**Cons**: Higher price than Bio Bidet, installation is slightly more complex, limited color options.

**Verdict**: The Kohler Novita BH-90E is perfect for design-conscious buyers who want a bidet seat that blends seamlessly into a smart home ecosystem. It’s a worthy competitor to the Toto, especially for Apple HomeKit users.

### Important Note on Electrical Requirements

All smart bidet seats require a nearby grounded electrical outlet. If your bathroom lacks one, budget for an electrician to install a GFCI outlet (typically $150–$300). Some models offer battery-powered options, but they lack the full feature set.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Process

Installing a bidet seat is a DIY-friendly project that most homeowners can complete in under an hour. Here’s what to expect:

1. **Turn off water supply** and flush the toilet to empty the tank.
2. **Remove the existing toilet seat** and clean the mounting area.
3. **Install the mounting plate** that comes with the bidet seat.
4. **Connect the T-adapter** to the water supply line—this splits water between the toilet and bidet.
5. **Attach the bidet seat** to the mounting plate and connect the water hose.
6. **Plug in the power cord** and turn on the water supply.
7. **Test all functions** using the remote or app.

**Pro Tip**: Watch a manufacturer’s installation video before starting—it saves time and prevents mistakes.

### Smart Home Integration

For a truly connected bathroom, consider these integration tips:

- **Voice Commands**: With the Kohler or Toto, you can say “Hey Google, start the bidet” or “Alexa, warm the seat.”
- **Automation**: Use the Kohler’s occupancy sensor to trigger bathroom lights, exhaust fans, or even a smart speaker to play music.
- **Energy Management**: All three models have schedules—set the seat to warm up 15 minutes before your usual wake-up time.
- **Maintenance Alerts**: Apps will notify you when it’s time to clean the nozzle or replace filters.

**Compatibility Note**: The Toto and Kohler work with most smart home hubs, but the Bio Bidet 770 requires its own app and lacks third-party integration.
