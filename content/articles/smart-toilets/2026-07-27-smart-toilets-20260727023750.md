# The Ultimate Guide to Smart Toilets: 2026's Best High-Tech Thrones Reviewed

## Introduction

Imagine a toilet that knows your preferences before you sit down, warms your seat on a chilly morning, and even analyzes your health. Welcome to the world of smart toilets—the bathroom revolution that combines luxury, hygiene, and cutting-edge technology. Once considered a novelty reserved for tech billionaires, smart toilets have evolved into accessible, feature-rich fixtures that can transform any modern home. From self-cleaning nozzles to integrated bidets, heated seats, and voice controls, today's smart toilets offer more than just a flush. They promise a personalized, hands-free experience that reduces waste, improves sanitation, and even tracks wellness metrics. As we move through 2026, the market is flooded with options ranging from budget-friendly models to premium, all-in-one units. In this comprehensive review, I'll break down the top three smart toilets worth your investment, explore critical features to consider, and help you decide which high-tech throne deserves a spot in your home. Whether you're a tech enthusiast, a hygiene fanatic, or someone seeking comfort, this guide has you covered.

## Top 3 Product Recommendations

### 1. TOTO Neorest NX2 – The Gold Standard

**Price:** $6,500–$7,200 (2026 estimate)

**Overview:** TOTO's Neorest line has dominated the premium smart toilet market for years, and the NX2 continues that legacy. This is the Rolls-Royce of smart toilets—built for those who demand the absolute best in performance, design, and technology.

**Key Features:**
- **EWater+ Technology:** Uses electrolyzed water to clean the bowl and nozzle, eliminating 99.9% of bacteria without harsh chemicals.
- **Premist System:** Mists the bowl before use to prevent waste from sticking, reducing cleaning frequency.
- **Actilight UV:** UV light disinfects the nozzle after each use.
- **Automatic Flush:** Sensor-based flush that detects when you stand up.
- **Heated Seat with Temperature Control:** Adjustable from 85°F to 104°F.
- **Washlet with Oscillating and Pulsating Modes:** Customizable water pressure, temperature, and spray pattern.
- **Warm Air Dryer:** Adjustable temperature and speed.
- **Deodorizing System:** Carbon filter removes odors immediately.
- **Quiet Flush Technology:** Only 1.0 GPF (gallons per flush) with powerful siphon jet action.

**Performance:** The NX2 is whisper-quiet. The flush is powerful yet uses minimal water—a win for eco-conscious users. The bidet functions are precise, with multiple spray patterns that cater to both men and women. The heated seat is a game-changer in winter, and the dryer actually works (unlike many competitors). The self-cleaning features mean you'll rarely need to scrub the bowl.

**Pros:** Exceptional build quality; superior hygiene features; quiet operation; customizable bidet settings; long warranty (typically 3 years on electronics).
**Cons:** Extremely expensive; requires professional installation; large footprint may not fit small bathrooms; no smart home voice control (yet).

**Verdict:** If budget is no object, the TOTO Neorest NX2 is the best smart toilet money can buy. It's an investment in luxury and hygiene that will last for years.

### 2. Kohler Veil Intelligent Toilet – The Design Marvel

**Price:** $4,800–$5,500 (2026 estimate)

**Overview:** Kohler's Veil series blends minimalist design with robust smart features. It's a close competitor to TOTO, offering a sleek, modern aesthetic that fits seamlessly into contemporary bathrooms.

**Key Features:**
- **Kohler Konnect App:** Control all functions via smartphone—seat temperature, bidet settings, flush, and nightlight.
- **Voice Control:** Compatible with Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant for hands-free operation.
- **Self-Cleaning Wand:** UV light and electrolyzed water sanitize the bidet wand.
- **Automatic Open/Close Lid:** Motion sensor detects approach.
- **Heated Seat:** Multi-level temperature settings.
- **Bidet with Front and Rear Wash:** Adjustable pressure, temperature, and position.
- **Warm Air Dryer:** Three-speed dryer with temperature control.
- **Deodorizer:** Carbon filter activated by seat sensor.
- **Dual-Flush:** 0.8/1.28 GPF for water efficiency.
- **Nightlight:** Soft LED glow for late-night visits.

**Performance:** The Veil excels in connectivity. The app is intuitive, allowing you to create custom profiles for different family members. Voice control works well with Alexa—just say "Alexa, warm the toilet seat" and it responds. The flush is efficient, though slightly louder than the TOTO. The bidet spray is comfortable, and the dryer is effective but takes a bit longer than the NX2.

**Pros:** Excellent smart home integration; beautiful design; easy-to-use app; voice control; good water efficiency.
**Cons:** Expensive; occasional connectivity issues with Wi-Fi; dryer could be more powerful; larger than standard toilets.

**Verdict:** The Kohler Veil is perfect for tech-savvy homeowners who want a smart toilet that integrates with their existing smart home ecosystem. It's slightly more affordable than the TOTO while offering comparable core features.

### 3. Bio Bidet Ultimate 770 – The Best Value

**Price:** $1,200–$1,500 (2026 estimate)

**Overview:** For those who want smart toilet features without breaking the bank, the Bio Bidet Ultimate 770 offers an impressive array of functions at a fraction of the premium brands' prices. It's a "smart bidet seat" that attaches to most standard toilets, but it's so feature-rich it deserves a spot on this list.

**Key Features:**
- **Heated Seat:** Adjustable from 86°F to 104°F.
- **Bidet with Enema Mode:** Front and rear wash with oscillating and pulsating options.
- **Warm Air Dryer:** Three temperature levels.
- **Deodorizer:** Built-in carbon filter.
- **Remote Control:** Wireless remote with LCD display for easy operation.
- **Self-Cleaning Nozzle:** Stainless steel nozzle with UV sterilization.
- **User Presets:** Memory for two users (seat temp, water temp, pressure, nozzle position).
- **Instant Water Heating:** No tank—continuous warm water.
- **Slow-Close Seat:** Prevents slamming.

**Performance:** The Ultimate 770 punches above its weight. The heated seat is just as comfortable as premium models. The bidet offers excellent pressure and temperature control, and the enema mode is a unique feature for deep cleaning. The remote is intuitive, though it takes some getting used to. The dryer is adequate but not as powerful as the TOTO or Kohler. Installation is DIY-friendly—it took me about 30 minutes.

**Pros:** Affordable; easy installation; excellent bidet performance; user presets; good warranty (5 years on seat, 2 on electronics).
**Cons:** Not a complete toilet (requires existing toilet); plastic build feels less premium; no automatic lid; remote can be lost easily.

**Verdict:** The Bio Bidet Ultimate 770 is the best value smart bidet seat on the market. It's ideal for renters or homeowners who want smart features without replacing their entire toilet.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

- **Tech Enthusiasts:** If you love smart home gadgets, a connected toilet with app control and voice commands is a natural addition.
- **Hygiene-Conscious Users:** Smart toilets eliminate the need for toilet paper and reduce contact with surfaces, ideal for those with OCD or germophobia.
- **Elderly or Mobility-Impaired Individuals:** Heated seats, automatic open/close, and bidet functions make bathroom visits safer and more comfortable.
- **Families with Children:** User presets allow each family member to have personalized settings.
- **Eco-Conscious Consumers:** Low-flow flushes and reduced paper waste appeal to those looking to minimize environmental impact.

### Who Should Skip?

- **Budget-Conscious Buyers:** Even the "value" options cost over $1,000. Standard toilets with separate bidet seats are cheaper.
- **Renters:** Unless you have permission to modify plumbing, a smart toilet seat (like Bio Bidet) is a better choice.
- **Small Bathrooms:** Many smart toilets are larger than standard models. Measure your space carefully.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $1,000:** Look for smart bidet seats (e.g., Bio Bidet 770, Tushy Ace). These attach to existing toilets and offer heated seats, bidets, and dryers.
- **$1,000–$3,000:** Mid-range smart toilets from brands like American Standard or WoodBridge offer basic features but may lack premium materials.
- **$4,000–$7,000+:** Premium models from TOTO, Kohler, and Duravit deliver the best performance, durability, and smart home integration.

**2026 Pricing Trends:** Expect prices to remain stable or slightly decrease as competition grows. However, tariffs on imported components (especially from Japan and Germany) could push prices up 5–10% by late 2026.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation

**Professional Installation (Recommended for Full Units):**
- **Cost:** $200–$500 depending on complexity.
- **What's involved:** Removing old toilet, installing new flange if needed, connecting water line and electrical outlet (most smart toilets require a GFCI outlet nearby).
- **Time:** 2–4 hours.

**DIY Installation (Bidet Seats Only):**
- **Difficulty:** Moderate. Requires basic tools (wrench, screwdriver).
- **Steps:** Attach mounting bracket, connect water supply (T-valve), plug into outlet.
- **Time:** 30–60 minutes.

**Important:** Always check your bathroom's electrical setup. Many older homes lack an outlet near the toilet. You may need an electrician to install one.

### Smart Home Integration

- **Kohler Konnect:** Works with Alexa and Google Assistant. You can create routines like "Good Morning" to warm the seat and dim the nightlight.
- **TOTO:** Currently limited to basic remote control. No native voice integration, but third-party hubs (like SmartThings) can bridge some functions.
- **Bio Bidet:** No smart home integration. All controls are via the included remote.

**Pro Tip:** If smart home integration is critical, go with Kohler. For maximum hygiene and build quality, TOTO is still king.
