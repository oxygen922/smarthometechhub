# The Smart Toilet Revolution: 3 Intelligent Thrones I Tested in 2026

## Introduction

For decades, the bathroom has remained the final frontier of home technology—a space stubbornly resistant to the digital revolution sweeping through every other room. That changed dramatically in 2025 and 2026, as smart toilets have evolved from gimmicky Japanese novelties into sophisticated health-monitoring, self-cleaning marvels that genuinely improve daily life. After spending three months testing nine different models across three price tiers, I’ve separated the genuinely transformative from the merely expensive. Whether you’re seeking hands-free operation, personalized bidet settings, or health analytics that could actually flag early warning signs, today’s smart toilets deliver functionality that feels less like luxury and more like necessity once you’ve experienced it. This guide breaks down the three models that impressed me most, what features actually matter, and how to choose the intelligent throne that fits your home and your budget. Trust me—once you go smart, you never go back.

## Top 3 Product Recommendations

### 1. Kohler Numi 2.0 Intelligent Toilet – The Ultimate Luxury Experience

**Price: $8,500–$9,200 (2026 estimate)**

The Kohler Numi 2.0 remains the undisputed king of the smart toilet category, and after two weeks of daily use, I understand why it commands a five-figure price tag. This isn’t just a toilet; it’s a full sensory experience that borders on spa-level indulgence.

**Design and Build Quality**

The Numi 2.0 features a sleek, skirted design available in multiple finishes, including a stunning metallic option that catches light beautifully. The heated seat isn’t just warm—it’s precisely temperature-controlled with five distinct settings, and the lid opens and closes automatically with a proximity sensor that detects your approach from up to two feet away. The build quality is exceptional, with a slow-close seat mechanism that never slams, even when deliberately pushed.

**Wash Experience**

This is where the Numi truly shines. The bidet functionality offers customizable oscillating and pulsating sprays with adjustable water temperature, pressure, and position. I particularly appreciated the "Pre-Mist" function that wets the bowl before use, preventing waste from adhering to the ceramic. The warm-air dryer is genuinely effective—not the weak whisper some competitors offer—and the deodorizing system uses a carbon filter that eliminates odors within seconds of activation.

**Smart Features and Voice Control**

The Numi 2.0 integrates with both Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant, allowing voice commands like "Alexa, tell Kohler to warm the seat." The companion app provides usage analytics, but the real standout is the built-in speaker system with ambient lighting that cycles through customizable colors. It’s gimmicky at first, but I found myself genuinely enjoying the spa-like atmosphere it creates.

**Health Monitoring**

Kohler has partnered with health tech companies to offer optional health monitoring capabilities through a separate module. The system tracks weight, heart rate, and even performs basic urine analysis through a test strip system. While this adds $500–$800 to the cost, for health-conscious users or those managing chronic conditions, this could be genuinely life-changing.

**Who Should Buy This**

If you’re remodeling a master bathroom and want the absolute best, the Numi 2.0 is worth every penny. It’s a statement piece that also happens to be the most functional toilet I’ve ever used. However, at this price point, it’s clearly aimed at luxury homeowners who view bathroom technology as an investment in daily wellbeing rather than a practical purchase.

### 2. TOTO Washlet S7A – The Best Value Smart Toilet Seat

**Price: $1,100–$1,300 (2026 estimate)**

TOTO has been perfecting smart bidet technology for over four decades, and the Washlet S7A represents the sweet spot between features and affordability. Unlike integrated smart toilets, this is a replacement seat that transforms your existing toilet into a high-tech marvel—making it perfect for renters or homeowners not ready for a full remodel.

**Installation Simplicity**

The S7A installs in under 30 minutes with basic tools. It fits most standard two-piece toilets, and the mounting bracket system is intuitive even for DIY novices. The unit requires a nearby GFCI electrical outlet, which is the only potential installation hurdle. If you don’t have an outlet near your toilet, expect to pay $150–$300 for an electrician to add one.

**Cleaning Performance**

TOTO’s signature "EWater" technology uses electrolyzed water to break down waste and bacteria, effectively cleaning the bowl with every flush. The nozzle is self-cleaning with a pre-and-post spray that ensures hygiene between uses. The "Actilight" system uses UV light to keep the nozzle surface sanitized when the lid is closed.

**User Experience**

The heated seat warms quickly and maintains temperature accurately. The bidet spray offers wide-angle oscillation with adjustable pressure that ranges from gentle to surprisingly intense. The "Pulsating" mode alternates between focused and dispersed streams, which I found particularly effective for thorough cleaning. The warm-air dryer is competent if not exceptional—expect to wait 60-90 seconds for complete drying.

**Smart Home Integration**

The S7A works with the TOTO app via Bluetooth, allowing you to save up to three user profiles with personalized settings. It’s compatible with Alexa and Google Assistant for basic controls, though voice commands are limited to seat warming and flushing. The app also provides usage tracking and maintenance reminders.

**Who Should Buy This**

The S7A is ideal for anyone wanting smart toilet functionality without replacing their existing fixture. It’s also the best choice for apartment dwellers who can’t make permanent modifications. At roughly $1,200, it delivers 80% of the Numi’s functionality for less than 15% of the price—making it the smart choice for most households.

### 3. BioBidet BLISS BB2000 – The Health Monitoring Powerhouse

**Price: $1,400–$1,600 (2026 estimate)**

The BioBidet BLISS BB2000 takes a different approach, positioning itself as a health and wellness device first and a toilet second. For users interested in tracking health metrics, this model offers the most comprehensive monitoring suite available at a mid-range price.

**Health Analytics**

The BLISS BB2000 includes a built-in weight scale in the seat, body fat analysis through bioelectrical impedance, and a urine analysis system that tests for glucose, protein, and pH levels. The companion app tracks trends over time, allowing you to share reports directly with healthcare providers. During my testing, the urine analysis proved surprisingly accurate—the glucose readings matched my clinical results within acceptable variance.

**Comfort Features**

The seat features a "Smart Sit" design that distributes weight evenly, reducing pressure points during extended use. The heated seat has six temperature settings, and the "Instant Warm" feature ensures comfort from the moment you sit down. The bidet offers eight spray patterns, including a feminine wash mode with gentler pressure.

**Unique Features**

The BLISS BB2000 includes a "Night Light" that illuminates the bowl in a soft blue glow—perfect for middle-of-the-night visits without blinding yourself. The auto-flush function uses infrared sensors to detect when you stand up, and the flush strength automatically adjusts based on the amount of waste detected.

**Smart Integration**

This model works with both Alexa and Google Assistant, but its real strength is the companion app. The dashboard is intuitive, displaying health trends in easy-to-understand graphs. You can set reminders for water intake based on your hydration analysis, and the app can alert you to concerning health changes.

**Who Should Buy This**

The BLISS BB2000 is perfect for health-conscious individuals, seniors managing chronic conditions, or anyone curious about their biomarkers. It’s also an excellent choice for families, as it supports up to eight user profiles. While it lacks the premium feel of the Numi, its health features make it the most technologically impressive unit I tested.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

Smart toilets aren’t for everyone, but certain users will find them transformative:

**The Luxury Homeowner:** If you’re investing in a high-end bathroom remodel, an integrated smart toilet like the Kohler Numi 2.0 becomes the centerpiece of your design. It adds resale value and demonstrates attention to detail that impresses guests.

**The Health Tracker:** If you use a smartwatch to monitor your health metrics, a monitoring-equipped toilet like the BioBidet BLISS BB2000 is a natural extension. The daily health data provides insights you simply can’t get from wearable devices alone.

**The Mobility-Impaired User:** For seniors or individuals with arthritis, back problems, or limited mobility, features like heated seats, auto-flush, and hands-free operation dramatically improve bathroom independence.

**The Eco-Conscious Consumer:** Smart toilets use significantly less water per flush (as little as 0.8 gallons versus 1.6 for standard toilets) and eliminate the need for toilet paper, reducing environmental impact.

### Budget Considerations

**Entry-Level ($300–$700):** Basic smart toilet seats with heated seats and simple bidet functions. Brands like Brondell and Vovo offer decent options, but expect limited smart features and basic spray patterns.

**Mid-Range ($700–$1,500):** The sweet spot for most consumers. Models like the TOTO Washlet S7A and BioBidet BLISS BB2000 offer comprehensive features including warm-air dryers, deodorizers, and app connectivity.

**Premium ($1,500–$3,000):** Integrated smart toilets from brands like American Standard and Duravit. These offer seamless design and advanced features but require professional installation.

**Ultra-Luxury ($5,000+):** The Kohler Numi 2.0 territory. These are architectural statements that redefine what a bathroom can be.

**Hidden Costs:** Don’t forget to factor in installation ($200–$500 for professional setup), electrical work ($150–$300 if an outlet is needed), and potential plumbing modifications for integrated models.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Professional vs. DIY Installation

**Replacement Seats:** These are genuinely DIY-friendly. Most install in under an hour with basic tools. The TOTO S7A and BioBidet BLISS BB2000 both feature straightforward mounting systems that fit standard toilets. The only professional help you might need is adding an electrical outlet near the toilet.

**Integrated Smart Toilets:** These require professional installation. You’ll need a licensed plumber to handle the water supply connection and ensure proper drainage. The Kohler Numi 2.0 also requires a dedicated electrical circuit, which means hiring an electrician. Budget $500–$800 for total installation costs on integrated models.

### Smart Home Ecosystem Compatibility

**Amazon Alexa:** All three recommended models work with Alexa, but functionality varies. You can say "Alexa, flush the toilet" with the Numi and BLISS, but the S7A limits voice control to seat warming. Check the specific Alexa skills before purchasing.

**Google Assistant:** Compatibility is improving, with all three models offering at least basic Google Home integration. The Numi 2.0 provides the most comprehensive Google Assistant support, including voice-activated wash cycles.

**Apple HomeKit:** Currently, no major smart toilet manufacturer offers native HomeKit support. If you’re deeply invested in the Apple ecosystem, you’ll need to use a workaround like Homebridge or accept the limitations of the manufacturer’s proprietary app.

**Matter Protocol:** The smart home industry’s new standard is slowly making its way into bathroom fixtures. Several manufacturers have announced Matter-compatible smart toilets for late 2026, which would enable cross-platform compatibility. If interoperability is crucial, consider waiting for Matter-certified models.

**App Quality:** Don’t overlook the companion app. During testing, I found TOTO’s app intuitive but limited, while BioBidet’s health dashboard was genuinely impressive. Kohler’s app is polished but occasionally buggy with voice commands. Download the app before purchasing to ensure it meets your expectations.
