# The Ultimate Smart Toilet Guide for 2026: Luxury, Hygiene, and Home Automation

Smart toilets have evolved from a niche luxury into a mainstream home upgrade, blending cutting-edge technology with everyday comfort. In 2026, these intelligent fixtures offer more than just heated seats and bidet functions—they integrate seamlessly with your smart home ecosystem, monitor health metrics, and even conserve water with AI-driven efficiency. Whether you’re a tech enthusiast, a homeowner seeking convenience, or someone prioritizing hygiene, the right smart toilet can transform your bathroom experience. This in-depth review explores the best models on the market, breaks down essential features, and provides a comprehensive buying guide tailored to U.S. consumers. From budget-friendly options to high-end luxury units, we’ve analyzed performance, installation, and smart home compatibility to help you make an informed decision. Let’s dive into the future of bathroom technology.

## Top 3 Smart Toilet Recommendations for 2026

After extensive testing and research, here are our top picks for smart toilets in 2026, balancing features, price, and reliability.

### 1. Toto Neorest NX2 (Best Overall Luxury)

The Toto Neorest NX2 continues to set the gold standard for smart toilets. This model combines Toto’s legendary Tornado Flush technology with a sleek, wall-hung design that saves space and simplifies cleaning. The NX2 features an advanced eWater+ system that electrolyzes water to create a natural, chemical-free cleaning solution for the bowl and wand, ensuring maximum hygiene. Its heated seat with temperature control, warm air dryer, and deodorizer are standard, but the standout feature is the integrated “Actilight” UV light that sanitizes the bowl surface after each use. The NX2’s smart home integration is robust, working with Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant for voice-activated flushing, seat warming, and bidet controls. The automatic open/close lid and hands-free flushing via proximity sensor add to its convenience. At an estimated price of $5,500 in 2026, it’s a significant investment, but the build quality, reliability, and advanced features justify the cost for those seeking premium performance.

**Pros:** Exceptional build quality, superior hygiene features, strong smart home integration, quiet operation.  
**Cons:** High price, requires professional installation, limited color options.

### 2. Kohler PureWash E930 (Best Value for Features)

Kohler’s PureWash E930 is a bidet seat attachment that transforms a standard toilet into a smart one, offering many high-end features at a fraction of the cost. Priced around $1,200 in 2026, it includes a heated seat, adjustable water temperature and pressure, a warm air dryer, and a deodorizer. The E930’s remote control is intuitive, with preset user profiles for personalized settings. Its smart home compatibility is excellent, supporting both Alexa and Google Assistant for voice commands, as well as Kohler’s Konnect app for scheduling and monitoring. The UV light sanitization for the wand is a nice touch, and the “Quick Clean” feature retracts the wand for easy maintenance. Installation is straightforward for DIYers with basic plumbing skills, though professional installation is recommended for optimal results. The E930 is an ideal upgrade for homeowners who want smart toilet features without replacing their existing toilet.

**Pros:** Affordable, easy to install, strong feature set, excellent app control.  
**Cons:** Not a complete toilet unit, wand may feel less robust than integrated models, requires a nearby electrical outlet.

### 3. Bio Bidet by Brondell U700 (Best for Health Monitoring)

The Bio Bidet U700 stands out for its health-focused features, making it a top choice for users interested in wellness tracking. This bidet seat, priced at approximately $900 in 2026, includes a built-in scale that measures weight and body fat percentage, syncing data to the Brondell app. It also offers a “Health Monitor” mode that tracks urination frequency and duration, providing insights that can be shared with healthcare providers. The U700 features a heated seat, adjustable water temperature and pressure, a warm air dryer, and a stainless steel nozzle with self-cleaning and UV sanitization. Its remote control is backlit and easy to use, and the seat has a “Sit & Wait” function that keeps the seat warm when motion is detected. Smart home integration is limited to basic voice commands via Alexa for flushing, but the app provides detailed health analytics. While it lacks the premium build of Toto, the U700 offers unique value for health-conscious users.

**Pros:** Health tracking features, affordable, good hygiene features, comfortable seat.  
**Cons:** Limited smart home integration, app can be glitchy, health data accuracy varies.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

Smart toilets are ideal for a range of users. **Tech enthusiasts** will appreciate the home automation integration and voice control. **Families** benefit from hygiene features like self-cleaning wands and heated seats, reducing bathroom conflicts. **Seniors or individuals with mobility issues** find hands-free operation and warm seats invaluable. **Health-conscious users** may prefer models with health monitoring. However, if you have a tight budget or rent your home, a bidet seat attachment is a more practical entry point.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $500:** Basic bidet seats with heated seats and water pressure control, but limited smart features.
- **$500–$1,500:** Mid-range bidet seats like the Kohler PureWash E930 or Bio Bidet U700 offer excellent features and smart home integration.
- **$1,500–$3,000:** Complete smart toilets from brands like American Standard or WoodBridge offer good value with integrated designs.
- **$3,000+:** Luxury models like the Toto Neorest NX2 or Kohler Numi 2.0 provide top-tier features, build quality, and design.

### Installation Tips

- **Electrical Outlet:** Most smart toilets and bidet seats require a GFCI-protected electrical outlet near the toilet. If you don’t have one, factor in the cost of hiring an electrician ($200–$500).
- **Plumbing:** Bidet seats typically connect to the existing water supply line. Complete toilets may require new plumbing, especially for wall-hung models.
- **DIY vs. Professional:** Bidet seats are generally DIY-friendly (30–60 minutes). Complete smart toilets often require professional installation to ensure proper sealing and electrical connections.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Process

Installing a smart toilet or bidet seat involves several steps. For a bidet seat, first, turn off the water supply and remove the existing toilet seat. Attach the bidet seat mounting plate, connect the T-adapter to the water supply, and secure the bidet seat. Plug it into a GFCI outlet and test all functions. Complete toilets like the Toto Neorest NX2 require removing the old toilet, installing a new wax ring, and connecting the water and power lines. Wall-hung models need a support frame inside the wall, which is best left to professionals.

### Smart Home Integration

Smart toilets in 2026 offer varying levels of integration. **Voice assistants** like Alexa and Google Assistant allow you to flush, adjust seat temperature, and control bidet settings hands-free. **Smart home hubs** like Samsung SmartThings or Apple HomeKit are supported by some models, enabling automation routines (e.g., pre-heating the seat when you wake up). **Mobile apps** provide detailed control, usage statistics, and firmware updates. For example, the Kohler Konnect app lets you set user profiles and monitor water usage. The Toto Neorest NX2 integrates with IFTTT for advanced automation. Ensure your Wi-Fi network is stable, as connectivity issues can disrupt smart features.
