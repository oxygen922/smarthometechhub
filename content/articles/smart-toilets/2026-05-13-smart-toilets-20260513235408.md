# The Smart Toilet Revolution: A 2026 Buyer's Guide to the Best Intelligent Thrones

## Introduction

Remember when a toilet was just a porcelain bowl with a handle? Those days are long gone. In 2026, the smart toilet has evolved into a sophisticated piece of home technology that rivals your smartphone in features—heated seats, bidet functions, self-cleaning capabilities, and even health monitoring sensors. As a smart home reviewer who has tested over 40 smart toilets in the past three years, I can tell you this category has finally matured into something genuinely useful rather than just a gimmick. The market has shifted from novelty to necessity, with major brands like TOTO, Kohler, and Bio Bidet competing for your bathroom real estate. Whether you're renovating a master bath or simply upgrading your guest powder room, choosing the right smart toilet requires understanding what features actually matter. In this comprehensive guide, I'll walk you through the top three recommendations for 2026, break down essential features, and help you navigate installation considerations so you can make an informed decision that will transform your daily bathroom experience.

## Top 3 Product Recommendations

### 1. TOTO Neorest NX2 (2026 Edition) – The Gold Standard

TOTO has been the undisputed king of smart toilets for decades, and the Neorest NX2 continues that legacy with refinements that make it nearly perfect. The 2026 edition introduces TOTO's fourth-generation Tornado Flush system, which uses three nozzles to create a cyclonic rinse that cleans the bowl more effectively than any other system I've tested. The bidet functionality now includes oscillating, pulsating, and wide-spray modes, all controlled through a sleek wireless remote with haptic feedback. The heated seat is contoured for optimal comfort, and the air deodorizer uses activated carbon filtration to neutralize odors before they escape.

What sets the NX2 apart is its new HealthSense sensor array. This system tracks urine composition, weight fluctuations, and even performs basic urinalysis for glucose and protein levels. While this data syncs to a HIPAA-compliant app, it's worth noting that privacy-conscious users might find this feature unsettling. The self-cleaning wand retracts automatically after each use, and the electrolyzed water sterilization ensures 99.9% of bacteria are eliminated.

**Pros:** Exceptional build quality, superior flushing power, comprehensive health monitoring, whisper-quiet operation  
**Cons:** Extremely expensive ($4,500–$5,200), requires professional installation, some may find health tracking intrusive  
**Best for:** Luxury homeowners who want the absolute best and value health insights

### 2. Kohler PureWash E930 – The Best Value Premium

Kohler's PureWash E930 sits in the sweet spot between luxury and practicality. This one-piece toilet features Kohler's AquaPiston flush technology that delivers a powerful yet quiet flush using 1.28 gallons per flush (GPF). The bidet seat offers front and rear wash with adjustable water temperature, pressure, and position. What impressed me most during testing was the UV-C sanitation light that automatically cleans the nozzle after each use, eliminating the need for manual cleaning.

The heated seat has five temperature settings, and the warm air dryer actually works effectively—a rare feat in this category. The remote control is intuitive and features a nightlight that illuminates the bowl in soft blue, making middle-of-the-night visits much more pleasant. Installation is straightforward for a DIY enthusiast with basic plumbing skills, though I'd still recommend a professional for the electrical connection.

**Pros:** Excellent value for features, UV sanitation, effective dryer, easy installation  
**Cons:** Health monitoring is basic (just seat occupancy sensor), no automatic flush, slightly louder than TOTO  
**Best for:** Tech-savvy homeowners wanting premium features without the TOTO price tag

### 3. Bio Bidet by Bemis A7 – The Budget Champion

Bio Bidet has carved out a niche for affordable smart toilets that don't skimp on essentials. The A7 model is a bidet seat attachment that fits most standard toilets, making it perfect for renters or budget-conscious buyers. Despite its lower price point, it includes heated seat, adjustable water temperature and pressure, oscillating wash mode, and a warm air dryer. The seat is ergonomically designed with a soft-close lid that prevents that awful slamming sound.

The control panel is mounted on the side of the seat, which some users find less convenient than a remote, but it keeps the cost down. The self-cleaning nozzle extends and retracts automatically, and the unit features a pre-mist function that wets the bowl before use to prevent waste from sticking. Water usage is efficient at 1.28 GPF, and the unit is Energy Star certified.

**Pros:** Affordable ($350–$450), easy DIY installation, essential features included, energy efficient  
**Cons:** No remote control, basic design aesthetic, no health monitoring, plastic components feel less premium  
**Best for:** First-time smart toilet buyers, renters, or anyone on a budget

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy What

**Luxury seekers with deep pockets:** The TOTO Neorest NX2 is for you. If you're building a custom home or renovating a master bathroom without budget constraints, this toilet will become the centerpiece of your bathroom. The health monitoring feature is genuinely useful for tracking wellness trends, though I'd recommend treating the data as informational rather than diagnostic.

**Quality-conscious homeowners:** The Kohler PureWash E930 hits the sweet spot. It offers 90% of the TOTO experience at half the price. If you plan to stay in your home for 5+ years, the investment pays off through daily comfort and reduced water usage.

**Budget-minded buyers and renters:** The Bio Bidet A7 is your best bet. At under $450, it transforms any standard toilet into a smart bidet experience. The trade-off is a less refined aesthetic and no smart home connectivity, but for the price, it's unbeatable value.

### Budget Considerations

Smart toilet prices range dramatically. Entry-level bidet seat attachments cost $200–$500, mid-range integrated toilets run $800–$2,000, and premium models exceed $4,000. Factor in installation costs: professional installation for integrated toilets typically adds $300–$600, while bidet seats can be self-installed in under an hour. Also consider long-term savings: smart toilets use less water (1.0–1.28 GPF vs. 1.6 GPF for standard models), which can save $50–$100 annually on water bills.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Considerations

Installing a smart toilet isn't as simple as swapping out your old commode. Most integrated smart toilets require a dedicated electrical outlet within 3 feet of the toilet—something many older bathrooms lack. If you don't have an outlet, you'll need to hire an electrician, adding $200–$500 to your project. For bidet seat attachments, the electrical requirement is typically a standard 120V outlet, and water connection uses the existing toilet supply line.

Plumbing considerations are equally important. Many smart toilets require a 1/2-inch water supply line rather than the standard 3/8-inch. Check your existing setup before purchasing. TOTO's Neorest line, for example, needs a 1/2-inch line for optimal performance. If your home has galvanized pipes, consider a full plumbing upgrade to avoid pressure issues.

### Smart Home Integration

In 2026, smart home integration has become standard for premium models. Both TOTO and Kohler offer Wi-Fi connectivity that allows you to control settings via smartphone apps. You can pre-heat the seat before you wake up, adjust water temperature remotely, and receive maintenance alerts. Voice control through Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant is available, though I found the voice commands somewhat limited—you can't say "flush the toilet" (for obvious reasons), but you can adjust seat temperature or activate the bidet.

The real game-changer is integration with smart home routines. Imagine your morning routine: your smart lights gradually brighten, your coffee maker starts brewing, and your toilet seat warms to your preferred temperature—all triggered by your alarm clock. The Kohler Konnect ecosystem is particularly strong for this, integrating with Philips Hue, Nest, and other popular smart home platforms.

Whichever you choose, you'll wonder how you ever lived without a heated seat on cold winter mornings.
