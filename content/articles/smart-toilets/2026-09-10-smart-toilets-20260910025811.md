# Smart Toilet Review 2026: The Ultimate Guide to Upgrading Your Bathroom

## Introduction

The smart toilet has quietly become one of the most transformative additions to the modern American home. What was once a luxury reserved for high-end hotels and Japanese hospitality suites is now a mainstream bathroom upgrade, with prices ranging from a few hundred dollars for a bidet seat to several thousand for a fully integrated intelligent toilet. In 2026, the market has matured dramatically—manufacturers are packing heated seats, warm-water washing, UV sterilization, and even AI-powered personalization into units that connect seamlessly with your smart home ecosystem. But with dozens of options flooding the market, choosing the right smart toilet can feel overwhelming. In this in-depth smart toilet review, we'll break down the top products, the features that actually matter, and everything you need to know before you buy. Whether you're remodeling or simply want a more hygienic, comfortable bathroom experience, this guide will help you make an informed decision.

## Key Features to Consider

Before purchasing a smart toilet, it's essential to understand the features that separate a good model from a great one. Here's what to look for:

- **Bidet Functionality:** Adjustable water pressure, temperature, and spray patterns (front/rear cleansing) are must-haves. Higher-end models offer oscillating and pulsating modes.
- **Heated Seat:** A heated seat with adjustable temperature is one of the most appreciated features, especially in colder climates.
- **Warm Air Dryer:** Not all smart toilets include a dryer, and those that do vary in effectiveness. Look for adjustable temperature settings.
- **Self-Cleaning & Sanitization:** UV sterilization, electrolyzed water, and self-cleaning wands improve hygiene and reduce maintenance.
- **Flush Technology:** Tankless (continuous hot water) vs. tank-based. Tankless is generally preferred for unlimited hot water and space savings.
- **Smart Home Integration:** Compatibility with Alexa, Google Home, Apple HomeKit, and SmartThings varies. Check before buying if this matters to you.
- **Deodorizer:** An activated carbon filter neutralizes odors automatically.
- **Remote Control & App:** Physical remotes are common; app control adds convenience and scheduling.
- **Power Requirements:** Most smart toilets require a GFCI outlet within reach. Factor in electrical work if your bathroom lacks one.
- **Warranty:** Look for at least a 3-year limited warranty; premium brands often offer longer coverage.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

A smart toilet is a worthwhile investment for several types of buyers:

- **Homeowners remodeling a bathroom:** If you're already renovating, adding a smart toilet is a natural upgrade that boosts comfort and resale value.
- **People with mobility issues:** Heated seats, automatic lids, and bidet functions improve accessibility and independence.
- **Hygiene-conscious households:** Bidets reduce toilet paper usage and provide a more thorough clean, which is especially appealing to families.
- **Tech enthusiasts:** If you love smart home gadgets, a connected toilet is the ultimate bathroom upgrade.
- **Anyone in colder climates:** A heated seat is a game-changer during winter months.

### Budget Considerations

Smart toilets span a wide price range. Here's how to think about your budget:

- **Under $1,000:** You'll typically find bidet seats (not full toilets) with basic features. These are a great entry point if you're not ready for a full replacement.
- **$1,000–$2,000:** Full smart toilets with essential features like heated seats, bidets, and dryers. The Bio Bidet Discovery DLS is a standout here.
- **$2,000–$3,500:** Premium models with advanced smart home integration and superior build quality. The Kohler Numii fits this tier.
- **$3,500+:** Luxury flagship models with the best materials, performance, and features. The TOTO Neorest NX2 leads this category.

Don't forget to factor in installation costs, which can range from $200 to $600 depending on your existing plumbing and electrical setup. If you need to add an electrical outlet, budget an additional $150–$300 for an electrician.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Overview

Installing a smart toilet is more involved than swapping out a standard toilet, but it's manageable with the right preparation.

1. **Check your electrical setup:** Most smart toilets require a GFCI-protected outlet within 3 feet of the toilet. If you don't have one, hire a licensed electrician.
2. **Measure your space:** Ensure the rough-in distance (the distance from the wall to the drain) matches the toilet's requirements—typically 12 inches.
3. **Turn off the water supply:** Shut off the water valve and flush the old toilet to empty the tank.
4. **Remove the old toilet:** Disconnect the water line, unbolt the base, and lift it away. Clean the flange and replace the wax ring.
5. **Install the new toilet:** Set the smart toilet in place, connect the water supply, and secure it to the floor.
6. **Connect power and configure:** Plug in the unit, power it on, and follow the manufacturer's setup wizard to configure settings, connect to Wi-Fi, and pair with your smart home hub.

Professional installation is recommended for integrated smart toilets, especially if electrical work is needed. Expect to pay $300–$600 for professional installation.

### Smart Home Integration

One of the most exciting aspects of modern smart toilets is their ability to integrate with your broader smart home ecosystem. Here's what to expect:

- **Voice Control:** Alexa and Google Home support allows voice commands like "Alexa, open the toilet lid" or "Hey Google, warm the toilet seat." Kohler's Numii also supports Apple HomeKit.
- **Automation:** Create routines—for example, have the bathroom nightlight turn on when motion is detected at night, or warm the seat at your usual morning time.
- **App Control:** Manufacturer apps let you adjust settings, monitor water usage, set user profiles, and receive maintenance alerts.
- **Energy Monitoring:** Some models track energy consumption, helping you optimize efficiency.

Keep in mind that smart home integration varies significantly between brands. If HomeKit compatibility is important to you, the Kohler Numii is your best bet. For broader ecosystem support, TOTO and Bio Bidet cover the major platforms.
