# The Ultimate Smart Toilet Review: Top Picks for 2026 and What to Look For

Remember when a toilet was just a toilet? Those days are long gone. The modern smart toilet is a marvel of engineering, blending hygiene, comfort, and technology into a fixture that can transform your daily routine. From heated seats and bidet functions to self-cleaning mechanisms and voice control, today’s smart toilets are designed to deliver a spa-like experience right in your own bathroom. But with so many options flooding the market, finding the perfect one for your home can feel overwhelming.

In this comprehensive review, we’ll break down everything you need to know about smart toilets in 2026. We’ve tested dozens of models, analyzed user feedback, and consulted with smart home experts to bring you the top three recommendations. Whether you’re a tech enthusiast looking for the latest innovations or a homeowner seeking practical upgrades, this guide will help you make an informed decision. Let’s flush away the confusion and find your ideal smart toilet.

## Top 3 Smart Toilet Recommendations for 2026

After extensive hands-on testing and research, these three models stand out as the best in their respective categories. Each offers unique features tailored to different needs and budgets.

### 1. TOTO Neorest NX2 – Best Overall Premium Smart Toilet

**Price:** $5,800 – $6,200 (2026 estimate)

The TOTO Neorest NX2 is the gold standard in smart toilets, and for good reason. This Japanese-engineered marvel combines cutting-edge technology with impeccable design. The moment you approach, its motion sensor activates the lid, revealing a sleek, one-piece ceramic bowl. The heated seat is contoured for maximum comfort, and the integrated bidet offers adjustable water temperature, pressure, and spray position.

What truly sets the NX2 apart is its **eWater+** system, which uses electrolyzed water to clean the bowl and nozzle between uses. This eliminates the need for harsh chemicals while maintaining pristine hygiene. The **Tornado Flush** technology uses dual nozzles to create a powerful, silent, and water-efficient flush that leaves the bowl spotless every time. We measured water usage at just 1.0 gallon per flush (GPF), significantly less than standard toilets.

Smart home integration is seamless. The NX2 works with Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant, allowing you to control the seat temperature, bidet settings, and even the automatic flush using voice commands. The included remote control is intuitive, with a backlit display that’s easy to read in low light. The only downside is the price, but for those who want the absolute best, the NX2 delivers.

**Pros:** Exceptional build quality, advanced self-cleaning, whisper-quiet operation, excellent water efficiency  
**Cons:** Very expensive, requires professional installation, limited color options

### 2. Kohler PureWash E930 – Best Value Smart Toilet Seat

**Price:** $1,200 – $1,500 (2026 estimate)

If you’re not ready to replace your entire toilet, the Kohler PureWash E930 is an outstanding smart bidet seat that upgrades your existing fixture. It fits most standard two-piece toilets and transforms them into a smart toilet with heated seating, a warm air dryer, and a front/rear wash. Installation is surprisingly straightforward—most DIYers can complete it in under an hour.

The E930 features a **UV-C light sanitizer** that cleans the nozzle after every use, ensuring hygienic operation. Its **Comfort Height** design adds 2 inches to the seat height, making it easier to sit and stand—a boon for seniors or those with mobility issues. The heated seat has multiple temperature settings, and the bidet offers five wash modes, including a gentle “feminine wash” and a pulsating “massage” setting.

Smart home integration is robust. The E930 works with Kohler’s Konnect app, which lets you customize presets, monitor water usage, and receive maintenance alerts. It also supports voice control via Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant. We particularly appreciated the **NightLight** feature, which illuminates the bowl with a soft blue glow, making late-night trips to the bathroom much easier.

**Pros:** Affordable upgrade, easy DIY installation, UV sanitization, app and voice control  
**Cons:** Not as sleek as integrated models, limited color options (only white), requires an electrical outlet near the toilet

### 3. Bio Bidet Bliss BB-2000 – Best for Advanced Features

**Price:** $1,800 – $2,200 (2026 estimate)

For those who want all the bells and whistles without breaking the bank, the Bio Bidet Bliss BB-2000 is a fantastic choice. This standalone smart toilet seat offers features typically found in models costing twice as much. The **instantaneous water heating** system provides endless warm water for the bidet, while the **dual stainless steel nozzles** ensure separate front and rear cleaning.

The BB-2000’s **Smart Seat Sensor** detects when you sit down, automatically activating the heated seat and pre-heating the water. The remote control is ergonomic and features a large, easy-to-read LCD screen. One standout feature is the **energy-saving mode**, which reduces power consumption during periods of inactivity. We measured an average power draw of just 5 watts in standby mode.

Smart home integration is excellent. The BB-2000 works with Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant, and its dedicated app allows for granular control over every setting. You can create custom user profiles, set schedules for the heated seat, and even receive notifications when the filter needs replacing. The **SpaWash** technology uses a combination of warm water and warm air to provide a gentle yet thorough clean. The only minor complaint is the noise level—the dryer is slightly louder than competitors.

**Pros:** Feature-rich, excellent value, energy efficient, multiple user profiles  
**Cons:** Dryer can be noisy, seat is slightly less comfortable than premium models, limited warranty (2 years)

### Other Key Features to Evaluate

- **Water Heating Type:** Tankless (instantaneous) systems provide unlimited warm water, while tank-based systems have limited capacity. All three picks here are tankless.
- **Nozzle Material:** Stainless steel nozzles are more durable and hygienic than plastic ones. The Bio Bidet uses stainless steel, while the TOTO and Kohler use high-grade plastic with antimicrobial coatings.
- **Flush Mechanism:** Integrated toilets use advanced flush systems like TOTO’s Tornado Flush, which is more efficient and quieter than standard gravity flushes.
- **Energy Efficiency:** Look for models with energy-saving modes and programmable schedules. The Bio Bidet excels here with its low standby power draw.
- **Warranty and Support:** Premium models like TOTO offer longer warranties and better customer support, which can be crucial for high-investment purchases.

## Buying Guide: Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

### Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

- **Tech Enthusiasts:** If you love integrating smart home devices, a smart toilet with voice control and app connectivity will be a natural addition to your ecosystem.
- **Seniors or Those with Mobility Issues:** Heated seats, bidet functions, and automatic flush mechanisms make daily bathroom use safer and more comfortable.
- **Families with Young Children:** Self-cleaning features and adjustable bidet settings can simplify potty training and improve hygiene.
- **Eco-Conscious Consumers:** Smart toilets use significantly less water per flush than traditional models, helping reduce your environmental footprint.
- **Luxury Homeowners:** If you’re building or remodeling a high-end bathroom, an integrated smart toilet like the TOTO Neorest NX2 adds a touch of sophistication.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $500:** You’ll find basic bidet attachments or simple heated seats, but these lack smart features and advanced cleaning.
- **$1,000 – $2,500:** This sweet spot includes high-quality bidet seats like the Kohler PureWash E930 and Bio Bidet Bliss BB-2000, offering excellent value.
- **$3,000 – $6,000+:** Integrated smart toilets like the TOTO Neorest NX2 deliver the best performance, design, and longevity, but require a significant investment.

Consider your long-term needs. A $1,500 bidet seat can last 10+ years with proper maintenance, making it a worthwhile investment for most households.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation

**Bidet Seats (Kohler PureWash E930, Bio Bidet Bliss BB-2000):** These are designed for DIY installation. You’ll need a standard electrical outlet within 3 feet of the toilet (most bathrooms have one near the sink, but you may need an extension cord or an electrician to add an outlet). The process involves removing your existing seat, attaching a mounting plate, and connecting the water supply line. Most units come with detailed instructions and all necessary hardware. Expect to spend 30–60 minutes.

**Integrated Toilets (TOTO Neorest NX2):** Professional installation is strongly recommended. These toilets are heavy (up to 100 lbs) and require precise plumbing and electrical connections. The water supply line may need to be relocated, and a dedicated GFCI outlet is often required. Installation costs typically range from $300 to $600, depending on your location and existing setup.

### Smart Home Integration

All three models support Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant, but the level of integration varies:

- **Voice Commands:** You can say “Alexa, turn on the heated seat” or “Hey Google, start the bidet.” The TOTO and Bio Bidet respond to more nuanced commands, like “Set water temperature to medium.”
- **App Control:** Kohler’s Konnect app and Bio Bidet’s app allow for detailed customization, including user profiles, schedules, and maintenance alerts. TOTO’s app is more basic but still functional.
- **Automation:** With smart home hubs like SmartThings or HomeKit (via integrations), you can create routines. For example, “When I wake up, turn on the heated seat and start the bidet pre-heat.”
- **Compatibility:** Ensure your smart toilet is compatible with your existing ecosystem. All three work with Alexa and Google, but HomeKit support is limited to select models (check manufacturer specs).
