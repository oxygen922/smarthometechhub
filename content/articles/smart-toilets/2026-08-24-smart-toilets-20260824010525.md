# The Ultimate Smart Toilet Review Guide: 2026’s Best Intelligent Commodes for the Modern American Home

## Introduction

For decades, the humble toilet remained an afterthought in home design—a purely functional fixture that received little innovation beyond basic efficiency upgrades. That era is officially over. The smart toilet has emerged as the unexpected centerpiece of the modern connected bathroom, combining advanced bidet functionality, automated cleaning, health monitoring, and seamless smart home integration into a single, luxurious package. As we move through 2026, the market has exploded with options ranging from sub-$800 self-cleaning models to $8,000+ luxury units that resemble something from a sci-fi film. But with so many choices, how do you separate genuine innovation from gimmickry? This comprehensive review breaks down the three best smart toilets available today, provides a detailed buying guide, and examines the critical installation and integration considerations every American homeowner needs to know before making this significant investment.

---

## Top 3 Product Recommendations

### 1. TOTO Neorest NX2 (2026 Edition) – The Gold Standard

**Rating: 9.8/10 | Price: $7,999**

When discussing premium smart toilets, TOTO remains the undisputed industry leader, and the Neorest NX2 continues to set the benchmark for what a truly intelligent toilet can achieve. This is not merely a toilet with a bidet attachment; it is a fully integrated system designed around the Japanese concept of *omotenashi*—selfless hospitality.

**Performance and Features**

The NX2’s signature feature is its **ACTILIGHT system**, which combines an automatic opening and closing lid with a soft-close mechanism that operates almost silently. The integrated **EWATER+ technology** uses electrolyzed water to break down waste and mineral buildup, ensuring the bowl remains pristine between cleanings. The **CYCLONE FLUSH** system delivers a powerful yet remarkably quiet 1.0 GPF flush that consistently clears the bowl on the first attempt—a critical metric for any toilet.

The bidet functionality is where the NX2 truly shines. Users can choose from multiple spray patterns, including posterior, soft, and feminine washes, all adjustable for water pressure, temperature, and spray position via the included wireless remote. The **PRE-MIST** function sprays the bowl before use, preventing waste from adhering to the surface, while the **WARM AIR DRYER** provides a gentle, adjustable-temperature finish.

**Health Monitoring and Smart Features**

TOTO has integrated a **built-in air purification system** that uses a zeolite-based deodorizer to neutralize odors continuously, not just during use. The toilet connects to the TOTO app via Wi-Fi, allowing users to customize settings, track usage patterns, and receive maintenance reminders. The NX2 also includes a **night light** with adjustable brightness, making 2 AM bathroom trips safer without blinding you.

**Pros and Cons**

The NX2’s build quality is exceptional—the glazed ceramic and soft-close mechanisms feel like they will last decades. The self-cleaning features genuinely reduce maintenance time. However, the price is prohibitive for most households, and the proprietary TOTO app, while functional, lacks the polish of competitors like Kohler. Installation requires a dedicated GFCI outlet near the toilet.

**Verdict:** If budget is no object and you demand the absolute best, the Neorest NX2 is the definitive choice. It is the Lexus of smart toilets—luxurious, reliable, and packed with thoughtful features.

### 2. Kohler Numi 2.0 – The Tech Enthusiast’s Dream

**Rating: 9.5/10 | Price: $9,995 (often discounted to $8,500)**

Kohler’s Numi 2.0 takes a different approach, positioning itself as the most technologically advanced toilet on the market. It is unapologetically futuristic, featuring a sleek, angular design that looks like it belongs in a modern art museum rather than a bathroom.

**Performance and Features**

The Numi 2.0 is the first toilet to feature **built-in Amazon Alexa integration**. You can literally ask the toilet to flush, open the lid, adjust the seat temperature, or play your favorite podcast while you handle business. The **DTV+ (Digital Thermal Valve)** system provides instantaneous water temperature control, ensuring there is never a cold spray—a common complaint with lesser bidet toilets.

The **heated seat** is among the best in the industry, with five temperature levels and a pre-heat function that warms the seat before you enter the bathroom (when connected to your smart home routine). The Numi 2.0 also includes **ambient LED lighting** with 16.8 million color options, allowing you to set the mood—whether that means a calming blue or a vibrant red.

**Smart Home Integration**

This is where the Numi 2.0 truly differentiates itself. It supports **Google Home, Apple HomeKit, and Alexa**, making it the most ecosystem-friendly smart toilet available. You can create automation routines, such as having the toilet pre-heat its seat and open its lid when your morning alarm goes off. The Kohler Konnect app is well-designed, offering granular control over every setting and providing usage analytics.

**Pros and Cons**

The Numi 2.0’s integration capabilities are unmatched, and its water heating system is superior to most competitors. The self-cleaning function uses a UV light to sanitize the bowl, which is effective but requires periodic bulb replacement. The price is even higher than the TOTO, and the angular design may not suit traditional bathroom aesthetics. Additionally, the Alexa integration, while impressive, can feel gimmicky to some users.

**Verdict:** If you are a smart home enthusiast who wants every device in your house to work together seamlessly, the Numi 2.0 is the clear winner. It is the Tesla Model S of toilets—innovative, connected, and unapologetically modern.

### 3. Bio Bidet Flow 3000 – The Best Value Smart Toilet

**Rating: 9.0/10 | Price: $1,199**

For the vast majority of American households, spending $8,000 on a toilet is simply not feasible. The Bio Bidet Flow 3000 offers 80% of the features found on premium models at just 15% of the price. It is the smart toilet that finally makes this technology accessible to the mainstream.

**Performance and Features**

The Flow 3000 includes all the essential features you need: a **heated seat** with adjustable temperature, **warm water bidet** with front and rear wash modes, **warm air dryer**, and **deodorizer**. The controls are integrated into a side panel on the toilet body, which is convenient but less ergonomic than a remote control.

The flush system uses a **dual-flush mechanism** (1.1 GPF for liquid, 1.6 GPF for solid), which is water-efficient and effective. The bowl features a **glazed ceramic surface** that resists staining, and the unit includes a **self-cleaning nozzle** that retracts and rinses itself after each use.

**Smart Features**

The Flow 3000 offers a **motion-activated lid** that opens when you approach and closes when you leave. It also includes a **night light** and a **soft-close seat**. While it lacks Wi-Fi connectivity and app control, it does feature a **remote control** with an LCD screen that allows you to save up to four personalized user profiles. This is a significant convenience for households with multiple users who prefer different water temperatures and spray pressures.

**Pros and Cons**

The Flow 3000’s build quality is surprisingly good for the price, and the installation is straightforward (more on that later). The main compromises are the lack of smart home integration and the absence of a pre-mist function. The dryer is also less powerful than premium models, taking slightly longer to achieve a fully dry finish.

**Verdict:** The Bio Bidet Flow 3000 is the smart toilet I recommend to most people. It delivers the core experience—heated seat, warm wash, and dryer—without the luxury markup. For most families, this is the perfect balance of price and performance.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

**The Luxury Buyer:** If you are renovating a master bathroom and want the ultimate in comfort and convenience, a premium smart toilet like the TOTO Neorest or Kohler Numi is a worthwhile investment. These units transform a daily necessity into a spa-like experience and add a unique selling point if you ever list your home.

**The Smart Home Enthusiast:** If you already have smart lights, thermostats, and locks, the Kohler Numi 2.0 is the only option that will truly integrate into your existing ecosystem. The ability to trigger a pre-warmed seat via your morning routine is a genuinely delightful experience.

**The Practical Family:** For most families, the Bio Bidet Flow 3000 is the sweet spot. It provides the core hygiene and comfort benefits—heated seat, warm wash, and drying—at a price that is comparable to a mid-range standard toilet plus a high-end bidet attachment.

**The Health-Conscious Individual:** Several premium models (including some not covered here) offer health monitoring features like urine analysis. However, these are still in their infancy and often require proprietary test strips. For now, the primary health benefit of any smart toilet is improved hygiene and reduced physical strain from wiping.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $1,000:** You will be limited to smart toilet seats (attachments for your existing toilet) rather than full smart toilets. These offer bidet and heated seat functions but lack the integrated bowl design.
- **$1,000–$2,500:** This is the "sweet spot" for full smart toilets. You can expect all core features, good build quality, and a reliable warranty. The Bio Bidet Flow 3000 sits comfortably here.
- **$2,500–$5,000:** This range offers better water heating systems (instant vs. tank), more powerful dryers, and more refined designs from brands like American Standard and Duravit.
- **$5,000+:** This is the luxury tier. You are paying for premium materials, advanced self-cleaning technology, superior design, and (in Kohler's case) full smart home integration.

**Hidden Costs:** Remember to budget for installation. If you are not handy, professional installation can cost $300–$600 depending on your bathroom's existing electrical and plumbing setup.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Requirements

This is the most critical—and most overlooked—aspect of buying a smart toilet. Unlike a standard toilet that only needs a water supply line, smart toilets require **both water and electricity**.

**Electrical Requirements:** You will need a **GFCI-protected electrical outlet** within reach of the toilet. Most units have a power cord that is 3–4 feet long. If you don't have an outlet near your toilet, you will need to hire an electrician to run one, which can cost $200–$500. This is a non-negotiable requirement; do not use an extension cord, as it violates electrical code and poses a safety hazard.

**Plumbing Requirements:** Most smart toilets use a standard 12-inch rough-in (the distance from the wall to the center of the drain pipe). However, some luxury models have specific requirements. Always check the manufacturer's specifications before purchasing. You will also need a dedicated shut-off valve for the toilet.

**Space Considerations:** Smart toilets are generally larger than standard toilets, especially the luxury models with integrated tanks. The Kohler Numi, for example, is quite long and may not fit in a small half-bathroom. Measure your space carefully, including the distance from the toilet to the opposite wall, as the lid opening mechanism needs clearance.

**DIY vs. Professional:** If you are replacing an existing toilet and have the electrical outlet in place, installation is a manageable weekend DIY project. You will need a basic understanding of plumbing (wax ring, flange, water line). However, if you need to run new electrical wiring or if your bathroom has non-standard plumbing, hire a licensed professional.

### Smart Home Integration

**Wi-Fi and Apps:** Premium models (TOTO, Kohler) connect to your home Wi-Fi network and use proprietary apps for control and customization. These apps allow you to set user profiles, adjust every setting remotely, and receive maintenance alerts.

**Voice Assistants:** Only the Kohler Numi 2.0 currently offers full voice control integration with Alexa, Google Assistant, and Apple HomeKit. This allows for voice commands like "Alexa, flush the toilet," which is convenient but can also be accidentally triggered by TV shows or conversations.

**Automation:** The true power of smart home integration lies in automation. You can set routines, such as:
- **Morning Routine:** Seat pre-heats to 100°F at 6:30 AM on weekdays.
- **Night Mode:** Night light activates at sunset and deactivates at sunrise.
- **Vacation Mode:** Toilet runs a self-cleaning cycle and closes its lid to prevent dust accumulation.

**Privacy Concerns:** It is worth noting that a connected toilet is a device on your network. While manufacturers claim data encryption, some privacy-conscious users may be uncomfortable with a device tracking their usage patterns. If this is a concern, opt for a model without Wi-Fi (like the Bio Bidet Flow 3000) and rely on its remote control.
