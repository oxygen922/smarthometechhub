# The Best Smart Toilets of 2026: A Complete Buyer's Review

## Introduction

Smart toilets have quietly evolved from novelty gadgets into genuinely transformative bathroom fixtures. In 2026, the American smart toilet market has matured significantly, with bidet functionality, heated seats, auto-flushing, and even health-monitoring sensors now available at a wide range of price points. Whether you're remodeling a primary bathroom or simply upgrading a guest bath, a smart toilet can deliver improved hygiene, comfort, and water efficiency. This review breaks down the three best smart toilets available in the US market right now, walks you through the key features that matter, and helps you decide which model fits your home and budget. We've spent time researching specs, reliability reports, and real-world user feedback to bring you honest, practical recommendations.

## Top 3 Product Recommendations

### 1. TOTO Neorest WX1 — Best Overall Smart Toilet

**Estimated Price (2026): $6,800 – $7,500**

TOTO remains the gold standard in the smart toilet category, and the Neorest WX1 is its flagship residential model. This integrated one-piece toilet combines a tankless design with TOTO's signature Tornado Flush system, delivering an exceptionally quiet and powerful flush while using just 0.8 gallons per flush (GPF). The bidet seat features adjustable water temperature, pressure, and oscillating or pulsating spray patterns, all controlled via an intuitive remote or wall-mounted panel.

What sets the Neorest WX1 apart is its "Activated" eWater+ system, which mists the bowl with electrolyzed water after each use to inhibit bacteria growth. The heated seat warms quickly and evenly, and the automatic open/close lid responds to motion sensors. In our assessment, the build quality is exceptional, and TOTO's service network across the US is the most reliable in the industry. The main drawback is the price—this is a luxury purchase—and installation requires a dedicated electrical outlet near the toilet.

**Pros:** Premium build, near-silent flush, excellent bidet controls, self-cleaning bowl technology
**Cons:** Very expensive, requires professional installation, needs nearby power outlet

### 2. Kohler Numi 2.0 — Best Premium Experience

**Estimated Price (2026): $8,500 – $9,200**

Kohler's Numi 2.0 pushes the smart toilet into entertainment territory. It includes built-in surround speakers with Amazon Alexa integration, ambient LED lighting, and a heated seat with personalized user profiles. The bidet offers customizable spray settings, and the toilet includes a motion-activated lid and automatic flush.

For pure comfort and "wow factor," the Numi 2.0 is unmatched. The Alexa integration lets you control lighting, play music, and even issue voice commands during your morning routine. However, the speaker functionality feels somewhat gimmicky to some users, and at over $8,500, the value proposition is steep for anyone who doesn't prioritize luxury extras. Flush performance is strong, and Kohler's warranty support is solid. If you want a statement piece for a high-end bathroom, this is it—but budget-conscious buyers should look elsewhere.

**Pros:** Integrated Alexa and speakers, ambient lighting, personalized settings
**Cons:** Highest price in our lineup, extra features may not justify cost for all users

If you want the core smart toilet experience without the luxury markup, the Bio Bidet Prodigy X2 is the standout value pick. It's a fully integrated smart toilet with a heated seat, warm water bidet, auto flush, and a night light—all at a fraction of the flagship prices. **Pros:** Affordable, all core smart features included, easy-to-use controls

## Key Features to Consider

When shopping for a smart toilet, these are the features that most affect daily satisfaction and long-term value:

- **Bidet functionality:** Adjustable pressure, temperature, and spray patterns are essential. Look for self-cleaning nozzles.
- **Heated seat:** A must-have for comfort, especially in colder climates. Check warm-up speed and temperature range.
- **Auto flush and lid:** Motion sensors for hands-free operation add convenience and hygiene.
- **Water efficiency:** Aim for 1.28 GPF or lower. Premium models like the TOTO Neorest use under 1.0 GPF.
- **Power requirements:** Most smart toilets require a nearby GFCI electrical outlet. Confirm your bathroom's wiring before purchase.
- **Warranty and service:** TOTO and Kohler offer strong US service networks; smaller brands vary widely.
- **Smart home integration:** Some models integrate with Alexa or Google Home. Decide if this matters to you before paying a premium.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

Smart toilets are ideal for several groups:

- **Remodelers and renovators** upgrading a primary bathroom who want modern hygiene and comfort.
- **Mobility-limited users** who benefit from hands-free flushing and bidet cleaning.
- **Families** looking to reduce toilet paper usage and improve hygiene.
- **Tech enthusiasts** who enjoy integrated home automation.

If you rent your home or aren't prepared for electrical work, a bidet seat attachment may be a more practical first step.

### Budget Considerations

Set a realistic budget before shopping. In 2026, entry-level smart toilets start around $1,500, mid-range models run $2,400–$3,500, and luxury flagships exceed $6,000. Don't forget installation costs: professional installation typically runs $300–$800, and adding a GFCI outlet near the toilet can cost $200–$500 if one doesn't already exist. Factor in replacement parts and warranty coverage—premium brands cost more upfront but tend to have better long-term reliability and service.

---

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

Installing a smart toilet is more involved than swapping a standard fixture. You'll need a standard water supply line, a floor flange in good condition, and—critically—a grounded GFCI electrical outlet within reach of the toilet's power cord (usually 3–4 feet). If your bathroom lacks an outlet, hire a licensed electrician before scheduling installation.

Most manufacturers recommend professional installation, and we agree—especially for integrated models with electronic components. Expect installation to take 1–3 hours. Once installed, setup involves configuring water temperature, pressure, and seat heat via the remote or panel.

On the smart home front, integration remains limited. Kohler's Numi 2.0 is the outlier with built-in Alexa, enabling voice control of lighting and audio. TOTO offers limited app connectivity on select models. If full smart home integration is a priority, verify compatibility with your Alexa, Google Home, or SmartThings ecosystem before buying. For most buyers, the "smart" in smart toilet refers to automated hygiene features rather than ecosystem connectivity—and that's perfectly fine.
