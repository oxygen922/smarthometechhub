# The Ultimate Smart Toilet Review: Elevating Your Bathroom Experience in 2026

## Introduction

The smart toilet has evolved from a luxury novelty into a practical, health-conscious, and eco-friendly home essential. As we move through 2026, these intelligent fixtures offer far more than heated seats and automated flushing. They now integrate seamlessly with smart home ecosystems, provide personalized bidet functions, and even offer health monitoring features that can track your wellness. Whether you’re a tech enthusiast looking to upgrade your master bathroom or a homeowner seeking water savings and hygiene improvements, the modern smart toilet is a compelling investment. In this comprehensive review, we’ll explore the top models on the market, break down essential features, and guide you through installation and integration. Our goal is to help you choose the perfect smart toilet that balances technology, comfort, and value for your home. Let’s dive into the future of bathroom technology.

## Top 3 Product Recommendations

### 1. TOTO Neorest NX2 (2026 Edition)
**Price Estimate:** $7,500 - $8,200

The TOTO Neorest NX2 remains the gold standard in the smart toilet category. This 2026 edition builds on TOTO’s legendary reliability with a redesigned, more powerful Tornado Flush system that uses just 1.0 gallon per flush (GPF) while delivering exceptional waste removal. The integrated eWater+ system uses electrolyzed water to clean the bowl between uses, reducing the need for harsh chemicals. Its heated seat, adjustable warm water bidet, and oscillating spray patterns provide a spa-like experience. The new “Auto Open” lid with motion sensing is now faster and more responsive, and the built-in air purifier with a carbon filter keeps the bathroom odor-free. Health monitoring features include a urine analysis sensor (for pH and specific gravity) and a seat occupancy sensor that tracks bathroom visits over time via the TOTO app.

**Pros:** Unmatched build quality, industry-leading flush performance, comprehensive health monitoring, quiet operation (less than 40 decibels).
**Cons:** Extremely high price, requires professional installation, limited color options (only white and bone).

### 2. Kohler PureWash E930 (2026 Model)
**Price Estimate:** $3,200 - $3,800

The Kohler PureWash E930 strikes an excellent balance between premium features and relative affordability. This model features Kohler’s Quiet-Close lid and seat with a gentle-close mechanism, a heated seat with multiple temperature settings, and a powerful yet whisper-quiet bidet with front and rear wash options. The 2026 update adds an integrated water softener to prevent mineral buildup, a UV sanitizing light for the nozzle, and enhanced smart home compatibility—now supporting Matter protocol for direct integration with Apple Home, Google Home, and Amazon Alexa. The “Smart Drying” feature uses a variable-speed fan with a ceramic heater for efficient drying. The remote control is intuitive, and the accompanying Kohler Konnect app allows you to create user profiles with personalized wash settings.

**Pros:** Excellent value for features, Matter protocol support for broad smart home integration, UV sanitization, easy-to-clean design.
**Cons:** No health monitoring sensors, flush performance is good but not as powerful as TOTO, slightly louder operation (45 decibels).

### 3. Bio Bidet by Bemis Aura 800
**Price Estimate:** $1,800 - $2,300

For budget-conscious buyers who don’t want to sacrifice core smart toilet features, the Bio Bidet Aura 800 is a standout. This 2026 model is a one-piece toilet with a sleek, elongated design. It includes a heated seat, warm water bidet with adjustable pressure and temperature, a warm air dryer, and a deodorizer. The flush system uses a dual-flush mechanism (1.1 GPF for liquid, 1.6 GPF for solid) that is both powerful and water-efficient. The Aura 800 now features a motion-activated lid and seat, a nightlight, and a self-cleaning nozzle. While it lacks advanced health tracking or Matter compatibility, it offers reliable performance and easy installation—it can be installed by a competent DIYer in about two hours. The remote control is wireless and wall-mountable.

**Pros:** Affordable entry point, easy DIY installation, good water efficiency, all essential features included.
**Cons:** No health monitoring, limited smart home integration (only works with Bemis app), less premium build quality compared to TOTO/Kohler.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

- **Tech Enthusiasts:** If you love integrating your home with smart devices, a smart toilet with Matter support (like the Kohler PureWash E930) will fit perfectly into your ecosystem. You can create routines, such as having the seat warm up when your morning alarm goes off.
- **Health-Conscious Individuals:** The TOTO Neorest NX2’s health monitoring features can provide valuable data for tracking hydration levels and urinary health. This is ideal for anyone managing chronic conditions or simply wanting to be proactive about wellness.
- **Eco-Conscious Homeowners:** All smart toilets use significantly less water than traditional models. The Bio Bidet Aura 800’s dual-flush system is particularly efficient, saving up to 30% more water than a standard 1.6 GPF toilet.
- **Luxury Homeowners:** For those building or renovating a high-end master bathroom, the TOTO Neorest NX2 offers an unparalleled experience. Its quiet operation, premium materials, and advanced features justify the investment.
- **DIY Beginners:** The Bio Bidet Aura 800 is the best choice for those who want to install the toilet themselves. It comes with clear instructions and standard plumbing connections.

### Budget Considerations

- **Entry-Level ($1,500 - $2,500):** The Bio Bidet Aura 800 and similar models offer essential smart features like heated seats, bidets, and automatic lids. You’ll get good water efficiency and basic app control. Perfect for a guest bathroom or a budget-friendly primary bathroom upgrade.
- **Mid-Range ($3,000 - $5,000):** The Kohler PureWash E930 falls here. You gain superior build quality, better smart home integration (Matter), UV sanitization, and a more refined user experience. This is the sweet spot for most homeowners.
- **Premium ($6,000+):** The TOTO Neorest NX2 dominates this tier. You get the best flush technology, comprehensive health monitoring, and the quietest operation. This is for those who want the absolute best and are willing to pay for it.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Considerations

Installing a smart toilet is more complex than a standard toilet due to the electrical and water connections required. Here’s what you need to know:

1. **Electrical Requirements:** All smart toilets need a nearby GFCI-protected electrical outlet. If you’re replacing an existing toilet, you may need to run a new electrical line. A licensed electrician can handle this for $200-$500. The Bio Bidet Aura 800 can plug into a standard outlet, while the TOTO and Kohler models often require a dedicated circuit.
2. **Water Supply:** These toilets use a direct water line connection, typically 1/2-inch or 3/8-inch flexible hose. Ensure your existing shut-off valve is compatible. If not, a plumber can replace it for $100-$200.
3. **Rough-In Measurement:** The standard rough-in distance is 12 inches from the wall to the center of the waste pipe. Measure yours before purchasing. Most smart toilets accommodate 10-inch, 12-inch, or 14-inch rough-ins, but confirm compatibility.
4. **Professional vs. DIY:** For the TOTO Neorest NX2, professional installation is strongly recommended due to its weight (over 100 lbs) and complex wiring. The Kohler model is also heavy but can be installed by a skilled DIYer. The Bio Bidet Aura 800 is the lightest and most straightforward.

### Smart Home Integration

- **Wi-Fi Setup:** All three models connect to your home Wi-Fi (2.4 GHz). Use the manufacturer’s app for initial setup. Expect to spend 15-30 minutes connecting.
- **Voice Control:** The Kohler PureWash E930 supports Alexa, Google Assistant, and Siri via Matter. You can say, “Alexa, warm up the toilet seat” or “Hey Google, activate bidet.” The TOTO and Bio Bidet models have limited voice control (only through their own apps).
- **Routines & Automation:** With Matter, you can create scenes like “Good Morning” that turns on the bathroom light, warms the seat, and starts the deodorizer. The TOTO app allows you to schedule seat heating and cleaning cycles.
- **Health Data:** The TOTO Neorest NX2 syncs health data to Apple Health or Google Fit (via the TOTO app). You can view trends in your bathroom habits and share reports with your doctor.
