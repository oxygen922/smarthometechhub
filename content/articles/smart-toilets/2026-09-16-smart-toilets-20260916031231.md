# Smart Toilet Review 2026: The Best Intelligent Toilets for Every Budget

## Introduction

Smart toilets have evolved from luxury novelties into genuinely practical bathroom upgrades, and 2026 is shaping up to be the year they finally go mainstream. If you've ever walked into a hotel bathroom, felt the warm seat, heard the gentle whoosh of a self-cleaning bidet, and thought "I need this at home," you're not alone. Today's smart toilets combine bidet functionality, heated seating, automatic flushing, deodorization, and even health-tracking sensors into a single integrated unit—no separate bidet attachment required.

But with prices ranging from around $600 to well over $5,000, choosing the right model can feel overwhelming. Brands like TOTO, Kohler, Bio Bidet, and Woodbridge all compete for your bathroom, and each promises a different mix of luxury, efficiency, and smart home connectivity. In this in-depth smart toilet review, we'll break down our top three recommendations, the features that actually matter, installation realities, and how to match a model to your budget and household. Let's find the right intelligent toilet for your home.

## Top 3 Smart Toilet Recommendations

### 1. TOTO Neorest AS: The Best Overall Smart Toilet

**Estimated 2026 Price: $3,200–$3,800**

TOTO essentially invented the modern washlet, and the Neorest AS remains the benchmark against which every other smart toilet is measured. This integrated one-piece unit pairs TOTO's legendary bidet engineering with a sleek, skirted design that hides plumbing and makes cleaning remarkably easy.

What sets the Neorest AS apart is the sheer polish of its user experience. The **Washlet cleansing system** offers front and rear washes with oscillating and pulsating modes, plus a heated seat with instant warmth rather than the gradual warm-up you'll find on cheaper units. The **Tornado Flush** system uses cyclonic water jets to clean the bowl thoroughly while staying remarkably quiet—a real advantage in a primary bathroom near a bedroom.

The **actilight self-cleaning technology** uses UV light and a titanium dioxide-coated bowl surface to break down waste and neutralize odors automatically after each use. In practice, this means less scrubbing and fewer chemical cleaners. The **EWATER+ system** mists the wand and bowl with electrolyzed water after every flush, keeping things hygienic between deep cleans.

Smart home integration is where the Neorest AS shines. It works with the **TOTO Smart Toilet app**, supports voice control through Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant, and includes a wireless remote with programmable user presets. You can set separate preferences for different family members, adjust water temperature and pressure, and even schedule energy-saving modes.

**Pros:**
- Best-in-class cleaning and drying performance
- Actilight UV self-cleaning genuinely reduces maintenance
- Quiet, powerful Tornado Flush
- Reliable app and voice assistant support

**Cons:**
- Premium price
- Requires a dedicated GFCI outlet near the toilet
- Professional installation strongly recommended

**Verdict:** If budget allows and you want the most refined, lowest-maintenance smart toilet available, the TOTO Neorest AS is the one to beat.

### 2. Kohler Numera: The Best Mid-Range Smart Toilet

**Estimated 2026 Price: $1,800–$2,400**

Kohler's Numera hits a sweet spot that many shoppers are looking for: genuine smart toilet features without the five-figure luxury markup. It's a one-piece elongated toilet with a clean, contemporary silhouette that fits comfortably in most standard bathrooms.

The Numera delivers the essentials exceptionally well. You get a **heated seat** with adjustable temperature, **warm air drying**, and a **bidet wand** with adjustable water pressure and temperature. The **dual-flush system** lets you choose between 0.8 and 1.28 gallons per flush, making it WaterSense-certified and notably efficient for a smart toilet.

Kohler's **quiet-close seat** and **nightlight** are small touches that make a big difference for middle-of-the-night trips. The integrated **deodorizer** uses a carbon filter to keep the bathroom smelling fresh, and the **touchless flush** sensor means you never have to touch a handle.

Where the Numera trails the Neorest is in advanced self-cleaning and the depth of its app ecosystem. The Kohler Konnect app works well for adjusting settings and monitoring usage, but it's less feature-rich than TOTO's offering, and there's no UV self-cleaning system. Still, for the price, the feature-to-dollar ratio is excellent.

**Pros:**
- Strong value for a fully integrated smart toilet
- WaterSense-certified dual flush
- Reliable Kohler build quality and warranty
- Good app and voice control support

**Cons:**
- No UV or electrolyzed-water self-cleaning
- Drying performance is adequate but not exceptional
- Remote sold separately on some configurations

**Verdict:** The Kohler Numera is the smart toilet we recommend to most households—premium enough to feel luxurious, affordable enough to justify.

### 3. Bio Bidet Prodigy: The Best Value Smart Toilet

**Estimated 2026 Price: $900–$1,300**

If you want smart toilet functionality without a luxury price tag, the Bio Bidet Prodigy is the standout value pick. It's a one-piece integrated unit that covers the core features—heated seat, warm water bidet, warm air dryer, and deodorizer—at a price that undercuts most competitors by hundreds of dollars.

The Prodigy's **stainless steel self-cleaning nozzle** is a nice touch at this price point, and the **heated seat** warms quickly. The **tankless design** saves space and delivers consistent water pressure, while the **dual-flush** system keeps water usage reasonable. The included **wireless remote** is intuitive and lets you save two user presets.

You will notice the compromises. The build materials feel lighter than TOTO or Kohler units, the dryer is on the weaker side, and the app experience is basic compared to premium brands. Installation can also be trickier because the unit is heavier than a standard toilet and the electrical requirements are non-negotiable.

Even so, the Prodigy is the model we point budget-conscious buyers toward. It delivers roughly 80 percent of the premium experience for roughly 30 percent of the price.

**Pros:**
- Excellent price-to-feature ratio
- Stainless steel self-cleaning nozzle
- Tankless design with consistent pressure
- Includes wireless remote

**Cons:**
- Build quality below premium brands
- Weaker dryer performance
- Basic app functionality

**Verdict:** The Bio Bidet Prodigy is the best entry point into smart toilets for most American homes.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

Smart toilets make the most sense for:

- **Homeowners planning to stay put** who want to add lasting value and comfort to a primary bathroom.
- **Households with mobility concerns** — the hands-free flushing, heated seating, and bidet cleansing are genuinely helpful for seniors or anyone with limited mobility.
- **Bidet-curious buyers** who want an integrated solution rather than a bolt-on attachment.
- **Tech-forward homes** already using smart home ecosystems.

You might skip a smart toilet if you're renting, planning to move within a year, or if your bathroom lacks a nearby electrical outlet.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $1,000:** Expect core features—heated seat, bidet, basic dryer. Build quality and advanced self-cleaning will be limited.
- **$1,000–$2,500:** The value sweet spot. You get solid construction, reliable features, and often app control.
- **$2,500–$4,000:** Premium territory with UV self-cleaning, superior drying, and refined design.
- **$4,000+:** Luxury flagship models with every conceivable feature.

Don't forget **installation costs**, which can run $300–$800 for professional plumbing and electrical work, and the cost of a **GFCI outlet** if one isn't already near the toilet.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

Installing a smart toilet is more involved than swapping a standard toilet. Here's what to expect:

**Electrical requirements:** Every smart toilet needs a grounded GFCI outlet within roughly three feet of the unit. If your bathroom doesn't have one, you'll need an electrician—this is the most common hidden cost.

**Plumbing:** Most smart toilets use a standard 12-inch rough-in, but always verify your measurement before ordering. Tankless models require adequate water pressure (typically 20+ psi).

**Physical installation:** These units are heavy—often 80–120 pounds. Professional installation is strongly recommended, both to protect your investment and to ensure proper sealing and alignment.

**Smart home integration:** All three of our picks support app control, and TOTO and Kohler offer robust Alexa and Google Assistant integration. You can add voice commands like "Alexa, turn on the bidet" or schedule energy-saving modes. Some models also integrate with IFTTT for advanced automation, such as triggering a bathroom exhaust fan after flushing.

**Pro tip:** Set up your app and user presets immediately after installation. Programming personalized wash and heat settings for each family member is where smart toilets truly earn their keep.
