- **Eco-Conscious Consumers:** Smart bidet seats drastically reduce toilet paper usage, making them an environmentally friendly choice. Many models also have energy-saving modes that reduce power consumption when not in use.

- **Individuals with Mobility Issues:** The auto open/close lid, heated seat, and hands-free controls make smart bidet seats ideal for seniors or those with limited mobility. The cleansing functions also improve personal hygiene for those who may have difficulty with traditional cleaning methods.

- **Families with Children:** Multiple user profiles allow each family member to have their preferred settings. The gentle spray modes are safe for children, and the night light helps with nighttime bathroom visits.

- **$500-$900:** This is the sweet spot for most buyers. You get advanced features like multiple spray modes, auto lid, and full smart assistant support. The Toto Washlet S7Ae ($899) is the best overall performer in this range.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Process

Installing a smart bidet seat is generally straightforward, but there are a few key steps:

1. **Prepare the Area:** Ensure you have a GFCI-protected electrical outlet within 3 feet of the toilet. Measure your toilet to confirm it’s compatible (most models fit standard elongated or round bowls).

2. **Remove the Existing Seat:** Unscrew the bolts holding your current toilet seat in place and remove it.

3. **Install the Mounting Plate:** Attach the bidet seat’s mounting plate to the toilet using the provided hardware. This plate secures the seat in place.

4. **Connect the Water Supply:** Turn off the water supply valve behind the toilet. Disconnect the existing supply line from the toilet tank, then connect it to the bidet seat’s T-valve. Attach a new supply line from the T-valve to the toilet tank.

5. **Attach the Seat:** Slide the bidet seat onto the mounting plate until it clicks into place. Connect the power cord to the outlet.

6. **Test and Configure:** Turn on the water supply and check for leaks. Power on the unit and follow the manufacturer’s instructions to pair it with your smart home system.

### Smart Home Integration

Once installed, integrating your bidet seat with your smart home ecosystem is the next step:

- **Voice Control:** For Alexa and Google Assistant, enable the relevant skill or action in the respective app. You can then use commands like “Alexa, turn on the heated seat” or “Hey Google, start the bidet.”

- **Apple HomeKit:** If your model supports it (like the Kohler PureWash E930), add it to the Home app by scanning the QR code. You can then control it via Siri or include it in automations, such as “Turn on the bidet seat when the bathroom light turns on.”

- **Routines and Automation:** Create routines that trigger the bidet seat based on time or other events. For example, set the seat to warm up 15 minutes before your morning alarm, or have the night light activate when motion is detected in the bathroom.

- **Multiple User Profiles:** Most apps allow you to create profiles for each family member. Each profile can store preferred settings for spray temperature, pressure, seat heat, and more. The seat can automatically switch profiles based on weight detection or manual selection.
