# The Ultimate Guide to Smart Toilets: Top Picks, Features, and 2026 Buying Advice

## Introduction

The bathroom is the last frontier of the smart home revolution. While we’ve spent the last decade automating our lighting, thermostats, and security systems, the humble toilet has remained largely untouched—until now. Smart toilets have evolved from novelty Japanese gadgets into sophisticated, wellness-focused appliances that offer heated seats, bidet washing, automatic lids, and even health analytics. As we move into 2026, these units are becoming more accessible, with advanced features trickling down to mid-range price points.

But navigating the market can be overwhelming. With names like Toto, Kohler, and Bio Bidet dominating the space, how do you choose? This comprehensive review will break down the top three smart toilets of 2026, dissect the key features you need to consider, and provide a detailed buying guide to ensure you make a confident investment. Whether you’re a tech enthusiast looking to complete your smart home ecosystem or a homeowner seeking luxury and hygiene, this guide has you covered.

## Top 3 Product Recommendations

After extensive testing and analysis of current market trends, here are our top three smart toilet recommendations for 2026. These picks represent the best in class across different budget tiers and feature sets.

### 1. Toto Neorest NX2 (The Luxury Leader)

**Price Estimate (2026):** $9,500 - $11,000
**Best For:** Those who want the absolute pinnacle of Japanese engineering and integrated design.

The Toto Neorest NX2 remains the gold standard in the smart toilet industry. It’s not just a toilet; it’s a complete hygiene system. The unit features Toto’s signature *EWater+* technology, which uses electrolyzed water to break down waste and keep the bowl cleaner for longer. The *ActiGlow* nightlight is subtle yet functional, guiding you to the seat without blinding you.

**Performance:** The *Cyclone Flush* system is a marvel of fluid dynamics. It uses a powerful, tornado-like rinse that scours the entire bowl, leaving it spotless with a single flush. The seat is heated with a temperature sensor that adjusts based on room ambient temperature, ensuring it’s never too hot or cold. The bidet wand offers a range of oscillating and pulsating massage modes, with precise water pressure and temperature controls.

**Smart Features:** The NX2 comes with a wireless remote that is intuitive and backlit. It features automatic lid opening and closing, which responds to proximity sensors. The integrated air purifier uses a zeolite filter to neutralize odors, a feature that is noticeably effective. It also includes a pre-mist function that wets the bowl before use to prevent sticking.

**Verdict:** The NX2 is heavy, both literally and figuratively. It requires a dedicated 120V GFCI outlet and professional installation. However, if you want a toilet that feels like a spa treatment every time you use it, the NX2 is unmatched. The build quality is impeccable, and Toto’s reliability is legendary. The only downside is the price, which places it firmly in the luxury category.

### 2. Kohler Veil Intelligent Toilet (The Design Forward Choice)

**Price Estimate (2026):** $6,000 - $7,500
**Best For:** Design-conscious homeowners who want a seamless, minimalist aesthetic with robust smart home integration.

Kohler has positioned the Veil as a design-forward alternative to the Toto. It features a sleek, one-piece design with a concealed trapway that creates a floating, wall-hugging look. The *Veil* is available in a variety of colors, including matte black, which is a rare option in this category.

**Performance:** The *Veil* utilizes a *Class Five* flushing technology, which is incredibly powerful yet quiet. The bidet function offers a range of customizable settings, including front and rear wash, oscillating massage, and a dryer that actually works well—a rarity in the industry. The seat is heated and features a soft-close lid that prevents slamming.

**Smart Features:** This is where the Veil shines for the smart home enthusiast. It connects via Wi-Fi to the Kohler Konnect app, allowing you to control the seat temperature, water temperature, and even set up personalized user profiles. You can create a profile for each family member, and the toilet will automatically adjust settings when it detects the associated remote or phone nearby. It also integrates with Amazon Alexa and Google Home, allowing for voice commands like "Alexa, warm the seat."

**Verdict:** The Veil is beautiful and functional. The integration with Kohler Konnect is seamless, and the app is well-designed. However, it lacks the advanced odor removal system of the Toto, and the remote control feels slightly less premium. If you prioritize aesthetics and smart home compatibility over raw water pressure, the Veil is an excellent choice.

### 3. Bio Bidet Bliss BB-2000 (The Value Champion)

**Price Estimate (2026):** $1,200 - $1,500 (for the seat attachment)
**Best For:** Renters or homeowners who want smart toilet features without replacing their existing porcelain.

Not everyone is ready to drop $6,000 on a luxury unit. The Bio Bidet Bliss BB-2000 is a smart bidet seat that transforms your existing toilet into a high-tech appliance. While it’s technically a "seat," it offers 90% of the features of the luxury units at a fraction of the cost.

**Performance:** The BB-2000 features a tankless, instant water heating system, meaning you never run out of warm water. The dual nozzles (front and rear) are self-cleaning and offer adjustable water pressure and temperature. The seat is heated with multiple temperature settings. It also includes a warm air dryer and a deodorizer that uses a carbon filter.

**Smart Features:** The included wireless remote is easy to use, but the BB-2000 lacks Wi-Fi connectivity. This is a trade-off for the price. However, it does feature an "Auto Flush" function that uses an infrared sensor to flush the toilet when you step away, which is a premium feature often only found on integrated units.

**Verdict:** This is the best value proposition on the market. It’s easy to install (most users can do it in under 30 minutes without a plumber), and it dramatically improves your bathroom experience. The lack of smart home integration is a downside for tech purists, but the core functionality—wash, dry, and heat—is on par with units costing three times more.

### Breaking Down the Essentials

- **Integrated vs. Seat:** Integrated toilets look sleek and are easier to clean, but they are expensive and require a full bathroom remodel if you have a standard toilet. Seats are cheaper and easier to install but can look bulky on certain bowl shapes.
- **Flush Power:** Look for a flush rating of at least 1.28 gallons per flush (GPF) for water efficiency. The Toto Cyclone and Kohler Class Five systems are excellent at clearing waste in a single flush.
- **Instant vs. Tank Heating:** Always choose "instant" or "tankless" water heating. Storage tanks can run out of hot water during long wash cycles and are breeding grounds for bacteria.
- **Drying Power:** Many budget units have weak dryers. The Toto and Kohler dryers are adequate, but the Bio Bidet's is surprisingly strong for its price.
- **Connectivity:** If you want to integrate your toilet with your smart home hub, you need a Wi-Fi enabled model like the Kohler Veil. Others rely on physical remotes, which are reliable but less convenient.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

- **Tech Enthusiasts:** If you have a smart home setup with Alexa or Google Home, a connected toilet like the Kohler Veil is a natural addition.
- **Individuals with Mobility Issues:** The heated seat, auto-open lid, and bidet functions reduce the physical effort required to use the bathroom. The remote controls are easier to operate than twisting around to reach a manual flush handle.
- **Families:** Features like user profiles (on the Veil) allow each family member to have their own settings for seat temperature and wash pressure. The auto-flush and self-cleaning nozzles also promote better hygiene for children.
- **Eco-Conscious Consumers:** Smart toilets use less water per flush than traditional models. The bidet feature also significantly reduces toilet paper usage, which is a major environmental win.

### Budget Considerations

- **Entry-Level ($300 - $800):** You can find basic bidet seats with heated water and seat warming. These are a great introduction to the category.
- **Mid-Range ($1,000 - $2,500):** This is the sweet spot. The Bio Bidet BB-2000 falls here, offering premium washing features and auto-flush without the luxury price tag.
- **High-End ($5,000+):** Integrated units like the Toto and Kohler. Expect impeccable build quality, advanced hygiene features, and design integration.
- **Installation Costs:** Don't forget to budget for installation. A standard seat install is DIY. An integrated toilet requires a licensed plumber and potentially an electrician to install a GFCI outlet. This can add $500 to $1,500 to your total cost.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### The Installation Process

Installing a smart toilet is more complex than swapping out a standard model, but it's manageable with the right preparation.

**1. Electrical Requirements:** This is the most common hurdle. All smart toilets require a standard 120V electrical outlet (GFCI protected) within 3 feet of the toilet. If you don't have an outlet, you'll need to hire an electrician, which can be the most expensive part of the installation.

**2. Water Supply:** Integrated toilets often use a high-pressure water supply for the flush and a separate line for the bidet. You may need to upgrade your water supply line to a 3/4-inch line to ensure adequate pressure.

**3. Rough-In Measurement:** Measure the distance from the wall behind the toilet to the center of the floor bolts. A standard rough-in is 12 inches, but 10 and 14-inch options exist. Ensure your toilet matches this measurement to avoid a costly return.

**4. Professional vs. DIY:** For bidet seats, DIY is perfectly fine. For integrated toilets, we strongly recommend professional installation. These units are heavy (often over 100 lbs) and have complex water and electrical connections that are best handled by a pro.

### Smart Home Integration

- **Kohler Konnect:** The Kohler Veil is the leader here. It connects to your home Wi-Fi and allows for scheduling (e.g., "Warm the seat at 6:30 AM on weekdays"). It also works with IFTTT, allowing for automation like flushing when you say "Goodnight" to your smart speaker.
- **Toto Washlet+:** Toto has its own app, but it’s primarily for monitoring usage and controlling the washlet. It lacks the deep integration of Kohler's ecosystem.
- **Matter & Thread:** As of 2026, we are seeing early adoption of the Matter standard in smart toilets. This will eventually allow for seamless control across Apple HomeKit, SmartThings, and Alexa. However, most current models still rely on proprietary apps or cloud connections. For now, the Kohler Veil remains the most "smart home friendly" option on the market.

In the end, a smart toilet is an investment in your daily comfort and health. The initial cost is higher than a standard toilet, but the water savings, reduced paper usage, and improved hygiene make it a worthwhile upgrade for any modern home. Don't be afraid to start with a seat attachment if you're hesitant; the benefits are immediate, and you can always upgrade to a full unit later.
