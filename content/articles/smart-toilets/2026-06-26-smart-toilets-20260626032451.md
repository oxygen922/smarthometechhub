# The Ultimate Guide to Smart Toilets: Top Picks, Features, and Buying Tips for 2026

## Introduction

The smart toilet has evolved from a futuristic luxury into a practical, health-conscious upgrade for the modern American home. In 2026, these high-tech thrones offer more than just heated seats and automated flushes—they integrate seamlessly with your smart home ecosystem, provide advanced bidet functions, and even analyze your health metrics. Whether you’re remodeling a master bathroom or simply looking to elevate your daily routine, a smart toilet can transform your experience with water efficiency, hygiene, and personalized comfort. With prices ranging from budget-friendly models under $600 to premium units exceeding $3,000, there’s a smart toilet for every household. In this comprehensive review, I’ll break down the top three products on the market, compare key features, and guide you through installation and integration. By the end, you’ll know exactly which smart toilet is right for your home and budget.

## Top 3 Product Recommendations

### 1. Toto Neorest NX2 – The Luxury Standard

The Toto Neorest NX2 is the gold standard in smart toilets, blending Japanese engineering with cutting-edge technology. Priced around $3,200 in 2026, it’s an investment, but one that pays off in comfort and durability. The NX2 features Toto’s signature Tornado Flush system, which uses dual-nozzle technology to create a powerful, clog-free flush using only 1.0 gallon of water per use. The heated seat is contoured for ergonomic support, and the bidet offers adjustable water temperature, pressure, and position. What sets the NX2 apart is its integrated air dryer and deodorizer, which activate automatically after use. The bowl is coated with Toto’s CeFiONtect glaze, preventing waste from sticking and reducing cleaning frequency. For smart home integration, the NX2 works with Toto’s proprietary app, allowing you to set user profiles, schedule cleaning cycles, and monitor water usage. The only downside is the price—it’s not for budget-conscious buyers—and installation requires a dedicated electrical outlet and water line. If you want the ultimate bathroom experience, the NX2 is unmatched.

### 2. Kohler PureWash E930 – Best Mid-Range Value

For those seeking a balance between features and cost, the Kohler PureWash E930 is a standout at approximately $1,400. This bidet seat attachment fits most standard two-piece toilets, making it an excellent upgrade for existing bathrooms. The E930 offers a heated seat, warm water bidet with oscillating and pulsating modes, and a warm air dryer. Its remote control is intuitive, with presets for different users. The unit includes a UV sanitizing wand that cleans the nozzles after each use, ensuring hygiene. Smart home integration is robust: the E930 works with Amazon Alexa and Google Home, so you can start the bidet or warm the seat with voice commands. The Kohler Konnect app lets you track usage and adjust settings remotely. Installation is straightforward—it requires a power outlet near the toilet—and the unit fits elongated bowls. The only drawback is the lack of a built-in deodorizer, which some competitors include at this price. Overall, the E930 delivers premium features without breaking the bank.

### 3. Bio Bidet Flow 200 – Best Budget Smart Toilet

If you’re new to smart toilets or working with a tighter budget, the Bio Bidet Flow 200 is a fantastic entry point at roughly $550. This standalone smart toilet (not an add-on) includes a sleek, one-piece design with a skirted trapway for easy cleaning. The Flow 200 features a heated seat, front and rear bidet wash with adjustable pressure, and a warm air dryer. The flush is dual-flush (0.8/1.28 GPF) for water savings. While it lacks advanced features like UV sanitization or voice control, it does include a remote control and an LED nightlight. The self-cleaning nozzle retracts after use, maintaining hygiene. Installation is similar to a standard toilet but requires a GFCI outlet nearby. The build quality is solid for the price, though the seat is less ergonomic than premium models. The Flow 200 is ideal for guest bathrooms or budget-conscious homeowners who want the basics without the premium price tag.

## Key Features to Consider

When shopping for a smart toilet, focus on these five features to ensure you get the right model for your needs:

- **Bidet Functionality**: Look for adjustable water temperature, pressure, and nozzle position. Oscillating and pulsating modes enhance comfort.
- **Heated Seat**: Essential for cold climates. Some models offer variable temperature settings.
- **Flush Power**: Dual-flush systems (0.8/1.28 GPF) save water. Toto’s Tornado Flush is a benchmark for performance.
- **Smart Home Integration**: Compatibility with Amazon Alexa, Google Home, or Apple HomeKit allows voice control and automation.
- **Hygiene Features**: UV sanitization, self-cleaning nozzles, and deodorizers reduce maintenance and improve cleanliness.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

- **Tech Enthusiasts**: If you love smart home gadgets, a smart toilet integrates with your ecosystem and offers voice control.
- **Health-Conscious Users**: Bidet functions improve hygiene, and some models track health metrics like weight or urine analysis.
- **Eco-Friendly Homeowners**: Smart toilets use less water per flush than traditional models, saving on utility bills.
- **Seniors or Individuals with Mobility Issues**: Heated seats, automatic open/close lids, and bidet functions reduce physical strain.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $1,000**: Best for basic features like heated seats and bidet. Models like the Bio Bidet Flow 200 offer good value but lack advanced smart integration.
- **$1,000–$2,000**: Mid-range options like the Kohler PureWash E930 provide excellent smart features and hygiene upgrades.
- **Over $2,500**: Premium models like the Toto Neorest NX2 deliver top-tier comfort, durability, and health analytics.

Factor in installation costs: hiring a plumber for electrical and water line work can add $200–$500. Also, consider long-term savings on water and toilet paper (up to $100/year).

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Tips

Most smart toilets require a standard 110V GFCI outlet within 3 feet of the toilet. If you’re replacing a standard toilet, you’ll need to run a power line, which may require an electrician. For bidet seat attachments like the Kohler PureWash E930, installation is simpler: remove the existing seat, attach the unit to the bowl, and connect the T-valve to the water supply. Standalone smart toilets (e.g., Toto Neorest) are heavier and may need two people for placement. Always check the manufacturer’s clearance requirements—some models need extra space behind the toilet for the control unit.

### Smart Home Integration

- **Alexa/Google Home**: With the Kohler E930, you can say, “Alexa, start the bidet” or “Hey Google, warm the seat.” This is ideal for hands-free operation.
- **Apple HomeKit**: Currently limited—most smart toilets don’t support HomeKit natively. However, you can use a smart plug to control power, though features like bidet and dryer won’t be accessible.
- **Proprietary Apps**: Toto and Kohler offer dedicated apps for scheduling, user profiles, and maintenance alerts. These apps are essential for unlocking full functionality.
- **Routine Automation**: Use IFTTT or routines to preheat the seat before your morning alarm or turn on the nightlight when motion is detected.
