## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Overview

Installing a smart bidet seat is a DIY-friendly project that typically takes 30–60 minutes. Here’s what to expect:

1. **Check Compatibility:** Most bidet seats fit standard two-piece toilets with elongated or round bowls. Measure your toilet’s mounting hole spacing (typically 5.5 inches on center) and bowl shape before purchasing.
2. **Electrical Requirements:** You’ll need a GFCI-protected outlet within 3 feet of the toilet. If you don’t have one, hire an electrician—this is not a DIY task.
3. **Water Supply:** The kit includes a T-adapter that connects to your existing toilet water supply line. No plumbing skills required.
4. **Mounting:** Slide the seat onto the mounting plate and secure it. Most models use a quick-release mechanism for easy removal and cleaning.

**Pro Tip:** If you’re renting, consider a non-electric bidet attachment as a temporary solution. They cost under $100 and install in minutes without any electrical work.

### Smart Home Integration

All three recommended models support voice control and app integration, but the experience varies:

- **Amazon Alexa & Google Assistant:** The Toto S550e and Bio Bidet BB-2000 offer the most responsive voice control. You can say commands like, “Alexa, turn on the bidet,” or “Hey Google, set the seat temperature to high.” The Kohler BH-90 also works well but requires the Konnect skill.
- **Apps:** The Bio Bidet app is the most intuitive, allowing you to create schedules, monitor water usage, and adjust settings remotely. Toto’s app is functional but less polished, while Kohler’s Konnect app is excellent for those already in the Kohler ecosystem.
- **IFTTT & Routines:** For advanced users, the Toto and Kohler models support IFTTT (If This Then That) integration, enabling routines like “When I leave home, turn off the heated seat” or “When my morning alarm goes off, start the bidet pre-rinse.”

**Smart Home Tip:** For the best experience, pair your bidet seat with a smart bathroom fan and humidity sensor. This creates a fully automated bathroom that maintains comfort and prevents mold.
