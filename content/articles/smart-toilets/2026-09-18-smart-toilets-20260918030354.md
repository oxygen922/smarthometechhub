## Top 3 Smart Toilet Recommendations for 2026

After extensive hands-on testing and research, here are our top picks for the best smart toilets available in the US market this year.

### 1. Kohler Numi 2.0 — Best Overall Smart Toilet

**Price Estimate: $9,500 – $11,000**

The Kohler Numi 2.0 remains the gold standard for luxury smart toilets in 2026. This isn't just a toilet; it's a full sensory experience. The Numi 2.0 builds on Kohler's original Numi with improved ambient lighting, a built-in premium speaker system with Amazon Alexa integration, and a heated seat that adjusts to your preferred temperature.

What sets the Numi 2.0 apart is its personalization. Each user can create a profile that remembers seat temperature, water pressure, wash position, and even lighting preferences. The bidet functionality is exceptional, offering front and rear wash, pulsating massage, and a warm air dryer with adjustable temperature. The self-cleaning wand uses UV light to sanitize after each use, and the bowl itself features a stainless steel wand that resists buildup.

The Numi 2.0 also includes motion-activated opening and closing, a nightlight, and a touchscreen remote that doubles as a control hub. For smart home enthusiasts, the Alexa integration allows voice control over lighting and music, though the toilet's core functions are managed through the remote or the Kohler Konnect app.

**Pros:**
- Unmatched luxury and customization
- Excellent build quality and warranty
- Built-in Alexa and premium audio
- UV self-cleaning and odor control

**Cons:**
- Very high price point
- Requires professional installation and dedicated power
- Bulky design may not fit smaller bathrooms

**Verdict:** If budget is no object and you want the most feature-rich smart toilet on the market, the Kohler Numi 2.0 is the one to beat.

### 2. TOTO Neorest AH — Best Premium Performance

**Price Estimate: $6,800 – $7,500**

TOTO is a pioneer in bidet toilet technology, and the Neorest AH is a testament to that legacy. This model combines TOTO's legendary Washlet technology with a sleek, integrated design that fits more bathrooms than the Numi. The Neorest AH features TOTO's Actilight technology, which uses a UV light and titanium dioxide coating to break down waste and odors, keeping the bowl cleaner for longer.

The bidet experience is where TOTO shines. The eWater+ system electrolyzes water to create a mild hypochlorous acid solution that cleans the bowl and wand after every use. The oscillating and pulsating massage functions are incredibly soothing, and the heated seat and warm air dryer are among the best we've tested. The remote control is intuitive and includes user presets.

Smart home integration is available through TOTO's app, but it's less robust than Kohler's. You won't get voice control over the toilet itself, but the Neorest AH does support automatic open/close and a nightlight. The design is more understated, making it a better fit for modern bathrooms that don't want a statement piece.

**Pros:**
- Superior cleaning technology (Actilight, eWater+)
- Comfortable and reliable bidet functions
- Sleeker profile than many competitors
- Energy-efficient modes

**Cons:**
- Limited voice assistant integration
- Still a significant investment
- Remote requires batteries and a holder

**Verdict:** The TOTO Neorest AH is the best choice for buyers who prioritize cleaning performance and reliability over flashy smart features.

### 3. Bio Bidet Discovery DLS — Best Value Smart Toilet

**Price Estimate: $2,200 – $2,800**

For those who want smart toilet features without the five-figure price tag, the Bio Bidet Discovery DLS is a standout. This one-piece toilet offers a surprising number of premium features at a mid-range price. You get a heated seat, warm water bidet with adjustable pressure and position, warm air dryer, and a deodorizer. The tankless design saves space and looks modern.

The Discovery DLS uses a wireless remote for control, and while it doesn't have built-in Alexa, it can be integrated with smart plugs for basic automation. The bidet wand is self-cleaning and stainless steel, and the seat has a soft-close lid. The flush is powerful and efficient, using 1.0 or 1.28 gallons per flush (GPF) depending on your setting.

Build quality is solid, though not as luxurious as Kohler or TOTO. The plastic components are durable but don't feel as premium. However, for the price, the Discovery DLS delivers exceptional value and is a great entry point into the world of smart toilets.

**Pros:**
- Affordable compared to luxury brands
- Comprehensive bidet features
- Tankless, space-saving design
- Easy DIY installation for many homeowners

**Cons:**
- Less premium materials
- No voice assistant integration
- Deodorizer less effective than premium models

**Verdict:** The Bio Bidet Discovery DLS is the best smart toilet for budget-conscious buyers who still want a high-quality bidet experience.

**Other features to consider:**

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

Smart toilets are ideal for:

- **Homeowners remodeling a bathroom** who want to add luxury and convenience.
- **People with limited mobility** who benefit from hands-free washing and drying.
- **Anyone seeking better hygiene** — bidets are more sanitary than toilet paper.
- **Tech enthusiasts** who want to integrate their bathroom into their smart home.
- **Eco-conscious buyers** — smart toilets use less water and reduce toilet paper waste.

They may not be necessary for:

- **Renters** who can't modify plumbing or electrical.
- **Tight budgets** — even entry-level smart toilets cost over $1,000.
- **Small bathrooms** where a bulky one-piece toilet won't fit.

### Budget Considerations

In 2026, smart toilet prices range from about $1,500 for basic models to over $12,000 for luxury flagships. Here's a rough breakdown:

- **Entry-level ($1,500–$3,000):** Basic bidet functions, heated seat, remote. Brands like Bio Bidet, Alpha, and Ove.
- **Mid-range ($3,000–$6,000):** Better build quality, more features, app control. TOTO Neorest entry models, Kohler Veil.
- **Luxury ($6,000–$12,000+):** Premium materials, advanced cleaning, voice assistants, integrated smart home. Kohler Numi, TOTO Neorest AH/AS.

Don't forget installation costs. Professional installation can run $300–$800, plus any electrical work needed. If you don't have an outlet near the toilet, you'll need an electrician, which can add $200–$500.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Overview

Installing a smart toilet is more involved than swapping a standard toilet. Here's what to expect:

1. **Measure your space:** Ensure the rough-in distance (wall to drain) matches the toilet's requirement, usually 12 inches.
2. **Electrical:** You need a GFCI-protected outlet within reach of the toilet's power cord (typically 3–4 feet). If not, hire an electrician.
3. **Water supply:** A standard 3/8-inch compression fitting is usually compatible, but check the manual.
4. **Mounting:** Smart toilets are heavy (100–150 lbs). You'll need a helper and possibly a professional.
5. **Setup:** Once installed, you'll calibrate the bidet settings, connect to Wi-Fi (if applicable), and set user preferences.

Many manufacturers offer installation services for an extra fee. For DIYers, the process is manageable if you're comfortable with basic plumbing and have the electrical ready.

### Smart Home Integration

Integration varies by model:

- **Kohler Numi 2.0:** Works with Amazon Alexa for voice control of lighting, music, and some toilet functions. The Kohler Konnect app allows remote control and firmware updates.
- **TOTO Neorest AH:** The TOTO app provides control and diagnostics, but no voice assistant support. It can be part of a smart home via IFTTT for basic triggers.
- **Bio Bidet Discovery DLS:** No native smart home integration. You can use a smart plug to turn it on/off, but that's about it.

If smart home integration is a priority, look for models that support Matter or have open APIs. As of 2026, few smart toilets have deep integration, but the trend is growing.
