## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation

A common misconception is that installing a bidet seat requires a plumber. In most cases, it is a straightforward DIY project that takes 30-45 minutes. Here’s what you need:

1.  **Water Supply:** You need access to the toilet's water supply line. The kit will include a T-adapter to divert water to the bidet seat.
2.  **Electrical Outlet:** This is the most critical requirement. All electric bidet seats require a standard 3-prong GFCI (Ground Fault Circuit Interrupter) outlet. If you don't have one near your toilet, you will need an electrician to install one. This is a non-negotiable safety requirement.
3.  **Mounting:** The seat slides onto a mounting plate that attaches to the toilet's existing bolt holes. Ensure your toilet is a standard elongated or round shape, as the seat must match.

**Pro Tip:** Before purchasing, measure the distance between the mounting bolts and the bowl's shape. Most models, like the ones reviewed, offer both round and elongated versions.

### Smart Home Integration

The 2026 generation of bidet seats is more connected than ever, but integration levels vary.

- **Wi-Fi (TOTO):** The TOTO S7A connects directly to your home Wi-Fi. This allows for over-the-air firmware updates and integration with IFTTT (If This Then That) for advanced automation. For example, you can have the bidet seat pre-heat when your smart thermostat detects you are on your way home.
- **Bluetooth (Bio Bidet):** The Flow 200 uses Bluetooth for app control. This is a short-range connection (within 15-20 feet), meaning you'll need to be in the bathroom to use the app. It's perfect for setting presets but doesn't allow for remote control from outside the home.
- **Analog (Kohlo):** The Kohlo has no smart integration, offering a purely analog experience. This reduces potential security risks and complexity.

For voice control, you will typically need a smart home hub. For instance, if you have a SmartThings hub, you can often create routines that trigger a bidet's "night mode" or "eco mode" via a voice command to Alexa. However, be aware that no bidet seat currently features built-in microphones for direct voice control; they rely on the hub.
