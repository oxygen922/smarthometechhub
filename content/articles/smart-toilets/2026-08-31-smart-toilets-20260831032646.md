# The Ultimate Smart Toilet Review: Transform Your Bathroom Into a High-Tech Sanctuary

## Introduction

The smart toilet has evolved from a novelty found in Tokyo hotels to a bona fide home improvement essential for tech-savvy Americans. As we move through 2026, the market has exploded with options that promise heated seats, bidet functions, and even health monitoring—all controlled via smartphone apps. But with price tags ranging from $500 to over $8,000, choosing the right model can feel overwhelming. Whether you're building a new master bath or retrofitting an existing powder room, the right smart toilet can reduce toilet paper usage by up to 75%, improve personal hygiene, and add a "wow factor" that impresses every guest. In this comprehensive review, I'll break down the top three smart toilets currently dominating the US market, what features genuinely matter, and how to integrate these high-tech thrones into your existing smart home ecosystem.

---

## Top 3 Product Recommendations

### 1. TOTO Neorest NX2 (Best Overall Premium Choice)

**Price (2026 Estimate):** $7,800–$8,500

TOTO remains the gold standard in Japanese toilet technology, and the Neorest NX2 is their flagship masterpiece. This unit features their signature "ACTILIGHT" self-cleaning system that uses electrolyzed water to break down waste and prevent bacterial buildup. The "POWER TORNADO" flush system creates a dual-nozzle vortex that cleans the bowl more effectively than gravity-fed alternatives while using 20% less water than standard toilets.

**Performance Highlights:**
- **Washlet Technology:** The NX2 offers oscillating, pulsating, and wide-angle spray patterns with adjustable water temperature, pressure, and position. The "EWATER+" system pre-moistens the bowl before use, preventing waste from sticking.
- **Comfort Features:** The heated seat (with temperature memory for up to three users), warm air dryer with three heat settings, and deodorizing system (activated by motion sensor) make this feel like a spa experience.
- **Smart Capabilities:** The NX2 integrates with TOTO's "Washlet+" app, allowing you to customize spray patterns, monitor usage, and receive cleaning reminders. It also supports Alexa and Google Home voice commands—"Alexa, flush the toilet" works flawlessly.

**Pros:**
- Unmatched build quality; TOTO has been perfecting this technology for four decades
- Whisper-quiet operation (29 decibels—quieter than a library)
- The self-cleaning bowl genuinely reduces manual scrubbing to near zero

**Cons:**
- Astronomical price point; you're paying for the brand name
- Requires a dedicated 120V GFCI outlet within 3 feet of the toilet
- The app, while functional, feels dated compared to newer competitors

**Verdict:** If budget isn't a concern and you want the best-engineered smart toilet on the market, the NX2 is your answer. It's built to last 15+ years with minimal maintenance.

---

### 2. Kohler Veil Intelligent Toilet (Best Mid-Range Value)

**Price (2026 Estimate):** $3,200–$3,800

Kohler has positioned the Veil as the smart toilet for the American homeowner who wants premium features without the Japanese import markup. The Veil features a sleek, one-piece design with a concealed trapway that makes cleaning the exterior remarkably easy—there are no crevices for dust or grime to collect.

**Performance Highlights:**
- **Comfort Height Design:** The Veil sits at a 16.5-inch bowl height (toilet seat height, not including the seat), which is ADA-compliant and comfortable for taller users.
- **Dual-Flush Efficiency:** The Veil offers both 1.1 and 1.6 gallons per flush options, making it WaterSense certified. The "ForceFlush" technology ensures a powerful, clog-free flush even with the lower water volume.
- **Smart Features:** The "Kohler Konnect" app provides full control over seat temperature, water pressure, and dryer settings. It also tracks usage patterns and can alert you to potential leaks or malfunctions.
- **Night Light:** The Veil includes a soft, ambient night light with color options (blue, green, white) that activates via motion sensor—a genuinely useful feature for middle-of-the-night bathroom trips.

**Pros:**
- Excellent value; you get 90% of the TOTO's features for 40% of the price
- The concealed trapway makes cleaning the exterior a 2-minute job
- Better app integration than TOTO, with a more intuitive user interface

**Cons:**
- The seat is slightly less ergonomic than TOTO's design; longer sits can cause discomfort
- The dryer is less powerful than TOTO's, taking 30-40 seconds longer to fully dry
- No built-in health monitoring features (more on that below)

**Verdict:** The Veil is the sweet spot for most homeowners. It delivers a premium experience without the premium shock to your bank account.

### 3. BioBidet Flow 9000 (Best Budget-Friendly Option)

**Price (2026 Estimate):** $1,100–$1,400

BioBidet has carved out a niche as the accessible smart toilet brand, and the Flow 9000 is their most compelling offering. This is a "smart seat" that attaches to your existing toilet bowl, which keeps the price down significantly while still providing the core smart toilet experience.

**Performance Highlights:**
- **Installation Flexibility:** The Flow 9000 fits most standard two-piece toilets with elongated bowls. Installation takes about 30 minutes with basic tools (adjustable wrench and screwdriver).
- **Core Features:** You get the essentials: heated seat (with adjustable temperature), warm water bidet (with front and rear cleaning modes), and a warm air dryer. The water temperature is consistent and comfortable.
- **User Presets:** The seat memory function stores profiles for up to two users, remembering preferred water pressure, temperature, and spray position.
- **Manual Override:** Unlike the TOTO and Kohler, the Flow 9000 has a physical control panel on the side, so you don't need the app to use any features. This is a significant advantage for guests who aren't tech-savvy.

**Pros:**
- The most affordable way to get into the smart toilet game
- Simple, reliable operation; no firmware updates or connectivity issues
- The side control panel is intuitive and easy for guests to understand

**Cons:**
- No smart home integration (no Alexa, Google Home, or app control)
- Doesn't include a self-cleaning bowl or deodorizer
- The seat is thinner and less comfortable than the premium options
- Requires a GFCI outlet near the toilet

**Verdict:** If you're a renter or just want to test the smart toilet waters without committing to a full replacement, the Flow 9000 is a smart entry point.

**The Verdict on Features:**

- **Health Monitoring:** This is the most overhyped feature in the smart toilet category. Some high-end models (like the Kohler Numi 2.0) claim to analyze urine for glucose and protein levels, but the FDA hasn't cleared any consumer smart toilet for medical diagnostics as of early 2026. Don't pay a premium for this.
- **Self-Cleaning:** This is genuinely transformative. If you hate scrubbing toilets, the electrolyzed water or UV light systems reduce manual cleaning to once a month (versus weekly for standard toilets).
- **Heated Seats:** This is the feature that converts skeptics. The moment you sit on a cold seat after using a heated one, you'll never want to go back.
- **Deodorizers:** These work reasonably well for masking odors, but they're not magic. If you have a small, poorly ventilated bathroom, you'll still want an exhaust fan.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

**Ideal candidates include:**
- **Families with young children:** The bidet function helps with potty training and reduces messes.
- **Seniors or individuals with mobility issues:** Heated seats, hands-free flushing, and no need to twist around to wipe make bathroom visits easier and safer.
- **Eco-conscious homeowners:** Smart toilets use significantly less water and reduce toilet paper consumption.
- **Tech enthusiasts:** If you already have smart lights, thermostats, and locks, a smart toilet is a natural addition to your ecosystem.

**Who should skip it:**
- **Renters:** Unless you can install a seat-style unit (like the BioBidet) and take it with you when you move, a full smart toilet is a permanent fixture.
- **Those with older plumbing:** Smart toilets require a dedicated electrical outlet and, in some cases, a water pressure of at least 20 PSI. If your home has galvanized pipes or low pressure, you may need a plumber to assess compatibility.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $1,500:** Stick with smart seat attachments. You'll get the bidet and heated seat experience without the premium flush and cleaning systems.
- **$1,500–$4,000:** This is the sweet spot for full smart toilets. The Kohler Veil and similar models from American Standard and Duravit offer excellent performance at this price.
- **$4,000–$8,000+:** You're paying for brand prestige, premium materials, and the most advanced cleaning systems. The TOTO NX2 and Kohler Numi 2.0 live here.

**Hidden Costs to Factor In:**
- Electrical work: If you don't have a GFCI outlet near your toilet, expect to pay $150–$300 for an electrician.
- Professional installation: Full smart toilets require a plumber for proper sealing and water line connection. Budget $200–$400.
- Extended warranty: Most smart toilets come with 1–3 year warranties. Extended coverage adds $100–$200 but may be worth it for the electronics.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Process

**Full Smart Toilet Installation:**
1. **Pre-Installation Check:** Ensure your bathroom has a GFCI outlet within 3 feet of the toilet bowl. If not, hire an electrician before the plumber arrives.
2. **Water Supply:** The smart toilet will use the existing water supply line, but you may need an adapter kit. Most premium models include one.
3. **Plumber vs. DIY:** I strongly recommend hiring a licensed plumber for full toilet installations. The risk of a poor seal (leading to leaks) or damage to the internal electronics is too high for DIY if you're not experienced. Expect the job to take 1–2 hours.
4. **Post-Installation:** Run the self-diagnostic cycle (most models have this) to ensure all sensors and jets are working correctly.

**Smart Seat Installation (BioBidet Flow 9000):**
1. Turn off the water supply and flush to empty the tank.
2. Remove the existing seat and install the mounting bracket.
3. Connect the T-connector to the water supply line.
4. Attach the seat and plug in the power cord.
5. Test the bidet function before reinstalling the tank lid.

### Smart Home Integration

**Voice Control:**
- **Alexa:** "Alexa, turn on the bidet" works with both TOTO and Kohler (via their respective skills). You can also set routines—for example, "Alexa, good morning" could trigger a pre-heated seat and a gentle spray.
- **Google Home:** Similar functionality, but commands are less granular. You can adjust temperature and pressure, but not position.

**App Control:**
- **TOTO Washlet+:** The app is primarily for monitoring (usage stats, cleaning reminders) and customizing settings. It's not a daily-use app.
- **Kohler Konnect:** More polished, with a cleaner UI and the ability to set schedules (e.g., seat warm at 7 AM every day).

**Automation Tips:**
- Set a "guest mode" via the app that resets settings to defaults after a visitor uses the toilet.
- Use motion sensors to trigger the deodorizer and night light automatically.
- If you have a smart water leak detector (like Flo by Moen), place one near the toilet base to catch any slow leaks before they cause damage.

**Compatibility Note:** Neither TOTO nor Kohler currently supports Apple HomeKit or Matter protocol. If you're deep in the Apple ecosystem, you'll need to bridge via a Homebridge plugin (for tech-savvy users) or stick with a non-smart toilet.

The hygiene benefits are undeniable—you genuinely feel cleaner after using a bidet than with toilet paper alone. The self-cleaning bowls on the premium models are a close second, saving me hours of scrubbing over the course of a year.

**The Bottom Line:** A smart toilet is one of the few home upgrades that delivers both luxury and practicality. It's not a gimmick—it's a daily quality-of-life improvement. If your budget allows, skip the smart speaker or the new TV this year and invest in a smart toilet. Your backside will thank you.
