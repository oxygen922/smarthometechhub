# The Best Smart Toilets of 2026: A Complete Buyer's Review

## Introduction

The bathroom has quietly become the most interesting room in the smart home. While we've spent the last decade obsessing over connected lights and voice assistants, the smart toilet has evolved from a luxury curiosity into a genuinely useful appliance—one that heats your seat on a cold January morning, washes with precision, dries you off, and even analyzes your health. In 2026, the market has matured considerably. Prices have dropped, installation has simplified, and the feature sets have become more practical rather than gimmicky.

But with that maturity comes confusion. There are now dozens of smart toilets and smart toilet seats flooding the US market, ranging from $300 bidet attachments to $10,000 integrated luxury fixtures. How do you know which one is worth your money? In this review, we'll break down the three best smart toilets for most American households, walk through the features that actually matter, and help you decide whether it's finally time to upgrade your throne. Let's dive in.

---

## Top 3 Smart Toilet Recommendations

### 1. TOTO Washlet S7A — Best Overall Smart Toilet Seat

If you want the smart toilet experience without ripping out your existing fixture, the TOTO Washlet S7A is the gold standard. TOTO practically invented the modern bidet seat, and the S7A represents the company's most refined residential offering to date.

**Design and Build Quality**

The S7A features a slim, low-profile silhouette that looks far less bulky than earlier Washlet models. The seat is constructed from a durable urea resin with a soft-close lid, and the remote control is wall-mountable—a thoughtful touch that keeps your bathroom counter clear. Available in both elongated and round configurations, it fits most standard US toilets.

**Performance**

The wash experience is where TOTO earns its reputation. The S7A offers multiple spray modes, including front, rear, and a gentle "soft" setting, all with adjustable temperature and pressure. The warm air dryer is genuinely effective—not the weak puff you get on cheaper units. The heated seat has five temperature levels and warms up quickly.

**Smart Features**

The S7A includes auto open/close for the lid, a built-in deodorizer that activates automatically, and TOTO's eWater+ system, which mists the bowl with electrolyzed water to keep it cleaner between flushes. It connects to the TOTO app via Bluetooth for personalized settings, though it lacks Wi-Fi and voice assistant integration.

**Verdict**

At roughly **$1,100–$1,300 in 2026**, the S7A is a premium purchase, but it's the most reliable, best-supported bidet seat on the market. If you're dipping a toe into smart toilet territory, start here.

---

### 2. Kohler Innate Intelligent Toilet — Best Integrated Smart Toilet

For a fully integrated, one-piece smart toilet that looks as good as it functions, the Kohler Innate is our top pick. This is the toilet you install when you're remodeling and want a showpiece.

**Design and Build Quality**

The Innate features a sleek, skirted one-piece design that hides the trapway and makes cleaning effortless. The tankless, wall-hung or floor-mounted options give it a contemporary, hotel-like aesthetic. Kohler's vitreous china construction is top-tier, and the fit and finish are impeccable.

**Performance**

The Innate offers a full bidet experience: adjustable water temperature and pressure, oscillating and pulsating spray modes, a heated seat, and a warm air dryer. The flush is powerful and quiet, and the unit includes a backup manual flush for peace of mind during power outages.

**Smart Features**

This is where the Innate shines. It's fully integrated with Kohler's Konnect smart home platform, meaning it works with Amazon Alexa, Google Assistant, and Apple Home. You can set personalized user profiles (up to four), control the nightlight, and adjust settings via the Kohler Konnect app. Motion-activated lid opening and auto-flush round out the experience.

**Verdict**

Priced around **$3,500–$4,200 in 2026** depending on configuration, the Kohler Innate is a significant investment. But for a full bathroom remodel, it delivers the complete smart toilet package with the best smart home integration available.

### 3. Bio Bidet BB-2000 — Best Budget Smart Toilet Seat

Not everyone wants to spend four figures on a bathroom upgrade. The Bio Bidet BB-2000 proves you can get a genuinely excellent smart toilet seat for well under $500.

**Design and Build Quality**

The BB-2000 is a bit bulkier than the TOTO, but it's solidly built and available in elongated and round sizes. The wireless remote is intuitive, and installation is straightforward—most DIYers can handle it in under an hour with basic tools.

**Performance**

Don't let the price fool you. The BB-2000 offers a heated seat, adjustable warm water cleansing with front and rear washes, a turbo wash for stronger pressure, and a warm air dryer. The "smart" features include a nightlight, a deodorizer, and a sensor that prevents accidental activation.

**Smart Features**

The BB-2000 doesn't have app connectivity or voice control, but it does include a "smart" economy mode that reduces energy use when the bathroom is unoccupied. For most users, the wireless remote covers all the functionality they need.

**Verdict**

At approximately **$350–$450 in 2026**, the BB-2000 is the best value in the category. It's an ideal entry point for anyone curious about bidet seats without committing to a full smart toilet.

### The Features That Actually Matter

**Heated seat and warm water.** These are the two features that convert skeptics. Once you experience a warm seat in winter, there's no going back.

**Adjustable pressure and temperature.** Cheap units offer fixed settings; good ones let you dial in your preferences. Look for at least three pressure levels.

**Deodorizer.** A built-in carbon filter makes a real difference in small bathrooms. Don't overlook it.

**Self-cleaning and sanitation.** Features like TOTO's eWater+ or UV sterilization reduce how often you need to scrub the bowl.

**Smart home integration.** If you're building a connected home, Wi-Fi and voice control (like the Kohler Innate) are worth the premium. If not, a Bluetooth or remote-only unit is perfectly fine.

**Backup flush.** On integrated toilets, a manual flush override is essential—you don't want a dead battery to leave you stranded.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

**Retrofit buyers.** If your current toilet is in good shape and you just want the bidet experience, a smart toilet seat (like the TOTO S7A or Bio Bidet BB-2000) is the smart move. Installation is simple, and you avoid the cost and complexity of replacing the fixture.

**Remodelers.** If you're gutting a bathroom anyway, an integrated smart toilet like the Kohler Innate makes sense. You'll get a cleaner look, better integration, and features that retrofit seats can't match.

**Accessibility needs.** Smart toilets are genuinely life-changing for people with limited mobility, arthritis, or post-surgery recovery. The hands-free washing and drying reduce reliance on toilet paper and improve hygiene.

**Health-conscious users.** Some 2026 models now include urine analysis and health tracking. If that appeals to you, look at premium integrated models—but be aware the technology is still maturing.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $500:** A quality bidet seat (Bio Bidet BB-2000) gets you 80% of the experience.
- **$1,000–$1,500:** Premium bidet seats (TOTO S7A) with the best build quality and reliability.
- **$2,500–$4,500:** Integrated smart toilets (Kohler Innate) with full smart home integration.
- **$5,000+:** Luxury integrated models with health tracking, auto-clean, and designer finishes.

Don't forget ongoing costs: integrated toilets may require a dedicated electrical outlet, and some premium models use proprietary filters or cleaning cartridges.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Realities

**Bidet seats** are the easiest path. Most install on a standard two-bolt toilet in 30–60 minutes. You'll need a nearby GFCI-protected outlet—if your bathroom lacks one, budget $200–$400 for an electrician.

**Integrated smart toilets** are a bigger project. They require a dedicated circuit, and some models (especially wall-hung units) need professional installation. Expect $500–$1,500 in installation costs on top of the purchase price.

### Smart Home Integration

The Kohler Innate leads here, with native support for Amazon Alexa, Google Assistant, and Apple Home. You can create routines—for example, turning on the bathroom nightlight and heated seat when motion is detected at night. The TOTO S7A offers app-based personalization via Bluetooth but no voice control. The Bio Bidet BB-2000 is entirely offline, which some privacy-minded buyers actually prefer.

**A note on privacy:** Connected toilets collect usage data. Read the privacy policy, and if you're uncomfortable with data collection, choose a non-connected model.
