# The Ultimate Smart Toilet Review: Elevate Your Bathroom Experience in 2026

## Introduction

The smart toilet has evolved from a futuristic novelty into a must-have fixture for the modern American home. These intelligent commodes offer more than just hands-free flushing; they bring personalized comfort, improved hygiene, and seamless smart home integration directly into your bathroom. As we move through 2026, the smart toilet market has matured significantly, with models offering self-cleaning bidets, heated seats, automatic lids, and even health monitoring features. Whether you're looking to upgrade your master bath, add convenience to a guest bathroom, or simply reduce your toilet paper usage, a smart toilet is an investment in daily wellness and home efficiency. In this comprehensive review, we'll dive into the top three smart toilet models for 2026, break down the key features you should consider, and help you decide which one is right for your home and budget.

## Top 3 Product Recommendations with Detailed Reviews

### 1. Toto Neorest NX2: The Gold Standard of Luxury

The Toto Neorest NX2 continues to dominate the high-end smart toilet market in 2026. This is not just a toilet; it's a fully integrated wellness system. The NX2 features Toto's renowned Tornado Flush system, which uses a powerful, centrifugal rinse to clean the bowl with minimal water usage—just 1.0 gallon per flush (GPF). The bidet functionality is second to none, offering adjustable water temperature, pressure, and position, along with a warm air dryer and a deodorizer that activates automatically.

What sets the NX2 apart is its intuitive remote control and smart sensor technology. The lid opens and closes automatically as you approach and leave, and the seat is preheated to your preferred temperature. The built-in EWATER+ system electrolyzes tap water to create a gentle cleaning solution that keeps the bowl and wand sanitized between uses. For health-conscious users, the NX2 includes a health monitoring feature that tracks weight, body fat, and urine analysis via an optional add-on module.

**Pros:** Exceptional build quality, powerful yet quiet flush, comprehensive bidet features, long-lasting durability.
**Cons:** Very high price point (around $6,500), requires professional installation, limited color options (only white and bone).

### 2. Kohler PureWash E930: The Best Value Premium Choice

For homeowners who want luxury without breaking the bank, the Kohler PureWash E930 is a fantastic alternative. This smart toilet combines a sleek, modern design with a robust set of features at a more accessible price point. The E930 uses Kohler's AquaPiston flush technology, which delivers a 1.28 GPF flush that is both powerful and water-efficient. The bidet wand offers front and rear cleansing with oscillating and pulsating modes, plus an adjustable water temperature and pressure.

One of the standout features of the E930 is its voice control compatibility. It works seamlessly with Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant, allowing you to activate the bidet, flush, or open the lid using simple voice commands. The heated seat has multiple temperature settings, and the integrated nightlight makes late-night trips to the bathroom safer and more pleasant. The E930 also includes a self-cleaning wand and a carbon filter deodorizer to keep the air fresh.

**Pros:** Excellent smart home integration, good value for the features offered, reliable flush performance, comfortable seat.
**Cons:** The remote control is not as intuitive as Toto's, the bidet dryer is less powerful than premium models, and the seat may feel slightly narrow for larger users.

### 3. Bio Bidet Bliss BB-2000: The Tech-Savvy Powerhouse

If you're a tech enthusiast looking for the most feature-packed smart toilet on the market, the Bio Bidet Bliss BB-2000 is your best bet. This model is loaded with advanced technology, including a heated seat with six temperature levels, a powerful instant water heater for endless warm water, and a multi-function bidet with adjustable spray patterns. The BB-2000 also features a user-friendly touchscreen remote with memory presets for up to four users.

What truly sets the BB-2000 apart is its health monitoring capabilities. It includes a built-in scale that measures weight and body composition, as well as a urine analysis system that can track key health markers like glucose and ketones. The data syncs with a companion app on your smartphone, giving you a daily snapshot of your wellness. The flush system uses a powerful siphonic jet that handles solid waste with ease, and the self-cleaning wand is UV-sanitized after each use.

**Pros:** Unmatched health tracking features, excellent app integration, powerful bidet with many modes, good for multi-user households.
**Cons:** The app can be buggy at times, the toilet is quite large and may not fit smaller bathrooms, and the price is on the higher end ($3,800).

## Key Features to Consider (with Comparison Table)

When shopping for a smart toilet, understanding the key features is crucial. Here’s a breakdown of what to look for:

**1. Flush Technology:** Look for models with powerful yet water-efficient flush systems. Toto's Tornado Flush and Kohler's AquaPiston are industry leaders. A 1.0 to 1.28 GPF rating is ideal for saving water without sacrificing performance.

**2. Bidet Functionality:** The bidet is the heart of a smart toilet. Key considerations include adjustable water temperature (preferably with an instant heater), adjustable pressure, and multiple spray modes (front, rear, oscillating, pulsating). A warm air dryer is a nice bonus but varies in effectiveness.

**3. Seat Comfort and Heating:** A heated seat is essential for cold mornings. Look for models with multiple temperature settings and a comfortable, ergonomic shape. Some seats are more padded or wider than others.

**4. Smart Home Integration:** Voice control via Alexa or Google Assistant is becoming standard. Some models also offer app control for remote operation and health data tracking. Compatibility with your existing smart home ecosystem is important.

**5. Self-Cleaning and Hygiene:** A self-cleaning wand with UV sanitization or electrolyzed water (like Toto's EWATER+) keeps the toilet hygienic between manual cleanings. Built-in deodorizers are also valuable.

**6. Health Monitoring:** Advanced models include weight scales, body composition analysis, and urine testing. This is a premium feature but can be valuable for health-conscious users.

## Buying Guide: Who Should Buy and Budget Considerations

**Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?**

- **Luxury Homeowners:** If you're building or remodeling a high-end master bathroom, the Toto Neorest NX2 is the ultimate statement piece. It offers unparalleled comfort and craftsmanship.
- **Tech-Savvy Families:** The Bio Bidet Bliss BB-2000 is perfect for families who love gadgets and want to track their health. Its multi-user presets and app integration make it ideal for households with multiple people.
- **Practical Upgraders:** The Kohler PureWash E930 is for those who want a significant upgrade from a standard toilet without spending a fortune. It offers excellent core features and voice control at a reasonable price.
- **Eco-Conscious Consumers:** All three models are WaterSense certified, but the Toto's 1.0 GPF flush is the most water-efficient.
- **Individuals with Mobility Issues:** The automatic lid, heated seat, and bidet functionality of any smart toilet can greatly improve independence and comfort for seniors or people with disabilities.

**Budget Considerations:**

- **Entry-Level ($1,000 - $2,500):** You can find smart toilet seats (bidet attachments) that convert your existing toilet, but full smart toilets in this range are rare. The Kohler PureWash E930 sits at the top of this bracket and offers excellent value.
- **Mid-Range ($2,500 - $4,500):** The Bio Bidet Bliss BB-2000 falls here, offering advanced features and health monitoring. This is the sweet spot for most buyers.
- **Premium ($4,500+):** The Toto Neorest NX2 is the clear leader here. You're paying for top-tier build quality, the best flush system, and a brand reputation that has defined the category.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

**Installation Considerations:**

Installing a smart toilet is more complex than a standard toilet. Most models require a dedicated electrical outlet near the toilet (typically on the wall behind or beside it). If you don't have one, you'll need an electrician to install a GFCI outlet. Additionally, some models require a specific water supply line and may need a plumber to ensure proper connections.

- **DIY vs. Professional:** The Kohler PureWash E930 and Bio Bidet Bliss BB-2000 come with clear instructions and can be installed by a confident DIYer with basic plumbing and electrical knowledge. However, the Toto Neorest NX2, due to its complexity and high cost, is best left to a professional installer.
- **Space Requirements:** Smart toilets are generally larger than standard toilets. Measure your bathroom carefully, especially the rough-in distance (distance from the wall to the center of the drain pipe). Standard rough-in is 12 inches, but some models require 10 or 14 inches.

**Smart Home Integration:**

- **Voice Control:** The Kohler PureWash E930 is the standout here, with native support for Alexa and Google Assistant. You can say, "Alexa, flush the toilet," or "Hey Google, turn on the bidet." The Toto and Bio Bidet models lack native voice control but can be integrated via third-party smart home hubs (like SmartThings) in some cases.
- **App Integration:** The Bio Bidet Bliss BB-2000 has the most robust app, allowing you to view health data, adjust settings, and receive maintenance reminders. The Toto and Kohler models do not have dedicated apps.
- **Automation:** You can create routines like "Goodnight" to automatically close the lid and turn off the seat heater. The Bio Bidet's health data can even be shared with Apple Health or Google Fit for a more holistic wellness view.
