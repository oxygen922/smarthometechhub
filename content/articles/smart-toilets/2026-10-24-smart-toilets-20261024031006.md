# The Best Smart Toilets of 2026: A Complete Buyer's Review

## Introduction

Smart toilets have quietly become one of the most transformative upgrades you can make to a modern bathroom. In 2026, these fixtures have moved well beyond novelty status—they now combine bidet functionality, heated seating, self-cleaning systems, and full smart home connectivity into a single sleek package. Whether you're remodeling a primary bathroom, looking to improve hygiene and accessibility, or simply want to add genuine luxury to your daily routine, the right smart toilet can deliver a surprisingly meaningful return on comfort and convenience.

The market has matured significantly this year, with established brands refining their flagship models and new contenders pushing prices lower without sacrificing core features. That's great news for buyers, but it also makes choosing the right unit more complicated. In this review, we break down the top three smart toilets worth your attention in 2026, walk through the features that actually matter, and help you decide which model fits your home, your plumbing, and your budget.

## Top 3 Smart Toilet Recommendations

### 1. TOTO Neorest WX1 — Best Overall Smart Toilet

**Estimated Price: $9,800–$11,500 (2026)**

TOTO remains the benchmark in the smart toilet category, and the Neorest WX1 is the company's most refined residential model to date. It combines TOTO's proven Washlet cleansing technology with a completely redesigned, low-profile silhouette that looks more like contemporary sculpture than plumbing.

The WX1's standout feature is its **Activated Ionized Electrolysis Water system**, which continuously treats the bowl and wand with electrolyzed water to reduce bacteria between uses. Combined with the new **Premist+** bowl pre-misting, the unit stays noticeably cleaner for longer—one of the biggest real-world pain points with any smart toilet. The heated seat offers five temperature settings, and the bidet includes adjustable pressure, position, and oscillating or pulsating modes.

Performance is where the WX1 justifies its premium. The **Tornado Flush** system delivers a powerful, quiet 1.0/0.8 GPF dual flush, and the eWater+ cleaning cycle runs automatically after each use. The auto open/close lid and hands-free flush are responsive and reliable, and the integrated night light is subtle enough for middle-of-the-night trips.

Smart home integration is solid: the WX1 supports the TOTO app over Wi-Fi, works with Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant for basic commands, and stores personalized user profiles for up to four people. The remote is intuitive, though the app occasionally lags when adjusting settings remotely.

**Pros:** Best-in-class cleaning technology, refined design, reliable flushing, strong app support
**Cons:** Very expensive, requires a dedicated GFCI outlet near the toilet, professional installation strongly recommended

**Verdict:** If budget is not a constraint, the Neorest WX1 is the smart toilet to beat in 2026.

### 2. Kohler Innate Intelligent Toilet — Best Mid-Range Pick

**Estimated Price: $3,200–$4,100 (2026)**

Kohler's Innate line has long been the sweet spot for buyers who want genuine smart functionality without a five-figure investment, and the 2026 refresh makes it even more compelling. This model delivers the essentials—heated seat, warm-water bidet with adjustable settings, auto flush, and a motion-activated lid—at roughly a third of the Neorest's price.

The Innate uses Kohler's **Revolution 360** flush technology with a 1.28 GPF single flush, which is efficient and dependable. The bidet wand features a self-cleaning cycle and stainless steel construction, and the seat heating is even and quick to warm. The **quiet-close lid** and hands-free operation feel polished for the price tier.

Where the Innate compromises is in advanced water treatment. It doesn't offer the electrolyzed-water sanitizing found on premium TOTO models, relying instead on a UV sanitizing option (available on select SKUs) and standard self-cleaning. That's a reasonable trade-off for most households.

Smart home support is where the Innate shines for its class: it works with **Kohler Konnect**, integrating with Amazon Alexa, Google Assistant, and Apple HomeKit, so you can trigger the bidet, adjust seat temperature, or flush via voice. The Kohler app is clean and stable.

**Pros:** Excellent value, full voice assistant support, dependable flush, attractive design
**Cons:** No advanced water sanitizing on base models, fewer user profiles than premium units

**Verdict:** The best balance of features and price for most US households in 2026.

### 3. Bio Bidet Prodigy Smart Toilet — Best Budget-Friendly Smart Toilet

**Estimated Price: $1,600–$2,200 (2026)**

For buyers who want smart toilet functionality without a major renovation budget, the Bio Bidet Prodigy is the standout value pick of 2026. It's a fully integrated one-piece toilet with a built-in bidet, heated seat, and auto flush—features that would have commanded twice the price just a few years ago.

The Prodigy offers a warm-water bidet with adjustable pressure and nozzle position, a heated seat with three settings, and a **hybrid flush system** rated at 1.0/1.28 GPF. The wireless remote is straightforward, and the unit includes a night light and automatic deodorizer—small touches that make a real difference day to day.

Build quality is respectable for the price, though the plastic seat and lid feel less premium than ceramic or higher-grade composites on pricier models. Water heating is tankless, so you get consistent warm water without a reservoir, but the maximum temperature runs slightly cooler than the Kohler or TOTO.

Smart home connectivity is limited: there's no native Alexa or Google integration, though the Prodigy can be paired with a smart plug for basic voice-triggered power control. For most budget buyers, the remote-based operation is entirely sufficient.

**Pros:** Affordable entry point, tankless water heating, solid core features, easy retrofit
**Cons:** No native voice assistant support, less premium materials, limited app ecosystem

**Verdict:** The smart toilet to buy if you want the experience without the luxury price tag.

## Buying Guide: Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

**Who should buy:**
- **Households prioritizing hygiene and accessibility** — smart toilets are excellent for elderly family members, people with limited mobility, or anyone managing medical conditions where bidet cleansing is beneficial.
- **Bathroom remodelers** — if you're already renovating, the incremental cost of running a GFCI outlet and upgrading to a smart toilet is far easier to absorb.
- **Comfort-focused upgraders** — heated seats and warm-water cleansing genuinely change the daily experience, especially in colder climates.
- **Tech-forward homeowners** — if your home already runs on Alexa, Google, or HomeKit, a connected toilet fits naturally into your ecosystem.

**Who should wait:**
- Renters or anyone planning to move within a year—installation and electrical work aren't easily reversible.
- Households without a nearby power source who aren't ready for an electrician visit.
- Buyers on a tight budget who'd be better served by a high-quality bidet seat attachment on an existing toilet.

**Budget considerations:**
Plan for the **total installed cost**, not just the sticker price. Add $150–$400 for an electrician to install a GFCI outlet if one isn't already near the toilet, and consider professional installation ($200–$500) for integrated units, which are heavy and require a precise flange seal. In 2026, expect to spend:

- **Budget:** $1,600–$2,200
- **Mid-range:** $3,200–$4,100
- **Premium:** $9,800–$11,500

Also factor in long-term costs: replacement filters and descaling kits run $30–$80 per year on premium models.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

Installing a smart toilet is more involved than swapping a standard fixture. Integrated units weigh 80–120 pounds and require a **standard 120V GFCI outlet within roughly three feet** of the toilet. If your bathroom lacks one, budget for an electrician—this is non-negotiable for safety and code compliance.

Most manufacturers recommend professional installation, and we agree, especially for premium models. The rough-in distance (typically 12 inches) must match your floor flange, and the water supply line needs to reach the unit's inlet. Measure carefully before ordering; returns on these fixtures are costly.

**Smart home integration** varies widely. Premium TOTO and Kohler models offer native Wi-Fi with app control and voice assistant support through Alexa, Google Assistant, and—on Kohler—Apple HomeKit. Budget models like the Bio Bidet Prodigy rely on remotes, though a smart plug can add basic voice on/off control.

A few practical tips: keep the remote mounted away from the shower spray, update firmware after installation, and set up user profiles early so each household member gets personalized seat temperature and bidet settings from day one. If privacy matters, check whether the app requires cloud accounts or supports local control.
