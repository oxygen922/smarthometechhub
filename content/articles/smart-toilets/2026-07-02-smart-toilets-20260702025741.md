## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Overview

Most smart bidet seats are designed for DIY installation, but you’ll need basic plumbing skills and a few tools. Here’s what to expect:

1. **Turn off water supply** and flush the toilet to empty the tank.
2. **Remove the existing toilet seat** by unscrewing the hinges.
3. **Install the bidet seat** using the included mounting bracket. Ensure it’s level.
4. **Connect the T-connector** to the toilet’s water supply line. Attach the bidet hose to the T-connector.
5. **Plug in the power cord** to a nearby GFCI outlet. If no outlet exists, hire an electrician.
6. **Test all functions** before finalizing the seat position.

**Time:** 30–60 minutes for most models.

### Smart Home Integration

Smart bidet seats like the Kohler Novita BH-90 offer voice control and app-based scheduling. For example:
- **Alexa:** “Alexa, tell Kohler to start the warm water wash.”
- **Google Assistant:** “Hey Google, set the seat temperature to medium.”
- **Apple HomeKit:** Automate seat heating to turn on 30 minutes before your morning routine.

**Compatibility Note:** Ensure your Wi-Fi router supports 2.4 GHz networks, as many smart devices don’t work with 5 GHz-only setups.
