## Buying Guide: Who Should Buy and Budget Considerations

### Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

- **Tech Enthusiasts:** If you already automate your lights, thermostat, and security, a smart toilet is the logical next step. Look for models with strong API support and voice control compatibility.
- **Health-Conscious Individuals:** The Toto Neorest NX2’s urinalysis feature is a game-changer for tracking hydration, kidney health, and potential issues early. However, consult your doctor before relying on it.
- **Eco-Conscious Homeowners:** Smart toilets use significantly less water per flush (1.0–1.28 GPF) compared to older models (1.6–3.5 GPF). Over a year, you can save thousands of gallons.
- **Families with Elderly or Disabled Members:** Heated seats, bidet functions, and touchless flush reduce physical strain and improve hygiene. The Kohler Veil’s voice control is especially helpful.
- **Renters:** A bidet seat attachment like the Bio Bidet BB-2000 is ideal. It’s easy to install and remove when you move out.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $2,000:** Focus on bidet seat attachments. You’ll get heated seats, warm water bidet, and air drying. Brands like Bio Bidet, Brondell, and Tushy offer excellent options.
- **$2,000 – $5,000:** Mid-range integrated toilets like the Kohler Veil or American Standard Clean+. These offer better design, stronger flush, and limited smart home features.
- **$5,000+:** Premium integrated toilets like Toto Neorest or Duravit SensoWash. Expect health monitoring, advanced self-cleaning, and full smart home integration.

**Pro Tip:** Factor in installation costs ($200–$500 for professional setup) and electrical work (some models require a GFCI outlet near the toilet). If you’re remodeling, this is the perfect time to add a dedicated circuit.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Considerations

- **Power Requirements:** All smart toilets need a nearby electrical outlet (120V, GFCI-protected). If you don’t have one, hire an electrician to run a line—costs range from $150 to $400.
- **Water Supply:** Most models use standard 3/8-inch compression fittings. Ensure your water shut-off valve is accessible and in good condition.
- **Rough-In Distance:** Measure from the wall to the center of the toilet flange. Standard is 12 inches, but 10- and 14-inch options exist. Integrated toilets are typically fixed, so measure carefully.
- **Flooring:** Smart toilets are heavy (80–120 lbs). Ensure your bathroom floor can support the weight, especially if you have tile over a wooden subfloor.

### Smart Home Integration Tips

- **Voice Control:** For Alexa or Google Home, enable the skill in the respective app. The Kohler Veil works best with voice commands like “Alexa, flush the toilet” or “Hey Google, warm the seat.”
- **Automations:** Use IFTTT or Apple Shortcuts to create routines. Example: “When I leave the house (geofencing), set the toilet to Eco Mode and turn off the seat warmer.”
- **Energy Management:** Many smart toilets have a “smart energy” feature that learns your usage patterns. The Toto Neorest NX2, for instance, will preheat the seat only during your typical morning and evening hours.
- **Security:** If your smart toilet has health monitoring, ensure the app uses end-to-end encryption. Toto and Kohler both encrypt data in transit and at rest.
