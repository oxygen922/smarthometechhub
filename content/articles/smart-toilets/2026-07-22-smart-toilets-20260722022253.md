# The Ultimate Guide to Smart Toilets: Top Picks for 2026

## Introduction

The smart toilet has evolved from a futuristic novelty into a cornerstone of the modern smart home. In 2026, these high-tech thrones offer more than just heated seats and self-cleaning functions; they integrate seamlessly with your home ecosystem, track health metrics, and even adjust to your personal preferences automatically. As a professional smart home technology reviewer, I’ve tested dozens of models over the past year, from luxury flagship units to budget-friendly retrofits. The market has matured significantly, with brands like Toto, Kohler, and BioBidet leading the charge with innovative features such as voice control, UV sterilization, and biometric health monitoring. Whether you’re a tech enthusiast looking to upgrade your bathroom or a homeowner seeking enhanced hygiene and convenience, this in-depth review will help you navigate the best smart toilets of 2026. ## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

- **Tech Enthusiasts:** If you already have a smart home hub and enjoy automating everything, a smart toilet is the next logical step. The BioBidet Flow 9000’s Matter compatibility makes it ideal for this group.
- **Health-Conscious Individuals:** The Toto Neorest NX2’s health monitoring can provide valuable data for those tracking wellness metrics.
- **Elderly or Mobility-Challenged Users:** Features like heated seats, automatic open/close, and bidet functions significantly improve bathroom accessibility.
- **Eco-Conscious Homeowners:** Smart toilets use less water than traditional models, and many offer water usage tracking.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $1,500:** Stick with a high-quality bidet seat like the Kohler PureWash E930. You get most smart features without replacing your toilet.
- **$2,000 – $3,500:** This sweet spot offers full smart toilets with excellent features. The BioBidet Flow 9000 is the best value here.
- **Over $5,000:** Luxury models like the Toto Neorest NX2 offer premium materials, health monitoring, and the best warranty. Only consider this if you have a high budget and want the absolute best.

### Installation Considerations

- **Power Source:** Smart toilets require a nearby electrical outlet (GFCI protected). If you don’t have one, factor in the cost of hiring an electrician ($150–$300).
- **Plumbing:** Most smart toilets use standard 12-inch rough-in measurements. Measure your existing toilet’s rough-in before purchasing.
- **DIY vs. Professional:** Bidet seats are generally DIY-friendly. Full smart toilets often require a plumber for proper installation and calibration.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Tips

1. **Check Your Rough-In:** Measure from the wall to the center of the toilet flange. Standard is 12 inches, but 10-inch and 14-inch options exist.
2. **Electrical Requirements:** Ensure you have a GFCI outlet within 3 feet of the toilet. If not, hire a licensed electrician.
3. **Water Supply:** Most smart toilets use a standard 3/8-inch compression fitting. Check the manufacturer’s specifications.
4. **Floor Reinforcement:** Some luxury models are heavy (over 100 lbs). Ensure your bathroom floor can support the weight.

### Smart Home Integration

- **Voice Control:** All three recommended models work with Alexa and Google Assistant. Use commands like “Alexa, flush the toilet” or “Hey Google, warm the seat.”
- **Routines:** Create morning routines that trigger the toilet to preheat the seat and activate the bidet at a specific time.
- **Health Data Integration:** The Toto Neorest NX2 can sync health data with Apple Health and Google Fit, allowing you to track trends over time.
- **Matter Protocol:** The BioBidet Flow 9000 supports Matter, making it compatible with future smart home ecosystems. This is a future-proof choice.

### Common Integration Issues

- **Wi-Fi Connectivity:** Some models struggle with weak Wi-Fi signals in bathrooms. Use a mesh network or Wi-Fi extender.
- **App Updates:** Keep the toilet’s firmware updated for optimal performance and security.
- **Privacy Concerns:** Health monitoring data is stored locally or encrypted. Review the privacy policy before purchasing.
