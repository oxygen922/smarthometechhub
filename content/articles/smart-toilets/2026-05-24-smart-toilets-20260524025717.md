# The Ultimate Guide to Smart Toilets: Top Picks, Features, and Buying Advice for 2026

## Introduction

The smart toilet has evolved from a luxury novelty into a mainstream home essential, blending cutting-edge technology with everyday hygiene. As we step into 2026, these intelligent fixtures offer features that rival the most advanced smart home devices: heated seats, bidet functions, self-cleaning capabilities, and even health monitoring. For US homeowners, a smart toilet isn't just about comfort—it's about water efficiency, improved sanitation, and seamless integration with your existing smart ecosystem. Whether you're remodeling a master bath or upgrading a guest powder room, choosing the right model can transform your daily routine. In this comprehensive review, we’ll explore the top three smart toilets of 2026, break down must-have features, and provide a practical buying guide to help you make an informed decision. Let’s dive into the future of bathroom technology.

## Top 3 Product Recommendations

### 1. Toto Neorest NX2 (2026 Edition)

**Overview:** Toto remains the gold standard in smart toilets, and the Neorest NX2 is their flagship model for 2026. It combines Japanese engineering precision with US-standard electrical and plumbing compatibility, making it a top choice for discerning homeowners.

**Key Features:**
- **EWater+ System:** Uses electrolyzed water to sanitize the bowl, rim, and nozzles automatically between uses.
- **Actilight UV:** A UV light that sterilizes the bowl surface after each flush, reducing bacteria buildup.
- **Premist Technology:** Sprays the bowl with water before use to prevent waste from sticking, improving flush efficiency.
- **Heated Seat with Temperature Memory:** Customizable warmth settings that remember your preference.
- **Washlet with Multiple Modes:** Rear cleanse, front cleanse, oscillating spray, and warm air dryer with adjustable temperature.
- **SanaGloss Glaze:** A special glaze that prevents debris from adhering to the ceramic.
- **Health Monitoring:** Built-in sensors track urine flow and frequency, alerting you to potential health issues via the Toto app.
- **Smart Home Integration:** Compatible with Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant for voice control of seat warming, flushing, and lighting.

**Performance:** The NX2 delivers a powerful, quiet flush using Toto’s Tornado Flush technology, which uses dual nozzles to create a centrifugal cleaning action. The bidet function offers precise water pressure and temperature control, and the dryer is surprisingly effective—though it still takes a bit longer than towel drying. The health monitoring feature is a standout for 2026, providing valuable data without being intrusive.

**Price:** $6,800–$7,200 (estimated 2026 MSRP, varies by retailer and installation package)

**Pros:**
- Exceptional build quality and durability
- Comprehensive health monitoring
- Seamless smart home integration
- Ultra-quiet operation

**Cons:**
- Very high price point
- Requires professional installation (not DIY-friendly)
- The app can be occasionally slow to sync data

### 2. Kohler K-5401 Veil Intelligent Toilet (2026 Model)

**Overview:** Kohler’s Veil series has long been a favorite for its sleek, modern design and reliable performance. The 2026 update focuses on improved water efficiency and deeper smart home integration, making it a strong competitor to Toto.

**Key Features:**
- **Advanced Bidet with Heated Seat:** Offers front and rear wash, oscillating pulse massage, and a warm air dryer. The seat heats to five levels.
- **Touchless Flush:** Motion sensor initiates flush when you step away—no touching needed.
- **Self-Cleaning Wand:** The bidet wand retracts and cleans itself with UV light before and after each use.
- **AquaPiston Flush Technology:** A canister-based flush that delivers a powerful, clog-resistant flush using 1.28 gallons per flush (GPF).
- **NightLight:** Soft ambient LED lighting that illuminates the bowl for nighttime use.
- **Kohler Konnect App:** Controls all settings, monitors water usage, and provides maintenance alerts via your smartphone.
- **Voice Control:** Works with Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant for flushing, seat warming, and bidet activation.
- **Health Monitoring:** Basic urine analysis (pH and specific gravity) available as an optional module.

**Performance:** The Veil excels in user experience—the seat is exceptionally comfortable, and the bidet spray is gentle yet effective. The touchless flush is responsive and hygienic, and the self-cleaning wand reduces maintenance. Flush power is excellent, handling solid waste without issues. The NightLight is a subtle but appreciated touch. However, the health monitoring module is an extra cost ($400) and less comprehensive than Toto’s built-in system.

**Price:** $4,500–$5,000 (estimated 2026 MSRP, plus $400 for health module)

**Pros:**
- Sleek, minimalist design
- Excellent bidet and dryer performance
- Strong smart home integration
- Lower price than Toto flagship

**Cons:**
- Health monitoring is optional and basic
- No UV bowl sterilization (only wand)
- Slightly louder flush than Toto

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

- **Tech Enthusiasts:** If you already have a smart home ecosystem (Alexa, Google Home), a smart toilet is the next logical upgrade. Voice-controlled flushing and seat warming are surprisingly convenient.
- **Elderly or Mobility-Impaired Users:** Heated seats, touchless flushing, and bidet functions reduce physical strain and improve hygiene.
- **Eco-Conscious Homeowners:** Smart toilets use significantly less water than traditional models (1.28 GPF vs. 1.6 GPF), and some offer dual-flush options for even greater savings.
- **Families with Young Children:** Self-cleaning features and touchless operation minimize germ spread. Some models have child-friendly bidet settings.
- **Luxury Remodelers:** If you’re investing in a high-end bathroom, a smart toilet from Toto or Kohler adds a wow factor and can increase home resale value.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $3,000:** BioBidet BB-2000 is your best bet. You’ll get excellent bidet and smart features without health monitoring. Also consider models from WoodBridge or HomeTECH.
- **$3,000–$5,000:** Kohler Veil K-5401 offers a great balance of design, performance, and smart integration. You can also look at American Standard’s VorMax.
- **$5,000+:** Toto Neorest NX2 is the ultimate choice for those who want the best in health monitoring, self-cleaning, and durability. Expect professional installation costs to add $500–$1,000.

**Hidden Costs:** Don’t forget electrical work (most smart toilets need a nearby GFCI outlet), water line installation, and potential plumbing modifications if your rough-in isn’t standard.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Overview

Smart toilets are not DIY-friendly for most homeowners. Here’s what to expect:

1. **Electrical Requirements:** A dedicated GFCI outlet within 3 feet of the toilet is standard. If you don’t have one, hire a licensed electrician ($200–$500).
2. **Plumbing:** The toilet connects to your existing water supply. Some models require a separate line for the bidet (usually included). Ensure your shut-off valve is accessible.
3. **Rough-In Measurement:** Measure from the wall to the center of the toilet flange. Standard is 12 inches, but 10- and 14-inch options exist. Most smart toilets are 12-inch only.
4. **Floor Reinforcement:** Smart toilets are heavier than standard models (80–120 lbs). If you have a tile floor, ensure the subfloor can support the weight.
5. **Professional Installation:** Expect to pay $300–$800 for a plumber to install the toilet and connect electrical. Some retailers offer installation packages.

### Smart Home Integration

All three models reviewed work with Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant, allowing voice commands like:
- “Alexa, flush the toilet.”
- “Hey Google, warm the seat.”
- “Alexa, activate bidet.”

**Integration Tips:**
- Use IFTTT (If This Then That) to create routines, e.g., “When I say ‘Goodnight,’ lower the seat temperature and turn off the nightlight.”
- Connect to your smart home hub (e.g., Samsung SmartThings) for unified control.
- Some toilets offer geofencing—automatically warming the seat when your phone enters the home.

**Limitations:** Voice control for flushing can be a privacy concern. Most manufacturers allow you to disable voice flush if desired. Also, smart toilet apps can sometimes lag or require firmware updates—ensure your Wi-Fi is stable near the bathroom.
