## Top 3 Bidet Seat Recommendations for 2026

### 1. TOTO Washlet S7A — Best Overall

If there's a gold standard in the bidet seat world, it's TOTO's Washlet line, and the S7A represents the company's most refined residential model yet. TOTO practically invented the modern bidet seat, and that decades of engineering shows in every detail.

**Performance & Comfort:** The S7A uses TOTO's proprietary eWater+ electrolyzed water system, which pre-mists the bowl before use and cleans the wand after every cycle—a genuine hygiene advantage you'll notice over cheaper competitors. The heated seat offers three temperature settings, and the warm air dryer is actually powerful enough to be useful rather than decorative.

**Cleaning Modes:** You get front and rear cleansing, a oscillating "wide" spray, a pulsating massage mode, and adjustable water pressure and temperature. The wand position is memory-adjustable, so two users can save their preferred settings.

**Design & Build:** The slim, low-profile silhouette fits seamlessly on most elongated toilets, and the nightlight is subtle rather than garish. The remote control is intuitive, with large buttons and a clear LCD display.

**The Verdict:** The S7A is expensive, but it's the closest thing to a hotel-spa experience you can install in a standard bathroom. If you want the best and plan to keep it for a decade, this is the one.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation: Easier Than You Think

Installing a bidet seat is a DIY-friendly project for most homeowners. Here's the basic process:

1. **Turn off the water supply** and flush the toilet to empty the tank.
2. **Remove your existing seat** by unscrewing the mounting bolts.
3. **Install the T-adapter** onto your toilet's fill valve to split the water line.
4. **Mount the bidet seat** using the included bracket and bolts.
5. **Connect the water hose** from the T-adapter to the seat.
6. **Plug in the power cord** to a GFCI outlet.
7. **Turn the water back on** and test for leaks.

**Time required:** 30–60 minutes for most models.

**Tools needed:** Adjustable wrench, screwdriver, and possibly a drill for the mounting bracket.

**The outlet problem:** This is the biggest installation hurdle. Bidet seats require a grounded GFCI outlet within about 3 feet of the toilet. If you don't have one, an electrician will charge $150–$400 to install one. Factor this into your budget.

### Smart Home Integration

Smart bidet seats typically connect via Wi-Fi or Bluetooth to a manufacturer app. Here's what you can actually do:

- **Kohler Konnect:** Works with Alexa and Google Assistant. You can create user profiles, adjust temperature and pressure, and schedule nightlight activation.
- **TOTO:** The S7A uses a physical remote rather than app control, though TOTO's higher-end Neorest toilets offer app integration.
- **Bio Bidet:** The BB-2000 is remote-only, but Bio Bidet's newer flagship models are adding app support.

**Is smart home integration worth it?** For most people, no—the physical remote is faster and more reliable. But if you love automating your home, the Kohler E930 is the most capable option.

**Voice control reality check:** Saying "Alexa, turn on the bidet" works, but it's slower than pressing a button. It's a fun demo, not a daily necessity.
