# The Ultimate Smart Toilet Review: Revolutionizing Your Bathroom Experience in 2026

## Introduction

For decades, the humble toilet remained largely unchanged—a porcelain throne that served a single purpose. But welcome to 2026, where the smart toilet has emerged as the crown jewel of the modern bathroom, blending cutting-edge technology with everyday hygiene. These aren't just toilets; they're intelligent sanitation systems that learn your preferences, monitor your health, and integrate seamlessly with your smart home ecosystem. From heated seats and bidet functions to automatic flushing and voice control, today's smart toilets offer a level of comfort and efficiency that was unimaginable just a few years ago. However, with prices ranging from $800 to over $8,000, choosing the right model can be overwhelming. In this in-depth review, we’ll explore the top three smart toilets on the market, break down the essential features to consider, and help you decide which one is worth the investment for your home. Whether you're a tech enthusiast, a hygiene fanatic, or someone looking to add luxury to their daily routine, this guide has you covered.

## Top 3 Product Recommendations with Detailed Reviews

### 1. Kohler Numi 2.0 Intelligent Toilet (2026 Edition)

**Price:** $7,995 (estimated MSRP)

**Overview:** The Kohler Numi 2.0 remains the gold standard for luxury smart toilets. This 2026 edition builds on its predecessor with upgraded sensors, improved water efficiency, and deeper smart home integration. It’s the closest thing to a personal bathroom assistant you can buy.

**Design & Build:** The Numi 2.0 features a sleek, modern silhouette with a seamless, skirted design that’s easy to clean. The elongated bowl is comfort-height, and the lid closes automatically with a whisper-quiet motor. The ceramic is coated with Kohler’s proprietary CleanCoat technology, which repels debris and bacteria.

**Performance:** The bidet function offers adjustable water temperature, pressure, and position, with a warm air dryer that actually works (unlike many competitors). The self-cleaning wand retracts and sanitizes itself after each use. The dual-flush system is incredibly efficient, using just 0.8 gallons for liquid waste and 1.6 gallons for solid. The heated seat is a game-changer during winter months, and the built-in nightlight is a thoughtful touch.

**Smart Features:** The Numi 2.0 is compatible with Amazon Alexa, Google Assistant, and Apple HomeKit. You can control everything via voice or the KOHLER Konnect app. The 2026 model adds a new “Health Monitor” feature that tracks urine frequency and color, providing insights via the app (data is encrypted and stored locally). The automatic flush uses radar sensors, so it never misses.

**Pros:**
- Exceptional build quality and design
- Comprehensive smart home integration
- Health monitoring feature is innovative
- Very quiet operation

**Cons:**
- Extremely expensive
- Requires professional installation and dedicated electrical outlet
- Health data may raise privacy concerns for some

**Verdict:** The Numi 2.0 is the ultimate smart toilet for those who want the best and have the budget. It’s a statement piece that delivers on every promise.

### 2. TOTO Washlet G5 (2026 Model)

**Price:** $1,299 (estimated MSRP)

**Overview:** TOTO has been a pioneer in bidet technology for decades, and the Washlet G5 represents the sweet spot between luxury and affordability. It’s a bidet seat that fits most standard toilets, making it an excellent upgrade without replacing your entire bowl.

**Design & Build:** The G5 has a streamlined, low-profile design that looks modern without being ostentatious. It’s available in elongated and round versions. The seat is ergonomically contoured and heated, with a soft-close lid. The remote control is wireless and wall-mountable, with intuitive buttons and a clear LCD display.

**Performance:** TOTO’s signature “eWater+” system uses electrolyzed water to clean the bowl and nozzle, reducing the need for harsh chemicals. The bidet spray is precise and adjustable, with a “Pulsating” mode for enhanced cleaning. The warm air dryer is effective, and the “Premist” function sprays the bowl before use to prevent waste from sticking. The automatic flush sensor is highly responsive.

**Smart Features:** The G5 integrates with Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant for voice control of seat heating, bidet functions, and deodorizer. The TOTO app allows for custom presets for multiple users. The 2026 model adds a “Sleep Mode” that reduces power consumption overnight.

**Pros:**
- Excellent value for the features offered
- Easy to install on existing toilets
- eWater+ system is eco-friendly
- Great water efficiency

**Cons:**
- Not a full toilet replacement (seat only)
- Lacks advanced health monitoring
- Remote can be lost or misplaced

**Verdict:** The Washlet G5 is the best smart bidet seat for most households. It offers 90% of the luxury features of the Numi 2.0 at a fraction of the cost.

## Buying Guide: Who Should Buy and Budget Considerations

**Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?**

- **Tech Enthusiasts:** If you have a smart home with multiple connected devices, a smart toilet is a natural addition. The Kohler Numi 2.0 or TOTO Washlet G5 will integrate seamlessly.
- **Hygiene-Conscious Individuals:** Bidets are proven to be more hygienic than toilet paper. Any of these models will significantly improve your bathroom hygiene.
- **Elderly or Mobility-Impaired Users:** Heated seats, automatic flushing, and bidet functions reduce physical strain. The BioBidet BB-2000 is a great entry point.
- **Eco-Conscious Homeowners:** Smart toilets use significantly less water than traditional models. The TOTO G5’s eWater+ system also reduces chemical usage.
- **Luxury Seekers:** The Kohler Numi 2.0 is for those who want the absolute best in design, technology, and comfort.

**Budget Considerations:**

- **Under $1,000:** The BioBidet BB-2000 Supreme is your best bet. It offers core smart features without breaking the bank.
- **$1,000 - $2,000:** The TOTO Washlet G5 is the sweet spot. You get premium build quality and excellent performance.
- **Over $5,000:** The Kohler Numi 2.0 is the only option in this range. It’s an investment in luxury and health monitoring.

**Pro Tip:** If you have a standard toilet that’s in good condition, a bidet seat like the TOTO G5 or BioBidet BB-2000 is a cost-effective upgrade. If you’re remodeling your bathroom, a complete smart toilet like the Numi 2.0 is worth the splurge.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

**Installation:**

- **Complete Toilets (Kohler Numi 2.0):** Professional installation is mandatory. You’ll need a dedicated 120V electrical outlet within 3 feet of the toilet, plus a standard water supply line. The toilet itself is heavy and requires precise leveling. Expect to pay $300-$500 for installation.
- **Bidet Seats (TOTO G5, BioBidet BB-2000):** These are surprisingly DIY-friendly. The seat attaches to your existing toilet bowl using a mounting plate. You’ll need access to a GFCI-protected electrical outlet (usually within 2-3 feet). The water connection is a simple T-adapter that splits your existing supply line. Most people can install these in under 30 minutes with basic tools.

**Smart Home Integration:**

- **Voice Control:** All three models support Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant. Common commands include "Alexa, turn on the bidet" or "Hey Google, warm the toilet seat." The Kohler Numi 2.0 also supports Apple HomeKit, which is rare and valuable for Apple-centric homes.
- **App Control:** The Kohler Konnect app is the most polished, offering detailed customization, usage statistics, and health data. The TOTO app is solid but less feature-rich. The BioBidet app is functional but can be buggy.
- **Automation:** You can create routines like "Good Morning" that turn on the seat heater and nightlight. The Numi 2.0’s health data can be integrated with health platforms like Apple Health (with user permission).
- **Privacy:** All three manufacturers claim data encryption, but only Kohler offers local storage for health data. If privacy is a concern, avoid models that require cloud storage for core functions.

**Pro Tip:** Ensure your bathroom has a GFCI outlet near the toilet before purchasing. If not, hire an electrician to install one—it’s a safety requirement for any electronic device near water.
