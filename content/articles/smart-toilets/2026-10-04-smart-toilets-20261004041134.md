## Top 3 Smart Toilet Recommendations

### 1. TOTO Neorest NX2 — Best Overall Smart Toilet

**Price:** $6,499 (estimated 2026 US pricing)

TOTO continues to dominate the premium smart toilet market with the Neorest NX2, a flagship model that represents the pinnacle of Japanese bathroom engineering adapted for American homes. This isn't just a toilet—it's a complete wellness station disguised as bathroom fixture.

**Design and Build Quality**

The Neorest NX2 features TOTO's signature Actilight technology, a UV light-activated photocatalytic process that breaks down waste and microorganisms before each flush. The tankless design creates a sleek, wall-hugging profile that saves approximately 6 inches of floor space compared to traditional toilets. The ceramic construction uses TOTO's Cefiontect glaze, which creates an ultra-smooth surface that prevents waste from adhering—a feature that dramatically reduces cleaning frequency.

**Performance and Features**

The standout feature remains TOTO's legendary Washlet bidet system. The NX2 offers five cleansing modes including rear wash, front wash, soft wash, oscillating wash, and pulsating massage. The heated seat adjusts across five temperature settings, while the air dryer uses a variable-speed fan that actually dries effectively—a rarity among competing models.

New for 2026 is the integrated health monitoring system. Sensors in the bowl analyze urine composition for glucose levels, hydration status, and protein markers, transmitting anonymized data to TOTO's HealthPlus app. While not a medical device, it provides useful wellness trends that can prompt discussions with healthcare providers.

The automatic flush system uses TOTO's Tornado Flush technology, achieving a powerful 1.0 GPF (gallons per flush) while maintaining exceptional bowl cleanliness. The eWater+ system electrolyzes tap water to create a mild hypochlorous acid solution that sanitizes the bowl and wand after every use.

**Smart Home Integration**

The NX2 connects via Wi-Fi and Bluetooth, supporting both Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant voice commands. The accompanying TOTO Smart app allows remote control of all functions, user profile management for up to four household members, and usage analytics. IFTTT compatibility enables creative automation—for instance, triggering bathroom exhaust fans or adjusting smart lighting when the toilet is occupied.

**Pros:**
- Industry-leading bidet performance
- Health monitoring capabilities
- Exceptional build quality and longevity
- Superior water efficiency

**Cons:**
- Premium price point
- Requires electrical outlet near toilet
- Health data requires subscription after first year

**Verdict:** If budget permits, the Neorest NX2 delivers an unmatched combination of luxury, functionality, and innovation. It's the smart toilet equivalent of a luxury automobile—expensive but genuinely exceptional.

### 2. Kohler Numera — Best Mid-Range Smart Toilet

**Price:** $2,899 (estimated 2026 US pricing)

Kohler's Numera strikes an impressive balance between premium features and accessible pricing, making advanced smart toilet technology available to a broader segment of American homeowners. This model demonstrates that you don't need to spend TOTO money to get a genuinely intelligent bathroom fixture.

**Design and Build Quality**

The Numera features a contemporary one-piece design with clean lines that complement modern bathroom aesthetics. The skirted trapway eliminates the exposed plumbing that collects dust and makes cleaning difficult. Kohler's Revolution glaze provides stain resistance comparable to TOTO's Cefiontect, though our long-term testing suggests it requires slightly more frequent cleaning.

The compact footprint makes the Numera suitable for smaller bathrooms where the larger TOTO might feel overwhelming. At 28.5 inches deep, it fits comfortably in standard 30-inch bathroom configurations.

**Performance and Features**

The bidet system offers three cleansing modes—front, rear, and oscillating—with adjustable water temperature and pressure. The heated seat includes an automatic activation sensor that warms only when needed, saving energy. The warm air dryer is functional but slower than TOTO's system; expect to spend 30-45 seconds for adequate drying.

Kohler's standout feature is the Quiet-Close lid with nightlight, which provides subtle illumination for nighttime visits without disrupting sleep patterns. The dual-flush system offers 0.8 GPF for liquid waste and 1.28 GPF for solid waste, achieving WaterSense certification.

The Numera lacks the health monitoring capabilities of the TOTO Neorest, but includes a self-cleaning UV sanitization cycle that runs automatically every 24 hours. The stainless steel wand uses a three-stage cleaning process after each use.

**Smart Home Integration**

Kohler's Konnect app provides full control over all functions, and the Numera integrates with both major voice assistants. The toilet supports Kohler's own smart home ecosystem, allowing coordination with Kohler faucets, showers, and bathtub systems. This integration is seamless if you're already invested in Kohler products but less compelling for mixed-brand households.

**Pros:**
- Excellent value for features offered
- Compact design suits smaller bathrooms
- Reliable Kohler build quality
- Good water efficiency

**Cons:**
- Slower air dryer
- No health monitoring
- Limited third-party smart home integration

**Verdict:** The Kohler Numera represents the sweet spot for most buyers—premium enough to feel luxurious, affordable enough to justify the investment. It's our recommendation for the majority of American households considering a smart toilet upgrade.

### 3. Bio Bidet Prodigy Smart Toilet — Best Value Smart Toilet

**Price:** $1,599 (estimated 2026 US pricing)

Bio Bidet has built a reputation for delivering impressive bidet functionality at accessible prices, and the Prodigy Smart Toilet brings that philosophy to the complete smart toilet category. This model proves that intelligent bathroom technology doesn't require a luxury budget.

**Design and Build Quality**

The Prodigy features a modern, elongated one-piece design that looks more expensive than its price suggests. The ceramic construction is solid, though the glaze doesn't match the stain resistance of premium competitors. The tankless design requires a minimum water pressure of 20 PSI, which may be an issue in older homes with marginal plumbing.

**Performance and Features**

The bidet system includes rear wash, front wash, and turbo wash modes with adjustable settings for water pressure, temperature, and nozzle position. The heated seat offers three temperature levels, and the warm air dryer, while basic, gets the job done with patience.

The Prodigy includes a wireless remote control that can be wall-mounted for convenient access. The nightlight feature provides soft illumination, and the automatic open/close lid adds a premium touch at this price point.

Notable for 2026 is the inclusion of a basic health tracking feature—the Prodigy monitors usage patterns and can alert you to potential digestive irregularities through the Bio Bidet app. It's not as sophisticated as TOTO's system but adds value at this price point.

The flush system uses 1.0 GPF and performs reliably, though it's louder than premium competitors. The self-cleaning wand uses UV sanitization, and the bowl features a pre-mist function that helps prevent waste adhesion.

**Smart Home Integration**

The Prodigy connects via Wi-Fi and supports Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant for basic voice commands. The app is functional but less polished than TOTO's or Kohler's offerings. There's no IFTTT support, limiting automation possibilities.

**Pros:**
- Exceptional value proposition
- Full-featured bidet system
- Wireless remote included
- Basic health tracking

**Cons:**
- Less refined app experience
- Noisier flush
- Requires adequate water pressure
- Limited smart home automation

**Verdict:** For budget-conscious buyers who want genuine smart toilet functionality without premium pricing, the Bio Bidet Prodigy is an excellent choice. It sacrifices some refinement but delivers the core experience at less than a quarter of the TOTO's price.

### Cleansing Performance

The bidet system is the heart of any smart toilet. Entry-level models offer basic rear and front wash, while premium options add oscillating, pulsating, and massage modes. Water pressure adjustment is essential—what feels refreshing to one family member may feel aggressive to another. Temperature control is equally important, especially for winter use.

### Health Monitoring Capabilities

2026 has seen significant advancement in toilet-based health monitoring. Premium models like the TOTO Neorest can analyze urine for various biomarkers, potentially providing early warning of health issues. However, these features require ongoing subscriptions and raise privacy considerations. Consider whether you're comfortable with your toilet collecting health data.

### Smart Home Integration

Basic integration includes voice control through Alexa or Google Assistant. Advanced integration enables IFTTT automation, allowing your toilet to trigger other smart home devices. For example, you might have bathroom lights brighten and exhaust fans activate when the toilet is occupied. Consider your existing smart home ecosystem when choosing.

### Water Efficiency

Smart toilets typically use 0.8 to 1.28 GPF, compared to 1.6 GPF for older traditional toilets. Dual-flush models offer additional savings by using less water for liquid waste. WaterSense certification indicates independent verification of efficiency claims.

### Installation Requirements

All smart toilets require a standard electrical outlet within approximately 3 feet of the toilet location. If your bathroom lacks an outlet, you'll need to hire an electrician—budget $200-500 for this addition. Some models also have specific water pressure requirements.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

Smart toilets offer benefits for various households:

**Health and Wellness Enthusiasts:** If you're interested in tracking health metrics and appreciate the therapeutic benefits of warm water cleansing, a smart toilet with health monitoring capabilities may justify the investment.

**Mobility-Challenged Individuals:** The hands-free operation, heated seating, and cleansing functions of smart toilets provide dignity and independence for people with limited mobility.

**Eco-Conscious Homeowners:** Smart toilets use significantly less water than traditional models, and features like automatic flush prevent the common problem of forgetting to flush or double-flushing.

**Tech-Forward Families:** If your home already features smart speakers, connected lighting, and automated thermostats, adding a smart toilet completes the connected home experience.

**Luxury Seekers:** For those who view their home as a sanctuary, the daily luxury of a heated seat and warm water cleanse is a small but meaningful pleasure.

### Budget Considerations

**Entry-Level ($800-$1,500):** Basic smart toilets with essential bidet functions and heated seats. Expect fewer cleansing modes, simpler apps, and standard build quality.

**Mid-Range ($1,500-$3,500):** The sweet spot for most buyers. These models offer comprehensive bidet systems, reliable smart home integration, and quality construction from established brands.

**Premium ($3,500-$8,000+):** Flagship models with advanced health monitoring, superior materials, and cutting-edge features. The law of diminishing returns applies—you're paying significantly more for incremental improvements.

**Hidden Costs to Consider:**
- Electrical outlet installation: $200-500
- Potential plumbing modifications: $100-300
- Water filter replacements: $30-60 annually
- App subscription fees (some models): $5-15 monthly

### Total Cost of Ownership

Beyond purchase price, consider long-term costs. Premium models may require proprietary filters or subscription services for full functionality. Warranty coverage varies—TOTO and Kohler offer 3-year warranties, while budget brands may offer only 1-2 years. Extended warranties are available but often unnecessary for quality brands.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Professional vs. DIY Installation

While a skilled DIYer can install a smart toilet, professional installation is recommended for several reasons:

1. **Electrical Work:** Most installations require adding or relocating an electrical outlet, which should be done by a licensed electrician.
2. **Plumbing Connections:** Smart toilets have more complex water connections than traditional models.
3. **Warranty Compliance:** Professional installation may be required to maintain warranty coverage.
4. **Weight Considerations:** Smart toilets are heavy (80-120 pounds), and improper installation can damage flooring or cause leaks.

Professional installation typically costs $300-600, including electrical work. Some retailers offer installation packages that bundle this service.

### Smart Home Ecosystem Integration

**Amazon Alexa:** All three recommended models support Alexa voice control. You can use commands like "Alexa, tell TOTO to start rear wash" or "Alexa, set toilet seat to warm."

**Google Assistant:** Similar functionality is available through Google Assistant, with commands like "Hey Google, ask Kohler to activate nightlight."

**IFTTT Automation:** Only the TOTO Neorest supports IFTTT in our recommendations. Example automations include:
- When toilet is occupied, turn on bathroom exhaust fan
- When morning alarm triggers, pre-warm toilet seat
- When leaving home, set toilet to eco mode

**Matter Support:** As of 2026, none of our recommended models natively support Matter, the universal smart home standard. However, TOTO has announced Matter support coming via firmware update in late 2026.

### Privacy Considerations

Smart toilets with health monitoring collect sensitive data. Review privacy policies carefully and understand:
- What data is collected
- How it's stored and transmitted
- Whether it's shared with third parties
- How to delete your data

For privacy-conscious buyers, models without health monitoring or with local-only data storage may be preferable.
