### Water Pressure and Temperature

The most critical aspect of any bidet is the quality of the wash. Look for models with **adjustable water pressure** (at least 3 levels) and **instantaneous water heating**. Tank-based heaters can run out of hot water, while tankless systems provide endless warm water. The Toto S7+ leads here with its “Pulsating” mode, but the Bio Bidet BB-2000 is close behind.

### Seat Comfort and Design

Seat shape and material matter. Elongated seats are standard in the US, but some models offer “comfort height” versions. Heated seats with memory foam padding (like the Toto) are far more comfortable than basic plastic. Also check the “soft close” mechanism—it prevents loud slamming and is a sign of quality.

### Smart Home Integration

In 2026, true smart home integration means more than just an app. Look for **voice control** (Alexa and Google), **automation routines** (e.g., turn on heated seat at 7 AM), and **user profiles** for families. The Toto S7+ is the only model here that offers multiple profiles, which is a significant advantage for shared bathrooms.

### Ease of Cleaning

Bidet seats can be a pain to clean if they have crevices and hard-to-reach areas. Models with **self-cleaning nozzles** (especially UV or electrolyzed water) are worth the premium. The Toto’s eWater system is the best, but the Bio Bidet’s UV cleaning is also effective.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Overview

Installing a bidet seat is a DIY-friendly project that takes 30-60 minutes. Here’s what you need:

1. **Tools:** Adjustable wrench, screwdriver, plumber’s tape.
2. **Electrical:** A GFCI outlet within 3 feet of the toilet. If you don’t have one, hire an electrician (cost: $150-$300).
3. **Water supply:** Most models include a T-connector that splits your existing toilet water line.

**Step-by-Step:**
- Turn off the water supply and flush the toilet.
- Remove the existing seat.
- Install the mounting bracket.
- Connect the T-connector to the water supply line.
- Attach the bidet seat to the bracket.
- Connect the water hose and power cord.
- Turn on the water and test for leaks.

### Smart Home Integration

All three recommended models connect to your home Wi-Fi (2.4 GHz only). Here’s how they integrate:

- **Amazon Alexa:** “Alexa, turn on the heated seat.” “Alexa, start the bidet wash.”
- **Google Home:** “Hey Google, set the bidet seat to medium warm.”
- **Automation:** Create routines like “Good Morning” that turns on the seat heat and night light at 7 AM.

**Note:** The Toto S7+ requires the Toto Smart App for initial setup, but after that, voice control works seamlessly. The Bio Bidet BB-2000 and Kohler BH-90 are simpler but less customizable.

### Troubleshooting Common Issues

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| No power | Check GFCI outlet; reset if tripped |
| Weak water pressure | Clean the water filter (usually behind the seat) |
| Wi-Fi connection drops | Move router closer or use a Wi-Fi extender |
| Seat not heating | Check temperature setting; may need to reset breaker |
