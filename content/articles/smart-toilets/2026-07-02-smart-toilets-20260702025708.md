# The Ultimate Guide to Smart Toilets: 2026’s Best High-Tech Thrones Reviewed

## Introduction

The bathroom has long been the last frontier of the smart home revolution—until now. Smart toilets have evolved from quirky Japanese novelties into essential home upgrades that combine hygiene, comfort, and cutting-edge technology. In 2026, these intelligent fixtures offer everything from heated seats and bidet functions to voice controls, self-cleaning capabilities, and even health monitoring sensors. Whether you’re building a new home or upgrading an existing bathroom, a smart toilet can dramatically improve your daily routine while adding significant value to your property. But with dozens of models flooding the market, finding the right one can feel overwhelming. This comprehensive guide breaks down the top smart toilets of 2026, compares key features, and helps you make an informed purchase. We’ve tested the latest models from industry leaders and emerging brands to bring you honest, detailed reviews. Let’s dive into the future of bathroom technology.

## Top 3 Product Recommendations

### 1. Toto Neorest NX2 (2026 Edition) – The Gold Standard

**Price:** $6,800 – $7,200  
**Rating:** 9.5/10

Toto continues to dominate the premium smart toilet segment with the Neorest NX2. This 2026 edition refines an already exceptional product with improved sensor accuracy and a quieter flush mechanism. The NX2 features Toto’s signature Tornado Flush technology, which uses dual nozzles to create a powerful centrifugal cleaning action while using 30% less water than traditional toilets. The heated seat now offers six temperature settings, and the bidet wand has been upgraded with oscillating patterns and a warm air dryer that reaches 140°F in under three seconds.

What truly sets the NX2 apart is its self-cleaning system. After each use, the bowl receives an electrolyzed water rinse that kills 99.9% of bacteria without harsh chemicals. The automatic open/close lid responds to motion sensors with a 5-foot detection range, and the remote control includes a nightlight mode that illuminates the bowl in soft blue. For tech enthusiasts, the NX2 now supports Wi-Fi connectivity through Toto’s app, allowing you to adjust water temperature, seat warmth, and even track usage patterns. Installation requires a standard 110V outlet and a 3/8-inch water line. The only downside is the price—this is a luxury investment. But for those seeking the absolute best, the NX2 delivers unmatched reliability and comfort.

### 2. Kohler Veil Intelligent Toilet – Best Value Premium

**Price:** $4,500 – $5,000  
**Rating:** 9.0/10

Kohler’s Veil Intelligent Toilet strikes an impressive balance between premium features and reasonable pricing. The Veil offers a sleek, skirted design that hides all plumbing connections, creating a minimalist aesthetic that complements modern bathrooms. Its bidet system includes adjustable water pressure, temperature, and spray position, along with a front wash for female users. The heated seat warms to your preset preference within 60 seconds, and the quiet-close lid prevents slamming.

One standout feature is Kohler’s PureWash technology, which uses UV light to sanitize the bidet nozzle after each use. The Veil also includes a touchless flush via infrared sensors, and the included remote control mounts on the wall for easy access. Voice control compatibility with Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant is built-in, allowing you to say, “Alexa, flush the toilet” or “Hey Google, warm the seat.” The 2026 model adds improved battery backup for the electronics and a more efficient pump that works with low water pressure homes. Installation is straightforward with a standard 120V outlet, though Kohler recommends professional installation for warranty purposes. The Veil is an excellent choice for tech-savvy homeowners who want luxury without breaking the bank.

### 3. Bio Bidet Flow 900 – Best Mid-Range Smart Toilet

**Price:** $2,200 – $2,600  
**Rating:** 8.5/10

Bio Bidet has carved out a reputation for bringing smart toilet technology to more accessible price points, and the Flow 900 is their best effort yet. This one-piece toilet includes a heated seat with five temperature levels, a dual-nozzle bidet system with front and rear wash modes, and a warm air dryer that adjusts fan speed and temperature. The Flow 900 uses a siphon-flush system that is both powerful and whisper-quiet—rated at just 38 decibels, quieter than most bathroom fans.

Where the Flow 900 truly shines is its user-friendly interface. The side control panel is intuitive, with large buttons labeled in English, and the included wireless remote adds convenience for elderly users or those with mobility issues. The toilet features a soft-close lid and seat, a pre-mist function that wets the bowl before use to prevent waste from sticking, and a deodorizing carbon filter that activates automatically. For health-conscious users, the Flow 900 includes a nightlight and a slow-close seat that prevents pinched fingers. While it lacks Wi-Fi connectivity and voice control, the Flow 900 delivers core smart toilet functions at a fraction of the cost of premium models. It’s ideal for guest bathrooms or secondary residences where you want smart features without the luxury price tag.

## Key Features to Consider

When shopping for a smart toilet in 2026, understanding the technology behind the features helps you make a better choice. Here’s what to look for:

| Feature | Budget ($800-$2,000) | Mid-Range ($2,000-$4,000) | Premium ($4,000+) |
|---------|---------------------|--------------------------|-------------------|
| **Bidet System** | Basic rear wash only | Front & rear wash, adjustable pressure | Oscillating patterns, temperature control, self-cleaning nozzles |
| **Seat Heating** | Single temperature | 3-4 temperature settings | 5-6 settings with memory |
| **Warm Air Dryer** | Low heat, fixed speed | Adjustable heat & speed | High-velocity, instant heat |
| **Flush Technology** | Gravity flush | Pressure-assisted | Tornado/siphon flush, quiet operation |
| **Self-Cleaning** | Manual cleaning | UV nozzle sanitation | Electrolyzed water bowl cleaning |
| **Smart Features** | Remote control only | App connectivity | Voice control, health monitoring, usage tracking |
| **Installation** | Standard outlet required | GFCI outlet recommended | Hardwired or professional install |

**Additional considerations include:**

- **Water Efficiency:** Look for WaterSense certification (1.28 GPF or less)
- **Bowl Shape:** Elongated bowls offer more comfort than round
- **Height:** Comfort height (17-19 inches) is ADA-compliant
- **Warranty:** Premium models typically offer 3-5 years on electronics, lifetime on ceramics
- **Power Outage Backup:** Manual flush capability is essential

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

Smart toilets aren’t for everyone, but they excel in specific scenarios. **Families with young children** benefit from the hygiene features and automatic lids that reduce germ transfer. **Elderly users or those with mobility issues** appreciate the heated seats, bidet functions that eliminate the need for reaching, and touchless operation. **Tech enthusiasts** will enjoy the app connectivity and voice control integration. If you’re remodeling a master bathroom or building a new home, a smart toilet is a worthwhile investment that adds both convenience and resale value.

However, if you’re on a tight budget or renting your home, a standard toilet with a bidet attachment (starting at $50) may be a more practical choice. Smart toilets require electrical outlets near the toilet, which may not be available in older homes without renovation.

### Budget Considerations

Smart toilet prices range from $800 for basic models to over $8,000 for luxury units. Here’s how to budget wisely:

- **Under $2,000:** You’ll get a heated seat, basic bidet, and remote control. Models like the Toto Washlet C200 or Bio Bidet A8 fit here.
- **$2,000-$4,000:** Mid-range options include dual-nozzle bidets, warm air dryers, and self-cleaning features. The Kohler Veil and Bio Bidet Flow 900 are excellent choices.
- **$4,000-$7,000:** Premium toilets offer all features plus voice control, health sensors, and app integration. The Toto Neorest NX2 leads this category.
- **$7,000+:** Ultra-luxury models like the Duravit SensoWash Starck f or the Villeroy & Boch ViClean add designer aesthetics, customizable lighting, and advanced health diagnostics.

Factor in installation costs: $200-$500 for professional installation, and potential electrical work ($150-$500) if you need an outlet installed near the toilet.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Requirements

Installing a smart toilet is more involved than a standard toilet. Most models require:

1. **Electrical Outlet:** A 110-120V GFCI-protected outlet within 3 feet of the toilet. If you don’t have one, hire a licensed electrician.
2. **Water Supply:** A 3/8-inch compression fitting water line. Some models require a dedicated shut-off valve.
3. **Drain Connection:** Standard 12-inch rough-in (distance from wall to drain center). Measure before purchasing.
4. **Floor Reinforcement:** Smart toilets weigh 80-120 pounds, significantly more than standard toilets. Ensure your floor can support the weight.

**Pro tip:** Purchase a toilet with a “skirted” design (concealed plumbing) for easier cleaning and a cleaner look.

### Smart Home Integration

The best smart toilets in 2026 integrate seamlessly with your existing ecosystem:

- **Amazon Alexa:** Enable voice commands like “Alexa, warm the seat to 95 degrees” or “Alexa, flush the toilet.” Works with Kohler, Toto (via third-party skills), and Bio Bidet.
- **Google Assistant:** Similar functionality. “Hey Google, turn on the bidet” adjusts water temperature and pressure.
- **Apple HomeKit:** Limited support currently, but Toto’s app works with Siri Shortcuts for basic commands.
- **Smart Home Hubs:** Some models integrate with Samsung SmartThings or Hubitat for advanced automation. Example: “When motion sensor detects entry, warm the seat and turn on nightlight.”

**Health monitoring features** are becoming more common. Premium models can track water usage, flush frequency, and even analyze urine output for health trends. Data syncs to your smartphone via the manufacturer’s app, though privacy concerns remain a consideration.
