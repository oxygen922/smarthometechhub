# Smart Toilet Review 2026: The Ultimate Guide to Intelligent Bathroom Upgrades

## Introduction

The smart toilet has evolved from a luxury novelty into one of the most sought-after smart home investments of 2026. Gone are the days when a heated seat was considered cutting-edge—today's intelligent bidet toilets offer self-cleaning nozzles, UV sterilization, personalized user profiles, and even voice control through Alexa and Google Assistant. With the global smart toilet market projected to exceed $13 billion by the end of the decade, American homeowners are finally embracing what European and Japanese households have enjoyed for years: a bathroom experience that's cleaner, more comfortable, and surprisingly eco-friendly. In this comprehensive smart toilet review, we'll break down the top models worth your money, the features that actually matter, and everything you need to know before upgrading your bathroom in 2026.

## Top 3 Smart Toilet Recommendations

### 1. TOTO Neorest NX1 — Best Overall Smart Toilet

**Estimated Price (2026): $9,800–$11,500**

If there's a gold standard in the smart toilet world, it's the TOTO Neorest NX1. TOTO practically invented the modern bidet toilet seat, and the NX1 represents the pinnacle of that decades-long engineering heritage.

The NX1's flagship feature is its **Actilight self-cleaning technology**, which uses a UV light combined with a titanium dioxide-coated bowl surface to break down waste and neutralize bacteria after every use. In practice, this means you can go weeks without scrubbing—a genuine quality-of-life upgrade that traditional toilets simply can't match.

Beyond cleanliness, the NX1 delivers a spa-like experience. The **ewater+ system** electrolyzes tap water to create a mild hypochlorous acid solution that sanitizes both the bowl and the wand after each flush. The **Washlet bidet functions** include front and rear cleansing, adjustable water temperature and pressure, a warm air dryer, and a heated seat with energy-saving scheduling.

What impressed us most during testing was the **sensor-driven lid and flush automation**. The lid opens as you approach, the seat warms to your preferred temperature, and the toilet flushes automatically when you walk away. TOTO's "Tornado Flush" system is also remarkably quiet and powerful, using just 1.0 gallons per flush—well below the EPA's 1.28 gpf standard.

**Pros:**
- Industry-leading self-cleaning and sterilization
- Exceptionally quiet, powerful flush
- Premium build quality with a 3-year limited warranty

**Cons:**
- Eye-watering price tag
- Requires a dedicated electrical outlet near the toilet
- Installation is best handled by a professional plumber

**Verdict:** The TOTO Neorest NX1 is the smart toilet to beat. If budget is no object and you want the absolute best, this is it.

### 2. Kohler Numera — Best Smart Toilet for Smart Home Integration

**Estimated Price (2026): $4,200–$5,600**

Kohler's Numera smart toilet is the model we recommend to anyone building a connected home ecosystem. It's the rare smart toilet that plays genuinely well with others—supporting **Amazon Alexa, Google Home, and Apple HomeKit** out of the box.

The Numera's standout feature is its **Kohler Konnect app integration**, which lets you create personalized cleansing profiles for up to four users. Each profile remembers your preferred wand position, water pressure, temperature, and dryer settings. Voice commands like "Alexa, ask Kohler to start my favorite cleanse" actually work, which is more than we can say for many competitors' half-baked voice features.

Cleansing performance is excellent, with a **stainless steel wand** that self-cleans before and after each use. The heated seat offers adjustable warmth, and the **UV light sanitation** cycles automatically. Kohler also includes a **motion-activated lid** and a **nightlight**—a small touch that makes a big difference for midnight bathroom trips.

One area where the Numera trails TOTO is flush technology. Its flush is reliable and efficient at 1.28 gpf, but it's noticeably louder than the Neorest's Tornado Flush.

**Pros:**
- Best-in-class smart home compatibility (Alexa, Google, HomeKit)
- Four personalized user profiles
- Sleek, modern design that fits most bathroom aesthetics

**Cons:**
- Louder flush than premium competitors
- App can be sluggish after firmware updates
- Mid-range price still represents a significant investment

**Verdict:** For smart home enthusiasts, the Kohler Numera is the most connected smart toilet on the market—and a joy to use daily.

### 3. Bio Bidet Bliss BB-2000 — Best Value Smart Toilet

**Estimated Price (2026): $1,100–$1,500**

Not everyone wants to spend five figures on a bathroom fixture, and the Bio Bidet Bliss BB-2000 proves you don't have to. This **bidet toilet seat** (it attaches to your existing toilet) delivers roughly 85% of the premium experience at roughly 15% of the price.

The BB-2000 features a **wireless remote control**, three cleansing modes (posterior, feminine, and turbo), adjustable water pressure and temperature, a heated seat, and a warm air dryer. Its **stainless steel nozzle** self-cleans automatically, and the unit includes a **deodorizer** that genuinely reduces odors rather than just masking them.

Installation is refreshingly simple. If you can turn a wrench and have a GFCI outlet near your toilet, you can install the BB-2000 in about 45 minutes. It fits most standard elongated toilets, though you'll want to measure before buying.

The trade-offs are real: no auto-flush, no UV sterilization, and the build quality—while solid—isn't in the same league as TOTO or Kohler. But for first-time smart toilet buyers or anyone remodeling on a budget, the Bliss BB-2000 is the smartest entry point available.

**Pros:**

**Cons:**

- No smart home or app connectivity
- Plastic components feel less premium

**Verdict:** The Bio Bidet Bliss BB-2000 is the best smart toilet seat for budget-conscious buyers who still want the core bidet experience.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

Smart toilets aren't for everyone, but they make exceptional sense for specific buyers:

- **Homeowners planning to age in place:** Heated seats, bidet cleansing, and auto-flush features dramatically improve accessibility for seniors or anyone with limited mobility. The hands-free operation alone is worth the investment for many families.
- **Smart home enthusiasts:** If your home already runs on Alexa, Google Home, or HomeKit, a connected smart toilet like the Kohler Numera slots naturally into your ecosystem.
- **Eco-conscious households:** Bidet toilets reduce toilet paper consumption by up to 80%, and efficient models use less water per flush than older toilets.
- **Anyone with medical needs:** Physicians frequently recommend bidet cleansing for patients with hemorrhoids, IBS, or post-surgical recovery needs.
- **Remodelers and luxury buyers:** A smart toilet is one of the most impactful upgrades you can make to a master bathroom, both for daily enjoyment and resale appeal.

### Who Should Skip It?

- **Renters:** Installation requires plumbing and electrical work that most landlords won't approve.
- **Tight bathroom layouts:** One-piece smart toilets need adequate clearance and a nearby outlet.
- **Budget-first buyers:** A basic bidet seat ($200–$400) delivers core cleansing without the smart features.

### Budget Considerations

Set realistic expectations by tier:

- **Under $500:** Basic bidet seats with heated water and adjustable nozzles. No smart features.
- **$500–$1,500:** Premium bidet seats like the Bio Bidet Bliss BB-2000. Best value tier.
- **$1,500–$4,000:** Entry-level one-piece smart toilets from brands like WoodBridge and Ove.
- **$4,000–$7,000:** Mid-to-high-end smart toilets with app control and premium finishes (Kohler, American Standard).
- **$7,000+:** Flagship luxury models (TOTO Neorest series).

Don't forget hidden costs: **professional installation ($300–$800)**, potential **electrical outlet installation ($200–$500)**, and **old toilet disposal ($50–$150)** .

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Basics

**One-piece smart toilets** require three connections: water supply, floor flange, and a 120V GFCI electrical outlet within roughly three feet of the unit. If your bathroom lacks an outlet near the toilet, budget for an electrician. Most manufacturers—including TOTO and Kohler—strongly recommend professional installation, and doing so typically preserves your warranty.

**Bidet seats** are far simpler. They mount between your existing toilet bowl and seat using the same bolts, then connect to the tank's water supply with an included T-adapter. Expect 30–60 minutes for a confident DIYer.

### Smart Home Integration

This is where the market has matured dramatically. In 2026:

- **Kohler Numera** works with Alexa, Google Home, and Apple HomeKit, supporting voice commands for cleansing, seat warming, and flush.
- **TOTO** offers app-based control via Bluetooth, but its ecosystem is more closed—no native Alexa or Google integration on the NX1.
- **Budget models** generally skip connectivity entirely, relying on physical remotes.

For most buyers, smart home integration is a "nice to have" rather than a must. The core bidet functions are operated by remote or side panel regardless. But if you love voice control, prioritize Kohler or check for Matter compatibility, which more manufacturers are adopting in 2026.

### Maintenance Tips

- Run the self-cleaning cycle regularly, even if you don't use the bidet daily.
- Descale the wand monthly in hard-water areas.
- Keep the remote away from direct water exposure.
- Replace the deodorizer cartridge every 3–6 months (varies by model).
