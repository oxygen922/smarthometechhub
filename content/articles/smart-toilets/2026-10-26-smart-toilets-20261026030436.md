# Smart Toilet Review 2026: Are High-Tech Toilets Worth the Investment?

## Introduction

Smart toilets have evolved from futuristic novelties into serious home upgrades. If you're remodeling a bathroom or simply tired of cold seats and toilet paper runs, a smart toilet can transform your daily routine with heated seating, bidet washing, self-cleaning cycles, and even health tracking. In 2026, the market has matured significantly, with reliable models available across a wide price range—from budget-friendly bidet seats to luxury all-in-one units with app connectivity.

But not every smart toilet is worth your money. Some offer gimmicks, others deliver genuine comfort and hygiene benefits. After testing more than a dozen models over the past year, we've narrowed down the best options for US households. This review covers our top three recommendations, the features that matter most, a practical buying guide, and everything you need to know about installation and smart home integration. Whether you're a tech enthusiast or a first-time buyer, you'll find clear, honest guidance here.

## Top 3 Product Recommendations

### 1. TOTO Neorest WX1 — Best Overall Smart Toilet

**Price (2026 estimate): $4,200–$4,800**

TOTO remains the gold standard in the smart toilet category, and the Neorest WX1 is its most refined residential model yet. This all-in-one unit combines a sleek, skirted design with TOTO's legendary Washlet technology, and the 2026 iteration adds improved sensor accuracy and a quieter flush mechanism.

Performance is exceptional. The ActiGlide wand system uses electrolyzed water to clean itself before and after each use, and the premist feature wets the bowl before you sit down, reducing waste adhesion. The heated seat offers five temperature settings, and the warm air dryer is genuinely effective—not the weak puff you find on cheaper units. The automatic lid opens as you approach, and the flush triggers when you walk away, which feels like magic the first week and becomes indispensable by the second.

The Neorest WX1 also includes TOTO's eWater+ system, which keeps the bowl cleaner between uses. The integrated night light is subtle and useful. Installation is more involved than a standard toilet—you'll need a dedicated GFCI outlet and a water line near the fixture—but TOTO's installation guide is thorough, and professional installation typically runs $300–$500.

**Pros:** Superior build quality, effective bidet and dryer, reliable sensors, quiet flush
**Cons:** Premium price, requires professional installation, no app connectivity

**Verdict:** If you want the best smart toilet money can buy and don't need app integration, the Neorest WX1 is the one to beat.

**Price (2026 estimate): $2,800–$3,400**

Kohler's Innate brings app connectivity and voice control to the smart toilet space at a price that undercuts TOTO's flagship. The companion Kohler Konnect app lets you adjust temperature, pressure, and position settings from your phone, and the unit works with Amazon Alexa and Google Home—so "Hey Google, flush" is actually a thing.

The bidet offers a front-and-rear wash with adjustable pressure and oscillating spray, and the stainless steel wand self-cleans with UV light. The heated seat has three settings, and the dryer is adequate if not quite as powerful as TOTO's. The Innate's standout feature is its personalized user profiles: up to four people can save their preferred settings, which the toilet remembers automatically.

Aesthetic design is a strong point—the Innate has a clean, contemporary look that suits modern bathrooms, and the skirted base makes cleaning easy. The touchscreen remote is intuitive, though it requires batteries and is easy to misplace. Kohler's customer support is responsive, and the warranty covers three years on electronics.

**Pros:** App and voice control, user profiles, attractive design, strong warranty
**Cons:** Dryer less powerful than TOTO, remote dependency, occasional app sync issues

**Verdict:** Best choice for smart home enthusiasts who want connectivity and customization without TOTO's price tag.

## Key Features to Consider

Understanding what separates a good smart toilet from a great one starts with the feature set. Here's what matters most:

**Bidet Washing:** Look for adjustable water pressure and temperature, and both front (feminine) and rear wash modes. Tankless water heating is superior—it provides unlimited warm water and takes up less space.

**Heated Seat:** Nearly universal on smart toilets, but temperature range and consistency vary. Multi-level settings are standard; check for even heat distribution.

**Dryer:** Often the weakest feature on budget models. Higher-end units offer adjustable temperature and stronger airflow. Don't expect a hair-dryer experience—think gentle warmth.

**Self-Cleaning:** Stainless steel nozzles with self-rinse or UV sterilization are the standard. Some premium models use electrolyzed water for bowl cleaning.

**Automation:** Auto open/close lids and auto flush are convenience features found on all-in-one units. Sensor reliability matters—cheaper sensors misfire.

**Connectivity:** App control, voice assistant integration, and user profiles are increasingly common. Useful for multi-user households; not essential for everyone.

**Deodorizer:** Carbon filter systems neutralize odors effectively on mid-range and premium models.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

**Full smart toilets** make sense if you're remodeling a bathroom, replacing an aging fixture, or building a new home. The all-in-one form factor looks cleaner, performs better, and integrates flush automation—but it requires professional installation and a dedicated outlet.

**Bidet seats** are ideal for renters, budget-conscious buyers, or anyone whose current toilet works fine. They deliver the hygiene and comfort benefits without the installation hassle or expense.

**Smart home enthusiasts** should prioritize models with app and voice control, like the Kohler Innate. If you use Alexa or Google Home daily, the integration is genuinely convenient.

**Households with limited mobility** benefit enormously from auto open/close, heated seats, and bidet washing. Smart toilets are increasingly recommended by occupational therapists for aging-in-place setups.

### Budget Considerations

Set realistic expectations by budget tier:

- **Under $600:** Bidet seats. Expect heated seats, warm water, and dryers—no automation.
- **$1,500–$3,000:** Entry-level all-in-one smart toilets from brands like WoodBridge and HOROW. Solid features, fewer refinements.
- **$3,000–$5,000:** Premium territory. TOTO and Kohler flagships with superior build quality and reliability.

Don't forget installation costs ($300–$500 for professional), potential electrical work ($200–$600 if you need an outlet installed), and ongoing costs—smart toilets use minimal electricity, typically $30–$60 per year.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Realities

Bidet seats are genuinely DIY-friendly—most install in 30–60 minutes with a wrench and screwdriver. The main hurdle is the outlet: if your bathroom lacks a GFCI outlet near the toilet, budget for an electrician.

All-in-one smart toilets are heavier (80–120 lbs) and require proper floor anchoring, a water supply connection, and a dedicated electrical circuit. Professional installation is strongly recommended. Expect $300–$500 for labor, plus any electrical work. Measure your rough-in distance (typically 12 inches) before ordering—getting this wrong means a return.

### Smart Home Integration

The Kohler Innate leads here, with native Alexa and Google Home support. You can flush by voice, adjust settings, and receive maintenance reminders. TOTO's Neorest line focuses on reliability over connectivity—no app, but sensors that simply work. Bio Bidet seats are standalone devices with no smart home integration.

For most households, app control is a nice-to-have rather than a must. The features you'll use daily—heated seat, bidet wash, auto flush—work without any connectivity. But if you're building a connected home, Kohler's ecosystem is the most mature in this category.
