# Smart Toilet Review 2026: The Best Intelligent Bidet Toilets for Your Smart Home

## Introduction

The humble bathroom has quietly become one of the most exciting frontiers in home automation. In 2026, smart toilets have evolved from novelty gadgets into genuinely useful fixtures that combine hygiene, comfort, and connectivity in ways that traditional toilets simply cannot match. These intelligent bidet toilets offer heated seats, warm-water washing, self-cleaning nozzles, automatic lids, and even voice control through Alexa and Google Assistant. If you have ever winced at cold toilet seats in winter or wrestled with a roll of toilet paper, a smart toilet might be the upgrade you have been waiting for. In this in-depth smart toilet review, we will break down the best models of 2026, explain the key features that matter, and help you decide which intelligent toilet belongs in your home. Whether you are remodeling a master bath or dipping your toes into smart home technology, this guide covers everything you need to know before you buy.

## Top 3 Smart Toilet Recommendations

### 1. TOTO Neorest NX2 — Best Overall Smart Toilet

TOTO has long been the gold standard in the bidet toilet world, and the Neorest NX2 continues that legacy in 2026. This flagship model is a complete toilet-and-bidet system that delivers a spa-like experience every single day.

**Design and Build Quality**

The Neorest NX2 features a sleek, skirted one-piece design that hides the trapway and makes cleaning remarkably easy. The ceramic bowl uses TOTO's proprietary CeFiONtect glaze, which repels waste and bacteria, keeping the surface smoother for longer. At roughly 21 inches high, the seat sits at a comfortable ADA-friendly height for most adults.

**Performance and Features**

The star of the show is TOTO's Tornado Flush system, which uses a powerful cyclonic rinse to clear the bowl with just 0.8 to 1.0 gallons per flush. The bidet functions are equally impressive: you get front and rear warm-water washing, a heated seat with temperature memory, a warm-air dryer, and an automatic deodorizer that neutralizes odors within seconds.

The NX2 also includes TOTO's Actilight technology, which uses UV light and a titanium dioxide coating to break down bacteria after each use. The lid opens automatically as you approach and closes when you leave, and the nightlight softly illuminates the bowl for late-night visits.

**Smart Home Integration**

The Neorest NX2 connects to TOTO's app via Bluetooth, allowing you to save user profiles for multiple family members. Each profile remembers seat temperature, water pressure, and nozzle position. It does not natively support Alexa or Google Assistant, which is a minor drawback, but the app experience is polished and reliable.

**Pricing and Verdict**

At an estimated $9,500 to $11,000 in 2026, the Neorest NX2 is a serious investment. However, for buyers who want the absolute best in hygiene, comfort, and craftsmanship, it remains the smart toilet to beat. If you are building a luxury bathroom and want a fixture that will last a decade or more, this is our top pick.

### 2. Kohler Numera — Best Smart Home Integration

Kohler has been pushing hard into the connected home space, and the Numera is its most ambitious smart toilet yet. This model is designed for buyers who want their bathroom to talk to the rest of their smart home ecosystem.

**Design and Build Quality**

The Numera sports a modern, minimalist silhouette with a tankless design that saves space and looks stunning in contemporary bathrooms. The seat is slim and heated, and the stainless steel wand retracts discreetly when not in use. Build quality is excellent, with a durable vitreous china bowl and a soft-close lid that feels premium.

**Performance and Features**

The Numera offers a full suite of bidet functions: adjustable warm-water cleansing, a heated seat, a warm-air dryer, and an automatic deodorizer. The flush is powerful and efficient, using 1.28 gallons per flush. Kohler's AquaPiston flushing technology ensures a consistent, reliable flush every time.

What sets the Numera apart is its integration with Kohler Konnect, the company's smart home platform. You can control the toilet through the Kohler Konnect app, and it works with Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant. Imagine saying, "Alexa, ask Kohler to open the lid," or setting a nighttime routine that lowers the seat temperature and dims the lights. It is a genuinely useful level of integration that few competitors match.

**Smart Home Integration**

Beyond voice control, the Numera can sync with other Kohler Konnect products, such as smart showers and faucets, to create coordinated bathroom scenes. You can also set user profiles so each family member gets their preferred settings automatically.

**Pricing and Verdict**

Expect to pay around $6,500 to $8,000 for the Kohler Numera in 2026. It is priced below the TOTO flagship but offers superior smart home connectivity. If you are building an Alexa- or Google-centric home and want your bathroom to be part of the ecosystem, the Numera is the best choice.

### 3. Bio Bidet Bliss BB-2000 — Best Value Smart Toilet

Not everyone wants to spend five figures on a toilet. The Bio Bidet Bliss BB-2000 offers many of the same comforts as luxury models at a fraction of the price, making it the best value pick for 2026.

**Design and Build Quality**

The BB-2000 is a bidet seat that attaches to your existing toilet, rather than a complete toilet. This makes it a more affordable and less invasive upgrade. The seat is sturdy, with a slim profile that fits most standard elongated toilets. The remote control is intuitive, with clearly labeled buttons and a backlit display.

**Performance and Features**

Despite its lower price, the BB-2000 punches well above its weight. It offers adjustable warm-water washing with a stainless steel nozzle, a heated seat, a warm-air dryer, and a deodorizer. The water pressure and temperature are fully adjustable, and the nozzle self-cleans before and after each use. You also get a massage mode and a "turbo" wash for extra cleaning power.

One standout feature is the BB-2000's unlimited warm water, thanks to its tankless heating system. Many budget bidets run out of warm water after a minute or two, but the BB-2000 keeps going as long as you need it.

**Smart Home Integration**

The BB-2000 does not offer native Alexa or Google Assistant support, but it does include a wireless remote and a nightlight. For most users, the lack of voice control is a fair trade-off for the low price.

**Pricing and Verdict**

At roughly $600 to $800 in 2026, the Bio Bidet Bliss BB-2000 is an outstanding value. It delivers 80 percent of the luxury experience for 10 percent of the price. If you are curious about smart toilets but not ready to commit to a full remodel, this is the perfect entry point.

**Warm-Water Washing**

This is the core function of any smart toilet. Look for adjustable temperature and pressure, and a nozzle that can move forward and back for thorough cleaning. Stainless steel nozzles are more hygienic than plastic ones.

**Heated Seat**

A heated seat is one of the most appreciated features, especially in colder climates. Adjustable temperature settings are a plus, as some people prefer a warmer seat than others.

**Warm-Air Dryer**

A dryer reduces the need for toilet paper, but not all dryers are equally effective. Higher-end models like the TOTO Neorest NX2 have stronger, warmer airflow.

**Auto Open/Close Lid**

This hands-free feature is convenient and hygienic. It is typically found only on complete toilet models, not bidet seats.

**Deodorizer**

An automatic deodorizer uses a carbon filter or catalyst to neutralize odors. It is a subtle but valuable feature in shared bathrooms.

**Smart Home Integration**

If you want voice control or app-based automation, look for models that support Alexa, Google Assistant, or a dedicated app. Kohler leads here, while TOTO focuses on its own app.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

Smart toilets are ideal for several types of buyers:

- **Comfort seekers**: If you hate cold seats or want a spa-like bathroom experience, a smart toilet is a daily luxury.
- **People with mobility issues**: The hands-free lid, auto flush, and bidet washing can be helpful for seniors or anyone with limited mobility.
- **Eco-conscious households**: Bidets reduce toilet paper usage, which saves trees and money over time.
- **Smart home enthusiasts**: If your home is already full of connected devices, a smart toilet with Alexa or Google support is a natural addition.
- **Families**: User profiles and memory settings make it easy for multiple people to share one toilet.

### Budget Considerations

Smart toilets range from a few hundred dollars to well over $10,000. Here is how to think about your budget:

- **Under $1,000**: Bidet seats like the Bio Bidet Bliss BB-2000 offer excellent value. You keep your existing toilet and add smart features.
- **$2,000–$5,000**: Mid-range complete smart toilets from brands like Kohler and American Standard offer a good balance of features and price.
- **$6,000–$12,000**: Luxury models like the TOTO Neorest NX2 deliver the best craftsmanship, performance, and longevity.

Remember to factor in installation costs, which can range from $200 to $1,000 depending on whether you need electrical work or a new outlet near the toilet.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Requirements

Complete smart toilets require a few things that traditional toilets do not:

1. **Electrical outlet**: Most smart toilets need a GFCI-protected outlet within reach of the toilet. If you do not have one, you will need to hire an electrician.
2. **Water supply**: A standard water line is usually sufficient, but check the manufacturer's specifications.
3. **Floor flange**: Ensure your floor flange matches the toilet's requirements, typically a 12-inch rough-in.

Bidet seats are much easier to install. Most can be attached to an existing toilet in under an hour with basic tools, though you still need an outlet nearby.

### Smart Home Integration Tips

- **Voice control**: If you use Alexa or Google Assistant, choose a toilet that supports them, like the Kohler Numera.
- **Scenes and routines**: Set up a "night mode" that dims lights and lowers seat temperature when you get up in the middle of the night.
- **User profiles**: Take advantage of memory settings so each family member gets their preferred wash and seat settings.
- **Energy saving**: Many smart toilets have eco modes that reduce seat heating when not in use. Enable these to save electricity.
