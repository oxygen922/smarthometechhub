# The Ultimate Smart Toilet Buyer's Guide for 2026: Top Picks, Features, and Installation Tips

In 2026, the market is flooded with options that promise heated seats, bidet functions, self-cleaning capabilities, and seamless integration with your smart home ecosystem. This comprehensive guide cuts through the noise, providing you with in-depth reviews of the top three smart toilets of 2026, a detailed breakdown of key features, practical buying advice, and essential installation tips. Whether you're a tech enthusiast building a fully connected home or a homeowner seeking everyday convenience and improved sanitation, this review will help you make an informed decision. ## Top 3 Product Recommendations

### 1. Toto Neorest NX2 (2026 Edition) – The Gold Standard

The Toto Neorest NX2 remains the benchmark for luxury smart toilets. This latest iteration refines an already exceptional experience. The signature feature is Toto's advanced Tornado Flush system, which uses dual-nozzle cyclonic jets to clean the bowl with remarkable efficiency and near-silent operation. The bidet function offers adjustable water temperature, pressure, and position, with a dedicated warm air dryer that is surprisingly powerful and quiet. The heated seat is now even more responsive, with multiple temperature zones. The NX2 also includes an automatic lid and seat, a built-in deodorizer, and a self-cleaning wand that sanitizes before and after each use. The 2026 model features an upgraded UV light sterilization cycle for the bowl. It integrates flawlessly with Toto's proprietary app and works with Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant for voice control. While the price is premium, the build quality, reliability, and superior cleaning performance justify the investment for those who want the best.

**Pros:** Unmatched flush power, exceptional build quality, quiet operation, comprehensive bidet features.
**Cons:** Very high price, complex installation may require a professional, limited color options.

**Price:** $6,500 - $7,200 (2026 estimate)

### 2. Kohler PureWash E930 – The Best Value for Smart Features

For homeowners seeking a premium experience without the Toto price tag, the Kohler PureWash E930 is an outstanding choice. This is a bidet seat that can be added to most standard two-piece toilets, making it a more accessible upgrade. The E930 features a sleek, low-profile design with a heated seat, a warm water bidet with oscillating and pulsating spray patterns, and a powerful warm air dryer. Its standout feature is the advanced user presets, which allow up to two users to save their preferred water temperature, pressure, and seat temperature. The remote control is intuitive and backlit. Kohler has improved the self-cleaning wand with a new anti-microbial coating. The E930 integrates with Kohler's Konnect app and is compatible with Alexa and Google Assistant. The installation is straightforward for a DIY enthusiast, as it replaces the existing toilet seat and connects to the water supply. While it lacks the integrated toilet design of the Toto, its performance is exceptional for the price, and it offers a fantastic upgrade path for existing bathrooms.

**Pros:** Excellent value, easy DIY installation, user presets, strong bidet performance.
**Cons:** Not an integrated toilet, plastic seat feels less premium than Toto, dryer is less powerful.

**Price:** $1,200 - $1,500 (2026 estimate)

### 3. Bio Bidet Bliss BB-2000 – The Tech Lover's Choice

The Bio Bidet Bliss BB-2000 is designed for the connected home enthusiast. This integrated smart toilet combines a sleek, skirted design with a wealth of technological features. The bidet offers a wide range of spray options, including a child wash and a powerful enema wash. The heated seat is exceptionally comfortable, and the warm air dryer is among the best in its class. The BB-2000's party trick is its advanced smart home integration. It works natively with Apple HomeKit, in addition to Alexa and Google Assistant, allowing for Siri voice control and automation routines. For example, you can trigger the seat warmer to turn on when your morning alarm goes off. The toilet also features a motion-activated night light, a slow-close lid, and a dual-flush system for water conservation. The self-cleaning function is robust, with a pre-mist and UV light. While the installation is more involved than a seat-only model, the integrated design is beautiful and easy to clean. The BB-2000 is a fantastic choice for Apple users and anyone who wants deep smart home integration.

**Pros:** Native Apple HomeKit support, excellent bidet and dryer, beautiful integrated design, great water efficiency.
**Cons:** Can be difficult to find replacement parts, customer support can be slow, price is still high.

**Price:** $3,800 - $4,500 (2026 estimate)

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy What?

- **The Luxury Seeker (Budget >$6,000):** The **Toto Neorest NX2** is for you. It is the ultimate in comfort, reliability, and performance. Ideal for master bathrooms in high-end homes.
- **The Smart Home Integrator (Budget $3,500-$4,500):** The **Bio Bidet Bliss BB-2000** is your best bet, especially if you are in the Apple ecosystem. The HomeKit support is a game-changer for automation.
- **The Value-Conscious Upgrader (Budget $1,000-$1,500):** The **Kohler PureWash E930** offers 90% of the premium experience at a fraction of the cost. Perfect for upgrading an existing toilet.
- **The First-Time Buyer:** Start with a bidet seat like the Kohler PureWash E930. It's less expensive and easier to install, allowing you to test the waters before committing to a full integrated toilet.

### Budget Considerations

- **Entry-Level (Bidet Seats):** $300 - $800. These offer basic heated seats and bidet functions but lack dryers and smart features.
- **Mid-Range (Bidet Seats & Basic Integrated Toilets):** $1,000 - $3,000. This is the sweet spot for most buyers. You get excellent features like warm air drying, user presets, and app control.
- **High-End (Premium Integrated Toilets):** $4,000 - $8,000+. This tier offers the best design, most powerful features, and highest build quality. The Toto Neorest NX2 sits here.

**Hidden Costs:** Factor in professional installation ($200-$500), electrical outlet installation (if not already present near the toilet, $150-$400), and potential plumbing modifications.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation

- **Bidet Seats (e.g., Kohler PureWash E930):** These are designed for DIY installation. You remove your existing toilet seat, attach the bidet seat, and connect a T-adapter to your water supply line. Most models come with clear instructions and all necessary hardware. Expect a 30-60 minute job.
- **Integrated Toilets (e.g., Toto Neorest NX2, Bio Bidet Bliss BB-2000):** Professional installation is highly recommended. These toilets are heavier, require precise leveling, and often need a dedicated electrical outlet (GFCI protected) within reach. The plumbing connections can be more complex. Incorrect installation can void the warranty.

### Smart Home Integration

All three recommended models offer robust smart home integration, but the level of control varies.

- **Alexa & Google Assistant:** All three support basic voice commands like "Alexa, turn on the bidet" or "Hey Google, warm the seat." You can also integrate them into routines.
- **Apple HomeKit:** This is exclusive to the **Bio Bidet Bliss BB-2000**. It allows for deeper integration with your Apple ecosystem. You can use Siri, create automations based on time of day or other HomeKit sensors, and control the toilet from the Home app.
- **Proprietary Apps:** Each brand has its own app (Toto, Kohler Konnect, Bio Bidet) for more detailed control, firmware updates, and user profile management.

**Pro Tip:** Before buying, ensure your Wi-Fi signal reaches your bathroom. A strong 2.4 GHz signal is best for smart home devices.
