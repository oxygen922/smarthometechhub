# The Best Smart Toilets of 2026: A Complete Buyer's Guide and Review

## Introduction

Walk into any modern American bathroom in 2026, and you might notice something different: toilets that warm your seat before you sit, clean you with precision-positioned water jets, and even analyze your health through your morning routine. Smart toilets have officially moved from luxury novelty to mainstream home upgrade, and the technology has matured dramatically over the past two years.

The global smart toilet market is projected to surpass $12 billion by 2027, driven by aging-in-place preferences, growing awareness of hygiene benefits, and a surge in bathroom remodeling projects. Major brands like Kohler, TOTO, and Bio Bidet are competing fiercely, while newer entrants keep pushing prices down and features up.

But here's the challenge: with bidet seats, integrated smart toilets, and everything in between, choosing the right model can feel overwhelming. Tankless? Heated seat? Remote control? Self-cleaning? This guide breaks down the best smart toilets of 2026, what features actually matter, and how to pick the right one for your bathroom and budget. Let's dive in.

## Top 3 Smart Toilet Recommendations for 2026

### 1. Kohler Numi 2.0 Pro — Best Overall Smart Toilet

Kohler's flagship Numi 2.0 Pro remains the gold standard for integrated smart toilets, and the 2026 refresh brings meaningful upgrades that justify its eye-watering price tag for those who can afford it.

The headline addition is an upgraded AI-powered health sensing suite. Using optical and biosensor arrays built into the bowl, the Numi 2.0 Pro can track hydration markers, detect urinary tract infection indicators, and log digestive patterns over time through the Kohler Konnect app. It's not a medical device, and Kohler is careful to frame it as wellness tracking rather than diagnostics, but the data is surprisingly granular.

Beyond health tech, the Numi 2.0 Pro delivers on pure comfort. The heated seat adjusts across five temperature zones, the bidet offers front, rear, and oscillating wash modes with adjustable pressure and position, and the warm air dryer actually works—a rarity in this category. Ambient lighting syncs with your smart home scenes, and the built-in Bluetooth speaker system sounds genuinely good, not like a tinny afterthought.

**What we love:**

- Motion-activated lid and flush

**What could be better:**
- The price is prohibitive for most households
- Requires a dedicated GFCI outlet near the toilet

**Verdict:** If budget is no object and you want the most feature-complete smart toilet available in 2026, the Numi 2.0 Pro is it. For everyone else, keep reading.

### 2. TOTO Neorest AH Dual Flush — Best Value Integrated Smart Toilet

**Price: $4,200–$5,000 (estimated 2026)**

TOTO practically invented the modern bidet toilet, and the Neorest AH represents the sweet spot in their lineup—professional-grade performance without the six-figure bathroom remodel energy of the Numi.

The Neorest AH uses TOTO's celebrated Tornado Flush system with dual flush options (1.0 and 0.8 gallons per flush), making it one of the most water-efficient smart toilets on the market. The bidet functionality is where TOTO shines: the eWater+ electrolyzed water system sanitizes both the bowl and the wand after every use, reducing the need for harsh chemicals.

The heated seat is comfortably warm within seconds, and the remote control is intuitive—a rare compliment in the smart toilet world, where remotes often resemble confused TV clickers. The lid opens automatically via proximity sensor, and the nightlight is subtle enough not to wake your partner.

Where the Neorest AH falls short of the Numi is in smart home integration and health tracking. There's no voice assistant support, and the app is functional but basic. You get notifications when the wand needs cleaning or the filter requires replacement, and that's about it.

**What we love:**
- Exceptional water efficiency (Watersense certified)
- eWater+ sanitization is genuinely effective
- Reliable, proven TOTO engineering
- Comfortable for users of all heights and mobility levels

**What could be better:**
- Limited smart home ecosystem support
- No health or wellness tracking
- Remote requires batteries and a wall mount you'll need to install

**Verdict:** The Neorest AH is the smart toilet we recommend to most people who want integrated luxury without the bleeding-edge price. It's the Honda Accord of smart toilets: not flashy, but exceptionally well-engineered.

### Feature Deep Dive

**Bidet wash quality** is the single most important differentiator between a good and great smart toilet. Look for models with adjustable water pressure, position control, and at least two wash modes (front and rear). Premium models add oscillating and pulsating options, which sound gimmicky until you try them.

**Heating systems** come in two flavors: reservoir (tank) and tankless. Reservoir models heat a limited amount of water and can run cold during extended use. Tankless systems heat water on demand and are worth the upgrade if your budget allows.

**Smart home integration** is where the market is heading fastest. Matter support is becoming standard in 2026, which means your smart toilet can finally talk to your smart home hub without proprietary bridges. If you're building a connected home, prioritize Matter-compatible models.

**Health tracking** is the newest frontier. Kohler and TOTO both offer some form of wellness monitoring, but the technology is still maturing. Treat these features as nice-to-have rather than deciding factors—they're not replacements for medical advice.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

**Integrated smart toilets** (the $3,000+ models) make sense for:
- Homeowners planning to stay in their home for 5+ years
- People with mobility challenges who benefit from hands-free operation
- Households remodeling a primary bathroom
- Anyone with a diagnosed medical condition where bidet use provides therapeutic benefit

**Smart toilet seats** (the $300–$1,000 models) make sense for:
- Renters who can't replace fixtures
- Budget-conscious buyers who want the core experience
- Anyone unsure whether they'll enjoy a bidet long-term
- Secondary bathrooms where full integration isn't necessary

### Budget Considerations

Set realistic expectations by price tier:

- **Under $400:** Basic bidet seats with heated water but limited features. Expect manual nozzles and no dryer.
- **$400–$1,000:** The sweet spot for seats. You get heated seats, warm air dryers, remote controls, and self-cleaning nozzles.
- **$1,000–$3,000:** Entry-level integrated smart toilets. One-piece construction, auto flush, and basic smart features.
- **$3,000–$7,000:** Mid-range integrated toilets with premium bidet functions, auto open/close, and app connectivity.
- **$7,000+:** Flagship models with health tracking, voice control, and every bell and whistle imaginable.

Don't forget hidden costs: electrical work ($200–$500 if you need an outlet installed), potential plumbing adjustments, and replacement filters or descaling cartridges ($30–$80 annually).

### Electrical Requirements

Every smart toilet and most smart seats require a GFCI-protected outlet within cord reach (typically 3–4 feet). If your bathroom doesn't have one near the toilet, budget for an electrician. This is non-negotiable—do not use extension cords in a bathroom.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Overview

**Smart toilet seats** are a DIY-friendly project. Most install in 30–60 minutes using a screwdriver and adjustable wrench. You'll remove your existing seat, attach a T-adapter to your water supply, mount the bidet seat, and plug it in. The most common hiccup is a short water supply line—pick up a longer braided hose for about $10 before you start.

**Integrated smart toilets** are a different story. These units weigh 80–120 pounds and require:
- Removing your old toilet and disposing of it
- Checking that your floor flange and rough-in measurements match the new unit
- Connecting the water supply and electrical
- Potentially hiring a licensed plumber ($300–$600) and electrician

If you're not comfortable with plumbing, hire a professional. A botched toilet install is a expensive mistake.

### Smart Home Integration in 2026

The smart toilet ecosystem has improved dramatically thanks to Matter. Here's what's possible:

- **Voice control:** "Alexa, open the toilet lid" or "Hey Google, activate the bidet"
- **Scenes:** Trigger warm seat and nightlight when your "Good Morning" routine runs
- **Automation:** Toilet flushes when everyone leaves home; nightlight activates after sunset
- **Health data:** Sync wellness tracking to Apple Health or Google Fit (Kohler and TOTO only)

Kohler's Konnect platform remains the most robust, supporting Alexa, Google Assistant, and IFTTT. TOTO has improved its app but still lags in third-party integrations. Budget seats generally have no smart home connectivity at all—a key trade-off to consider.

**Pro tip:** If smart home integration matters to you, verify Matter support before buying. It's the difference between a toilet that works with everything and one locked into a single ecosystem.
