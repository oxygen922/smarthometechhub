# The Ultimate Smart Toilet Review: Elevate Your Bathroom Experience in 2026

Smart toilets have evolved from a niche luxury to a mainstream necessity for tech-savvy homeowners. In 2026, these intelligent fixtures offer more than just a heated seat—they integrate with your smart home ecosystem, provide personalized hygiene, and even analyze your health. Whether you're remodeling your master bath or simply want to upgrade your daily routine, choosing the right smart toilet can be overwhelming. This comprehensive review breaks down the top models, key features, and everything you need to know before making a purchase. From bidet functions to voice control, we've tested the latest offerings to help you find the perfect fit for your home and budget.

## Top 3 Smart Toilet Recommendations for 2026

After extensive testing and analysis of current models, these three smart toilets stand out for their performance, features, and value.

### 1. Toto Neorest NX2 – The Premium Powerhouse

**Price:** $6,500 – $7,200 (2026 estimate)

The Toto Neorest NX2 continues to set the gold standard for luxury smart toilets. This model builds on Toto's legendary engineering with a sleek, one-piece design that eliminates hard-to-clean crevices. The standout feature is the eWater+ system, which uses electrolyzed water to clean the bowl and wand automatically after every use, reducing manual cleaning to near zero.

The NX2 offers a fully heated seat with temperature presets, a warm air dryer with adjustable intensity, and Toto's signature front and rear bidet wash with oscillating and pulsating modes. The user interface has been upgraded to a large, backlit remote control with intuitive icons, and the toilet now supports Wi-Fi connectivity for firmware updates and usage tracking via Toto's app.

**Performance:** The wash is precise and powerful, with a self-cleaning wand that retracts automatically. The dual-flush system (1.0/1.28 GPF) is both efficient and powerful, handling solid waste without clogs. The seat occupancy sensor ensures the toilet only activates when someone is seated, preventing accidental sprays.

**Pros:** Exceptional build quality, advanced self-cleaning, quiet operation, excellent warranty.
**Cons:** Very high price point, requires professional installation, large footprint.

### 2. Kohler Veil Intelligent Toilet – The Best All-Rounder

**Price:** $3,800 – $4,500 (2026 estimate)

Kohler's Veil Intelligent Toilet strikes an impressive balance between luxury features and reasonable cost. This model features a modern, skirted design that hides plumbing and creates a clean aesthetic. The highlight is Kohler's PureWash technology, which offers a heated seat, adjustable water temperature, and a warm air dryer with three speed settings.

The Veil includes a touchscreen remote with customizable presets for up to four users, allowing each family member to save their preferred seat temperature, wash position, and water pressure. It also features a UV light for sanitizing the wand and a motion-activated lid that opens automatically when you approach. The toilet connects to Kohler's Konnect app for remote control and usage monitoring.

**Performance:** The bidet function is gentle yet effective, with a wide spray pattern that covers more area. The flush is powerful and quiet, using Kohler's AquaPiston technology for consistent performance. The seat is contoured for comfort, and the slow-close lid prevents slamming.

**Pros:** Excellent value for features, multiple user presets, reliable performance, good app integration.
**Cons:** Installation requires a dedicated electrical outlet, remote can be complex for some users.

### 3. Bio Bidet Ultimate 770 – The Budget-Friendly Champion

**Price:** $1,200 – $1,500 (2026 estimate)

For those who want smart toilet features without breaking the bank, the Bio Bidet Ultimate 770 is a standout choice. This model is a bidet seat attachment that fits most standard two-piece toilets, making it an affordable upgrade path. It includes a heated seat, warm water bidet with front and rear wash, and a warm air dryer.

The Ultimate 770 features a side-mounted control panel with large, easy-to-press buttons and an LED display that shows water temperature and seat status. It also includes an energy-saving mode that reduces power consumption during periods of inactivity. The self-cleaning wand uses a stainless steel nozzle that retracts after each use.

**Performance:** The bidet spray is adjustable in pressure and position, with a gentle "child" mode for sensitive users. The seat heats quickly and maintains temperature well. Installation is straightforward for DIYers, requiring only a T-adapter to connect to the water supply and a GFCI outlet.

**Pros:** Affordable price point, easy installation, reliable performance, good warranty.
**Cons:** Limited smart home integration, no automatic lid, smaller seat may not fit all toilet shapes.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

- **Tech Enthusiasts:** If you have a smart home ecosystem (Alexa, Google Home, Apple HomeKit), a Wi-Fi-enabled smart toilet like the Kohler Veil or Toto NX2 allows for voice control and automation routines.
- **Health-Conscious Individuals:** Features like warm water bidet wash, adjustable pressure, and self-cleaning functions promote better hygiene and reduce toilet paper usage.
- **Seniors or Individuals with Mobility Issues:** Heated seats, automatic lids, and hands-free operation make smart toilets more accessible and comfortable.
- **Luxury Homeowners:** If you're remodeling a master bathroom and want a premium experience, the Toto Neorest NX2 is the ultimate statement piece.
- **Budget-Conscious Upgraders:** The Bio Bidet Ultimate 770 provides many benefits of a smart toilet without the high cost or complex installation.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $2,000:** Bidet seat attachments like the Bio Bidet Ultimate 770 are your best bet. They offer heated seats, warm water wash, and dryers at a fraction of the cost of integrated toilets. Expect to spend $1,000–$1,500.
- **$2,000–$5,000:** This range includes full smart toilets like the Kohler Veil. You get integrated design, automatic lids, multiple user presets, and app connectivity. Professional installation is recommended.
- **Over $5,000:** The premium tier includes models like the Toto Neorest NX2. Expect the best materials, advanced self-cleaning systems, and comprehensive warranties. These are long-term investments for high-end bathrooms.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Requirements

Smart toilets require both a water supply and a nearby electrical outlet (GFCI protected). For integrated models like the Toto NX2 and Kohler Veil, professional installation is strongly recommended. The process typically involves:

1. Removing the existing toilet and wax ring.
2. Installing the smart toilet's mounting bracket.
3. Connecting the water supply line and electrical wiring.
4. Securing the toilet and sealing the base.
5. Testing all functions and calibrating sensors.

Bidet seat attachments are much simpler. Most can be installed in under 30 minutes using basic tools:

1. Turn off the water supply and flush the toilet.
2. Remove the existing toilet seat.
3. Install the T-adapter on the water supply line.
4. Connect the bidet seat to the water and power.
5. Mount the seat and test.

### Smart Home Integration

- **Wi-Fi Connectivity:** Both the Toto NX2 and Kohler Veil connect to your home Wi-Fi network. This enables remote control via smartphone apps, firmware updates, and usage tracking.
- **Voice Control:** The Kohler Veil works with Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant, allowing you to activate the bidet, adjust seat temperature, or flush using voice commands. The Toto NX2 currently lacks native voice control but may receive updates.
- **Automation Routines:** With smart home hubs, you can create routines like "Morning" that preheats the seat and activates the bidet at a specific time.
- **Energy Management:** Both premium models offer energy-saving modes that reduce power consumption when the toilet is not in use. The Kohler app provides detailed energy usage reports.
