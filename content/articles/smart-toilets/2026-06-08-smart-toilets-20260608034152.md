# The Ultimate Smart Toilet Review: Elevate Your Bathroom Experience in 2026

## Introduction

The smart toilet has evolved from a luxury novelty into a cornerstone of the modern smart home. In 2026, these intelligent fixtures are no longer just about heated seats and bidet functions—they integrate seamlessly with home automation systems, offer health monitoring capabilities, and deliver unprecedented water efficiency. Whether you’re a tech enthusiast looking to connect your bathroom to your smart hub or a homeowner seeking enhanced comfort and hygiene, the right smart toilet can transform your daily routine. This in-depth review explores the top three smart toilets on the market, breaks down essential features, and provides a comprehensive buying guide tailored to US consumers. From installation considerations to integration with Amazon Alexa, Google Home, and Apple HomeKit, we leave no flush unturned. Let’s dive into the world of intelligent sanitation and discover which model deserves a place in your home.

## Top 3 Product Recommendations

### 1. Toto Neorest NX2 – The Gold Standard

**Overview**
Toto’s Neorest NX2 remains the benchmark for luxury smart toilets in 2026. Building on decades of Japanese engineering, this model combines the company’s signature Tornado Flush technology with a sleek, skirted design that’s both powerful and whisper-quiet. The NX2 is fully integrated, meaning no separate bidet seat—everything is built into the ceramic bowl for a seamless appearance.

**Key Performance**
The Tornado Flush uses dual nozzles to create a centrifugal rinsing action that cleans the bowl without streaks or clogs. In testing, it handled waste and toilet paper with zero issues, even during high-volume use. The heated seat is adjustable from 80°F to 104°F, and the bidet wand offers oscillating, pulsating, and wide-spray modes. The automatic open/close lid responds to motion sensors with a 3-foot range, and the built-in air purifier neutralizes odors within seconds.

**Smart Home Integration**
The NX2 connects via Wi-Fi to the Toto Washlet+ app (iOS/Android), allowing you to adjust water temperature, seat temperature, and nozzle position. It also integrates with Amazon Alexa and Google Home for voice commands like “Alexa, flush the toilet.” However, it lacks native Apple HomeKit support, though a third-party bridge can enable it.

**Pros & Cons**
- Pros: Exceptional flush power, premium build quality, extensive app controls, quiet operation.
- Cons: High price ($4,500–$5,200), requires a dedicated electrical outlet (GFCI), bulky footprint may not fit small bathrooms.

**Verdict**
The Toto Neorest NX2 is ideal for homeowners who demand the best and have a budget to match. It’s a long-term investment that delivers reliability and luxury.

### 2. Kohler Veil Intelligent Toilet – Best for Smart Home Integration

**Overview**
Kohler’s Veil Intelligent Toilet is a close rival to the Neorest, with a focus on seamless smart home connectivity. Priced slightly lower at $3,800–$4,200, the Veil offers many of the same features but with a more modern, minimalist aesthetic. It’s available in both one-piece and two-piece configurations, though the one-piece is recommended for easier cleaning.

**Key Performance**
The Veil uses Kohler’s AquaPiston flush technology, which delivers 1.28 gallons per flush (gpf) while maintaining a powerful 1,000-gram waste removal rating. The bidet features adjustable water pressure and temperature, plus a heated seat with six comfort settings. The automatic lid opens when you approach and closes after you leave, and the built-in deodorizer uses a carbon filter to eliminate odors.

**Smart Home Integration**
This is where the Veil shines. It works natively with Kohler’s Konnect app, plus Amazon Alexa, Google Assistant, and Apple HomeKit. You can create routines like “Good Morning” to adjust seat temperature and activate a gentle warm-water rinse. Voice control is responsive, and the app offers granular control over every parameter.

**Pros & Cons**
- Pros: Excellent smart home compatibility, elegant design, reliable flush, good value for features.
- Cons: Bidet spray pattern could be wider, seat sensor sometimes triggers accidentally, installation requires precise plumbing alignment.

**Verdict**
The Kohler Veil is the best choice for tech-savvy users who want a toilet that plays well with their existing smart ecosystem. It’s a strong contender for the smart home enthusiast.

### 3. Bio Bidet Ultimate BB-2000 – Best Value for Features

**Overview**
For budget-conscious buyers who still want premium features, the Bio Bidet Ultimate BB-2000 is a standout. Priced at $1,200–$1,500, it’s a bidet seat that attaches to most standard toilets, rather than a fully integrated unit. However, its feature set rivals many high-end models, making it an exceptional value.

**Key Performance**
The BB-2000 offers a heated seat, adjustable water temperature, and a dual-nozzle system (one for rear, one for front). The flush is manual (it uses your existing toilet), but the bidet functions are fully electronic. The water pressure is strong but gentle, and the seat is comfortable for long sits. The deodorizer uses a carbon filter that lasts six months.

**Smart Home Integration**
While not fully integrated, the BB-2000 includes a wireless remote with LED display. It does not have Wi-Fi or voice control natively, but you can pair it with a smart plug for basic automation (e.g., turning on the seat heater via Alexa). This is a compromise for the price.

**Pros & Cons**
- Pros: Affordable, easy to install, excellent bidet performance, slim profile.
- Cons: No smart home features out of the box, manual flush, less durable than integrated units, may not fit all toilet shapes.

**Verdict**
The Bio Bidet Ultimate BB-2000 is perfect for renters or homeowners on a budget who want a high-quality bidet experience without breaking the bank. It’s a smart upgrade for any existing toilet.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

- **Tech Enthusiasts:** If you already have a smart home ecosystem (Alexa, Google, HomeKit), a fully integrated smart toilet like the Kohler Veil is a natural addition. You can create routines that sync with your morning alarm or bedtime schedule.
- **Luxury Seekers:** For those who value premium materials and flawless performance, the Toto Neorest NX2 is unmatched. It’s a statement piece that elevates any master bathroom.
- **Budget-Conscious Homeowners:** The Bio Bidet BB-2000 offers 80% of the features at 30% of the cost. It’s ideal for upgrading an existing toilet without a full renovation.
- **Renters:** Bidet seats are portable and easy to remove, making them perfect for apartments. The BB-2000 is a top pick here.

### Budget Considerations

- **Entry-Level ($500–$1,000):** Basic bidet seats with heated seats and manual controls. Brands like Brondell and Alpha Bidet offer good options, but smart features are limited.
- **Mid-Range ($1,000–$2,500):** Integrated toilets from brands like American Standard or Duravit, or high-end bidet seats like the Bio Bidet BB-2000. You get automatic lids and deodorizers but limited smart home integration.
- **Premium ($3,000–$5,500):** Fully integrated models from Toto, Kohler, and Geberit. These offer the best performance, design, and connectivity.

**Hidden Costs:** Factor in electrical work (GFCI outlet installation costs $150–$300), plumbing modifications (if needed), and professional installation ($200–$500). Integrated toilets often require a 10-inch rough-in distance, so measure your existing setup.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Steps

1. **Measure Your Space:** Ensure the rough-in distance (wall to center of drain) is 10–12 inches. Most smart toilets require 10 inches.
2. **Electrical Requirements:** All smart toilets need a GFCI-protected outlet within 3 feet of the toilet. If you don’t have one, hire a licensed electrician.
3. **Water Supply:** A standard 1/2-inch water line with a shut-off valve is needed. Some models require a dedicated supply line.
4. **Remove Old Toilet:** For integrated units, you’ll need to remove the existing toilet and install the new one using a wax ring or rubber gasket.
5. **Connect Smart Features:** Download the app, connect to Wi-Fi, and pair with your smart home hub. Follow the manufacturer’s instructions for voice commands.

**Pro Tip:** For bidet seats like the BB-2000, installation is a DIY project—just attach the seat to the toilet bowl and connect the T-adapter to the water supply. It takes about an hour.

### Smart Home Integration

- **Amazon Alexa:** Use voice commands like “Alexa, turn on the bidet” or “Alexa, set seat temperature to 95 degrees.” All three top models support Alexa, though the BB-2000 requires a smart plug.
- **Google Home:** Similar to Alexa, with routines like “Goodnight” to close the lid and turn off the seat heater.
- **Apple HomeKit:** Only the Kohler Veil offers native HomeKit support. For other models, use a bridge like Homebridge to add compatibility.
- **Automation Ideas:** Set the seat to warm up 15 minutes before your wake-up time, or have the deodorizer activate when the bathroom light turns on.
