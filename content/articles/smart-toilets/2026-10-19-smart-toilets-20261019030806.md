# The Best Smart Toilets of 2026: A Complete Buyer's Guide and Review

## Introduction

Smart toilets have quietly evolved from luxury showroom curiosities into genuinely practical bathroom upgrades. In 2026, these fixtures combine bidet washing, heated seating, automatic flushing, deodorization, and app-based control into a single integrated unit—and prices have finally started to make sense for mainstream US households. Whether you're remodeling a primary bathroom, retrofitting an older home, or simply tired of toilet paper, the right smart toilet can deliver comfort, hygiene, and water savings that pay back over time.

But the category is also confusing. Brands mix and match features, plumbing requirements vary, and "smart" can mean anything from a motion sensor to full voice control. In this review, I'll walk you through the three best smart toilets for most US buyers in 2026, break down the features that actually matter, and explain installation and smart home integration so you can buy with confidence. Every recommendation below reflects current pricing estimates and real-world usability rather than spec-sheet hype.

## Top 3 Product Recommendations

### 1. TOTO Neorest AS: Best Overall Smart Toilet

**Estimated price (2026): $4,200–$4,800**

TOTO remains the benchmark in the smart toilet category, and the Neorest AS is the most balanced model in its lineup. It pairs the company's proven WASHLET bidet technology with a sleek, skirted one-piece design that hides plumbing and makes cleaning dramatically easier.

The headline feature is TOTO's ActiLight and Premist systems: the bowl is misted with water before use to reduce waste adhesion, and a UV-light sanitization cycle runs after each flush. The heated seat offers five temperature settings, and the remote control is intuitive enough that guests can figure it out without a manual. The tornado flush is both quiet and effective, and the unit is WaterSense certified, using roughly 1.0 gallons per flush.

What sets the Neorest AS apart is reliability. TOTO's parts network in the US is the strongest in the category, and service technicians are widely available in metro areas. Downsides: it requires a dedicated GFCI outlet near the toilet, and the price is steep. But if you're investing in a long-term fixture, this is the safest bet in 2026.

**Pros:** Excellent build quality, strong service network, UV sanitization, quiet flush
**Cons:** High price, requires nearby power outlet

### 2. Kohler Veil Intelligent Toilet: Best Design and Smart Features

**Estimated price (2026): $3,600–$4,200**

Kohler's Veil is the smart toilet for people who care as much about aesthetics as function. The minimalist, wall-hugging silhouette looks like modern sculpture, and the touchless flush sensor works reliably without false triggers. The bidet wand offers adjustable position, pressure, and temperature, plus a warm-air dryer that's genuinely usable rather than an afterthought.

The Veil's standout is its integration with the Kohler Konnect app, which supports voice control through Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant. You can set user profiles, adjust seat temperature, and monitor water usage. The self-cleaning UV system and stainless steel wand are welcome hygiene touches.

The tradeoff is installation complexity. The Veil is designed for a wall-hung or close-coupled setup and often benefits from professional installation. Replacement parts are pricier than TOTO's, and the app occasionally requires re-pairing. Still, for design-forward bathrooms and smart-home enthusiasts, it's a compelling choice.

**Pros:** Striking design, strong app and voice integration, effective dryer
**Cons:** Complex installation, higher part costs

### 3. Bio Bidet Aura Elite: Best Value Smart Toilet

**Estimated price (2026): $1,800–$2,300**

If the premium models feel out of reach, the Bio Bidet Aura Elite delivers the core smart toilet experience at roughly half the price. You get a heated seat, adjustable bidet wash, warm-air dryer, deodorizer, and a night light—essentially the features most people actually use daily.

The Aura Elite uses a standard 12-inch rough-in and a conventional floor-mounted design, which makes it far easier to retrofit into existing bathrooms than wall-hung premium units. It still requires a power outlet, but many buyers can handle installation with a licensed plumber in a single visit.

Build quality is a step below TOTO and Kohler—the plastic components feel less premium, and the flush is louder—but the value proposition is hard to argue with. For first-time smart toilet buyers or guest bathrooms, it's the smart money pick in 2026.

**Pros:** Affordable, easy retrofit, all essential features

## Key Features to Consider

Before you buy, understand what separates a good smart toilet from an overpriced gadget.

**Bidet washing:** Look for adjustable water pressure, temperature, and nozzle position. Dual-nozzle designs (front and rear wash) are standard on quality units.

**Heated seat:** Nearly universal in this category, but check for adjustable temperature settings and energy-saving modes.

**Dryer:** Warm-air dryers vary widely in effectiveness. Higher-end models (TOTO, Kohler) dry faster; budget models often require a towel assist.

**Self-cleaning and sanitization:** UV sterilization and electrolyzed water systems reduce bacteria. Premist-style bowl coating helps prevent waste adhesion.

**Flush technology:** Tornado and vortex flushes are quieter and more effective than standard gravity flushes. Check WaterSense certification for water efficiency.

**Smart controls:** Remote controls are standard; app and voice control are premium features. Consider whether you'll actually use them.

**Power and plumbing requirements:** Every smart toilet needs a GFCI outlet within cord reach (typically 3–4 feet). Check rough-in dimensions (10, 12, or 14 inches) before ordering.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

Smart toilets make the most sense for:

- **Remodelers:** If you're already opening walls or running electrical, adding a GFCI outlet near the toilet is cheap and unlocks the full category.
- **Comfort seekers:** Anyone dealing with mobility issues, cold seats in winter, or irritation from toilet paper will notice immediate benefits.
- **Efficiency-focused households:** WaterSense-certified models use less water than many older toilets.
- **Smart home enthusiasts:** If you already run Alexa or Google routines, app-integrated models fit naturally.

### Who Should Skip It?

Renters, households on tight budgets, and anyone unwilling to add an electrical outlet should consider a bidet seat attachment instead—it delivers 80% of the experience for a fraction of the cost.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $1,000:** Bidet seat attachments on an existing toilet. Not a full smart toilet, but a smart compromise.
- **$1,800–$2,500:** Entry-level integrated smart toilets (Bio Bidet Aura Elite). Best value for most buyers.
- **$3,500–$5,000:** Premium models (Kohler Veil, TOTO Neorest AS) with superior build quality, sanitization, and service support.

Factor in installation ($200–$600 for a plumber, more if electrical work is needed) and consider extended warranties on premium units. Also budget for replacement filters and descaling solution—roughly $30–$60 per year.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Realities

Every smart toilet needs three things: a standard water supply, a drain with the correct rough-in, and a GFCI-protected electrical outlet within cord reach. The electrical requirement is the biggest hurdle for retrofits. If your bathroom lacks an outlet near the toilet, hire a licensed electrician—this typically runs $150–$400 depending on wall access.

Floor-mounted models like the Bio Bidet Aura Elite are the easiest to install and often replace an existing toilet with minimal modification. Wall-hung models like the Kohler Veil require in-wall carriers and are best handled by professionals during a remodel.

### Smart Home Integration

In 2026, integration is improving but still uneven. Kohler's Konnect platform offers the most mature experience, with Alexa and Google Assistant support for seat temperature, flushing, and usage monitoring. TOTO's app support is more limited but reliable. Budget models generally skip app control entirely—which, honestly, most users won't miss.

If you're building a smart bathroom ecosystem, prioritize models with app support and check for Matter compatibility where available. And remember: a smart toilet is a long-term fixture. Choose based on build quality and service network first, smart features second.
