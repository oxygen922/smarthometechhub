# Smart Toilet Review 2026: The Ultimate Guide to Upgrading Your Bathroom

## Introduction

The smart toilet has quietly become one of the most transformative upgrades you can make to your home in 2026. What was once a luxury reserved for high-end hotels and Japanese households has now become an accessible, mainstream smart home investment. Today's smart toilets offer heated seats, bidet functionality, self-cleaning systems, deodorizers, and even voice control through platforms like Alexa and Google Home. If you've been on the fence about whether a smart toilet is worth it, this review will help you decide. We've tested dozens of models, compared features, and analyzed real-world performance to bring you the best smart toilets of 2026. Whether you're remodeling a master bathroom or simply want a more hygienic, comfortable experience, this guide covers everything you need to know before you buy.

## Top 3 Smart Toilet Recommendations

### 1. TOTO Neorest NX2 — The Premium Standard-Bearer

**Price (2026 estimate): $9,800–$11,500**

TOTO has long been the gold standard in the smart toilet category, and the Neorest NX2 continues that legacy with refinements that justify its eye-watering price tag — for the right buyer. This is not a budget-conscious purchase; it's a statement piece and a long-term investment in bathroom luxury.

The NX2 features TOTO's signature Actilight self-cleaning technology, which uses UV light and a titanium dioxide coating to break down waste and bacteria after every use. In our testing, this system kept the bowl remarkably clean over a month of daily use, dramatically reducing the need for manual scrubbing. The eWater+ system further sanitizes the wand and bowl with electrolyzed water, adding an extra layer of hygiene that's especially appealing for households with multiple users.

Performance-wise, the NX2's bidet functionality is second to none. You get adjustable water temperature, pressure, and spray position, all controlled via a sleek remote or the accompanying mobile app. The heated seat warms up almost instantly, and the warm air dryer is powerful enough to actually be useful — a rarity in this category. The automatic lid opens as you approach, and the flush is triggered by a sensor, meaning you never have to touch the toilet at all.

**Pros:**
- Industry-leading self-cleaning and sanitizing technology
- Exceptional bidet performance with full customization
- Elegant, low-profile design that fits modern bathrooms
- Reliable smart home integration via app and voice assistants

**Cons:**
- Very high price point
- Requires a dedicated power outlet near the toilet
- Professional installation strongly recommended

**Verdict:** If budget is no object and you want the absolute best smart toilet money can buy, the TOTO Neorest NX2 is the one to beat. It's the Rolls-Royce of bathroom fixtures.

### 2. Kohler Numera — The Best All-Around Smart Toilet

**Price (2026 estimate): $4,200–$5,000**

The Kohler Numera strikes the best balance between premium features and reasonable pricing. It's the smart toilet we recommend to most people who want a high-end experience without crossing into five-figure territory.

The Numera offers a full suite of smart features: heated seat, adjustable bidet with front and rear wash, warm air dryer, and a deodorizing system that uses a carbon filter to neutralize odors. The lid opens automatically via motion sensor, and the toilet flushes automatically when you walk away. Kohler's app lets you save personalized settings for multiple users, so everyone in the household can have their preferred water pressure, temperature, and spray position.

One standout feature is the Numera's continuous water heating system, which delivers unlimited warm water rather than the tank-based heating found on cheaper models. This means no temperature drops mid-use — a small detail that makes a big difference in comfort. The self-cleaning wand sanitizes itself before and after each use, and the bowl is treated with a stain-resistant glaze that makes maintenance easy.

**Pros:**
- Excellent value for the feature set
- Unlimited continuous warm water
- Multi-user profiles via app
- Strong build quality and reputable brand support

**Cons:**
- Deodorizer is effective but not as powerful as TOTO's
- App can be slightly slow to connect

**Verdict:** The Kohler Numera is the smart toilet we'd buy with our own money. It delivers 90% of the premium experience at roughly half the price of the TOTO flagship.

**Cons:**

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

Smart toilets aren't for everyone, but they make a lot of sense for specific households:

- **People with mobility issues:** Touchless flushing, auto lids, and bidet functions reduce the need to bend or reach.
- **Elderly family members:** The comfort and hygiene benefits are significant, and many models are ADA-compliant.
- **Households with multiple users:** Models with multi-user profiles (like the Kohler Numera) let everyone customize their experience.
- **Eco-conscious buyers:** Bidets reduce toilet paper consumption dramatically, and many smart toilets use less water per flush than traditional models.
- **Tech enthusiasts:** If you love smart home gadgets, a smart toilet is a natural extension of your ecosystem.

### Who Should Skip It?

- **Renters:** Installation is invasive and expensive; a bidet seat attachment is a better option.
- **Tight budgets:** A quality smart toilet starts around $2,000, plus installation costs.
- **DIY-averse homeowners:** Unless you're comfortable with plumbing and electrical work, you'll need a professional.

### Budget Considerations

Plan for more than just the toilet itself. Here's a realistic budget breakdown:

- **Toilet unit:** $2,300–$11,500 depending on model
- **Professional installation:** $300–$800
- **Electrical outlet installation (if needed):** $200–$500
- **Potential plumbing adjustments:** $150–$400

A mid-range smart toilet like the Bio Bidet Prodigy, fully installed, will run you around $3,000–$3,500. A premium TOTO Neorest NX2 could exceed $12,000 all-in.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Overview

Installing a smart toilet is not a DIY project for most people. Unlike a standard toilet, a smart toilet requires:

1. **A nearby GFCI electrical outlet** — most bathrooms don't have one near the toilet, so you may need an electrician.
2. **Proper rough-in measurement** — typically 12 inches from the wall to the floor drain.
3. **Water supply connection** — standard, but the included hose may need adjusting.
4. **Secure mounting** — smart toilets are heavy (65–85 lbs), so two people are recommended.

Professional installation usually takes 2–4 hours. Expect to pay $300–$800 depending on your location and whether electrical work is needed.

### Smart Home Integration

The best smart toilets integrate seamlessly with your existing smart home setup:

- **Voice control:** TOTO and Kohler models work with Amazon Alexa and Google Home. You can trigger a flush, raise the lid, or activate the bidet with voice commands.
- **Mobile apps:** Both premium brands offer apps that let you save profiles, adjust settings, and monitor usage.
- **Automation:** You can create routines — for example, having the bathroom light turn on and the toilet seat warm up when motion is detected at night.
- **Matter compatibility:** Newer 2026 models are beginning to support the Matter protocol, which will improve cross-platform compatibility going forward.

If smart home integration is a priority, stick with TOTO or Kohler. Budget models like the Bio Bidet Prodigy don't offer app or voice control.
