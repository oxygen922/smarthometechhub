# The Ultimate Smart Toilet Guide: Top Picks for 2026

## Introduction

The smart toilet has evolved from a luxury novelty into a genuine home upgrade that combines hygiene, comfort, and technology. In 2026, the market offers everything from basic bidet seats to fully automated units with voice control, heated seats, and self-cleaning functions. These intelligent fixtures promise to reduce toilet paper usage, improve personal hygiene, and even monitor health metrics. But with prices ranging from $500 to over $8,000, choosing the right model requires careful consideration.

This comprehensive review examines the current smart toilet landscape, focusing on three standout models that represent the best value, luxury, and innovation available today. Whether you're a tech enthusiast seeking the latest smart home integration or someone looking for practical comfort upgrades, this guide will help you make an informed decision. We'll explore key features, installation requirements, and budget considerations specific to the US market, ensuring you find the perfect match for your bathroom and lifestyle.

## Top 3 Product Recommendations

### 1. Toto Neorest NX2 – The Luxury Benchmark

**Price:** $6,800 – $7,500 (2026 estimate)

The Toto Neorest NX2 remains the gold standard in smart toilets, and for good reason. This fully integrated unit combines Toto's legendary Washlet technology with a sleek, one-piece design that eliminates visible plumbing. The NX2 features the company's signature Tornado Flush, which uses dual nozzles to create a centrifugal cleaning action that thoroughly cleans the bowl with minimal water usage—just 1.0 gallon per flush (GPF).

What sets the NX2 apart is its comprehensive feature set. The heated seat offers multiple temperature settings, while the warm water bidet provides oscillating, pulsating, and front/rear cleaning modes. The air dryer is powerful and adjustable, and the deodorizing system uses activated carbon to neutralize odors before they escape. The unit also includes a nightlight, automatic open/close lid, and a remote control with intuitive buttons.

**Smart Home Integration:** The NX2 works with Toto's proprietary app, allowing you to adjust settings, monitor water usage, and receive maintenance alerts. While it doesn't natively support Alexa or Google Home, third-party smart plugs can enable basic voice control for power functions.

**Pros:** Exceptional build quality, powerful flush, comprehensive features, quiet operation
**Cons:** Very expensive, requires professional installation, limited voice assistant integration

**Best for:** Luxury homeowners who want the absolute best and are willing to invest in premium bathroom technology.

### 2. Kohler PureWash E930 – Best Value Premium

**Price:** $1,200 – $1,500 (2026 estimate)

Kohler's PureWash E930 strikes an excellent balance between features and affordability. This bidet seat attachment fits most standard two-piece toilets, making it an accessible upgrade for existing bathrooms. The E930 offers heated seat, warm water bidet with adjustable temperature and pressure, and a warm air dryer with three speed settings.

The unit's standout feature is its UV nozzle sterilization, which automatically cleans the bidet wand after each use, ensuring hygiene between cleanings. The E930 also includes a nightlight, heated seat with occupancy sensor, and a remote control with a sleek, wall-mountable design. Water usage is minimal at 0.5 GPF for the bidet function, though the toilet's main flush remains unchanged.

**Smart Home Integration:** The PureWash E930 connects to Kohler's Konnect app, which allows you to customize settings, create user profiles, and receive usage reports. The app also supports Alexa and Google Assistant for basic commands like turning on the heated seat or activating the bidet. However, voice control is limited to on/off functions rather than precise temperature adjustments.

**Pros:** Excellent value, easy installation, UV sterilization, good app integration
**Cons:** Not fully integrated (requires existing toilet), less powerful dryer than premium models, limited voice control

**Best for:** Homeowners wanting smart toilet features without replacing their entire toilet, or those on a mid-range budget.

### 3. Bio Bidet Ultimate BB-2000 – Feature-Packed Alternative

**Price:** $900 – $1,100 (2026 estimate)

Bio Bidet's BB-2000 offers an impressive array of features at a competitive price point. This bidet seat attachment includes a heated seat, warm water bidet with five pressure settings, and a warm air dryer with adjustable temperature. The unit also features a stainless steel dual nozzle system that provides both posterior and feminine wash options.

The BB-2000's standout feature is its proprietary "Instantaneous Water Heating" technology, which eliminates the need for a storage tank and ensures unlimited warm water. The unit also includes a deodorizing carbon filter, a nightlight, and a "Sitz Bath" mode for soothing relief. The remote control is wireless and includes a wall-mounting bracket.

**Smart Home Integration:** The BB-2000 does not offer native smart home connectivity, which is its primary drawback. However, it can be used with a smart plug for basic power control. The lack of app or voice integration may be a dealbreaker for some, but the unit's mechanical reliability and feature set compensate for this limitation.

**Pros:** Excellent water heating, durable construction, comprehensive cleaning modes, affordable
**Cons:** No smart home integration, less refined design than Toto or Kohler, limited warranty compared to competitors

**Best for:** Budget-conscious buyers who prioritize bidet features over smart home connectivity, or those in rental properties where permanent modifications aren't possible.

### Additional Considerations

- **Installation Type:** Fully integrated toilets require professional plumbing and electrical work, while bidet seats can often be DIY installed in under an hour.
- **Power Requirements:** Most smart toilets need a GFCI-protected electrical outlet near the toilet. Some models offer battery backup for bidet functions during power outages.
- **Water Connection:** Bidet seats typically use a T-valve to connect to the existing toilet water supply, while integrated toilets require dedicated water lines.
- **User Profiles:** Premium models allow multiple users to save personalized settings for seat temperature, water pressure, and cleaning modes.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

**Ideal Candidates:**
- **Tech enthusiasts** who enjoy integrating smart home devices and want voice-controlled bathroom features
- **Individuals with mobility issues** who benefit from automated lid opening, heated seats, and hands-free cleaning
- **Families** looking to reduce toilet paper usage and improve bathroom hygiene
- **Luxury homeowners** remodeling bathrooms and seeking premium fixtures that add value

**Who Might Skip:**
- **Renters** who cannot modify bathroom plumbing or electrical systems
- **Budget-conscious buyers** who prefer standard toilets and separate bidet attachments
- **Those in older homes** without accessible electrical outlets near the toilet (though retrofit options exist)

### Budget Considerations

- **Entry Level ($500-$1,000):** Basic bidet seats with heated seats and warm water. These offer core functionality without smart features. Brands like Brondell and Bio Bidet dominate this segment.
- **Mid-Range ($1,000-$2,500):** Bidet seats with advanced features like UV sterilization, deodorizers, and app connectivity. Kohler's PureWash series and Toto's Washlet+ line fit here.
- **Premium ($2,500-$5,000):** Fully integrated toilets with automatic lids, multiple cleaning modes, and basic smart home integration. Brands include American Standard and Duravit.
- **Luxury ($5,000+):** Top-tier integrated toilets with comprehensive features, premium materials, and advanced smart home capabilities. Toto Neorest and Kohler's Numi series lead this category.

### Additional Costs

- **Professional Installation:** $200-$500 for electrical and plumbing work
- **Electrical Outlet Installation:** $150-$300 if none exists near the toilet
- **Extended Warranty:** $50-$200 per year depending on model

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Considerations

**Electrical Requirements:**
All smart toilets require a nearby electrical outlet. For bidet seats, a standard 120V outlet within 3-4 feet of the toilet is sufficient. Integrated toilets may require dedicated 15-amp circuits. If no outlet exists, hire a licensed electrician to install a GFCI-protected outlet. This typically costs $150-$300 and takes 1-3 hours.

**Plumbing Requirements:**
Bidet seats connect to the existing toilet water supply via a T-valve. Most models include the necessary hardware, and installation takes 30-60 minutes for DIYers. Integrated toilets require connection to the home's water supply and drainage system, which often necessitates removing the existing toilet and modifying the floor flange.

**DIY vs. Professional:**
Bidet seat installation is generally DIY-friendly, especially with Kohler and Bio Bidet models that include clear instructions. Integrated toilets strongly recommend professional installation due to the complexity of plumbing, electrical, and structural requirements. Improper installation can lead to leaks, electrical hazards, or voided warranties.

### Smart Home Integration

**App Connectivity:**
- **Toto:** Proprietary app for settings, usage monitoring, and alerts. No third-party integration.
- **Kohler:** Konnect app with Alexa and Google Assistant support. Allows voice commands for seat heating, bidet activation, and nightlight control.
- **Bio Bidet:** No app or voice integration. Relies on remote control only.

**Automation Possibilities:**
- **Routines:** With Kohler's Konnect, you can create morning routines that preheat the seat and activate the bidet at scheduled times.
- **Sensors:** Some models detect occupancy and automatically adjust settings based on user presence.
- **Energy Saving:** Smart toilets can integrate with energy management systems to reduce power consumption during peak hours.

**Limitations:**
Voice control is currently limited to basic on/off functions for most models. Precise temperature, pressure, and cleaning mode adjustments still require the remote control or app. Privacy concerns also exist, as connected devices could potentially transmit usage data.
