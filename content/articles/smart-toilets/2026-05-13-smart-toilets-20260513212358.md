## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation

Installing a bidet seat is a DIY-friendly project that takes 30–60 minutes, but there are important considerations:

1. **Electrical Outlet:** All smart bidet seats require a standard 120V outlet near the toilet. If you don’t have one, you’ll need to hire an electrician. GFCI protection is recommended for bathroom safety.
2. **Water Supply:** Most seats use a T-adapter that connects to your existing toilet water supply line. Ensure you have a standard 1/2-inch connection.
3. **Toilet Compatibility:** Check the shape (elongated vs. round) and the mounting hole spacing. Most modern toilets are elongated, but older models may be round.
4. **Tools Required:** Adjustable wrench, screwdriver, and a towel for minor water spills.

**Step-by-Step (Simplified):**
1. Turn off the water supply and flush the toilet to empty the tank.
2. Disconnect the water supply line from the toilet tank.
3. Attach the included T-adapter to the toilet fill valve.
4. Connect the bidet seat’s water hose to the T-adapter.
5. Mount the seat bracket onto the toilet bowl (use the included hardware).
6. Slide the bidet seat onto the bracket until it clicks.
7. Plug the seat into the electrical outlet and turn the water back on.
8. Test all functions.

### Smart Home Integration

- **TOTO S700e:** Uses Wi-Fi (2.4GHz) and Bluetooth for setup. Works with Alexa (skills: “TOTO Washlet”), Google Home, and Apple HomeKit. You can create routines, but the integration is one-way (you can control the seat but the seat can’t trigger other devices).
- **BioBidet SL-500:** No Wi-Fi or Matter. Instead, it has a built-in microphone for basic voice commands. This is a simpler, more private approach—no cloud connection needed.
- **Kohler Novita BH-3000:** The gold standard for integration. With Matter support, it connects directly to your smart home hub (e.g., Apple HomePod, Amazon Echo, SmartThings). You can create complex routines like: “When I sit down, turn on the bathroom fan and dim the lights.” It also works offline via Bluetooth.

**Privacy Note:** All three brands claim data encryption and no storage of personal health data. However, if you’re concerned about cloud privacy, the BioBidet’s offline voice control may appeal to you.
