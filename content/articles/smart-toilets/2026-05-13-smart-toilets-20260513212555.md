## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Overview

All three models use a standard T-shaped adapter that connects to your toilet’s water supply. Installation typically takes 30–60 minutes and requires only a wrench and a screwdriver. Key steps:

1. **Shut off water** and flush to empty the tank.
2. **Remove the existing toilet seat** and clean the bowl.
3. **Install the mounting bracket** and attach the bidet seat.
4. **Connect the water supply** using the included T-adapter and hose.
5. **Plug in the power cord** to a GFCI outlet (required by code in most US states).

**Important:** Ensure your bathroom has a GFCI outlet within 3 feet of the toilet. If not, hire an electrician—this is not a DIY electrical job.

### Smart Home Integration Tips

- **Voice assistants:** For the best experience, set up routines like “Good Night” to turn off the seat heater and activate the deodorizer. The Toto and Kohler models respond to “Alexa, turn on the bidet” directly.
- **IFTTT:** With Toto or Bio Bidet, you can create triggers like “If my smart scale detects I’ve weighed in, turn on the bidet seat warmer.” This is a fun, practical automation for daily use.
- **HomeKit:** Only Kohler supports native HomeKit via its Konnect bridge. If you’re an Apple user, this is a significant advantage.
- **Scheduling:** Use the app to schedule seat warming for 6:00 AM on weekdays and 8:00 AM on weekends—energy savings without sacrificing comfort.
