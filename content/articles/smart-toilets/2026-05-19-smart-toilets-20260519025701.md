## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Bidet Seat?

- **Hygiene Enthusiasts:** Anyone who wants a more thorough clean than toilet paper can provide. Bidets reduce irritation and are more eco-friendly.
- **People with Mobility Issues:** Heated seats and hands-free operation are a boon for seniors or those with arthritis.
- **Parents with Young Children:** Many bidet seats have a “child mode” with gentler spray pressure.
- **Smart Home Hobbyists:** Models like the Kohler E930 integrate with your existing ecosystem for voice control and automation.
- **Eco-Conscious Consumers:** Bidets drastically reduce toilet paper usage, saving trees and lowering your carbon footprint.

### Budget Considerations

- **Entry-Level ($200–$400):** Basic models with cold water only or limited features. Good for renters or tight budgets, but lack heated seats and warm water.
- **Mid-Range ($500–$800):** The sweet spot. Models like the Bio Bidet BB-2000 offer warm water, heated seats, and user presets without the luxury price.
- **Premium ($900+):** Top-tier options like the Toto Washlet S7A and Kohler E930. You pay for brand reputation, advanced sanitization, and smart home features.

**Pro Tip:** Factor in installation costs (DIY vs. professional) and potential electrical work. Most bidet seats require a GFCI outlet within 3 feet of the toilet.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Overview

Installing a smart bidet seat is a weekend project for most DIYers, but there are key steps:

1. **Turn off water supply** and flush toilet to empty the tank.
2. **Remove existing seat** and clean the toilet bowl rim.
3. **Install the mounting bracket** (usually included) according to the bidet’s template.
4. **Connect the water line** using the included T-valve adapter. Ensure no leaks.
5. **Plug in the bidet** to a GFCI outlet. If you don’t have one nearby, hire an electrician.
6. **Attach the bidet seat** and test all functions.

**Time required:** 30–60 minutes for a first-time install.

### Smart Home Integration

- **Wi-Fi vs. Bluetooth:** Most smart bidets use Wi-Fi for cloud connectivity, allowing remote control and firmware updates. Bluetooth is limited to short-range control.
- **Voice Assistants:** The Kohler E930 works with Alexa and Google Assistant. Commands like “Alexa, start the bidet” are possible, but be mindful of privacy in the bathroom.
- **Automation:** Use IFTTT or SmartThings to create routines. For example, “When I leave the house, turn off the bidet seat heater.”
- **App Features:** The Kohler Konnect app allows scheduling (e.g., pre-heat the seat at 7 AM) and usage tracking.

**Compatibility Check:** Before buying, ensure your toilet has a standard two-bolt seat mounting pattern (most US toilets do). Elongated vs. round bowls require specific models.
