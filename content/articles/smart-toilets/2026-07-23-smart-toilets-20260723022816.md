# The Ultimate Smart Toilet Review: Top 3 Models for a Smarter, Cleaner Bathroom in 2026

## Introduction

The smart toilet has evolved from a luxury novelty into a genuine home wellness upgrade. In 2026, these high-tech thrones offer heated seats, bidet washes, self-cleaning functions, and even health monitoring—all controlled via app or voice assistant. Whether you're remodeling a master bath or simply tired of cold toilet seats, a smart toilet can elevate your daily routine with hygiene, comfort, and convenience. But with dozens of models on the market, choosing the right one can feel overwhelming. This in-depth review cuts through the noise, highlighting three top-tier smart toilets for 2026, breaking down key features, installation considerations, and budget tips. By the end, you’ll know exactly which model fits your home and lifestyle. Let’s dive into the future of bathroom technology.

## Top 3 Product Recommendations with Detailed Reviews

### 1. TOTO Neorest NX2 – The Gold Standard for Luxury

The TOTO Neorest NX2 remains the benchmark for premium smart toilets in 2026. It combines TOTO’s legendary Tornado Flush technology with a sleek, one-piece design that integrates a heated seat, warm water bidet, and a powerful air dryer. The bidet offers adjustable water pressure, temperature, and spray position, including a front wash for women and a posterior wash. The auto-open lid and hands-free flush make every visit effortless. The NX2 also features a premist function that coats the bowl before use to prevent waste adhesion, plus a self-cleaning nozzle that sanitizes after each use. The included wireless remote is intuitive, but you can also control the toilet via the TOTO mobile app or voice commands through Alexa and Google Assistant.

**Pros:**  
- Exceptional build quality and durability  
- Powerful, quiet flush with Tornado technology  
- Comprehensive bidet and drying functions  
- Easy smart home integration  

**Cons:**  
- Very expensive (around $4,500–$5,200)  
- Requires professional installation and a nearby electrical outlet  
- No health monitoring features  

**Who it’s for:** Homeowners seeking a no-compromise luxury experience with proven reliability.

### 2. Kohler PureWash E930 – Best Mid-Range Bidet Seat

Not ready for a full toilet replacement? The Kohler PureWash E930 bidet seat transforms your existing toilet into a smart toilet for a fraction of the cost. It features a heated seat, warm water bidet with adjustable spray patterns, a warm air dryer, and a deodorizer. The intuitive remote control includes preset user profiles, so each family member can save their preferred settings. The PureWash E930 also offers a UV sanitization cycle for the nozzle and a quick-release mechanism for easy cleaning. It integrates with Kohler’s Konnect app and works with voice assistants, allowing you to preheat the seat or start the bidet from your phone.

**Pros:**  
- Affordable upgrade for existing toilets  
- Multiple spray modes and user profiles  
- UV self-cleaning and deodorizer  
- Easy DIY installation with included hardware  

**Cons:**  
- Requires a nearby electrical outlet (GFCI recommended)  
- Not as sleek as a one-piece smart toilet  
- Drying function could be stronger  

**Who it’s for:** Renters or homeowners who want smart toilet features without replacing their entire fixture.

### 3. Bio Bidet Bliss BB-2000 – Best Value with Health Monitoring

The Bio Bidet Bliss BB-2000 stands out in 2026 by offering health monitoring alongside standard smart toilet features. This bidet seat includes a heated seat, adjustable warm water wash, air dryer, and a carbon-filtered deodorizer. Its unique selling point is the built-in urinalysis system: a small test strip in the bowl analyzes urine for glucose, protein, and pH levels, sending results to the companion app. While not a substitute for medical advice, it provides useful trend data for health-conscious users. The BB-2000 also features an LED nightlight, a wireless remote with a backlit display, and voice control via Alexa.

**Pros:**  
- Health monitoring adds unique value  
- Excellent wash and dry performance  
- Easy installation with standard T-connector  
- Competitive price around $800–$1,000  

**Cons:**  
- Health data accuracy varies; not FDA-approved  
- App interface could be more intuitive  
- Seat is slightly bulkier than premium models  

**Who it’s for:** Tech-savvy users interested in tracking wellness metrics without a luxury budget.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

- **Luxury Seekers:** If you want the ultimate bathroom experience and have a budget over $4,000, the TOTO Neorest NX2 is unmatched.
- **Mid-Range Upgraders:** The Kohler PureWash E930 is perfect for homeowners who want comfort and convenience without replacing their toilet. It’s also ideal for renters.
- **Health-Conscious Users:** The Bio Bidet Bliss BB-2000 offers unique wellness tracking at a reasonable price, making it a standout for data-driven individuals.
- **Families:** Models with user profiles (like the Kohler and Bio Bidet) allow each family member to save their preferred settings.

### Budget Considerations

- **Entry Level ($300–$600):** Basic bidet seats with heated seats and warm water, but fewer smart features. Brands like Brondell and SmartBidet offer solid options.
- **Mid-Range ($700–$1,500):** Full-featured bidet seats with drying, deodorizing, and app control. The Kohler PureWash E930 and Bio Bidet Bliss BB-2000 fall here.
- **Premium ($3,000+):** One-piece smart toilets with auto-open, integrated bidets, and premium flush systems. TOTO and Kohler dominate this segment.

**Hidden Costs:**  
- Electrical installation (if no outlet near toilet): $150–$400  
- Professional plumbing for full toilet: $200–$500  
- Water filter replacement (some models): $20–$50 annually

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Tips

- **Bidet Seats (Kohler, Bio Bidet):** Installation is DIY-friendly. You’ll need a nearby GFCI electrical outlet and a standard T-connector for the water supply. Most kits include all necessary hardware. Allow 30–60 minutes.
- **Full Smart Toilets (TOTO Neorest):** Professional installation is strongly recommended. These units require a dedicated electrical outlet (often behind the toilet) and precise water line connection. Expect a 2–4 hour installation.
- **Water Supply:** Ensure your existing water line is compatible. Some bidet seats require a 3/8-inch compression fitting.
- **Space:** Measure your bathroom. Smart toilets and bidet seats add depth (usually 2–4 inches) to the toilet footprint.

### Smart Home Integration

All three recommended models support voice control via Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant. Common commands include:
- “Alexa, turn on the bidet.”
- “Hey Google, warm the toilet seat.”
- “Alexa, flush the toilet.”

Mobile apps (TOTO, Kohler Konnect, Bio Bidet) allow you to:
- Adjust settings remotely
- Create user profiles
- Monitor health data (Bio Bidet)
- Receive maintenance reminders (e.g., filter changes)

**Pro Tip:** For the best experience, ensure your smart toilet is on a 2.4GHz Wi-Fi network (most smart home devices don’t support 5GHz).
