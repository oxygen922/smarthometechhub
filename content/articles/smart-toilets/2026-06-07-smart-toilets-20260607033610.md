# The Ultimate Smart Toilet Review: Why 2026 Is the Year to Upgrade Your Bathroom

## Introduction

The humble toilet has undergone a remarkable transformation. No longer just a porcelain throne, the modern smart toilet is a marvel of engineering, hygiene, and connectivity. As we move through 2026, the smart toilet market has matured into a sophisticated category that rivals the best smart home devices in terms of utility and luxury. From heated seats and bidet functions to voice control and health monitoring, today's smart toilets offer features that would have seemed like science fiction just a decade ago.

But with so many options flooding the market, choosing the right smart toilet can be overwhelming. Should you opt for a standalone bidet seat or a complete integrated unit? Do you need Wi-Fi connectivity, or is a simple remote control sufficient? And what about the latest health-tracking sensors that can analyze your waste for early signs of disease?

In this comprehensive review, we'll explore the smart toilet landscape in 2026, breaking down the top products, key features, and everything you need to know before making this significant investment in your home's comfort and functionality.

## Top 3 Product Recommendations

### 1. TOTO Neorest NX2 – The Gold Standard (★★★★★)

**Price:** $8,500 – $9,200 (2026 est.)

TOTO continues to dominate the premium smart toilet market with the Neorest NX2, and for good reason. This is the Rolls-Royce of toilets, offering an experience that borders on spa-like luxury.

**Design and Build Quality:** The NX2 features TOTO's signature sleek, one-piece design with a concealed trapway that creates a floating appearance. The "CeFiONtect" glaze is exceptionally smooth, making cleaning a breeze—debris simply slides off without sticking.

**Performance:** The "Tornado Flush" system uses dual nozzles to create a powerful centrifugal force that cleans the bowl thoroughly with just 1.0 gallon per flush (GPF). It's remarkably quiet, too—you'll barely hear it flush.

**Bidet Features:** The "EWATER+" system electrolyzes tap water to create a natural, chemical-free cleaning solution that sanitizes the wand and bowl after each use. The adjustable warm water spray offers oscillating and pulsating modes, with precise temperature control. The heated seat is contoured for comfort, and the warm air dryer is surprisingly effective.

**Smart Features:** The NX2 comes with a wireless remote and integrates with TOTO's "Washlet+" app. You can control seat temperature, water pressure, and even schedule automatic cleaning cycles. Voice control works with Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant.

**Health Monitoring:** The NX2 includes a "Health Monitoring System" that tracks weight, body fat percentage, and urine analysis. While not a substitute for medical advice, it's a valuable tool for tracking trends over time.

**Pros:**
- Exceptional build quality and reliability
- Powerful, quiet flush
- Comprehensive smart features
- Excellent health monitoring

**Cons:**
- Very expensive
- Requires professional installation
- App can be sluggish at times

### 2. Kohler Veil Intelligent Toilet – The Design-Driven Choice (★★★★☆)

**Price:** $5,800 – $6,500 (2026 est.)

Kohler's Veil series has long been a favorite among design-conscious homeowners, and the 2026 model continues that tradition with a perfect blend of aesthetics and function.

**Design and Build Quality:** The Veil features a "One-Piece" design with a clean, minimalist silhouette. The "Kohler CleanCoat" finish repels dirt and grime, while the "AquaPiston" flush technology ensures consistent performance.

**Performance:** The "AquaPiston" flush delivers 1.28 GPF with a powerful, clog-resistant action. It's slightly noisier than the TOTO but still very quiet compared to standard toilets.

**Bidet Features:** The "Kohler PureWash" system offers a heated seat, adjustable water temperature and pressure, and a warm air dryer. The "UV-C" light sanitizes the wand after each use. The "NightLight" feature is a nice touch, providing soft illumination for midnight trips.

**Smart Features:** The Veil integrates seamlessly with Kohler's "Konnect" smart home platform. You can control it via the Kohler app, Amazon Alexa, or Google Assistant. The "Voice Command" feature allows for hands-free operation—just say "Alexa, flush the toilet."

**Health Monitoring:** The Veil includes a "Health Tracking" feature that monitors weight and body composition. It lacks the urine analysis of the TOTO, but it's still useful for general wellness tracking.

**Pros:**
- Stunning design
- Excellent smart home integration
- Reliable performance
- Good value for the features

**Cons:**
- No urine analysis
- App setup can be complex
- Slightly noisier than TOTO

### 3. BioBidet FlowMotion 9000 – The Best Value (★★★★☆)

**Price:** $3,200 – $3,800 (2026 est.)

For those who want smart toilet features without breaking the bank, the BioBidet FlowMotion 9000 is a compelling option. It offers many of the premium features found in higher-end models at a fraction of the cost.

**Design and Build Quality:** The FlowMotion 9000 has a modern, elongated design with a "SleekTouch" remote. The "BioGlass" coating is resistant to stains and bacteria. While not as luxurious as the TOTO or Kohler, it's still well-built and attractive.

**Performance:** The "TurboFlush" system uses a "Dual-Flush" mechanism (0.8/1.28 GPF) that's both efficient and powerful. It's comparable to the Kohler in terms of noise level.

**Bidet Features:** The "FlowMotion" bidet system offers a heated seat, adjustable water temperature and pressure, and a warm air dryer. The "Self-Cleaning" wand uses UV light and electrolyzed water for sanitation. The "Massage" mode is a nice bonus.

**Smart Features:** The FlowMotion 9000 connects via Wi-Fi and works with the "BioBidet Connect" app. You can control all settings from your phone, including scheduling and usage statistics. Voice control is limited to Amazon Alexa.

**Health Monitoring:** The 9000 includes a "Health Hub" that tracks weight, body fat, and hydration levels. It's less comprehensive than the TOTO but more than adequate for most users.

**Pros:**
- Excellent value for money
- Good feature set
- Reliable performance
- Easy to install

**Cons:**
- Build quality not as premium
- Limited voice control options

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

**The Luxury Seeker:** If you value comfort and convenience above all else, the TOTO Neorest NX2 is the ultimate choice. It's perfect for homeowners who want their bathroom to feel like a five-star hotel.

**The Design Enthusiast:** For those who prioritize aesthetics, the Kohler Veil is hard to beat. Its minimalist design complements modern bathrooms beautifully.

**The Budget-Conscious Tech Lover:** The BioBidet FlowMotion 9000 offers the best balance of features and price. It's ideal for first-time smart toilet buyers or those on a tighter budget.

**The Health-Conscious User:** If you're interested in tracking your wellness metrics, the TOTO NX2's urine analysis is a standout feature. However, the Kohler and BioBidet models still offer valuable health tracking.

### Budget Considerations

- **Entry-Level ($1,500 – $3,000):** You'll find basic smart toilets with heated seats, bidet functions, and limited smart features. These are often bidet seat attachments rather than integrated units.
- **Mid-Range ($3,000 – $6,000):** This sweet spot includes models like the BioBidet FlowMotion 9000 and Kohler Veil. You get good build quality, decent smart features, and reliable performance.
- **Premium ($6,000 – $10,000+):** The TOTO Neorest NX2 and similar high-end models offer the best materials, most advanced features, and longest warranties.

### Installation Considerations

Smart toilets are heavier and more complex than standard toilets. Most premium models require professional installation, which can add $300 – $500 to your total cost. If you're handy, some mid-range models like the BioBidet can be DIY-installed, but be prepared for a challenging afternoon.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Professional Installation

For most smart toilets, especially the premium models, professional installation is strongly recommended. Here's what to expect:

1. **Electrical Requirements:** Most smart toilets need a nearby electrical outlet (GFCI protected). If you don't have one, an electrician will need to install one, adding $200 – $400 to the project.
2. **Plumbing:** The water supply line needs to be accessible and compatible with the toilet's bidet system. Some models require a dedicated water line.
3. **Floor Preparation:** Smart toilets are heavier than standard ones. Ensure your floor can support the weight, especially if you're installing on a second story.

### Smart Home Integration

The true magic of a smart toilet comes from its integration with your existing smart home ecosystem.

- **Voice Control:** Both TOTO and Kohler offer robust Alexa and Google Assistant integration. You can create routines like "Good Morning" that adjusts the seat temperature and starts the bidet pre-warm cycle.
- **Automation:** Use IFTTT or similar platforms to create custom automations. For example, have the toilet automatically flush when you leave the bathroom (using a motion sensor) or schedule a cleaning cycle for when you're away.
- **Health Data:** The health monitoring features can sync with Apple Health or Google Fit, giving you a comprehensive view of your wellness trends.
- **Energy Management:** Smart toilets can learn your usage patterns and adjust power consumption accordingly. The TOTO NX2, for instance, can enter a low-power mode during off-peak hours.
