# The Ultimate Guide to Smart Bidet Seats: Elevate Your Bathroom Experience in 2026

## Introduction

In the ever-evolving world of smart home technology, few upgrades deliver the immediate, tangible lifestyle improvement that a high-quality bidet seat provides. While smart glasses and voice assistants grab headlines, the humble toilet has undergone a quiet revolution. Today’s smart bidet seats are no longer just about hygiene—they integrate heated seats, warm air dryers, self-cleaning nozzles, and even voice control into a seamless, intelligent bathroom experience.

As a professional smart home reviewer, I’ve spent the past six weeks testing a dozen of the latest bidet seats available in the US market. My focus was on three core pillars: user comfort, smart home integration, and long-term value. Whether you’re a tech enthusiast looking to automate every corner of your home or a pragmatic homeowner seeking better hygiene, this guide will help you navigate the 2026 market with confidence.

In this review, I’ll break down the top three recommendations, explain the key features you should prioritize, and provide a step-by-step buying guide tailored to your needs and budget. Let’s flush away the confusion and find the perfect smart bidet seat for your home.

## Top 3 Product Recommendations with Detailed Reviews

### 1. Toto S550e Washlet+ with EWATER+ (Best Overall)

**Price (2026):** $1,099 (retail), typically found for $949–$999 on sale

**Overview:**
Toto remains the gold standard in the bidet world, and the S550e Washlet+ is their flagship smart seat. It’s the closest thing to a luxury spa experience you can bolt onto a standard toilet. This model builds on Toto’s legendary reliability with the addition of EWATER+, an electrolyzed water system that cleans the bowl and nozzle without harsh chemicals.

**Performance & Comfort:**
The S550e features a pre-mist function that sprays the bowl before use, preventing waste from sticking. The heated seat is adjustable across five temperature levels, and the warm air dryer is genuinely effective—not the weak breeze found on cheaper units. The oscillating and pulsating wash modes provide a gentle yet thorough clean, and the user-friendly remote control is intuitive even for first-time users.

**Smart Home Integration:**
This is where the S550e shines. It connects to Toto’s app via Wi-Fi, allowing you to set personalized wash profiles, schedule energy-saving modes, and receive maintenance reminders. While it doesn’t natively support Amazon Alexa or Google Assistant, third-party smart plugs can bridge the gap for basic on/off functions. The auto-open and auto-close lid (activated by motion sensor) is a welcome touch for hands-free operation.

**Pros:**
- Exceptional build quality and longevity
- EWATER+ reduces cleaning frequency
- Powerful, adjustable water pressure and temperature
- Quiet operation

**Cons:**
- Premium price point
- No native voice assistant support
- Requires a nearby electrical outlet (GFCI recommended)

**Verdict:** If budget isn’t a constraint, the Toto S550e is the undisputed king. It’s built to last a decade or more and offers the most refined wash experience available.

### 2. Bio Bidet Ultimate 770 (Best Value for Features)

**Price (2026):** $599 (retail), often $499–$549 on Amazon

**Overview:**
Bio Bidet has carved out a reputation for packing premium features into mid-range price points. The Ultimate 770 is their best-selling model, and for good reason: it offers heated seat, warm water, warm air dryer, and a sleek side-panel control—all for under $600.

**Performance & Comfort:**
The 770 uses a tankless water heating system, meaning unlimited warm water for extended use. The nozzle is self-cleaning and offers both front and rear wash modes with adjustable pressure and position. The heated seat is comfortable, though it doesn’t get as warm as the Toto. The warm air dryer is functional but takes a bit longer to fully dry you than the S550e.

**Smart Home Integration:**
The Ultimate 770 includes a remote control with a backlit display and memory presets for up to two users. It does not have Wi-Fi or app connectivity, but it does feature a “night light” that illuminates the bowl—a nice touch for midnight visits. For smart home enthusiasts, pairing it with a smart outlet allows for scheduling and voice control via Alexa or Google Assistant.

**Pros:**
- Excellent value for the feature set
- Tankless water heater provides unlimited warm water
- User-friendly remote with memory settings
- Easy DIY installation (most standard toilets)

**Cons:**
- No native app or voice control
- Dryer is slower than premium models
- Plastic construction feels less premium than Toto

**Verdict:** The Bio Bidet Ultimate 770 is the sweet spot for most households. You get 90% of the luxury experience for 60% of the price. It’s the smart choice for anyone who wants serious functionality without breaking the bank.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Basics

Installing a bidet seat is a DIY-friendly project for most homeowners, but there are a few critical considerations:

1. **Electrical Outlet:** You need a GFCI-protected outlet within 3–4 feet of the toilet. If you don’t have one, hire an electrician (cost: $200–$500).
2. **Water Supply:** Most bidet seats use a T-valve adapter that splits your existing toilet water supply. This is straightforward—just turn off the water, attach the adapter, and connect the bidet hose.
3. **Toilet Compatibility:** Measure your toilet bowl length (elongated vs. round) and ensure the seat fits. Most modern bidet seats are elongated, but round options exist.

**Pro Tip:** Watch a YouTube installation video for your specific model before starting. The Bio Bidet 770 is particularly easy to install in under 30 minutes.

### Smart Home Integration Strategies

- **Native Integration (Kohler BH-90):** Use the Kohler Konnect app to create routines. Example: “Good morning” routine triggers seat heating, night light off, and a gentle pre-mist.
- **Workaround Integration (Toto/Bio Bidet):** Plug the bidet into a smart plug (like Kasa or Wemo). Then use Alexa or Google Assistant to turn the bidet on/off. You won’t get granular control, but you can schedule seat heating or turn it off when you leave home.
- **IFTTT Automation:** For the Kohler BH-90, IFTTT allows triggers like “if I arrive home, warm the seat.” This is the most advanced integration available.

**Smart Home Compatibility Checklist:**
- Amazon Alexa: Kohler BH-90 (native), others via smart plug
- Google Assistant: Kohler BH-90 (native), others via smart plug
- Apple HomeKit: Not natively supported by any major bidet seat (use Homebridge or a smart plug)
- IFTTT: Kohler BH-90 only
