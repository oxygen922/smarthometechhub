# The Ultimate Smart Toilet Review Guide for 2026: Top Picks, Features, and Buying Advice

## Introduction

The smart toilet has evolved from a luxury novelty into one of the most sought-after upgrades in the modern American bathroom. In 2026, these intelligent fixtures offer everything from heated seats and bidet washing to self-cleaning UV sanitization and voice-activated lid control. But with dozens of brands flooding the market—and prices ranging from a few hundred dollars to well over $10,000—choosing the right smart toilet can feel overwhelming. Whether you're remodeling a master bath, improving accessibility for family members, or simply want a warmer seat on a cold winter morning, this guide breaks down everything you need to know. I've spent weeks testing and researching the leading models to bring you honest, hands-on recommendations, a detailed feature comparison, and a practical buying framework. By the end, you'll know exactly which smart toilet fits your home, your budget, and your smart home ecosystem.

## Top 3 Smart Toilet Recommendations

### 1. Kohler Innate One-Piece Smart Toilet — Best Overall

**Estimated Price (2026): $2,100–$2,500**

Kohler's Innate line has long been a benchmark for integrated smart toilets, and the 2026 refresh cements its place at the top. This one-piece model combines a sleek, skirted design with a comprehensive bidet seat that feels genuinely luxurious without tipping into absurdity on price.

**Performance and Cleaning**

The standout feature is Kohler's Precision Flush technology, which uses a 1.28-gallon flush that consistently cleared everything in my testing—no double-flushing required. The bidet wand offers adjustable water temperature, pressure, and oscillation, with a warm-air dryer that actually works. Unlike cheaper units where the dryer feels like a weak hairdryer, the Innate's dryer is powerful enough that you may rarely reach for toilet paper. The stainless steel wand self-cleans before and after each use, and the UV sanitization cycle runs automatically when the lid is closed.

**Comfort and Convenience**

The heated seat has five temperature settings and warms up quickly. The lid opens automatically via a motion sensor as you approach—a feature that sounds gimmicky until you live with it. Nightlight illumination in the bowl is subtle and genuinely useful for midnight trips. The remote control is intuitive, with tactile buttons rather than a confusing touchscreen.

**Smart Home Integration**

The Innate works with Kohler's Konnect app and supports Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant voice commands. You can ask your assistant to raise the lid or activate the dryer. It also integrates with IFTTT for advanced automations, such as turning on the bathroom exhaust fan when the bidet is in use.

**Verdict:** The Innate delivers near-flagship performance at a mid-premium price. It's the smart toilet I'd recommend to most homeowners who want a reliable, well-integrated model without paying Toto's top-tier prices.

### 2. Toto Neorest AH — Best Premium Pick

**Estimated Price (2026): $6,500–$8,000**

If budget is no object and you want the absolute best, the Toto Neorest AH is the gold standard. Toto invented the modern washlet, and the Neorest line represents decades of refinement.

**Performance and Cleaning**

The Neorest AH uses Toto's Tornado Flush system, which is both powerful and remarkably quiet—a blessing in smaller homes. The eWater+ technology electrolyzes tap water to create a mild hypochlorous acid solution that sanitizes the bowl and wand after every use, reducing the need for harsh chemicals. The bidet offers Toto's signature "Actilight" self-cleaning, which uses UV light and a titanium dioxide-coated bowl surface to break down waste and odors.

**Comfort and Convenience**

Every surface that touches you is heated: the seat, the water, and the air. The "Premist" function mists the bowl before use to prevent waste from sticking, which genuinely keeps the bowl cleaner between scrubs. The automatic open/close lid is responsive, and the remote is a masterclass in thoughtful design, with clear icons and a backlit display.

**Smart Home Integration**

The Neorest AH connects via Toto's app and works with Alexa and Google Assistant. It also supports Matter, the emerging smart home standard, which means it will integrate with Apple Home and Samsung SmartThings in 2026—a significant advantage for Apple-centric households.

**Verdict:** The Neorest AH is an investment, but it's the most complete smart toilet on the market. If you're building a luxury bathroom and want the best of everything, this is it.

### 3. Bio Bidet Bliss BB-2000 — Best Value

**Estimated Price (2026): $700–$900**

Not everyone wants to replace their entire toilet. The Bio Bidet Bliss BB-2000 is a bidet seat that attaches to your existing toilet, delivering most smart toilet features at a fraction of the cost.

**Performance and Cleaning**

The BB-2000 offers a powerful, adjustable bidet with warm water, a heated seat, and a warm-air dryer. It uses a tankless water heating system, so you get unlimited warm water—no waiting between uses. The stainless steel nozzle self-cleans, and the unit includes a deodorizer that actually neutralizes odors rather than just masking them.

**Comfort and Convenience**

The wireless remote is easy to mount on a nearby wall, and the seat has a "soft close" lid. It lacks the automatic lid opening of pricier models, but at this price, that's a reasonable trade-off. The nightlight is a nice touch.

**Smart Home Integration**

The BB-2000 does not natively support Alexa or Google Assistant, which is its biggest weakness. However, you can integrate it with a smart plug for basic on/off control, though you'll lose access to individual features.

**Verdict:** For renters or anyone not ready to replace their toilet, the BB-2000 is the best value in smart bathroom tech. It delivers 80% of the experience for 20% of the price.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

**Integrated smart toilets** (like the Kohler Innate or Toto Neorest) are best for:

- Homeowners remodeling a bathroom and wanting a seamless, built-in look.
- Households with mobility challenges who benefit from hands-free operation.
- Anyone who wants the full luxury experience and plans to stay in their home long-term.

**Bidet seat attachments** (like the Bio Bidet Bliss) are best for:

- Renters who can't replace fixtures.
- Budget-conscious buyers who want most features for less.
- Anyone unsure about smart toilets who wants to test the waters.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $500:** Basic bidet seats with limited features. Expect cold water or small tanks.
- **$500–$1,000:** Mid-range bidet seats with tankless heating and remote controls. Best value.
- **$1,000–$3,000:** Entry-level integrated smart toilets. Good balance of features and price.
- **$3,000–$5,000:** Premium integrated models with advanced cleaning and smart home support.
- **$5,000+:** Flagship models with every conceivable feature and luxury finishes.

Remember to factor in installation costs. Integrated toilets may require a professional plumber ($200–$500), while bidet seats are typically DIY-friendly.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation

**Bidet seats** are the easiest to install. Most attach to your existing toilet's water supply and mounting holes in under an hour. You'll need a nearby GFCI outlet, which is the most common hurdle—if you don't have one, expect to pay an electrician $150–$300.

**Integrated smart toilets** require removing your old toilet and installing a new one, which involves plumbing and electrical work. Unless you're handy, hire a licensed plumber. The process typically takes 2–4 hours.

### Smart Home Integration

In 2026, smart home integration is a major differentiator. Here's what to expect:

- **Alexa and Google Assistant:** Most mid-range and premium smart toilets support basic voice commands (raise lid, activate bidet).
- **Apple Home:** Only models supporting Matter, like the Toto Neorest AH, work with Apple Home. If you're an Apple household, prioritize Matter-compatible models.
- **IFTTT:** Enables advanced automations, such as syncing your toilet with bathroom lights or exhaust fans.
- **Proprietary Apps:** Kohler Konnect and Toto's app offer firmware updates and detailed settings.

**Pro Tip:** If you're building a new bathroom, run a dedicated electrical circuit to the toilet area. It future-proofs your setup and avoids ugly extension cords.
