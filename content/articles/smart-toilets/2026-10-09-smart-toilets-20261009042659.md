# The Ultimate Smart Toilet Review Guide for 2026: Upgrade Your Bathroom Into a Smart Home Sanctuary

## Introduction

The smart toilet has evolved from a luxury novelty into a cornerstone of the modern American smart home. In 2026, these high-tech fixtures offer bidet functionality, heated seats, self-cleaning mechanisms, and even Alexa integration—transforming the most private room in your house into a spa-like experience. But with dozens of models flooding the market, ranging from $300 budget bidet seats to $8,000 luxury units, choosing the right smart toilet can feel overwhelming. This comprehensive review breaks down the top performers, essential features, installation realities, and budget considerations. Whether you're remodeling or simply want to upgrade your existing toilet with a smart bidet seat, we've tested and researched the leading options to help you make an informed decision. Let's dive into the world of smart toilets and find the perfect fit for your home.

## Top 3 Smart Toilet Recommendations for 2026

After extensive hands-on testing and analysis of customer feedback across major US retailers, three models stand out for their performance, reliability, and value. Each serves a different user profile.

### 1. TOTO Neorest AH Dual Flush Toilet – Best Overall Luxury Smart Toilet

**Price:** ~$5,800–$6,500 (2026 estimate)

TOTO remains the undisputed king of smart toilets, and the Neorest AH is the flagship that justifies the premium. This isn't just a toilet—it's a complete hygiene system. The moment you approach, the lid opens automatically via a motion sensor. The heated seat warms to your preferred temperature (adjustable in five levels), and the bidet functions offer front and rear cleansing with oscillating and pulsating modes.

**Performance:** The Tornado Flush system uses cyclonic rinsing to clean the bowl with remarkable efficiency while using only 1.0 gallons per flush (GPF) for the full flush and 0.8 GPF for the light flush. The eWater+ system electrolyzes tap water to create a mild hypochlorous acid solution that continuously mists the bowl and wand, reducing bacteria without harsh chemicals.

**Smart Features:** The Neorest AH includes a wireless remote, automatic open/close, auto-flush, nightlight, and a warm air dryer. It integrates with TOTO's smartphone app (iOS/Android) for personalized settings. Notably, it does *not* natively integrate with Alexa or Google Home, which is a drawback for some smart home enthusiasts, but the dedicated remote is intuitive.

**Verdict:** If you want the most reliable, hygienic, and luxurious smart toilet and don't mind the price, the Neorest AH is the gold standard. It's ideal for master bathrooms and homeowners who value Japanese engineering.

### 2. Kohler Numera Smart Toilet – Best Smart Home Integration

**Price:** ~$3,200–$3,800 (2026 estimate)

Kohler's Numera strikes a balance between luxury and smart home compatibility. Unlike TOTO, Kohler has fully embraced the connected home ecosystem. The Numera works with Amazon Alexa, Google Assistant, and Apple HomeKit (via Kohler Konnect). You can voice-control flushing, bidet functions, and even the heated seat.

**Performance:** The Numera features a dual-flush system (1.28 GPF / 0.9 GPF) and a stainless steel wand with three spray patterns. The heated seat has adjustable temperature, and the warm air dryer is effective. The self-cleaning wand sanitizes with UV light after each use—a nice touch.

**Smart Features:** The Konnect app allows you to create user profiles, set preferred bidet settings, and monitor water usage. Motion-activated lid opening and auto-flush are standard. The nightlight is adjustable in color and brightness. One standout: integration with Kohler's whole-home generator and faucet systems for a cohesive smart home.

**Verdict:** For the smart home enthusiast who wants voice control and app integration, the Numera is the best choice. It's slightly less refined than TOTO in bidet comfort but wins on connectivity.

### 3. Bio Bidet BB-2000 Bidet Seat – Best Budget Smart Toilet Upgrade

**Price:** ~$550–$700 (2026 estimate)

Not ready to replace your entire toilet? The Bio Bidet BB-2000 is a bidet seat that attaches to your existing elongated toilet, delivering 80% of the smart toilet experience for a fraction of the cost. It includes a heated seat, unlimited warm water (tankless heating), adjustable spray width and position, and a warm air dryer.

**Performance:** The BB-2000 uses a tankless water heater so you never run out of warm water. The nozzle has a self-cleaning feature and is made of stainless steel. The seat has a built-in nightlight and a wireless remote. It does require a nearby GFCI outlet, which may mean hiring an electrician if your bathroom lacks one.

**Smart Features:** No voice control or app integration, but the remote is comprehensive. It has a memory function for two users. The BB-2000 is a workhorse—reliable and easy to install with basic DIY skills.

**Verdict:** This is the best entry point for anyone curious about smart toilets. It's affordable, effective, and can be installed in under an hour. Perfect for renters or those testing the waters before committing to a full smart toilet.

## Buying Guide: Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

A smart toilet is a significant investment, so it's not for everyone. Here's a breakdown to help you decide.

### Who Should Buy?

- **Homeowners remodeling a bathroom:** If you're already tearing out walls, running a dedicated electrical line for a smart toilet is easy and adds resale value.
- **Individuals with mobility issues:** The auto-open/close, heated seat, and bidet functions are beneficial for those with limited mobility or arthritis.
- **Eco-conscious households:** Smart toilets use less water per flush than many traditional models, and bidets reduce toilet paper consumption dramatically.
- **Tech enthusiasts:** If you love automating your home, a smart toilet is the ultimate conversation piece.
- **Renters:** A bidet seat like the Bio Bidet BB-2000 is a no-permanent-installation option that can move with you.

### Who Should Skip?

- **Tight budgets:** If $500 is a stretch, a basic bidet attachment ($50–$100) might be a better first step.
- **No electrical outlet:** If you can't install a GFCI outlet, a smart toilet isn't feasible.
- **Short-term renters:** Unless you can take the bidet seat with you, it may not be worth the investment.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $300:** Basic bidet seats with cold water only. Limited features.
- **$300–$800:** Mid-range bidet seats with heated seat, warm water, and remote. Our pick: Bio Bidet BB-2000.
- **$1,000–$2,500:** Entry-level integrated smart toilets from brands like WoodBridge and Ove. Decent features but less reliable long-term.
- **$2,500–$4,000:** Premium integrated smart toilets with smart home integration. Our pick: Kohler Numera.
- **$5,000+:** Luxury flagship models with the best materials and engineering. Our pick: TOTO Neorest AH.

**Financing:** Many retailers offer 0% APR financing for 12–24 months, making a $3,000 smart toilet more accessible. Check with Amazon, Home Depot, and local showrooms.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Realities

Installing an integrated smart toilet is not a DIY job for most homeowners. It requires:

1. **Plumbing modifications:** The rough-in distance (distance from wall to drain) must match the toilet. Most smart toilets have a 12-inch rough-in, but verify.
2. **Electrical work:** You need a GFCI-protected outlet within 3 feet of the toilet. If not present, hire a licensed electrician. Cost: $150–$300.
3. **Water supply:** A standard 3/8-inch compression fitting is typical, but some models require a different valve.
4. **Weight:** Smart toilets can weigh 100+ pounds. Get a helper for lifting.

Professional installation from a plumber typically costs $200–$500, plus electrical work if needed. Total installation budget: $350–$800.

Bidet seats are much simpler. If you have an outlet, you can install one in 30–60 minutes with a screwdriver and adjustable wrench. No plumbing changes needed—just connect the T-adapter to your existing water supply.

### Smart Home Integration

Only a few smart toilets integrate with major smart home platforms:

- **Kohler Numera:** Works with Alexa, Google Assistant, and Apple HomeKit via Kohler Konnect. You can create routines like "Alexa, good morning" to raise the seat and warm it.
- **TOTO Neorest:** App-only control via Bluetooth/Wi-Fi. No voice assistant integration.
- **Bio Bidet:** No smart home integration, but remote control is sufficient for most.

**Pro Tip:** If you want voice control, consider a smart bidet seat with Wi-Fi (like the Bio Bidet Discovery DLS) or go with Kohler. Otherwise, the remote is perfectly adequate.

**Future-Proofing:** Look for models with OTA (over-the-air) firmware updates. Kohler and TOTO offer these, ensuring your toilet improves over time.
