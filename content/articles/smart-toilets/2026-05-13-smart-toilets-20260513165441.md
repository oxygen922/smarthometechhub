## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Overview

Most smart bidet seats are designed for DIY installation, but there are a few key requirements:

1. **Electrical Outlet:** You need a GFCI-protected outlet within 3–4 feet of the toilet. If you don’t have one, hire an electrician.
2. **Water Supply:** A standard 1/2-inch water supply line is required. Most kits include a T-adapter.
3. **Toilet Compatibility:** Ensure your toilet bowl is elongated or round—most bidet seats are designed for elongated bowls. Check the manufacturer’s specifications.

**Installation Steps (Simplified):**
1. Turn off water supply and flush toilet to empty tank.
2. Remove existing toilet seat.
3. Install mounting bracket and attach bidet seat.
4. Connect water supply using included T-adapter.
5. Plug in electrical cord and test.

**Time Required:** 30–60 minutes for most users.

### Smart Home Integration

**Wi-Fi Connectivity:** Models like the Kohler PureWash E930 require a dedicated hub (e.g., Kohler Konnect) for full smart functionality. Others, like the Toto S7, use Bluetooth or direct Wi-Fi.

**Voice Control:** Alexa and Google Assistant allow commands like:
- “Alexa, turn on the bidet seat.”
- “Hey Google, warm the seat.”

**App Features:** Schedule seat warming, track water usage, and set user profiles. Some apps even offer health insights, like water consumption reminders.

**Automation:** Use routines to have the seat warm up at a specific time each morning or turn off when you leave the house.

**Compatibility Check:** Always verify that the bidet seat works with your existing smart home hub (e.g., Amazon Echo, Google Nest, Apple HomePod).
