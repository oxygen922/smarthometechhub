# Smart Toilet Review 2026: The Ultimate Guide to Smart Toilets, Bidet Seats, and AI-Powered Bathroom Tech

## Introduction

The smart toilet has quietly become one of the most compelling upgrades in the modern American smart home. What was once a luxury reserved for high-end Japanese hotels has now become a mainstream category, with heated seats, warm-water bidet washing, self-cleaning bowls, and even AI-powered health monitoring landing in bathrooms across the United States. In 2026, the smart toilet market has matured dramatically: prices have dropped, installation has simplified, and features that once felt gimmicky now feel genuinely useful. Whether you're looking to ditch toilet paper for good, add a touch of spa luxury to your daily routine, or track wellness metrics from the comfort of your own home, there's a smart toilet or smart bidet seat for nearly every budget. In this in-depth review, we'll break down the top products, the features that actually matter, and everything you need to know before you buy.

If you've been on the fence about upgrading your bathroom, this guide will help you decide whether a smart toilet is worth it in 2026 — and which model fits your home, your plumbing, and your wallet.

## Top 3 Smart Toilet Recommendations

### 1. TOTO Neorest NX2 — Best Overall Smart Toilet

**Estimated Price (2026): $8,500–$9,800**

TOTO remains the undisputed gold standard in the smart toilet world, and the Neorest NX2 is the company's flagship statement piece. If you want the absolute best smart toilet money can buy, this is it.

The NX2 combines TOTO's legendary Actilight self-cleaning technology — which uses UV light and a titanium dioxide-coated bowl to break down waste and bacteria — with an ultra-efficient 1.0/0.8 GPF tornado flush that's both powerful and whisper-quiet. The bidet functions are where TOTO truly shines: the eWater+ system electrolyzes tap water to create a mild hypochlorous acid solution that sanitizes the wand and bowl after every use, a feature that gives real peace of mind.

The heated seat is comfortable and adjustable across multiple temperature settings, and the warm air dryer is genuinely effective rather than an afterthought. The remote control is intuitive, and the lid opens automatically via proximity sensor — a small touch that never stops feeling futuristic.

**Pros:**
- Industry-leading self-cleaning and sanitizing technology
- Exceptionally quiet and efficient flush
- Premium build quality that lasts decades
- Automatic lid, flush, and deodorizer

**Cons:**
- Very expensive
- Requires a dedicated electrical outlet near the toilet
- Professional installation strongly recommended

**Who it's for:** Buyers who want the best smart toilet available and are willing to pay for uncompromising quality. This is the model to choose if budget is not a primary concern.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Toilet?

A smart toilet is a worthwhile investment for several types of buyers:

- **Comfort seekers:** If you hate cold toilet seats in winter or want a spa-like bathroom experience, a smart toilet or bidet seat is a game-changer.
- **Eco-conscious households:** Bidet washing dramatically reduces toilet paper consumption, which saves money and reduces environmental impact over time.
- **People with mobility limitations:** Features like auto-open lids, remote controls, and hands-free flushing make bathrooms more accessible.
- **Wellness enthusiasts:** If you enjoy tracking health data with wearables, a smart toilet with health monitoring (like the Kohler Numera) adds another data point to your routine.
- **Smart home adopters:** If your home already runs on Alexa, Google Home, or Apple Home, adding a connected toilet is a natural extension.

### Who Should Skip It?

- **Renters without landlord approval:** Full smart toilets require plumbing and electrical work. Bidet seats are a better fit.
- **Bathrooms without nearby outlets:** Every smart toilet and most bidet seats need power. If there's no outlet, you'll need an electrician.
- **Budget-only shoppers:** A basic bidet attachment for under $100 can deliver a surprisingly good experience if you don't need heating or drying.

### Budget Considerations

In 2026, smart toilet pricing breaks down into three tiers:

- **Under $800:** Smart bidet seats like the Bio Bidet BB-2000. Best value, easiest installation.
- **$1,500–$4,000:** Mid-range smart toilets with integrated tanks, auto lids, and app control.
- **$6,000–$10,000+:** Flagship smart toilets with self-cleaning technology, health monitoring, and premium materials.

Don't forget to factor in installation costs ($200–$600 for a full toilet replacement) and potential electrical work ($150–$400 if you need a new outlet).

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Overview

**Bidet seats** are the easiest entry point. Most models, including the Bio Bidet BB-2000, can be installed in under an hour with basic tools. You'll connect the seat to your existing water supply using a T-adapter and plug it into a nearby GFCI outlet. No plumber required for most setups.

**Full smart toilets** are more involved. You'll need to remove your existing toilet, ensure the flange and flooring are in good condition, connect the water supply, and plug in the unit. While confident DIYers can handle this, professional installation is recommended — especially for premium models where a mistake could be costly.

**Electrical requirements** are the most common hurdle. Smart toilets need a grounded GFCI outlet within reach of the unit's power cord (usually 3–4 feet). If your bathroom doesn't have one, an electrician can install one for $150–$400.

### Smart Home Integration

In 2026, smart toilets increasingly integrate with major smart home ecosystems:

- **Amazon Alexa & Google Home:** Many models support voice commands for flushing, activating the bidet, or adjusting seat temperature.
- **Apple Home:** Support is growing, though still less common than Alexa and Google.
- **Manufacturer Apps:** TOTO, Kohler, and Bio Bidet all offer apps for scheduling, energy-saving modes, and firmware updates.
- **Health Data Sync:** The Kohler Numera can share wellness insights with its app, and future integrations with platforms like Apple Health are expected.

For most users, app control and voice commands are nice-to-haves rather than must-haves. The core experience — heated seat, warm water, and drying — works perfectly without any connectivity at all.
