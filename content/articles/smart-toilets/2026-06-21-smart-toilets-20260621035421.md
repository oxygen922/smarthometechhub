# The Ultimate Guide to Smart Bidet Seats: 2026’s Best Options for a Luxurious, Hygienic, and Tech-Forward Bathroom

## Introduction

The bathroom has quietly undergone a technological revolution, and at the center of it is the smart bidet seat. No longer a niche luxury reserved for high-end hotels, the bidet seat has become a staple of the modern smart home, offering unparalleled hygiene, comfort, and even health monitoring. In 2026, these devices have evolved beyond simple warm-water sprays into sophisticated IoT-enabled fixtures that integrate seamlessly with your home automation system. Whether you’re looking to reduce toilet paper usage, improve personal cleanliness, or simply add a touch of spa-like luxury to your daily routine, a smart bidet seat is a game-changer. This in-depth review will guide you through the top three products on the market, break down the essential features to consider, and help you decide which model is right for your home and budget.

## Top 3 Product Recommendations

### 1. TOTO Washlet S7A (Model: S7A-01)

**Best Overall High-End Smart Bidet Seat**

TOTO remains the gold standard in bidet technology, and the Washlet S7A is their most advanced model to date. This seat is a marvel of engineering, featuring TOTO’s proprietary PREMIST technology that sprays a fine mist of electrolyzed water onto the bowl before use, preventing waste from sticking and reducing cleaning frequency. The S7A also includes the eWater+ system, which uses electrolyzed water for self-cleaning the wand and nozzle, ensuring impeccable hygiene without harsh chemicals.

The user experience is refined to perfection. The heated seat is contoured for maximum comfort, and the remote control is intuitive with a large, backlit LCD screen. The spray patterns are customizable, offering options like oscillating, pulsating, and a wide, gentle spray for sensitive users. The air dryer is powerful and warm, drying you effectively in under a minute. For 2026, TOTO has added enhanced Wi-Fi connectivity, allowing you to control the seat via the TOTO Smart Home app, set user profiles, and even monitor water usage. The deodorizing system uses a carbon filter to neutralize odors instantly, making the bathroom a more pleasant space.

**Pros:** Unmatched build quality, advanced self-cleaning, excellent spray and dryer performance, robust smart home integration.
**Cons:** Very expensive, requires a nearby electrical outlet, installation can be tricky for non-DIYers.
**Best For:** Homeowners who want the absolute best and are willing to pay a premium for top-tier technology and durability.

### 2. Bio Bidet by Bemis Ultimate 800

**Best Mid-Range Smart Bidet Seat with Health Monitoring**

The Bio Bidet Ultimate 800 has carved out a unique niche by integrating health-focused features that go beyond basic hygiene. This model includes a built-in sensor that can track your weight, body fat percentage, and even heart rate variability (HRV) while you sit. This data is synced via Bluetooth to the Bio Bidet Health app, providing a daily snapshot of your wellness. For users interested in long-term health trends, this is a compelling feature.

Beyond the health tracking, the Ultimate 800 delivers a premium bidet experience. It offers a stainless steel, self-cleaning nozzle with five spray modes, including a child-friendly gentle spray. The seat is heated and features an energy-saving mode. The remote control is ergonomic and easy to use, with a clear display. The warm air dryer is effective, and the deodorizer uses a catalytic filter to break down odors. The unit also has a nightlight, which is a thoughtful touch for late-night bathroom visits.

**Pros:** Innovative health tracking, excellent value for the feature set, comfortable seat, good spray variety.
**Cons:** Health data may not be medically certified, app can be buggy at times, slightly louder than TOTO.
**Best For:** Tech enthusiasts and health-conscious users who want a smart bathroom experience that provides actionable wellness data.

### 3. Kohler PureWash E930

**Best Value Smart Bidet Seat for Smart Home Integration**

Kohler’s PureWash E930 is the ideal entry point for those looking to upgrade their bathroom without breaking the bank. While it lacks the health tracking of the Bio Bidet and the premium feel of the TOTO, it excels in core functionality and smart home compatibility. The E930 is fully compatible with Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant, allowing you to start a warm water wash, adjust seat temperature, or activate the dryer with simple voice commands. This level of integration is rare at this price point.

The seat itself is comfortable and contoured, with a heated seat and a warm water heater that provides an endless supply of warm water. The wand is self-cleaning and offers a front and rear wash. The remote control is simple with large buttons, making it easy for all ages to use. The air dryer is adequate, though not as powerful as the more expensive models. The unit also features a slow-close lid and seat, which is a welcome touch for noise reduction. In 2026, Kohler has updated the firmware to support Matter, the new smart home standard, ensuring future-proof compatibility.

**Pros:** Excellent smart home integration with voice control, great value for money, reliable performance, easy to install.
**Cons:** No health tracking, dryer is less powerful, fewer spray customization options.
**Best For:** Smart home enthusiasts on a budget, or anyone wanting a reliable, voice-controlled bidet seat without the premium price tag.

- **Premium ($900+):** The TOTO Washlet S7A dominates this tier. You pay for the best materials, the most advanced self-cleaning technology, and the most refined user experience. If you plan to keep your bidet seat for 10+ years, the premium is justified.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation

Installing a smart bidet seat is a manageable DIY project for most homeowners. The process typically involves:

1.  **Shut off water supply** to the toilet.
2.  **Remove the existing toilet seat.**
3.  **Install the mounting plate** using the provided hardware.
4.  **Connect the T-adapter** to the water supply line and the bidet seat.
5.  **Plug in the bidet seat** to a GFCI-protected electrical outlet near the toilet.
6.  **Attach the seat** to the mounting plate and secure it.

All three models reviewed here are designed for standard two-piece toilets. The Kohler PureWash E930 is the easiest to install, with clear instructions and a simple mounting system. The TOTO Washlet S7A is more complex due to the additional water line for the PREMIST feature. If you are not comfortable with basic plumbing, hiring a professional handyman is recommended. The electrical requirement is a standard 3-prong outlet; if you don’t have one near your toilet, you will need an electrician.

### Smart Home Integration

- **TOTO Washlet S7A:** Connects via Wi-Fi to the TOTO Smart Home app. You can control all functions, create user profiles, and set schedules. It works with Alexa and Google Assistant for basic voice commands (e.g., “Alexa, turn on the warm water wash”).
- **Bio Bidet Ultimate 800:** Uses Bluetooth to sync health data to the Bio Bidet Health app. It does not support voice assistants for bidet functions, but the health data is accessible on your smartphone.
- **Kohler PureWash E930:** The most integrated of the three. It connects via Wi-Fi and supports Alexa, Google Assistant, and Matter. You can create routines like “Good Morning” that automatically warm the seat and prepare the bidet. Voice control is seamless and responsive.
