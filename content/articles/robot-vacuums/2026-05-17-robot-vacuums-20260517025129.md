## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Robot Mop?

- **Busy Professionals:** If you work long hours and want floors cleaned without effort, a self-emptying model like the iRobot j9+ Combo is perfect. It requires minimal maintenance.
- **Pet Owners:** Look for high suction (5,000+ Pa) and good obstacle avoidance to avoid pet accidents. Roborock Q Revo Max and Ecovacs X2 Omni are strong choices.
- **Families with Kids:** You need robust mopping for spills and stains. The Roborock’s rotating pads excel here.
- **Tech Enthusiasts:** If you love smart home gadgets, the Ecovacs X2 Omni with its voice assistant and camera is a fun, innovative option.
- **Apartment Dwellers:** Smaller spaces can get by with a budget model, but the self-cleaning base is still worth it for convenience.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $500:** You’ll find basic models without self-emptying bases, like the Roomba i5+ or Roborock Q5. These require manual dustbin emptying and mop pad washing. Good for small apartments or occasional use.
- **$500–$900:** Mid-range options like the Roborock Q Revo Max offer excellent mopping and self-maintenance. This is the sweet spot for most buyers.
- **$900–$1,300:** Premium models like the iRobot j9+ Combo and Ecovacs X2 Omni provide top-tier performance, obstacle avoidance, and smart features. Best for large homes or those who want zero maintenance.

**Tip:** Factor in replacement costs for mop pads (every 3–6 months) and filters (every 6–12 months). Budget around $50–$100 per year for consumables.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Process

Setting up a robot mop in 2026 is straightforward:

1. **Unbox and Charge:** Place the robot on its base and let it charge fully (2–4 hours).
2. **Download the App:** Install the manufacturer’s app (iRobot Home, Roborock, or Ecovacs) and create an account.
3. **Connect to Wi-Fi:** Follow in-app instructions to connect the robot to your 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network.
4. **Map Your Home:** The robot will perform a mapping run, scanning rooms and identifying obstacles. This takes 30–60 minutes.
5. **Set No-Go Zones:** Use the app to mark areas you want the robot to avoid (e.g., pet bowls, cords).
6. **Schedule Cleaning:** Set daily or weekly schedules. Most apps allow room-specific cleaning.
7. **Integrate with Smart Home:** Link to Alexa, Google Assistant, or Apple HomeKit via the app’s settings.

### Smart Home Integration Tips

- **Voice Commands:** Use phrases like “Alexa, ask Roomba to clean the kitchen” or “Hey Google, start the Roborock in the living room.”
- **Automation:** Create routines like “When I leave home, start the vacuum” using IFTTT or the app’s built-in automations.
- **Sensor Triggers:** Some models work with smart sensors (e.g., motion detectors) to start cleaning when a room is empty.
- **Privacy:** For camera-equipped models (iRobot j9+, Ecovacs X2), disable video monitoring when not needed. Check app permissions to limit data sharing.

**Pro Tip:** Place the self-emptying base near a wall outlet and away from direct sunlight. Ensure the base has at least 2 feet of clearance on each side for the robot to dock easily.
