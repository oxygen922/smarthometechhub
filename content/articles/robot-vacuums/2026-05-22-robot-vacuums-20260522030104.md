# The Ultimate Guide to Robot Vacuum Cleaners in 2026: Top Picks, Features, and Buying Advice

- **Suction Power:** For homes with carpets, aim for at least 5,000Pa. Hardwood floors require less suction but benefit from strong airflow.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation

Setting up a robot vacuum is straightforward:
1. **Unbox and charge:** Most models take 2–4 hours to fully charge.
2. **Download the app:** iOS or Android app guides you through Wi-Fi setup (2.4GHz network recommended).
3. **Place the dock:** Ensure the dock has at least 1.5 feet of clearance on each side and 3 feet in front.
4. **Map your home:** Run the initial cleaning cycle to generate a floor plan. Most vacuums take 2–3 runs to create an accurate map.
5. **Set no-go zones:** Use the app to block off areas like pet bowls or charging stations.

### Smart Home Integration

- **Amazon Alexa:** Use voice commands like “Alexa, ask Roomba to start cleaning the kitchen.”
- **Google Assistant:** “Hey Google, tell Roborock to vacuum the living room.”
- **Apple HomeKit:** Supported on select models (e.g., Roborock S8 MaxV Ultra), allowing Siri control.
- **Routines:** Automate cleaning based on time (e.g., “Clean at 10 AM daily”) or triggers (e.g., “When I leave home, start vacuuming”).

**Pro Tip:** For seamless integration, ensure your Wi-Fi router supports 2.4GHz, as many robot vacuums don’t support 5GHz networks.
