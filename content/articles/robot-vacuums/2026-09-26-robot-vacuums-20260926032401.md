## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Robot Mop?

Robot mops are ideal for:
- **Busy professionals** who want to maintain clean floors without daily effort.
- **Pet owners** dealing with paw prints, hair, and occasional accidents.
- **Families with young children** who need frequent sanitization of play areas.
- **Seniors or individuals with mobility issues** who find traditional mopping difficult.
- **Smart home enthusiasts** who want to integrate cleaning into their automated routines.

However, robot mops are not for everyone. If your home is cluttered with cables, rugs, and tight spaces, a robot mop may struggle. They also require regular maintenance—emptying water tanks, cleaning brushes, and replacing pads.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $300:** Basic robot mops with limited navigation and no self-cleaning. Good for small apartments with light mopping needs.
- **$300–$600:** Mid-range models like the iLux MopMaster 700 offer solid performance, larger tanks, and better navigation.
- **$600–$1,000:** Premium models like the Samsung JetBot and RoboClean AquaPro X9 include advanced features like self-cleaning docks, AI obstacle avoidance, and stronger suction.
- **$1,000+:** High-end models with all the bells and whistles, including self-emptying, self-washing, and multi-floor mapping.

Consider long-term costs: replacement mop pads, filters, and cleaning solutions can add $50–$150 per year.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

Setting up a robot mop is generally straightforward:

1. **Unbox and charge:** Most models come partially charged. Fully charge before first use.
2. **Install the app:** Download the manufacturer's app and create an account.
3. **Connect to Wi-Fi:** Ensure your robot is on a 2.4GHz network (most don't support 5GHz).
4. **Map your home:** Run a mapping cycle. The robot will create a floor plan you can customize.
5. **Set no-go zones:** Use the app to block off areas like pet bowls or cable clusters.
6. **Integrate with smart home:** Link to Alexa, Google Assistant, or Apple HomeKit for voice control. For Samsung, use SmartThings.

**Smart Home Integration Tips:**
- Create routines: "Alexa, start mopping the kitchen."
- Schedule cleanings when you're away.
- Use IFTTT for advanced automations (e.g., start mopping when you leave home).
- Pair with smart sensors: Some robots can trigger cleaning when a leak sensor detects water.

**Installation Considerations:**
- The dock needs access to a power outlet and space for water tanks.
- Keep floors clear of small objects before running.
- For multi-floor homes, you may need to carry the robot between levels unless it supports multi-floor mapping with a base on each floor.
