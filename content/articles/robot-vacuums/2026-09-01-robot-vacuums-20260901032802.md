## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy What

**First-Time Buyers:** If you're new to robot vacuums, we recommend starting with a mid-range model like the Roborock Q5 Pro+ ($449) or the iRobot Roomba i5+ ($499). These offer solid navigation, decent suction, and auto-empty functionality without the premium price tag. You'll learn your preferences and can upgrade later if needed.

**Pet Owners:** Prioritize models with dual rubber brushes (Roborock, Dreame) or the iRobot's new Multi-Surface Rubber Brushes. These prevent hair tangling significantly better than bristle brushes. The Dreame L20 Ultra and Roborock S8 MaxV Ultra are our top picks for pet households.

**Multi-Story Homes:** Look for models with advanced mapping that can store multiple floor plans. The Roborock and Dreame apps handle this exceptionally well, automatically detecting which floor the robot is on and loading the correct map.

**Budget-Conscious Shoppers:** The best time to buy is during Labor Day sales, where we expect 25-30% discounts on premium models. If you can stretch your budget to $700-800 during the sale period, you'll get significantly better technology than what's available at the $400-500 price point.

### Budget Considerations

- **Entry Level ($250-400):** Basic robot vacuums with random navigation. These will clean, but inefficiently. Expect frequent missed spots and potential for the robot to get stuck.
- **Mid-Range ($400-800):** The sweet spot for most buyers. You get LiDAR navigation, app control, and often an auto-empty dock. Perfect for routine maintenance cleaning.
- **Premium ($800-1,200):** Flagship-level cleaning performance with advanced obstacle avoidance, mopping, and self-cleaning features. Ideal for busy households that want minimal interaction with their vacuum.
- **Ultra-Premium ($1,200+):** The absolute best technology available. If you have a large home, pets, and value convenience above all else, these models justify their cost through time savings and superior cleaning.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Setting Up Your Robot Vacuum

Modern robot vacuums are remarkably easy to set up, but there are a few steps to ensure optimal performance:

**Step 1: Unboxing and Placement**
Choose a location for the charging dock that provides at least 3 feet of clearance on either side and 5 feet in front. Avoid placing it under furniture or in tight corners. The dock should be on a hard, level surface.

**Step 2: Initial Setup**
Download the manufacturer's app and follow the on-screen instructions to connect the robot to your Wi-Fi network. Most models support 2.4GHz networks; some newer models also support 5GHz. The setup process typically takes 5-10 minutes.

**Step 3: Creating Your Map**
Allow the robot to complete a "mapping run" before you set any cleaning schedules. This initial pass creates a detailed map of your home, identifying rooms, obstacles, and flooring types. For best results, clear the floors of loose cables and small objects before this run.

**Step 4: Customizing Zones**
Once the map is created, use the app to set up:
- **No-Go Zones:** Keep the robot away from areas with fragile objects or pet food bowls.
- **Room Dividers:** Set specific cleaning modes for each room (e.g., "vacuum only" for carpeted bedrooms, "vacuum + mop" for hard-surface kitchens).
- **Schedules:** Program daily or weekly cleaning times that work with your routine.

### Smart Home Ecosystem Integration

All three recommended models work with major smart home platforms, but the integration depth varies:

**Amazon Alexa:** Ask Alexa to "start the robot vacuum" or "tell the vacuum to clean the kitchen." You can also create routines—for example, "When I leave for work, start the vacuum."

**Google Home:** Similar voice commands are available, and the integration is generally seamless. You can add the vacuum to your morning routine alongside your smart lights and thermostat.

**Apple HomeKit:** Limited support—only select models from iRobot and Roborock offer HomeKit compatibility. If you're heavily invested in the Apple ecosystem, verify compatibility before purchasing.

**IFTTT (If This Then That):** Advanced users can create custom automations, such as "If the weather forecast predicts rain, skip today's vacuuming" or "When the front door unlocks, pause the robot."

**Pro Tip:** Set up a "vacation mode" that runs your robot vacuum daily while you're away. Not only does this keep your home clean, but it also gives the appearance that someone is home—a subtle security benefit.
