## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Setting Up Your Robot Vacuum

Installation is surprisingly simple for most models. Here's what to expect:

1. **Unboxing:** Remove all packaging, including protective tape on sensors and the charging base.
2. **Charging Base Placement:** Find a location with at least 3 feet of clearance on each side and 5 feet in front. Avoid placing it in direct sunlight or near heat sources.
3. **First Charge:** Most models require 3-4 hours for the initial full charge. The app will guide you through this.
4. **Mapping Run:** The first cleaning session is a mapping run. Let the robot explore your entire home without interference. This takes 30-60 minutes depending on home size.
5. **App Configuration:** Set up no-go zones (for areas like pet bowls or kids' play areas), schedule cleanings, and connect to your smart home assistant.

### Smart Home Integration Tips

- **Voice Commands:** Most models work with "Alexa, start [robot name]" or "Hey Google, clean the living room." For Siri, you may need to create shortcuts through the Shortcuts app.
- **Routines:** Create morning routines that start the vacuum while you're getting ready. I have a "Leaving for Work" routine that cleans the entire house.
- **Geofencing:** Some apps allow the vacuum to start cleaning when you leave home and stop when you return. This is a game-changer for pet owners.
- **Notifications:** Enable push notifications for when the vacuum finishes cleaning, gets stuck, or needs maintenance.

### Common Installation Issues and Solutions

- **Wi-Fi Connectivity:** If the robot won't connect, ensure you're using a 2.4GHz network (many robots don't support 5GHz). Move the robot closer to your router during setup.
- **Thresholds:** If your robot struggles with high thresholds (over 0.8 inches), consider threshold ramps available from third-party sellers.
- **Carpet Fringes:** Some robots get tangled in carpet fringes. Use no-go zones or trim fringes to prevent this.
