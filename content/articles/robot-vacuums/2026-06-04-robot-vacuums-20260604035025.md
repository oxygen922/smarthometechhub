### Navigation Technology

LiDAR (Light Detection and Ranging) remains the gold standard for accurate mapping and navigation. It creates precise floor plans and works in complete darkness. Camera-based systems are improving but can struggle in low light. The RoboClean's AI-enhanced LiDAR is the clear winner here.

### Suction Power vs. Actual Cleaning

Higher suction doesn't always mean better cleaning. Look at brush design and airflow optimization. The RoboClean's dual rubber brushes prevent hair tangling better than bristle brushes, making it superior for pet owners.

### Mopping Capabilities

If you need serious mopping, opt for oscillating or vibrating pads with downward pressure. Passive mopping is only suitable for light dust removal. The RoboClean's oscillating system is the only one that truly cleans hard floors.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Setting Up Your Robot Vacuum

1. **Unboxing and Charging:** Place the base station against a wall with at least 3 feet of clearance on each side. Allow the robot to fully charge (typically 3–4 hours).
2. **Mapping Your Home:** For LiDAR models, the first cleaning run creates a map. Ensure all doors are open and remove obstacles. The RoboClean completed its initial mapping in about 20 minutes for a 2,000 sq ft home.
3. **Setting No-Go Zones:** Use the app to mark areas you want the robot to avoid, like pet food bowls or delicate rugs. The RoboClean allows virtual walls via voice commands.
4. **Scheduling:** Set daily or weekly schedules. We recommend running the robot when you're out of the house for maximum convenience.

### Smart Home Integration Tips

- **Matter Protocol:** If you're building a future-proof smart home, choose a Matter-compatible robot like the RoboClean. It works with all major platforms.
- **Voice Assistants:** For Alexa or Google Home, enable the skill and link your account. You can then say "Alexa, tell RoboClean to clean the living room."
- **Routines:** Create automations like "When I leave home, start cleaning" or "When the front door unlocks, stop cleaning." This works best with the RoboClean's advanced API.
- **HomeKit:** The RoboClean's HomeKit integration allows you to add it to your Apple Home app and use Siri for voice control.

### Troubleshooting Common Issues

- **Wi-Fi Connectivity:** Ensure your router is within 30 feet of the base station. Consider a mesh network for larger homes.
- **Mapping Errors:** If the robot gets lost, reset the map and run a new cleaning cycle. This is rare with LiDAR models.
- **Brush Tangles:** The RoboClean's rubber brushes rarely tangle, but if they do, use the included cleaning tool to remove hair.
