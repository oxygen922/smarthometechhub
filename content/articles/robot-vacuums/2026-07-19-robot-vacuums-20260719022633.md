## Key Features to Consider

When shopping for a robot vacuum, not all features are created equal. Here’s what you should prioritize based on your home’s needs.

| Feature | Why It Matters | Best For | Budget Tip |

| **Suction Power (Pa)** | Determines how well the vacuum picks up debris from carpets and hard floors. Higher is better for thick carpets. | Homes with carpets or pets | 2,000+ Pa is sufficient for most homes |
| **Navigation System** | LiDAR is faster and more accurate than camera-based systems. It creates precise maps for room-by-room cleaning. | Large, multi-room homes | LiDAR models start around $400 |
| **Obstacle Avoidance** | Prevents the vacuum from getting stuck on cables, shoes, or pet waste. Essential for homes with clutter. | Families with kids or pets | Look for AI-powered avoidance |
| **Auto-Empty Dock** | Allows the vacuum to empty its dustbin into a larger bag, reducing maintenance to once every 30-60 days. | Busy households | Adds $150–$300 to the price |
| **Mopping Function** | Adds wet cleaning capability. Look for vibrating or rotating pads for better stain removal. | Homes with hard floors | Basic mopping starts at $300; advanced at $800+ |
| **Battery Life** | Longer runtime means the vacuum can clean larger areas on a single charge. | Homes over 2,000 sq ft | Aim for 120+ minutes |
| **Smart Home Integration** | Compatibility with Alexa, Google Assistant, or Apple HomeKit allows voice control and automation routines. | Tech-savvy users | Most models support Alexa and Google |

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

Setting up a modern robot vacuum is surprisingly simple. Here’s a step-by-step overview:

1. **Unbox and place the dock:** Find a spot with Wi-Fi coverage and at least 3 feet of clearance on each side. Plug it in.
2. **Charge the vacuum:** Most models come partially charged. Let it top off fully before the first run.
3. **Download the app:** Connect the vacuum to your home Wi-Fi network via the manufacturer’s app. Follow the on-screen instructions.
4. **Create a map:** Allow the vacuum to perform a “mapping run” to learn your floor plan. This takes 20–60 minutes depending on home size.
5. **Set no-go zones:** In the app, draw virtual boundaries to keep the vacuum away from pet bowls, children’s play areas, or fragile items.
6. **Schedule cleaning:** Set a daily or weekly schedule. Most models allow room-specific cleaning times.
7. **Integrate with smart home:** Link the vacuum to Alexa or Google Assistant for voice control. For example, “Alexa, ask Roomba to clean the kitchen.”

**Smart Home Tips:**
- Use a smart plug for the dock to automate power cycles if needed.
- Create routines like “Goodnight” that trigger the vacuum to clean after everyone goes to bed.
- Enable “pet mode” if available, which reduces suction near pet beds to avoid startling them.
