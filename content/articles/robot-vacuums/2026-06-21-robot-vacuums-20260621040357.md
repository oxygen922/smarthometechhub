## Key Features to Consider (with Comparison Table)

When shopping for a robot mop, focus on these critical features:

- **Mopping System:** Oscillating pads, rotating brushes, or vibrating plates? Oscillating pads offer deep scrubbing, while rotating brushes are better for edges.
- **Navigation:** LiDAR is more accurate in dark rooms, while camera-based systems handle object recognition better. Hybrid systems combine both.
- **Self-Cleaning Base:** Models that wash and dry mop pads reduce manual labor. Some also empty dustbins and refill water tanks.
- **Suction Power:** Higher Pa (Pascal) ratings improve carpet cleaning. Look for at least 3,000 Pa for mixed flooring.
- **Battery Life:** Aim for 150+ minutes to cover large homes without recharging.
- **Smart Home Integration:** Compatibility with Alexa, Google Assistant, Apple HomeKit, or IFTTT enables voice control and automation routines.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

Setting up a robot mop is straightforward, but smart home integration adds convenience. Here’s a step-by-step guide:

1. **Unbox and Charge:** Place the robot on its charging dock and let it fully charge (typically 3-4 hours). Install the app on your smartphone.
2. **Map Your Home:** Run a “quick map” cycle. Most robots use LiDAR or cameras to create a digital floor plan. Walk through to ensure no cords or obstacles are present.
3. **Set No-Go Zones:** In the app, mark areas like pet beds, rug fringes, or thresholds to prevent the robot from getting stuck.
4. **Connect to Smart Home:** For Alexa, enable the skill and link your account. For Google Home, add the device via the app. Commands like “Alexa, tell RoboClean to mop the kitchen” work seamlessly.
5. **Create Routines:** Use IFTTT or native app features to schedule mopping after vacuuming, or trigger cleaning when you leave home (via geofencing).
6. **Maintenance:** Empty dustbins, refill water tanks, and clean mop pads weekly. Self-cleaning bases reduce this to monthly checks.

**Integration Tips:** Place the dock near a Wi-Fi router for stable connectivity. Avoid areas with direct sunlight, as it can interfere with sensors. For multi-story homes, most robots save up to three maps—just move the dock between floors.
