## Buying Guide

- **Pet Owners:** The Roborock S8 Pro Ultra+ is our top pick for pet hair, thanks to its dual rubber brushes that resist tangling. The iRobot j9+ Combo is also great for avoiding pet accidents.
- **Families with Kids:** The iRobot j9+ Combo’s excellent object avoidance helps avoid toys and clutter, while its retractable mop handles spills.
- **Small Apartment Dwellers:** A budget-friendly model like the Roborock Q5 Pro (not reviewed here) offers good performance for under $500.
- **Tech Enthusiasts:** For the latest innovations, the Dyson Vis Nav Pro’s AI mapping and Roborock’s self-cleaning dock are cutting-edge.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $500:** Look for basic models with LiDAR navigation and self-emptying bases (e.g., Roborock Q5 Pro, iRobot Roomba i3+).
- **$500–$900:** Mid-range options like the iRobot j9+ Combo offer mopping and smart avoidance.
- **$1,000 and above:** Premium models like the Dyson Vis Nav Pro and Roborock S8 Pro Ultra+ deliver top performance and automation.

**Pro Tip:** Consider the long-term cost of replacement parts (e.g., brushes, filters, mop pads). Roborock and iRobot have affordable accessories, while Dyson’s parts are pricier.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Setup Process

Most robot vacuums in 2026 are easy to set up. Here’s a typical process:

1. **Unbox and Charge:** Remove the robot and charging dock. Place the dock against a wall with clear space on both sides (at least 1.5 feet). Plug it in and let the robot charge fully (2–3 hours).
2. **Connect to App:** Download the manufacturer’s app (e.g., Dyson Link, Roborock App, iRobot Home). Create an account and follow the on-screen instructions to connect via Wi-Fi (2.4 GHz is required for most models).
3. **Map Your Home:** Run a “quick map” or “training” cycle. The robot will explore your home, creating a floor plan. You can then label rooms, set no-go zones, and adjust cleaning preferences.
4. **Integrate with Smart Home:** Link the app to your voice assistant (Alexa, Google Home, or Apple HomeKit). For example, say, “Alexa, ask Roomba to start cleaning the kitchen.”
5. **Schedule Cleanings:** Set daily or weekly schedules in the app. Most models allow you to specify rooms and cleaning modes (e.g., “quiet” for nighttime).

### Smart Home Integration Tips

- **Voice Commands:** Use natural language like “Hey Google, start the Roborock” or “Siri, ask the Dyson to vacuum the living room.”
- **Automation:** Create routines in Alexa or Google Home (e.g., “When I leave home, start the robot vacuum”).
- **Notifications:** Enable app alerts for when cleaning is complete, the bin is full, or the battery is low.
- **Third-Party Compatibility:** Most models work with IFTTT (If This Then That) for advanced automation, but check compatibility on the manufacturer’s website.

**Common Pitfalls:** Ensure your Wi-Fi is strong near the dock. Avoid placing the dock in direct sunlight or near heat sources. For mopping models, use the recommended cleaning solution to avoid damaging floors.
