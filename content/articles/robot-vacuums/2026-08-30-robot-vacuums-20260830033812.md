## Key Features to Consider

When shopping for a robot vacuum in 2026, these are the features that actually matter:

### Suction Power
Measured in Pascals (Pa), this determines how effectively the vacuum picks up debris. Entry-level models offer 2,000-3,000Pa, mid-range models offer 5,000-8,000Pa, and premium models exceed 10,000Pa. For most homes, 5,000Pa is sufficient; for homes with pets or high-pile carpet, look for 8,000Pa or more.

### Navigation Technology
- **Random Navigation:** Older technology that bounces around the room. Avoid these unless budget is your only consideration.
- **Gyroscope Navigation:** Better than random but still inefficient.
- **LiDAR Mapping:** Uses lasers to create a precise map of your home. This is the gold standard—look for it.
- **Camera-Based Navigation:** Uses visual sensors to navigate. Works well but can struggle in low-light conditions.

### Obstacle Avoidance
AI-powered obstacle avoidance uses cameras and sensors to identify and avoid objects like phone chargers, pet waste, and toys. This is crucial if you have a cluttered home or pets that leave surprises.

### Self-Emptying Base
A self-emptying base means the vacuum automatically empties its dust bin into a larger container in the base station. You'll only need to empty the base every 30-60 days instead of after every cleaning session.

### Mopping Capabilities
If you want mopping, look for models with:
- **Rotating Mop Pads:** More effective at scrubbing than dragging.
- **Auto-Lift:** The mop lifts when transitioning to carpet.
- **Self-Cleaning:** The dock washes and dries the mop pads.

### Smart Home Integration
Most models work with Alexa and Google Home. Apple HomeKit support is less common but available on select models like the Roborock Qrevo Master.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Getting Started

Setting up a modern robot vacuum takes about 15-20 minutes:

1. **Unbox and place the dock:** Choose a location with WiFi coverage and clearance on all sides.
2. **Connect to the app:** Download the manufacturer's app, create an account, and connect the vacuum to your 2.4GHz WiFi network.
3. **Charge and map:** Let the vacuum charge fully, then run a mapping cycle. Most models will map your entire home in one session.
4. **Set up no-go zones:** Use the map to set virtual boundaries for areas you don't want the vacuum to go (pet food bowls, children's play areas, etc.).
5. **Schedule cleaning:** Set a daily or weekly schedule that works with your routine.

### Smart Home Integration

**Alexa and Google Home:** All three models support basic voice commands like "Alexa, tell Roborock to vacuum the living room." For more advanced integration, you can create routines that trigger cleaning based on other smart home events—for example, "When the front door locks, start vacuuming."

**Apple HomeKit:** Only the Roborock Qrevo Master supports HomeKit, which is a significant advantage for Apple-centric households. With HomeKit, you can integrate the vacuum into scenes like "Good Morning" or "Leaving Home."

**IFTTT and Automation:** All three models support IFTTT, allowing you to create custom automations. For example, you can have the vacuum start cleaning when your smart thermostat detects you've left the house or when your security camera stops detecting motion.

**Pro Tip:** For the best smart home experience, consider the ecosystem you're already invested in. If you're all-in on Apple, the HomeKit support on the Roborock is a major advantage. If you're using Alexa or Google Home, all three models will work equally well.
