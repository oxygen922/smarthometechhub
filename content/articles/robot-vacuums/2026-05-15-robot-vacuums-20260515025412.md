## Key Features to Consider (With Comparison Table)

When shopping for a robot mop, not all features are created equal. Here’s what matters most:

- **Mopping System:** Look for rotating or vibrating pads that scrub, not just drag. Auto-lift mop pads are essential if you have carpets.
- **Dock Functionality:** Self-washing and self-drying docks save you time and mess. Self-emptying docks are great for vacuuming but less critical for mopping.
- **Navigation:** LiDAR is the gold standard for speed and accuracy. Camera-based systems (like iRobot’s) offer better obstacle avoidance.
- **Suction Power:** Above 4,000Pa is ideal for pet hair and deep cleaning. Lower is fine for hard floors only.
- **Smart Home Integration:** Ensure compatibility with your ecosystem (Alexa, Google, Apple HomeKit, etc.).

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Robot Mop?

- **Pet Owners:** If you have dogs or cats, a robot mop is a lifesaver. It handles muddy paw prints, drool, and occasional accidents. Look for strong suction (4,500Pa+) and good obstacle avoidance to avoid pet waste disasters.
- **Busy Professionals:** If you’re short on time, a self-washing dock (like the Roborock or DreameBot) is a must. It eliminates the chore of cleaning the mop pads.
- **Families with Kids:** Robot mops are great for daily maintenance of crumbs and spills. The iRobot’s obstacle avoidance is ideal for toy-laden floors.
- **Apartment Dwellers:** For smaller spaces, a mid-range model like the DreameBot is sufficient. Its compact dock fits in tight corners.
- **Carpet Homes:** Only consider models with auto-lift mop pads (all three above have this). Avoid models that drag a wet pad over carpet.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $500:** You’ll find basic robot mops with no self-cleaning dock and weaker suction. These are fine for small, hard-floor-only homes but require more manual work.
- **$700 – $1,000:** This is the sweet spot. Models like the DreameBot L10s Ultra offer premium features (self-washing dock, strong suction) at a reasonable price.
- **$1,000 – $1,400:** Flagship models like the Roborock S8 Pro Ultra and iRobot Combo j9+ deliver the best performance, longest battery life, and most advanced features. Worth it if you want the absolute best.

### Installation & Smart Home Integration

Setting up a robot mop is straightforward, but there are a few key steps:

1. **Unboxing and Charging:** Place the dock on a hard, level surface with at least 2 feet of clearance on each side. Plug it in and let the robot charge fully (usually 3-4 hours).
2. **Mapping Your Home:** The first run will create a map. Clear the floor of loose items, cables, and small objects. Most robots use LiDAR or cameras to map in under 10 minutes.
3. **Setting No-Go Zones:** Use the app to set virtual walls or no-mop zones (e.g., under furniture, near electronics). This is critical to avoid damage.
4. **Smart Home Integration:** Connect to your preferred voice assistant via the app. For example, say “Alexa, start mopping the kitchen” after linking your robot.

**Tips for Seamless Integration:**
- Ensure your Wi-Fi is strong (2.4GHz band is often required).
- Update the robot’s firmware after setup for best performance.
- Use routines: For example, set a schedule via Google Home to mop every Monday and Thursday at 10 AM.
