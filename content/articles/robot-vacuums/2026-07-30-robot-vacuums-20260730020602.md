## Key Features to Consider

When choosing a robot vacuum, not all features are created equal. Here’s what matters most in 2026:

### Navigation & Mapping
- **LiDAR vs. Camera:** LiDAR (laser) navigation is faster and works in the dark. Camera-based systems (like iRobot’s) can identify objects but struggle in low light.
- **Mapping:** Look for models that save multiple floor plans and allow no-go zones.

### Suction Power
- **Measured in Pascals (Pa):** 2,500–3,000Pa is good for hard floors; 5,000Pa+ is needed for carpets. The Roborock S10 Pro Ultra leads at 6,000Pa.

### Mopping
- **Basic vs. Sonic vs. Rotating:** Basic mopping drags a wet cloth. Sonic scrubbing (Roborock) vibrates the pad. Rotating pads (Dreame) spin for better cleaning. All three are better than no mopping at all.

### Self-Emptying
- **Bag vs. Bagless:** Bagless systems (like iRobot’s) save money long-term but require more frequent cleaning of the dock. Bagged systems are cleaner but add recurring costs.

### Battery Life
- **Typical range:** 90–180 minutes. Larger homes (2,000+ sq ft) need at least 150 minutes.

### Smart Home Integration
- **Alexa, Google, HomeKit:** Most support at least two. HomeKit is still rare but growing (Roborock added it in 2025).

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy What?

- **You live in a large home (2,000+ sq ft) with mixed flooring:** Get the Roborock S10 Pro Ultra. Its long battery life, powerful suction, and advanced mopping handle everything from hardwood to area rugs.
- **You have pets and allergies:** Choose the iRobot Roomba j9+. The HEPA filter and pet-hair-specific mode make it a lifesaver for shedding season.
- **You're a renter or first-time buyer:** The DreameBot L40 Ultra gives you 90% of the premium experience at 60% of the cost. Perfect for apartments and smaller homes.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $500:** Look for mid-range models like the Roborock Q5 Pro or Eufy X10. You'll get good suction and mapping but no self-emptying or advanced mopping.
- **$500–$1,000:** The sweet spot. You get self-emptying docks, strong suction, and decent mopping. The DreameBot L40 Ultra is the best value here.
- **Over $1,000:** Flagship territory. Expect the best navigation, longest battery life, and premium mopping. The Roborock S10 Pro Ultra is the clear winner.

### Pro Tip
Don't ignore the "refurbished" market. Many premium models from 2024–2025 (like the Roomba j7+ or Roborock S8 Pro Ultra) can be found for 30–40% off and still perform excellently.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Unboxing and Setup
All three models take about 15–20 minutes to set up. You’ll need to:
1. Remove packaging tape and install side brushes.
2. Place the dock near a wall outlet (leave 1.5 feet of clearance on each side).
3. Connect to Wi-Fi (2.4GHz required for most).
4. Run an initial mapping cycle (20–30 minutes).

### Smart Home Tips
- **Voice Commands:** "Alexa, ask Roborock to clean the kitchen." Works out of the box for all three.
- **Routines:** Use Google Home or Apple Shortcuts to schedule cleaning when you leave for work.
- **Integration with Other Devices:** If you have a smart lock (like August or Yale), you can set the vacuum to start when you lock the door. This is possible via IFTTT or native app automations.

### What to Watch Out For
- **Wi-Fi Range:** If your router is far from the dock, consider a mesh system. Robot vacuums often struggle with weak signals.
- **Floor Transition:** Thick carpets (1 inch+) can stall some models. The Roborock handles them best; the Dreame may need a no-go zone.
- **Pet Hazards:** Always pick up small toys and cords before running. Even the best obstacle avoidance isn't perfect.
