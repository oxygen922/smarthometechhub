## Key Features to Consider

Before purchasing a robot mop in 2026, you must understand the core technologies that define the experience. Here is a breakdown of what matters most.

### 1. Mopping Mechanism
- **Vibrating Pad (e.g., Roborock S8):** Uses a flat pad that vibrates rapidly. Great for scrubbing, but requires the robot to pass over an area multiple times.
- **Dual Spinning Pads (e.g., Dreame X40):** Two round pads spin at high RPM, pressing down on the floor. Generally better for lifting stuck-on grime.
- **Single Spinning Pad (e.g., Ecovacs T30):** A compromise, often lacking the pressure of dual systems.

### 2. The Dock (Self-Cleaning vs. Basic)
This is the most critical decision point. A "Basic" dock requires you to manually remove the water tank and clean the mop pads after every use. A "Ultra" or "Omni" dock automatically washes the pads with hot water and dries them. **We strongly recommend choosing a model with a self-cleaning dock.** Without it, the robot mop becomes a chore in itself.

### 3. Obstacle Avoidance
- **Lidar Mapping:** Essential for navigation and efficient cleaning paths.
- **AI Camera (Visual):** Allows the robot to recognize and avoid obstacles like cables, shoes, and pet waste. *This is non-negotiable in 2026.* Without it, you risk the robot smearing pet waste across your entire floor.

### 4. Carpet Detection
If you have a mix of carpet and hard floors, look for a model with **retractable mop pads** or a **mop-lifting mechanism**. This prevents the wet pad from dragging across your carpet and ruining it.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

**Installation:**
All three units require similar setup. You need to find a location for the dock near a power outlet. The dock will have a clean water tank and a dirty water tank that you need to fill and empty manually (roughly every 3-5 days for a standard 1,500 sq ft home).

1.  **Unboxing:** Remove all tape and install the side brushes (and the FlexiArm brush on the Roborock).
2.  **App Setup:** Download the respective app (Roborock, Dreame, or Ecovacs). The app will guide you through connecting to your 2.4GHz Wi-Fi network.
3.  **Mapping:** Do not start cleaning immediately. Let the robot do a "Mapping Run" first. This allows it to scan your home without cleaning, creating a precise map.
4.  **Calibration:** Set your "No-Go Zones" (e.g., in front of pet bowls) and "Invisible Walls" (e.g., for a baby's room).

**Smart Home Integration:**
- **Amazon Alexa & Google Home:** All three models work flawlessly with both ecosystems. You can say, "Alexa, ask Roborock to mop the kitchen," and it will trigger a specific routine.
- **Apple HomeKit:** *Caveat:* Neither the Dreame nor the Ecovacs natively supports HomeKit (as of early 2026). The Roborock S8 MaxV Ultra *does* support HomeKit, allowing you to integrate it into your Apple automation scenes (e.g., "Good Night" scene triggers a mop run).
- **Automation:** We recommend setting up a schedule where the robot mops the kitchen 30 minutes after your dinner time, ensuring the floors are clean before breakfast without you having to lift a finger.
