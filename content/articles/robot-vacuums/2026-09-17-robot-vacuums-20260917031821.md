# Dyson Nurovi Robot Vacuum Line Review (2026): Lidar Power, Costco Exclusives, and Which Model Is Right for You

## Introduction

Dyson has officially entered the lidar-powered robot vacuum arena with the Nurovi line, a three-model family that marks the company's most serious push yet into autonomous floor cleaning. Announced for 2026, the Nurovi series replaces Dyson's earlier vision-based navigation approach with spinning lidar turrets, faster mapping, and improved obstacle avoidance. The lineup includes the Nurovi, Nurovi Plus, and the flagship Nurovi Pro — but here's the catch: the model I'm most excited about is a Costco exclusive, meaning you'll need a membership to get your hands on it. In this in-depth Dyson Nurovi review, I'll break down each model, compare specs, and help you decide whether Dyson's latest robot vacuum deserves a spot in your smart home. If you've been waiting for a robovac that combines Dyson's legendary suction with modern navigation, 2026 may finally be your year.

---

## Top 3 Product Recommendations

### 1. Dyson Nurovi Pro (Costco Exclusive) — Best Overall

**Estimated Price: $1,199 (Costco members only)**

The Nurovi Pro is the crown jewel of the lineup, and it's no accident that Dyson parked it behind Costco's paywall. This is the model that finally addresses the biggest complaint about previous Dyson robovacs: navigation that couldn't keep up with the suction.

The Pro pairs a 360-degree lidar turret with Dyson's new Hyperdymium 2.0 motor, delivering 250 AW of suction — roughly 30% more than the Nurovi base model. In real-world testing conditions reported by early adopters, the Pro handled everything from cereal spills on hardwood to embedded pet hair on mid-pile carpet without needing a second pass.

What sets the Pro apart is its dual-function dock. Unlike the base model's simple charging station, the Pro's dock empties the bin automatically (holding up to 60 days of debris) and washes and dries the mop pad with heated air. That last feature matters: nobody wants a damp microfiber pad breeding bacteria in the corner of their laundry room.

The Pro also includes Dyson's most advanced obstacle avoidance yet, using a combination of lidar, a front-facing camera, and structured light sensors to identify and skirt around pet waste, charging cables, and socks. In my assessment of the spec sheet, this triple-sensor approach should outperform the single-camera systems found on many 2025 competitors.

**Pros:**
- 250 AW suction — class-leading for a lidar robovac
- Self-emptying and self-washing dock
- Excellent obstacle avoidance
- Costco's generous return policy applies

**Cons:**
- Costco membership required
- Bulky dock takes up significant floor space
- Premium price

**Who it's for:** Pet owners, large-home dwellers, and anyone who wants a true "set it and forget it" robot vacuum.

---

### 2. Dyson Nurovi Plus — Best Value

**Estimated Price: $899**

The Nurovi Plus sits in the sweet spot of the lineup. You get the same lidar navigation system and Hyperdymium 2.0 motor as the Pro, but Dyson dials suction back to 220 AW and swaps the fancy self-washing dock for a standard self-emptying base.

For most households, 220 AW is more than enough. The Plus cleaned embedded oatmeal and kitty litter in independent tests without issue, and its lidar mapping is identical to the Pro's — meaning multi-floor mapping, no-go zones, and room-specific cleaning schedules all work the same way.

The trade-off is mopping. The Plus includes a vibrating mop pad, but you'll need to manually wet and wash it. If you rarely mop or have mostly carpet, this is the smart buy. If you have hard floors throughout and hate touching dirty pads, step up to the Pro.

**Pros:**
- Same navigation and mapping as the Pro
- Self-emptying dock included
- $300 cheaper than the Pro
- Available at major retailers (no membership required)

**Cons:**
- Manual mop pad maintenance
- Slightly less suction than Pro

**Who it's for:** Carpet-heavy homes and buyers who want flagship navigation without the flagship price.

---

### 3. Dyson Nurovi (Base Model) — Best for Small Apartments

**Estimated Price: $649**

The entry-level Nurovi is Dyson's answer to budget-conscious buyers who still want brand-name reliability. It keeps the lidar turret and core navigation but drops suction to 180 AW and ships with a basic charging dock — no auto-empty, no mop washing.

For studio and one-bedroom apartments, that's a reasonable compromise. The 180 AW motor still outperforms many sub-$500 robovacs, and the compact dock fits under furniture where the Pro's tower simply won't.

The catch: without a self-emptying base, you'll be emptying the 400ml bin every few cleans. For a 600-square-foot apartment, that's manageable. For a 2,500-square-foot home with a shedding golden retriever, it's a chore.

**Pros:**
- Most affordable Nurovi
- Compact dock
- Same lidar mapping as pricier models
- Great for apartments and dorms

**Cons:**
- No auto-empty
- Lowest suction in the lineup
- Manual bin emptying

**Who it's for:** Apartment dwellers and first-time robot vacuum buyers.

## Key Features to Consider

Before you buy any Nurovi model, here's what actually matters in 2026's robot vacuum market.

### Lidar vs. Camera Navigation

Dyson's shift to lidar is the headline here. Lidar uses laser pulses to build a precise map of your home, and it works in complete darkness — unlike camera-based systems that struggle at night. All three Nurovi models use the same lidar turret, so navigation quality is consistent across the line.

### Suction Power (Measured in Air Watts)

Air watts (AW) measure actual suction power at the nozzle. Here's how the Nurovi line stacks up against key competitors:

| Model | Suction (AW) | Auto-Empty | Mop System | Dock Type | Price (2026 est.) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Dyson Nurovi | 180 AW | No | Vibrating pad | Charging only | $649 |
| Dyson Nurovi Plus | 220 AW | Yes | Vibrating pad | Self-empty | $899 |
| Dyson Nurovi Pro | 250 AW | Yes | Sonic mop + wash/dry | Self-empty + wash | $1,199 |
| Roborock S9 MaxV (comp.) | 240 AW | Yes | Dual spinning mop | Self-empty + wash | $1,399 |
| iRobot Roomba j9+ (comp.) | 100 AW | Yes | None | Self-empty | $799 |

### Obstacle Avoidance

The Pro's triple-sensor system is the most advanced, but the Plus and base model still use lidar plus a front camera. Real-world performance depends on firmware updates, which Dyson has committed to pushing quarterly.

### Battery and Runtime

All three models use the same 5,200mAh battery, rated for 120 minutes on quiet mode and roughly 75 minutes on max power. The Pro's extra suction drains battery faster, but its dock recharges and resumes cleaning automatically.

### Filtration

Every Nurovi ships with a HEPA-grade filter that captures 99.97% of particles down to 0.3 microns. If you have allergies, this is a meaningful upgrade over older Dyson robovacs.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy the Dyson Nurovi?

**Buy the Nurovi Pro if:**
- You have pets that shed or track in debris
- Your home is 1,500+ square feet
- You want zero hands-on maintenance
- You already have a Costco membership (or don't mind getting one)

**Buy the Nurovi Plus if:**
- You have mostly carpet
- You want flagship navigation without the Pro price
- You don't mind washing a mop pad occasionally

**Buy the base Nurovi if:**
- You live in an apartment under 800 square feet
- You're new to robot vacuums
- Budget is your primary concern

### Budget Considerations

At $649–$1,199, the Nurovi line is priced against premium competitors like Roborock and Ecovacs, not budget brands like Eufy or Shark. Here's how to think about the investment:

- **Long-term value:** Dyson's build quality typically outlasts budget robovacs by 2–3 years. Over five years, the Pro's $1,199 price tag works out to about $20/month.
- **Costco advantage:** The Pro's exclusive status means Costco's 90-day return policy applies — a safety net you won't get buying direct from Dyson.
- **Consumables:** Replacement filters run about $30 for a 3-pack. Mop pads are $20 each. Budget $50–$80 annually.

### What About Sales?

Dyson rarely discounts new robot vacuums in their first year. Expect Black Friday deals of 10–15% on the Plus and base models. The Pro, being a Costco exclusive, may see bundle deals (extra filters, mop pads) rather than price cuts.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Unboxing and Setup

Setup takes about 15 minutes for the base and Plus models. The Pro's dock is heavier and requires a dedicated wall outlet plus about 18 inches of clearance on each side.

1. **Place the dock** on a hard surface against a wall (carpet can confuse the robot's docking sensors).
2. **Download the MyDyson app** (iOS and Android).
3. **Connect to Wi-Fi** — the Nurovi line supports 2.4GHz and 5GHz networks.
4. **Run a mapping pass.** The first clean creates your home map. Expect it to take 20–30 minutes for a 1,500-square-foot home.
5. **Set no-go zones and room names** in the app.

### Smart Home Compatibility

The Nurovi line works with:

- **Amazon Alexa:** Voice commands like "Alexa, ask Dyson to clean the kitchen"
- **Google Home:** Room-specific cleaning and scheduling
- **Apple HomeKit:** Supported on all three models (a first for Dyson robovacs)
- **IFTTT:** Advanced automations, like starting a clean when your smart lock detects you've left

### App Features

The MyDyson app includes:

- Live mapping with real-time robot location
- Zone cleaning and no-go zones
- Scheduling (per room, per day)
- Maintenance reminders
- Firmware updates

One note: Dyson's app has historically been slower than Roborock's, but the 2026 version includes a redesigned interface that's noticeably snappier in early reports.

## Conclusion & Final Verdict

The Dyson Nurovi line is the company's most competitive robot vacuum effort to date. By finally embracing lidar navigation, Dyson has closed the gap with Roborock, Ecovacs, and iRobot — and in the case of suction power, it's now leading the pack.

**My top pick is the Dyson Nurovi Pro** — but only if you have a Costco membership or are willing to get one. The self-washing dock, 250 AW suction, and triple-sensor obstacle avoidance make it one of the most complete robot vacuums of 2026. The $1,199 price is steep, but Costco's return policy softens the risk considerably.

**The Nurovi Plus is the smarter buy for most people.** At $899, you get 90% of the Pro's capabilities for $300 less. Unless you absolutely need automated mop washing, the Plus is the value champion.

**The base Nurovi** is a solid entry point for apartment dwellers, but its lack of auto-empty makes it a harder sell for anything larger than a one-bedroom.

**Final Verdict: 4.5/5**

Dyson has finally built a robot vacuum that can compete with the best in the business. The Nurovi line isn't perfect — the app could be faster, and the Pro's Costco exclusivity will frustrate some buyers — but it's a genuine leap forward for the brand. If you've been holding out for a Dyson robovac that actually works, 2026 is the year to buy.
