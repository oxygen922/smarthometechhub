## Key Features to Consider

When choosing a robot vacuum, several factors determine how well it will perform in your home. Here's what to look for:

### Suction Power
Measured in Pascals (Pa), suction power determines how well the vacuum picks up debris. Budget models offer 1,500-2,500Pa, while premium models can reach 5,000-6,000Pa. For homes with pets or carpets, aim for at least 3,000Pa.

### Navigation Technology
- **LiDAR:** Uses lasers to create precise maps. Works in the dark and is highly accurate.
- **Camera-based:** Uses visual sensors for navigation. May struggle in low light.
- **Gyroscope:** Basic navigation using rotation sensors. Less accurate but cheaper.

### Self-Emptying Capability
A self-emptying base automatically transfers debris from the vacuum's dustbin into a larger bag or container, reducing maintenance. Most models hold 30-60 days of debris.

### Mopping Functionality
Some models include a water tank and mopping pad for wet cleaning. Look for models with sonic vibration or scrubbing action for better results.

### Battery Life
Most robot vacuums run for 90-180 minutes on a single charge. Larger homes may require models with longer battery life or automatic recharge and resume.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy What?

**For Pet Owners:** The iRobot Roomba j9+ is the best choice. Its superior object avoidance prevents it from running over pet toys or accidents, and the self-emptying base means you don't have to handle fur-filled dustbins.

**For Homes with Hard Floors:** The Roborock S8 Pro Ultra is unmatched for mopping. If you have tile, hardwood, or laminate, the sonic mopping system will keep your floors spotless.

**For Budget-Conscious Buyers:** The Eufy RoboVac X10 Pro offers excellent value. It's perfect for apartments or smaller homes where you don't need premium features.

**For Tech Enthusiasts:** The Roborock S8 Pro Ultra offers the most advanced features, including self-washing mopping pads and automatic detergent dispensing.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $400:** You'll get good navigation and solid cleaning, but no self-emptying or mopping. The Eufy RoboVac X10 Pro is the best in this range.

### Floor Type Considerations

- **Hardwood/Tile:** Look for models with mopping capability and soft brushes that won't scratch floors.

- **Mixed Flooring:** Models with automatic carpet detection and adjustable suction are ideal.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Unboxing and Setup

All three recommended models are easy to set up. Here's a general process:

1. **Unbox** and remove all packaging materials.
2. **Charge** the robot vacuum fully (typically 3-4 hours).
3. **Download** the manufacturer's app and create an account.
4. **Connect** the robot to your Wi-Fi network (2.4GHz is recommended for most models).
5. **Place** the charging base in an open area with good Wi-Fi signal.
6. **Run** the first cleaning cycle to map your home.

### Smart Home Integration

**Amazon Alexa:** "Alexa, ask Roomba to start cleaning."
**Google Assistant:** "Hey Google, tell Roborock to clean the kitchen."
**Apple HomeKit:** "Hey Siri, start cleaning the living room."

For advanced automation, use the IFTTT (If This Then That) service. For example:
- "If your smart lock detects you've left, start the robot vacuum."
- "If your motion sensor detects no movement for 30 minutes, start cleaning."

### Maintenance Tips

- **Empty the dustbin** after each use if you don't have a self-emptying model.
- **Clean sensors** weekly with a dry cloth to maintain navigation accuracy.
- **Replace filters** every 2-3 months for optimal performance.
- **Check brushes** monthly for hair tangles, especially if you have pets.
- **Update firmware** regularly through the app to get new features and improvements.
