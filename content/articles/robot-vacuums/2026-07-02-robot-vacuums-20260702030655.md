## Key Features to Consider

When shopping for a robot vacuum in 2026, keep these factors in mind:

| Feature | iRobot Roomba j9+ | Roborock S8 Pro Ultra | Eufy X10 Pro Omni |
|---------|-------------------|-----------------------|-------------------|
| **Suction Power** | 2500Pa | 6000Pa | 4000Pa |
| **Navigation** | Camera + AI | LiDAR | LiDAR + 3D |
| **Self-Emptying** | Yes (60 days) | Yes (7 weeks) | Yes (45 days) |
| **Mopping** | No | Yes (VibraRise) | Yes (rotating) |
| **Battery Life** | 120 min | 180 min | 150 min |
| **Smart Home** | Alexa, Google, Apple HomeKit | Alexa, Google, Siri Shortcuts | Alexa, Google |
| **Price (2026)** | $899 | $1,199 | $549 |

**Key Considerations:**
- **Suction Power:** Higher is better for carpets and pet hair. 2500Pa is adequate for hard floors, but 4000Pa+ is ideal for rugs.
- **Navigation:** LiDAR is faster and more accurate than camera-based systems, but cameras offer better object recognition.
- **Self-Emptying:** Essential for hands-free use. Check bin capacity—larger means fewer changes.
- **Mopping:** Look for models with vibrating or rotating pads for effective cleaning. Some can lift the mop on carpets.
- **Battery Life:** Aim for at least 120 minutes to cover average homes. Larger homes need 150+ minutes.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy Which Model?

- **iRobot Roomba j9+:** Best for Apple HomeKit users, families with pets (due to strong object avoidance), and those who want a reliable, no-fuss vacuum without mopping.
- **Roborock S8 Pro Ultra:** Ideal for large homes (2000+ sq ft), pet owners who need heavy-duty suction, and users who want a fully automated mopping system.
- **Eufy X10 Pro Omni:** Great for first-time buyers, renters, or anyone on a budget who still wants self-emptying and mopping capabilities.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $600:** The Eufy X10 Pro Omni offers the best bang for your buck. It’s a complete package with self-emptying and decent mopping.
- **$600–$1,000:** The Roomba j9+ is a solid mid-range choice, especially if you prioritize smart home integration over mopping.
- **$1,000+:** The Roborock S8 Pro Ultra is the premium pick, delivering top-tier performance and automation.

Remember to factor in long-term costs like replacement filters, brushes, and mop pads. Roborock and iRobot offer subscription plans for consumables, while Eufy’s parts are generally cheaper.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation

Setting up a robot vacuum is straightforward. Most models come with a quick-start guide:
1. **Unbox and charge:** Place the robot on its dock and let it charge fully (2–4 hours).
2. **Download the app:** Create an account and follow on-screen instructions to connect via Wi-Fi (2.4GHz band required).
3. **Map your home:** Let the robot run a mapping cycle (10–20 minutes) to learn your floor plan.
4. **Customize settings:** Set no-go zones, schedules, and cleaning preferences.

**Pro Tip:** Clear floors of loose cables and small objects before the first run to ensure accurate mapping.

### Smart Home Integration

- **Alexa:** Use voice commands like “Alexa, tell Roomba to start cleaning.” All three models support this.
- **Google Home:** Similar voice controls are available for all three.
- **Apple HomeKit:** Only the Roomba j9+ offers native support. For Roborock, you’ll need a third-party bridge like Homebridge.
- **Routines:** Create automated scenes—e.g., “When I leave home, vacuum the living room.” This works seamlessly with iRobot and Roborock via their apps.

For advanced users, Roborock’s API allows integration with Home Assistant, giving you full control over custom automations.
