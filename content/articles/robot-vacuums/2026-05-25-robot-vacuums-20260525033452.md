## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Setting Up Your Robot Mop

1. **Unbox and Charge:** Remove all packaging, plug in the dock, and place the robot on it. Full charge takes 3–4 hours.
2. **Download the App:** Install the manufacturer’s app (iRobot Home, Roborock, or Narwal). Create an account and follow the on-screen instructions to connect via Wi-Fi.
3. **Mapping Run:** Let the robot perform an initial mapping run. This takes 30–60 minutes. Clear the floor of obstacles for best results.
4. **Customize Settings:** Set no-mop zones, adjust water flow, and schedule cleaning times. Enable “Carpet Boost” if available.
5. **Voice Control:** Link the app to Alexa, Google Assistant, or HomeKit. Use voice commands like “Alexa, ask Roomba to clean the kitchen.”

### Smart Home Integration Tips

- **Routines:** Create a morning routine: “Good morning, Google” triggers the robot mop to clean the kitchen while you brew coffee.
- **IFTTT:** For advanced automation, use IFTTT applets. Example: “If it rains, skip mopping today to avoid tracking mud.”
- **Geofencing:** Many apps support geofencing—the robot starts cleaning when you leave home and stops when you return.
- **Multi-Floor Homes:** Most premium models store multiple floor plans. Tell the robot which floor to clean via the app or voice command.

### Potential Issues

- **Wi-Fi Interference:** Place the dock within 30 feet of your router. Use a 2.4GHz band for stable connections.
- **Firmware Updates:** Keep the app updated for new features and bug fixes. Updates often improve obstacle avoidance.
- **Maintenance:** Clean the mop pads weekly, empty the dirty water tank, and replace filters every 3–6 months.
