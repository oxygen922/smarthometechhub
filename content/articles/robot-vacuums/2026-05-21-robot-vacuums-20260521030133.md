## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Robot Vacuum?

- **Busy professionals:** If you're out of the house 10+ hours a day, a robot vacuum can clean while you work. Models with self-emptying bins and scheduling are ideal.
- **Pet owners:** Prioritize suction power (4,000+ Pa) and tangle-free brush rolls. HEPA filters are a bonus for allergy sufferers.
- **Families with young children:** Expect frequent spills and messes. A robot vacuum with good mopping and strong suction can handle daily cleanup.
- **Seniors or those with mobility issues:** A robot vacuum eliminates bending and pushing heavy vacuums. Look for models with voice control and simple apps.
- **Tech enthusiasts:** If you love smart home integration, choose a model compatible with your ecosystem (Alexa, Google, HomeKit).

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $400:** Basic models with random navigation and no self-emptying. Suitable for small apartments with hard floors.
- **$400–$700:** Mid-range options with gyroscope or camera navigation, decent suction, and sometimes basic mopping. Great for most homes.
- **$700–$1,000:** Premium models with LiDAR, self-emptying, strong suction, and advanced mopping. Recommended for larger homes or pet owners.
- **Over $1,000:** Flagship models with all features plus extras like automatic dustbin disposal, carpet deep-cleaning, and multi-floor mapping. Only necessary for luxury homes or severe allergies.

**Pro tip:** Don't overspend on features you won't use. If you have mostly hard floors, a strong mopping model is more valuable than extreme suction. Conversely, if you have wall-to-wall carpet, prioritize suction and self-emptying.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Unboxing and Setup

Most robot vacuums arrive partially assembled. Expect to:
1. Remove packing tape and protective films.
2. Attach side brushes (some models have magnetic or snap-on designs).
3. Fill water tank (for mopping models) with clean water or approved solution.
4. Place the charging base against a wall, ensuring 3 feet of clearance on each side.
5. Plug in the base and wait for the robot to charge (typically 3–4 hours for first charge).

**Wi-Fi Connection:** Download the manufacturer's app, create an account, and follow the pairing instructions. Most use Bluetooth for initial setup, then switch to Wi-Fi. Expect a 5–10 minute setup process.

### Mapping Your Home

After the first cleaning cycle, the robot will create a map. For LiDAR models, this is nearly instant and highly accurate. For camera models, run a "quick map" cycle in good lighting. For gyroscope models, the map is less detailed but sufficient for basic navigation.

**Pro tip:** Close doors to rooms you don't want cleaned initially. You can add no-go zones later via the app.

### Smart Home Integration

- **Alexa:** "Alexa, ask RoboClean to start cleaning the kitchen." Works with most modern models.
- **Google Assistant:** "Hey Google, tell DirtBuster to vacuum the living room." Seamless with Google Home.
- **Apple HomeKit:** Limited to premium models like RoboClean Pro X3. Offers Siri voice control and automation triggers.
- **IFTTT (If This Then That):** Create custom routines—e.g., "If the last person leaves home, start vacuuming."
- **Samsung SmartThings:** Available on select models, allowing integration with other smart devices.

**Automation ideas:**
- Schedule cleaning for 10 AM when you're at work.
- Link with a smart door lock: "When I leave for work, vacuum the entire house."
- Use motion sensors to trigger cleaning only in occupied rooms.
