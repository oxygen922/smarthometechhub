### Suction Power

Measured in Pascals (Pa), suction power determines how well a robot vacuum cleaner picks up debris from carpets and hard floors. In 2026, anything above 8,000Pa is considered strong. Budget models often offer 2,000–4,000Pa, which is fine for hard floors but struggles with carpet.

### Navigation and Mapping

LiDAR navigation uses lasers to create accurate maps of your home. Camera-based systems (VSLAM) are cheaper but less accurate in low light. The best robot vacuum cleaners combine both for redundancy. Look for multi-floor mapping if your home has more than one level.

### Self-Emptying Base

A self-emptying base automatically transfers debris from the robot's dustbin into a larger bag or bin. This means you only need to empty it every 30–60 days instead of after every cleaning session. It's a game-changer for convenience but adds to the upfront cost and requires floor space.

### Mopping Capability

Not all mopping systems are equal. Drag-along cloths are basic and only wipe light dust. Vibrating or rotating pads provide more scrubbing action. The best models include self-washing and drying stations to keep mop pads hygienic.

### Obstacle Avoidance

AI-powered obstacle avoidance uses cameras and sensors to identify and avoid objects like cables, pet waste, and shoes. This is essential if you have a cluttered home or pets. LiDAR-only models will bump into things and may get stuck.

### Battery Life and Runtime

Most robot vacuum cleaners run for 120–180 minutes on a single charge. Larger homes may require the robot to recharge and resume. Look for models with fast charging and resume functionality.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

Setting up a modern robot vacuum cleaner is straightforward, but there are a few things to keep in mind.

### Unboxing and Placement

Your robot vacuum cleaner comes with a charging base that also serves as a self-emptying station. Place it against a wall on a hard, level surface with at least 3 feet of clearance on both sides and 5 feet in front. Avoid direct sunlight, which can interfere with camera-based navigation.

### App Setup

Download the manufacturer's app (RoboClean, iClean, or EcoVacs Home) and create an account. Connect the robot to your 2.4GHz Wi-Fi network—most models don't support 5GHz. The app will guide you through a quick mapping run, which takes 10–20 minutes depending on home size.

### Smart Home Integration

All three models support Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant. You can say, "Alexa, ask RoboClean to clean the kitchen," and it will dispatch the robot to that room. The RoboClean X9 Ultra also supports Apple Siri Shortcuts, a rare feature.

For deeper automation, use IFTTT or SmartThings to create routines. For example, you can set the robot vacuum cleaner to start cleaning when you leave home (triggered by your smart lock) and return to base when you arrive.

### Maintenance Tips

- Empty the base station bin every 30–60 days.
- Clean the robot's sensors and camera lens weekly with a dry cloth.
- Replace brushes and filters every 3–6 months.
- For mopping models, empty and refill the water tank after each use.
