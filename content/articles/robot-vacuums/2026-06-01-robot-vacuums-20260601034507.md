### Installation & Smart Home Integration

**Setting Up Your Robot Mop:**

1. **Unbox and Charge:** Most robots take 3-4 hours for a full charge. Place the dock near a power outlet and away from direct sunlight.
2. **Download the App:** All three brands require their respective apps (Ecovacs Home, Roborock, iRobot Home). Create an account and follow the on-screen setup.
3. **Map Your Home:** The first run will be a mapping mission. Clear the floor of clutter. The robot will create a map of your home, which you can then label (e.g., "Kitchen," "Living Room").
4. **Set No-Go Zones:** Use the app to set virtual boundaries for areas you don't want mopped (e.g., carpeted rooms, pet feeding stations).
5. **Connect to Smart Home:** In the app, link to Alexa, Google Home, or Apple HomeKit. You can then create routines like "Alexa, start mopping when I leave for work."

**Smart Home Integration Tips:**
- **Routines:** Combine with smart locks or motion sensors. For example, have the robot mop start when you lock the door and leave.
- **Voice Control:** Use specific room names. "Hey Google, mop the kitchen" works if you've labeled rooms in the app.
- **Scheduling:** Set the robot to mop during off-peak hours (e.g., 10 AM when the house is empty). Avoid scheduling when you're home—it's loud.

**Installation Challenges:**
- **Wi-Fi Range:** Ensure your router is within range of the dock. Mesh Wi-Fi systems help in large homes.
- **Floor Transitions:** If you have high thresholds (over 0.5 inches), the robot might struggle. Consider threshold ramps.
- **Pet Safety:** Supervise the first few runs if you have pets. Some robots can spook animals.
