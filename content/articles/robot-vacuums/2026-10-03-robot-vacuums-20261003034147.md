## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Robot Vacuum Cleaner?

- **Busy professionals and families:** If you'd rather spend weekends relaxing than vacuuming, an auto-emptying robot vacuum pays for itself in time saved.
- **Pet owners:** Models with tangle-free brushes and pet-waste avoidance are worth every penny. The Roomba j9+ Combo is purpose-built for this.
- **Smart home enthusiasts:** If you're running Alexa, Google Home, or Apple HomeKit routines, choose a robot with Matter support and robust app automation.
- **Apartment dwellers:** Compact models without huge docks fit small spaces better — consider a mid-range unit without a self-emptying base.
- **Multi-story homes:** Prioritize multi-floor mapping and recharge-and-resume functionality.

### Budget Considerations for 2026

- **Under $300:** Entry-level robot vacuums. Expect basic navigation, no auto-empty, and simple drag mopping. Best for single-room or apartment use.
- **$300–$700:** The sweet spot. LiDAR navigation, decent suction, and often a self-emptying dock. Great value.
- **$700–$1,000:** Flagship-adjacent. Strong suction, advanced obstacle avoidance, and self-washing mops.
- **$1,000+:** The best of everything. Maximum suction, AI navigation, fully self-maintaining docks, and premium build quality.

**Pro tip:** Prices on previous-generation flagship models drop significantly when new models launch. A 2025 flagship at 40% off is often a smarter buy than a 2026 mid-ranger.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

Setting up a modern robot vacuum cleaner is refreshingly simple, but a few steps ensure optimal performance.

1. **Choose your dock location:** Place the base station against a wall on a hard, level surface with at least 3 feet of clearance on both sides and 5 feet in front. Avoid direct sunlight, which can interfere with camera-based navigation.
2. **Charge fully before first use:** Most robots need a full charge (3–4 hours) before their inaugural mapping run.
3. **Run a mapping cycle:** Let the robot explore your home without interference. This creates the digital floor plan you'll use for no-go zones and room-specific cleaning.
4. **Label rooms and set zones:** Name each room in the app, then create virtual walls around pet bowls, cables, or delicate furniture.
5. **Connect to your smart home:** Link the robot to Alexa, Google Assistant, or Apple Home. With Matter support now common in 2026, cross-platform integration is smoother than ever.
6. **Create routines:** Schedule cleaning when you're away, or trigger the robot via a "Goodbye" routine. Some systems can even pause cleaning when your smart lock detects you've arrived home.

**Integration highlights:**
- **Alexa/Google:** Voice commands like "Alexa, ask RoboClean to vacuum the kitchen."
- **Apple Home:** Automations based on occupancy sensors and time of day.
- **IFTTT:** Advanced users can trigger cleaning based on calendar events or weather.
- **Matter:** The 2026 standard ensures your robot works across ecosystems without proprietary bridges.

For best results, keep firmware updated and clean the sensors and brushes weekly. A well-maintained robot vacuum cleaner can last 4–6 years.
