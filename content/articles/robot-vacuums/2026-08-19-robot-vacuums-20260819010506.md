## Key Features to Consider

When shopping for a robot mop, the spec sheet can be confusing. Here is what actually matters:

- **Mop Type:** Look for "Vibrating" or "Spinning" pads. Vibrating pads (Roborock, Dreame) scrub aggressively. Spinning pads (iRobot) are gentler but often less effective on dried grime.
- **Self-Cleaning Dock:** This is the "game-changer" feature. It washes the mop pads and dries them, preventing bacteria and bad smells. If you don't want to touch a dirty mop, this is mandatory.
- **Mop Lifting Mechanism:** Essential for homes with rugs. The robot must lift the mop pad at least 7mm to avoid soaking carpets.
- **Navigation:** LiDAR (laser) is faster and works in the dark. Camera-based is cheaper but can be fooled by dim lighting.
- **App Control:** Ensure you can set "No-Mop Zones" (for carpets) and adjust water flow per room.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Setting Up Your Robot Mop

Setting up a high-end robot mop is surprisingly simple. Here is the standard process:

1.  **Unboxing:** Remove the robot and the dock. Place the dock against a wall with space on either side (at least 1.5 feet).
2.  **Water Fill:** Fill the clean water tank in the dock with water (and optional cleaning solution).
3.  **Power On:** Plug in the dock and place the robot on it to charge.
4.  **App Setup:** Download the manufacturer's app (Roborock, Dreame, or iRobot). Create an account and add the device via Wi-Fi (2.4GHz is required).
5.  **Mapping:** Run a "Quick Map" or "Fast Mapping" cycle. The robot will roam your house to create a floor plan. This takes about 10 minutes per room.
6.  **Customize:** Once mapped, set "No-Go Zones" for areas like pet bowls or playpens. Adjust the water flow for high-traffic areas like the kitchen.

### Integration with Smart Home Hubs

All three recommended models support voice assistants.

- **Alexa:** "Alexa, ask Roborock to clean the kitchen."
- **Google Home:** "Hey Google, start the mop."
- **Apple Home (Dreame only):** The X40 Ultra is the only one with native HomeKit support via Matter, allowing you to trigger cleaning with your iPhone's "Scene" automation.

**Automation Ideas:** You can use IFTTT or the native app to trigger cleaning when you leave the house (geofencing). This ensures the floors are clean when you return without the robot running while you are home.
