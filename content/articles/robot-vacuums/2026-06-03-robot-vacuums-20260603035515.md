## Key Features to Consider

When choosing a robot vacuum, understanding the key features is crucial. Here’s what you should look for in 2026:

### Navigation and Mapping
- **LiDAR vs. Camera:** LiDAR (laser) navigation is faster and works in the dark, while camera-based systems (like those in the Roomba j9+) offer better object recognition. For most users, LiDAR is the better choice, but AI cameras are superior for avoiding small obstacles.
- **No-Go Zones and Virtual Walls:** Essential for keeping the robot away from pet bowls, kids’ toys, or delicate rugs. Look for models that allow you to set these in the app.

### Suction Power
- Measured in Pascals (Pa). For hardwood and tile, 2,000-3,000 Pa is sufficient. For carpets with pet hair, aim for 5,000 Pa or more. High-end models like the Roomba j9+ offer 25x the suction of base models, but that’s a marketing figure—real-world performance matters more.

### Mopping Capabilities
- **Standard Mop:** A simple pad that drags behind. Good for light maintenance.
- **Vibrating or Spinning Mop:** Better for scrubbing. Models like the Roborock Q Revo MaxV and Eufy X10 Pro Omni offer spinning pads that self-wash.
- **Self-Washing and Drying:** A premium feature that prevents mold and odors. Essential for daily mopping.

### Self-Emptying Base
- Most high-end models now include a self-emptying base. Look for bags that hold 30-60 days of debris. Some models (like the Roomba j9+) also include scent cartridges.

### Smart Home Integration
- **Voice Assistants:** Alexa, Google Home, and Siri (via HomeKit or Matter) are standard.
- **Matter Protocol:** The new industry standard for interoperability. If you have an Apple HomeKit or smart home hub, a Matter-compatible robot vacuum is a wise choice.
- **App Features:** Look for scheduling, room-specific cleaning, and cleaning history.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Process

Setting up a modern robot vacuum is straightforward, but there are a few steps to ensure optimal performance:

1. **Unbox and Charge:** Remove all packaging, place the robot on its dock, and let it charge fully (usually 3-4 hours).
2. **Download the App:** Install the manufacturer’s app (iRobot Home, Roborock, or Eufy Clean) and create an account.
3. **Connect to Wi-Fi:** Follow the app’s instructions to connect the robot to your 2.4 GHz home network. Note: Some models now support 5 GHz, but 2.4 GHz is more reliable for smart home devices.
4. **First Mapping Run:** Let the robot do a “mapping run” (usually a full cleaning cycle). This creates a floor plan it will use for future cleanings.
5. **Set No-Go Zones:** Use the app to mark areas you want the robot to avoid (pet bowls, kids’ play areas, etc.).
6. **Schedule Cleaning:** Set a daily or weekly schedule. Most users prefer to run the robot while they’re at work or asleep.

### Smart Home Integration

- **Alexa & Google Home:** All three recommended models work seamlessly. Simply enable the skill in your smart home app, and you can say, “Alexa, tell Roomba to start cleaning the kitchen.”
- **Apple HomeKit:** Only the Roborock Q Revo MaxV supports Matter, which means it can be added to Apple Home. This is a major advantage for iPhone users who want to automate cleaning based on triggers (e.g., “When I leave home, start vacuuming”).
- **Routines:** Create advanced routines. For example: “When the sun sets, have the robot vacuum the living room and dim the lights.” This requires a smart home hub (like an Amazon Echo Plus or Apple TV).

**Pro Tip:** For best results, keep the robot’s charging dock in a central location, away from walls, and ensure there’s at least 3 feet of clearance on each side. This allows the robot to dock easily.
