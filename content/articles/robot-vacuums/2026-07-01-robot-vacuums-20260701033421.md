## Key Features to Consider

When shopping for a robot mop in 2026, understanding the technology behind these devices is crucial. Here’s what you need to know:

### Navigation Systems
- **LiDAR:** Uses lasers to create precise maps. Best for complex layouts and dark rooms. Found in high-end models.
- **Camera-based:** Uses visual SLAM to navigate. Good for well-lit homes but can struggle in low light.
- **Gyroscope:** Relies on inertial sensors. Affordable but less accurate, prone to drift over time.

### Mopping Technology
- **Oscillating pads:** Scrub back and forth. Excellent for deep cleaning.
- **Vibrating pads:** Shake at high frequencies. Good for daily maintenance.
- **Spinning pads:** Rotate in circles. Effective but can leave streaks if not maintained.

### Water Management
- **Dual-tank systems:** Keep clean and dirty water separate. Hygienic and efficient.
- **Single-tank systems:** Use a single reservoir. Simpler but can spread dirty water.
- **Auto-refill docks:** Connect to your home’s water line (rare, premium feature).

### Smart Features
- **No-mop zones:** Essential for area rugs and carpets.
- **Multi-floor mapping:** Saves separate maps for different levels of your home.
- **Scheduling:** Set specific times for cleaning.
- **Stain detection:** High-end models can identify and target specific spills.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

Setting up a robot mop is surprisingly simple, but a few steps can ensure optimal performance.

### Installation Steps
1. **Unbox and charge:** Most models come partially charged, but a full charge takes 3–4 hours.
2. **Download the app:** Connect to Wi-Fi (2.4 GHz is still standard for most devices).
3. **Map your home:** Let the robot do an initial run to create a floor plan. This takes 20–60 minutes depending on size.
4. **Set no-mop zones:** Mark areas with carpets or rugs in the app.
5. **Schedule cleanings:** Set daily or weekly schedules.

### Smart Home Integration Tips
- **Voice commands:** Use Alexa or Google Assistant to start, stop, or pause cleaning.
- **Automation:** Create routines like “Start mopping when I leave for work” using IFTTT or Apple Shortcuts.
- **Multi-device coordination:** Some models can sync with smart vacuums to clean in tandem.
- **Maintenance reminders:** Enable notifications for pad replacement, water refills, and filter cleaning.

**Pro Tip:** For best results, clear floors of small obstacles like toys, cables, and shoes before each cleaning session. Most modern robots can avoid larger objects, but small items can still cause issues.
