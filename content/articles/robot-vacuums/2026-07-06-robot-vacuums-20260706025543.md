**Navigation & Mapping:** LiDAR (Light Detection and Ranging) is the gold standard. It uses lasers to create a precise map of your home, working perfectly in the dark. Camera-based navigation (vSLAM) is also effective but can struggle in low light and raises privacy concerns for some users. Look for systems that allow you to create no-go zones, virtual walls, and multi-floor maps.

**Suction Power:** Measured in Pascals (Pa), this indicates the vacuum's raw cleaning strength. For homes with mostly hard floors, 2,000-3,000 Pa is sufficient. For thick carpets or homes with pets, aim for 5,000 Pa or more. The top-tier models now exceed 7,000 Pa.

**Mopping Capability:** If you have hard floors, mopping is a valuable feature. Basic models drag a wet cloth. Premium models vibrate or spin the mop for scrubbing. The best now include self-washing and self-drying mop pads, which are a massive convenience upgrade.

**Obstacle Avoidance:** This is a crucial feature for 2026. High-end models use AI and cameras to identify and avoid cables, socks, shoes, and even pet waste. This prevents the robot from getting stuck or dragging mess through your home. The iRobot P.O.O.P. guarantee is a bold statement of confidence.

**Self-Emptying & Maintenance Base Stations:** This is the single biggest convenience factor. A base station that automatically empties the robot's dustbin into a larger bag means you only have to think about it once every 30-60 days. The most advanced stations also wash, dry, and refill the mop tank, making them truly hands-off.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

Setting up a modern robot vacuum is surprisingly simple. Most models follow a similar process:
1.  **Unbox and Place the Base Station:** Find a permanent spot for the base station with good Wi-Fi signal and access to a power outlet. Ensure there is at least 1-2 feet of clearance on either side.
2.  **Connect to the App:** Download the manufacturer's app (Roborock, iRobot Home, Dreamehome), create an account, and follow the on-screen instructions to connect the robot to your 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network.
3.  **Initial Mapping Run:** The robot will perform an initial "exploration" run to map your home. This can take 20-40 minutes depending on the size of your home. Do not move the robot during this time.
4.  **Customize Your Map:** Once the map is created, you can name rooms (e.g., "Living Room," "Kitchen"), set no-go zones (e.g., around pet bowls or cords), and schedule cleaning times.
5.  **Smart Home Voice Control:** All three recommended models work seamlessly with Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant. Simply enable the skill in your voice assistant's app and say, "Alexa, ask Roborock to clean the kitchen," or "Hey Google, start vacuuming the living room." The Roborock S9 Max Ultra also supports Apple HomeKit for Siri control.

**Pro Tip:** For the best experience, place your robot vacuum in a central location on the same floor you want it to clean. Ensure that small objects like loose cables, toys, and clothes are picked up before the robot runs, especially for the first few times until you trust its obstacle avoidance.
