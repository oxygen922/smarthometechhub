### Suction Power
Measured in Pascals (Pa), suction determines how much dirt a robot vacuum cleaner can pull from carpets and crevices. Anything above 8,000Pa is strong for residential use. Flagships now exceed 12,000Pa.

### Navigation & Mapping
LiDAR uses laser pulses to build a map, while camera-based AI adds object recognition. The best models combine both for superior obstacle avoidance.

### Self-Emptying Docks
These bases automatically transfer debris from the robot into a sealed bag or bin, letting you go weeks or months without emptying. Look for 60+ day capacity.

### Mopping Capability
Most robot vacuums offer basic mopping. Vibrating or rotating pads clean better, and auto-lift features prevent wet carpet disasters.

### Smart Home Integration
Ensure your chosen model works with your ecosystem—Alexa, Google Assistant, or Apple Home. App quality matters for scheduling and zone control.

### Battery Life & Coverage
Aim for at least 150 minutes of runtime or 2,000+ square feet per charge for larger homes.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Robot Vacuum Cleaner?

- **Busy professionals:** If you'd rather spend weekends relaxing than vacuuming, a robot vacuum cleaner is a no-brainer.
- **Pet owners:** Models with anti-tangle brushes and HEPA filters are essential.
- **Allergy sufferers:** Sealed docks and HEPA filtration reduce airborne dust.
- **Tech enthusiasts:** Flagships with AI navigation and app automation offer the most fun.
- **Small apartment dwellers:** A mid-range model without a bulky dock may suffice.

### Who Should Skip?

- **Homes with mostly high-pile carpet:** Robots struggle compared to upright vacuums.
- **Extremely cluttered floors:** Even the best AI can't navigate a floor covered in cables and clothes.
- **Tight budgets under $300:** You'll sacrifice navigation and suction quality.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $400:** Basic navigation, no self-emptying. Good for small apartments.
- **$400–$700:** Sweet spot. LiDAR, decent suction, sometimes self-emptying.
- **$700–$1,000:** Premium features, strong suction, reliable AI.
- **$1,000+:** Flagship performance, best obstacle avoidance, largest docks.

For most US households, the **$600–$900 range** delivers the best balance of performance and value in 2026.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

Setting up a modern robot vacuum cleaner takes about 15–20 minutes. Here's what to expect:

1. **Unbox and place the dock:** Choose a wall outlet near your main cleaning area. Ensure 3 feet of clearance on each side.
2. **Download the app:** Create an account and connect via Wi-Fi (2.4GHz is most reliable).
3. **Run the first mapping cycle:** Let the robot explore your home. This takes 20–40 minutes depending on size.
4. **Customize zones:** Set no-go zones, room names, and schedules.
5. **Link smart home:** Connect to Alexa, Google Assistant, or Apple Home for voice commands.

**Smart home integration tips:**
- Use routines like "Alexa, start cleaning the kitchen."
- Pair with smart locks or presence sensors to trigger cleaning when you leave.
- Integrate with IFTTT for advanced automations, such as cleaning after your smart thermostat switches to "away" mode.

All three recommended models support major US smart home platforms. The RoboClean Apex X9 Pro offers the deepest integration, including Apple Home, which is rare in this category.
