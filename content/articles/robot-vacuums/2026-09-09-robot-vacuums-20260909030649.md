## Key Features to Consider

| Feature | What to Look For | Why It Matters |
|---------|-----------------|----------------|
| **Navigation** | LiDAR vs. Camera | LiDAR works in the dark and maps more accurately; camera systems are cheaper but less reliable in low light |
| **Suction Power** | 2,500–12,000 Pa | Higher suction improves carpet performance but isn't critical for hard floors |
| **Self-Emptying Dock** | 2–7 week capacity | Reduces maintenance frequency; consider bag cost and availability |
| **Mop Function** | Vibrating vs. spinning | Vibrating pads scrub better; spinning pads cover more area but may streak |
| **Obstacle Avoidance** | AI camera + sensors | Prevents tangled cables, pet accidents, and stuck robots |
| **Smart Home Compatibility** | Alexa, Google, HomeKit | Voice control and automation integration for hands-free operation |
| **Battery Life** | 120–240 minutes | Larger homes need extended runtime or auto-recharge/resume |
| **Pet Hair Handling** | Rubber vs. bristle brushes | Rubber brushes resist tangling and are quieter on hard floors |
| **Carpet Detection** | Height sensors | Automatically boosts suction on carpet and retracts mop when needed |
| **App Features** | Multi-floor mapping, no-go zones | Customization for different rooms and floor types |

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Initial Setup Process

Setting up a modern robot vacuum takes approximately 15–30 minutes:

1. **Unbox and charge:** Most models require a 3–4 hour initial charge before first use.
2. **Download the app:** Create an account and connect the robot to your 2.4GHz Wi-Fi network (5GHz isn't supported by most units).
3. **Install the dock:** Place the charging dock in an open area with at least 3 feet of clearance on each side and 5 feet in front.
4. **First mapping run:** Allow the robot to complete a full mapping pass without obstructions. This typically takes 30–60 minutes depending on home size.
5. **Customize settings:** Set no-go zones, adjust suction levels per room, and configure cleaning schedules.

**Pro Tip:** Remove loose cables, pet toys, and small objects before the first run. This allows for more accurate map creation and prevents the robot from getting stuck.

### Automation Ideas

- **Morning Routine:** Trigger a kitchen and dining area clean when your smart lights turn on at 7 AM
- **Geofencing:** Start cleaning when you leave for work (iRobot and Roborock support this natively)
- **Vacation Mode:** Schedule a full-home clean every other day while away to maintain freshness
- **Pet Alert:** Use a smart camera to detect when your dog enters the house and trigger a targeted clean

### Troubleshooting Common Issues

**Wi-Fi connectivity problems:** Ensure your router broadcasts a 2.4GHz network. Some users find that disabling 5GHz temporarily during setup helps.

**Robot getting stuck:** Review the map in your app and add no-go zones around problematic areas like tight under-furniture gaps.

**Poor cleaning performance:** Check for clogged filters or full dust bags. Most apps now provide maintenance alerts and usage statistics.
