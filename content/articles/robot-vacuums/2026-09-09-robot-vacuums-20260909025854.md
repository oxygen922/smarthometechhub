## Key Features to Consider When Buying a Robot Mop

When shopping for a robot mop in 2026, you cannot simply look at suction power. The mopping system is the star of the show. Here is what you need to evaluate:

### Mopping Technology
- **Contact Mop:** A basic pad that drags across the floor. Cheap but ineffective on stuck-on grime.
- **Sonic Vibration:** A plate that vibrates rapidly to scrub the floor (e.g., Roborock). Excellent for sticky messes.
- **Rotating Pads:** Two circular pads that spin to polish the floor (e.g., Dreame, Ecovacs). Great for general grime and edges.
- **Pressure Application:** Some high-end models (like the S8 MaxV Ultra) apply downward force to simulate hand scrubbing.

### Dock Functionality
The dock is more important than the robot. Look for:
- **Auto-Cleaning:** Does the dock wash the mop pads? (Essential)
- **Hot Water Wash:** Does it use hot water to dissolve grease and sanitize? (Crucial for kitchens)
- **Auto-Drying:** Does it blow air to dry the pads? (Prevents mold and odor)
- **Auto-Empty:** Does it empty the dustbin? (Reduces maintenance)
- **Auto-Refill:** Does it refill the robot’s water tank? (Allows extended autonomous operation)

### Navigation and Obstacle Avoidance
- **Lidar vs. Camera:** Lidar (rotating top) is best for dark rooms; cameras can identify objects (shoes, cables) to avoid them.
- **Carpet Detection:** The robot must know when to lift the mop or stop mopping to avoid soaking your rugs.

### Carpet Handling
- **Lifting:** The mop pad lifts up to 10-15mm to traverse carpets.
- **Retracting:** The mop arm physically moves out of the way (iRobot). This is the safest method for high-pile carpets.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Setup
Setting up a modern robot mop is surprisingly simple. Most units require you to:
1.  **Find a Dock Location:** You need a hard, level surface with a power outlet. Ensure the dock has at least 1.5 feet of clearance on either side for the robot to enter.
2.  **Install the Dock:** Remove the water tank and fill it with clean water (and optional cleaning solution). For models with auto-emptying bags, insert the bag.
3.  **Map the Home:** Download the respective app (Roborock, iRobot Home, Dreame). The robot will perform a "Quick Map" run to scan your home. I recommend doing this during the day with all interior doors open to ensure the robot can create a complete map.

### Smart Home Integration
This is where these devices shine as part of a connected ecosystem:

- **Amazon Alexa & Google Assistant:** All three models support voice control. You can say, "Alexa, ask Roborock to clean the kitchen," or "Hey Google, start the mop." This is essential for quick, hands-free cleanups.
- **Apple HomeKit:** iRobot is the most reliable for HomeKit support (on select models). Roborock and Dreame primarily rely on Alexa/Google, though some newer models have added Matter support for HomeKit integration.
- **Siri Shortcuts:** If you are an Apple user with a non-HomeKit robot, you can use the app's Shortcuts integration to trigger cleaning via Siri.
- **Automations:** The real power comes from automation. You can set a routine in the app to mop the kitchen every day at 2:00 PM when you are at work. Or, if you have a smart lock, you can set the robot to start mopping 10 minutes after you leave the house (via IFTTT or the app's location-based triggers).
