## Key Features to Consider

When shopping for a robot vacuum in 2026, focus on these critical features:

- **Navigation Technology:** LiDAR (laser) navigation is superior to camera-based systems for speed and accuracy, especially in low light. Look for models with AI obstacle avoidance to dodge cables and toys.
- **Suction Power:** Measured in Pascals (Pa). For carpets, aim for 2,500 Pa or more. Hard floors need less, but higher suction handles debris better.
- **Self-Emptying and Self-Washing:** Self-emptying bins (holding 30-60 days of dust) and self-washing mops (with drying) are now standard on mid-to-high-end models. They reduce maintenance dramatically.
- **Mop Functionality:** If you have hard floors, consider a model with a lifting mop pad or dual mop system to avoid wet carpets. Some mops also use sonic vibration for deeper cleaning.
- **Battery Life:** Most models run 90-180 minutes. Larger homes (over 2,000 sq ft) need longer battery life or a recharge-and-resume feature.
- **Smart Home Integration:** Compatibility with Alexa, Google Assistant, and Apple HomeKit (less common) allows voice control and automation. IFTTT support adds flexibility.
- **App Features:** Look for multi-floor mapping, no-go zones, room-specific cleaning, and scheduling. A good app makes the device truly “smart.”

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

Setting up a modern robot vacuum is straightforward. Most models include a quick-start guide and an app that walks you through Wi-Fi setup and mapping. Here’s what to expect:

1. **Unbox and Charge:** Place the dock on a hard, level surface with clearance on both sides. Plug it in and let the vacuum charge fully (2–4 hours).
2. **Download the App:** Create an account and connect the vacuum to your 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network. The app will guide you through firmware updates.
3. **First Mapping Run:** Allow the vacuum to explore your home. This takes 30–60 minutes. Afterward, you can set no-go zones, room labels, and cleaning schedules.
4. **Smart Home Setup:** For Alexa, enable the “Roborock” or “iRobot” skill in the Alexa app and link your account. For Google Home, add the device through the Google Home app. You can then say commands like “Alexa, ask Roborock to clean the kitchen.”
5. **Automation Ideas:** Use IFTTT to trigger cleaning when you leave home (via phone location) or set a routine where the vacuum starts after you say “Goodnight” to Alexa.

**Pro Tip:** Ensure your home has clear pathways—remove loose cables and small items from floors. Most vacuums handle obstacles well, but a clear path speeds up cleaning.
