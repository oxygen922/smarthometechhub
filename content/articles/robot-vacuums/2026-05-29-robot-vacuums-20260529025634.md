## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Process

Setting up a modern robot vacuum is straightforward:

1. **Unbox and Charge:** Place the robot on its dock and allow a full charge (about 3-4 hours).
2. **Download the App:** Install the manufacturer's app (iRobot Home, Roborock, or Ecovacs Home) and create an account.
3. **Connect to Wi-Fi:** Follow in-app prompts to connect the robot to your 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network. Most models support 5 GHz now, but 2.4 GHz is more reliable.
4. **Map Your Home:** Run a "mapping" or "explore" cycle. This allows the robot to create a floor plan. For best results, remove obstacles and close doors to rooms you don't want mapped.
5. **Customize Settings:** Set no-go zones, virtual walls, and cleaning schedules. Most apps let you label rooms (e.g., "Kitchen," "Living Room").
6. **Integrate with Smart Home:** Use the app to link with Alexa, Google Assistant, or Apple HomeKit. You can then use voice commands like "Alexa, ask Roomba to clean the kitchen."

### Smart Home Integration Tips

- **Automation Routines:** Create routines in Alexa or Google Home. For example, "Start vacuuming when I leave home" or "Clean the kitchen after dinner."
- **Geofencing:** Many apps support geofencing so the vacuum starts cleaning when you leave and returns to base before you return.
- **IFTTT (If This Then That):** Ecovacs models support IFTTT, allowing advanced automations like "If it rains, skip outdoor mopping."
- **HomeKit Limitations:** Only iRobot models natively support Apple HomeKit. For other brands, you may need a bridge like Homebridge.

**Pro Tip:** Place the dock in a central location with at least 3 feet of clearance on each side for the robot to dock easily. Avoid placing it under furniture.
