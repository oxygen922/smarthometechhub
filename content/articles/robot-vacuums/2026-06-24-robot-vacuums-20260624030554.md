## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Robot Vacuum?

Robot vacuums are ideal for:
- **Busy professionals** who want a clean home without spending time on manual vacuuming.
- **Pet owners** who need daily hair removal from floors and carpets.
- **Families with young children** who are constantly dealing with crumbs and messes.
- **Elderly or mobility-challenged individuals** who find traditional vacuuming difficult.

However, if you have mostly high-pile carpets, steep thresholds, or a very cluttered home with lots of obstacles, a robot vacuum might not be the best fit—you’ll still need a traditional upright or canister vacuum for deep cleaning.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $300:** You’ll find basic models without mapping or self-emptying. These are fine for small apartments but won’t handle carpets well.
- **$300–$600:** This is the sweet spot for 2026. The Eufy X10 Pro Omni falls here on sale and offers excellent value.
- **$600–$900:** Premium features like self-emptying, advanced navigation, and good mopping. The iRobot j9+ is a top contender.
- **$1,000+:** Flagship models like the Roborock S8 Pro Ultra that do it all, including self-cleaning mop pads and the highest suction.

Pro tip: Always check for deals during Prime Day, Black Friday, and back-to-school sales. You can often save $200–$300 on high-end models.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

Setting up a modern robot vacuum is surprisingly simple, but there are a few steps to ensure you get the best experience.

**Installation Process:**
1. **Unbox and charge:** Most models come partially charged but need a full charge (2–4 hours) before first use.
2. **Install the app:** Download the manufacturer’s app (iRobot Home, Roborock, or Eufy Home) and create an account.
3. **Connect to Wi-Fi:** The robot will guide you through connecting to your home’s 2.4GHz Wi-Fi network. Note: 5GHz networks are not supported by most robot vacuums.
4. **Map your home:** Run a “mapping run” or “quick map” to let the robot learn your floor plan. This takes 15–30 minutes per floor.
5. **Set no-go zones:** Use the app to mark areas you want the robot to avoid, like pet bowls, play areas, or delicate rugs.
6. **Schedule cleaning:** Set a daily or weekly schedule. Many users prefer running the vacuum while they’re at work or asleep.

**Smart Home Integration:**
- **Amazon Alexa:** “Alexa, ask Roomba to start cleaning.”
- **Google Assistant:** “Hey Google, tell Roborock to clean the kitchen.”
- **Apple HomeKit:** Only available on certain models like the Roborock S8 Pro Ultra. You can add it to your Home app and create automations (e.g., “When I leave home, start vacuuming”).
- **IFTTT:** Some models support IFTTT for advanced automations, like starting cleaning when your smart lock detects you’ve left.

**Tips for best results:**
- Clear floors of cords, small toys, and clutter before each cleaning.
- Use virtual barriers or no-go zones to protect fragile items.
- Regularly clean the robot’s sensors and brushes to maintain performance.
