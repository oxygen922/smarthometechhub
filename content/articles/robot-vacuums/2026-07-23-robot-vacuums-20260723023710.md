## Installation & Smart Home Integration

Setting up a modern robot vacuum is surprisingly simple, but getting the most out of it requires some planning.

**Step 1: Physical Setup**
Place the charging dock against a wall with at least 3 feet of clearance on each side and 5 feet in front. Ensure it’s on a level surface and connected to power. Most docks have a cable management system to keep things tidy.

**Step 2: App Setup**
Download the manufacturer’s app (iRobot Home, Roborock, SmartThings, etc.). Create an account and follow the on-screen instructions to connect the vacuum to your Wi-Fi network (2.4GHz is recommended for stability). The app will guide you through the first mapping run.

**Step 3: Mapping Your Home**
Allow the vacuum to do a full “exploration” run. This takes 30-60 minutes depending on home size. During this run, the vacuum creates a detailed map. After mapping, you can:
- Set no-go zones (areas to avoid)
- Set no-mop zones (for carpets)
- Name rooms (Kitchen, Living Room, etc.)
- Create cleaning schedules per room

**Step 4: Smart Home Integration**
- **Amazon Alexa:** Enable the skill in the Alexa app. Use voice commands like “Alexa, ask Roomba to start cleaning.”
- **Google Assistant:** Link the vacuum in the Google Home app. Say “Hey Google, start vacuuming the kitchen.”
- **Apple HomeKit:** For compatible models (like Roomba j9+), add the vacuum to the Home app. Create automations like “When I leave home, start cleaning.”
- **IFTTT:** Advanced users can create custom applets. For example, “If the weather forecast says rain, skip mopping today.”

**Pro Tip:** For the best experience, integrate your vacuum with your smart lock or security system. Set it to start cleaning automatically when you lock the door and leave for work. Many premium models now support geofencing for this exact purpose.
