## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Robot Mop?

Robot mops are ideal for:

- **Busy professionals** who want to come home to clean floors without lifting a finger.
- **Pet owners** who need to manage pet hair, dander, and occasional accidents.
- **Families with young children** who want to keep floors free of crumbs, spills, and germs.
- **Seniors or individuals with mobility issues** who find traditional mopping physically challenging.
- **Tech enthusiasts** who love integrating smart home devices and automating daily tasks.

### Who Should Skip?

Robot mops may not be the best fit for:

- **Homes with mostly carpeted floors** — while some models can vacuum carpets, mopping is limited to hard surfaces.
- **Very cluttered homes** — robot mops need relatively clear floors to navigate effectively.
- **Tight budgets** — while entry-level models exist, they often lack the advanced features that make robot mops truly convenient.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $500:** Basic models with limited navigation and no self-cleaning dock. Good for small apartments and light cleaning.
- **$500–$900:** Mid-range models with better navigation, larger tanks, and some smart features. Best value for most households.
- **$900–$1,500:** Premium models with advanced scrubbing, self-cleaning docks, and top-tier navigation. Ideal for large homes and heavy use.
- **$1,500+:** Ultra-premium models with cutting-edge AI, multi-function docks, and luxury features. Diminishing returns for most users.

**Money-Saving Tips:**

- Look for sales during Amazon Prime Day, Black Friday, and Cyber Monday.
- Consider refurbished models from reputable brands — they often come with a warranty.
- Check if your utility company offers rebates for smart home devices.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

Setting up a robot mop is generally straightforward, but there are a few steps to ensure optimal performance.

### Unboxing and Placement

1. **Choose a location for the dock:** Place it against a wall on a hard, level surface. Ensure there's at least 3 feet of clearance on both sides and 5 feet in front.
2. **Power on the dock:** Plug it into a standard outlet. Avoid using extension cords.
3. **Install the mop pads:** Most models require you to attach the pads to the bottom of the robot. Some premium models come with pre-installed pads.
4. **Fill the water tank:** Use only water or manufacturer-recommended cleaning solutions. Avoid harsh chemicals that can damage the tank or floors.

### App Setup and Mapping

1. **Download the companion app:** Available on iOS and Android.
2. **Create an account:** You'll need to register your device.
3. **Connect to Wi-Fi:** Ensure your robot is within range of a 2.4GHz network (most models don't support 5GHz).
4. **Run a mapping cycle:** Let the robot explore your home to create a map. This may take a few runs to perfect.
5. **Customize settings:** Set no-go zones, adjust water flow, and schedule cleanings.

### Smart Home Integration

- **Amazon Alexa:** "Alexa, ask RoboClean to start mopping."
- **Google Assistant:** "Hey Google, tell iLift to clean the kitchen."
- **Apple HomeKit:** Available on select models like the AquaMax Pro 3000. You can add the robot to scenes and automations.
- **IFTTT:** Create custom applets, such as "If I leave home, start mopping."

### Maintenance Tips

- **Empty and rinse the dirty water tank** after each use to prevent odors.
- **Clean the mop pads** regularly — most are machine washable, but check the manufacturer's instructions.
- **Replace filters** every 3–6 months.
- **Clean sensors** with a dry cloth to maintain navigation accuracy.
- **Update firmware** via the app to get the latest features and improvements.
