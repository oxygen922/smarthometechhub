## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Robot Mop?

- **Busy professionals:** If you barely have time to sweep, a robot mop keeps floors clean with zero effort.
- **Pet owners:** Models with strong suction and mopping handle pet hair and muddy paw prints.
- **Allergy sufferers:** Robot mops with HEPA filters reduce dust and dander.
- **Tech enthusiasts:** Smart home integration and AI features make cleaning futuristic.

### Who Should Skip?

- **Homes with thick, high-pile carpets:** Most robot mops can't deep clean shag carpets.
- **Tight budgets:** A manual mop and bucket cost under $30—robot mops are a luxury.
- **Extremely cluttered spaces:** Navigation struggles with lots of obstacles.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $300:** Basic mopping, no smart mapping. Good for small apartments.
- **$300–$600:** Best value. Includes mapping, scheduling, and decent suction.
- **$600–$1,000:** Premium features like self-cleaning, AI avoidance, and auto-empty.

**2026 Pricing Note:** Prices are stabilizing after 2024's supply chain issues. Expect discounts during Black Friday and Prime Day.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

Setting up a robot mop is straightforward, but a few steps ensure optimal performance.

### Unboxing and First Charge
Most models arrive partially charged. Place the base station on a hard, level surface near a power outlet. Charge fully (2–4 hours) before first use.

### App Setup
Download the manufacturer's app (e.g., RoboClean, iLife, SmartThings). Create an account, connect to Wi-Fi (2.4 GHz recommended), and pair the robot. The app will guide you through firmware updates.

### Mapping Your Home
Run a "quick map" cycle. The robot will explore and create a floor plan. You can then label rooms, set no-go zones, and adjust cleaning preferences.

### Smart Home Integration
- **Alexa:** "Alexa, ask RoboClean to mop the kitchen."
- **Google Assistant:** "Hey Google, start mopping."
- **Apple Home:** Add to scenes for automatic cleaning when you leave.
- **IFTTT:** Create custom triggers (e.g., mop when humidity drops).

### Maintenance Tips
- Empty the water tank and rinse mop pads after each use.
- Clean sensors and brushes weekly.
- Descale the base station monthly (if self-cleaning).
