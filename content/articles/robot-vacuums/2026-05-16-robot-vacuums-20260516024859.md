### Understanding the Key Metrics

- **Suction Power:** Measured in Pascals (Pa), this indicates how much force the vacuum can generate. Higher suction is better for carpets and heavy debris. For hardwood, 2,000-3,000 Pa is sufficient; for thick carpets, aim for 4,000 Pa or more.

- **Navigation:** LiDAR (Light Detection and Ranging) uses lasers to map rooms with high precision, even in the dark. Camera-based systems are cheaper but can struggle in low-light conditions. AI-powered obstacle avoidance uses machine learning to identify and avoid common hazards.

- **Self-Emptying vs. Self-Cleaning:** A self-emptying dock automatically empties the dustbin into a larger bag, reducing maintenance to every 30-60 days. Self-cleaning mops also wash and dry the mop pad, preventing odors and bacterial growth.

- **Battery Life:** Most robot vacuums can clean for 90-180 minutes before needing to recharge. Larger homes (over 2,000 sq ft) should prioritize models with longer battery life or the ability to resume cleaning after charging.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Setting Up Your Robot Vacuum

Getting started with a robot vacuum is straightforward, but a few tips can ensure optimal performance:

1. **Unbox and charge:** Most models come partially charged, but it's best to fully charge before the first use (typically 4-6 hours).

2. **Install the dock:** Place the dock against a wall with at least 3 feet of clearance on each side and 5 feet in front. Avoid placing it in direct sunlight or near heat sources.

3. **Download the app:** Create an account and follow the in-app instructions to connect the vacuum to your Wi-Fi network (2.4 GHz is usually recommended for stability).

4. **First cleaning run:** Let the vacuum map your home in a "Quick Map" or "Explore" mode. This may take 20-40 minutes depending on home size. Do not interrupt this process.

5. **Customize settings:** After mapping, you can set no-go zones, define room boundaries, and create cleaning schedules. Most apps allow you to name rooms (e.g., "Kitchen" or "Master Bedroom") for targeted cleaning.

### Smart Home Integration

All three recommended models support Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant, allowing you to start, stop, or pause cleaning with voice commands. For example, you can say, "Alexa, ask Roborock to clean the living room," or "Hey Google, start vacuuming."

**Apple HomeKit** support is less common but available on select models like the **iRobot Roomba j9+** (via the iRobot Home app bridge) and some **Roborock** models with firmware updates. If HomeKit integration is a priority, verify compatibility before purchasing.

**Advanced Automation:** Use platforms like IFTTT (If This Then That) or the native app's routines to create complex automations. For instance, you could set your robot vacuum to start cleaning when your smart lock detects you've left the house, or to pause when your smart speaker plays music.
