## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Setting Up Your Robot Vacuum

Modern robot vacuums are remarkably easy to set up, but proper installation ensures optimal performance:

**Step 1: Choose the Right Location**
Place the charging dock against a wall in an open area with at least 2 feet of clearance on each side and 5 feet in front. Avoid placing it in direct sunlight or near heat sources, which can affect charging efficiency.

**Step 2: Initial Mapping**
Allow your robot to complete at least two full cleaning cycles before creating customized schedules. This ensures the mapping system captures your home's layout accurately, including any temporary obstacles like furniture that may have been moved.

**Step 3: Create No-Go Zones**
Use your companion app to set up virtual boundaries for areas you want to avoid — pet feeding stations, areas with loose cables, or rooms with delicate items. Most 2026 models support both physical boundary strips and virtual no-go zones.

**Step 4: Configure Schedules**
Set cleaning schedules that align with your daily routine. For most households, daily cleaning during work hours works best. The Eufy X10 Pro Omni and Roborock Q Revo MaxV allow different schedules for different days, which is useful for adjusting to your weekly routine.

### Smart Home Ecosystem Integration

**Amazon Alexa:**
All three recommended models work with Alexa, allowing voice commands like "Alexa, ask Roborock to clean the kitchen." You can also create routines that trigger cleaning when you say "Alexa, I'm leaving."

**Google Assistant:**
Similar functionality is available through Google Assistant. The integration is slightly less polished than Alexa but still reliable for basic commands and scheduling.

**Apple HomeKit:**
Only the iRobot Roomba Combo j9+ offers native HomeKit support. This allows you to add the robot to your Home app, create automation scenes, and control it through Siri. For Apple-centric households, this is a significant advantage.

**IFTTT and Advanced Automation:**
Both the Eufy and Roborock support IFTTT, allowing you to create custom automations with other smart home devices. For example, you can trigger cleaning when your smart lock detects you've left, or pause cleaning when your security camera detects motion.

**Pro Tip:** For the best experience, ensure your robot vacuum is connected to a 2.4GHz Wi-Fi network. Many smart home devices have compatibility issues with 5GHz networks, and a dedicated IoT network can improve reliability.
