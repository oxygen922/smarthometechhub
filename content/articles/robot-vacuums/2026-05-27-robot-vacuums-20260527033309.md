## Key Features to Consider

When choosing a robot vacuum, focus on these critical features:

- **Navigation:** LiDAR (laser) vs. camera-based. LiDAR offers better performance in low light, while cameras can identify objects more accurately.
- **Suction Power:** Measured in Pascals (Pa). 2,500Pa is sufficient for hard floors; 5,000Pa+ is needed for thick carpets.
- **Battery Life:** Look for 100-180 minutes for larger homes. Self-emptying bases reduce the need for frequent recharging.
- **Obstacle Avoidance:** AI-based systems (e.g., iRobot’s P.O.O.P., Roborock’s ReactiveAI) are crucial for homes with pets or children.
- **Mopping Function:** For hard floors, a dedicated mopping pad or sonic mopping is more effective than a simple wet cloth.
- **Dustbin Capacity:** Larger bins (400ml+) and self-emptying bases reduce maintenance frequency.
- **Smart Home Integration:** Compatibility with Alexa, Google Assistant, and Apple HomeKit ensures seamless automation.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Setup Process

1. **Unboxing:** All three models come with a charging dock, power adapter, and user manual. The Roborock S8 MaxV Ultra includes a mopping pad and water tank.
2. **Charging:** Place the dock against a wall with 3 feet of clearance on each side. Allow 3-4 hours for initial charging.
3. **App Setup:** Download the respective app (iRobot Home, Roborock, SmartThings). Connect to Wi-Fi (2.4GHz recommended for stability).
4. **Mapping:** Run the first cleaning cycle to generate a map. For LiDAR models, this takes 15-30 minutes. Camera-based models may require a second pass.
5. **Zone Management:** Set up no-go zones, room labels, and schedules in the app.

### Smart Home Integration

- **Alexa:** “Alexa, start Roomba” or “Alexa, ask Roborock to clean the kitchen.”
- **Google Assistant:** “Hey Google, tell Roomba to vacuum the living room.”
- **Apple HomeKit:** Only Roborock S8 MaxV Ultra supports HomeKit natively. For iRobot, use a third-party bridge.
- **Automation:** Create routines like “Goodnight” that trigger vacuuming after you leave for work. All models support IFTTT for advanced automation.

### Maintenance Tips

- Empty dustbins weekly (or use self-emptying bases).
- Clean brushes and sensors monthly to maintain performance.
- Replace filters every 3-6 months.
- For mopping models, rinse mop pads after each use to prevent odor.
