### Installation & Smart Home Integration

Setting up a modern robot vacuum is surprisingly simple. Here's a typical process:

1. **Unbox and Place the Base:** Find a location with Wi-Fi signal, near an outlet, and with at least 3 feet of clearance on each side. The base should be on a hard, level surface.
2. **Connect to Wi-Fi:** Download the manufacturer's app, create an account, and follow the on-screen instructions. Most vacuums now support dual-band Wi-Fi (2.4 GHz and 5 GHz), but 2.4 GHz is more reliable for initial setup.
3. **Map Your Home:** Let the vacuum run a "mapping run" where it explores your home and creates a floor plan. This takes 15–30 minutes. During this run, remove obstacles like loose cables and small toys.
4. **Customize Zones:** After mapping, you can label rooms (e.g., "Kitchen," "Living Room"), set no-go zones (e.g., around pet bowls), and schedule cleaning times.
5. **Smart Home Integration:** For Apple HomeKit, scan the QR code in the app. For Alexa or Google Home, enable the appropriate skill and link your account. For Matter devices, it's even simpler—just scan the Matter QR code from the device or box.

**Pro Tip:** If you have a Matter-compatible hub (like an Apple TV 4K or Amazon Echo 4th gen), you can control the vacuum without needing the manufacturer's app for basic commands. However, advanced features like zone cleaning still require the app.
