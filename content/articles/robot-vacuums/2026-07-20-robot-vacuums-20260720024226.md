### Mopping Technology Explained

**Oscillating mops** (like the ECOVACS) use a back-and-forth motion combined with downward pressure to scrub floors. These are most effective for stuck-on messes.

**Sonic vibration mops** (like the Roborock) vibrate at high frequencies to dislodge dirt. They're excellent for general cleaning but may struggle with dried spots.

**Wet roll mops** (like the iRobot) use a spinning or rolling pad that applies water evenly. These are gentler on floors but less aggressive for deep cleaning.

### Navigation Systems

- **LiDAR** (ECOVACS): Uses lasers for precise mapping, works in darkness, no privacy concerns
- **Camera-based** (Roborock, iRobot): Uses visual recognition for object identification, may have privacy implications
- **Hybrid** (ECOVACS): Combines LiDAR with AI for best-in-class navigation

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Setup Process

All three models follow a similar setup process:
1. Unbox and place the base station near a power outlet
2. Download the manufacturer's app (ECOVACS Home, Roborock, or iRobot Home)
3. Connect the robot to your 2.4GHz Wi-Fi network
4. Allow the robot to perform an initial mapping run
5. Set up no-mop zones, schedules, and preferences in the app

**Time required:** 15-30 minutes for initial setup, 45-60 minutes for first mapping run.

### Smart Home Integration Tips

**For Amazon Alexa users:** Enable the respective skill in the Alexa app, then use voice commands like "Alexa, ask [robot name] to start mopping the kitchen."

**For Google Assistant users:** Link your robot account in the Google Home app. Commands like "Hey Google, start mopping" work seamlessly.

**For Apple HomeKit users:** Only the ECOVACS T30 Omni offers native HomeKit support. Scan the HomeKit code on the robot to add it to your home.

**Automation ideas:**
- Set your robot mop to start cleaning when your smart lock detects you've left the house
- Schedule mopping after your robot vacuum finishes (many can work together)
- Use presence sensors to trigger cleaning only when rooms are empty
