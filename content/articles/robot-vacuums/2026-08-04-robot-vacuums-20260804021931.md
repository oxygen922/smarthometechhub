## Key Features to Consider

When shopping for a robot vacuum in 2026, you cannot just look at suction power. The industry has shifted toward "autonomy." Here is what you need to look for:

### 1. Obstacle Avoidance (AI vs. LiDAR)
- **LiDAR (Laser):** Uses spinning lasers to map the room. Great for navigation, but it cannot see objects on the floor (like cables).
- **AI Camera:** Uses a camera to visually identify objects. Essential for homes with clutter or pets.
- **Best Combo:** A hybrid system (LiDAR + Camera) as seen in the Roborock and Dreame.

### 2. Self-Cleaning Dock Capabilities
- **Auto-Empty:** The dock sucks debris into a bag. Non-negotiable for convenience.
- **Mop Washing:** The dock washes the mop pad with clean water. This prevents dirty water from spreading.
- **Hot Air Drying:** Dries the mop to prevent mildew. Essential in humid climates.
- **Auto-Refill:** The dock refills the robot’s water tank. A luxury feature, but one you'll appreciate.

### 3. Mop Technology
- **Sonic Vibration:** Scrapes the floor at high frequency (Roborock).
- **Spinning Pads:** Rotates two pads to scrub (Dreame).
- **Retractable Lift:** The mop lifts up when moving to carpet. This is a must-have for mixed floors.

### 4. Suction Power
- **Under 5,000Pa:** Fine for hard floors, weak on carpet.
- **5,000-7,000Pa:** Good for low-pile carpet.
- **8,000Pa+:** Necessary for high-pile carpet and pet hair.

### 5. Battery Life & Auto-Resume
- Look for at least 180 minutes of runtime. The robot should automatically return to the dock to recharge and then continue cleaning where it left off.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy Which?

- **The Busy Professional (Roborock S8 MaxV Ultra):** If you have a large home, a busy schedule, and money is less of a concern, the Roborock offers the most "hands-off" experience. The superior drying and refill station mean you only think about it once a month.

- **The Tech Enthusiast (Dreame X40 Ultra):** If you enjoy tweaking settings, want the best edge-cleaning performance, and want to save $400, the Dreame is your match. It performs 90% as well as the Roborock for 75% of the price.

- **The Apple Household (iRobot Combo 10 Max):** If you live in the Apple ecosystem and rely on Siri shortcuts, the iRobot is the only flagship that natively supports HomeKit. It is a smart home hub first, a vacuum second.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $400:** You are looking at "dumb" vacuums. They will random-bounce and lack self-emptying. They are fine for small apartments with no pets.
- **$400 - $800:** This is the "sweet spot" for mid-range. Look for models with LiDAR mapping and self-emptying but skip the mop washing.
- **$800 - $1,200:** This range offers the best value. You get AI obstacle avoidance and self-cleaning docks, but perhaps with smaller water tanks.
- **$1,200+:** This is the flagship tier. You are paying for the absolute best autonomy, build quality, and brand reliability. If you have the budget, the convenience is worth it.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Setting Up Your Robot

Installation is surprisingly simple. Most units take 20 minutes to set up out of the box. Here’s the process:

1.  **Place the Dock:** Choose a location with a hard, level surface. Leave 0.5 meters of clearance on each side and 1.5 meters in front.
2.  **Connect to App:** Download the respective app (Roborock, Dreame, or iRobot Home). The app will guide you through connecting to your 2.4GHz Wi-Fi network.
3.  **Mapping Run:** The robot will do a "fast map" run, navigating the perimeter of your home. This takes about 10 minutes per 1,000 sq ft.
4.  **Configure Zones:** After mapping, you can set "No-Go" zones (e.g., around pet bowls) and "No-Mop" zones (e.g., on thick rugs).

### Smart Home Integration

- **Voice Control:** All three support Alexa and Google Assistant. You can say, "Alexa, ask Roborock to clean the living room."
- **Routines:** Use the Google Home app to trigger cleaning when you leave for work (based on location) or when your security system arms.
- **HomeKit:** Only the iRobot offers native HomeKit support, allowing you to add it to your "Good Night" scene (e.g., "Good Night" turns off lights, locks doors, and starts the vacuum).
- **Matter Support:** As of 2026, Matter is still rolling out for robot vacuums. Roborock has pledged support via a firmware update, but iRobot is currently the only one with stable Matter certification.

**Pro Tip:** Do not run the robot while you are on a video call. The mapping LiDAR can be heard as a low hum on high-end microphones.
