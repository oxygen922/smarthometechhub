### Navigation Systems: LiDAR vs. Camera

This is the most critical distinction. **LiDAR (Light Detection and Ranging)** uses a spinning laser to map your home in 3D. It works perfectly in the dark and is exceptionally fast and accurate. Both the Roborock and Dreame use this system. **Camera-based systems (like the iRobot)** use visual recognition to identify objects. They are more intelligent in recognizing *what* an object is (e.g., a cable vs. a shoe) but are slower and struggle in low-light conditions without a built-in light.

### The Importance of the Dock

The dock is where the "smart" in smart home comes to life. A basic dock is just a charging station. A **premium dock** transforms the vacuum into a self-sustaining appliance. Look for:
- **Auto-Empty:** Transfers dirt from the vacuum bin to a larger bag or bin in the dock.
- **Mop Washing:** Cleans the mop pad after use to prevent smells and re-spreading dirt.
- **Hot Air Drying:** Dries the mop to prevent mildew growth.
- **Auto Water Refill:** Automatically refills the vacuum’s water tank.

### Obstacle Avoidance

If you have a cluttered home, this is vital. Older vacuums would blindly bump into objects. Modern units use AI to recognize and avoid obstacles. The best models can avoid cables, shoes, and even pet waste, ensuring they don't get stuck or smear messes across your floor.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

Setting up a modern robot vacuum is surprisingly simple, but proper integration takes a few steps to get right.

### Step 1: Physical Setup
1.  **Place the Dock:** Choose a location with open space on both sides (at least 1.5 feet) and about 4 feet of clearance in front. This allows the vacuum to find and dock easily. Avoid placing it in direct sunlight, as this can confuse the cliff sensors.
2.  **Power Up:** Connect the dock to a wall outlet. Plug the vacuum onto the dock to charge it fully before the first run. This can take 3-4 hours.
3.  **Connect to App:** Download the manufacturer's app (Roborock, Dreame, or iRobot). Create an account and follow the in-app instructions to connect the vacuum to your 2.4GHz Wi-Fi network.

### Step 2: Mapping Your Home
The first run should be a "mapping" run. Allow the vacuum to roam freely to create a full map of your home. Do not interfere with it during this time. Once complete, you can use the app to:
- **Set No-Go Zones:** Draw virtual boundaries to keep the vacuum out of areas with cables or pet food bowls.
- **Set Invisible Walls:** Create a barrier to prevent it from entering a specific room.
- **Name Rooms:** Label rooms (e.g., "Kitchen," "Living Room") to allow for room-specific cleaning commands.

### Step 3: Voice Control Integration
All top models support major voice assistants.

- **Amazon Alexa:** "Alexa, ask Roborock to clean the kitchen."
- **Google Assistant:** "Hey Google, start cleaning the living room."
- **Apple Siri:** With Shortcuts, you can trigger cleaning routines or specific modes.

### Step 4: Automation and Routines
This is where the "smart home" magic happens. Use the app or IFTTT to create routines.
- **Scheduling:** Set the vacuum to clean every day at 9:00 AM while you are at work.
- **Trigger-Based Actions:** In the app, you can set it to start cleaning when you say "Goodnight" to your smart speaker, or when you leave a specific geofenced area (using your phone's GPS).
