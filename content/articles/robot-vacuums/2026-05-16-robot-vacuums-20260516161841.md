## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Setup Process

All three recommended models follow a similar setup:

1. **Unbox and place the dock:** Find a spot with 1–2 feet of clearance on each side. The dock needs access to Wi-Fi and a power outlet.
2. **Connect to the app:** Download the manufacturer’s app (iRobot Home, Roborock, or Dreamehome). Follow the guided setup to connect to your 2.4GHz Wi-Fi network.
3. **First mapping run:** Allow the robot to explore your home. This takes 10–20 minutes. Do not move furniture during this time.
4. **Customize zones:** In the app, label rooms (e.g., “Kitchen,” “Living Room”), set no-go zones, and schedule cleaning times.
5. **Integrate with smart home:** In the app, link to your preferred assistant (e.g., “Alexa, ask Roomba to clean the kitchen”).

### Smart Home Automation Ideas

- **Morning routine:** “Good morning, Google. Start cleaning the living room.”
- **Geofencing:** The robot automatically starts cleaning when you leave home and docks when you return.
- **IFTTT recipes:** “If the weather forecast calls for rain, skip mopping today.”
- **HomeKit scenes:** “Movie night” triggers the robot to clean the floor before you settle in.

### Common Pitfalls to Avoid

- **Wi-Fi dead zones:** Place your router centrally or use a mesh system. Weak Wi-Fi causes mapping errors.
- **Cluttered floors:** Even the best AI can’t avoid everything. Pick up loose cables, socks, and small toys before scheduled cleanings.
- **Dark furniture:** Some robots struggle with black or dark brown furniture. Add virtual no-go zones around these items.
