### Suction Power

Measured in Pascals (Pa), suction power determines how well a robot vacuum cleaner picks up debris. Entry-level models offer 2,000-3,000Pa, mid-range models 4,000-6,000Pa, and premium models 7,000Pa and above. For homes with carpets and pets, aim for at least 5,000Pa.

### Navigation Technology

- **LiDAR:** Uses laser mapping for precise navigation. Best for complex floor plans.
- **Camera-based:** Uses visual recognition. Good for obstacle avoidance but less accurate in low light.
- **Gyroscope:** Budget option. Works but less efficient.

### Self-Emptying Base Stations

These docks automatically empty the vacuum's dustbin into a larger bag or container. Capacity ranges from 30 to 60 days. Some premium models also wash and dry mop pads.

### Smart Home Integration

Most 2026 robot vacuums support Alexa and Google Assistant. For Apple users, look for HomeKit compatibility. Matter support is becoming standard and ensures future compatibility across platforms.

### Battery Life and Coverage

Most robot vacuums run 120-180 minutes per charge, covering 1,000-2,500 square feet. If you have a large home, consider a model with recharge-and-resume functionality.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Unboxing and Setup

Setting up a modern robot vacuum cleaner takes 15-30 minutes. The process typically involves:

1. **Unboxing and charging:** Most models arrive partially charged but need a full charge (3-4 hours) before first use.
2. **Base station placement:** Choose a location with Wi-Fi signal and wall space. Avoid direct sunlight and heat sources.
3. **App download and pairing:** Download the manufacturer's app, create an account, and connect via Wi-Fi (2.4GHz is usually required).
4. **Initial mapping run:** Let the vacuum explore your home to create a floor plan. This takes 30-60 minutes depending on home size.
5. **Customization:** Set no-go zones, room names, schedules, and cleaning preferences.

### Smart Home Integration Tips

- **Voice control:** Link your vacuum to Alexa, Google Assistant, or Siri for hands-free operation.
- **Routines:** Create routines like "Goodnight" that trigger vacuuming after you go to bed.
- **Geofencing:** Some apps let the vacuum start when you leave home.
- **IFTTT:** Advanced users can create custom automations with IFTTT.

### Maintenance Schedule

To keep your robot vacuum cleaner performing optimally:

- **Weekly:** Empty dustbin (if not self-emptying), check brushes for tangles
- **Monthly:** Clean sensors and cameras, wipe down base station
- **Quarterly:** Replace filters, inspect brushes for wear
- **Annually:** Replace brushes and filters, deep clean base station
