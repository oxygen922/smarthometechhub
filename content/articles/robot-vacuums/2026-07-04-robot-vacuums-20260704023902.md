## Key Features to Consider

When shopping for a robot mop, not all features are created equal. Here’s what you need to evaluate before making a purchase:

| Feature | What It Does | Why It Matters |
|---------|--------------|----------------|
| **Mopping Mechanism** | Rotating pads vs. oscillating vs. vibrating | Rotating pads scrub deeper; vibrating pads are gentler on delicate floors. |
| **Suction Power** | Measured in Pascals (Pa) | Higher suction (4,000+ Pa) ensures debris is picked up before mopping. |
| **Water Tank Capacity** | 300ml–600ml | Larger tanks mean fewer refills for big homes. |
| **Navigation System** | LiDAR, 3D mapping, or AI cameras | Better navigation avoids obstacles and ensures thorough coverage. |
| **Self-Cleaning Dock** | Automatically washes and dries mop pads | Saves manual effort and maintains hygiene. |
| **Carpet Detection** | Lifts mop pads on rugs | Prevents wet carpets and damage. |
| **Smart Home Integration** | Alexa, Google Assistant, IFTTT | Allows voice control and automation routines. |
| **Battery Life** | 90–180 minutes | Longer battery covers more area without recharging. |
| **No-Mop Zones** | Virtual boundaries in app | Keeps mops away from carpets or sensitive areas. |

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

Setting up a robot mop in 2026 is easier than ever, but there are a few key steps to ensure seamless operation.

### Installation Process

1. **Unbox and Charge:** Most models come partially charged, but a full charge (2–3 hours) is recommended before first use.
2. **Download the App:** Install the manufacturer’s app (compatible with iOS and Android) and create an account.
3. **Map Your Home:** Run the initial mapping cycle. The robot will explore your home and create a digital layout. You can then label rooms and set no-mop zones.
4. **Place the Dock:** Ensure the dock is on a hard, level surface with at least 3 feet of clearance on each side.
5. **Fill the Water Tank:** Use clean water or a manufacturer-recommended cleaning solution. Avoid vinegar or bleach, which can damage components.

### Smart Home Integration

- **Voice Control:** Connect to Alexa or Google Assistant to start, stop, or schedule cleaning with simple commands like “Alexa, ask [robot name] to mop the kitchen.”
- **Automation:** Use IFTTT or SmartThings to trigger cleaning based on events—for example, when you leave home (via geofencing) or when a motion sensor detects activity.
- **Scheduling:** Set daily or weekly schedules in the app. Most robots can handle multiple cleaning sessions per day.
- **Firmware Updates:** Keep the robot’s software updated via the app to access new features and bug fixes.

**Pro Tip:** Place virtual no-go zones around pet food bowls and water dishes to avoid spills.
