### Navigation Technology

This is the most critical differentiator. **LiDAR** (Light Detection and Ranging) uses lasers to create a precise map of your home. It works in total darkness and is highly accurate. **Camera-based systems** add visual recognition, allowing the robot to identify specific objects like shoes or cables. The best systems combine both. If you have a cluttered home with lots of obstacles, prioritize AI obstacle avoidance. If your floors are relatively clear, a LiDAR-only system will suffice.

### Self-Emptying and Self-Cleaning

A robot vacuum is only as good as its maintenance routine. **Self-emptying bases** are now standard on premium models, and they're a game-changer. Instead of emptying the dustbin after every use, you'll only need to replace the bag every 4-8 weeks. **Self-washing mop docks** take it a step further, cleaning and drying the mop pads so you never have to touch a dirty cloth. These features add significant cost but dramatically improve the ownership experience.

### Suction Power

Measured in Pascals (Pa), suction power ranges from 2,000Pa (basic) to 12,000Pa (flagship). For hard floors, anything above 3,000Pa is sufficient. For carpets, you'll want at least 6,000Pa. Pet owners should prioritize higher suction and look for tangle-resistant brush designs.

### Mopping Capability

Most modern robot vacuums are actually **robot vacuum and mop combos**. The quality of mopping varies widely. Vibrating pads (like Roborock) scrub in place, while rotating pads (like Dreame) mimic a manual mop. Both work well for maintenance cleaning, but neither will replace a dedicated mop for deep cleaning. Look for models that automatically lift the mop pads when they detect carpet.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Setting Up Your Robot Vacuum

Setting up a modern robot vacuum is refreshingly simple:

1. **Unbox and Charge:** Place the robot on the dock and let it fully charge (typically 3-4 hours).
2. **Download the App:** Each brand has its own app (Roborock, Dreame, Eufy). Create an account and follow the pairing instructions. This usually involves connecting to the robot's Wi-Fi network temporarily.
3. **Create Your Map:** The first cleaning run will be a "mapping" run. The robot will explore your home, creating a detailed floor plan. This usually takes 20-40 minutes depending on the size of your home.
4. **Customize:** Once mapped, you can set no-go zones (e.g., around pet bowls), virtual walls, and room-specific cleaning schedules. You can also adjust suction power and water flow for mopping.
5. **Schedule:** Set a daily cleaning schedule. Most users find that running the robot every day or every other day keeps floors consistently clean.

### Smart Home Integration

All three recommended models integrate seamlessly with the major smart home platforms:

- **Amazon Alexa:** "Alexa, tell Roborock to clean the kitchen." You can enable the skill and then use voice commands to start, stop, or check the status of your robot.
- **Google Home:** Similar voice control is available. You can also add the robot to Google Home routines. For example, have it start cleaning when you say "Good morning."
- **Apple HomeKit:** This is where things differ. As of 2026, **Eufy** is the only brand with native HomeKit support. Roborock and Dreame rely on Alexa and Google. If you're deeply invested in the Apple ecosystem, this is a significant consideration.
- **IFTTT and Automation:** For power users, IFTTT (If This Then That) applets allow for complex automations. For instance, you can have the robot start cleaning when your smart door lock detects you've left for work.

### Privacy Considerations

Robot vacuums with cameras (Roborock and Dreame) have raised privacy concerns. The cameras are used for obstacle avoidance and are not meant for surveillance. However, the data is processed on-device, and the manufacturers have privacy policies that restrict data sharing. If this concerns you, the Eufy X10 Pro Omni's LiDAR-only navigation is the safer choice.
