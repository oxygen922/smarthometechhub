## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Robot Vacuum?

Robot vacuums are ideal for:
- **Busy professionals** who want clean floors without daily effort
- **Pet owners** dealing with constant shedding
- **Families with kids** who track in dirt and crumbs
- **Seniors** who find traditional vacuuming physically demanding
- **Tech enthusiasts** who enjoy automation

They're less suitable for:
- **Homes with thick, high-pile carpets** (most robots struggle)
- **Cluttered spaces** with many obstacles
- **Those on a tight budget** (good models start around $300)

### Budget Considerations

- **Entry-Level ($200-$400):** Basic navigation, lower suction, no self-emptying. Good for small apartments with hard floors.
- **Mid-Range ($400-$700):** Better navigation, self-emptying optional, decent mopping. Best value for most homes.
- **Premium ($700-$1,200):** Top-tier suction, advanced obstacle avoidance, self-emptying, excellent mopping. For large homes and pet owners.

### Floor Type Compatibility

- **Hardwood/Tile:** Any robot works well. Look for mopping functionality.
- **Low-Pile Carpet:** 3,000+ Pa suction required.
- **Medium-Pile Carpet:** 4,000+ Pa with self-adjusting brush roll.
- **High-Pile Carpet/Rugs:** Most robots struggle. Consider a dedicated upright vacuum instead.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Unboxing and Setup

Setting up a modern robot vacuum takes about 15-20 minutes. Here's the typical process:

1. **Unpack and charge:** Remove all packaging, plug in the base station, and place the robot on it for initial charging (usually 3-4 hours).
2. **Download the app:** Most brands have dedicated apps for iOS and Android. Create an account and follow the pairing instructions.
3. **Map your home:** Run the first cleaning cycle with the robot exploring your space. This creates a digital map for future cleanings.
4. **Set no-go zones:** In the app, mark areas you want the robot to avoid (pet bowls, cords, kids' toys).
5. **Schedule cleanings:** Set daily or weekly schedules. Many robots can clean specific rooms on different days.

### Smart Home Integration

All three recommended models work with major smart home platforms:

- **Amazon Alexa:** "Alexa, ask [robot name] to clean the kitchen."
- **Google Assistant:** "Hey Google, start vacuuming."
- **Apple HomeKit:** Available on select models (Eufy X10 supports it).

Advanced integrations include:
- **IFTTT:** Create routines like "When I leave home, start vacuuming."
- **Geofencing:** Automatically clean when you're away.
- **Voice control:** Pause, resume, or direct to specific rooms.

### Maintenance Tips

- **Empty the dustbin** after every 2-3 cleanings (or let the self-emptying base handle it).
- **Clean the brush roll** weekly to remove hair tangles.
- **Replace filters** every 3-6 months depending on usage.
- **Wipe sensors** monthly with a dry cloth for accurate navigation.
