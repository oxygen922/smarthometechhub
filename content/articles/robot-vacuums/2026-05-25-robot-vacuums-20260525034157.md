## Key Features to Consider

When buying a robot vacuum, not all features are created equal. Here’s what you need to look for:

| Feature | Why It Matters | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| **Suction Power** | Higher suction (measured in Pa) lifts dirt from carpets. 2,500Pa+ is good; 5,000Pa+ is excellent. | Homes with carpets and pets |
| **Navigation** | LiDAR (laser) is best for fast, accurate mapping. Camera-based systems can avoid obstacles better. | All homes |
| **Obstacle Avoidance** | Essential for homes with cables, pet bowls, or kids’ toys. AI cameras are best. | Families and pet owners |
| **Self-Emptying** | Automatically empties dustbin into a bag. Saves you from emptying daily. | Busy households |
| **Mopping** | Some vacuums also mop. Look for ones with liftable mop pads to avoid wetting carpets. | Homes with hard floors |
| **Mop Washing/Drying** | High-end models wash and dry the mop pad in the dock. Prevents mold and smells. | Users who mop frequently |
| **App & Voice Control** | Must work with Alexa/Google Assistant. App should allow scheduling, zone cleaning, and map editing. | Smart home users |
| **Battery Life** | 120–180 minutes is standard. Larger homes need longer runtime or auto-recharge. | Homes over 2,000 sq ft |

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Robot Vacuum?

- **Busy Professionals & Families:** If you don’t have time to vacuum daily, a robot vacuum can keep floors clean with minimal effort.
- **Pet Owners:** Models with high suction, tangle-free brushes, and good filters are essential for pet hair and dander.
- **Seniors or People with Mobility Issues:** A robot vacuum removes the physical strain of pushing a heavy vacuum.
- **Tech Enthusiasts:** If you love smart home gadgets, a robot vacuum is a natural addition to your ecosystem.

### Who Should Skip It?

- **Homes with High-Pile Carpets:** Most robot vacuums struggle with thick shag carpets. You’ll need a powerful upright vacuum.
- **Tight Budgets:** Quality robot vacuums start around $250. Cheaper models often have poor navigation and low suction.
- **Very Small Apartments:** If your entire home is under 500 sq ft, a robot vacuum may not be worth the investment.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $300:** Basic models like the **iRobot Roomba 694** or **Eufy RoboVac 11S** are good for small apartments with hard floors. No self-emptying, basic navigation.
- **$300–$600:** Mid-range models like the **Roborock Q5** or **iRobot Roomba j7** offer better suction, LiDAR navigation, and some smart features.
- **$600–$1,000:** Premium models like the **Dreame Bot L10s Ultra** or **iRobot Roomba j9+** include self-emptying, mopping, and advanced obstacle avoidance.
- **$1,000+:** Flagship models like the **Roborock S8 Pro Ultra** offer the best performance, self-washing mop pads, and the most advanced navigation.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Unboxing and Setup

Most robot vacuums are easy to set up. Here’s the general process:

1. **Unbox** the vacuum, charging dock, and any accessories.
2. **Find a location** for the dock near a power outlet and with enough clearance (usually 1.5 feet on each side).
3. **Charge** the vacuum fully (typically 3–4 hours for first charge).
4. **Download the app** (Roborock, iRobot, Dreame, etc.) and create an account.
5. **Connect** the vacuum to your Wi-Fi (2.4 GHz is recommended for best stability).
6. **Run a mapping run** – let the vacuum explore your home to create a floor plan.
7. **Customize** – set no-go zones, room names, and schedules.

### Smart Home Integration Tips

- **Voice Commands:** Use Alexa or Google Assistant to start cleaning a specific room. Example: "Alexa, ask Roborock to clean the kitchen."
- **Automations:** Set up routines like "Clean the living room when I leave for work" using your smart home hub.
- **Compatibility:** Always check if the vacuum supports your preferred ecosystem (Apple HomeKit is rare; Alexa and Google are standard).
- **Firmware Updates:** Keep the vacuum’s firmware updated for bug fixes and new features.
