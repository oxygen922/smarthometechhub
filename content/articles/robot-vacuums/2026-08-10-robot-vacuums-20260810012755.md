## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Setting Up Your Robot Mop

Most modern robot mops arrive partially assembled, but installation still requires some effort. Here's what to expect:

1. **Dock Placement:** Position the dock against a wall with at least 1.5 feet of clearance on each side and 3 feet in front. Ensure it's on a level surface and near a power outlet.

2. **Water Connection:** Premium models like the Roborock S8 MaxV Ultra can connect directly to your plumbing for automatic water filling and drainage. Simpler models require manual water tank refilling.

3. **App Setup:** Download the manufacturer's app, create an account, and connect your robot to your 2.4GHz Wi-Fi network. The app will guide you through the initial mapping process, which typically takes 10-15 minutes for an average-sized home.

### Smart Home Integration

**Voice Control:**
All three recommended robots work with Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant. You can create routines like "Alexa, clean the kitchen" or schedule cleaning sessions using voice commands. The Ecovacs additionally supports its own YIKO voice assistant for direct commands.

**Automation Platforms:**
- **Apple HomeKit:** While none of these robots natively support HomeKit, you can bridge them using platforms like Home Assistant or IFTTT for basic automation.
- **IFTTT:** Create custom automations like triggering a cleaning session when you leave home or when motion sensors detect no activity for extended periods.
- **Smart Sensors:** Some advanced users integrate robot mops with water leak sensors—if a leak is detected, the robot can be dispatched to mop up the spill.

**Scheduling and Zones:**
The app interfaces allow you to create custom cleaning schedules for each day of the week. You can designate specific rooms, set no-go zones for areas with loose rugs or pet beds, and adjust water flow levels for different floor types. For example, you might use high water flow in the kitchen and low flow on hardwood in the living room.
