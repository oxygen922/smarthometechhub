## Key Features to Consider

Before you buy a robot mop vacuum, understanding the technology behind these machines is crucial. Here’s what you need to know.

### Mopping Systems
- **Single Pad (Oscillating):** Found on iRobot models, these scrub in a side-to-side motion. Good for light cleaning but struggle with dried stains.
- **Dual Spinning Pads:** Used by Roborock and Dreame, these rotate at high speeds, providing deep scrubbing action. Ideal for sticky messes.
- **Ultrasonic or Vibration:** Some models vibrate the pad thousands of times per minute for added cleaning power.

### Navigation and Mapping
- **LiDAR:** Uses lasers to create precise maps. Works in the dark and is highly accurate. Found on Roborock and Dreame.
- **Camera-Based:** Uses visual sensors to identify objects. Good for obstacle avoidance but can struggle in low light. Used by iRobot.
- **Gyroscope:** Cheaper robots use this, but mapping is less accurate.

### Self-Cleaning Docks
- **Auto-Empty:** Vacuums empty their dustbin into a larger bag in the dock.
- **Auto-Mop Wash:** Docks wash the mop pads after each cleaning.
- **Auto-Drying:** Some docks dry the pads with warm air to prevent mold.
- **Heated Washing:** Premium docks use hot water for better cleaning.

### Suction Power
Measured in Pascals (Pa), higher numbers mean better pickup. For homes with carpets, aim for 5,000 Pa or more. For hard floors, 2,500 Pa is sufficient.

### Obstacle Avoidance
Look for models with AI-powered object recognition. This prevents the robot from getting stuck on cords, shoes, or pet waste.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy What?

- **Tech Enthusiasts & Deep Cleaners:** The **Roborock S8 Pro Ultra** is your best bet. Its heated self-cleaning dock and powerful mopping make it a true hands-off experience. If you have mostly hard floors and want the deepest clean possible, this is it.

- **Budget-Conscious Homeowners:** The **Dreame Bot L10s Ultra** offers 90% of the Roborock’s performance for 35% less. If you can live without heated pad washing, this is an incredible value. Perfect for families with mixed flooring.

- **Carpet-Heavy Homes:** The **iRobot Roomba Combo j9+** is your go-to. Its full mop retraction is unmatched for carpet safety. If you have more rugs than hard floors, this is the most reliable choice.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $500:** Look for models like the **Eufy L60** or **Shark AI Ultra**. These offer basic mopping (a water tank and cloth) but lack self-cleaning docks. Good for small apartments with minimal mopping needs.

- **$500–$1,000:** The sweet spot. The **Dreame Bot L10s Ultra** is here, along with the **Roborock Q Revo**. You get strong suction, good mopping, and auto-empty docks.

- **Over $1,000:** Premium territory. The **Roborock S8 Pro Ultra** and **iRobot j9+** live here. Expect self-cleaning docks, advanced navigation, and top-tier performance.

### Installation & Smart Home Integration

Setting up a robot mop vacuum is straightforward, but a few steps ensure optimal performance.

**Installation Process:**
1. **Unboxing:** Remove all packaging and install the side brushes and mop pads.
2. **Dock Placement:** Place the dock on a flat surface against a wall, with at least 3 feet of clearance on each side. Plug it into a grounded outlet.
3. **App Setup:** Download the manufacturer’s app (Roborock, Dreamehome, or iRobot Home). Create an account and follow the on-screen instructions to connect the robot to your Wi-Fi (2.4 GHz is required for most models).
4. **Mapping:** Allow the robot to perform a “Quick Map” run. This takes 10–20 minutes. After mapping, you can edit rooms, set no-go zones, and adjust cleaning schedules.

**Smart Home Integration:**
- **Voice Assistants:** All three recommended models work with **Amazon Alexa** and **Google Assistant**. Simply enable the skill in your voice app and link your robot account. Commands like “Alexa, ask Roborock to clean the kitchen” work flawlessly.
- **Apple HomeKit:** Only the Roborock S8 Pro Ultra supports Siri shortcuts via the Roborock app. The iRobot j9+ has limited HomeKit support through third-party bridges.
- **Routines:** You can create smart home routines, e.g., “When I leave home, start the robot vacuum and mop.” This works with Alexa Routines and Google Home Routines.
- **IFTTT:** For advanced users, IFTTT applets allow integration with other smart devices, like turning off the robot when your security system arms.

**Pro Tip:** For best results, ensure your robot has a clear path under furniture. Raise chair legs and remove low-hanging cables. Most robots can navigate around objects, but a clean floor plan improves efficiency.
