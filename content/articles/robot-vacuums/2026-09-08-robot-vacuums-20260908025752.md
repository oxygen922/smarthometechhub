## Key Features to Consider (With Comparison Table)

Choosing a robot vacuum can feel like learning a new language. Here’s what you need to know to make an informed decision.

**Navigation & Mapping:**
- **LiDAR (Light Detection and Ranging):** Uses a spinning laser to create a precise map of your home. It's fast, accurate, and works in the dark.
- **Camera/VSLAM:** Uses a camera to navigate. Often cheaper but can be less accurate in low light.
- **Gyroscope:** The most basic type, leading to random or less efficient cleaning patterns. **We recommend choosing a model with LiDAR navigation.**

**Suction Power:**
Measured in Pascals (Pa). While higher numbers are better, they aren't everything. 2,000-3,000Pa is fine for hard floors, while 5,000Pa+ is recommended for carpets. In 2026, flagship models boast 10,000-12,000Pa, which is more than enough for any home.

**Mopping System:**
- **Basic (Static Pad):** A simple cloth dragged behind the robot. Not very effective.
- **Vibrating/Spinning Pads:** These scrub the floor more effectively. Look for models with **dual spinning pads** and downward pressure.
- **Mop Lifting:** A crucial feature if you have carpet. The robot lifts the mop pads when it detects carpet to avoid soaking them.

**Dock / Base Station:**
- **Basic:** Just a charging pad; you must empty the bin manually.
- **Self-Emptying:** The dock has a larger dustbin that the robot empties itself into (usually holding 60+ days of dirt).
- **Ultra Dock (All-in-One):** Self-empties, washes mop pads, dries them with hot air, and refills the water tank. This is the gold standard in 2026.

**Obstacle Avoidance:**
A camera and AI allow the robot to recognize and avoid objects like cables, shoes, and pet waste. This is essential for a truly hands-free experience.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

Setting up a modern robot vacuum is surprisingly simple, but there are a few steps to ensure it integrates perfectly into your smart home ecosystem.

**1. Installation and Setup:**
- **Placement:** Choose a permanent spot for the dock. Ensure it has at least 1.5 feet of clearance on each side and 3 feet in front for the robot to dock and undock easily. Connect the dock to power.
- **App Setup:** Download the manufacturer's app (e.g., Roborock, Dreame, eufy Clean). Create an account and add the device by scanning the QR code on the robot or dock. The app will guide you through connecting to your 2.4GHz Wi-Fi network.
- **Mapping Run:** The first time you run the robot, allow it to do a full "mapping run" without cleaning. This allows it to scan your home and create an accurate floor plan. It's best to declutter the floor before this initial run.

**2. Smart Home Integration:**
- **Amazon Alexa & Google Assistant:** All three of our recommended models support voice control. Once linked in the Alexa or Google Home app, you can simply say, "Alexa, ask Roborock to clean the living room," or "Hey Google, start the vacuum." This is the easiest way to start a quick clean.
- **Apple Home / Siri:** Integration is often less direct. You may need to use Siri Shortcuts within the manufacturer's app to create a voice command like "Start the robot" which will trigger a specific cleaning routine.
- **Automation & Routines:** The real power of smart home integration lies in automation. You can create a routine in the manufacturer's app to have the robot vacuum and mop your kitchen every day at 10 AM, and then have it automatically return to the dock to wash and dry its mops. This allows for a completely hands-free cleaning schedule.
