## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Robot Vacuum Cleaner?

- **Pet owners:** Look for high suction, rubber brushes, and strong obstacle avoidance to handle fur and accidents.
- **Families with kids:** Self-emptying bases and scheduled cleaning keep floors crumb-free with zero effort.
- **Apartment dwellers:** Compact budget models handle smaller spaces beautifully.
- **Multi-story homeowners:** Choose a model with multi-floor mapping and long battery life.
- **Tech enthusiasts:** Flagship models with AI cameras and app integrations offer the most automation.

### Who Should Skip One?

If your home is entirely deep-pile carpet, a robot vacuum cleaner is a supplement, not a replacement, for a full-size upright. Likewise, if your budget is extremely tight, a good cordless stick vacuum may serve you better than a cheap robot.

### Budget Considerations

Set your budget around your priorities:

- **Under $300:** Expect basic navigation and no self-emptying. Great for hard floors and small spaces.
- **$400–$700:** The sweet spot. You get LiDAR navigation, decent suction, and often mopping.
- **$800–$1,200:** Flagship territory. Self-emptying, AI avoidance, premium mopping, and the best app experiences.

Remember to factor in long-term costs: replacement brushes, filters, mop pads, and dust bags typically run $40–$100 per year. Many brands offer subscription kits that simplify this.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

Setting up a modern robot vacuum cleaner takes about 15 minutes from unboxing to first clean.

1. **Unbox and charge:** Most units arrive partially charged but need a full charge before the first run. Place the charging dock against a wall on a level surface with at least three feet of clearance on each side.
2. **Download the app:** Create an account and pair the robot via Wi-Fi (2.4GHz is the most reliable band).
3. **Run a mapping pass:** Let the robot explore your home to build its map. This first run may be slower as it learns.
4. **Set no-go zones:** Use the app to block areas like pet bowls, cables, or bathroom thresholds.
5. **Connect voice assistants:** Link to Amazon Alexa, Google Assistant, or Apple Home for hands-free control.

**Smart home integration** is where these devices shine. You can trigger cleaning routines when you leave the house via geofencing, ask your smart speaker to "start vacuuming the kitchen," or schedule cleans around your calendar. Many premium models also integrate with IFTTT and Matter, future-proofing them for years to come.

For best results, keep firmware updated. Manufacturers regularly push navigation improvements and new features over the air.
