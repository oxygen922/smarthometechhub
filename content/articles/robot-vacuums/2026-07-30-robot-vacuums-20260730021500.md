## Installation & Smart Home Integration

**Installation Process:**

1. **Unbox and Charge:** Remove the robot and dock from packaging. Place the dock on a hard, level surface near a power outlet. Allow the robot to fully charge (typically 3–4 hours).
2. **Download the App:** Install the manufacturer's app (iRobot Home, Roborock, Ecovacs Home) on your smartphone and create an account.
3. **Connect to Wi-Fi:** Follow the app's instructions to connect the robot to your home's 2.4GHz Wi-Fi network. Most models require Bluetooth for initial setup.
4. **Map Your Home:** Run a mapping cycle (usually 30–60 minutes) to allow the robot to learn your floor plan. You can then customize no-go zones, cleaning schedules, and room labels.
5. **Set Up Voice Control:** Enable the appropriate skill or action in your smart speaker app (e.g., Amazon Alexa, Google Home) to link the robot mop.

**Smart Home Integration Tips:**

- **Routines:** Create routines like "Good Morning" that trigger the robot mop to clean the kitchen while you make coffee.
- **Geofencing:** Use app-based geofencing to start cleaning when you leave home and stop when you return.
- **Multi-Device Integration:** Pair with smart locks, lights, or thermostats for advanced automation. For example, have the robot mop clean the living room when you arm your security system.
- **Maintenance Reminders:** Set up notifications for filter cleaning, brush replacement, and pad washing to keep your robot performing optimally.

**Common Issues and Solutions:**
- **Wi-Fi Connectivity:** Ensure the robot is within range of your router. Use a Wi-Fi extender if needed.
- **Mapping Errors:** Clear the floor of clutter and run a fresh mapping cycle if the robot gets confused.
- **Dock Placement:** Keep the dock away from direct sunlight, vents, and high-traffic areas to prevent interference.
