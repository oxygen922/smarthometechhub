### Understanding the Specs

- **Suction Power:** Measured in Pascals (Pa), higher numbers mean better pickup on carpets. For homes with pets or high-pile rugs, aim for 4,000Pa or more.
- **Mapping Technology:** LiDAR is the gold standard for accuracy and speed. Camera-based systems are good but can struggle in low light. Gyro navigation is adequate for smaller spaces.
- **Self-Emptying Base:** A game-changer for convenience. Models without it require manual emptying every 2-3 days.
- **Battery Life:** Consider your home’s square footage. Larger homes benefit from 150+ minute runtimes.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

Setting up a modern robot vacuum is refreshingly simple. Most models follow a similar process:

1. **Unbox and Charge:** Place the vacuum on its charging dock and let it fully charge (typically 3-4 hours).
2. **App Setup:** Download the manufacturer’s app, create an account, and connect the vacuum to your Wi-Fi (2.4GHz is recommended for stability).
3. **Mapping Run:** Allow the vacuum to perform an initial mapping cycle. Clear floors of loose items, and be patient—this can take 30-60 minutes.
4. **Customize Settings:** In the app, label rooms, set no-go zones, and create schedules. For example, schedule the living room to clean daily at 10 AM and bedrooms twice a week.

### Smart Home Integration Tips

- **Alexa/Google Assistant:** Use voice commands like “Alexa, ask RoboClean to start cleaning the kitchen.”
- **Apple HomeKit:** The iRobot j9+ supports Siri shortcuts. Create a “Good Morning” scene that triggers the vacuum to clean while you eat breakfast.
- **IFTTT (If This Then That):** Advanced users can create automations, like “If the last person leaves home, start the vacuum.”
- **Automation Routines:** In the Google Home app, set a routine that turns off lights, arms your security system, and starts the vacuum when you say, “Goodnight.”

Pro tip: Place the charging dock in a central location with at least 3 feet of clearance on either side and 5 feet in front for easy docking.
