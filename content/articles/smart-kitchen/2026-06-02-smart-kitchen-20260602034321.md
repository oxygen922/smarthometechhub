## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy What?

- **The Busy Professional:** If you're short on time but value home-cooked meals, the June Oven is your best bet. Its automatic cooking presets eliminate guesswork, and its compact size fits small apartments.

- **The Family Chef:** The Samsung Bespoke AI Family Hub refrigerator is ideal for households with 3+ people. Its inventory management reduces food waste, and the family hub screen keeps everyone connected.

- **The Culinary Enthusiast:** Serious home cooks will appreciate the Anova Precision Oven Pro. Its sous vide and steam capabilities unlock professional techniques, though it requires more manual input.

- **The Budget-Conscious Buyer:** Look for mid-range models from brands like GE Profile or LG. Their smart ovens and refrigerators offer solid features without the premium price tag (expect $1,500-$2,500 for a smart fridge, $800-$1,200 for a smart oven).

### Budget Considerations

- **Entry Level ($500-$1,000):** Smart air fryers, Wi-Fi-enabled slow cookers, and basic smart refrigerators with app control. These lack advanced AI but still offer convenience.

- **Mid-Range ($1,000-$2,500):** The sweet spot. You'll get reliable connectivity, decent app integration, and some AI features. Consider the GE Profile Smart Oven or LG InstaView refrigerator.

- **Premium ($2,500+):** Top-tier performance with full AI, large touchscreens, and premium build. The Samsung Bespoke and June Oven fall here. Expect to pay $4,000-$6,000 for a flagship smart fridge.

**Pro Tip:** Look for manufacturer rebates and bundle deals. Many brands offer discounts when you purchase multiple smart appliances together.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Considerations

- **Refrigerators:** Measure your space carefully, including door swing clearance. Most smart fridges require a dedicated 15-amp outlet and a water line for ice makers. Professional installation is recommended ($150-$300).

- **Ovens:** Countertop models like the June and Anova require a standard 120V outlet and at least 6 inches of clearance on all sides for ventilation. Built-in models need a 240V circuit and professional installation.

- **Connectivity Setup:** All three recommended models use a companion app for initial setup. Ensure your Wi-Fi network is stable and supports 2.4GHz and 5GHz bands. For best performance, place your router within 30 feet of the kitchen.

### Smart Home Integration

- **Samsung Bespoke:** Works seamlessly with Samsung SmartThings, Alexa, and Google Assistant. You can create routines like "Good Morning" that turns on the fridge's display to show your calendar and news.

- **June Oven:** Supports Alexa, Google Home, and Apple HomeKit. Use voice commands to preheat, set timers, or check cooking status. June also integrates with IFTTT for custom automations.

- **Anova Precision Oven:** Compatible with Alexa and Google Assistant, plus IFTTT. The app allows you to start cooking remotely, which is perfect for meal prep.

**Integration Tip:** Use a smart plug with energy monitoring for older appliances. This adds basic smart control without replacing your entire kitchen.
