### Sensor Technology & Precision Cooking
The most significant advancement in intelligent ovens is sensor technology. Look for models with multiple temperature sensors, infrared scanning, and weight detection. A smart oven is only as good as its companion app. Evaluate whether the app offers comprehensive recipe libraries, remote monitoring, and push notifications. The best apps provide step-by-step guidance that adapts based on real-time cooking progress.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Requirements

**Countertop Models:** These require minimal setup—simply place on a stable, heat-resistant surface with adequate clearance for airflow. Ensure your Wi-Fi router is within range for reliable connectivity.

**Built-In Ovens:** Professional installation is strongly recommended. You'll need to verify your kitchen's electrical requirements (typically 240V for full-size ovens), cabinet dimensions, and ventilation systems. Hire a licensed electrician and installer to ensure safety and warranty compliance.

### Smart Home Integration Steps

1. **Network Setup:** Connect your oven to your 2.4GHz Wi-Fi network (most smart appliances don't support 5GHz). Check that your router provides adequate coverage in the kitchen area.

2. **App Configuration:** Download the manufacturer's app and create an account. Follow the pairing process, which typically involves scanning a QR code or entering a setup code.

3. **Voice Assistant Linking:** Connect your oven to your preferred voice assistant by enabling the appropriate skill or action in the assistant's app. This allows commands like "Alexa, preheat oven to 350 degrees."

4. **Automation Creation:** Use your smart home platform to create routines. For example, set a morning routine that preheats the oven when you say "Good morning" or schedule a Sunday roast to start cooking at 2 PM.

5. **Matter Integration:** If your oven supports Matter, add it to your Matter-enabled hub for enhanced cross-platform compatibility and local control that works even without internet connectivity.

### Troubleshooting Common Issues
- **Connection Drops:** Move your router closer or install a Wi-Fi mesh system to improve coverage.
- **App Sync Problems:** Restart both the app and oven, then re-pair the devices.
- **Voice Command Failures:** Ensure your voice assistant has the latest version and that the oven skill is properly enabled.
