## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Challenges

- **Major Appliances (Refrigerators):** The ChillHub 360 requires a standard 36-inch opening and a dedicated 115V outlet. However, its water line for the ice maker must be installed by a professional if you don't have an existing one. The unit is heavy (approx. 400 lbs), so delivery and installation services are essential.
- **Countertop Appliances:** The ChefBot and AutoChef are plug-and-play. The only requirement is a stable 15-amp circuit. Do not plug them into a power strip with other high-draw devices, as this can cause tripped breakers.

### Smart Home Integration Setup

1.  **Start with the Hub:** If you have a SmartThings or HomeKit hub, ensure your new appliance supports the same protocol. For the ChillHub 360, it *is* the hub, so setup is straightforward.
2.  **Use Matter for Cross-Platform Control:** In 2026, Matter is the gold standard. Ensure your router is Matter-compatible (most Wi-Fi 6E and Wi-Fi 7 routers are). This allows your ChefBot to be controlled by Alexa in the living room and Google Assistant in the bedroom simultaneously.
3.  **Automation Scenarios:**
    - **Morning Routine:** Set your coffee maker to start when your smart alarm goes off, and have the AutoChef begin slow-cooking dinner at 3 PM via a voice command.
    - **Safety Scenarios:** Use the ChillHub’s door sensor to send an alert if the fridge is left open for more than 60 seconds, and automatically cut power to the ChefBot if a smoke detector triggers.
4.  **Network Requirements:** All three devices require a 2.4GHz network for initial setup. However, for the best performance, a tri-band Wi-Fi 6E router is recommended to keep IoT traffic isolated from your streaming and gaming devices.
