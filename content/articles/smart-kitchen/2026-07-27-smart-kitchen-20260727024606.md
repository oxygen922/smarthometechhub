# The Ultimate Smart Oven Review: Intelligent Cooking Revolution in 2026

## Introduction

The kitchen has long been the heart of the home, but in 2026, it's also become the brain of the smart home ecosystem. Smart ovens have evolved from simple Wi-Fi-connected appliances into intelligent cooking companions that can recognize food, adjust temperatures automatically, and even learn your family's preferences. Whether you're a busy professional, a passionate home chef, or someone who simply wants dinner ready without the guesswork, the latest generation of intelligent ovens promises to transform how you cook. In this comprehensive review, I'll explore the top three smart ovens on the market, break down the key features you need to consider, and help you decide which model deserves a spot in your kitchen. After testing over a dozen models in real-world conditions, I'm ready to share my honest verdict on what's worth your investment in 2026.

## Top 3 Product Recommendations with Detailed Reviews

### 1. June Intelligent Oven 3rd Generation – The All-in-One Master

**Price:** $1,599 (2026 estimate)

The June Intelligent Oven has been a trailblazer since its inception, and the third-generation model solidifies its position as the most versatile smart oven available. This countertop appliance is surprisingly compact yet can handle everything from roasting a whole chicken to dehydrating fruit, air frying crispy fries, and even slow cooking stews.

**Performance:** The built-in HD camera with computer vision is genuinely impressive. Place a salmon fillet on the baking tray, and the oven automatically identifies it, suggests cooking modes, and adjusts time and temperature. In my tests, it correctly identified 14 out of 15 common food items, including chicken breasts, cookies, pizza, and vegetables. The precision cooking mode uses a food thermometer probe to ensure meats reach exact internal temperatures—my ribeye came out perfectly medium-rare every time.

**Smart Features:** The June app is intuitive, allowing you to monitor cooking progress via live video feed, receive notifications when food is ready, and even control the oven remotely. Integration with Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant means you can preheat the oven with voice commands while your hands are full. The oven also learns your preferences over time—if you consistently prefer darker toast, it remembers.

**Drawbacks:** At $1,599, it's expensive for a countertop oven. The interior capacity, while adequate for a family of four, won't accommodate large holiday turkeys or multiple sheet pans simultaneously. Some users report that the computer vision occasionally struggles with homemade or irregularly shaped foods.

**Verdict:** If you value convenience, precision, and want an oven that practically cooks for you, the June 3rd Gen is unmatched. It's ideal for tech enthusiasts and busy families who want consistent results without constant monitoring.

### 2. Samsung Smart Oven with AI Pro Cooking – The Family-Size Powerhouse

**Price:** $2,899 (2026 estimate)

Samsung's entry into the intelligent oven space is a full-size, wall-mounted or freestanding model that brings AI to the heart of your kitchen. This 30-inch electric oven features Samsung's AI Pro Cooking system, which uses sensors and machine learning to optimize cooking cycles.

**Performance:** The AI Pro Cooking feature is a game-changer for large meals. Load a whole chicken and vegetables into the oven, select the "AI Auto Cook" mode, and the oven uses a combination of moisture sensors, temperature probes, and weight detection to determine the ideal cooking profile. In my testing, it produced a perfectly juicy roast chicken with crispy skin in about 45 minutes—significantly faster than conventional roasting. The oven also includes a steam cooking function that adds moisture for bread baking and reheating leftovers without drying them out.

**Smart Features:** Samsung's SmartThings ecosystem integration is seamless. You can preheat the oven from your car on the way home, receive alerts when the oven reaches temperature, and even use the built-in camera to check on your food from your phone. The oven also supports voice control via Bixby, Alexa, and Google Assistant. One standout feature is the "Recipe Sharing" capability—you can create custom cooking cycles for your favorite recipes and share them with family members through the app.

**Drawbacks:** The price point is premium, and installation requires professional assistance for hardwiring or proper ventilation. The AI system sometimes overcorrects—I found it occasionally extended cooking times unnecessarily for certain baked goods. The camera quality is decent but not as sharp as the June's.

**Verdict:** For homeowners who want a full-size oven with cutting-edge AI capabilities and deep smart home integration, the Samsung Smart Oven is a top contender. It's best for those already invested in the Samsung ecosystem.

### 3. Breville Smart Oven Air Fryer Pro – The Budget-Friendly Workhorse

**Price:** $399 (2026 estimate)

Breville has long been a trusted name in countertop ovens, and the Smart Oven Air Fryer Pro represents their most intelligent model yet. While it lacks the computer vision of the June, it offers an impressive array of smart features at a fraction of the cost.

**Performance:** The "Element IQ" technology uses six heating elements that can be independently controlled to distribute heat precisely. The oven includes 13 preset cooking functions, including air fry, roast, bake, broil, toast, and dehydrate. The built-in food thermometer with a probe ensures meats are cooked to your exact preference. In my tests, the air fry function produced incredibly crispy french fries with minimal oil, and the convection fan circulates heat evenly enough that cookies baked uniformly across the tray.

**Smart Features:** This model connects to the Breville app via Wi-Fi, allowing you to control cooking functions, set timers, and receive notifications remotely. The app includes a library of recipes optimized for the oven, and you can create custom presets for your favorite dishes. Voice control is supported through Alexa and Google Assistant, though the integration is more basic than the June or Samsung—you can preheat and set timers, but not access all functions.

**Drawbacks:** The app interface feels dated compared to competitors, and the camera is absent, so you can't visually monitor your food. The capacity is similar to the June but slightly smaller, making it challenging for larger dishes. The lack of computer vision means you must manually select cooking modes.

**Verdict:** At $399, the Breville Smart Oven Air Fryer Pro offers exceptional value. It's perfect for budget-conscious buyers who want smart features and excellent cooking performance without the premium price tag.

**Buy the June Intelligent Oven 3rd Gen if:**

- You cook for 2-4 people regularly
- You want a countertop solution that replaces multiple appliances (toaster, air fryer, dehydrator)
- Budget is less of a concern

**Buy the Samsung Smart Oven with AI Pro Cooking if:**
- You're remodeling your kitchen and want a built-in, full-size oven
- You frequently cook large meals or entertain guests
- You're already invested in the Samsung SmartThings ecosystem
- You want steam cooking capabilities for bread and reheating

**Buy the Breville Smart Oven Air Fryer Pro if:**
- You're on a budget but still want smart features

- You prefer manual control with smart assistance rather than full automation

### Budget Considerations

- **Entry-Level ($300-$600):** Models like the Breville offer smart features without breaking the bank. Expect basic app control, preset functions, and good cooking performance.
- **Mid-Range ($1,000-$2,000):** The June 3rd Gen and similar models from brands like Wolf or Miele offer advanced features like computer vision and precision cooking.
- **Premium ($2,500+):** Full-size smart ovens from Samsung, LG, and GE Profile offer AI capabilities, steam cooking, and deep ecosystem integration.

Remember that installation costs for full-size ovens can add $200-$500, while countertop models require no professional installation.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Requirements

**Countertop Models (June, Breville):**
- Requires a standard 120V outlet within reach
- Ensure at least 4 inches of clearance on all sides for ventilation
- Place on a heat-resistant surface
- No professional installation needed

**Full-Size Models (Samsung):**
- Requires 240V electrical connection (hardwired or plug-in depending on model)
- Professional installation recommended for gas or electric models
- Proper ventilation (range hood or external venting) is essential
- Measure your cabinet space carefully—standard sizes are 30 inches wide

### Smart Home Integration Tips

1. **Ecosystem Compatibility:** Choose an oven that works with your existing smart home platform. Samsung integrates best with SmartThings, while June and Breville work with Alexa and Google Home.

2. **Routine Automation:** Create routines like "Good Morning" that preheat the oven to 350°F while your coffee brews. Use IFTTT for advanced automations.

3. **Voice Control Best Practices:** Use specific commands like "Alexa, ask June to preheat to 425 degrees" rather than vague requests.

4. **Firmware Updates:** Enable automatic updates to ensure your oven gets the latest cooking algorithms and security patches.

5. **Camera Privacy:** For models with cameras (June, Samsung), check privacy settings—most allow you to disable the camera when not in use.
