- **App Connectivity:** Allows remote preheating, monitoring, and recipe guidance.

### Installation Tips

- **Countertop Models:** Simply plug into a standard 120V outlet. Ensure at least 4 inches of clearance on all sides for ventilation.
- **Built-in Models:** Requires a 30-inch cutout and a dedicated 240V circuit. Professional installation is strongly recommended to ensure proper electrical and ventilation connections.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Setting Up Your Smart Oven

1. **Unbox and Position:** Place the oven on a level, heat-resistant surface. For countertop models, avoid placing near the edge.
2. **Connect to Wi-Fi:** Use the manufacturer’s app to connect the oven to your 2.4 GHz home network. Most models require this for full smart functionality.
3. **Pair with Voice Assistants:** Enable the appropriate skill in your Alexa or Google Home app. You can then say commands like, “Alexa, preheat the oven to 375°F.”
4. **Configure Routines:** Use IFTTT or the app’s built-in automation to create routines. For example, “When I leave work, preheat the oven to 400°F.”

### Smart Home Ecosystem Compatibility

- **Amazon Alexa:** All three ovens support basic commands like preheating, setting timers, and checking status.
- **Google Assistant:** Supported by June and GE Profile; Breville only offers Alexa.
- **Apple HomeKit:** Not natively supported by any of these models, but you can use a third-party bridge like Homebridge for limited integration.
- **IFTTT:** GE Profile offers the most robust IFTTT integration, allowing you to connect with smart lights, sensors, and more.

### Troubleshooting Common Issues

- **Wi-Fi Disconnects:** Ensure your router is within range and consider using a mesh network for larger homes.
- **App Not Syncing:** Reboot the oven and your phone, then re-pair via the app.
- **Uneven Cooking:** Check that the oven is level and not overcrowded. Use the convection setting for better air circulation.
