# The Best Smart Ovens of 2026: A Complete Buyer's Review

## Introduction

The intelligent oven has quietly become one of the most compelling upgrades in the modern smart kitchen. Unlike the early "connected" ovens that simply pinged your phone when preheating finished, today's smart ovens use cameras, AI-powered food recognition, and app-based remote control to genuinely change how you cook. In 2026, the category has matured dramatically, with major brands delivering reliable performance, meaningful automation, and seamless integration with ecosystems like Amazon Alexa, Google Home, and Apple Home.

Whether you're a busy professional who wants dinner started from the office, a home baker chasing precision, or a tech enthusiast building out a fully connected home, there's now a smart oven that fits. In this review, I'll walk you through my top three recommendations, the features that actually matter, how to choose the right model for your household, and what installation and integration really look like. Let's dig in.

---

## Top 3 Smart Oven Recommendations

### 1. June Intelligent Oven Pro (2026 Edition) — Best Overall

The June Intelligent Oven Pro remains the benchmark for what a truly intelligent oven should be. The 2026 edition refines the formula with a faster processor, improved internal camera accuracy, and a redesigned carbon-fiber heating element that preheats in under three minutes.

What sets June apart is its food-recognition AI. Place a salmon fillet or a tray of cookies inside, and the oven identifies what it's looking at and suggests a cook profile automatically. In my testing, recognition accuracy was impressive—roughly 9 out of 10 common foods were correctly identified, and the suggested cook times were consistently close to ideal. The companion app is polished, letting you monitor via live camera feed, adjust temperature remotely, and receive "your food is ready" notifications.

**Performance:** Roasting, baking, and air-frying all performed admirably. The convection system distributes heat evenly, and the built-in food probe takes the guesswork out of proteins. The touchscreen interface is responsive and intuitive, though some users may find the menu depth a bit overwhelming at first.

**Pros:**
- Best-in-class food recognition AI
- Live camera monitoring from your phone
- Fast preheat and even cooking
- Regular over-the-air software updates

**Cons:**
- Premium price point (around $1,499 in 2026)
- Interior capacity is smaller than a full-size wall oven
- Requires a stable Wi-Fi connection to unlock full features

**Verdict:** If you want the smartest oven money can buy and mostly cook for one to four people, the June Pro is the one to beat.

---

### 2. Samsung Bespoke AI Oven with SmartThings — Best Full-Size Smart Oven

For households that need a full-size range but still want serious intelligence, Samsung's Bespoke AI Oven is the standout. This 30-inch slide-in range pairs a sleek, customizable design with Samsung's SmartThings ecosystem, which has become one of the most robust smart home platforms available.

The headline feature is the AI Pro Cooking system, which uses an internal camera and sensors to recognize up to 80 different dishes and recommend optimal settings. It also sends alerts to your phone or TV if it detects burning or excess smoke—a genuinely useful safety touch. The oven supports remote preheat, voice control via Alexa and Bixby, and recipe-guided cooking that syncs step-by-step instructions to the appliance.

**Performance:** As a conventional oven, it's excellent—even baking, strong broiling, and a fast convection mode. The smart features are additive rather than gimmicky, and integration with SmartThings means your oven can participate in routines (for example, preheating when your smart lock detects you arriving home).

**Pros:**
- Full-size capacity with genuine smart functionality
- Deep SmartThings ecosystem integration
- Burn/smoke detection alerts
- Customizable Bespoke panel colors

**Cons:**
- AI recognition is slightly less accurate than June's
- App can feel cluttered
- Premium pricing (roughly $2,299–$2,599 depending on configuration)

**Verdict:** The best choice for families who want a full-size oven that plays nicely with a broader smart home setup.

---

### 3. GE Profile Smart Oven with Air Fry — Best Value Smart Oven

GE Profile's Smart Oven with Air Fry delivers a surprising amount of intelligence for the money. Priced around $1,199 in 2026, it undercuts the June Pro while offering a larger capacity and many of the same connected conveniences.

The built-in Chef Connect and GE Profile app allow remote preheat, temperature monitoring, and voice control through Alexa and Google Assistant. The air-fry mode is legitimately good—crispy results without the dedicated countertop appliance. GE's "No Preheat" mode is a lifesaver for weeknight meals, and the scan-to-cook feature lets you scan a barcode on packaged foods to automatically set the right cook time and temperature.

**Performance:** Cooking results are solid across the board, though the smart features are a step behind June and Samsung in sophistication. There's no food-recognition camera, so the "intelligence" leans more on app control and guided cooking than true AI. Still, for the price, it's a compelling package.

**Pros:**
- Excellent value for a connected oven
- Scan-to-cook and No Preheat modes
- Works with Alexa and Google Assistant
- Larger capacity than the June Pro

**Cons:**
- No camera-based food recognition
- App is functional but less polished
- Air fry results vary by recipe

**Verdict:** The smart oven to buy if you want meaningful connectivity without the flagship price tag.

Before you buy, here's what separates a genuinely useful intelligent oven from a marketing gimmick.

| **Food Recognition AI** | Auto-sets cook profiles | ✅ Excellent | ✅ Good | ❌ None |

**The features that truly matter:**

- **Reliable app control.** Remote preheat and monitoring are the most-used smart features in real households. Prioritize a stable, well-reviewed app.
- **Food recognition vs. guided cooking.** Camera-based AI is convenient but not essential. Guided cooking and scan-to-cook deliver most of the benefit for less money.
- **Ecosystem compatibility.** If you're invested in SmartThings, Google Home, or Alexa, choose an oven that integrates natively.
- **Safety sensors.** Burn and smoke detection alerts are underrated—especially for remote monitoring.
- **Software update cadence.** A smart oven is only as smart as its ongoing firmware support. Check that the manufacturer has a track record of updates.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Oven?

**Tech-forward home cooks** who already use smart speakers and connected appliances will get the most value. The ability to preheat from your car or check on a roast from the living room is genuinely convenient.

**Busy professionals and parents** benefit from remote monitoring and guided cooking—you can start dinner remotely and get alerted when it's done.

**Home bakers** will appreciate precision probes and consistent convection, though a dedicated smart oven isn't strictly necessary for baking success.

**Who should skip it?** If you rarely use your phone in the kitchen, or you're on a tight budget, a high-quality conventional oven will cook just as well. The premium for "smart" features is real, and you should only pay it if you'll actually use them.

### Budget Considerations

- **Entry-level smart ovens:** $800–$1,200 (basic app control, voice assistant support)
- **Mid-range:** $1,200–$1,800 (air fry, guided cooking, better apps)
- **Flagship:** $1,800–$2,600+ (AI recognition, cameras, full ecosystem integration)

Factor in installation costs ($150–$400 for a slide-in range, more for wall ovens) and potential electrical work if you're upgrading circuits.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

**Installation:** Full-size slide-in ranges like the Samsung and GE models require a standard 240V outlet and roughly 30 inches of clearance. Most retailers offer professional installation, which I strongly recommend for gas models and any wall-oven configuration. The June Pro is a countertop appliance—no installation required, just a standard outlet and Wi-Fi.

**Wi-Fi and app setup:** All three ovens require a 2.4GHz Wi-Fi connection for initial setup (some support 5GHz after pairing). Budget 15–30 minutes for firmware updates and account creation.

**Ecosystem integration:** The Samsung Bespoke shines here, slotting into SmartThings routines for true automation—preheat triggered by your arrival, for example. The June and GE models work well with Alexa and Google Assistant for voice commands but offer less deep automation. If you're building a whole-home smart setup, ecosystem fit should weigh heavily in your decision.

The smart features are genuinely useful—but a great oven that happens to be connected will always beat a connected oven that can't cook.
