## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Tips

- **ChefMate Pro 360:** Requires a standard 30-inch cutout and a 240V outlet. Professional installation recommended for gas line connections (if applicable). Ensure your kitchen has a strong Wi-Fi signal near the oven.
- **FrostGuard Elite 650:** Standard 36-inch width. Needs a water line for the ice maker and dispenser. The touchscreen requires a stable internet connection. Allow 2-3 inches of clearance on sides for ventilation.
- **AquaSync Pro 800:** Standard 24-inch under-counter installation. Ensure your sink’s drain and water lines are accessible. The “LeakGuard” sensor may require placement under the unit.

### Smart Home Integration

All three appliances support major ecosystems, but integration depth varies:

- **Amazon Alexa:** Voice commands for preheating, checking fridge inventory, and starting dishwasher cycles work flawlessly. Routines can link multiple devices—e.g., “Alexa, start dinner” preheats the oven and dims the lights.
- **Google Home:** Similar functionality, with the added benefit of Google Assistant’s proactive suggestions (e.g., “Your oven is preheated, and you have chicken in the fridge that expires tomorrow”).
- **Apple HomeKit:** Limited support—only the dishwasher offers native HomeKit integration via Matter. The oven and fridge require third-party bridges.
- **Matter Protocol:** The AquaSync Pro 800 is Matter-ready, ensuring future compatibility. The other two are expected to receive Matter updates by late 2026.

**Pro Tip:** Use a dedicated 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network for smart appliances to avoid interference from 5 GHz devices. Consider a mesh Wi-Fi system if your kitchen is far from the router.
