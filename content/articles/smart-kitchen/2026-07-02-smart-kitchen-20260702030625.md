# The Smart Kitchen Revolution: 2026’s Best Intelligent Ovens Reviewed

## Introduction

The kitchen has long been the heart of the home, but in 2026, it’s also become the brain of the smart home ecosystem. Intelligent ovens have evolved far beyond simple programmable timers; they now feature AI-powered cooking assistants, voice control integration, and seamless connectivity with other smart devices. Whether you’re a busy professional looking to streamline meal prep, a tech enthusiast eager to automate every aspect of your home, or a home chef seeking precision and consistency, the right smart oven can transform your cooking experience. In this comprehensive review, I’ll break down the top three intelligent ovens on the market, compare key features, and help you decide which model best fits your lifestyle and budget. Let’s dive into the future of cooking.

## Top 3 Product Recommendations with Detailed Reviews

### 1. June Oven Pro 2026 Edition

The June Oven has long been a favorite among smart kitchen enthusiasts, and the 2026 Pro Edition takes things to a new level. This countertop intelligent oven combines a convection oven, air fryer, toaster, and dehydrator into one sleek, app-controlled unit. Its standout feature is the built-in AI camera system that can identify over 200 different foods—from chicken breasts to cookies—and automatically set the optimal cooking time and temperature. The 2026 model adds a new “Chef Mode” that learns your preferences over time, suggesting recipes based on what’s in your fridge (via integration with smart fridges) and your past cooking habits.

**Performance:** I tested the June Oven Pro with a variety of dishes. Roasting a whole chicken took 45 minutes, resulting in juicy meat and crispy skin. The air fryer function produced perfectly golden french fries in 15 minutes without preheating. The temperature accuracy is impressive, staying within 5°F of the set point. The app interface is intuitive, offering step-by-step guided cooking with real-time video of your food.

**Smart Home Integration:** The June Oven works with Amazon Alexa, Google Assistant, and Apple HomeKit. You can preheat the oven with a voice command, receive notifications when your food is done, and even adjust cooking settings remotely. The 2026 model adds Matter support, ensuring compatibility with a wider range of smart home hubs.

**Pros:** Exceptional food recognition, precise cooking, easy cleanup with non-stick interior.
**Cons:** High price point, limited capacity (only fits a 9x13 pan), requires consistent Wi-Fi.

**Price:** $1,299 (estimated 2026 retail)

### 2. Samsung Smart Oven with AI Pro Cooking (Model NE63T8711SS)

Samsung’s entry into the intelligent oven market is a full-size built-in model that leverages the company’s broader smart home ecosystem. The AI Pro Cooking system uses a combination of sensors and a front-facing camera to detect what you’re cooking and adjust temperature, humidity, and cook time in real time. The 2026 version introduces “SmartThings Cooking,” which syncs with Samsung’s Family Hub refrigerators to suggest recipes based on available ingredients and then sends cooking instructions directly to the oven.

**Performance:** This oven excels at multi-stage cooking. I used it to bake a lasagna that required a high initial temperature to brown the cheese, followed by a lower temperature to cook through. The oven automatically transitioned between stages without any intervention. The convection system is powerful and even, producing consistent results across all racks. The self-cleaning cycle uses steam and high heat, reducing the need for manual scrubbing.

**Smart Home Integration:** The Samsung Smart Oven is a hub for Samsung’s SmartThings ecosystem. It can trigger your range hood to turn on when cooking, adjust lighting in your kitchen, and even send alerts to your phone if you leave the oven on. It also supports Alexa and Google Assistant for voice control. The 7-inch touchscreen display on the oven door allows you to access recipes, streaming services (like YouTube cooking channels), and even make video calls.

**Pros:** Large capacity (6.3 cu. ft.), excellent multi-stage cooking, deep smart home integration.
**Cons:** Requires Samsung SmartThings hub for full features, premium price, learning curve for advanced settings.

**Price:** $2,499 (estimated 2026 retail)

### 3. Breville Smart Oven Air Fryer Pro (BOV900BSS)

For those who prefer a countertop model but want professional-grade performance, the Breville Smart Oven Air Fryer Pro is a top contender. This 2026 update includes “Element IQ” technology that adjusts the power of eight heating elements in real time, ensuring even cooking without hot spots. The new “Smart Cook” feature uses a built-in food probe and humidity sensor to automatically determine doneness for meats and baked goods.

**Performance:** This oven is a workhorse. I tested it with a 12-pound turkey breast—the probe accurately detected when the internal temperature reached 165°F and automatically switched to a keep-warm mode. The air fryer function is among the best I’ve tested, producing crispy wings with minimal oil. The oven also has a dedicated “Pizza” setting that cranks the top elements to 500°F for a perfect Neapolitan-style crust. The 13 preset cooking functions cover everything from bagels to slow cooking.

**Smart Home Integration:** The Breville Smart Oven supports Wi-Fi connectivity via the Breville Smart Kitchen app. You can download new cooking presets, receive push notifications, and control the oven remotely. However, it lacks native voice assistant support—you’ll need to use a smart plug or IFTTT for basic voice control. This is a minor drawback for a device that prioritizes cooking performance over ecosystem lock-in.

**Pros:** Excellent cooking quality, versatile presets, durable build, reasonable price.
**Cons:** Limited smart home integration, no camera for food recognition, app could be more polished.

**Price:** $599 (estimated 2026 retail)

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy an Intelligent Oven?

- **Busy Professionals:** If you often come home late and need dinner ready fast, an oven with remote preheating and guided cooking can save you 30–45 minutes per meal. The June Oven Pro is ideal for this group.
- **Smart Home Enthusiasts:** If you already have a Samsung SmartThings, Amazon Alexa, or Apple HomeKit setup, the Samsung Smart Oven offers the deepest integration. You can create routines like “Start preheating when I leave work” or “Turn on oven light when motion detected.”
- **Home Chefs:** For those who love cooking but want precision, the Breville Smart Oven Air Fryer Pro delivers restaurant-quality results at a fraction of the cost of a full-size smart oven. Its probe and humidity sensor are game-changers for meat and bread.
- **Large Families:** A full-size built-in oven like the Samsung model is best if you frequently cook for 6+ people or need to roast large turkeys or multiple dishes simultaneously.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $700:** The Breville Smart Oven Air Fryer Pro offers the best value. You sacrifice some smart features but gain outstanding cooking performance and durability.
- **$1,000–$1,500:** The June Oven Pro is the sweet spot for those who want AI-powered cooking without a major remodel. It’s a premium countertop option that replaces multiple appliances.
- **$2,000+:** The Samsung Smart Oven is a long-term investment. Factor in installation costs (electricians may be needed for 240V models) and the potential need for a SmartThings hub ($69). However, if you’re building or renovating a kitchen, this oven adds significant resale value.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation

- **Countertop Models (June, Breville):** These are plug-and-play. Simply place on a heat-resistant surface near a standard 120V outlet. Ensure at least 6 inches of clearance above and behind for ventilation. No professional installation required.
- **Full-Size Built-In (Samsung):** This requires a dedicated 240V outlet (30–40 amp circuit) and proper cabinet cutouts. Hire a licensed electrician if you don’t have existing wiring. The oven weighs around 150 pounds, so have a helper or professional installer for lifting.

### Smart Home Integration Tips

1. **Create Routines:** Use your smart home app to set up automations. For example:
   - “Good Morning” routine: Turn on oven light, preheat to 350°F, and start brewing coffee.
   - “Dinner Time” routine: Preheat oven to 425°F, dim kitchen lights, and play cooking playlist.

2. **Voice Control:** Enable skills for your preferred assistant. With the Samsung oven, you can say, “Alexa, tell Samsung oven to preheat to 400°F.” For June, “Hey Google, ask June to start roasting chicken.”

3. **Notifications:** Set up alerts for when preheating is complete, when food is done, or if the oven is left on accidentally. This is especially useful for safety.

4. **Integration with Other Devices:** Pair your oven with a smart smoke detector (like Nest Protect) that can automatically turn off the oven if smoke is detected. Or, connect to a smart plug for the Breville to enable basic on/off voice control.
