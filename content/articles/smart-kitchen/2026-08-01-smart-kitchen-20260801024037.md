## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy What

**For the Versatile Home Cook:** The Thermomix TM7 is ideal for those who cook frequently and want to replace multiple appliances. It's particularly valuable for families who appreciate the guided cooking experience and the ability to prepare complete meals in one device. If you have $1,899 to invest and limited counter space, this is the ultimate space-saver.

**For the Tech-Savvy Foodie:** The June Oven Pro 3.0 is perfect for those who love gadgets and want intelligent assistance without a massive investment. At $799, it's the most accessible premium smart appliance. It's excellent for busy professionals who want to cook properly but don't have time to babysit their food.

**For the Serious Home Chef:** If you're renovating your kitchen and want a professional-grade range with smart features, the Samsung Bespoke is the clear choice. It's an investment, but it delivers restaurant-quality results and will last for years. This is for those who view cooking as both a necessity and a passion.

### Budget Considerations

Smart kitchen appliances range from budget-friendly to premium. Consider your long-term value:

- **Entry-level ($200-$500):** Smart air fryers, Wi-Fi-enabled slow cookers, and smart scales
- **Mid-range ($500-$1,000):** Advanced countertop ovens, smart coffee makers, and multi-cookers
- **Premium ($1,000+):** Full-size smart ranges, high-end multi-cookers, and integrated systems

Remember to factor in installation costs for built-in appliances and potential electrical upgrades for induction ranges.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Requirements

**Countertop Appliances (Thermomix, June Oven):**
- No special installation required
- Need access to a standard 120V outlet
- Ensure adequate ventilation clearance (at least 4 inches on all sides)
- Place on a stable, heat-resistant surface

**Full-Size Range (Samsung Bespoke):**
- Requires professional installation
- Needs a dedicated 240V circuit (40-50 amp)
- May require cabinet modifications for proper fit
- Consider hiring a licensed electrician for outlet installation

### Smart Home Integration

All three appliances integrate with major smart home platforms, but the experience varies:

**Thermomix TM7:** The app works independently but can sync with Google Calendar for meal planning. It doesn't natively support voice assistants, but you can use IFTTT for basic automation.

**June Oven Pro 3.0:** Excellent integration with Alexa and Google Assistant. You can preheat the oven with voice commands, receive notifications through smart speakers, and even start cooking from your phone while commuting.

**Samsung Bespoke Range:** This is the most integrated option, especially if you have other Samsung appliances. The SmartThings app creates a unified dashboard for all your connected devices. You can set scenes like "Dinner Time" that trigger the oven, lighting, and music simultaneously.

**Pro Integration Tips:**
- Connect appliances to a 2.4GHz Wi-Fi network for stable connections
- Use smart plugs with energy monitoring to track power consumption
- Set up automation routines: "When I arrive home, preheat oven to 425°F"
- Use smart displays like the Echo Show or Google Nest Hub to view recipe instructions while cooking
