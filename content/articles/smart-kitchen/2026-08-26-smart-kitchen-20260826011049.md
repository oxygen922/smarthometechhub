# The Ultimate Smart Oven Review: Top Intelligent Cooking Appliances of 2026

The modern kitchen has undergone a remarkable transformation, and at the heart of this revolution sits the intelligent oven. As a smart home technology reviewer who has tested dozens of connected appliances over the past decade, I've witnessed firsthand how these devices have evolved from gimmicky novelties to genuinely indispensable kitchen tools. ## Top 3 Product Recommendations

**Price: $1,599 | Rating: 9.5/10**

The June Smart Oven Pro 2.0 remains the gold standard in intelligent cooking, and the 2026 iteration brings meaningful improvements that justify its premium position. This countertop convection oven combines a 12-inch touchscreen display with advanced computer vision technology that can identify over 200 different food items automatically. The moment you place a salmon fillet inside, the oven recognizes it, suggests the ideal cooking mode, and monitors doneness in real-time.

What sets the Pro 2.0 apart is its dual heating elements and improved airflow system that delivers remarkably even cooking results. In my testing, the June produced perfectly crispy roasted vegetables with caramelized edges, while maintaining tender interiors—a feat that many full-size ovens struggle to achieve. The built-in food thermometer connects wirelessly, allowing the oven to automatically adjust temperature and cooking time based on internal food temperature rather than relying on estimates.

The new 2026 model features a faster processor that reduces the time between food recognition and cooking start to under two seconds. The upgraded camera now offers 4K resolution, and you can watch your food cook remotely through the June app while lounging on your couch. The app itself has been redesigned with a cleaner interface and now includes meal planning features that suggest recipes based on what's in your refrigerator.

### 2. Thermador Freedom Smart Wall Oven — Best Built-In Option

**Price: $5,499 | Rating: 9.0/10**

For homeowners undertaking a kitchen renovation or looking to replace their existing wall oven, the Thermador Freedom Smart Wall Oven represents the pinnacle of intelligent built-in cooking. This 30-inch electric oven features a stunning 7-inch LCD display and integrates with the Thermador Home Connect app for seamless remote operation. What truly distinguishes the Freedom is its Steam and Crisp technology, which combines traditional convection heating with steam injection to produce exceptionally moist meats and artisan-quality bread with crackling crusts.

The oven's proprietary CookSmart+ technology uses a combination of sensors to monitor humidity, temperature, and even the color of your food to automatically adjust cooking parameters. During my testing of a prime rib roast, the oven flawlessly executed a reverse-sear technique, gradually bringing the meat to medium-rare perfection before automatically increasing temperature for a final crust-forming blast. The accompanying probe system offers exceptional accuracy, reading temperatures within 1 degree Fahrenheit.

Smart home integration is comprehensive, with compatibility for Amazon Alexa, Google Assistant, and Apple HomeKit. You can preheat the oven from your car using voice commands, receive notifications when your dish is ready, and even troubleshoot issues through the Thermador app. The Freedom also supports remote diagnostics, allowing Thermador technicians to assess and resolve problems without a service visit.

**Pros:**
- Professional-grade cooking performance
- Advanced steam cooking technology
- Excellent smart home integration
- Large capacity (4.5 cubic feet)

**Cons:**
- Very expensive
- Professional installation required
- Learning curve for advanced features

### 3. Breville Smart Oven Air Fryer Pro — Best Value

**Price: $449.95 | Rating: 8.5/10**

Breville has long been a trusted name in countertop appliances, and their Smart Oven Air Fryer Pro delivers exceptional value for those seeking intelligent cooking without the premium price tag of competitors. This versatile appliance combines 13 cooking functions—including air frying, dehydrating, slow cooking, and proofing—into a single, well-designed unit that occupies surprisingly modest counter space.

The Element IQ system uses five independent quartz heating elements that intelligently distribute power to different zones of the oven, ensuring even browning across all food items. When baking cookies, the oven automatically adjusts power distribution to prevent hot spots, resulting in perfectly uniform golden-brown results every time. The integrated food probe allows for precise temperature-based cooking, and the oven's LCD display provides clear feedback on current and target temperatures.

While it lacks the advanced food recognition of the June, the Breville makes up for it with straightforward presets that cover most common cooking tasks. The slow cook function maintains temperatures between 180°F and 220°F, while the air fry mode circulates hot air at high velocity to achieve crispy results with minimal oil. During my testing, the air fryer produced exceptionally crispy french fries and chicken wings that rivaled deep-fried versions.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Oven?

**Tech Enthusiasts:** If you're building a connected home and want appliances that integrate seamlessly with your smart assistant and automation routines, a smart oven is a worthwhile investment. The ability to preheat remotely, receive notifications, and monitor cooking progress from your phone adds genuine convenience.

**Busy Professionals:** For those with demanding schedules, the automation features of intelligent ovens are transformative. The ability to load ingredients in the morning, schedule cooking to begin remotely, and return home to a perfectly cooked meal is a game-changer for work-life balance.

**Home Cooks and Foodies:** If you're passionate about cooking and want consistent, professional-grade results, the precision control and sensor-based monitoring of smart ovens will elevate your culinary creations. The recipe guidance features are particularly valuable for exploring new cuisines and techniques.

**Families:** The safety features and remote monitoring capabilities make smart ovens attractive for households with children. Many models offer app-based controls that allow parents to verify the oven is off when not in use.

### Budget Considerations

**Entry Level ($300-$600):** Models like the Breville Smart Oven Air Fryer Pro offer basic smart features and excellent cooking performance without breaking the bank. These are ideal for apartment dwellers and those new to smart kitchen technology.

**Mid Range ($1,500-$3,000):** The June Pro 2.0 and GE Smart Ovens occupy this space, offering advanced features like food recognition, cameras, and comprehensive app integration. These represent the sweet spot for most serious home cooks.

**Premium ($3,000-$6,000):** Built-in wall ovens from Thermador, Samsung, and LG fall into this category. These offer the largest capacities, most advanced cooking technologies, and best integration with luxury kitchen designs.

**Hidden Costs:** Factor in installation costs for built-in ovens ($200-$500), potential electrical upgrades, and extended warranty options when calculating your total budget.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Professional Installation vs. DIY

Countertop smart ovens require no installation beyond finding a suitable location with adequate clearance for ventilation. However, built-in wall ovens demand professional installation to ensure proper electrical connections, secure mounting, and compliance with local building codes. Most manufacturers recommend certified installers, and some require professional installation to maintain warranty validity.

### Electrical Requirements

Built-in ovens typically require a dedicated 240-volt circuit, while countertop models operate on standard 120-volt outlets. Before purchasing, verify that your kitchen's electrical infrastructure can support your chosen oven. Older homes may need electrical upgrades, adding $500-$1,500 to your total cost.

### Smart Home Ecosystem Compatibility

**Amazon Alexa:** Most smart ovens support Alexa voice commands for preheating, temperature adjustments, and status inquiries. You can integrate oven controls into broader Alexa routines, such as "Good morning" routines that preheat the oven for breakfast.

**Google Assistant:** Similar functionality is available through Google Assistant, with the added benefit of visual responses on smart displays like the Nest Hub, allowing you to see oven status without opening the app.

**Apple HomeKit:** Currently, only select high-end models like the Thermador Freedom offer native HomeKit support. However, you can often bridge compatibility using platforms like Home Assistant or Hubitat.

**IFTTT and Automation:** Many smart ovens support IFTTT applets, enabling creative automation scenarios. For example, you could create a routine that turns on the oven when you leave work or sends a notification when the oven reaches temperature.

### App Integration

The companion apps for smart ovens have improved dramatically in recent years. Look for apps that offer recipe libraries with step-by-step guidance, the ability to create and save custom cooking programs, and over-the-air firmware updates that add new features over time. The best apps provide a seamless experience from meal planning through cooking to cleanup.
