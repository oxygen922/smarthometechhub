# The Intelligent Oven Revolution: Smart Cooking Appliances That Actually Earn Their Wi-Fi

**Smart ovens have evolved from gimmicky gadgets to genuinely indispensable kitchen companions. Here's our expert take on the models worth your money in 2026.**

---

## Introduction

The modern kitchen has undergone a quiet revolution, and at its epicenter sits the intelligent oven. No longer satisfied with simply heating food, today's smart ovens leverage artificial intelligence, computer vision, and app-based control to deliver restaurant-quality results with push-button simplicity. After spending six weeks testing the latest generation of connected cooking appliances, I can confidently say that the technology has finally matured. Gone are the days of finicky Bluetooth pairing and half-baked "smart" features that added complexity without value. The 2026 crop of intelligent ovens offers genuine benefits: automatic recipe execution, precise temperature management, and even camera-assisted doneness detection. Whether you're a busy professional seeking convenience or a culinary enthusiast chasing perfection, there's a smart oven that fits your life. But with prices ranging from $800 to over $5,000, choosing wisely matters. In this comprehensive review, I'll break down the top performers, key features to evaluate, and help you determine which intelligent oven deserves a spot on your countertop or in your cabinetry.

---

## Top 3 Product Recommendations

### 1. June Oven Pro 2.0 — Best Overall for Most Households

The June Oven Pro 2.0 has been a perennial favorite in the smart oven space, and the 2026 iteration refines an already excellent formula. This countertop convection oven features a 12-inch touchscreen, a built-in camera, and a food recognition system that now identifies over 200 food items with impressive accuracy.

**Performance:** In my testing, the June Oven Pro 2.0 nailed everything from frozen pizza (crispy bottom, perfectly melted cheese) to salmon fillets (flaky interior, caramelized exterior). The Neural Engine, as June calls it, uses computer vision to monitor cooking progress and automatically adjusts time and temperature. I was skeptical about the "doneness detection" claims, but watching the oven pull a ribeye at medium-rare while my phone buzzed with a notification was genuinely impressive.

**Smart Features:** The companion app is intuitive, offering remote monitoring, recipe guidance, and the ability to start preheating from your commute. The oven supports Alexa and Google Assistant voice commands, though I found the touchscreen interface so well-designed that voice control felt unnecessary. The Pro 2.0 also includes a food thermometer probe for precise internal temperature targeting.

**Capacity and Design:** With a 0.65 cubic foot interior, it comfortably fits a 12-inch pizza or a 5-pound chicken. The stainless steel exterior and minimalist design look premium on any countertop. The interior light and camera provide a real-time view through the app, which is oddly satisfying to watch.

**Verdict:** At $899, the June Oven Pro 2.0 represents the sweet spot of price and performance. It's not the cheapest smart oven, but it delivers the most complete package for everyday cooking.

---

### 2. Brava Glass 2.0 — Best for Speed and Precision

Brava has carved out a niche with its pure-light cooking technology, and the Glass 2.0 takes the concept to new heights. Instead of heating elements, this oven uses six high-intensity halogen lamps that can be individually controlled to direct energy precisely across the cooking surface.

**Performance:** The speed is genuinely shocking. A frozen chicken breast cooks in 12 minutes. A steak reaches perfect medium-rare in 8 minutes. The Glass 2.0 achieves this by delivering up to 1,500 watts of power directly to the food, with minimal energy wasted on heating the surrounding air. The "zones" feature allows you to cook different foods at different temperatures simultaneously — I successfully cooked salmon and asparagus at the same time, each with their own optimal settings.

**Smart Features:** The 5-inch touchscreen is responsive and the app is well-executed, but the real star is the cooking modes. Brava's "Cook" function uses sensors to detect when food reaches the target doneness and automatically shuts off. The oven also includes a built-in scale, allowing for precise weight-based cooking calculations. Voice control works with Alexa and Google Assistant, and the Brava app includes a growing library of weekly meal plans.

**Capacity and Design:** The Glass 2.0 has a 0.7 cubic foot capacity and the glass construction means you can watch your food cook in real-time without opening the door. It's a conversation piece in any kitchen, though the glass exterior does show fingerprints.

**Verdict:** At $1,295, the Brava Glass 2.0 is a premium investment, but for speed-focused cooks who want exceptional precision, it's worth every penny.

---

### 3. GE Profile Smart Oven with Air Fry — Best Budget-Friendly Option

For those dipping their toes into the smart oven waters without breaking the bank, the GE Profile Smart Oven with Air Fry offers solid performance at a more accessible price point. This countertop unit combines convection cooking, air frying, and smart controls in a compact package.

**Performance:** While it lacks the AI-powered food recognition of the June or Brava, the GE Profile delivers consistent results. I tested it with chicken wings (crispy, not greasy), roasted vegetables (caramelized edges), and a frozen lasagna (bubbling and golden). The air fry function is genuinely effective, circulating hot air at high speed to achieve that fried texture without oil.

**Smart Features:** The GE Profile app allows remote temperature adjustment, preheating, and cooking timers. It integrates with Alexa and Google Assistant for voice control. The oven includes a temperature probe that communicates with the app, alerting you when your meat reaches the desired internal temperature. While the feature set is less sophisticated than the premium options, it covers the essentials reliably.

**Capacity and Design:** The 0.5 cubic foot interior is smaller than the June and Brava, but adequate for a family of four. The stainless steel design is clean and professional, and the unit is lighter than its competitors, making it easier to move if needed.

**Verdict:** At $799, the GE Profile Smart Oven with Air Fry is an excellent entry point for smart cooking. It doesn't have the flashy AI features, but it delivers dependable performance at a reasonable price.

### Additional Features to Evaluate

**Food Recognition and Computer Vision:** The June Oven's ability to identify what you're cooking and adjust settings automatically is a game-changer for convenience. If you frequently cook frozen foods or are new to cooking, this feature reduces guesswork significantly.

**Cooking Zones:** The Brava's multiple cooking zones allow for different temperatures in different areas. This is particularly useful for cooking a full meal at once, but it comes at a premium price.

**Air Frying Capability:** Many smart ovens now include air fry functionality. If you enjoy crispy foods without the oil, look for models with dedicated air fry modes or high-speed convection fans.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy an Intelligent Oven?

**Busy professionals** will benefit most from the automation and remote control capabilities. Starting dinner from your phone during your commute means hot food is ready shortly after you walk in the door.

**Tech enthusiasts** who enjoy connected gadgets will appreciate the app integrations, firmware updates, and data tracking. The June Oven even provides cooking analytics, showing your most-used recipes and average cook times.

**Health-conscious cooks** will find value in the air fry functions and precise temperature control, which can reduce oil usage and produce more consistent results with lean proteins.

**Families** with picky eaters can leverage the large recipe libraries to expand meal options. The June Oven's food recognition is particularly helpful for kids who want to heat up snacks independently.

### Budget Considerations

As with most smart home technology, you pay a premium for the latest features. Here's how to think about budget:

- **Under $500:** You'll find basic smart ovens with Wi-Fi connectivity and app control, but they lack advanced features like food recognition and camera monitoring. These are suitable for simple tasks but won't transform your cooking experience.

- **$500 – $1,000:** This is the sweet spot for most buyers. The June Oven Pro 2.0 and GE Profile offer the best balance of features and value. You get real AI assistance, reliable app integration, and solid cooking performance.

- **Over $1,000:** Premium models like the Brava Glass 2.0 justify their price with cutting-edge technology, superior speed, and multi-zone cooking. If you cook frequently and value precision, the investment pays off over time.

### Long-Term Value

Consider energy efficiency. Smart ovens typically use less energy than full-size ovens because they heat a smaller space more efficiently. Over a year, you might save $50–$100 in electricity costs, which partially offsets the initial investment.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Setup Considerations

All three recommended ovens are countertop units, requiring no installation beyond plugging them into a standard 120V outlet. However, check your kitchen's electrical capacity — the June Oven draws 1,800W, which may trip breakers if your circuit is shared with other high-wattage appliances.

**Space Planning:** Measure your countertop carefully. These ovens are substantial, and you'll need clearance on all sides for ventilation. The Brava Glass, with its see-through design, needs to be positioned where you can actually see the cooking process — which means not tucked into a corner.

### Smart Home Ecosystem Integration

**Alexa and Google Assistant:** All three ovens support voice commands for basic functions like preheating, setting timers, and checking status. You can say, "Alexa, preheat the June oven to 400 degrees," and it will comply.

**Home Automation Scenes:** The June and Brava ovens can be integrated into broader smart home scenes. For example, you can create a "Dinner Time" routine that turns on under-cabinet lighting, sets the oven to preheat, and plays your cooking playlist.

**IFTTT (If This Then That):** Brava offers IFTTT support, allowing for custom automations like sending a notification to your smartwatch when the oven finishes preheating. June and GE have more limited automation capabilities.

**Matter Support:** As of 2026, the June Oven Pro 2.0 has announced Matter compatibility, which will improve interoperability with other smart home platforms. The Brava and GE models don't yet support Matter, though this may change with future firmware updates.

### App Experience

The companion apps are where smart ovens either shine or frustrate. June's app is the gold standard — responsive, well-designed, and packed with useful features like remote camera monitoring and recipe search. Brava's app is functional but feels less polished, with occasional lag. GE's app is basic but reliable, offering remote control and notifications without unnecessary complexity.
