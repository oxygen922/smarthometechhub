## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Oven?

**Perfect for:**
- **Tech enthusiasts** who want to control every aspect of their kitchen from their smartphone
- **Busy professionals** who need to start dinner remotely or want automated cooking processes
- **Home cooks** who struggle with timing and temperature control
- **Health-conscious individuals** who want to reduce oil consumption through air frying
- **Smart home integrators** who want seamless connectivity with existing systems

**Consider alternatives if:**
- You rarely cook and primarily use your oven for frozen meals
- You prefer traditional, hands-on cooking methods
- Your kitchen lacks reliable Wi-Fi coverage
- You're on a very tight budget (basic smart ovens start around $200)

### Budget Considerations

**Entry Level ($200-$400):** Basic smart ovens with app control and limited features. The Breville Smart Oven Air Fryer Pro falls at the upper end of this range and offers excellent value.

**Mid Range ($400-$900):** Ovens with more cooking modes, better sensors, and improved app integration. The June Intelligent Oven Pro at $899 offers the best AI features in this category.

**Premium ($900-$2,500+):** Full-size built-in smart ovens with advanced features like camera monitoring, multi-zone cooking, and professional-grade temperature control. The GE Profile at $2,299 represents the sweet spot for built-in options.

**Long-term cost considerations:**
- Energy efficiency: Smart ovens with better insulation and heating elements can reduce electricity costs
- Replacement parts: Factor in potential costs for accessories like probes and trays
- App subscriptions: Some manufacturers may offer premium recipe services for a monthly fee

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Requirements

**Countertop Models (June, Breville):**
- Requires a dedicated 15-amp electrical outlet
- Needs 6 inches of clearance on all sides for proper ventilation
- Place on a heat-resistant surface
- No professional installation required

**Built-In Models (GE Profile):**
- Professional installation strongly recommended
- Requires 240-volt electrical connection
- Standard 30-inch cabinet cutout (28.5" width x 29.5" height x 24" depth)
- May require additional ventilation if not using a standard wall oven configuration

### Smart Home Ecosystem Integration

**Amazon Alexa:**
- Voice commands: "Alexa, preheat the oven to 350 degrees"
- Status updates: "Alexa, ask June when the chicken will be done"
- Routine automation: Include oven commands in morning or evening routines

**Google Assistant:**
- Similar voice control capabilities to Alexa
- Better integration with Android devices and Google Home displays
- Can display oven status on Nest Hub screens

**Apple HomeKit:**
- Limited native support currently; use manufacturer apps or IFTTT for integration
- June offers HomeKit compatibility through their app

**IFTTT (If This Then That):**
- Create custom automations like "If the oven is preheated, send me a notification"
- Connect with other smart devices for complex routines

**Manufacturer Apps:**
- June: iOS and Android apps with live video streaming, recipe library, and multi-user support
- GE SmartHQ: Comprehensive app with recipe integration and energy monitoring
- Breville: Simple app with preset customization and push notifications

**Pro Tip:** For the best experience, ensure your smart oven is within range of your Wi-Fi router. Consider upgrading to a mesh Wi-Fi system if you experience connectivity issues.
