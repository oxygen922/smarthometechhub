## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy an Intelligent Oven?

- **Busy Professionals:** If you’re short on time but want home-cooked meals, an oven with AI food recognition and remote control (like the June Oven Pro) can dramatically reduce effort.
- **Avid Bakers:** The Bosch Serie 8 with PerfectBake Sensor is ideal for those who demand precision and consistency in baking.
- **Large Families:** The Samsung Smart Oven’s Dual Cook Flex and large capacity make it perfect for cooking multiple dishes at once.
- **Smart Home Enthusiasts:** If you have a connected home ecosystem, choose an oven that integrates seamlessly with your existing devices (Samsung for SmartThings, Bosch for Home Connect).

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $1,500:** Countertop models like the June Oven Pro offer advanced features in a compact, affordable package.
- **$1,500–$2,500:** Mid-range built-in ovens like the Samsung Smart Oven provide excellent value with dual-zone cooking and AI.
- **Over $2,500:** High-end built-in ovens like the Bosch Serie 8 offer premium build quality and specialized sensors for serious cooks.

**Pro Tip:** Factor in installation costs for built-in models ($150–$400) and consider energy savings over time—a more efficient oven can reduce your electricity bill by up to 20%.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Requirements

- **Countertop Ovens (June Oven Pro):** Simply plug into a standard 120V outlet. Ensure at least 4 inches of clearance on all sides for ventilation. No professional help needed.
- **Built-in Ovens (Bosch, Samsung):** Require a 240V outlet and a dedicated circuit (typically 30–40 amps). Professional installation is recommended to ensure proper electrical connection and ventilation. Measure your cabinet cutout carefully—standard sizes are 27 or 30 inches wide.

### Smart Home Integration

- **Amazon Alexa & Google Assistant:** All three ovens support voice commands for preheating, setting timers, and checking status. For example, “Alexa, ask June to preheat to 350°F.”
- **Apple HomeKit:** Currently, only the June Oven Pro offers limited HomeKit support via Siri Shortcuts.
- **Proprietary Ecosystems:** Samsung’s SmartThings and Bosch’s Home Connect allow for deeper integration—create routines like “Good Morning” that preheat the oven while turning on the coffee maker.
- **IFTTT (If This Then That):** All three ovens support IFTTT applets for custom automations, such as sending a notification when cooking is complete.

**Integration Tip:** For a seamless experience, choose an oven that matches your existing smart home platform. If you use Alexa, any of these will work; if you’re invested in Apple HomeKit, the June is your best bet.
