When shopping for smart kitchen appliances, keep these features in mind:

- **Connectivity:** Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, or Zigbee/Z-Wave for integration with your smart home hub.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Tips

1. **Check Your Wi-Fi:** Most smart appliances require a 2.4GHz network. Ensure your router is within range.
2. **Clear Space:** For countertop appliances, leave at least 6 inches of clearance for ventilation.
3. **Update Firmware:** Before first use, connect to the app and install any updates.
4. **Read the Manual:** Each device has unique setup steps—don’t skip this.

### Smart Home Integration

- **Voice Control:** Pair with Alexa or Google Assistant for hands-free commands like “Alexa, preheat the oven to 350°F.”
- **Routines:** Create automations, e.g., when you leave work, your oven preheats and your kettle boils.
- **IFTTT (If This Then That):** Some appliances support IFTTT for advanced triggers, like sending a notification when your fridge door is left open.

For a unified experience, consider a smart hub like Samsung SmartThings or Amazon Echo Plus.
