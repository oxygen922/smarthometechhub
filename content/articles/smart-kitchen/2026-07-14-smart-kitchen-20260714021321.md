## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Oven?

- **Tech Enthusiasts:** If you love gadgets and automation, a smart oven with AI food recognition and voice control will delight you. The June is your best bet.
- **Busy Professionals:** Time is precious. A smart oven that preheats on command and automatically sets cooking parameters saves minutes every day. The Samsung offers great value without breaking the bank.
- **Families:** You need capacity and versatility. The GE Profile Smart Oven handles large meals and multiple dishes simultaneously, making it ideal for households with kids.
- **Health-Conscious Cooks:** Air frying is a game-changer for reducing oil. All three models offer air fry, but the GE’s dual-zone feature lets you cook healthier sides alongside mains.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $1,000:** The Samsung Bespoke Smart Oven is the best entry-level option. It offers AI features, air frying, and smart home integration without the premium price tag.
- **$1,000–$1,500:** The June Intelligent Oven delivers the most advanced AI and cooking precision. It’s a splurge, but the consistent results justify the cost for serious home cooks.
- **Over $1,500:** The GE Profile Smart Oven is a full-size investment. If you’re remodeling your kitchen and want a long-term smart appliance, this is the choice. Professional installation adds $200–$400.

### What to Avoid
- **Cheap “smart” ovens under $500:** These often have clunky apps, poor food recognition, and unreliable connectivity. You’re better off with a traditional oven and a separate air fryer.
- **Models without a camera:** Food recognition is the killer feature of smart ovens. Without it, you’re just paying for a connected oven with limited benefits.
- **Exclusive ecosystems:** If you use Google Home, ensure the oven works with it. Some models only support their brand’s assistant (e.g., Bixby for Samsung).

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Considerations

- **Countertop Models (June, Samsung):** These are plug-and-play. Place them on a heat-resistant surface with at least 3 inches of clearance on all sides. No special electrical work is needed—they use standard 120V outlets.
- **Wall Ovens (GE Profile):** Professional installation is required. You’ll need a dedicated 240V circuit, and the oven must be built into a cabinet cutout. Expect installation costs of $200–$400. Some models require a neutral wire, so check your home’s wiring.

### Smart Home Integration Tips

- **Voice Control:** Set up routines like “Alexa, preheat the oven to 350°F” or “Hey Google, start air fry mode.” All three models support these commands.
- **Automation with IFTTT:** For advanced users, create triggers like “When I arrive home, preheat the oven to 375°F” using your phone’s location.
- **App Notifications:** Enable push alerts for preheat completion, cook time reminders, and food readiness. This is especially useful for multitasking.
- **Multi-Device Sync:** If you have a smart fridge (like Samsung Family Hub), the oven can suggest recipes based on available ingredients. This ecosystem integration adds convenience.

### Troubleshooting Common Issues
- **Wi-Fi Disconnects:** Ensure your router is within range. Mesh Wi-Fi systems improve reliability for smart kitchens.
- **App Crashes:** Keep the app updated. Most manufacturers release patches monthly.
- **Food Recognition Errors:** Clean the camera lens regularly. Oily residue can blur images.
