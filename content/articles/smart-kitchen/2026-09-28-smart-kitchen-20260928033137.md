# The Best Intelligent Ovens of 2026: A Smart Kitchen Review

## Introduction

The intelligent oven has quietly become one of the most compelling upgrades in the modern smart kitchen. Unlike the early "smart" ovens that simply added Wi-Fi and a clunky app, today's models use computer vision, multi-zone heating, and AI-driven cooking profiles to genuinely improve results. If you've ever burned a sheet-pan dinner because your old oven runs 25°F hot, or wished you could start roasting from your phone on the commute home, this category was built for you. In 2026, intelligent ovens range from sub-$1,000 countertop combos to $6,000+ built-in wall units, and the gap between them is wider than the price tags suggest. In this review, I'll break down the three best intelligent ovens I've tested this year, explain the features that actually matter, and help you decide whether your kitchen is ready for one.

---

## Top 3 Product Recommendations

### 1. June Intelligent Oven (2026 Edition) — Best Overall Countertop Intelligent Oven

**Estimated price: $1,499**

The June has been the benchmark for countertop intelligent ovens for years, and the 2026 Edition finally fixes the two complaints that held earlier models back: interior capacity and cook-time accuracy. The new model bumps usable volume to roughly 0.9 cubic feet—still small, but enough for a 12-inch pizza, a whole chicken, or two sheet-pan sides at once.

What makes the June genuinely "intelligent" is its camera-and-scale system. The oven identifies what you place inside (it recognized salmon, frozen pizza, and cookie dough in my tests with no manual input), weighs it, and selects a cook profile automatically. The onboard food thermometer is now wireless and dishwasher-safe, which is a meaningful upgrade over the wired probe on the 2023 model.

In practice, the June nailed a medium-rare ribeye within about 4°F of my target, and its air-fry mode produced fries that rivaled a dedicated countertop air fryer. The companion app is polished, with live camera streaming so you can watch your food from another room. Downsides? It's expensive for a countertop unit, and the non-stick interior coating requires gentle cleaning. But if you want the smartest cooking brain in the smallest footprint, this is it.

**Pros:** Best-in-class food recognition, wireless thermometer, excellent app
**Cons:** Premium price, modest capacity, coating needs care

---

### 2. Samsung Bespoke AI Oven (Built-In, 2026) — Best Full-Size Intelligent Oven

**Estimated price: $3,299**

For anyone remodeling a kitchen, the Samsung Bespoke AI Oven is the most complete built-in intelligent oven on the market. This is a 30-inch wall oven with a 5.1-cubic-foot capacity, dual convection, and a built-in AI camera that recognizes up to 80 dishes and suggests cook settings on the 7-inch touchscreen or your phone.

The headline feature for 2026 is Samsung's improved AI Pro Cooking mode, which now coordinates with the SmartThings app to preheat based on your recipe and send a push notification when your dish is within two minutes of done. In my testing, the camera-based recognition was accurate about 85% of the time—impressive, though I still manually confirmed settings for anything unusual. The steam-clean cycle and air-fry tray make it versatile, and the matte-glass finish looks genuinely premium.

Integration is where Samsung shines: it pairs with SmartThings-compatible range hoods, so the hood automatically boosts when the oven hits high heat. If you're already in the Samsung ecosystem, this is the obvious pick. If you're not, the value proposition is weaker, since many best features assume a connected home.

**Pros:** Large capacity, deep SmartThings integration, gorgeous design
**Cons:** Ecosystem lock-in, AI recognition isn't perfect, high price

---

### 3. Breville PolyScience HydroPro Smart Oven — Best for Precision Cooking

**Estimated price: $899**

The Breville PolyScience HydroPro is the intelligent oven for people who care more about precision than gimmicks. It combines a countertop convection oven with sous-vide-style steam injection and a highly accurate PID temperature controller. There's no camera and no food recognition—instead, Breville focused on nailing temperature stability to within ±2°F, which is exceptional at this price.

The smart features are understated but useful: a companion app with guided recipes, Wi-Fi control, and the ability to save custom multi-stage cook programs (for example, steam at 200°F for 20 minutes, then roast at 425°F for 10). For bread bakers, the steam injection produces a crust that's genuinely bakery-quality. For meat, the precision mode delivered a pork tenderloin that was evenly pink from edge to edge.

It's smaller than the June and lacks the "wow" factor of AI recognition, but for cooks who want repeatable, restaurant-grade results, the HydroPro is the best value in the intelligent oven category. Note that it's heavier than it looks and needs decent counter clearance for steam venting.

**Pros:** Outstanding temperature precision, steam injection, great for bread and proteins
**Cons:** No food recognition, smaller capacity, steam requires water refills

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy an Intelligent Oven?

- **Busy professionals and families:** If you want to start dinner remotely or rely on auto-cook profiles, a countertop intelligent oven like the June pays for itself in convenience.
- **Home bakers and precision cooks:** The Breville HydroPro is the clear winner if repeatability matters more than AI features.
- **Kitchen remodelers:** If you're already replacing a wall oven, the Samsung Bespoke is the most future-proof built-in option—provided you're comfortable with the SmartThings ecosystem.
- **Small-space dwellers:** Countertop models replace both a toaster oven and an air fryer, saving counter space overall.

### Who Should Skip It?

If you rarely cook, or you're happy with a basic oven and a $30 probe thermometer, the premium isn't justified. Intelligent ovens shine with frequent use.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $1,000:** Breville HydroPro and similar precision countertop ovens. You get accuracy, not AI.
- **$1,000–$1,800:** June and comparable AI countertop ovens. Best balance of smarts and size.
- **$2,500–$4,000+:** Built-in intelligent wall ovens. Expect professional installation costs of $150–$400 on top.

Factor in long-term costs: replacement probes ($30–$80), descaling for steam models, and potential app subscription fees (rare, but check before buying).

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

**Countertop models** are plug-and-play: place on a heat-safe surface with 4–6 inches of clearance, connect to 2.4GHz Wi-Fi during setup, and you're cooking in 15 minutes. Most apps guide you through pairing via QR code.

**Built-in ovens** require professional installation, a dedicated 240V circuit, and cabinet cutout dimensions that match your model exactly. Measure twice—returns on installed appliances are painful.

For smart home integration, the ecosystem matters:

- **Samsung Bespoke:** Works with SmartThings, Alexa, and Google Assistant. Best automation potential, including hood sync.
- **June:** Works with Alexa and Google Assistant for basic commands; deeper control lives in its own app.
- **Breville:** Limited voice support; app-first experience.

A practical automation to set up: a "cooking mode" routine that dims kitchen lights, starts the range hood, and sends a "dinner's ready" notification when the probe hits target temperature.

## Conclusion & Final Verdict

The intelligent oven category has matured to the point where "smart" features genuinely improve outcomes—not just add app bloat. After weeks of testing, my picks stand:

- **Best overall countertop:** June Intelligent Oven (2026) — the smartest, most convenient all-rounder.
- **Best built-in:** Samsung Bespoke AI Oven — the most capable full-size option if you're in the Samsung ecosystem.
- **Best for precision:** Breville PolyScience HydroPro — unbeatable accuracy and steam at a fair price.

**Final verdict:** If you cook several times a week and value convenience, an intelligent oven is a worthwhile investment in 2026. Start with the Breville if budget is tight, the June if you want maximum intelligence in a small footprint, and the Samsung if you're building a connected kitchen from the ground up. Skip the category entirely if you're a casual cook—your money is better spent on good ingredients and a reliable thermometer.
