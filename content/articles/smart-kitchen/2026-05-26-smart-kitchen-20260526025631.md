## Key Features to Consider (with Comparison Table)

When shopping for smart kitchen appliances in 2026, focus on these critical features:

- **AI & Machine Learning:** How well does the appliance learn your habits? Look for systems that adapt to your cooking style, suggest recipes, and optimize energy use.
- **Connectivity:** Wi-Fi is standard, but check for Bluetooth, Zigbee, or Matter protocol support for seamless integration with other smart home devices.
- **App Quality:** A well-designed, responsive app is crucial. Look for features like remote monitoring, recipe libraries, and push notifications.
- **Voice Control:** Compatibility with Alexa, Google Assistant, or Apple HomeKit adds convenience, especially when your hands are messy.
- **Energy Efficiency:** Smart appliances should save energy. Look for Energy Star certification and features like eco modes or smart defrost.
- **Durability & Warranty:** Premium appliances are an investment. Check for stainless steel construction, scratch-resistant surfaces, and at least a 2-year warranty.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Considerations

- **Refrigerators:** Ensure your doorway and kitchen space can accommodate the size. Smart fridges often require a dedicated 20-amp circuit. Professional installation is recommended for water line connections (for ice makers).
- **Countertop Ovens:** These are plug-and-play. Just ensure your counter can handle the weight (June is about 30 lbs) and heat. Leave 4 inches of clearance on all sides.
- **Induction Cooktops:** Installation is complex and requires a professional electrician. You’ll need a 240V circuit and a compatible countertop cutout. Induction cooktops are heavy (60+ lbs) and require precise leveling.

### Smart Home Integration Tips

- **Matter Protocol:** In 2026, Matter is the gold standard for cross-platform compatibility. Ensure your appliances support Matter for seamless integration with Apple Home, Google Home, and Amazon Alexa.
- **Routines & Automation:** Use your smart home hub to create routines. For example, set your June Oven to preheat when your smart lock detects you’re 10 minutes away. Or have your Samsung fridge send a notification to your phone when you’re low on milk.
- **Voice Control:** For hands-free operation, place a smart speaker (e.g., Echo Show or Nest Hub) in your kitchen. This allows you to start cooking, set timers, or adjust oven temperature without touching a screen.
- **Security & Privacy:** Smart appliances with cameras (like the June Oven or Samsung fridge) raise privacy concerns. Check the manufacturer’s privacy policy and disable cameras when not in use. Use strong Wi-Fi passwords and enable two-factor authentication on your app.
