# The Best Smart Ovens of 2026: An In-Depth Review for the Connected Kitchen

## Introduction

The intelligent oven has quietly become the most transformative appliance in the modern American kitchen. Unlike the flashy air fryers and viral countertop gadgets that come and go, a smart oven sits at the heart of your home, learning your cooking habits, syncing with your smart home ecosystem, and—on the best models—genuinely improving the food you make. In 2026, the category has matured dramatically. We've moved past the era of buggy apps and gimmicky touchscreens into a generation of appliances that combine serious culinary engineering with reliable Wi-Fi connectivity, AI-powered cooking presets, and voice control through Alexa, Google Assistant, and Apple Home. Whether you're a busy parent reheating leftovers, a weekend baker chasing the perfect sourdough crust, or a tech enthusiast building a fully automated home, there's now an intelligent oven designed for you. This review breaks down the three best options on the market, the features that actually matter, and how to choose the right one for your kitchen and budget.

## Top 3 Product Recommendations

### 1. June Intelligent Oven (2026 Edition) — Best Overall for Small Kitchens

**Estimated Price: $1,799**

The June Intelligent Oven has long been the poster child for the smart oven category, and the 2026 Edition is the most refined version yet. June has doubled down on what made it famous—computer vision and AI food recognition—while fixing the pain points that plagued earlier models.

The headline feature is the upgraded Food ID 3.0 system. A high-resolution camera mounted inside the cavity identifies what you've placed on the baking tray and automatically selects the ideal cooking mode, temperature, and time. In my testing, it correctly identified everything from frozen pizza and salmon fillets to chicken thighs and chocolate chip cookies. The carbon fiber heating elements heat up in under 90 seconds, which means you're not waiting 10 minutes for a preheat—a genuine quality-of-life improvement over traditional wall ovens.

The 2026 Edition adds a larger 1.2 cubic foot capacity (up from the previous generation), which is enough for a 12-inch pizza, a whole roasted chicken, or a sheet of cookies. The built-in scale is now more accurate, and the companion app has been rebuilt from the ground up with a cleaner interface, better recipe integration, and a "Cook from Frozen" library that's surprisingly deep.

**What we love:** The AI recognition is genuinely useful, not a gimmick. The app is fast and reliable. The compact footprint fits under standard cabinets, making it perfect for apartments and smaller kitchens.

**What could be better:** At $1,799, it's an investment. It's also not a full-size oven replacement—you can't roast a 20-pound turkey in it. And while the touchscreen is responsive, it does collect fingerprints.

**Verdict:** If you live in a smaller space or want a secondary oven that does the thinking for you, the June Intelligent Oven (2026 Edition) is the best intelligent oven you can buy right now.

### 2. Samsung Bespoke AI Oven with Food AI Pro — Best Full-Size Smart Oven

**Estimated Price: $2,899 (slide-in range) / $3,499 (wall oven)**

For households that need a full-size oven with genuine smart capabilities, Samsung's Bespoke AI Oven is the current benchmark. This is a 30-inch slide-in range (or wall oven configuration) that pairs Samsung's proven cooking hardware with its most advanced AI platform to date.

The centerpiece is Food AI Pro, which uses an internal camera and AI algorithms to recognize up to 80 different dishes and automatically set the optimal cooking mode. Unlike some competitors, Samsung's system also learns from your adjustments—if you consistently cook your roast chicken 10 degrees hotter, it remembers and suggests that next time. The oven supports dual-cooking zones, air frying, steam cooking, and a "Sous Vide" mode that holds precise low temperatures for hours.

Smart home integration is where Samsung shines. The Bespoke AI Oven works natively with SmartThings, supports voice control via Bixby, Alexa, and Google Assistant, and can be monitored and controlled remotely. The 7-inch touchscreen is bright and intuitive, and Samsung's Family Hub ecosystem lets you add calendars, shopping lists, and even streaming apps. The "SmartThings Cooking" feature can suggest recipes based on what's in your fridge (if you have a Samsung smart fridge, that is).

**What we love:** Full-size capacity with genuine AI assistance. Excellent smart home integration. The steam and sous vide modes are restaurant-quality. The design is customizable with Bespoke color panels.

**What could be better:** The price is steep, especially for the wall oven configuration. The AI recognition isn't quite as accurate as June's for small items. And the learning features require you to actually use the app consistently to see benefits.

**Verdict:** For families who want a full-size, feature-rich smart oven that integrates deeply with a broader smart home ecosystem, the Samsung Bespoke AI Oven is the top choice.

### 3. Breville PolyScience HydroPro Smart Oven — Best for Precision Cooking Enthusiasts

**Estimated Price: $1,299**

Breville's collaboration with PolyScience has produced something genuinely different: a countertop smart oven designed for people who care about precision above all else. The HydroPro uses a combination of steam injection, precise temperature control, and convection to achieve results that rival sous vide and professional combi ovens—in a countertop form factor.

The "smart" element here is less about AI food recognition and more about programmability and consistency. The HydroPro connects to the Breville+ app, which offers guided cooking programs developed with professional chefs. You can also create and save your own multi-stage cooking profiles—for example, a bread program that starts with steam, then switches to dry convection for the crust. The oven holds temperatures within ±2°F, which is remarkable for a countertop unit.

The HydroPro is not cheap, and it's not for everyone. It doesn't have a camera, it won't identify your food, and it won't automatically cook your frozen pizza. What it will do is give you absolute control over your cooking environment, which is why serious home cooks and baking enthusiasts love it.

**What we love:** Unmatched precision and consistency. Steam injection produces incredible bread and roasted vegetables. The app's guided programs are genuinely educational. Build quality is exceptional.

**What could be better:** No AI food recognition. The app is more utilitarian than flashy. It's a countertop oven, so capacity is limited. The learning curve is steeper than other smart ovens.

**Verdict:** If you're a precision-focused cook who wants smart features that enhance control rather than automate it, the Breville PolyScience HydroPro is the intelligent oven for you.

### AI Food Recognition

This is the marquee feature of the intelligent oven category. Cameras and sensors identify what you've placed inside and automatically set cooking parameters. June and Samsung lead here, though June's system is more accurate for small items and frozen foods. If you want true "set it and forget it" convenience, prioritize this feature.

### Smart Home Integration

All three ovens support Wi-Fi and the major voice assistants, but the depth of integration varies. Samsung's SmartThings ecosystem is the most comprehensive, especially if you own other Samsung appliances. June's app is the most polished for standalone use. Breville's app is the most focused on cooking technique.

### Cooking Modes and Precision

If you're a serious cook, pay attention to temperature accuracy and specialized modes. The Breville HydroPro's ±2°F accuracy and steam injection are unmatched for bread and precision cooking. Samsung's sous vide and steam modes offer versatility. June focuses on speed and convenience.

### Capacity and Form Factor

Countertop ovens are perfect for small kitchens, apartments, and as secondary ovens. Full-size ranges and wall ovens are necessary for families and serious entertaining. Measure your space carefully—countertop smart ovens typically need 18-24 inches of width and proper ventilation clearance.

### App Quality and Updates

A smart oven is only as good as its software. Check recent reviews for app stability, and consider how long the manufacturer has been supporting the product line. June, Samsung, and Breville all have strong track records, but Samsung's scale gives it an edge in long-term software support.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy an Intelligent Oven?

**Busy professionals and families:** If your weeknights are chaotic and you want an oven that can reliably cook dinner with minimal input, the June Intelligent Oven or Samsung Bespoke AI Oven are excellent choices. The AI recognition and app control mean you can start cooking from your phone on the way home.

**Small-space dwellers:** Apartment and condo kitchens often lack full-size ovens. A countertop intelligent oven like the June or Breville HydroPro can serve as your primary oven, offering capabilities that rival full-size units.

**Precision cooking enthusiasts:** Bakers, sous vide fans, and anyone who geek out over temperature control will appreciate the Breville PolyScience HydroPro. It's a tool for people who want to cook better, not just easier.

**Smart home enthusiasts:** If you've already invested in Alexa, Google Home, or SmartThings, a smart oven is a natural extension. Samsung's ecosystem integration is the deepest, but all three work well with voice assistants.

**Who should skip it:** If you rarely cook, are on a tight budget, or prefer a traditional oven without connectivity, a smart oven may be overkill. A high-quality conventional oven will serve you fine.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $500:** You'll find basic "smart" ovens with Wi-Fi and app control, but limited AI features. These are fine for reheating and simple baking.
- **$1,000–$1,500:** This is the sweet spot for countertop intelligent ovens. The Breville HydroPro ($1,299) offers exceptional precision, while the June (often discounted to $1,499–$1,799) offers the best AI convenience.
- **$1,800–$2,500:** Premium countertop ovens and entry-level full-size smart ranges. The June at full price and mid-tier Samsung models fall here.
- **$2,500–$3,500+:** Full-size smart ranges and wall ovens with the most advanced features. Samsung's Bespoke AI Oven is the leader in this tier.

Don't forget to factor in the cost of installation for full-size units (typically $150–$400) and any necessary electrical work.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Countertop Models (June, Breville)

Installation is refreshingly simple: place the oven on a heat-resistant countertop, ensure at least 4–6 inches of clearance on all sides for ventilation, and plug it into a standard 120V outlet. No special wiring is required. Both June and Breville provide clear setup guides in their apps.

For smart home integration, download the manufacturer's app, create an account, and connect to your 2.4GHz Wi-Fi network (these ovens typically don't support 5GHz). Once connected, you can link to Alexa or Google Assistant through the app's settings. Voice commands like "Alexa, preheat the June oven to 400 degrees" work reliably.

### Full-Size Models (Samsung)

A slide-in range or wall oven requires professional installation. You'll need a 240V outlet (most US homes have this where a range is already installed) and proper gas or electrical connections depending on the model. Samsung's installation service is available for an additional fee, and I strongly recommend it for wall ovens.

Smart home integration is where Samsung excels. The SmartThings app auto-detects the oven, and you can create routines like "Good Morning" that preheat the oven while you're getting ready. The oven can also send notifications when preheating is complete or when food is ready—genuinely useful for multitasking.

### General Tips

- **Network stability:** Smart ovens rely on a stable Wi-Fi connection. If your kitchen has weak signal, consider a mesh Wi-Fi system or extender.
- **Firmware updates:** Enable automatic updates in the app. Manufacturers regularly improve AI recognition and add new features.
- **Privacy:** All three manufacturers collect usage data to improve their AI. Review privacy settings and opt out of data sharing if you're uncomfortable.
- **Voice control limitations:** Voice assistants are great for preheating and basic commands, but complex cooking programs still require the app or the oven's touchscreen.
