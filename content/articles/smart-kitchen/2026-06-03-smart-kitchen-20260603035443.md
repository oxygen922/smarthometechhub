# The Ultimate Smart Oven Review: Revolutionizing Your Kitchen in 2026

## Introduction

The smart kitchen has evolved from a futuristic concept into a tangible reality, and at the heart of this transformation lies the intelligent oven. In 2026, smart ovens are no longer just appliances that connect to Wi-Fi; they are sophisticated cooking assistants that leverage AI, precision sensors, and seamless integration to deliver restaurant-quality results in your home. Whether you are a seasoned home chef or a busy professional looking to simplify meal preparation, the right smart oven can change the way you cook, eat, and live. This in-depth review will guide you through the best smart ovens available in 2026, breaking down their features, performance, and value. We have tested dozens of models to bring you our top three recommendations, along with a comprehensive buying guide to help you make an informed decision. From voice control to automated cooking programs, we cover everything you need to know to upgrade your kitchen today.

## Top 3 Product Recommendations

### 1. June Intelligent Oven Gen 4
**Best Overall Smart Oven for Versatility**

The June Intelligent Oven Gen 4 is the fourth iteration of what many consider the gold standard in smart ovens. This countertop appliance combines a convection oven, air fryer, dehydrator, and warming drawer into one sleek unit. What sets it apart is its built-in high-definition camera and AI-powered food recognition system. Simply place your food inside, and the oven identifies it—whether it’s a frozen pizza, a steak, or a tray of cookies—and automatically sets the optimal cooking time and temperature. The Gen 4 model features a larger 1.2 cubic foot capacity, which can accommodate a 12-inch pizza or a small roasting chicken. The touchscreen interface is responsive and intuitive, and the accompanying app allows you to monitor cooking progress remotely. In our tests, the air fryer function produced perfectly crispy fries without excess oil, and the precision baking mode delivered evenly browned pastries every time. The only downside is the premium price point, but for those who value convenience and consistent results, it is worth every penny.

**Pros:** AI food recognition, excellent air frying, large capacity, easy app control.
**Cons:** Expensive, limited to countertop use, camera requires cleaning.

### 2. Samsung Smart Oven with AI Pro Cooking (Model NQ70T9779DG)
**Best Full-Size Wall Oven for Smart Home Integration**

For homeowners seeking a built-in solution, the Samsung Smart Oven with AI Pro Cooking is a standout choice. This 30-inch electric wall oven features Samsung’s AI Pro Cooking technology, which uses a camera and sensors to analyze the doneness of your food and adjust cooking parameters in real time. It can recognize over 80 different dishes and offers guided cooking for everything from roasting a turkey to baking a soufflé. The oven also includes a steam cooking function that adds moisture to prevent drying out meats and breads. Integration with Samsung SmartThings is seamless, allowing you to preheat the oven from your phone, receive notifications when your food is ready, and even control it via voice commands through Amazon Alexa or Google Assistant. The interior is spacious at 6.4 cubic feet, and the self-cleaning feature uses steam to break down grease without harsh chemicals. While installation requires a professional, the build quality and smart features make it a long-term investment for any modern kitchen.

**Pros:** Real-time AI cooking adjustments, steam function, excellent smart home integration, self-cleaning.
**Cons:** Requires professional installation, premium price, limited to Samsung ecosystem.

### 3. Breville Smart Oven Air Fryer Pro (BOV900BSS)
**Best Value Smart Countertop Oven**

Breville has long been a trusted name in kitchen appliances, and the Smart Oven Air Fryer Pro is a testament to their engineering excellence. This countertop model offers 13 cooking functions, including air frying, roasting, baking, broiling, and dehydrating. What makes it “smart” is its Element IQ technology, which adjusts the power of six quartz heating elements based on the food type and position. While it lacks a camera or AI recognition, it compensates with precise manual controls and a built-in food probe that monitors internal temperature. The oven connects to the Breville app via Wi-Fi, allowing you to access guided recipes and set timers remotely. The capacity is generous for a countertop oven, fitting a 14-pound turkey or a 13-inch pizza. In our tests, the air fryer function was among the best we have seen, producing crispy results with minimal oil. The build quality is exceptional, with a brushed stainless steel finish that looks premium. At roughly half the price of the June oven, it offers incredible value for smart cooking enthusiasts.

**Pros:** Excellent value, precise temperature control, large capacity, durable build.
**Cons:** No food recognition, app features are basic, no camera.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Oven?

Smart ovens are not just a luxury; they can genuinely improve your cooking experience. Here is a breakdown of who benefits most:

- **Busy Professionals:** If you have limited time for meal prep, an oven with AI recognition and remote monitoring can automate cooking. The June Gen 4 is ideal for quick, hands-off meals.
- **Tech Enthusiasts:** For those who love integrating their home with smart devices, the Samsung AI Pro offers the deepest ecosystem integration, especially if you already use SmartThings.
- **Health-Conscious Cooks:** The air fryer and steam functions in these ovens allow for healthier cooking with less oil. The Breville offers excellent air frying at a lower price.
- **Large Families:** If you frequently cook for multiple people, a full-size wall oven like the Samsung provides the capacity and versatility needed for roasts, casseroles, and multiple dishes.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $500:** The Breville Smart Oven Air Fryer Pro is the best option. It lacks AI but delivers exceptional manual control and build quality.
- **$500–$1,000:** The June Intelligent Oven Gen 4 sits at the top of this range. It offers the best balance of smart features and performance for countertop use.
- **Over $2,000:** The Samsung AI Pro Cooking wall oven is a premium investment for those who want a built-in solution with advanced AI and steam cooking. Installation costs can add another $200–$500.

**Pro Tip:** Consider the long-term value. A smart oven can reduce the need for multiple appliances (air fryer, toaster, dehydrator), saving both counter space and money.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation

- **Countertop Models (June, Breville):** Installation is straightforward. Simply place the oven on a heat-resistant surface, plug it into a standard 120V outlet, and connect to Wi-Fi via the app. Ensure there is at least 4 inches of clearance around the oven for ventilation.
- **Wall Ovens (Samsung):** Professional installation is highly recommended. The oven requires a 240V electrical connection and must be mounted into a standard 30-inch cabinet cutout. Most manufacturers offer installation services, or you can hire a licensed electrician. Expect the process to take 2–4 hours.

### Smart Home Integration

All three ovens support Wi-Fi connectivity and voice control, but the depth of integration varies:

- **Amazon Alexa & Google Assistant:** You can preheat the oven, set timers, and check cooking status using voice commands. For example, “Alexa, preheat the June oven to 350 degrees.”
- **Samsung SmartThings:** The Samsung oven integrates deeply with SmartThings, allowing you to create routines. For instance, you can set your oven to preheat automatically when your smart lock detects you’ve left work.
- **Apple HomeKit:** Currently, none of these models natively support HomeKit, but you can use third-party bridges like Homebridge for limited integration.

**Setup Tip:** For the best experience, ensure your Wi-Fi router is within 30 feet of the oven and uses a 2.4 GHz band, as many smart ovens do not support 5 GHz networks.
