## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy Smart Kitchen Appliances?

- **Tech-Savvy Homeowners:** If you already have a smart home ecosystem (e.g., smart lights, thermostats), adding a smart fridge or oven enhances convenience. You’ll appreciate the ability to control everything from one app.
- **Busy Professionals:** Time-saving features like remote preheating, automatic cooking settings, and grocery tracking can free up hours each week. The June Oven is ideal here.
- **Families:** A smart fridge like the Family Hub helps manage grocery lists and reduce food waste, which can save money over time.
- **Hobbyist Bakers:** The KitchenAid Smart Mixer offers precision that traditional mixers can’t match, making it worth the splurge for serious bakers.

### Budget Considerations

- **Entry-Level ($200–$500):** Look for smart slow cookers, air fryers, or coffee makers. Brands like Instant Pot and Breville offer basic connectivity.
- **Mid-Range ($500–$1,500):** You can get a June Oven or a smart countertop oven. Also consider smart refrigerators from LG or GE with smaller screens.
- **Premium ($1,500+):** The Samsung Family Hub and other high-end fridges fall here. These offer the most features but require a significant investment.

**Pro Tip:** Don’t overspend on connectivity if you rarely use your phone for cooking. Focus on core performance first, then add smart features as a bonus.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Tips

- **Smart Refrigerators:** Ensure your kitchen has a dedicated water line for ice makers and water dispensers. Measure the space carefully—these fridges are often wider than standard models. Professional installation is recommended for water connections.
- **Smart Ovens:** Countertop models like the June Oven require only a standard outlet and ventilation. Built-in models may need a 240V outlet and professional installation.
- **Smart Mixers:** No special installation needed, but ensure your counter can support the weight (around 25 pounds).

### Smart Home Integration

All three products reviewed work with major smart home platforms:
- **Amazon Alexa:** Use voice commands to preheat the June Oven or check fridge contents.
- **Google Assistant:** Similar functionality; ask your Google Nest Hub to display recipe instructions.
- **Apple HomeKit:** Limited support; check compatibility before buying.

**Best Practices:**
- Place your Wi-Fi router within 30 feet of the kitchen for stable connections.
- Use a dedicated 2.4GHz network for older devices (some smart appliances don’t support 5GHz).
- Update firmware regularly to fix bugs and add features.
