## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy an Intelligent Oven?

**Busy Professionals:** If you're often stuck at work when you should be cooking dinner, a smart oven with app control and food recognition can be a game-changer. Simply place your food in the oven, start it remotely, and it will handle the rest.

**Novice Cooks:** The AI-driven features of ovens like the June can help build confidence in the kitchen. Automatic food recognition takes the guesswork out of cooking times and temperatures.

**Large Families:** Full-size smart ranges with features like the Dual Cook Flex allow you to prepare multiple dishes simultaneously, making weeknight dinners and holiday meals much more manageable.

**Tech Enthusiasts:** If you love having the latest gadgets, a smart oven is a natural addition to your connected home. The ability to control your oven with voice commands and monitor it via smartphone is genuinely satisfying.

### Budget Considerations

Intelligent ovens range from around $300 to over $4,000. Here's what you should expect at different price points:

**Budget ($300-500):** You'll get basic smart features like temperature control and preset programs, but likely no Wi-Fi or camera. The Breville Smart Oven Air Fryer Pro is the standout in this category.

**Mid-Range ($500-1,500):** This is the sweet spot for most buyers. The June Oven 2.0 falls here and offers the full smart oven experience with AI, camera, and app connectivity.

**Premium ($1,500+):** Full-size built-in ranges with advanced features. You're paying for capacity, professional-grade performance, and integration with your kitchen's design.

**Value for Money:** We believe the Breville offers the best value if you're on a budget, while the June Oven provides the best overall experience for the price. The Samsung is worth the investment if you need a full-size replacement for an aging range.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Requirements

**Countertop Ovens (June, Breville):**
- Standard 120V outlet (15-amp circuit recommended)
- Minimum 18 inches of clearance above the oven
- 6 inches of clearance on all sides for ventilation
- Heat-resistant countertop surface
- No professional installation required

**Full-Size Ranges (Samsung):**
- Dedicated 240V electrical circuit (40-amp minimum)
- Professional installation recommended
- Proper ventilation hood required for steam cooking
- Anti-tip bracket installation for safety
- Consideration for gas line if you're replacing a gas range

### Smart Home Integration

All three ovens we tested integrate seamlessly with major smart home ecosystems:

**Amazon Alexa:** You can preheat your oven, set timers, and check cooking status with simple voice commands. Example: "Alexa, preheat the June oven to 400 degrees."

**Google Assistant:** Similar functionality to Alexa, with the added benefit of visual responses on Google Nest Hub displays showing camera feeds from compatible ovens.

**Apple HomeKit:** Currently, only the June Oven offers native HomeKit support, allowing you to include it in Apple Home automations and scenes.

**IFTTT (If This Then That):** Both the June and Samsung ovens support IFTTT applets, enabling creative automation like turning on your kitchen lights when the oven timer goes off.

**Pro Tip:** For the best experience, ensure your oven is on a dedicated 2.4GHz Wi-Fi network. Many smart home devices struggle with 5GHz networks, and having your oven on the same network as your other smart devices will improve reliability.
