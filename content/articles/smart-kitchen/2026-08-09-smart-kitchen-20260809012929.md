## Introduction

It recognizes over 200 different food types automatically—from a raw salmon fillet to a tray of Brussels sprouts—and sets the perfect cooking profile without any manual input.

**Performance:** During our testing, the June oven nailed a medium-rare ribeye steak with a perfect sear, using its integrated broiler and precision temperature probe. **Verdict:** The June Intelligent Oven is the perfect entry point for the home cook who wants convenience without sacrificing quality. It is not cheap, but its versatility replaces multiple appliances—toaster oven, air fryer, and slow cooker—making it a worthy investment for most households.

### 2. Combustion Inc. G2 Smart Oven - Best for Precision Enthusiasts

If you are a serious home chef who obsesses over temperature gradients and perfect crusts, the Combustion Inc. G2 Smart Oven is a game-changer. Unlike the June, which is a countertop appliance, the G2 is a full-size, 24-inch built-in wall oven. Its core innovation is the "Predictive Cooking" system, which uses a unique multi-sensor thermometer that is wirelessly connected to the oven's controls. The thermometer is inserted into your meat, and the oven automatically adjusts its temperature to ensure the outside reaches the perfect crust at the exact moment the inside hits your target temperature.

**Performance:** This is the most accurate oven we have ever tested. The temperature stability is within ±1°F of the set point, a feat rarely achieved outside of professional kitchens. The "Predictive" mode is flawless for large cuts like prime rib or pork shoulder, eliminating the dreaded "stalled" cooking phase. The oven also features a built-in steam injection system, which is a godsend for baking artisan bread. The resulting crust is glossy, crackly, and deeply caramelized.

**Smart Features:** The app is data-rich, showing you a live graph of your food's internal temperature rise. It also offers a "Cookbook" feature with recipes that are specifically calibrated to the oven's algorithms. However, the learning curve is steeper than the June. This is not a set-and-forget appliance; it is a tool for those who want to actively engage with the science of cooking.

**Verdict:** The Combustion Inc. G2 is the ultimate upgrade for a kitchen renovation. It is expensive and requires a dedicated circuit and cabinet space, but for the precision-focused cook, there is nothing better on the market.

**Price:** $3,999 (2026 Estimate)

For those looking to upgrade their existing kitchen without a complete remodel, the GE Profile Smart Oven (Model PTD7000SNSS) offers a compelling mix of intelligence and practicality. The oven is also compatible with Alexa and Google Assistant, allowing for voice commands like "Hey Google, preheat the oven to 375 degrees."

**Verdict:** The GE Profile is the best option for families who want smart functionality without paying a premium. It is reliable, spacious (5.1 cubic feet), and the air fry mode makes it a versatile workhorse.

### Key Takeaway on Features

- **Computer Vision vs. Temperature Probe:** The June uses a camera to *see* the food. The Combustion G2 uses a sensor to *feel* the food. For baking and roasting, the camera is more helpful. For meat, the probe is superior. The GE Profile uses neither, relying on standard time and temperature settings.
- **Installation:** This is the most critical factor. A countertop oven (June) requires only a standard 120V outlet and is portable. Built-in ovens (Combustion, GE) require a 240V dedicated circuit and professional installation, which adds $300-$500 to the total cost.
- **App Ecosystem:** Look for an app that offers more than just remote start. The June's live video feed is a standout feature, while the Combustion's data analytics are unique. If you only need basic remote control, the GE app is sufficient.

In 2026, the intelligent oven market is segmented clearly:

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

**Countertop Models (June):** Installation is trivial. Just place it on a heat-resistant countertop, plug into a standard grounded outlet, and connect to your 2.4GHz Wi-Fi network. It takes about 10 minutes to set up. The June integrates seamlessly with Apple HomeKit, allowing you to start a preheat routine via Siri. It also works with Alexa and Google Home, but the integration is limited to voice commands for preheat and timer functions.

**Built-In Models (Combustion G2 & GE Profile):** These require professional installation. The process involves:
1.  **Electrical:** A dedicated 240V/30A or 40A circuit is required. Ensure your breaker panel has capacity.
2.  **Cabinet Fit:** Verify the cutout dimensions. The Combustion G2 has a unique 24-inch width, which is non-standard in the US (most are 30 inches). Measure carefully.
3.  **Venting:** Built-in ovens vent from the front, so no rear clearance is needed, but the oven must be pushed back into the cavity without kinking the electrical cable.

**Integration:** The GE Profile works with the SmartHQ app and supports Matter, which is the new standard for smart home connectivity. This allows it to work with Apple Home, Google Home, and Amazon Alexa without any proprietary bridge. The Combustion G2 uses a proprietary bridge for its thermometer, but the oven itself is Matter-enabled.

**Pro Tip:** For the best smart home experience, create an "Oven" routine. For example, "Good Morning" could trigger the GE Profile to start a no-preheat cycle while your smart kettle boils water for coffee.
