## Top 3 Intelligent Oven Recommendations for 2026

### 1. June Intelligent Oven (2026 Edition) — Best Overall Smart Oven

**Price: $1,799**

The June Intelligent Oven has been a staple in the smart kitchen space for years, and the 2026 Edition represents the most refined version yet. This countertop convection oven uses a combination of computer vision and weight sensors to identify over 200 different foods—from salmon fillets to chocolate chip cookies—and automatically selects the optimal cooking profile.

**What I love:** The food recognition is genuinely impressive. Place a raw chicken breast on the included baking tray, and the oven identifies it within seconds, suggesting a roasting profile that cooks it to a safe internal temperature without drying it out. The built-in camera lets you monitor progress from your phone, and the companion app sends push notifications when your food is ready.

**Performance:** The June oven reaches 500°F and uses six heating elements to ensure even cooking. In my testing, it outperformed many full-sized ovens for tasks like roasting vegetables and baking small batches of pastries. The air fry mode is particularly effective, producing crispy results with minimal oil.

**Smart features:** Wi-Fi connectivity, voice control via Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant, and a 5-inch touchscreen display. The 2026 model adds Matter compatibility, meaning it integrates seamlessly with Apple Home, Samsung SmartThings, and other major smart home ecosystems.

**Drawbacks:** The interior capacity is limited—you can't fit a full turkey or a large sheet pan. The $1,799 price tag is steep for a countertop appliance, though frequent sales bring it closer to $1,500.

**Verdict:** If you want the most intelligent, user-friendly smart oven for everyday cooking, the June 2026 Edition is the one to beat.

### 2. Samsung Bespoke AI Oven with SmartThings Hub — Best Full-Size Intelligent Oven

**Price: $3,299 (slide-in range with induction cooktop)**

For those who need a full-sized oven that doubles as the centerpiece of a smart kitchen, Samsung's Bespoke AI Oven is a compelling option. This slide-in range combines a powerful induction cooktop with a spacious convection oven, all controlled through Samsung's SmartThings platform.

**What I love:** The AI Pro Cooking feature uses a built-in camera to recognize up to 80 different dishes and automatically sets the cooking mode, temperature, and time. It even sends a notification to your phone when it detects that your food is nearing completion based on visual cues like browning.

**Performance:** The oven offers 6.0 cubic feet of capacity—enough for multiple dishes simultaneously. The convection system is excellent, and the induction cooktop heats up faster than traditional electric or gas. The "Air Sous Vide" mode is a standout feature, allowing you to cook vacuum-sealed foods at precise low temperatures without a dedicated sous vide machine.

**Smart features:** As expected from Samsung, SmartThings integration is flawless. You can preheat the oven remotely, adjust settings via voice commands, and even sync cooking times with other Samsung appliances. The 7-inch LCD screen on the range is bright and responsive.

**Drawbacks:** The AI recognition isn't as accurate as June's—it occasionally misidentifies dishes, requiring manual adjustments. The price is significant, and installation requires a dedicated 240V outlet.

**Verdict:** For families who want a full-size intelligent oven that anchors a broader smart home ecosystem, the Samsung Bespoke AI Oven is an excellent choice.

### 3. Breville PolyScience HydroPro Smart Oven — Best for Precision Cooking Enthusiasts

**Price: $2,499**

Breville's collaboration with PolyScience has produced an intelligent oven that caters to serious cooks who demand precise temperature control. The HydroPro uses steam injection and a highly accurate thermostat to maintain temperatures within ±1°F, making it ideal for sous vide-style cooking, proofing bread, and delicate baking.

**What I love:** The precision is unmatched in this price range. The oven's built-in humidity sensor adjusts steam levels automatically, which is a game-changer for baking artisan bread with a perfect crust. The "Element IQ" system distributes heat intelligently across five quartz elements, eliminating hot spots.

**Performance:** This oven excels at low-and-slow cooking. I tested it with a 12-hour beef short rib recipe, and the results were restaurant-quality. The steam-assist functions also make reheating leftovers surprisingly effective—no more dry pizza or rubbery chicken.

**Smart features:** Wi-Fi connectivity and a companion app provide remote monitoring and control. The app includes guided recipes that automatically set the oven's parameters. However, voice control is limited to basic on/off commands via Alexa and Google Assistant.

**Drawbacks:** The learning curve is steeper than other smart ovens—this isn't a "set it and forget it" appliance. The HydroPro also lacks the food recognition cameras found in June and Samsung models.

**Verdict:** If you're a precision-focused cook who values temperature accuracy over AI gimmicks, the Breville PolyScience HydroPro is worth the investment.

## Buying Guide: Who Should Buy an Intelligent Oven?

### Who Should Buy

- **Tech enthusiasts** who want their kitchen to be as connected as the rest of their home.
- **Busy professionals** who value the ability to preheat their oven remotely or receive cooking notifications.
- **Home cooks who struggle with timing**—AI-powered cooking profiles can eliminate guesswork.
- **Bakers** who want precise temperature control and steam injection for consistent results.
- **Families** who need a large capacity oven that can handle multiple dishes and integrate with existing smart home systems.

### Who Should Skip

- **Budget-conscious shoppers**—a basic convection oven costs a fraction of the price and cooks food just as well if you know what you're doing.
- **Cooking purists** who prefer manual control and distrust AI decision-making.
- **Renters** who can't install a new range or don't want to invest in appliances they can't take with them.
- **Anyone without reliable Wi-Fi**—many smart features become useless without a stable connection.

### Budget Considerations

Intelligent ovens span a wide price range in 2026:

- **Entry-level ($600–$1,000):** Basic smart ovens with app connectivity but limited AI features. Brands like Toshiba and COSORI offer decent options.
- **Mid-range ($1,000–$2,000):** The sweet spot for most buyers. June's 2026 Edition and similar countertop models fall here.
- **Premium ($2,000–$4,000):** Full-size ranges with advanced AI, steam injection, and premium build quality. Samsung Bespoke and Breville HydroPro compete in this tier.
- **Luxury ($5,000+):** Professional-grade intelligent ranges from brands like Miele, Wolf, and Thermador. These offer exceptional performance but diminishing returns on smart features.

**My advice:** Unless you're remodeling your kitchen or need a full-size range, start with a countertop intelligent oven like the June. It's a lower-risk investment that lets you experience smart cooking without committing to a major appliance purchase.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Considerations

**Countertop ovens:** These require no installation beyond placing them on a heat-resistant surface and plugging them into a standard 120V outlet. Ensure adequate clearance around the oven for ventilation.

**Slide-in and freestanding ranges:** These require a dedicated 240V outlet and may need professional installation, especially if you're replacing a gas range with an electric one (or vice versa). Measure your space carefully—standard range widths are 30 inches, but some models vary.

**Wall ovens:** These require cabinet modifications and professional installation. Budget $200–$500 for installation if you're not doing it yourself.

### Smart Home Integration

All three recommended ovens support Wi-Fi connectivity, but integration depth varies:

- **June 2026:** Matter support means it works natively with Apple Home, Google Home, Amazon Alexa, and Samsung SmartThings. You can create automations like "preheat the oven when I arrive home" using geofencing.
- **Samsung Bespoke AI:** Deep integration with SmartThings, including the ability to sync with Samsung refrigerators, dishwashers, and washing machines. Voice control via Bixby, Alexa, and Google Assistant.
- **Breville HydroPro:** Basic Wi-Fi connectivity with Alexa and Google Assistant support for on/off commands. No Matter compatibility, which limits future integration options.

**Pro tip:** If you're building a smart home from scratch, prioritize appliances with Matter support. It's becoming the universal standard and will save you headaches down the road.
