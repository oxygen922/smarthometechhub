## Installation & Smart Home Integration

Installing smart kitchen appliances requires some planning. Here’s a step-by-step guide:

1. **Check Compatibility:** Verify that your home’s electrical system supports the appliance. Induction cooktops require a dedicated 240V outlet, while smart ovens and refrigerators typically need standard 120V outlets.
2. **Network Requirements:** Ensure your Wi-Fi network is stable and covers your kitchen area. A mesh network is recommended for larger homes.
3. **Physical Installation:**
   - **Ovens:** Hire a professional for built-in models to ensure proper ventilation and electrical connections.
   - **Refrigerators:** Measure your space carefully, allowing for door clearance. Most smart fridges require a water line for ice makers.
   - **Cooktops:** Cutout dimensions must match your countertop. Induction cooktops are generally easier to install than gas.
4. **Smart Home Integration:**
   - **Voice Control:** Enable skills in Alexa or Google Home to control appliances with voice commands.
   - **Automation:** Use platforms like IFTTT or SmartThings to create routines (e.g., “Good Morning” triggers the fridge to show the weather on its display).
   - **App Setup:** Download the manufacturer’s app, create an account, and follow on-screen instructions to pair the appliance. Most will guide you through network setup.
5. **Testing:** Run a test cycle to ensure all features work. Check for firmware updates immediately.
