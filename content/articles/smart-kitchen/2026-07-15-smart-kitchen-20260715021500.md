## Installation & Smart Home Integration

Setting up smart kitchen appliances is generally straightforward, but there are nuances to consider:

**Step-by-Step Setup:**

1. **Unbox and Place:** Ensure proper ventilation for countertop appliances. Refrigerators need clearance for air circulation.
2. **Connect to Power:** Most smart appliances require a standard 120V outlet. Refrigerators may need dedicated circuits.
3. **Download the App:** Each brand has its own app (SmartChef Connect, FrostGuard app, AeroBake app). Create an account and follow on-screen instructions.
4. **Pair with Wi-Fi:** Use the app to connect the appliance to your 2.4GHz or 5GHz network. 2.4GHz is more reliable for long-range connections.
5. **Voice Assistant Setup:** Enable the appropriate skill in Alexa or Google Home to control the appliance with voice commands.
6. **Test Features:** Run a simple recipe or test cycle to ensure everything works.

**Integration Tips:**
- Use routines like "Good Morning" to preheat your oven or check refrigerator temperature.
- Connect to IFTTT for advanced automation (e.g., "If fridge door left open, send phone alert").
- For whole-home integration, consider a hub like SmartThings or Apple HomeKit.

**Common Issues:**
- Wi-Fi dropouts near metal appliances (place router centrally)
- App compatibility with older smartphones (update your device)
- Voice commands requiring specific phrasing (check app tutorials)
