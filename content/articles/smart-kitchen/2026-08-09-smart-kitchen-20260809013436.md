## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Requirements

**Refrigerators:** The Samsung Bespoke requires a dedicated water line for the ice maker and water dispenser, plus standard 110V electrical connection. Professional installation is recommended ($150-$300) to ensure proper leveling and water line connection. Ensure your doorways and hallways can accommodate the refrigerator's dimensions.

**Countertop Appliances:** The June Oven Pro 2.0 and GE Smart Mixer require minimal installation—simply place them on your countertop and plug them into a standard outlet. For the June Oven, ensure at least 4 inches of clearance on all sides for proper ventilation.

### Smart Home Integration Setup

**1. Wi-Fi Network Requirements:** Most smart appliances operate on 2.4GHz Wi-Fi networks. Ensure your router broadcasts this frequency, and consider a mesh Wi-Fi system for larger homes to eliminate dead zones in the kitchen area.

**2. App Setup Process:** Download the respective manufacturer apps (SmartThings, June App, GE Smart HQ) and follow the onboarding process. This typically involves scanning a QR code on the appliance, connecting to its local network temporarily, then joining your home Wi-Fi.

**3. Voice Assistant Configuration:** Link your appliance accounts to Alexa, Google Home, or Apple HomeKit for voice control. This usually takes just a few minutes through the voice assistant's app and unlocks hands-free operation.

**4. Automation Creation:** Take advantage of automation capabilities. For example, create a "Good Morning" routine that displays your calendar on the Family Hub screen and starts your coffee maker, or an "I'm Home" routine that preheats your oven when your smart lock detects your arrival.

**5. Firmware Updates:** Smart appliances receive regular updates that add features, improve performance, and patch security vulnerabilities. Enable automatic updates in the app settings to ensure your appliances stay current.

### Troubleshooting Common Issues

**Connectivity Problems:** If your appliance disconnects frequently, try moving your router closer, using a Wi-Fi extender, or ensuring your appliance isn't too far from the router. Many smart appliances also support Ethernet connections for more stable connectivity.

**App Syncing Issues:** If the app doesn't reflect appliance status accurately, force-close the app and reopen it, or check for app updates. Logging out and back in can also resolve sync issues.

**Voice Control Failures:** Ensure your voice assistant has the latest permissions for the appliance's skill/action. Sometimes re-linking the account resolves voice control issues.
