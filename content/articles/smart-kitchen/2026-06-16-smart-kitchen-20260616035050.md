### 2. Brava Glass 2.0

**Price Estimate:** $1,295

The Brava Glass 2.0 represents a radical departure from traditional oven design. Instead of heating the entire cavity, Brava uses patented Pure Light™ technology—high-intensity halogen lamps that cook food with visible light. This allows for incredibly fast preheating (under 30 seconds) and precise zone cooking, where different parts of the oven can cook at different temperatures simultaneously.

**Performance:** The Brava's speed is its standout feature. A frozen salmon fillet cooks in 8 minutes, and a whole chicken is ready in 25 minutes—roughly half the time of conventional ovens. The six-zone heating system means you can cook a steak to medium-rare on one side while roasting vegetables at a higher temperature on the other. The integrated temperature probe ensures meats are cooked to your exact preference.

**Smart Features:** The 7-inch color touchscreen is large and vibrant, with a cookbook of over 1,000 recipes designed specifically for the Brava's technology. The oven includes a built-in camera for live streaming your cooking progress to the app. Voice control works with Alexa and Google Assistant, and the Brava app allows for remote start, monitoring, and recipe downloads. The automatic steam-cleaning cycle is a welcome convenience.

**Build Quality:** The glass and aluminum construction is sleek and modern, but the glass exterior can show fingerprints. The interior is non-stick and easy to wipe down.

**Pros:**
- Incredibly fast cooking times
- Zone cooking for multi-dish meals
- Energy-efficient (uses 50% less energy than traditional ovens)
- Large capacity for a countertop oven (1.2 cubic feet)

**Cons:**

**Ideal For:** Busy families and individuals who need to get dinner on the table fast, and those who appreciate energy efficiency and innovative technology.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Oven?

- **Busy Professionals & Families:** If you're short on time but still want home-cooked meals, a smart oven with auto-cooking and fast preheating (like the Brava or June) can save 30-50% of cooking time.
- **Tech Enthusiasts:** If you love gadgets and smart home integration, any of these three models will impress. The June offers the most AI-driven experience, while the Brava is a true technological innovation.
- **Home Cooks & Bakers:** For those who value precision and consistency, the GE Profile's traditional heating with smart features provides the best of both worlds.
- **Eco-Conscious Users:** The Brava's energy efficiency makes it a strong choice for reducing your kitchen's carbon footprint.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $1,000:** Consider the Breville Smart Oven Air Fryer Pro ($399) or the Cuisinart AirFryer Toaster Oven with Wi-Fi ($299). These offer basic smart features like app control and preset programs but lack advanced AI and camera capabilities.
- **$1,000 - $1,500:** This is the sweet spot for premium countertop smart ovens. Both the June Pro and Brava Glass 2.0 fall here, offering the best balance of features, performance, and value.
- **Over $2,000:** Full-size built-in smart ovens like the GE Profile or Samsung Smart Oven with Camera ($2,499) are for those remodeling or building a new kitchen. They offer the largest capacity and most cooking modes.

### Installation Considerations

- **Countertop Models:** The June and Brava require standard counter space (about 20-22 inches wide) and a grounded outlet. They are plug-and-play.
- **Built-In Models:** The GE Profile requires professional installation, including electrical wiring (typically 240V) and cabinet cutouts. Factor in $200-$500 for installation.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Tips

- **Countertop Ovens:** Place your smart oven on a heat-resistant surface with at least 4 inches of clearance on all sides for ventilation. Ensure the outlet is not behind the oven to avoid cord strain.
- **Built-In Ovens:** Hire a licensed electrician for installation. The GE Profile requires a dedicated 240V circuit. Measure your cabinet cutout carefully—most built-in ovens have specific size requirements.
- **Wi-Fi Setup:** Most smart ovens require a 2.4GHz Wi-Fi network during initial setup. After that, they can often connect to 5GHz networks. Ensure your router is within range of the kitchen.

### Smart Home Integration

- **Voice Control:** All three models work with Alexa and Google Assistant. Example commands: "Alexa, set the June oven to air fry at 400 degrees" or "Hey Google, preheat the Brava to 350."
- **Routines & Automation:** Use IFTTT or your smart home platform to create routines. For example, "When I say 'Dinner time,' preheat the oven and turn on the kitchen lights."
- **Multi-Device Integration:** The GE Profile integrates with other GE smart appliances (refrigerators, dishwashers) through the Kitchen Hub. The June and Brava are standalone but can be part of broader smart home scenes.
- **Remote Monitoring:** All three apps allow you to check cooking progress, receive notifications, and adjust settings from anywhere. This is invaluable for multitasking.
