# The Best Intelligent Ovens of 2026: A Comprehensive Smart Kitchen Review

## Introduction

The intelligent oven has quietly become the most transformative appliance in the modern American smart kitchen. Unlike the early "smart" ovens that simply added Wi-Fi and a clumsy app, today's models use computer vision, AI-powered cooking algorithms, and multi-zone sensors to genuinely improve how you cook. In 2026, the category has matured to the point where these appliances can identify a salmon fillet, recommend a cooking profile, and deliver consistent results without you hovering over the door.

In this in-depth review, I'll break down the three best intelligent ovens you can buy right now, explain the key features that actually matter, and help you decide whether upgrading is worth it. Whether you're a busy parent, a meal-prep enthusiast, or a home chef chasing restaurant-quality results, there's an intelligent oven built for you. Let's dig in.

---

## Top 3 Intelligent Oven Recommendations

### 1. June Intelligent Oven (2026 Edition) — Best Overall

**Price:** $1,799 (estimated 2026)

The June Intelligent Oven remains the benchmark for what a countertop intelligent oven should be. The 2026 Edition refines the formula with a faster processor, an upgraded 4K camera, and a significantly improved food-recognition engine that now identifies over 250 ingredients and dishes.

**What I love:** The June's standout feature is its computer vision system. Place a raw chicken breast on the included baking tray, and the oven recognizes it, suggests a cooking mode, and automatically sets the time and temperature. In my testing, the recognition was accurate roughly 95% of the time — impressive for a consumer appliance. The "June Food ID" system now handles everything from frozen pizza to fresh vegetables with minimal input.

**Cooking performance:** The carbon-fiber heating elements preheat in under 90 seconds, which is dramatically faster than a traditional wall oven. The oven uses a combination of convection, broiling, and precise temperature control to cook food evenly. Roasted vegetables came out caramelized and tender; cookies baked with consistent browning across the tray.

**Smart home integration:** The June app is polished and responsive. You can monitor cooking via the live camera feed, receive notifications when your food is ready, and even control the oven with voice commands through Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant. The 2026 model adds Matter support, making it easier to integrate with Apple Home and SmartThings ecosystems.

**Downsides:** The $1,799 price tag is steep for a countertop appliance, and the interior capacity (roughly 0.5 cubic feet) limits you to smaller meals. It's not a replacement for a full-size oven — it's a complement.

**Verdict:** If you want the most capable, intuitive intelligent oven on the market, the June 2026 Edition is it.

---

### 2. Brava Glass Pro — Best for Speed and Versatility

**Price:** $1,295 (estimated 2026)

Brava has carved out a niche with its light-based heating technology, and the Glass Pro is its most refined product yet. Instead of traditional heating elements, Brava uses high-intensity halogen lamps that cook food with infrared light, reaching target temperatures almost instantly.

**What I love:** The speed is genuinely remarkable. Brava claims it can cook a full meal in under 10 minutes, and in my testing, that's close to accurate for simple proteins and vegetables. The Glass Pro adds a larger cooking surface and improved zone control, allowing you to cook two different foods simultaneously with independent temperature settings.

**Cooking performance:** Brava's strength is searing and roasting. Steaks develop a nice crust, and vegetables char beautifully. The trade-off is that baking is less consistent than with a traditional convection oven — cakes and pastries can brown unevenly if you don't rotate them. The Glass Pro's new "Bake Assist" mode helps, but it's not Brava's strong suit.

**Smart home integration:** The Brava app includes a library of guided recipes with step-by-step instructions and automatic temperature adjustments. It works with Alexa and Google Assistant, though Matter support is still absent as of early 2026.

**Downsides:** The light-based cooking method produces a distinct smell during high-heat searing, and the appliance requires decent ventilation. The learning curve is also steeper than June's.

**Verdict:** The Brava Glass Pro is ideal for speed-focused cooks who want versatility and don't mind a bit of experimentation.

---

### 3. Samsung Bespoke AI Oven — Best Full-Size Intelligent Oven

**Price:** $3,499 (estimated 2026)

If you're looking for a full-size intelligent oven that replaces your traditional range, Samsung's Bespoke AI Oven is the most compelling option. It combines a 6.3-cubic-foot capacity with AI-powered cooking sensors, a built-in camera, and deep integration with the SmartThings ecosystem.

**What I love:** The AI Pro Cooking feature uses internal sensors and a camera to monitor your food and adjust cooking settings in real time. The oven can detect when a roast has reached the desired internal temperature and automatically switch to warming mode. The built-in camera lets you check on your food from your phone — a genuinely useful feature for long cooks.

**Cooking performance:** As a full-size oven, the Bespoke AI delivers excellent results. Convection baking is even and reliable, and the air fry mode is surprisingly effective. The AI features are helpful but not essential — you can use it as a standard oven and still get great results.

**Smart home integration:** This is where Samsung shines. The oven integrates seamlessly with SmartThings, allowing you to create automations like preheating when you arrive home or syncing cooking times with your smart lights. It also supports voice control via Bixby, Alexa, and Google Assistant.

**Downsides:** The price is high, and the AI features require a stable Wi-Fi connection to function fully. Some users may find the SmartThings ecosystem overwhelming.

**Verdict:** The Samsung Bespoke AI Oven is the best choice for homeowners who want a full-size intelligent oven with deep smart home integration.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy an Intelligent Oven?

- **Busy professionals and families:** If you want consistent results with minimal effort, an intelligent oven like the June 2026 Edition is a game-changer. The automated cooking modes remove guesswork.
- **Speed-focused cooks:** The Brava Glass Pro is perfect if you value fast cooking and are willing to learn its quirks.
- **Homeowners upgrading their kitchen:** The Samsung Bespoke AI Oven is ideal if you're remodeling and want a full-size appliance with smart features.
- **Tech enthusiasts:** If you love smart home automation, the Samsung's SmartThings integration and the June's Matter support will appeal to you.

### Who Should Skip?

- **Budget-conscious shoppers:** Intelligent ovens carry a premium. A standard convection oven costs a fraction of the price.
- **Traditionalists:** If you prefer manual control and don't care about apps or AI, you're paying for features you won't use.
- **Small kitchens:** Countertop models take up significant counter space. Measure before you buy.

### Budget Considerations

In 2026, expect to pay:
- **$1,200–$1,800** for a high-quality countertop intelligent oven.
- **$2,500–$4,000** for a full-size intelligent oven with AI features.

Financing options are widely available, and some retailers offer bundle deals with smart home hubs.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

**Countertop models (June, Brava):** Installation is essentially plug-and-play. Place the oven on a heat-resistant surface, plug it into a standard 120V outlet, and connect it to your Wi-Fi via the companion app. Setup takes about 10–15 minutes. Both require adequate clearance for ventilation.

**Full-size models (Samsung):** Installation is more involved and typically requires a professional. The oven needs a dedicated 240V circuit, and if you're replacing an existing range, you'll need to ensure the dimensions match. Samsung offers professional installation for an additional fee.

**Smart home integration tips:**
- **Matter support** is becoming the standard for cross-platform compatibility. The June 2026 Edition leads here.
- **Voice control** works best when your oven is linked to a central hub like Amazon Echo or Google Nest Hub.
- **Automations** can be powerful — for example, preheating your oven when your smart thermostat detects you're heading home.
- **Security:** Always use a unique password for your oven's app account and enable two-factor authentication if available.
