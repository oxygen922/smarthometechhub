## Introduction

The intelligent oven has officially graduated from novelty gadget to genuine kitchen essential. In 2026, smart ovens are no longer just about Wi-Fi connectivity and voice commands—they're equipped with AI-powered cooking assistants, computer vision that recognizes what you're cooking, and sensors that can detect doneness with startling accuracy. After spending three months testing more than a dozen models in a real home kitchen, I can confidently say that the latest generation of intelligent ovens represents the biggest leap forward in home cooking since the convection fan.

But with prices ranging from $800 to over $6,000 and marketing claims that border on science fiction, choosing the right smart oven has never been more confusing. Some models promise to replace your sous chef; others simply add an app to a mediocre appliance. In this in-depth review, I'll break down the three best intelligent ovens of 2026, explain the key features that actually matter, and help you decide whether upgrading to a smart oven is worth it for your kitchen.

## Top 3 Intelligent Oven Recommendations for 2026

### 1. June Intelligent Oven (2026 Edition) — Best Overall Intelligent Oven

**Price: $1,799 (estimated 2026 pricing)**

The June Intelligent Oven has long been the poster child for AI-powered cooking, and the 2026 Edition is the most refined version yet. This countertop intelligent oven uses a combination of a high-resolution camera, a food-recognition neural network, and a built-in scale to identify over 200 types of food—from salmon fillets to chocolate chip cookies—and automatically select the ideal cooking profile.

**What I loved:** During testing, I placed a piece of raw salmon on the June's built-in scale, and within seconds the oven recognized it, suggested a cedar-plank preparation, and preheated to the perfect temperature. The result was restaurant-quality fish with a crisp skin and moist interior. The new 2026 model adds a "Multi-Item Mode" that can cook two different foods simultaneously—say, roasted vegetables and chicken thighs—by adjusting the heating elements independently.

The June app remains the best in the business. It sends push notifications when your food is nearly done, streams a live video feed of your roast (yes, you can watch your chicken brown from your office), and offers step-by-step guided recipes. The carbon fiber heating elements reach 500°F in under three minutes, which is faster than any other countertop intelligent oven I tested.

**What could be better:** The interior capacity is limited to a 12-inch pizza or a small whole chicken. If you're cooking for a family of four or more, this won't replace your main oven—it's a supplement. Also, the $1,799 price tag is steep for a countertop appliance.

**Verdict:** The June Intelligent Oven (2026 Edition) is the smartest, most intuitive intelligent oven on the market. If you want the ultimate AI cooking experience and have the budget, this is the one to buy.

### 2. Samsung Bespoke AI Oven with Vision AI — Best Built-In Intelligent Oven

**Price: $3,499 (estimated 2026 pricing)**

Samsung's Bespoke AI Oven is the first full-size built-in intelligent oven to integrate computer vision at this level. A camera inside the oven cavity identifies what you've placed inside—not just the type of food, but its approximate weight and thickness—and automatically sets the cooking mode, temperature, and time. The 2026 model improves on previous versions with a new "AI Pro Cooking" mode that can simultaneously cook two different dishes in the upper and lower compartments, adjusting each independently.

**What I loved:** The Vision AI is genuinely impressive. I placed a frozen pizza in the upper oven and a tray of brownies in the lower oven, and the Samsung correctly identified both, preheated each compartment to the right temperature, and finished both at the same time. The 7-inch touchscreen display is bright and responsive, and the oven integrates seamlessly with SmartThings, allowing you to preheat from your car or get a notification when your casserole is done.

The Bespoke AI Oven also features a "Food Reminder" that uses the internal camera to detect when you've left food inside after cooking—a small but surprisingly useful feature that prevents forgotten leftovers from becoming science experiments.

**What could be better:** The AI occasionally misidentified foods (it thought a cauliflower steak was a pork chop), and the correction process requires a few taps. The built-in camera also struggles in low-light conditions, though the oven light helps. At $3,499, this is a significant investment, and professional installation is recommended.

**Verdict:** For homeowners looking to upgrade their entire kitchen with a full-size intelligent oven, the Samsung Bespoke AI Oven is the best built-in option in 2026. It's a true smart oven that learns and adapts.

### 3. Brava Glass Smart Oven — Best Speed and Versatility

**Price: $1,295 (estimated 2026 pricing)**

The Brava Glass Smart Oven takes a different approach to intelligent cooking. Instead of a single heating element, it uses high-intensity halogen lamps that can be controlled independently across six zones. The result is an oven that can cook a steak in four minutes, roast vegetables in six, and even "grill" a piece of toast in 90 seconds—all with precise temperature control.

The 2026 Brava Glass adds a new "AI Chef" mode that uses a combination of timers, temperature sensors, and user feedback to learn your preferences. Over time, it adjusts cooking times and temperatures based on how you like your food. I found this particularly useful for roasting vegetables—after a few sessions, the Brava consistently delivered the crispy, caramelized edges I prefer.

**What I loved:** The speed is unmatched. The Brava can preheat in seconds (not minutes), and because it uses light rather than traditional heating elements, it cooks food up to five times faster than a conventional oven. The glass door and interior lighting make it easy to monitor progress without opening the door, and the touchscreen interface is intuitive.

**What could be better:** The Brava's small capacity (it fits a 12-inch pizza or a small casserole) limits its usefulness as a primary oven. The metal tray and glass door get extremely hot, so caution is required. And while the AI Chef mode is clever, it's not as accurate as the June's food recognition.

**Verdict:** The Brava Glass Smart Oven is perfect for busy professionals and small households who want speed and versatility. It's not a replacement for a full-size oven, but it's an excellent companion.

## Buying Guide: Who Should Buy an Intelligent Oven?

### Who Should Buy

**Tech enthusiasts and early adopters:** If you love the idea of an oven that can identify your food and cook it perfectly, the June Intelligent Oven is a dream come true. It's the closest thing to having a personal chef in your kitchen.

**Busy professionals and small families:** The Brava Glass Smart Oven is ideal if you want fast, healthy meals without the hassle of preheating and monitoring. It's perfect for singles, couples, or anyone who cooks for one or two people.

**Homeowners upgrading their kitchen:** The Samsung Bespoke AI Oven is a statement piece that adds genuine intelligence to a full-size oven. If you're remodeling your kitchen and want the latest smart home technology, this is the one to get.

### Who Should Skip

**Large families:** None of these intelligent ovens can replace a full-size oven for cooking large meals. If you're feeding a family of five or more, you'll still need a traditional oven.

**Budget-conscious shoppers:** Intelligent ovens are expensive. If you're looking for a basic oven with Wi-Fi connectivity, you can find one for under $800. But you'll miss out on the AI features that make these ovens special.

**Traditional cooks:** If you prefer to rely on your own instincts and recipes, an intelligent oven may feel like overkill. The AI features are designed to assist, not replace, your cooking skills.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $1,000:** You can find smart ovens with app control and basic presets, but they lack food recognition and AI learning.
- **$1,000–$2,000:** This is the sweet spot for countertop intelligent ovens like the June and Brava. You get advanced AI features and excellent cooking performance.
- **$2,000–$4,000:** Full-size built-in intelligent ovens like the Samsung Bespoke AI offer the best of both worlds: large capacity and advanced AI.
- **$4,000+:** High-end models from brands like Miele and Wolf offer professional-grade performance and AI features, but the returns diminish quickly.

**My advice:** If you're new to intelligent ovens, start with a countertop model like the June or Brava. They're less expensive, easier to install, and give you a taste of what AI cooking can do. If you love it, you can upgrade to a built-in model later.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation

**Countertop intelligent ovens (June, Brava):** These require no installation. Simply place them on your counter, plug them into a standard 120V outlet, and you're ready to cook. They do take up significant counter space, so measure before you buy.

**Built-in intelligent ovens (Samsung Bespoke AI):** These require professional installation. You'll need a 240V outlet and a cabinet cutout that matches the oven's dimensions. Expect to pay $200–$500 for installation, depending on your kitchen's layout. Samsung offers free installation with purchase in some regions.

### Smart Home Integration

All three ovens integrate with major smart home platforms:

- **June:** Works with Alexa and Google Assistant. The June app is available for iOS and Android.
- **Samsung Bespoke AI:** Integrates with SmartThings, Alexa, and Google Assistant. It also works with Samsung's Family Hub refrigerators.
- **Brava:** Works with Alexa and Google Assistant. The Brava app is available for iOS and Android.

**My experience:** The June app is the most polished and reliable. The Samsung SmartThings integration is excellent if you're already in the Samsung ecosystem. The Brava app is good but occasionally slow to connect.

**Pro tip:** If you have a smart home hub like Amazon Echo or Google Nest Hub, you can use voice commands to preheat your oven, set timers, and check cooking progress. It's a small convenience that adds up over time.
