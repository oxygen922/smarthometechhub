## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy What?

- **Busy Professionals & Small Households:** The June Intelligent Oven is perfect for those who want minimal effort with maximum results. If you're cooking for 1-2 people and value time over cost, this is your best bet.

- **Large Families & Meal Preppers:** The Samsung Bespoke AI Refrigerator excels when you need to manage a large inventory of food. It's ideal for families who cook in bulk and want to reduce waste through smart tracking.

- **Culinary Enthusiasts & Dietary-Conscious Cooks:** The Thermomix TM7 is unmatched for those who love cooking from scratch. If you enjoy experimenting with recipes but want precision guidance, this all-in-one device is worth every penny.

### Budget Considerations

Smart kitchen appliances represent a significant investment, but they can pay for themselves over time through reduced food waste, energy savings, and fewer takeout orders. Here's a realistic budget breakdown for 2026:

- **Entry-Level Smart Kitchen ($500-$1,000):** Focus on a smart instant pot or air fryer with basic app connectivity. These offer automation without breaking the bank.

- **Mid-Range Smart Kitchen ($1,000-$2,500):** This sweet spot includes the June Oven or Thermomix, plus a smart coffee maker or kettle. You'll get genuine AI assistance and robust app features.

- **Premium Smart Kitchen ($2,500+):** The Samsung Bespoke Refrigerator or similar flagship appliances. Expect full ecosystem integration, advanced AI, and premium build materials.

**Pro Tip:** Start with one core appliance—like a smart oven—and build your ecosystem gradually. Many brands offer discounts when you purchase multiple devices from the same ecosystem.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Considerations

- **June Intelligent Oven:** Requires a standard 120V outlet and minimal counter space (18" x 14"). No professional installation needed. Place it at least 4 inches from walls for proper ventilation.

- **Samsung Bespoke AI Refrigerator:** Requires a dedicated 120V outlet and proper clearance for doors to swing open. Professional installation is recommended for the water line connection (Auto Fill Water feature). Measure your doorway and kitchen space carefully—this is a large appliance.

- **Thermomix TM7:** Simply plug into any standard outlet. The device is portable, so you can store it in a cabinet when not in use. No installation required.

### Smart Home Integration

All three devices support integration with major smart home platforms, but the depth of integration varies:

- **Alexa & Google Assistant:** All three support basic commands like "Preheat to 350°F" or "What's in my fridge?" However, advanced features like recipe selection require the companion app.

- **Apple HomeKit:** Only the Samsung refrigerator supports HomeKit natively. June and Thermomix require workarounds via Shortcuts or third-party bridges.

- **Zigbee/Z-Wave:** None of these devices support these protocols natively, meaning they rely entirely on Wi-Fi. Ensure your home network is robust enough to handle multiple connected devices.

- **IFTTT & Automation:** The Thermomix offers the most flexible automation options, allowing you to trigger cooking sequences based on time of day or other smart home events. For example, you can set your coffee maker to start when the Thermomix finishes breakfast.

**Integration Tip:** For the best experience, ensure your Wi-Fi router is within range of your kitchen. Consider a mesh network if you experience connectivity issues.
