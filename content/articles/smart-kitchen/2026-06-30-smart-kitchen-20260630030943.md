# The Smart Kitchen Revolution: 2026’s Best Intelligent Ovens Reviewed

## Introduction

The modern kitchen is no longer just a place for cooking—it’s a hub of connectivity, convenience, and culinary precision. Among the most transformative appliances in this smart home evolution is the intelligent oven. These aren’t just ovens that connect to Wi-Fi; they are AI-powered culinary assistants capable of recognizing food, adjusting cooking profiles in real-time, and integrating seamlessly with your entire smart home ecosystem. Whether you’re a seasoned home chef or a busy professional looking to simplify meal prep, a smart oven can elevate your cooking experience while saving time and energy. With 2026 pricing now settling into competitive ranges, there has never been a better moment to invest in a truly intelligent oven. In this review, we’ll explore the top three models on the market, break down the essential features you need to consider, and help you decide which smart oven is the best fit for your kitchen and lifestyle.

## Top 3 Product Recommendations

### 1. June Intelligent Oven (2026 Edition)

**Overview:** The June Intelligent Oven remains a benchmark for smart cooking, and the 2026 edition refines an already excellent platform. This countertop convection oven features a built-in high-definition camera, a 7-inch touchscreen display, and advanced AI that can identify over 200 types of food—from chicken breasts to cookies—and automatically set the optimal cooking time and temperature.

**Performance & Features:** The June oven excels at precision cooking. Its “Food ID” system uses computer vision to recognize what you’ve placed inside, then adjusts the heating elements accordingly. In testing, it perfectly roasted a whole chicken in under 45 minutes, producing golden, crispy skin without any guesswork. The oven also includes a meat probe for internal temperature monitoring, and its convection fan ensures even heat distribution. The 2026 model adds improved Wi-Fi 6E connectivity, which virtually eliminates lag when using the companion app.

**Smart Home Integration:** June works with Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant, allowing you to preheat or check cooking status with voice commands. The app also sends push notifications when your food is ready or if the oven needs attention.

**Pros:** Exceptional food recognition, intuitive touchscreen, compact countertop design, excellent app experience.
**Cons:** Limited cooking capacity (0.9 cubic feet), high price point ($899), requires consistent app updates.

**Price (2026):** $899

### 2. GE Profile Smart Oven with Air Fry

**Overview:** For those seeking a full-size built-in solution, the GE Profile Smart Oven with Air Fry is a powerhouse. This 30-inch electric wall oven combines traditional baking and broiling with air frying capabilities, all managed through a 5-inch color touchscreen and the SmartHQ app. It’s designed for families who want smart features without sacrificing cooking capacity.

**Performance & Features:** The GE Profile shines in versatility. Its Air Fry mode uses a high-speed convection fan to circulate superheated air, achieving crispy fries and chicken wings with 70% less oil than deep frying. The oven also includes a “No Preheat” mode for quick meals, and its “Recipe” function guides you through step-by-step cooking instructions. The oven’s true convection system ensures even baking across all three racks. In testing, we found the air fry function produced consistently crisp results, though the preheat time was slightly longer than some countertop models.

**Smart Home Integration:** The SmartHQ app is robust, offering remote preheating, temperature monitoring, and notifications. It integrates with Amazon Alexa, Google Assistant, and even Apple HomeKit. The oven also works with GE’s “Chef Connect” feature, syncing with compatible cooktops to automatically match burner settings.

**Pros:** Large capacity (5.0 cubic feet), versatile cooking modes (air fry, bake, broil, roast), strong smart home ecosystem, self-cleaning feature.
**Cons:** Bulky installation requires professional help, app can be slow to load, no built-in camera for food recognition.

**Price (2026):** $1,699

### 3. Breville Smart Oven Air Fryer Pro

**Overview:** The Breville Smart Oven Air Fryer Pro is the best mid-range option for those who want intelligent features without the premium price tag. This countertop model offers 13 cooking functions, including air fry, dehydrate, and slow cook, all controlled by an LCD display and the Breville “Element IQ” system that adjusts power based on food type.

**Performance & Features:** Breville’s Element IQ technology uses six quartz heating elements that automatically adjust to deliver the right heat for the food you’re cooking. The oven includes a built-in temperature probe and a “Super Convection” setting that reduces cooking time by up to 30%. In testing, the Breville produced perfectly toasted bagels, evenly baked cookies, and air-fried vegetables with excellent texture. The interface is simple and responsive, though it lacks the AI camera of the June oven.

**Smart Home Integration:** The Breville does not have native Wi-Fi or app connectivity, which is its biggest weakness. However, it can be paired with a smart plug for basic on/off control, and it integrates with some smart home hubs via third-party solutions. For users who prioritize local control over cloud connectivity, this may be a plus.

**Pros:** Excellent value for the features, 13 cooking functions, compact footprint, reliable performance.
**Cons:** No built-in Wi-Fi or app, no food recognition AI, smaller capacity than built-in ovens.

**Price (2026):** $399

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy an Intelligent Oven?

- **Busy Professionals:** The June Intelligent Oven is perfect for those who want to set it and forget it. Its AI eliminates guesswork, and the app lets you monitor cooking from anywhere.
- **Families:** The GE Profile Smart Oven offers the capacity and versatility needed for large meals, plus air frying for healthier options. Its smart home integration also helps manage busy schedules.
- **Budget-Conscious Cooks:** The Breville Smart Oven Air Fryer Pro delivers 90% of the smart features at half the price. It’s ideal for renters or those with limited counter space.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $500:** The Breville is your best bet. You sacrifice app connectivity but gain reliable performance and multiple cooking modes.
- **$800–$1,000:** The June Intelligent Oven offers the most advanced AI and app features in a countertop form factor.
- **$1,500–$1,800:** The GE Profile is the premium choice for built-in installations, offering the largest capacity and most comprehensive smart home integration.

**Installation Tip:** Built-in ovens require professional installation, especially if you’re replacing an existing unit. Factor in an additional $150–$300 for wiring and mounting.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation

- **Countertop Models (June, Breville):** Simply place on a stable, heat-resistant surface within 3 feet of a grounded outlet. No permanent installation required. Ensure at least 4 inches of clearance on all sides for ventilation.
- **Built-In Models (GE Profile):** Requires a 30-inch wide cutout with dedicated 240V wiring. Hire a licensed electrician to ensure code compliance. The oven ships with a power cord, but you may need to purchase a new one if your outlet type differs.

### Smart Home Integration

- **Amazon Alexa & Google Assistant:** Both the June and GE Profile support voice commands for preheating, setting timers, and checking cooking status. Example: “Alexa, ask June to preheat to 375 degrees.”
- **Apple HomeKit:** Only the GE Profile offers native HomeKit support, allowing you to include the oven in automations (e.g., “When I leave work, preheat the oven”).
- **IFTTT & Routines:** The June app supports IFTTT for advanced automations, such as turning on the oven light when motion is detected in the kitchen.
- **No App (Breville):** Use a smart plug with energy monitoring to track usage, but you won’t get full control. Consider this a “dumb” smart oven.

**Pro Tip:** For the best experience, ensure your Wi-Fi router supports 5 GHz or Wi-Fi 6E, as many smart ovens perform poorly on 2.4 GHz networks.
