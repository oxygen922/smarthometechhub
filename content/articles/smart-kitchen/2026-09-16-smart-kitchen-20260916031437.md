**Verdict:** If you want a smart oven that genuinely simplifies cooking, the June Intelligent Oven is worth the investment. It's ideal for busy professionals and families who want consistent results with minimal effort.

**Verdict:** The Samsung Family Hub is a premium appliance with a premium price, but for families who want a true smart home hub in the kitchen, it's unmatched. It's also a great choice for those who already use Samsung's ecosystem.

**Verdict:** The Thermomix TM7 is a game-changer for anyone who wants to cook from scratch but lacks time or confidence. It's expensive, but it replaces multiple appliances and can pay for itself in convenience.

## Key Features to Consider

When shopping for smart kitchen appliances, not all features are created equal. Here's what to look for:

- **Connectivity:** Look for Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and support for Matter or Thread for future-proofing.
- **App Quality:** A good app should be intuitive, reliable, and offer remote control and monitoring.
- **Voice Assistant Compatibility:** Alexa and Google Assistant are standard; Apple HomeKit is less common but growing.
- **AI & Automation:** Features like food recognition, cooking presets, and learning algorithms can save time.
- **Energy Efficiency:** Smart appliances often include eco modes and energy monitoring.
- **Integration:** Ensure compatibility with your existing smart home ecosystem (e.g., SmartThings, HomeKit).

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy Smart Kitchen Appliances?

Smart kitchen appliances are no longer just for early adopters. They're for anyone who wants to save time, reduce waste, and make cooking more enjoyable. Here's a breakdown:

- **Busy Professionals:** Smart ovens and cooking assistants like the June Oven and Thermomix can automate meal prep, letting you focus on other tasks.
- **Families:** A smart refrigerator like the Samsung Family Hub helps manage groceries, schedules, and entertainment in one place.
- **Tech Enthusiasts:** If you love building a connected home, these appliances integrate with your existing smart home system.
- **Home Cooks:** Even if you enjoy cooking, smart appliances can help you achieve consistent results and explore new recipes.

### Budget Considerations

Smart kitchen appliances range from a few hundred dollars to several thousand. Here's how to think about your budget:

- **Entry-Level ($200–$500):** Smart slow cookers, air fryers, and coffee makers offer basic app control and voice commands.
- **Mid-Range ($500–$1,500):** Smart ovens, sous vide machines, and multi-cookers with advanced features.
- **High-End ($1,500+):** Premium refrigerators, built-in ovens, and cooking robots with AI and extensive integration.

Consider the long-term value: a high-end appliance that replaces multiple devices or saves you time may be worth the investment. Also, check for energy efficiency rebates and sales, especially during holiday weekends.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Tips

- **Smart Ovens:** Most countertop models are plug-and-play. Built-in models may require professional installation and dedicated circuits.
- **Smart Refrigerators:** Measure your space carefully; these are large and heavy. Ensure your doorway can accommodate the unit. A water line is needed for ice makers and water dispensers.
- **Cooking Assistants:** No installation required—just plug in and connect to Wi-Fi.

### Smart Home Integration

The true power of smart kitchen appliances lies in integration. Here's how to make it work:

1. **Choose a Hub:** If you have a Samsung Family Hub, it can act as a central hub for SmartThings. Otherwise, use a smart speaker or hub like Amazon Echo or Google Nest Hub.
2. **Use Matter and Thread:** These protocols ensure cross-compatibility. Look for the Matter logo when buying new appliances.
3. **Set Up Routines:** Create automations like "Good Morning" that preheats your oven and starts your coffee maker.
4. **Voice Control:** Connect appliances to Alexa or Google Assistant for hands-free operation.
5. **Monitor Energy:** Use apps to track energy usage and schedule appliances during off-peak hours.

**Pro Tip:** Keep your appliances' firmware updated to ensure security and access to new features.
