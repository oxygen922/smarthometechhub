# The Best Intelligent Ovens of 2026: A Comprehensive Review

## Introduction

The intelligent oven has evolved from a novelty gadget into one of the most transformative appliances in the modern smart kitchen. In 2026, these AI-powered appliances can recognize what you're cooking, adjust temperatures automatically, and even order groceries when you're running low. But with dozens of options flooding the market, choosing the right intelligent oven can feel overwhelming.

In this in-depth review, I'll break down the top intelligent ovens available in the US right now, compare their key features, and help you decide which model fits your cooking style and budget. Whether you're a busy parent looking to streamline weeknight dinners or a home chef eager to experiment with sous-vide and air frying, there's an intelligent oven built for you. Let's dive in and find your perfect match.

## Top 3 Product Recommendations

### 1. June Intelligent Oven (2026 Edition) — Best Overall

**Price:** $1,799 (estimated 2026 MSRP)

The June Intelligent Oven has long been the poster child of the smart oven category, and the 2026 Edition pushes the envelope even further. This countertop intelligent oven now features a 6-core AI processor paired with an upgraded 4K camera system that identifies over 250 food items with impressive accuracy.

What sets the June apart is its **Food ID** technology. Place a salmon fillet or a frozen pizza inside, and the oven automatically selects the ideal cooking profile—temperature, time, and even rack position. In my testing, the June correctly identified a homemade lasagna and a tray of chocolate chip cookies without any manual input, cooking both to near perfection.

The companion app has also matured significantly. You can now receive real-time notifications, stream a live video feed of your food, and even create custom cooking programs that sync across multiple June ovens. The 2026 model adds **voice-guided cooking** via Alexa and Google Assistant, walking you through complex recipes step by step.

**Pros:**
- Exceptional food recognition accuracy
- Fast preheating (under 90 seconds)
- Sleek, countertop-friendly design
- Regular over-the-air software updates

**Cons:**
- High price point
- Limited capacity for large families
- Requires stable Wi-Fi for full functionality

**Verdict:** If you want the most advanced intelligent oven on the market and don't mind paying a premium, the June 2026 Edition is the one to beat.

### 2. Samsung Smart Oven with AI Pro Cooking — Best Built-In Option

**Price:** $2,499 (estimated 2026 MSRP)

For homeowners seeking a seamless built-in solution, Samsung's Smart Oven with AI Pro Cooking is a standout. This 30-inch wall oven combines a powerful convection system with an AI camera that detects up to 80 different dishes and automatically sets the optimal cooking mode.

The **AI Pro Cooking** feature is particularly impressive for baking. It monitors browning in real time and adjusts the heating elements to prevent burning—a lifesaver for anyone who's ever ruined a batch of cookies. The oven also integrates deeply with Samsung's SmartThings ecosystem, allowing you to preheat remotely, receive alerts, and even sync cooking times with other smart appliances.

One of my favorite additions in the 2026 model is **Recipe Sync**, which pulls recipes from Samsung Food (formerly Whisk) and automatically configures the oven settings. You can also use the built-in camera to live-stream your cooking to social media—a fun feature for aspiring food influencers.

**Pros:**
- Large capacity (6.0 cu. ft.)
- Deep SmartThings integration
- Excellent baking performance
- Voice control via Bixby, Alexa, and Google Assistant

**Cons:**
- Requires professional installation
- AI features work best with Samsung's ecosystem
- Premium price

**Verdict:** The Samsung Smart Oven is ideal for tech-savvy homeowners building a fully connected kitchen. Its AI baking assistance is genuinely useful, not just a gimmick.

### 3. Brava Glass Smart Oven — Best for Speed and Versatility

**Price:** $1,295 (estimated 2026 MSRP)

The Brava Glass Smart Oven uses high-intensity light bulbs instead of traditional heating elements, cooking food up to five times faster than a conventional oven. This countertop intelligent oven is a favorite among busy professionals who want restaurant-quality meals in minutes.

The 2026 model introduces **Multi-Zone Cooking**, allowing you to cook different foods simultaneously on the same tray without flavor transfer. I tested this by cooking salmon, roasted vegetables, and a dinner roll at the same time—each came out perfectly cooked. The Brava app includes hundreds of guided recipes, and the oven automatically adjusts cooking times based on the food's weight and thickness.

Brava's **Pure Light** technology also means no preheating is required, which is a massive time-saver. The oven connects via Wi-Fi and supports voice control through Alexa and Google Assistant. While it lacks the food recognition camera of the June, its speed and versatility make it a compelling alternative.

**Pros:**
- Extremely fast cooking times
- No preheating needed
- Multi-zone cooking eliminates flavor mixing
- Compact and stylish design

**Cons:**
- Limited capacity
- Light bulbs require periodic replacement
- Steeper learning curve for traditional cooks

**Verdict:** The Brava Glass is perfect for small households and anyone who values speed above all else. It's not a replacement for a full-size oven, but it's an excellent companion.

### Budget Considerations

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

**Countertop Models (June, Brava):** These are as simple as unpacking and plugging into a standard 120V outlet. No special wiring is needed. Both connect to your Wi-Fi network via their respective apps, and setup takes about 10 minutes. For smart home integration, both work with Alexa and Google Assistant, allowing voice commands like "Alexa, preheat the June oven to 400 degrees."

**Built-In Models (Samsung):** Installation is more involved. You'll need a 240V outlet and a standard 30-inch wall cutout. I strongly recommend hiring a licensed electrician and installer. Once installed, the Samsung oven integrates seamlessly with SmartThings, letting you create routines like "Good Morning" that preheat the oven while you brew coffee.

**Matter and Thread Support:** As of 2026, the June and Samsung ovens support Matter, the universal smart home standard. This means they can communicate with Apple Home, Amazon Alexa, Google Home, and Samsung SmartThings without proprietary bridges. The Brava Glass currently supports Matter via a firmware update but lacks native Thread support.

**Pro Tip:** For the best experience, ensure your Wi-Fi router supports 2.4GHz and 5GHz bands. All three ovens performed best on a 5GHz network with a strong signal.
