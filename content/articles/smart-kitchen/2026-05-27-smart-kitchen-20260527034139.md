### Cooking Modes and Versatility
Smart ovens offer multiple cooking modes beyond traditional bake and broil. Look for air frying, dehydrating, slow cooking, and sous vide capabilities. The more modes, the more you can replace standalone appliances. The June and Breville excel here, while Bosch focuses on precision baking and roasting.

### AI and Sensor Technology
AI-driven features like food recognition and automatic adjustments save time and reduce errors. The June's camera-based system is ideal for beginners, while Bosch's sensor technology appeals to experienced cooks who want consistent results. Breville's Element IQ provides precise power distribution but lacks visual recognition.

### Connectivity and App Integration
Seamless integration with your smart home ecosystem is key. Matter protocol support (as seen in the June) ensures future-proof compatibility. Consider whether you need remote preheating, recipe guidance, or voice control. All three models offer robust app experiences, but Bosch's Home Connect ecosystem is more expansive for multi-appliance households.

### Self-Cleaning
Pyrolytic cleaning (Bosch) burns off residue at high temperatures, while steam cleaning (June and Breville) uses moisture to loosen grime. Pyrolytic is more thorough but uses more energy. Steam cleaning is faster and eco-friendly but may require manual wiping.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Oven?
- **Busy Professionals:** The June Intelligent Oven is perfect for those who want "set it and forget it" convenience. Its food recognition and app controls mean you can start dinner from your office.
- **Home Chefs:** The Bosch 800 Series suits serious cooks who need large capacity and precise control. Its sensor technology ensures consistent results for complex recipes.
- **Budget-Conscious Enthusiasts:** The Breville PolyScience offers excellent performance at a lower price. It's ideal for smaller households or those new to smart cooking.

### Budget Considerations
- **Under $1,000:** The Breville PolyScience ($799) provides great value with versatile cooking modes and solid app integration.
- **$1,000 - $1,500:** The June Intelligent Oven ($1,199) balances premium features with a reasonable price for its AI capabilities.
- **Over $2,000:** The Bosch 800 Series ($2,499) is a long-term investment for those building a dream kitchen. Its built-in design and professional-grade features justify the cost.

### Space and Installation
Countertop models (June, Breville) require 18-24 inches of clearance and a standard outlet. Built-in models (Bosch) need professional installation and may require electrical upgrades. Measure your kitchen carefully before purchasing.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Process
- **Countertop Ovens:** Simply plug into a grounded outlet and connect to Wi-Fi via the app. Ensure the oven is placed on a heat-resistant surface with at least 6 inches of clearance on all sides.
- **Built-in Ovens:** Hire a licensed electrician to ensure proper wiring (typically 240V, 30-amp circuit). Follow manufacturer guidelines for cabinet dimensions. Bosch offers a free installation consultation with purchase.

### Smart Home Integration
- **Voice Control:** All three models work with Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant. You can preheat, set timers, and check status hands-free. The June also supports Apple HomeKit for Siri commands.
- **Automation:** Use routines to trigger the oven. For example, "Alexa, start preheating when I leave work" via geofencing. The Bosch Home Connect app allows multi-appliance scenes, like turning on the oven and range hood simultaneously.
- **Matter Protocol:** The June's Matter support ensures compatibility with future smart home devices, making it a future-proof choice.

### Tips for Optimal Use
- Place the oven near your router for stable Wi-Fi.
- Update firmware regularly to access new features.
- Use the app's recipe library for guided cooking—many include step-by-step instructions and automatic adjustments.
