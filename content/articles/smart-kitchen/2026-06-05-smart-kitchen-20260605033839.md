## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy Smart Kitchen Appliances?

**Busy Professionals:** If you're short on time but want home-cooked meals, smart appliances like the June Oven or Thermomix TM8 can cut cooking time by 30-40% through automation and guided recipes.

**Families:** The Samsung Bespoke Refrigerator is ideal for parents who need to manage grocery lists, meal plans, and keep track of perishables. Its large capacity and family hub features make it a central command center.

**Cooking Enthusiasts:** For those who love experimenting, the Thermomix TM8 offers professional-grade capabilities in a compact package. The guided recipes help you master new techniques without the usual trial and error.

**Tech Early Adopters:** If you already have a smart home ecosystem, look for appliances that integrate with your existing platform (Alexa, Google, or Apple HomeKit). The June Oven and Samsung Bespoke offer the best compatibility.

### Budget Considerations

- **Entry-Level ($150-$400):** Smart multicookers like the Instant Pot Pro Plus or smart slow cookers offer basic connectivity and app control. These are great for testing the waters without a major investment.

- **Mid-Range ($400-$1,000):** Countertop ovens like the June Oven or Anova Precision Oven provide significant functionality. They're ideal for those who want genuine time savings and cooking versatility.

- **Premium ($1,000+):** Full-size smart refrigerators and high-end food processors like the Thermomix TM8 represent serious investments. Expect premium build quality, extensive features, and long-term reliability.

**Hidden Costs:** Factor in potential subscription fees for recipe services, replacement parts (e.g., filters for refrigerators), and energy usage. Some appliances require ongoing cloud subscriptions for full functionality.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Tips

**Countertop Appliances (June Oven, Thermomix TM8):**
- Ensure your countertop can support the weight (June Oven weighs 28 lbs)
- Leave at least 4 inches of clearance on all sides for ventilation
- Position near a dedicated outlet to avoid tripping breakers

**Refrigerators (Samsung Bespoke):**
- Measure your space carefully—these units require 1-inch clearance on sides and top
- Have a water line installed if you want the ice maker and water dispenser
- Consider a smart outlet to monitor energy usage

### Smart Home Integration

**Amazon Alexa:** Most smart kitchen appliances support Alexa for voice commands. You can say, "Alexa, preheat the June Oven to 375°F" or "Alexa, ask Thermomix to start the pasta recipe."

**Google Assistant:** Similar functionality, with the added benefit of Google Home routines. You can create a "Good Morning" routine that starts your coffee maker and sets the oven to warm.

**Apple HomeKit:** Support is less common but growing. The Anova Precision Oven and some Samsung appliances offer native HomeKit integration.

**Zigbee/Z-Wave:** For advanced users, some appliances support these protocols for local control without cloud dependency. This is ideal for privacy-conscious users.

**Pro Tip:** Before purchasing, verify that the appliance supports your preferred voice assistant. Some products only offer basic commands, while others provide full recipe navigation via voice.
