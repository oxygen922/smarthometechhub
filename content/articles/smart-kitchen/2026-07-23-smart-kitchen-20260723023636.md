## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Tips

- **Countertop Models (June, Breville):** Place on a heat-resistant surface (granite, quartz, or stainless steel) with at least 4 inches of clearance on all sides. Use a dedicated 15-amp circuit to avoid tripping breakers.
- **Wall Ovens (Samsung):** Hire a licensed electrician to install a 240V outlet (NEMA 14-50 or hardwired). Ensure the cutout dimensions match your cabinet (standard is 30 inches wide). Most units require a 30-amp breaker.
- **Ventilation:** Smart ovens produce heat and steam. If built-in, ensure proper ventilation per manufacturer specs. Countertop models should not be used in enclosed spaces.

### Smart Home Integration

All three reviewed ovens support major smart home platforms:

- **Amazon Alexa:** "Alexa, preheat the June oven to 375 degrees."
- **Google Assistant:** "Hey Google, check the Samsung oven status."
- **Apple HomeKit:** Not natively supported on most models (Samsung offers limited HomeKit via SmartThings bridge).
- **IFTTT (If This Then That):** Create automations like "If I arrive home, preheat the oven" (requires compatible app).

**Pro Tip:** For seamless integration, choose an oven that matches your existing ecosystem. If you're all-in on Samsung, the Bespoke oven is a no-brainer. If you use Alexa, the June is more responsive.
