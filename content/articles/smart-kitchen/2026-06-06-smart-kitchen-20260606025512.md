## Key Features to Consider (with Comparison Table)

When shopping for an intelligent oven in 2026, focus on these critical features:

- **Cooking Technology:** Look for convection, steam injection, or combination systems. Steam is great for baking and roasting, while convection ensures even heat distribution.
- **AI & Computer Vision:** Higher-end models use cameras and AI to identify food and set cooking parameters automatically. This saves time and reduces guesswork.
- **Smart Home Compatibility:** Ensure the oven works with your existing ecosystem (Alexa, Google Assistant, Apple HomeKit, or Matter).
- **Capacity & Size:** Countertop models are compact but limited. Full-size wall ovens offer more space but require professional installation.
- **App & Recipe Integration:** A robust app with guided recipes, remote monitoring, and push notifications enhances the user experience.
- **Additional Cooking Modes:** Air fry, dehydrate, slow cook, sous-vide, and rotisserie add versatility.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Considerations

- **Countertop Ovens (June):** These are plug-and-play. Simply place on a heat-resistant surface, plug into a standard 120V outlet, and connect to Wi-Fi. No professional installation required.
- **Full-Size Wall Ovens (Breville, GE Profile):** These require a dedicated 240V circuit and a standard 30-inch cutout in your cabinetry. Professional installation is strongly recommended for safety and to ensure proper ventilation. Expect installation costs between $150 and $400.

### Smart Home Integration Tips

- **Matter Protocol:** For maximum compatibility, choose an oven that supports Matter (like the June 2026). This ensures it works with Apple Home, Google Home, Amazon Alexa, and Samsung SmartThings seamlessly.
- **Routines & Automation:** Use your smart home app to create routines. For example, “Good Morning” could preheat the oven to 350°F while your coffee brews. “Dinner Time” could start a roast based on your schedule.
- **Voice Control:** Set timers, adjust temperature, or ask for cooking tips using voice commands. This is especially useful when your hands are full.
- **Remote Monitoring:** All three ovens allow you to check cooking progress from your phone. Use this to avoid overcooking or to get alerts when food is ready.
