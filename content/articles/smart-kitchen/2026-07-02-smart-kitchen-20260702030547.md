## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Requirements

**Refrigerators:**
- Standard 36-inch width opening (measure twice!)
- Dedicated 15-amp circuit (some models require 20-amp)
- Water line connection for ice makers and water dispensers
- Strong Wi-Fi signal near the installation location
- Allow 1-inch clearance on sides and back for ventilation

**Countertop Ovens (June):**
- Standard 120V outlet within 3 feet
- Heat-resistant countertop surface
- Minimum 6 inches clearance above and behind
- Avoid placing near curtains or flammable materials

**Indoor Smokers (GE Profile):**
- Standard 120V outlet
- Ventilation: While filtered, still needs some air circulation
- Sturdy countertop or cart (unit weighs approximately 45 lbs)
- Keep away from smoke detectors (though filtration is excellent)

### Smart Home Integration Tips

1. **Choose a Primary Ecosystem:** Decide if you're going all-in on Apple HomeKit, Google Home, or Amazon Alexa. Mixing ecosystems can create frustrating fragmentation.

2. **Network Requirements:** All three appliances require stable 2.4GHz Wi-Fi. Consider a mesh network system for larger homes to ensure consistent connectivity.

3. **Automation Ideas:**
   - Create a "Good Morning" routine that has your fridge display your calendar while the June oven preheats for breakfast
   - Set up notifications: "Refrigerator door open" or "Smoker reached target temperature"
   - Use geofencing: Have your oven start preheating when you leave work

4. **Future-Proofing:** Look for appliances with Matter protocol support, which is becoming the universal smart home standard in 2026. Samsung and GE have committed to Matter compatibility.

5. **Security Considerations:** Change default passwords, enable two-factor authentication on manufacturer apps, and keep firmware updated. Smart appliances are increasingly targeted by hackers.
