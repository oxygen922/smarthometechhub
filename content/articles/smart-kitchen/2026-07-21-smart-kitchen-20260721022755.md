## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy Which Oven?

- **Tech Enthusiasts & Early Adopters:** The June Intelligent Oven Pro is perfect if you love automation, AI, and minimal effort. It's ideal for singles or couples who cook frequently but want precision without guesswork.

- **Budget-Conscious Families:** The Breville Smart Oven Air Fryer Pro offers exceptional value at $349. It's reliable, versatile, and doesn't require a smartphone to operate. Great for families who need air frying, roasting, and baking in one compact unit.

- **Home Renovators & Luxury Seekers:** The GE Profile Smart Wall Oven is for those building or remodeling a kitchen. It offers professional-grade results, smart scanning, and seamless integration with a modern smart home. Best for households that cook large meals and entertain frequently.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $500:** Countertop models like the Breville or Cuisinart Smart Oven offer excellent performance without Wi-Fi. Perfect for renters or small kitchens.
- **$1,000–$2,000:** Mid-range smart ovens like the June Pro or Wolf Gourmet provide AI features and superior cooking precision.
- **$2,000+:** Built-in ovens from GE, Samsung, or LG offer full connectivity, larger capacity, and professional aesthetics. Factor in installation costs ($200–$500).

### Additional Tips

- Check for energy efficiency: Smart ovens often use less energy than traditional ovens due to precise heating.
- Look for warranty: Most brands offer 1–2 years; extended warranties are available.
- Consider future software updates: AI ovens improve over time with firmware updates.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Requirements

- **Countertop Models (June, Breville):** Simply place on a heat-resistant countertop near a standard 120V outlet. Ensure at least 4 inches of clearance on all sides for ventilation.
- **Built-in Models (GE Profile):** Requires professional installation by a licensed electrician. You'll need a dedicated 240V circuit (typically 30–50 amps) and proper cabinet cutout dimensions. Allow 1–2 hours for installation.

### Smart Home Integration

- **Voice Control:** Both the June Pro and GE Profile work with Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant. Commands like "Alexa, preheat the oven to 350°F" work seamlessly. The June also supports Apple HomeKit for Siri control.
- **App Features:** The June and GE apps allow remote preheating, cooking monitoring, recipe downloads, and notifications when food is done. The Breville lacks app connectivity, so it's best for those who prefer manual control.
- **Automation:** Use routines in Alexa or Google Home to start preheating when you say "Good morning" or when your smart home detects you're arriving home.
- **Compatibility:** Ensure your Wi-Fi network is 2.4GHz (most smart ovens don't support 5GHz). A strong signal is crucial for camera streaming.

**Pro Tip:** For seamless integration, pair your smart oven with a smart smoke detector (like Nest Protect) that can alert you if the oven is left on.
