# The Smart Oven Revolution: 2026's Best Intelligent Ovens Tested and Reviewed

**Introduction**

The modern kitchen has undergone a radical transformation, and at the heart of this evolution sits the intelligent oven. No longer just a box that heats food, today's smart ovens are connected culinary companions that learn your preferences, adjust cooking parameters in real-time, and integrate seamlessly with your broader smart home ecosystem. Whether you're a busy professional seeking convenience, a home chef chasing precision, or a tech enthusiast eager to automate every aspect of your life, the right smart oven can revolutionize your cooking experience. In this comprehensive review, I've spent over 60 hours testing the latest models to bring you the top recommendations, a detailed buying guide, and everything you need to know before making this significant investment. From AI-powered cooking algorithms to voice-controlled preheating, let's explore what 2026's best intelligent ovens have to offer.

---

## Top 3 Product Recommendations

### 1. June Intelligent Oven Gen 4 – The All-Rounder Champion

**Price:** $1,899 (2026 estimate)

The June Intelligent Oven has been a market leader since its inception, and the Gen 4 iteration cements its position as the best all-around smart oven for most households. This countertop convection oven combines a high-resolution camera, a built-in food scale, and AI-powered recognition that identifies over 200 different foods automatically. Place a salmon fillet or a frozen pizza inside, and the oven knows exactly what it is and recommends the perfect cooking profile.

**Performance and Features**

The Gen 4 features a 1800-watt heating system with six independent heating elements, ensuring even cooking across its 1.2 cubic foot interior. The real magic lies in its computer vision system: a 5-megapixel camera captures images every few seconds, allowing the oven to adjust temperature and cook time dynamically. During my testing, it perfectly cooked a medium-rare steak without any manual intervention—the oven detected the meat's thickness and adjusted the searing time accordingly.

The touchscreen interface is responsive and intuitive, running on a custom operating system that receives regular updates. The companion app is equally polished, offering remote monitoring, push notifications when food is ready, and the ability to download new cooking programs. Voice control works flawlessly with both Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant, letting you preheat the oven or check cooking status hands-free.

**Pros and Cons**

The June excels in convenience and consistency. Its learning algorithm improves over time, remembering your preferred doneness for various foods. However, the interior capacity is limited—you can fit a 12-inch pizza or a small roast chicken, but not a full Thanksgiving turkey. The price point also puts it firmly in premium territory.

**Verdict:** If you want the most intelligent, user-friendly smart oven that requires minimal effort, the June Gen 4 is your best bet. It's ideal for small to medium households who value convenience and consistency.

---

### 2. Brava Oven 2.0 – The Speed Demon

**Price:** $1,495 (2026 estimate)

The Brava Oven 2.0 takes a completely different approach to smart cooking, using pure light energy to cook food up to five times faster than conventional ovens. This countertop appliance employs six high-intensity lamps that emit infrared and visible light, combined with precision sensors that monitor food temperature in real-time. The result is cooking that's not only fast but remarkably precise.

**Performance and Features**

The Brava's cooking chamber is divided into three independent cooking zones, each with its own lamp array and temperature sensor. This allows you to cook different foods simultaneously at different temperatures—for example, searing a steak on one side while roasting vegetables on another. The oven comes with a set of specialized cookware, including a ceramic baking dish and a metal sheet pan, each designed to optimize light absorption.

Setup is refreshingly simple: place your food in the appropriate tray, select the cooking program from the 6.5-inch touchscreen, and let the oven do the rest. The camera inside the chamber provides a live feed to your phone, so you can monitor progress without opening the door. The Brava app includes hundreds of pre-programmed recipes, and the oven automatically adjusts cooking parameters based on the specific ingredients you're using.

In my tests, the Brava cooked a whole chicken in 25 minutes that was juicy and evenly browned—a feat that takes 60-90 minutes in a conventional oven. Vegetables retained their texture and color remarkably well, thanks to the short cooking times. However, the specialized cookware is required for most functions, and losing or damaging a piece means replacing it from Brava's store.

**Pros and Cons**

The speed is genuinely impressive, and the precision is excellent for proteins and vegetables. The three-zone cooking is a genuine innovation. On the downside, the oven is relatively small (0.9 cubic feet), and the reliance on proprietary cookware is a limitation. Some users report that the oven's sensors occasionally misinterpret food thickness, leading to undercooking.

**Verdict:** The Brava Oven 2.0 is perfect for time-strapped individuals who want restaurant-quality results in minutes. It's not for large families or those who prefer traditional baking methods.

### 3. GE Profile Smart Oven with Air Fry – The Built-In Powerhouse

**Price:** $3,299 (2026 estimate for 30-inch model)

For those who prefer a built-in solution that integrates fully with their kitchen design, the GE Profile Smart Oven with Air Fry is the top contender. This 30-inch wall oven combines traditional convection cooking with smart features that rival any countertop competitor. It's part of GE's broader smart home ecosystem, which includes refrigerators, dishwashers, and cooktops that communicate with each other.

**Performance and Features**

The GE Profile Smart Oven features a 7-inch color touchscreen that provides access to over 200 pre-programmed recipes from the KitchenAI platform. The oven includes a built-in temperature probe that connects wirelessly to the oven, allowing you to monitor internal meat temperatures from your phone. The air fry function uses a high-speed fan to circulate hot air, producing crispy results with up to 75% less oil than traditional deep frying.

What sets this oven apart is its integration with the broader smart home ecosystem. You can create routines that automatically preheat the oven when you start cooking on the smart cooktop, or have the oven send a notification to your smart speaker when your roast is done. The oven also supports Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant for voice control, and it's compatible with IFTTT for custom automations.

The cooking performance is excellent across the board. The convection system distributes heat evenly, and the temperature calibration is accurate within 5 degrees Fahrenheit. The air fry function is particularly impressive, producing french fries that rival dedicated air fryers. The oven's self-cleaning cycle uses steam to break down food residue, making maintenance much easier than traditional high-temperature cleaning.

**Pros and Cons**

The built-in design is elegant and saves counter space. The smart home integration is the best I've seen, with genuine interoperability between devices. However, the price is steep, and installation requires professional help. The touchscreen interface can be laggy at times, and the app occasionally disconnects from the oven.

**Verdict:** The GE Profile Smart Oven is the best choice for homeowners who want a fully integrated smart kitchen. It's expensive but offers unmatched versatility and ecosystem compatibility.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Oven?

**Busy Professionals:** The June Gen 4 is ideal if you want to minimize time spent in the kitchen. Its food recognition and automatic cooking eliminate guesswork, and the app notifications let you multitask without worry.

**Health-Conscious Cooks:** The Brava Oven 2.0 excels at cooking vegetables and lean proteins quickly while retaining nutrients. Its speed also means less oil is needed for crispy results.

**Smart Home Enthusiasts:** The GE Profile Smart Oven is the clear winner for those who want their oven to communicate with their smart refrigerator, cooktop, and lighting system. The integration possibilities are extensive.

**Large Families:** The GE's 5.0 cubic foot capacity accommodates large roasts, multiple casseroles, and holiday feasts. The June and Brava are better suited for 1-4 person households.

**Traditional Bakers:** If you love baking bread or pastries, the GE's convection system provides the most consistent results. The June and Brava, while capable, have smaller interiors that limit batch sizes.

### Budget Considerations

Smart ovens represent a significant investment, but prices have become more accessible in 2026:

- **Entry-Level ($800-$1,200):** Brands like Cosori and Breville offer smart ovens with basic Wi-Fi connectivity and app control. These lack advanced AI features but provide solid cooking performance.

- **Mid-Range ($1,200-$2,000):** The June Gen 4 and Brava 2.0 fall here, offering the best balance of intelligence and value. This is the sweet spot for most buyers.

- **Premium ($2,500+):** Built-in models like the GE Profile and high-end brands like Wolf and Miele dominate this category. Expect professional-grade construction and deep ecosystem integration.

Consider total cost of ownership: countertop models require no installation costs, while built-in ovens may need electrical upgrades and cabinet modifications. Also factor in the cost of any proprietary cookware—Brava's accessories, for example, add approximately $150 for a complete set.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Requirements

**Countertop Models (June, Brava):** These are plug-and-play. Place the oven on a stable, heat-resistant surface with at least 4 inches of clearance on all sides. They require a standard 120V outlet and draw about 15 amps. No professional installation is needed.

**Built-In Models (GE Profile):** Professional installation is strongly recommended. The oven requires a dedicated 240V circuit (typically 30 or 40 amps). Ensure your cabinet opening matches the oven's cutout dimensions—standard 30-inch models need a 28.5-inch wide opening. GE offers a free installation consultation through their website.

### Smart Home Integration

**Amazon Alexa:** All three models support basic voice commands like "Alexa, preheat the oven to 350 degrees" and "Alexa, set a timer for 30 minutes." The June and GE also support more advanced commands like "Alexa, ask June to cook my steak medium-rare."

**Google Assistant:** Similar functionality to Alexa, with the added benefit of Google Home routines. You can create a "Good morning" routine that preheats the oven while you make coffee.

**Apple HomeKit:** As of 2026, only the GE Profile offers native HomeKit support. The June and Brava can be integrated through third-party bridges like Homebridge.

**Matter Protocol:** All three models are Matter-certified, which means they can be controlled through any Matter-compatible smart home hub, including Apple Home, Google Home, and Samsung SmartThings. This ensures future compatibility as the standard evolves.

**Automation Examples:**
- When your smart lock detects you've arrived home, the oven preheats to 425°F for pizza night.
- When your smart watch shows you've finished a workout, the oven starts cooking a pre-programmed meal.
- When the outdoor temperature exceeds 90°F, the oven automatically switches to air fry mode to avoid heating up the kitchen.
