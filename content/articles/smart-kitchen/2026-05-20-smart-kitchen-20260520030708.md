## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Basics
- **Refrigerators:** Ensure your kitchen has a dedicated 15-amp outlet and enough space for ventilation. Most smart fridges need a stable Wi-Fi signal—consider a mesh network if your kitchen is far from the router.
- **Sous Vide:** No permanent installation required. Just plug it in, attach to a pot or container, and connect to the app.
- **Induction Cooktops:** Requires a 240V electrical connection. Hire a licensed electrician if you’re replacing a gas cooktop. The cooktop itself is flush-mounted into the countertop.

### Smart Home Integration Tips
1. **Choose a Hub:** If you have multiple smart appliances, invest in a Matter-compatible hub like the Amazon Echo Hub or Apple HomePod. This centralizes control and automations.
2. **Create Routines:** Use your smart home app to create routines. For example: “Good morning” routine could preheat the kettle, set the coffee maker, and check the refrigerator’s inventory.
3. **Voice Control Setup:** For the Thermador cooktop (which lacks a built-in assistant), pair it with a smart plug or a voice assistant device to enable “Turn on cooktop” commands.
4. **Security:** Keep your appliances’ firmware updated. Use a separate IoT network on your router to isolate smart devices from your main computers and phones.

### Common Integration Challenges
- **Compatibility:** Not all smart appliances work with every platform. Check the product page for “Works with Apple HomeKit,” “Works with Google Home,” etc.
- **Latency:** Some brands (especially budget ones) have slow app responses. Test the app before buying.
- **Power Outages:** Smart appliances may lose settings during a power outage. Look for models with battery backup for critical functions (e.g., refrigerator temperature memory).
