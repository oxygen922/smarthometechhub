## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Considerations

- **Refrigerators:** Ensure proper clearance for ventilation and water line connections for ice makers. Most smart fridges require a dedicated 15-amp circuit.
- **Countertop Appliances:** The June Oven and Philips Multi-Cooker simply need a standard 120V outlet. Place them on heat-resistant surfaces away from walls.
- **Wi-Fi Requirements:** All smart appliances require a stable 2.4GHz Wi-Fi network. For best performance, use a mesh Wi-Fi system to eliminate dead zones.

### Smart Home Integration

**Setting Up Your Ecosystem:**
1. **Choose a Hub:** If you use multiple smart home devices, consider a hub like Amazon Echo Plus or Samsung SmartThings.
2. **Enable Skills:** Add the manufacturer's skill to your voice assistant (e.g., "June Oven" for Alexa).
3. **Create Routines:** Set up automations like "Good Morning" to have your coffee maker start and fridge display show the weather.
4. **Use Matter Protocol:** For future-proofing, ensure new appliances support Matter for cross-platform compatibility.

**Common Integrations:**
- **Samsung Bespoke AI Fridge:** Works as a SmartThings hub, controlling lights, thermostats, and security cameras.
- **June Intelligent Oven:** Can be triggered by voice commands to preheat while you're still in the living room.
- **Philips Multi-Cooker Pro:** Sends push notifications when pressure cooking is complete, so you never overcook.
