## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Requirements

**Refrigerator (Samsung Bespoke AI):** Standard installation requires a dedicated 115V outlet and 36 inches of width. Allow 1 inch of clearance on each side for ventilation. No special plumbing is needed for the water dispenser if you have an existing line.

**Countertop Oven (June 3.0):** Simply plug into a standard 120V outlet. The oven draws 1,800 watts, so avoid using it on the same circuit as other high-wattage appliances. Place it on a heat-resistant surface with at least 6 inches of clearance above.

**Built-In Coffee Maker (GE Profile):** This requires professional installation. You'll need a dedicated water line (1/4-inch copper or plastic tubing) and a 120V outlet. The unit is designed to fit under standard 15-inch upper cabinets. Most homeowners hire a plumber or appliance installer, costing $150-$300.

### Smart Home Integration

All three products support the major smart home platforms, but there are nuances:

- **Samsung Bespoke AI:** Works best with Samsung SmartThings. It also supports Alexa and Google Assistant for basic commands (e.g., "Set refrigerator to 37°F"). The Family Hub screen can control other SmartThings devices, making it a de facto smart home hub.

- **June Oven 3.0:** Integrates natively with Alexa and Google Assistant. You can say "Alexa, ask June to preheat to 350°F" or create routines like "Start preheating the oven when I leave work." The app also supports IFTTT for advanced automations.

- **GE Profile Coffee Maker:** Connects via the SmartHQ app. It works with Alexa and Google Assistant for voice commands. You can schedule brewing through the app or set routines (e.g., "Brew coffee at 7 AM on weekdays").

**Pro Tip:** For a seamless experience, ensure your Wi-Fi router is within 30 feet of these appliances and supports 2.4GHz and 5GHz bands. Many smart appliances still rely on 2.4GHz for stability.
