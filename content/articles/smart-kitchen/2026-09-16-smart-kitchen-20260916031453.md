# The Best Intelligent Ovens of 2026: Smart Kitchen Upgrades That Actually Cook

## Introduction

The intelligent oven has quietly become one of the most compelling upgrades in the modern smart kitchen. Unlike the early "connected" ovens that merely let you preheat from your phone, today's models use cameras, food probes, AI-powered cooking programs, and app ecosystems to genuinely improve how you cook. In 2026, the category has matured to the point where a smart oven can identify what you've placed inside, recommend a cook mode, and deliver consistent results whether you're roasting a chicken or reheating leftovers.

But not every intelligent oven is worth its asking price. Some lean on gimmicks, while others deliver real, everyday value. In this review, I break down three standout models across different budgets, explain the features that matter most, and help you decide which intelligent oven fits your home. Whether you're remodeling a kitchen or simply want dinner to be easier, this guide will point you in the right direction.

## Top 3 Intelligent Oven Recommendations

### 1. June Intelligent Oven (2026 Edition) — Best Overall for AI Cooking

**Estimated Price: $1,799**

The June Intelligent Oven remains the benchmark for what an intelligent oven can be. The 2026 Edition refines the formula with a faster onboard processor, an upgraded high-definition internal camera, and a redesigned food probe that communicates wirelessly with the oven. The headline feature is still its food-recognition system: place a salmon fillet or a frozen pizza on the tray, and the oven identifies it and suggests the ideal cook mode and time.

In testing, the recognition was impressively accurate for common foods and only occasionally needed manual correction for unusual items. The carbon-fiber heating elements preheat quickly, and the convection system produced evenly browned results. The companion app is genuinely useful, offering live video of your food from anywhere and push notifications when your dish is nearly done.

**What we liked:** Excellent AI food recognition, fast preheating, sleek countertop design, strong app experience.

**What could improve:** The interior is small compared to a full-size wall oven, and the price is steep for a countertop appliance.

**Best for:** Tech-forward cooks who want a countertop oven that does the thinking for them.

### 2. Samsung Bespoke AI Oven with SmartThings — Best for Full-Size Kitchen Integration

**Estimated Price: $2,899**

If you're outfitting a full kitchen, Samsung's Bespoke AI Oven is the intelligent oven to beat. This 30-inch wall oven or range option integrates deeply with SmartThings, Samsung's smart home platform. Its standout feature is the AI Pro Cooking system, which uses an internal camera to recognize up to 80 dishes and automatically sets the optimal temperature and mode. It even sends a photo of your finished dish to your phone when cooking completes — a small touch that's surprisingly handy.

The oven also pairs with Samsung's SmartThings Cooking app, which suggests recipes and can preheat the oven to match a selected recipe's requirements. Voice control via Bixby, Alexa, or Google Assistant works reliably. Build quality is excellent, and the fingerprint-resistant finish holds up well in a busy kitchen.

**What we liked:** Deep smart home integration, large capacity, automatic cook settings, attractive design.

**What could improve:** Best features are locked into the Samsung ecosystem, and installation requires professional help.

**Best for:** Households already invested in SmartThings or Samsung appliances.

---

### 3. Breville PolyScience HydroPro Smart Oven — Best for Precision Cooking Enthusiasts

**Estimated Price: $1,299**

Breville's PolyScience line has long been a favorite among serious home cooks, and the HydroPro Smart Oven brings intelligent features to a more accessible price point. This model focuses less on flashy AI recognition and more on precision: dual temperature sensors, steam-assist cooking, and a smart probe that tracks internal food temperature in real time. The app lets you build custom cooking profiles and save them for repeat use.

Where the HydroPro shines is consistency. Sous-vide-style low-temperature cooking, proofing dough, and dehydrating all work beautifully. Its "Element IQ" system shifts power between heating elements to maintain even temperatures, and the results speak for themselves. It's not as automated as the June, but for cooks who want control with smart assistance, it's a superb choice.

**What we liked:** Outstanding precision, steam-assist cooking, durable construction, versatile cooking modes.

**What could improve:** Less AI automation, and the learning curve is steeper for beginners.

**Best for:** Enthusiast cooks who value precision over full automation.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy an Intelligent Oven?

An intelligent oven makes the most sense for three types of buyers:

- **Busy households** that want consistent results with minimal guesswork. AI recognition and automatic settings remove much of the trial and error from cooking.
- **Smart home enthusiasts** who already use platforms like SmartThings, Alexa, or Google Home and want their oven to be part of that ecosystem.
- **Precision-focused cooks** who enjoy experimenting with low-temperature cooking, proofing, or dehydrating and want smart tools to help.

If you rarely cook or you're happy with a basic oven, the premium price of an intelligent oven may not be justified. A standard convection oven will serve you well for far less.

### Budget Considerations

Intelligent ovens range widely in price:

- **Entry-level countertop models:** $800–$1,300. Expect smart probes and app control, but limited AI features.
- **Mid-range countertop models:** $1,300–$1,800. This is the sweet spot for AI food recognition and cameras.
- **Full-size smart ovens:** $2,000–$3,500+. Best for kitchen remodels and whole-home integration.

Also factor in installation costs for full-size models, which can run $150–$400 depending on your kitchen's wiring and cabinetry. Countertop models require no installation, making them a lower-risk entry point.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

**Countertop intelligent ovens** like the June and Breville models are essentially plug-and-play. Place them on a heat-safe counter with clearance for ventilation, plug into a standard outlet, and connect to your Wi-Fi through the companion app. Setup typically takes 10–15 minutes.

**Full-size smart ovens** like the Samsung Bespoke require professional installation, especially if you're replacing a gas model with electric or vice versa. Once installed, connect the oven to your smart home hub. With SmartThings, you can create routines such as preheating the oven when your phone's location shows you're heading home, or syncing oven notifications with your smart display.

For the best integration experience:

1. **Choose a platform first.** If you use Alexa throughout your home, prioritize ovens that support it natively.
2. **Check Wi-Fi reliability.** A weak signal in the kitchen can cause app and voice control dropouts.
3. **Update firmware.** Most smart ovens improve significantly after their first few software updates.
4. **Pair with compatible accessories.** Smart probes, meat thermometers, and even smart scales can enhance the experience.
