**Estimated Price:** $3,299 (2026, 30-inch slide-in model)

Samsung's Bespoke AI Oven brings intelligent cooking to a full-size, built-in form factor. The headline feature is **Vision AI**, an internal camera system that identifies up to 80 different dishes and automatically sets the optimal cooking mode, temperature, and time. It's powered by Samsung's on-device AI processor, so recognition happens locally rather than in the cloud—a meaningful privacy advantage.

During testing, the Vision AI correctly identified a tray of roasted vegetables and a sheet-pan dinner, then recommended convection roast settings. The oven also integrates deeply with **SmartThings**, Samsung's smart home platform, allowing voice control via Bixby, Alexa, or Google Assistant. You can preheat the oven from your car, receive a notification when your roast hits temperature, and even sync cooking with a SmartThings-compatible recipe.

The Bespoke line's customizable door panels (available in multiple colors and finishes) make it one of the most attractive intelligent ovens on the market. The dual convection system and steam-clean function round out a genuinely premium package.

**Pros:**

## Buying Guide: Who Should Buy an Intelligent Oven?

### Who Is an Intelligent Oven For?

Intelligent ovens are ideal for:

- **Busy professionals** who want consistent results with minimal attention
- **Tech enthusiasts** building out a connected smart home
- **Home cooks** who struggle with timing and temperature accuracy
- **Families** who want to reduce meal-prep friction

They're less essential for:

- **Serious bakers** who prefer manual control and precise, repeatable techniques
- **Budget shoppers** who rarely use advanced features
- **Renters** who can't install built-in appliances (countertop models are the exception)

### Budget Considerations

In 2026, intelligent ovens span three rough tiers:

- **Entry-level ($800–$1,500):** Basic Wi-Fi connectivity, app control, guided cooking. GE Profile sits at the top of this tier.
- **Mid-range ($1,500–$2,500):** Camera-based recognition, temperature probes, air frying. June's countertop oven fits here.
- **Premium ($2,500–$4,000+):** Full-size built-in models with advanced AI, premium finishes, and deep ecosystem integration. Samsung's Bespoke line leads this segment.

Don't forget to factor in **installation costs** for built-in models ($150–$400), potential **electrical upgrades** ($200–$800 if you need a new 240V line), and **extended warranties** ($100–$300).

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Basics

**Countertop intelligent ovens** like the June require no installation—just counter space and a standard 120V outlet. Place them near a vent or window for steam and heat dissipation.

**Built-in and slide-in models** are more involved. You'll need:

- A dedicated 240V outlet (most full-size ovens)
- Proper cabinet cutouts matching the manufacturer's specs
- A licensed installer for gas lines if applicable
- Clearance for ventilation and door swing

We strongly recommend professional installation for built-in intelligent ovens. Improper installation can void warranties and create fire hazards.

### Smart Home Integration

All three ovens support Wi-Fi and major voice assistants. Here's how they fit into a broader smart home:

- **June:** Works well with Alexa and Google Assistant. You can create routines like "Good morning" that preheat the oven while your smart lights turn on.
- **Samsung:** Best integration if you use SmartThings. The oven can trigger automations—for example, turning on the range hood when cooking starts.
- **GE Profile:** Solid SmartHQ integration with IFTTT support for advanced automations.

For the most seamless experience, match your oven brand to your existing smart home ecosystem. If you're building from scratch, SmartThings offers the widest appliance compatibility.
