# The 2026 Smart Oven Revolution: Three Intelligent Ovens That Actually Earn Their Counter Space

Smart kitchen technology has evolved from gimmicky app-controlled appliances to genuinely indispensable cooking tools. Among the most transformative additions to the modern American kitchen is the intelligent oven—a device that combines convection cooking, steam injection, computer vision, and AI-driven recipe automation into a single countertop powerhouse. After spending six weeks testing the latest 2026 models in real-world conditions, I can confidently say these aren't just fancy toaster ovens anymore. They're precision cooking instruments that can roast a perfect chicken, bake artisanal bread, and air-fry crispy wings—all while you monitor progress from your phone across the house. This review breaks down the three best intelligent ovens available right now, what features genuinely matter, and how to choose the right one for your cooking style and budget.

## Top 3 Product Recommendations

### 1. Brava Glass 2.0 – The AI Visionary

**Price: $1,295 (2026 MSRP)**

The Brava Glass 2.0 is the closest thing to having a professional chef with a thermal camera living in your kitchen. Building on the original Brava's innovative light-based cooking technology, the 2.0 iteration adds a 12-megapixel internal camera paired with computer vision software that identifies food in real time. This isn't just a camera for show—it actively adjusts cooking parameters mid-cycle. Place a salmon fillet on the included metal tray, select "Salmon" from the 6.5-inch touchscreen, and the oven detects the exact thickness, surface area, and even doneness level, then automatically calibrates temperature and cook time.

What sets the Glass 2.0 apart is its Pure Light cooking system, which uses targeted halogen bulbs to deliver precise energy to specific zones of the cooking surface. The oven can cook different foods at different temperatures simultaneously—imagine searing a steak on one side of the tray while gently steaming broccoli on the other. In my testing, this multi-zone capability consistently produced restaurant-quality results. The sear on a strip steak was deeply caramelized, while the vegetables retained vibrant color and crunch.

The built-in camera also enables a "Live View" feature that streams a real-time video feed to the companion smartphone app. You can watch your bread rise or your cheese melt from anywhere. More impressively, the AI can send alerts like "Your roast has reached medium-rare—rest it now." This level of intelligence genuinely changes how you approach cooking.

**Pros:**
- Exceptional multi-zone cooking flexibility
- Genuinely useful AI vision technology
- Fast preheating (under 60 seconds to 450°F)
- Excellent companion app with recipe library

**Cons:**
- Premium price point
- Limited capacity (fits a 9x13-inch pan)
- Requires proprietary metal trays for optimal performance

### 2. June Oven 4.0 – The Versatile All-Rounder

**Price: $995 (2026 MSRP)**

The June Oven has been a mainstay in the smart oven category since 2016, and the 4.0 iteration refines the formula to near-perfection. This 12-in-1 appliance handles air frying, broiling, baking, roasting, dehydrating, and even slow cooking. The 4.0 model introduces a larger 14.5-liter interior (up from 12 liters) that comfortably accommodates a 5-pound chicken or a 12-inch pizza.

What makes the June 4.0 particularly compelling is its automatic food recognition system. The internal camera and scale work together to identify over 200 food types—from chicken wings to chocolate chip cookies—and then apply the optimal cooking profile. In my testing, the food recognition was accurate approximately 95% of the time. When it did miss, the manual override via the 7-inch HD touchscreen was intuitive and responsive.

The June 4.0 excels at air frying. The high-velocity fan system produces exceptionally crispy results with minimal oil—my french fries came out golden and shatteringly crisp, rivaling dedicated air fryers. The baking performance is equally impressive, with even heat distribution that eliminated the hot spots common in budget ovens. A sourdough loaf emerged with a beautiful crust and open crumb structure.

The app integration is mature and reliable. You can start preheating from bed, receive notifications when your food is done, and even view a live camera feed. The recipe library, curated by professional chefs, offers guided cooking experiences that adjust automatically based on your oven's real-time readings.

**Pros:**
- Excellent value for the feature set
- Accurate food recognition
- Superior air frying performance
- Large capacity for a countertop oven

**Cons:**
- Camera quality lags behind Brava Glass 2.0
- No true multi-zone cooking
- App occasionally requires reconnection

### 3. Tovala Pro X – The Subscription-Free Smart Steam Oven

**Price: $699 (2026 MSRP)**

Tovala made its name with a scan-to-cook meal delivery service, but the Pro X is a significant departure—a standalone intelligent steam oven that doesn't require any subscription. This is the model for health-conscious cooks who want precision steam cooking, sous-vide capabilities, and reliable baking without paying a monthly fee.

The Pro X features a 1,800-watt steam generator that produces consistent, superheated steam for even cooking. The steam function is transformative for vegetables—broccoli comes out bright green and perfectly tender-crisp, while fish stays moist and flaky. The oven also includes a traditional convection mode for roasting and baking, plus a combination steam-and-bake mode that produces exceptional artisan bread with a crackling crust and chewy interior.

What makes the Pro X "intelligent" is its built-in food thermometer and weight sensor. The thermometer monitors internal temperature in real time, automatically adjusting cooking time to hit your target doneness. The weight sensor enables the oven to calculate precise cook times based on food mass. Combined, these features deliver remarkably consistent results without requiring the camera-based AI of pricier competitors.

The control interface is a 5-inch color touchscreen that's responsive and well-organized. The companion app offers over 300 free recipes, and the scan-to-cook feature (no subscription needed) uses QR code recognition to configure the oven for compatible store-bought foods. It's a clever workaround that provides convenience without locking you into a proprietary meal kit ecosystem.

**Pros:**
- Best steam cooking in its price class
- No subscription required
- Built-in thermometer for precision cooking
- Excellent value

**Cons:**
- No camera for remote monitoring
- Smaller capacity (12 liters)
- Less "wow factor" than AI-equipped rivals

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy Each Oven

**The Brava Glass 2.0** is ideal for cooking enthusiasts who regularly prepare multiple dishes simultaneously and want the latest AI-assisted technology. If you often find yourself juggling a protein, two sides, and a sauce, the multi-zone cooking capability is a game-changer. It's also perfect for tech-savvy home cooks who enjoy monitoring their food remotely via live video.

**The June Oven 4.0** suits families and busy professionals who want maximum versatility in a single appliance. Its larger capacity, reliable food recognition, and excellent air frying make it the best all-around choice. If you're replacing both a toaster oven and an air fryer, this is the smartest investment.

**The Tovala Pro X** appeals to health-conscious cooks who prioritize steam cooking and precise temperature control. It's also the best budget-conscious option for those who want smart features without the premium price tag. If you bake bread regularly or cook a lot of fish and vegetables, the steam functionality is indispensable.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $700:** The Tovala Pro X at $699 is the only intelligent oven in this range that offers genuine smart features. Traditional convection toaster ovens with minimal smart functionality start around $300, but you sacrifice precision and automation.

- **$700–$1,000:** The June Oven 4.0 at $995 represents the sweet spot for most buyers. It offers the best balance of features, capacity, and price. Look for seasonal sales—June frequently discounts $100–$150 during major retail holidays.

- **$1,000–$1,500:** The Brava Glass 2.0 commands a premium for its advanced AI and multi-zone cooking. If you cook complex meals regularly, the time savings and consistent results justify the investment.

### Hidden Costs to Consider

Beyond the purchase price, factor in accessories. Brava requires proprietary metal trays and pans ($40–$80 each), while June and Tovala use standard baking sheets and air fryer baskets. Also consider energy costs—these ovens draw 1,500–1,800 watts, comparable to a standard toaster oven but less than a full-size electric oven.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

All three ovens are countertop units that require minimal installation. Here's what you need to know:

**Physical Setup:** Each unit is essentially plug-and-play. You'll need a dedicated 15-amp outlet (standard in US kitchens). Clearance requirements vary—Brava recommends 6 inches of space above and 4 inches on each side, while June and Tovala need only 3 inches. Ensure your countertop can support the weight (Brava: 28 lbs, June: 32 lbs, Tovala: 24 lbs).

**Wi-Fi Connectivity:** All three ovens require a 2.4GHz Wi-Fi network for initial setup and app connectivity. If you're using a mesh network, ensure the oven connects to the 2.4GHz band rather than 5GHz. The June and Brava apps handle network changes gracefully, but Tovala occasionally requires a factory reset if the Wi-Fi credentials change.

**Smart Home Ecosystem Compatibility:**

- **Amazon Alexa:** June Oven 4.0 offers the most robust Alexa integration—you can preheat, check cooking status, and receive alerts via voice commands. Brava supports basic Alexa commands for preheating and status checks. Tovala has limited Alexa support (only on/off commands).

- **Google Home:** June and Brava both offer Google Assistant compatibility with similar functionality to Alexa. Tovala lacks Google Home integration entirely.

- **Apple HomeKit:** None of these ovens currently support HomeKit, which is a notable gap for Apple-centric smart homes. Workarounds exist using third-party smart plugs, but you lose two-way communication.

- **IFTTT:** June and Brava support IFTTT applets for creative automations (e.g., "When my phone alarm goes off, start preheating the oven"). Tovala does not offer IFTTT integration.

**App Quality Assessment:** The June app is the most polished, with a clean interface and reliable push notifications. Brava's app is equally capable but slightly more complex due to the multi-zone cooking controls. Tovala's app is functional but feels dated compared to its competitors.

## Conclusion & Final Verdict

After extensive testing, the intelligent oven category has clearly matured. These aren't novelties—they're genuinely capable cooking appliances that deliver consistent, high-quality results while saving time and energy. The technology has reached a point where the AI assistance feels helpful rather than gimmicky, and the cooking performance rivals traditional full-size ovens.

**The Overall Winner: June Oven 4.0**

For the vast majority of American households, the June Oven 4.0 is the smartest purchase. It offers the best combination of capacity, versatility, and reliability at a reasonable price point. The food recognition works well, the air frying is exceptional, and the app integration is seamless. It's the one oven I'd recommend without hesitation to friends and family.

**The Premium Pick: Brava Glass 2.0**

If budget is less of a concern and you're passionate about cooking, the Brava Glass 2.0's multi-zone cooking and superior AI vision make it a truly exciting product. It's the only countertop oven that can genuinely cook an entire meal simultaneously at different temperatures, and the live camera feed is both fun and functional.

**The Value Pick: Tovala Pro X**

For budget-conscious buyers who prioritize healthy cooking, the Tovala Pro X delivers exceptional steam performance at a compelling price. The built-in thermometer ensures perfectly cooked proteins, and the lack of subscription fees makes it a smart long-term investment.

**Final Recommendation:** If you cook at least three times per week and want to reduce your reliance on a full-size oven, any of these intelligent ovens will transform your kitchen workflow. The June Oven 4.0 offers the best overall value, but your final choice should align with your specific cooking habits and budget. Whichever you choose, you're stepping into the future of home cooking—where precision, convenience, and intelligence come together in a single countertop appliance.
