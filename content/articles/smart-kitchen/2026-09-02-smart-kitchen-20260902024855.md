## Key Features to Consider

When evaluating smart ovens, understanding the technology that matters will help you make an informed decision. Here's what to look for:

### AI Food Recognition
This feature uses cameras and computer vision to identify food and automatically set cooking parameters. It's not just a novelty—it genuinely reduces cooking guesswork and prevents over or undercooking.

### Temperature Precision
Look for ovens with temperature control within ±5 degrees Fahrenheit. Models with PID (proportional-integral-derivative) controllers offer the most precise temperature management, which is crucial for baking and sous vide cooking.

### Steam Capabilities
Steam-assisted cooking produces better results for bread, meats, and vegetables. Some ovens offer full humidity control, while others provide limited steam bursts. Determine your cooking style to decide which level of steam functionality you need.

### Connectivity and App Support
Consider the quality of the companion app. Look for features like remote monitoring, recipe libraries, firmware updates, and integration with other smart home platforms. A good app can significantly enhance the cooking experience.

### Capacity and Form Factor
Countertop models typically accommodate up to a 12x18-inch baking sheet, while built-in models offer full-size capacity. Consider your typical cooking volume and kitchen space when choosing between these options.

### Cooking Modes
Modern smart ovens offer anywhere from 8 to 15+ cooking modes. While more isn't always better, having options like air frying, dehydrating, and proofing can expand your culinary repertoire.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Countertop Models

Countertop smart ovens like the June and Anova require minimal installation. Simply place the oven on a stable, heat-resistant surface with adequate ventilation (at least 4 inches clearance on all sides). Most models come with a standard 120V power cord that plugs into any grounded outlet.

For optimal smart home integration, ensure your oven is within range of your Wi-Fi network. Some users report connectivity issues when ovens are placed far from routers. Consider using a mesh Wi-Fi system to ensure reliable connections throughout your kitchen.

### Built-In Models

Built-in smart ovens require professional installation. Expect to pay $200-$500 for installation, depending on your current kitchen setup. Key considerations include:

- **Electrical requirements:** Most built-in ovens require a dedicated 240V circuit
- **Cabinet modifications:** Ensure your existing cabinet space matches the oven's dimensions
- **Ventilation:** Proper airflow prevents overheating and ensures accurate temperature control

### Platform Compatibility

Before purchasing, verify that your chosen oven works with your existing smart home ecosystem:

- **Amazon Alexa:** All three recommended ovens support Alexa voice commands for basic controls
- **Google Assistant:** June, Samsung, and Anova all offer Google Assistant integration
- **Apple HomeKit:** Only Samsung offers HomeKit compatibility among our top picks
- **Zigbee/Z-Wave:** Some built-in ovens support these protocols for advanced automation

### Creating Automated Routines

The real power of smart ovens emerges when you create automated routines. For example:

- **Coming Home:** Use your smart lock to trigger the oven to preheat when you arrive home
- **Dinner Time:** Set a routine where the oven starts cooking 30 minutes before your scheduled dinner
- **Energy Saving:** Automatically lower oven temperature when your smart thermostat detects you're away
