## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Requirements

**Samsung Bespoke AI Family Hub Refrigerator:**
- **Electrical:** Standard 115V outlet within 6 feet of installation location.
- **Water Line:** Required for ice maker and water dispenser (optional but recommended).
- **Space:** Allow 1 inch clearance on sides and back for ventilation.
- **Wi-Fi:** Strong 2.4GHz or 5GHz connection required for smart features.

**June Intelligent Oven:**
- **Electrical:** Standard 120V outlet, 15-amp circuit.
- **Counter Space:** Requires 18 inches width, 14 inches height, and 20 inches depth.
- **Ventilation:** Place at least 4 inches from walls for proper airflow.
- **Wi-Fi:** 2.4GHz connection recommended for optimal performance.

**Thermidor Smart Induction Cooktop:**
- **Electrical:** Requires dedicated 240V circuit with 40-amp breaker (professional installation mandatory).
- **Cutout Dimensions:** 34 inches width, 19 inches depth for 36-inch model.
- **Ventilation:** Requires proper ventilation system (downdraft or overhead hood).
- **Cookware:** Must be induction-compatible (magnetic base).

### Smart Home Integration

All three products integrate with major smart home platforms, but the level of integration varies:

**Samsung SmartThings:** The Samsung refrigerator serves as a SmartThings hub, allowing you to control compatible devices directly from its display. You can monitor security cameras, adjust thermostats, and control lighting without leaving the kitchen.

**Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant:** Voice commands enable hands-free operation. Examples include:
- "Alexa, preheat the June oven to 350 degrees."
- "Hey Google, set the Thermidor cooktop to medium heat."
- "Alexa, what's in my Samsung refrigerator?"

**IFTTT (If This Then That):** Create custom automations, such as:
- If the refrigerator door is left open, send a smartphone notification.
- If the June oven finishes cooking, turn on the kitchen lights.

**Z-Wave and Zigbee Compatibility:** The Samsung refrigerator supports Z-Wave and Zigbee protocols, allowing integration with a wider range of smart home devices.
