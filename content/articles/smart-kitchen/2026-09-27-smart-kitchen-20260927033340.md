### What to Look For

## Buying Guide: Who Should Buy an Intelligent Oven?

### Who Should Buy

- **Busy Professionals:** If you want to start dinner from your phone during your commute, an intelligent oven with remote start is a game-changer.
- **Home Bakers:** Steam-injection models like the Breville HydroPro deliver professional results.
- **Smart Home Enthusiasts:** If your home runs on Alexa, Google, or Apple Home, a Matter-compatible oven integrates seamlessly.
- **Large Families:** Full-size built-in intelligent ovens like the Samsung Bespoke handle holiday meals with ease.

### Who Should Skip

- **Budget-Conscious Cooks:** A basic convection oven costs a fraction of the price. If you rarely use apps, the premium isn't worth it.
- **Renters:** Built-in models require installation; countertop models are a better fit.
- **Technophobes:** If you prefer knobs and dials, a traditional oven may serve you better.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $500:** Entry-level smart countertop ovens with basic app control.
- **$500–$1,500:** Mid-range intelligent ovens with AI features and cameras (June sits at the top of this range).
- **$1,500–$3,000:** Premium countertop and built-in models with steam, probes, and full smart home integration.
- **$3,000+:** Luxury built-in ovens with professional-grade features.

Factor in potential energy savings — intelligent ovens often use less power than traditional ovens thanks to precise heating and shorter preheat times.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Countertop Models

Countertop intelligent ovens like the June and Breville require no installation. Simply place them on a heat-resistant surface, plug into a standard 120V outlet, and connect to Wi-Fi via the companion app. Setup typically takes 10–15 minutes.

### Built-In Models

Full-size intelligent ovens like the Samsung Bespoke require professional installation. Expect to pay $200–$500 for installation, and confirm your cabinet cutout dimensions match the oven's specs. These models usually need a 240V outlet.

### Smart Home Integration Tips

1. **Choose Matter-compatible devices** for future-proof interoperability.
2. **Set up routines:** Use Alexa or Google Home routines to preheat your oven at a scheduled time.
3. **Enable notifications:** Get alerts when preheating finishes or food is ready.
4. **Secure your network:** Smart appliances are IoT devices — use a strong Wi-Fi password and consider a separate network for smart home devices.
5. **Update firmware regularly:** Manufacturers push performance and security updates through the app.
