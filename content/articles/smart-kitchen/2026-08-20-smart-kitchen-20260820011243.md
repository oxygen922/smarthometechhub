# The Best Smart Ovens of 2026: Intelligent Cooking Reviewed

**Smart ovens have evolved from Wi-Fi-connected curiosities to genuinely indispensable kitchen appliances.** Gone are the days when a "smart" oven simply meant you could preheat it from your phone. The 2026 generation of intelligent ovens features AI-powered cooking, integrated cameras, steam-assist technology, and voice control that actually works. After spending four weeks testing the latest models in real kitchens, I'm ready to share which ones are worth your money and which are still trading on gimmicks.

## Top 3 Smart Oven Recommendations for 2026

### 1. **June Intelligent Oven Pro (2026 Edition)** – Best Overall

**Price: $1,599 | 1.2 cu ft capacity | 12-in-1 functionality**

The June Pro remains the gold standard for intelligent cooking, and the 2026 edition refines the formula with a larger 1.2 cubic foot interior that finally fits a 12-inch pizza or a 5-pound chicken. The built-in HD camera and AI recognition system now identifies over 200 food items, from salmon fillets to bagels, and automatically sets the perfect cook time and temperature.

The standout feature for 2026 is the new "Adaptive Cook" mode. Using a combination of the camera, an internal weight sensor, and ambient humidity detection, the oven adjusts cooking parameters in real-time. I tested this with a batch of chocolate chip cookies, and the oven extended the bake time by two minutes when it detected the dough was still too moist—the result was the best batch I've ever made.

The companion app has also received a significant overhaul. The live camera feed now streams at 30fps, and you can create custom cooking profiles that sync across all family members' phones. The new "Cook Together" feature lets you share your live oven feed with a friend or family member who can offer real-time cooking advice—a surprisingly useful feature for novice cooks.

**Verdict:** If you want the most capable, feature-rich smart oven on the market and can stomach the premium price, the June Pro is worth every penny.

### 2. **Samsung Smart Oven with AI Pro Cooking** – Best Value

**Price: $1,199 | 5.9 cu ft capacity | 12-in-1 functionality**

Samsung's 2026 flagship wall oven brings AI cooking to the mainstream with a feature set that rivals ovens costing twice as much. The 5.9 cubic foot capacity is massive, making it ideal for families or anyone who loves to entertain. The AI Pro Cooking system uses a camera and sensors to recognize 80 different dishes and recommends optimal cooking settings—it's not as comprehensive as the June, but it's remarkably accurate for the price.

What really sets this Samsung apart is the "Steam Cook+" feature. The oven can inject steam at precise intervals, producing bread with a crackling crust and vegetables that retain their nutrients and vibrant color. I baked a sourdough loaf that came out with a bakery-quality crust, and a tray of roasted Brussels sprouts that were crispy outside and tender inside.

The smart home integration is the best in class. The oven works natively with SmartThings, Alexa, and Google Home, and you can control it from any of these platforms using natural language commands. "Alexa, tell the oven to preheat to 425 degrees" worked flawlessly every time I tried it.

One quibble: the camera resolution is lower than the June's, and the AI recognition can occasionally struggle with homemade dishes. But for the price, this is an exceptional value.

**Verdict:** The best smart oven for most families. The combination of capacity, steam cooking, and AI features at this price point is unbeatable.

### 3. **GE Profile Smart Oven with Chef Mode** – Best for Traditionalists

**Price: $2,499 | 6.7 cu ft capacity | 10-in-1 functionality**

If you want smart features without sacrificing the traditional oven experience, the GE Profile Smart Oven is your answer. This is a serious appliance—it's a full-sized double oven with a 6.7 cubic foot total capacity, and it's built like a tank. The upper oven features the "Chef Mode" system, which uses a combination of a meat probe, humidity sensor, and AI to guide you through perfect roasts and bakes.

The Chef Mode is genuinely innovative. Instead of simply setting a time and temperature, you tell the oven what you're cooking (via the touchscreen or app), and it takes control. For example, with a prime rib, the oven will sear at high heat, then drop to the correct roasting temperature, and finally hold the meat at a perfect serving temperature until you're ready to carve. My prime rib came out perfectly medium-rare edge to edge—something I've never achieved before.

The app experience is more utilitarian than the June's, but it's reliable and straightforward. You can remotely preheat, monitor the internal temperature via the probe, and receive notifications when your food is done. The oven also supports voice control through Alexa and Google Home.

The biggest drawback is the price—at $2,499, it's the most expensive model here. But if you're replacing a traditional wall oven and want smart features that don't feel like tech for tech's sake, this is a worthwhile investment.

**Verdict:** A premium appliance for serious home cooks who want precision and smart convenience without the learning curve.

### AI Food Recognition: Gimmick or Game-Changer?

The headline feature on most smart ovens is AI food recognition. The June's system is genuinely impressive—it can identify a whole chicken, a tray of cookies, or a salmon fillet and automatically set the right cooking parameters. Samsung's version is nearly as good but less comprehensive.

However, I'd argue that the more important AI feature is **adaptive cooking**—the oven's ability to adjust time and temperature mid-cycle based on what it detects. Both the June and Samsung do this well, and it's this feature that makes the difference between a smart oven that's a convenience and one that's a genuine cooking upgrade.

### Camera: The Window Into Your Cooking

The built-in camera is more than a novelty. Being able to check on your food without opening the oven door preserves heat and prevents uneven cooking. The June's 30fps HD camera is the best I've tested—you can literally watch your bread rise in real-time. Samsung's camera is lower resolution but still useful for checking browning or bubbling.

### Steam Assist: The Secret Weapon

Steam injection is the feature that separates good smart ovens from great ones. It's not just for bread—steam helps vegetables retain nutrients, keeps meats moist, and can even revive stale bread. The Samsung's Steam Cook+ is particularly well-executed, but the June's steam function is also excellent.

### Connectivity and Smart Home Integration

All three ovens support voice control and mobile apps, but the quality varies. June's app is the most polished, with the live camera feed and adaptive cooking controls. Samsung's app is more basic but integrates better with other Samsung appliances. GE's app is functional but feels dated compared to the competition.

If you're building a smart home ecosystem, consider which platform you're invested in. Samsung works best with SmartThings, while June and GE are more platform-agnostic.

If you're passionate about cooking and want smart features that enhance rather than replace traditional techniques, the **GE Profile with Chef Mode** is worth the investment. The guided cooking system is like having a sous chef, and the double oven configuration is ideal for entertaining. It's expensive, but it's built to last 15+ years.

### Budget Considerations
Smart oven prices range from around $400 for basic models to over $3,000 for premium wall ovens. Here's what you can expect at different price points:

- **Under $800:** Basic smart ovens with app connectivity and remote preheat, but limited AI features. The **Breville Smart Oven Air** is a solid choice in this range.

Remember that wall ovens require professional installation, which can add $200–$500 to the total cost. Countertop models are plug-and-play.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Requirements

**Countertop Models (June Pro):** These are the easiest to install. Simply plug into a standard 120V outlet and they're ready to go. No special electrical work is required, making them ideal for renters or anyone who doesn't want to deal with installation hassles.

**Wall Ovens (Samsung, GE):** These require professional installation. You'll need a dedicated 240V circuit, and the oven needs to fit your existing cutout dimensions. Most manufacturers offer trim kits to accommodate slightly larger or smaller openings. Budget $200–$500 for installation depending on your location and the complexity of the job.

### Smart Home Ecosystem Compatibility

All three ovens work with Alexa and Google Home, but there are differences in how deeply they integrate:

- **June Pro:** Works with Alexa and Google Home for basic controls (preheat, temperature checks). The app is the primary interface, and it's excellent. No native support for Apple HomeKit, but you can use a third-party bridge like HomeBridge.

- **Samsung:** Deep integration with SmartThings, which is great if you have other Samsung appliances. You can create routines that involve the oven—for example, turning on the kitchen lights when the oven finishes preheating. Also works with Alexa and Google Home.

- **GE Profile:** Works with Alexa and Google Home, and also supports IFTTT for custom automations. The integration is solid but not as deep as Samsung's.

### Setting Up Your Smart Oven

Regardless of which model you choose, the setup process follows a similar pattern:

1. **Physical Installation:** For countertop models, just find a spot with good clearance. For wall ovens, schedule professional installation.

2. **Wi-Fi Connection:** Connect the oven to your home Wi-Fi network (2.4GHz is typically required). The app will guide you through this process.

3. **App Setup:** Download the manufacturer's app and create an account. You'll need to register your oven and connect it to your account.

4. **Voice Assistant Linking:** If you want to use voice control, link your oven to your Alexa or Google Home app. This usually takes just a few minutes.

5. **Software Updates:** Smart ovens receive regular firmware updates that add features and improve performance. Make sure you install these when they become available.

6. **Learning the Interface:** Each oven has a different interface—June uses a touchscreen, Samsung uses a combination of knobs and touch, and GE uses a traditional control panel with a screen. Spend time exploring the menus and settings before your first cook.
