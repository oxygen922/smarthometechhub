- **Busy Families:** The June Smart Oven Pro is ideal for parents who need quick, reliable meals. Its one-button operation and remote monitoring save time and reduce stress.

### Budget Considerations

- **Entry-Level (Under $500):** Focus on smart plugs, smart bulbs, and a basic smart kettle. These won’t transform your kitchen but offer a taste of automation.

**Pro Tip:** Look for sales during Black Friday, Presidents’ Day, and Memorial Day. In 2026, many retailers offer 20-30% off smart appliances during these events. Also check for utility rebates—energy-efficient models often qualify for $50–$200 rebates.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Requirements

- **Samsung Bespoke AI Fridge:** Requires a dedicated 115V outlet and a 36-inch wide, 70-inch tall space. Water line connection is optional but recommended for the ice maker and beverage center. Professional installation is highly recommended due to weight (over 400 lbs).
- **June Smart Oven Pro:** Simply place on a countertop near an outlet. It requires a standard 120V, 15-amp circuit. No plumbing needed. The oven weighs 28 lbs and comes with a non-slip mat.
- **Bosch 800 Series Dishwasher:** Requires a standard 24-inch under-counter space, a hot water line, and a drain connection. Electrical connection is hardwired or plug-in (depending on model). Professional installation is strongly advised to ensure proper leveling and drainage.

### Smart Home Integration

All three products support the **Matter** protocol, which means they can be controlled via a single app (like Apple Home, Google Home, or SmartThings). Here’s how to set them up:

1. **Download the manufacturer’s app** (SmartThings, June, or Home Connect).
2. **Follow the in-app pairing instructions**—usually scanning a QR code on the appliance.
3. **Enable Matter bridge** in the app settings (if not automatic).
4. **Add the appliance to your preferred smart home platform** (e.g., Apple Home, Google Home).
5. **Create automations** (e.g., “When I leave home, turn off the oven” or “When the dishwasher finishes, send a notification”).

**Troubleshooting Tip:** If an appliance disconnects frequently, check your Wi-Fi router placement. Smart appliances perform best with a strong 5 GHz signal. Consider a mesh Wi-Fi system (like Eero or Nest Wi-Fi) for larger homes.
