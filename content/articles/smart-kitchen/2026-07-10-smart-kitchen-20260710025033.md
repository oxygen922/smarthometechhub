## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Considerations

- **Refrigerator:** The Samsung Bespoke AI Family Hub requires standard 36-inch width and 70-inch height clearance. Ensure you have a dedicated water line for the beverage center. Professional installation is recommended ($150–$300).

- **June Oven:** This is a countertop appliance—no installation needed. Just plug it into a standard 120V outlet. Ensure you have at least 6 inches of clearance on all sides for ventilation.

- **Bosch Dishwasher:** Standard 24-inch under-counter installation. Bosch recommends professional installation to ensure proper leveling and water line connection. Expect to pay $100–$200 for installation.

### Smart Home Integration

All three appliances support the **Matter** protocol, which is the new universal standard for smart home connectivity. This means they work with:

- **Apple HomeKit:** Control via the Home app or Siri.
- **Amazon Alexa:** Voice commands for cooking, checking inventory, or starting cycles.
- **Google Home:** Integration with routines and voice control.

**Setup Tips:**
1. Start by updating the appliance firmware after installation.
2. Connect to your 2.4GHz Wi-Fi network first (some older routers struggle with 5GHz for IoT devices).
3. Create routines—for example, “Good Morning” could have your June Oven preheat for breakfast while your Bosch dishwasher runs a quick cycle.
4. Use the companion apps to set notifications for filter changes, cycle completions, or food expiration alerts.

**Potential Pitfalls:**
- Weak Wi-Fi signals can cause connectivity issues. Consider a mesh network for larger homes.
- Matter support is still evolving—some features may require app updates.
- Voice control works best with clear, simple commands.
