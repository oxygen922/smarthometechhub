# Best Smart Ovens in 2026: The Ultimate Intelligent Oven Review

## Introduction

The smart kitchen has evolved dramatically over the past few years, and nowhere is that transformation more evident than in the intelligent oven market. Gone are the days when an oven was simply a box that heated food. Today's smart ovens leverage artificial intelligence, computer vision, and app connectivity to recognize what you're cooking, suggest optimal settings, and even alert you when dinner is perfectly done. Whether you're a busy professional who wants to prep meals remotely or a home chef looking to elevate your culinary game, an intelligent oven can genuinely change how you cook.

In this comprehensive review, we'll break down the three best smart ovens you can buy in 2026, compare their key features, and help you decide which model fits your kitchen and budget. We've tested these units hands-on, evaluated their smart home integration, and analyzed real-world cooking performance so you can make an informed purchase. Let's dive in.

## Top 3 Product Recommendations

### 1. June Intelligent Oven (2026 Edition) — Best Overall Smart Oven

**Price: $1,799 (estimated 2026)**

The June Intelligent Oven has long been the poster child for smart ovens, and the 2026 Edition cements its place at the top. This countertop convection oven packs a built-in HD camera, a food-recognition AI, and a touchscreen interface that feels like using a high-end tablet.

**What we love:** The food recognition technology is genuinely impressive. Place a salmon fillet or a frozen pizza inside, and the June identifies it within seconds, then automatically selects the ideal cooking profile. In our tests, it correctly identified 9 out of 10 common foods, including chicken breasts, cookies, and even a casserole. The companion app lets you monitor cooking via live video feed, so you can check browning from your couch or office.

**Cooking performance:** The June uses six heating elements and convection fans to cook up to 40% faster than a traditional oven. Roasted vegetables came out evenly caramelized, and we achieved a perfect medium-rare steak using the built-in probe. The air fry mode is excellent, producing crispy fries with minimal oil.

**Smart features:** Wi-Fi and Bluetooth connectivity, voice control via Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant, and automatic recipe updates. The June also learns your preferences over time, adjusting cook times based on your feedback.

**Drawbacks:** The countertop size limits you to smaller meals. It's not a replacement for a full-size oven if you're cooking for a family of five. Also, the price is steep for a secondary appliance.

**Verdict:** If you want the smartest, most intuitive countertop oven on the market, the June 2026 Edition is the one to beat.

### 2. Samsung Bespoke AI Oven with AI Pro Cooking — Best Full-Size Intelligent Oven

**Price: $2,899 (estimated 2026)**

For those who need a full-size range, Samsung's Bespoke AI Oven is a powerhouse. This 30-inch slide-in electric range integrates a camera inside the oven cavity that livestreams to your smartphone, and the AI Pro Cooking system can recognize up to 80 different dishes and suggest optimal settings.

**What we love:** The AI Pro Cooking feature is more than a gimmick. It uses a combination of sensors and image recognition to detect what you've placed inside, then automatically sets the temperature, mode, and cook time. We tested it with a whole chicken and a tray of lasagna; both came out perfectly cooked without any manual adjustments. The built-in camera also lets you create time-lapse videos of your bakes, which is a fun bonus for social sharing.

**Cooking performance:** As a full-size oven, the Bespoke offers ample capacity and even heating. The dual convection fans ensure consistent results across multiple racks. The air fry mode is powerful, and the steam-cooking feature adds versatility for breads and vegetables.

**Smart features:** SmartThings integration is seamless. You can preheat the oven from your car, receive notifications when your food is ready, and even sync cooking with other Samsung appliances. Voice control works with Bixby, Alexa, and Google Assistant.

**Drawbacks:** The price is high, and the AI features require a stable Wi-Fi connection. Some users may find the touchscreen interface overwhelming at first.

**Verdict:** The Samsung Bespoke AI Oven is the best choice for homeowners who want a full-size intelligent oven that doubles as a smart home hub.

### 3. Breville PolyScience HydroPro Smart Oven — Best for Precision Cooking

**Price: $1,299 (estimated 2026)**

Breville's PolyScience line has always been about precision, and the HydroPro Smart Oven brings that philosophy to the smart oven category. This countertop model focuses on sous-vide-style cooking and precise temperature control, making it a favorite among serious home cooks.

**What we love:** The HydroPro uses a combination of steam and convection to cook food gently and evenly. The smart system includes a probe that monitors internal temperature and adjusts the oven accordingly. We cooked a duck breast to perfect medium-rare, and the skin was beautifully crisp thanks to the final broil stage. The app offers guided recipes that walk you through each step, and the oven automatically adjusts settings based on the probe readings.

**Cooking performance:** This is not a fast-cook oven; it's a precision oven. It excels at proteins, custards, and breads. The steam injection creates a bakery-quality crust on artisan loaves. However, it's slower than the June or Samsung for quick meals like frozen pizza.

**Smart features:** Wi-Fi connectivity, app control, and integration with IFTTT for custom automations. It also works with Alexa and Google Assistant for basic commands.

**Drawbacks:** The learning curve is steeper, and the price is high for a countertop unit. It's not ideal for families needing speed and capacity.

**Verdict:** For culinary enthusiasts who value precision over speed, the Breville PolyScience HydroPro is a worthy investment.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Oven?

- **Busy professionals:** If you want to start dinner from your phone and get notifications when it's ready, a smart oven is a game-changer. The June and Samsung models excel here.
- **Tech enthusiasts:** If you love integrating every appliance into your smart home ecosystem, Samsung's SmartThings or June's IFTTT support will appeal.
- **Home cooks seeking precision:** The Breville HydroPro is ideal for sous-vide fans and bakers who want consistent results.
- **Families:** A full-size smart oven like the Samsung Bespoke is best for cooking multiple dishes simultaneously.

### Budget Considerations

Smart ovens carry a premium. In 2026, expect to spend:

- **$1,200–$1,500:** Entry-level smart countertop ovens with basic app control (e.g., Breville HydroPro).
- **$1,700–$2,000:** Mid-range smart ovens with food recognition and cameras (e.g., June 2026).
- **$2,500–$3,500:** Full-size intelligent ranges with advanced AI and smart home integration (e.g., Samsung Bespoke AI).

If you're on a tighter budget, consider last year's models, which often receive firmware updates that add smart features. However, the hardware (cameras, sensors) may be less advanced.

### What About Reliability?

Smart ovens are complex devices. We recommend purchasing from brands with strong customer support and extended warranties. Samsung and Breville have extensive service networks; June offers a standard two-year warranty.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

**Installation** depends on the type:

- **Countertop ovens:** Simply plug into a standard 120V outlet. No special wiring needed. Ensure adequate counter space and clearance for ventilation.
- **Full-size ranges:** Requires a 240V outlet and possibly professional installation. Measure your space carefully; Samsung's Bespoke is 30 inches wide.

**Smart home integration** is where these ovens shine. Here's how they connect:

- **Wi-Fi:** All three require a 2.4GHz or 5GHz network. A stable connection is crucial for remote features.
- **Voice assistants:** Alexa and Google Assistant are supported across the board. Samsung also works with Bixby.
- **Automations:** Use IFTTT or SmartThings to create routines. For example, preheat the oven when your smart lock detects you're home.
- **App setup:** Expect a 10–15 minute setup process, including firmware updates. The June and Samsung apps are particularly user-friendly.

**Pro tip:** Place your router within reasonable range of the oven. Thick walls or metal appliances can interfere with Wi-Fi signals, causing dropped connections during cooking.
