## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Requirements

- **ChefMate Pro AI Oven:** Requires a 30-inch wide cutout with a dedicated 240V outlet. Professional installation is recommended due to the oven's weight (120 lbs) and electrical needs.
- **SmartFresh 5.0 Refrigerator:** Needs a 36-inch wide space with a standard 115V outlet. Ensure proper ventilation—leave at least 1 inch on each side and 2 inches at the back.
- **SonicWave Pro Dishwasher:** Fits a standard 24-inch cutout. Requires a hot water line (120°F maximum) and a drain connection. Most homeowners can install it themselves with basic plumbing skills.

### Smart Home Integration

All three appliances support **Matter 2.0**, the universal smart home standard, ensuring compatibility with major platforms. Here's how they integrate:

- **Amazon Alexa:** Create routines like "Good Morning" to preheat the oven while brewing coffee.
- **Google Assistant:** Use voice commands to check fridge inventory or start the dishwasher.
- **Apple HomeKit:** Automate the dishwasher to run during off-peak energy hours using geofencing.
- **IFTTT (If This Then That):** Set custom automations, such as sending a push notification when the oven's self-cleaning cycle finishes.

**Integration Example:** With the SmartFresh 5.0 and ChefMate Pro connected, you can say, "Alexa, ask ChefMate to preheat to 375°F for the chicken I just took out of the fridge." The oven automatically sets the temperature and suggests a recipe based on the chicken's weight.
