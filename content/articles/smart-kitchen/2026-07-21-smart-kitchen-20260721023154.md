The only downside is the price—it's an investment, but for those who value convenience and precision, it's worth every penny.

**Pros:** Exceptional food recognition, versatile cooking modes, excellent app integration  
**Cons:** High price point, limited capacity for large families

### 2. Breville Smart Oven Air Fryer Pro – The Versatile Workhorse

**Price:** $399 (estimated 2026)

Breville's Smart Oven Air Fryer Pro is a countertop marvel that balances affordability with advanced features. This oven uses Element IQ technology, which adjusts the power of six heating elements to ensure even cooking. It comes with 13 preset cooking functions, including air fry, roast, bake, broil, and slow cook. The standout feature is the integrated temperature probe, which connects to the Breville app via Bluetooth, allowing you to monitor internal meat temperatures in real-time. The oven also features a "Super Convection" mode that uses a high-speed fan to circulate hot air, reducing cooking times by up to 30%. In my tests, it produced crispy air-fried french fries in 15 minutes and a perfectly roasted chicken in under an hour. The app provides step-by-step guided recipes, and the oven is compatible with Alexa for voice control. The build quality is excellent, with a stainless steel exterior and a non-stick interior. The 0.8 cubic foot capacity is suitable for families of four, and the oven fits easily under standard cabinets. For the price, this is the best value smart oven on the market.

**Pros:** Affordable, excellent cooking performance, temperature probe included  
**Cons:** Bluetooth range limited, no camera for food recognition

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Oven?

- **Busy Professionals:** If you're short on time but want healthy, home-cooked meals, a smart oven with food recognition and app control can automate the process. The June 2026 Edition is ideal for this group.
- **Culinary Enthusiasts:** If you love experimenting with recipes, the Samsung AI Pro's steam cooking and learning features will elevate your skills. It's a splurge but worth it for serious cooks.
- **Families:** The Breville Smart Oven Air Fryer Pro offers enough capacity for family meals without breaking the bank. Its ease of use makes it a great entry-level smart oven.
- **Smart Home Integrators:** If you already use SmartThings, Alexa, or Google Home, choose an oven that integrates seamlessly. Samsung's oven is the best choice for SmartThings users.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $500:** The Breville Smart Oven Air Fryer Pro is the best in this range, offering excellent value with essential smart features.
- **$1,000–$2,000:** The June 2026 Edition is the sweet spot for countertop ovens with advanced AI and camera features.
- **Over $3,000:** The Samsung AI Pro is for those who want a premium built-in oven with top-tier technology and large capacity.

Consider installation costs: countertop ovens require no installation, but built-in models like Samsung's may need professional wiring and cabinet modifications.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation

- **Countertop Ovens:** Simply place on a heat-resistant surface with adequate ventilation. Ensure at least 4 inches of clearance on all sides. No electrical work needed—plug into a standard 120V outlet.
- **Built-In Ovens:** Professional installation is recommended. You'll need a dedicated 240V circuit for electric models, and the oven must be secured per manufacturer guidelines. Expect installation costs of $200–$500.

### Smart Home Integration

All three ovens integrate with major voice assistants, but here's how they fit into broader ecosystems:

- **Amazon Alexa:** Use voice commands to preheat, set timers, or check cooking status. For example, "Alexa, ask June to preheat to 350°F."
- **Google Assistant:** Similar functionality, with routines that can trigger the oven based on time of day or other smart devices.
- **Apple HomeKit:** Currently, only the Breville offers HomeKit support via the Breville app, but it's limited.
- **IFTTT (If This Then That):** The June and Samsung ovens support IFTTT, allowing you to create custom automations like "If I leave work, preheat the oven."

For seamless integration, ensure your Wi-Fi network is robust. A mesh Wi-Fi system can prevent connectivity drops, especially with multiple smart devices.
