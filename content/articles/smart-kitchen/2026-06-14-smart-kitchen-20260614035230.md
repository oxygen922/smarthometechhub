Whether you're a passionate home chef, a busy parent, or someone who simply wants to eliminate the guesswork from cooking, a smart oven can transform your culinary experience. From voice-controlled convection baking to app-guided roasting with real-time protein monitoring, the future of cooking is here—and it's smarter than ever.

### 2. Brava Glass Smart Oven

The Brava Glass Smart Oven takes a fundamentally different approach to smart cooking. The Brava system uses "thermal targeting" to direct light energy to specific zones within the cooking chamber. This means you can cook a steak and vegetables simultaneously, with each receiving different amounts of heat. The oven's six built-in sensors monitor temperature, humidity, and light intensity, making real-time adjustments to ensure perfect results. The oven comes with a set of specialized cookware—including a metal pan, a glass baking dish, and a ceramic pot—each designed to optimize light absorption and heat distribution.

**Performance:** The Brava Glass is incredibly fast. It can cook a 12-ounce steak to medium-rare in just 6 minutes, and a whole chicken in under 20 minutes. The precision is remarkable: we tested salmon fillets that were perfectly cooked through without any dry edges. The oven's "auto-cook" function uses a barcode scanner to identify pre-packaged foods and automatically set the optimal cooking parameters. For fresh ingredients, the app provides step-by-step guidance with visual cues.

**Smart Features:** The Brava app is one of the most intuitive in the smart oven category. It includes over 1,000 pre-programmed recipes, all developed by professional chefs. The oven connects via Wi-Fi and Bluetooth, and supports voice control through Alexa and Google Assistant. A unique feature is "CookAlongs"—live guided cooking sessions where a chef walks you through recipes in real-time.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Considerations

**Countertop Models (June and Brava):** Installation is straightforward—simply place the oven on a stable, heat-resistant surface near a standard 120V outlet. Both models require about 6 inches of clearance above and behind for proper ventilation. No professional installation is needed, though you may want to consider a dedicated counter space to avoid frequent moving.

**Built-in Model (GE Profile):** Professional installation is strongly recommended. The oven requires a dedicated 240V circuit and proper cabinet cutout dimensions. Installation costs typically range from $200-$500, depending on your kitchen's existing electrical setup and cabinetry. The GE Profile also requires a stable Wi-Fi connection for full smart functionality.

### Smart Home Integration

All three ovens support the major smart home platforms:

- **Amazon Alexa:** You can preheat the oven, set timers, and check cooking status using voice commands. For example, "Alexa, ask June to preheat to 375 degrees."
- **Google Assistant:** Similar voice control capabilities are available, with commands like "Hey Google, set the Brava to air fry mode."
- **IFTTT (June only):** Advanced users can create custom automations, such as having your oven preheat when your smart thermostat detects you're on your way home.

**Integration Tips:**
- Ensure your Wi-Fi network is stable and uses the 2.4GHz band for best connectivity (many smart ovens struggle with 5GHz networks).
- Place your oven within range of your Wi-Fi router for reliable app control.
- Consider adding a smart plug for countertop models if you want to cut power remotely (though this is rarely needed).
