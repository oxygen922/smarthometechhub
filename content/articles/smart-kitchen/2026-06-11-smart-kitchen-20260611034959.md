## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Tips

- **Countertop Models:** Simply place on a heat-resistant surface, ensure proper ventilation (at least 3 inches clearance), and plug into a standard 120V outlet. No special wiring needed.
- **Built-in Models:** Hire a licensed electrician to install a dedicated 240V circuit if required. Measure your cabinet cutout carefully—Samsung’s Bespoke ovens come with a trim kit for a custom fit. Allow 1–2 hours for professional installation.
- **Wi-Fi Setup:** Use the manufacturer’s app (June, SmartThings, or Breville) for initial connection. Ensure your router supports 2.4GHz Wi-Fi for stable connectivity. Place the oven within 30 feet of your router for best performance.

### Smart Home Integration Best Practices

- **Voice Commands:** Program routines like “Goodnight, kitchen” to turn off the oven light and confirm it’s off. Use Alexa or Google routines to preheat the oven when you say, “I’m making dinner.”
- **Automation:** With IFTTT or Apple Shortcuts, create triggers like “When my phone leaves work, preheat the oven to 375°F.” This saves time and energy.
- **Ecosystem Synergy:** Pair a Samsung oven with a SmartThings Hub and compatible lights, locks, and sensors for a fully automated kitchen. For example, when you open the oven door, the kitchen lights dim.
- **Troubleshooting:** If connectivity drops, restart your router and oven, or check for firmware updates. Most brands offer 24/7 support.
