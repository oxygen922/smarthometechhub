## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy an Intelligent Oven?

- **Busy Professionals:** If you’re short on time, the AI cooking modes and remote preheating can save you 20–30 minutes per meal.
- **Tech Enthusiasts:** If you love integrating gadgets, these ovens pair with smart speakers, lights, and even refrigerators.
- **Home Bakers:** The precision temperature control and steam assist are ideal for bread, pastries, and pizza.
- **Health-Conscious Cooks:** Air frying and steam cooking reduce oil usage while retaining flavor.

### Budget Considerations

- **Entry-Level ($800–$1,200):** Look for models like the Breville Smart Oven Air Fryer Pro (not reviewed here) or the June (if on sale). These offer good smart features but smaller capacities.
- **Mid-Range ($1,200–$2,000):** The June and Wolf Gourmet fall here. You get excellent AI, build quality, and performance.
- **Premium ($2,000+):** The Samsung Smart Oven and luxury brands like Miele or Thermador offer built-in designs and larger capacities but require professional installation.

### Installation Tips

- **Countertop Models:** Most plug into standard 120V outlets. Ensure your counter can support the weight (30–50 lbs).
- **Built-In Models:** Require a 240V outlet and professional installation. Measure your cabinet space carefully—standard sizes are 27” or 30” wide.
- **Ventilation:** Some ovens generate significant heat. Keep clearance around the unit as per manufacturer specs.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Setting Up Your Intelligent Oven

1. **Unbox and Place:** For countertop models, find a stable, heat-resistant surface near an outlet. For built-ins, hire a licensed electrician.
2. **Connect to Wi-Fi:** Download the manufacturer’s app, create an account, and follow the pairing instructions. Most use Bluetooth for initial setup.
3. **Update Firmware:** Smart ovens receive regular updates that improve AI recognition and add features. Check for updates immediately.
4. **Calibrate Sensors:** Some models require a one-time calibration (e.g., running a preheat cycle). Follow the app’s prompts.

### Smart Home Ecosystem Compatibility

- **Amazon Alexa & Google Assistant:** All three reviewed models support voice commands like “Alexa, preheat the oven to 350°F” or “Hey Google, set a timer for 30 minutes.”
- **Apple HomeKit:** Only the June oven supports HomeKit in 2026. Samsung and Wolf are compatible via third-party integrations.
- **IFTTT (If This Then That):** Create automations like “If I leave work, preheat the oven” using your phone’s location.
- **Smart Displays:** View oven camera feeds on Echo Show or Nest Hub devices—handy for monitoring food without opening the door.

### Troubleshooting Common Issues

- **Wi-Fi Drops:** Ensure your router is within 30 feet. Use a mesh network for larger homes.
- **App Crashes:** Restart the oven and app. Clear cache if needed.
- **Inaccurate Cooking:** Recalibrate sensors via the app. Some ovens allow manual temperature adjustments.
