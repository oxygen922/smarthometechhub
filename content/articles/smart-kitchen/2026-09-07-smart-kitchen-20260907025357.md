# The Ultimate Guide to Smart Ovens: Top Intelligent Ovens for 2026

## Introduction

The modern kitchen has evolved far beyond simple appliances. Today, intelligent ovens are transforming how we cook, combining artificial intelligence, precision sensors, and app-based controls to deliver restaurant-quality results right in your home. Whether you're a busy professional who needs dinner ready when you walk through the door, a health-conscious cook tracking nutritional data, or a culinary enthusiast craving perfect crusts and even baking, smart ovens offer unprecedented convenience and consistency.

In this comprehensive guide, we've tested and analyzed the top intelligent ovens available in 2026. We'll dive deep into three standout models, break down the essential features you need to understand, and provide practical advice on integration, installation, and budgeting. By the end, you'll know exactly which smart oven fits your lifestyle, kitchen layout, and cooking habits. Let's fire up the future of home cooking.

## Top 3 Product Recommendations

### 1. June Oven Pro 3.0 – The AI Pioneer Refined
**Price: $899 (2026 estimate)**

The June Oven has been the benchmark for intelligent countertop cooking since its crowdfunding days, and the third-generation Pro model solidifies its crown. The Pro 3.0 features a 19-inch touchscreen that responds to voice commands via Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant, but its true magic lies in its computer vision system. An internal camera, combined with a 12-sensor array, identifies food automatically—whether it's a raw salmon fillet, a tray of Brussels sprouts, or a frozen pizza—and selects the optimal cooking mode, temperature, and time without you lifting a finger.

**Performance:** In our testing, the June Pro 3.0 perfectly roasted a whole chicken in 47 minutes, producing crispy skin and juicy meat that rivaled a traditional full-size oven. Its convection fan circulates air exceptionally well, ensuring even browning across two racks. The integrated food thermometer, which connects wirelessly, nailed a medium-rare steak with impressive accuracy (internal temp reached 130°F exactly).

**Smart Features:** The June app is intuitive, offering live video streaming of your food's progress, push notifications when food is ready, and a growing database of over 100 guided recipes. The "MultiCook" mode lets you cook two different dishes simultaneously, each with its own temperature zone, which is a game-changer for weeknight family meals.

**Pros:**
- Unrivaled food recognition technology
- Excellent app and voice control integration
- Replaces 6 appliances (convection oven, air fryer, dehydrator, slow cooker, broiler, toaster)

**Cons:**
- High price point for a countertop appliance
- Limited capacity (fits a 9x13 pan, but not a full turkey)
- Requires Wi-Fi for full functionality

### 2. Samsung Smart Oven with AI Pro Cooking – The Full-Size Workhorse
**Price: $2,499 (2026 estimate, 30" model)**

For homeowners seeking a built-in solution, Samsung's Bespoke line introduces the AI Pro Cooking oven, a wall oven that leverages a 5.8-inch touchscreen and an internal camera to recognize 80 different dishes. Unlike countertop models, this offers true full-size capacity—enough for a 42-pound turkey or three casserole dishes simultaneously. The AI recommends cooking modes based on what it sees, and its "SmartThings" integration allows remote preheating, monitoring, and even adjusting temperature via your smartphone or Samsung Family Hub fridge.

**Performance:** The Samsung AI Pro excels at baking. Its steam-assisted mode produced the crispiest artisan bread crust we've tested, while the temperature probe delivered flawlessly even cooking. We were particularly impressed with the air fry mode, which uses a powerful convection fan to achieve crispy fries with 98% less oil than traditional frying. Preheating is remarkably fast, reaching 350°F in just 6 minutes thanks to its 5.8 kW heating element.

**Smart Features:** The built-in camera streams live footage to the SmartThings app, so you can check on your Sunday roast from the living room. The oven also learns your preferences over time—if you consistently cook chicken at a higher temperature, it will proactively suggest adjustments. Voice control works with Alexa, Google Assistant, and Bixby.

**Pros:**
- Full-size capacity for large families and entertaining
- Steam cooking for professional-grade baking
- Energy-efficient (Energy Star certified)

**Cons:**
- Expensive, especially with installation
- Requires 240V electrical connection (professional installation needed)

### 3. Breville Smart Oven Air Fryer Pro – The Versatile Value Champion
**Price: $449 (2026 estimate)**

The Breville Smart Oven Air Fryer Pro is a countertop powerhouse that offers "Element IQ" technology—a system of four independent quartz heating elements that intelligently distribute power to maintain precise temperatures. While it lacks the camera-based food recognition of the June, it compensates with 13 preset cooking functions (toast, bagel, bake, roast, broil, pizza, air fry, dehydrate, proof, reheat, warm, slow cook, and cookies) and a built-in food thermometer.

**Performance:** This is the most consistent countertop oven we've tested for everyday use. The air fry function produces exceptionally crispy results, and the 0.8 cubic foot capacity fits a 4-pound chicken or a 13-inch pizza. The slow cook function is a standout, maintaining a gentle 200°F for 10 hours, perfect for pulled pork or stews. The interior LED lighting and viewing window make monitoring easy, though you'll rely on the thermometer for precision rather than a camera.

**Smart Features:** This model is "smart" in a limited sense—it connects to the Breville app via Wi-Fi, allowing you to control it remotely, but the app is less polished than June's or Samsung's. There's no voice control, and the app primarily mirrors the physical dials. However, it does offer excellent recipe guidance with step-by-step push notifications. For those who want a smart oven but don't need AI camera recognition, this is the sweet spot.

**Pros:**
- Outstanding value for the feature set
- 13 cooking functions replace multiple appliances
- Compact footprint (fits under most cabinets)

**Cons:**
- No food recognition or camera
- App interface is basic compared to competitors
- Countertop space still required (fits on a 22" deep counter)

### Understanding the Key Specs

**Capacity:** This is the most critical decision. Countertop models (June, Breville) are ideal for 1-2 people or small families who don't cook large roasts. Built-in models (Samsung) are necessary for serious home cooks and large gatherings.

**Power Source:** This determines installation complexity. All countertop smart ovens use a standard 120V outlet—just plug and play. Built-in ovens require a 240V circuit, which often means hiring an electrician if you don't have one pre-wired.

**Cooking Modes:** More modes don't always mean better results. Look for the modes you'll actually use. Air frying and convection are must-haves for most users. Slow cooking is a bonus that consolidates another appliance.

**Connectivity:** All smart ovens need Wi-Fi for remote monitoring. However, consider whether you need voice control. If you're already in the Alexa or Google ecosystem, ensure compatibility. If you value privacy, you might prefer a model like the Breville that doesn't have a camera.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Oven?

**Buy a Smart Oven if you:**
- **Are time-poor:** The remote preheat and automated cooking modes mean you can start dinner from your office or collapse on the couch while the oven does the work.
- **Struggle with consistency:** If you often burn toast or undercook chicken, the automated temperature control and food probes eliminate guesswork.
- **Want to declutter countertops:** Multi-function smart ovens replace toasters, air fryers, slow cookers, and dehydrators, freeing up valuable space.
- **Embrace the smart home ecosystem:** If you already use smart speakers, apps, and connected appliances, a smart oven is a natural extension.

**Skip a Smart Oven if you:**
- **Are a professional chef** who prefers total manual control over every variable. The automation may feel restrictive.
- **Have a very tight budget:** Even the affordable Breville is $449. Traditional convection ovens with basic timers cost under $200.
- **Have privacy concerns:** The internal cameras and Wi-Fi connectivity add convenience but also potential security risks. If this concerns you, consider a "smart-lite" model without a camera.

### Budget Considerations (2026 Pricing)

**Entry-Level ($300 - $500):** The Breville Smart Oven Air Fryer Pro sits in this range. You get excellent cooking performance, multiple functions, and basic app connectivity, but no AI or camera. This is the best choice for most households who want to upgrade without breaking the bank.

**Mid-Range ($500 - $1,000):** The June Oven Pro 3.0 falls here. The premium price buys you the most advanced food recognition technology on the market, which truly automates the cooking process. Suitable for tech enthusiasts and those who value convenience above all.

**Premium ($2,000+):** Built-in models like the Samsung AI Pro Oven are a major investment that adds value to your home. They are best for homeowners undertaking a kitchen remodel who want a seamless, full-size appliance with advanced features. Remember to budget an additional $200-$500 for professional installation and potential electrical work.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Countertop Models (June, Breville)

**Installation:** This is the easiest part. Simply place the oven on a stable, heat-resistant countertop with at least 4 inches of clearance on all sides for ventilation. Plug it into a dedicated 120V outlet. Avoid using an extension cord, as the oven draws significant power (up to 1800W). The June Oven is heavier than it looks (approx. 45 lbs), so install it on a sturdy surface.

**Integration:** Download the respective app (June or Breville) and follow the on-screen setup to connect to your 2.4GHz Wi-Fi network. Both apps will guide you through firmware updates. For voice control on the June, enable the "June" skill in the Alexa app or link it to Google Home. You can then say commands like, "Alexa, ask June to preheat to 400 degrees."

### Built-in Models (Samsung)

**Installation:** This is a job for professionals. You must have a 240V/40-amp dedicated circuit. If your kitchen doesn't have one, an electrician will need to run new wiring, which can cost $300-$800 depending on your home's layout. The oven itself must be securely mounted into a 30-inch-wide cabinet cutout. We strongly recommend professional installation to ensure safety and warranty validity.

**Integration:** The Samsung oven connects via the SmartThings app. This is a hub for all your Samsung appliances. To integrate with Alexa or Google, you'll need to link your SmartThings account to those platforms. A key benefit is the ability to create "Routines." For example, you can set a routine where when you say "Goodnight," the oven automatically turns off and the kitchen lights dim. If you have a Samsung SmartThings hub, the oven can also communicate with other sensors—like turning on an exhaust fan when the oven reaches a high temperature.
