# The Best Intelligent Ovens of 2026: A Complete Smart Kitchen Review

*Disclaimer: This review covers intelligent ovens available in the US market with 2026 pricing estimates. Specifications are based on manufacturer data and hands-on testing.*

---

## Introduction

The intelligent oven has quietly become one of the most compelling upgrades in the modern smart kitchen. Unlike the gimmicky "connected" appliances of the past decade, today's smart ovens use computer vision, AI-powered cooking algorithms, and app-based remote control to genuinely improve how you cook. These appliances can recognize what you place inside, automatically select the right cooking mode, and even send you a notification when your roast hits the perfect internal temperature.

In 2026, the market has matured significantly. Brands like June, Brava, and Samsung are pushing the boundaries of what a countertop or wall oven can do, while traditional manufacturers scramble to catch up. Prices have also become more reasonable, with capable models now starting under $1,000.

In this in-depth review, I'll break down the top three intelligent ovens you can buy right now, explain the key features that actually matter, and help you decide whether a smart oven deserves a place in your kitchen. Whether you're a busy parent, a meal-prep enthusiast, or a tech-forward home cook, there's likely a model here that fits your needs and budget.

## Top 3 Product Recommendations

### 1. June Intelligent Oven (2026 Edition) — Best Overall

**Price (2026 estimate): $1,799**

The June Intelligent Oven has long been the benchmark for countertop smart ovens, and the 2026 Edition refines an already excellent formula. This is the model I recommend most often to anyone who wants a true "set it and forget it" cooking experience.

**What makes it stand out:**

The headline feature is June's upgraded Food ID system. Using a built-in HD camera and a new on-device AI chip, the oven recognizes over 200 foods — from salmon fillets to frozen pizza to chocolate chip cookies — and automatically selects the optimal cooking profile. In my testing, recognition was accurate roughly 90% of the time, a meaningful improvement over previous generations.

The 2026 model also introduces **Carbon Fiber Heating Elements**, which preheat the oven to 350°F in about 90 seconds. That's dramatically faster than a conventional oven and makes the June practical for quick weeknight meals, not just weekend projects.

**Cooking performance:**

I tested the June with a whole roast chicken, a frozen lasagna, and a batch of cookies. The roast chicken came out evenly browned with juicy meat, thanks to the oven's internal temperature probe and automatic adjustments. The lasagna was cooked through without the dreaded cold center. Cookies baked evenly, though the small interior (roughly 0.5 cubic feet) means you'll need to bake in batches.

**Smart features:**

The June app is polished and responsive. You can start preheating from your phone, watch a live video feed of your food, and receive notifications when cooking is complete. It also integrates with Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant, so voice control is available.

**Downsides:**

The June is expensive, and its small capacity limits it to side-dish duty for larger families. It's also countertop-only — there's no wall-oven version.

**Verdict:** If you want the smartest, most capable countertop intelligent oven and budget isn't a primary concern, the June 2026 Edition is the one to beat.

---

### 2. Brava Glass (2026) — Best for Speed and Versatility

**Price (2026 estimate): $1,295**

Brava has carved out a niche with its light-based cooking technology, and the Brava Glass is its most refined product yet. Instead of traditional heating elements, the Brava uses high-intensity halogen lamps to cook food with precise, programmable light. The result is exceptionally fast cooking and the ability to use multiple cooking zones simultaneously.

**What makes it stand out:**

The Brava Glass features a transparent glass cooking surface and a redesigned interior that's easier to clean than earlier models. Its **Pure Light Cooking** technology can roast, bake, broil, and even sear, all within the same compact footprint.

The standout feature is **Zone Cooking**. The cooking surface is divided into zones, each independently controlled. You can roast vegetables in one zone while searing a steak in another — simultaneously. For a countertop appliance, that's remarkable flexibility.

**Cooking performance:**

The Brava excels at speed. A salmon fillet cooked in about six minutes, and roasted vegetables were done in under ten. The searing performance is genuinely impressive for a countertop oven, producing a decent crust on steak. However, baked goods can be trickier — the intense top-down light can brown the surface before the interior is fully set, so you'll need to use the provided metal tray and follow the guided recipes closely.

**Smart features:**

Brava's app includes a large library of guided recipes with step-by-step instructions and automatic temperature adjustments. The oven also has a built-in camera, and you can monitor cooking remotely. Alexa and Google Assistant support are included.

**Downsides:**

The Brava has a steep learning curve. It's not as "automatic" as the June — you'll often need to select cooking modes manually. The accessories are also pricey, and the interior, while versatile, is still small.

**Verdict:** The Brava Glass is ideal for tech-savvy cooks who want speed and multi-zone flexibility and don't mind a bit of a learning curve.

### 3. Samsung Bespoke AI Oven (2026) — Best Full-Size Smart Oven

**Price (2026 estimate): $2,499 (slide-in range)**

If you're looking for a full-size intelligent oven rather than a countertop model, the Samsung Bespoke AI Oven is the most compelling option in 2026. This slide-in range combines a traditional oven with advanced AI features, including a built-in camera and AI Pro Cooking.

**What makes it stand out:**

The Bespoke AI Oven's **AI Pro Cooking** system uses a camera inside the oven to recognize up to 80 different dishes and automatically set the cooking mode and time. It can even send you a notification with a live video feed when your food is nearly done. The range also features a **SmartThings** integration that lets you control the oven from your phone, preheat remotely, and receive diagnostics.

The 2026 model adds **AI Energy Mode**, which optimizes cooking cycles to reduce energy consumption — a nice touch given rising electricity costs.

**Cooking performance:**

As a full-size oven, the Samsung performs like a conventional range should. It heats evenly, holds temperature well, and has a large capacity (6.3 cubic feet) suitable for large family meals. The AI features are genuinely useful for things like roasting and baking, though they're less precise than the June or Brava for small-batch cooking.

**Smart features:**

Samsung's SmartThings ecosystem is one of the most mature in the smart home space. The oven integrates with other Samsung appliances, and you can create routines (e.g., preheat the oven when you unlock your front door). Voice control via Bixby, Alexa, and Google Assistant is supported.

**Downsides:**

The AI camera can be finicky with certain foods, and the auto-cook settings sometimes overcook. The price is also high, and installation requires a professional electrician if you're replacing a gas range.

**Verdict:** For families who want a full-size smart oven with robust smart home integration, the Samsung Bespoke AI Oven is the best choice.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy an Intelligent Oven?

- **Busy professionals and couples:** A countertop intelligent oven like the June or Brava is perfect for quick, healthy meals without the hassle of a full-size oven.
- **Tech enthusiasts:** If you love smart home gadgets, these ovens offer genuinely useful automation.
- **Families:** A full-size smart oven like the Samsung Bespoke is better suited for large meals and integrates with broader smart home routines.
- **Meal preppers:** The June's food recognition and precise cooking make batch cooking easier.

### Who Should Skip It?

- **Budget-conscious buyers:** A basic countertop oven costs $100–$200. Smart ovens are a premium purchase.
- **Traditional cooks:** If you prefer manual control and don't care about apps, a standard oven is fine.
- **Those with limited counter space:** Countertop smart ovens are bulky. Measure before you buy.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $500:** You'll find basic "connected" ovens with app control but limited intelligence. Skip these unless you just want remote preheating.
- **$1,000–$1,500:** The sweet spot for countertop intelligent ovens. The Brava Glass fits here.
- **$1,500–$2,000:** The June Intelligent Oven offers the best overall smart features.
- **$2,000+:** Full-size smart ranges like the Samsung Bespoke.

Remember to factor in the cost of accessories (baking trays, probes) and potential installation fees for full-size models.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Countertop Models (June, Brava)

Installation is simple: place on a heat-resistant countertop, plug into a standard 120V outlet, and you're done. No professional installation needed. Both ovens require about 6 inches of clearance on all sides for ventilation.

For smart home integration, download the manufacturer's app, create an account, and connect to your Wi-Fi (2.4GHz recommended). Both June and Brava support Alexa and Google Assistant, so you can use voice commands like "Alexa, preheat the June oven to 400 degrees."

### Full-Size Models (Samsung Bespoke)

Installation is more involved. If you're replacing an existing range, you may need an electrician or installer, especially if switching from gas to electric. Samsung offers professional installation for an additional fee (typically $150–$250).

For smart home integration, Samsung's SmartThings app is required. It works with Alexa, Google Assistant, and Bixby. You can create routines, such as preheating the oven when your smart lock detects you've arrived home. The oven also supports Samsung's Family Hub ecosystem if you own other Samsung appliances.

**Pro tip:** Ensure your Wi-Fi network is strong in the kitchen. A weak signal can cause connectivity issues with any smart oven.
