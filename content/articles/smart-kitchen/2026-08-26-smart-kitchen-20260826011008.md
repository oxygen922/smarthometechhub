## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Considerations

**Refrigerators:** Smart refrigerators are generally plug-and-play, but you must ensure your kitchen has the correct water line connection for the ice maker and water dispenser. The Samsung Bespoke model requires a standard 115-volt outlet. Allow at least 1 inch of clearance on all sides for proper ventilation.

**Induction Ranges:** This is the most complex installation. You'll need a dedicated 240-volt circuit with a 40-amp breaker. If you're converting from gas, hiring a licensed electrician is non-negotiable. The GE Profile range is 30 inches wide, so measure your existing cutout carefully. If you have a 36-inch cutout, you'll need a filler kit or choose a different model.

**Countertop Ovens:** The easiest to install—simply place on a stable countertop near a standard outlet. Ensure there's at least 4 inches of clearance above the oven for heat dissipation. Avoid placing it under low-hanging cabinets.

### Smart Home Integration Strategies

**1. Choose Your Ecosystem:**
While most appliances offer multi-assistant support, it's wise to commit to one primary ecosystem (Alexa, Google Home, or Apple HomeKit) for streamlined control. For example, if you're an Apple household, prioritize appliances with HomeKit compatibility like the June Oven.

**2. Create Scenes and Routines:**
Use your smart home hub to create automations. For example:
- "Good Morning" routine: Unlock smart locks, turn on the coffee maker, and set the oven to preheat for breakfast.
- "Cooking Mode" scene: Dim the kitchen lights, play your favorite playlist on smart speakers, and set the oven temperature.

**3. Leverage IFTTT (If This Then That):**
Many smart appliances support IFTTT applets, allowing for cross-brand automations. For instance, you can set up an applet that sends a notification to your smartwatch when your June Oven has finished cooking.

**4. Consider a Smart Display:**
Devices like the Amazon Echo Show 15 or Google Nest Hub Max can serve as a secondary control surface for your appliances. Mount one on your kitchen wall to view camera feeds from your smart refrigerator or to pull up recipes on your June Oven.

**5. Security and Privacy:**
Smart appliances with cameras (like the Samsung fridge) collect data. Ensure your home Wi-Fi network is secured with WPA3 encryption and change default passwords on all devices. Review the manufacturer's privacy policy to understand how your data is used.
