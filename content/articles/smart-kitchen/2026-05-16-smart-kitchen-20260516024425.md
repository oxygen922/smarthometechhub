## Key Features to Consider (with Comparison Table)

When shopping for a smart oven, focus on these critical features:

- **Cooking Modes:** Look for versatility—air fry, bake, broil, roast, dehydrate, sous-vide, and proofing are common. More modes mean more culinary options.
- **AI and Sensors:** Cameras, thermal sensors, and food probes enable automatic cooking adjustments. The best ovens can identify food and recommend settings.
- **Connectivity:** Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and Matter protocol support are essential for remote control and smart home integration. Ensure compatibility with your preferred voice assistant.
- **Capacity:** Countertop ovens typically hold 1-2 cubic feet, while built-in models offer 4-6 cubic feet. Consider your typical meal size and family needs.
- **Ease of Cleaning:** Self-cleaning cycles, ceramic or enamel interiors, and removable crumb trays reduce maintenance.
- **App Ecosystem:** A robust app with guided recipes, meal planning, and firmware updates adds long-term value.
- **Price vs. Value:** Higher price often correlates with more sensors, better build quality, and advanced AI. However, mid-range options may suffice for basic smart cooking.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Tips

- **Countertop Ovens:** Place on a heat-resistant surface with at least 4 inches of clearance on all sides. Ensure the outlet is GFCI-protected and can handle 15-20 amps. Avoid placing near sinks or dishwashers.

- **Built-In Ovens:** Hire a licensed electrician to verify your home's wiring supports the oven's voltage (typically 240V). The oven may require a dedicated circuit. Measure the cutout dimensions carefully—most built-in ovens require a 30-inch wide cabinet opening.

### Smart Home Integration

All three reviewed ovens work with Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant for basic commands like preheating, setting timers, and checking status. For advanced automation:

- **IFTTT:** The Breville and GE ovens support custom applets. For example, "If my smart lock unlocks at 5 PM, preheat the oven to 400°F."

- **Matter Protocol:** The GE Profile Oven supports Matter, allowing it to communicate with Apple HomeKit, Samsung SmartThings, and other Matter-compatible devices. This ensures future-proofing as the standard grows.

- **Routines:** Use the June app to create routines like "Good Morning" that preheats the oven and starts a breakfast recipe. GE's Chef Connect syncs with compatible cooktops to automatically adjust oven settings.

- **Security:** Ensure your Wi-Fi network is secure (WPA3 recommended). Keep oven firmware updated to patch vulnerabilities.
