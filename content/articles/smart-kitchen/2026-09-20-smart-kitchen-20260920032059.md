# The Best Intelligent Ovens of 2026: Smart Kitchen Review

*Last Updated: January 2026*

The intelligent oven has quietly become one of the most practical smart kitchen investments you can make in 2026. Unlike the early "connected" ovens that simply let you preheat from your phone, today's AI-powered models use computer vision, thermal sensors, and machine learning to recognize what you're cooking, adjust temperatures in real time, and genuinely improve your results. Whether you're a busy parent reheating leftovers or an aspiring home chef chasing the perfect roast, these appliances are earning their place in American kitchens. But not every smart oven is worth the premium—some are gimmicky, some are overpriced, and some have software that feels creepily invasive. In this review, we'll break down the top performers, the features that matter, and how to choose the right intelligent oven for your home.

---

## Top 3 Intelligent Oven Recommendations for 2026

### 1. June Intelligent Oven (Gen 4) — Best Overall

**Price: $1,499 (estimated 2026)**

June has been refining its countertop intelligent oven since 2015, and the fourth generation is the most polished yet. This is a countertop convection oven that uses a built-in HD camera and a food-recognition AI to identify over 200 types of food—everything from salmon fillets to frozen pizza—and automatically select the ideal cooking mode, temperature, and time.

What sets the Gen 4 apart is how much smarter the cooking engine has become. The new "Chef Mode" uses a combination of computer vision and weight sensors to track how food is progressing. If your chicken thighs are browning faster than expected, the oven adjusts the top and bottom heating elements independently. In my testing, it produced consistently juicy chicken and evenly melted cheese on a casserole—two things that are surprisingly hard to nail in a countertop oven.

The touchscreen interface is bright and responsive, and the companion app now supports remote monitoring with live video of your food. You can also save custom presets, which is handy if you have a family recipe you cook weekly. The downside? June's AI is only as good as its food database, and some less common dishes—like a specific brand of frozen dumplings—weren't recognized. You can manually select a mode, but then you lose some of the "intelligent" benefit.

**Pros:**
- Excellent food recognition and auto-adjustment
- Compact footprint for countertops
- Live video monitoring via app
- Fast preheat (under 90 seconds)

**Cons:**
- Expensive for a countertop unit
- Limited capacity (fits a 9x13 pan, not a full sheet pan)
- Requires Wi-Fi for full features

**Verdict:** If you want the smartest countertop oven and don't mind the price, the June Gen 4 is the best intelligent oven for most households.

---

### 2. Samsung Bespoke AI Oven with Vision AI — Best Full-Size Intelligent Oven

**Price: $2,899 (estimated 2026)**

Samsung's Bespoke line has brought serious AI to the full-size wall oven category. The 2026 model features an internal camera and "Vision AI" that identifies what you've placed inside—up to 80 different foods—and suggests cooking settings on the 7-inch touchscreen. It also integrates with Samsung's SmartThings ecosystem, so you can preheat from your phone, get notifications when dinner is ready, and even sync cooking with other appliances.

The standout feature here is the "AI Pro Cooking" mode, which uses a combination of sensors and camera data to prevent burning. In my tests, it did a commendable job with a whole roasted chicken, adjusting the convection fan speed as the skin browned. The oven also has a built-in air fryer and a steam-clean function, making it a versatile workhorse.

However, the AI isn't perfect. It occasionally misidentified a tray of roasted vegetables as "cookies," which would have been a disaster if I hadn't caught it. The learning curve for the touchscreen is real, and the constant prompts to connect to SmartThings can feel intrusive. Also, at nearly $2,900, this is a significant investment—but for a family that cooks daily, the convenience and energy savings (it preheats faster and uses less power than traditional ovens) can justify the cost.

**Pros:**
- Large capacity and full-size form factor
- Excellent integration with SmartThings
- Air fry and steam clean included
- Attractive Bespoke design

**Cons:**
- Very expensive
- AI misidentification happens occasionally
- Requires Samsung account for full features

**Verdict:** The best intelligent oven for tech-forward families who want a full-size appliance and are already invested in the Samsung ecosystem.

---

### 3. Brava Glass (2026 Edition) — Best for Speed and Precision

**Price: $1,295 (estimated 2026)**

Brava has always marched to the beat of its own drum. Instead of a traditional heating element, the Brava uses high-intensity halogen lamps that can be controlled individually across a grid. The 2026 Glass edition adds an improved camera system and a new "AI Chef" that can cook multiple foods simultaneously—think salmon on one side and roasted broccoli on the other—without mixing flavors.

The speed is the headline. Brava claims it can cook a full meal up to five times faster than a conventional oven, and in my testing, that's roughly accurate. A salmon fillet was done in about 7 minutes, and roasted vegetables in 10. The AI adjusts lamp intensity in real time based on the food's surface temperature, which it reads via infrared sensors.

The trade-off is capacity. The Brava is a countertop oven, and while it has two cooking zones, it can't handle a large turkey or a big batch of cookies. It's best for singles, couples, or small families who prioritize speed and precision over volume. The app is also required for most advanced features, and the learning curve is steeper than June's.

**Pros:**
- Extremely fast cooking
- Can cook two different foods simultaneously
- Precise temperature control
- Sleek, modern design

**Cons:**
- Small capacity
- Expensive for its size
- Requires app for full functionality

**Verdict:** The best intelligent oven for small households that value speed and don't need to cook for a crowd.

## Key Features to Consider in an Intelligent Oven

Before you buy, here's what actually matters:

| Feature | Why It Matters | June Gen 4 | Samsung Bespoke AI | Brava Glass |
|--------|----------------|------------|---------------------|-------------|
| **Food Recognition** | Auto-selects cooking settings | Yes (200+ foods) | Yes (80 foods) | Yes (limited) |

**The most important feature is food recognition accuracy.** A smart oven that misidentifies your food is worse than a dumb one—it can ruin dinner. Look for models with a large food database and a good track record.

**Second, consider capacity.** Countertop models are great for small spaces but can't handle a holiday meal. Full-size intelligent ovens are a bigger investment but more versatile.

**Third, think about ecosystem.** If you're already using SmartThings, Alexa, or Google Home, choose an oven that integrates natively. Otherwise, you'll be juggling multiple apps.

## Buying Guide: Who Should Buy an Intelligent Oven?

**Who should buy:**
- **Busy professionals** who want to cook healthy meals with minimal effort.
- **Tech enthusiasts** who enjoy automating their home and tracking cooking data.
- **Small families** who want a countertop appliance that can replace a toaster oven, air fryer, and microwave.
- **Home cooks** who struggle with timing and temperature—AI can help prevent overcooking.

**Who should skip:**
- **Large families** who need to cook big meals regularly (unless you buy a full-size model).
- **Budget-conscious shoppers** —a basic convection oven costs a fraction of the price.
- **Privacy-focused users** who are uncomfortable with cameras and always-on microphones in their kitchen.

**Budget considerations:**
- **Under $500:** You'll find "smart" ovens with app control but limited AI. These are essentially connected ovens, not truly intelligent.
- **$1,000–$1,500:** The sweet spot for countertop intelligent ovens with real food recognition (June, Brava).
- **$2,500–$3,500:** Full-size intelligent ovens with advanced AI and integration (Samsung, LG, GE).

Remember that smart ovens often require a stable Wi-Fi connection and may receive software updates that change functionality. Factor in the cost of extended warranties, as electronics in a hot kitchen can fail.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

**Installation:**
- **Countertop models** (June, Brava) are plug-and-play. Just place them on a heat-resistant surface and plug into a standard 120V outlet. No special wiring needed.
- **Full-size models** (Samsung Bespoke) require professional installation, especially if replacing a built-in oven. You'll need a 240V outlet and proper cabinetry. Expect $150–$300 for installation.

**Smart home integration:**
- **Wi-Fi:** All intelligent ovens require a 2.4GHz or 5GHz Wi-Fi network. A strong signal in the kitchen is essential.
- **Voice assistants:** Most support Alexa and Google Assistant. Samsung's Bespoke works best with Bixby but also supports Alexa.
- **Automations:** You can create routines like "Good Morning" that preheat the oven to 350°F at 7 AM. Some ovens can send notifications when preheating is complete.
- **Privacy:** Cameras and microphones are always a concern. Check if the oven has a physical shutter for the camera (most don't) and review the privacy policy. Data may be stored in the cloud.

**Pro tip:** If you're concerned about privacy, look for a model that allows you to disable the camera and microphone when not in use. The June Gen 4 has a software toggle, but no physical shutter.

**Our top pick** is the **June Intelligent Oven (Gen 4)** for most households. It's the best balance of intelligence, size, and price. It won't replace a full-size oven for big meals, but for daily cooking, it's a game-changer.

**If speed is your priority**, the **Brava Glass** is unmatched—just know its capacity limits.

Ultimately, an intelligent oven is an investment in convenience. If you cook often and value your time, the right model can pay for itself in fewer takeout orders and better meals. Just go in with realistic expectations: AI is a helpful assistant, not a Michelin-star chef.

**Final Verdict:** The intelligent oven is no longer a gimmick. In 2026, it's a smart buy for the right kitchen.
