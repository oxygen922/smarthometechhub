### Other Critical Features to Evaluate

1. **Connectivity Standards:** Look for appliances that support Matter, the new universal smart home protocol. Matter ensures cross-platform compatibility, so your smart oven from one brand can communicate with your smart fridge from another.

2. **App Quality:** The companion app should be intuitive, responsive, and regularly updated. Test the app's speed and reliability before purchasing, as a slow app can negate the convenience of smart features.

3. **Privacy and Security:** Smart appliances collect data about your cooking habits and home routines. Ensure the manufacturer offers robust encryption, transparent data policies, and the ability to disable cloud connectivity if desired.

4. **Energy Monitoring:** Many smart appliances now provide real-time energy usage data, helping you identify power-hungry devices and reduce utility bills. The Samsung Bespoke AI Refrigerator, for example, offers detailed energy reports in the SmartThings app.

5. **Voice Control Depth:** Basic voice commands like "turn on" are standard, but advanced voice control that allows you to set specific temperatures, timers, and cooking modes adds significant value.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Requirements

**Freestanding Appliances (Samsung Bespoke AI Refrigerator):**
- Standard 120V outlet within 6 feet
- Water line connection for ice maker and water dispenser (optional but recommended)
- Minimum 36-inch width opening
- Level floor and proper ventilation space

**Countertop Appliances (June Intelligent Oven):**
- Standard 120V outlet
- Minimum 6 inches of clearance on all sides for ventilation
- Stable, heat-resistant countertop surface
- No permanent installation required

**Built-in Appliances (Miele Dialog Oven):**
- Professional installation required (hardwired 240V connection)
- Custom cabinetry with proper cutout dimensions
- Ventilation system integration
- Typically requires licensed electrician

### Smart Home Integration Steps

1. **Choose Your Ecosystem:** Decide between Amazon Alexa, Google Assistant, or Apple HomeKit. Most smart appliances support multiple ecosystems, but consistency simplifies control.

2. **Set Up a Dedicated Network:** Smart appliances perform best on a dedicated 2.4 GHz network. Many devices don't support 5 GHz, so ensure your router broadcasts both bands.

3. **Use a Smart Home Hub:** While not mandatory, a hub like Samsung SmartThings or Amazon Echo Plus can centralize control and enable complex automations (e.g., "When I leave for work, turn off all kitchen appliances").

4. **Enable Automations:** Create routines that match your lifestyle. For example, set your smart oven to preheat at 5:30 PM on weekdays, or have your refrigerator send a notification when the door has been open for more than two minutes.

5. **Regular Firmware Updates:** Enable automatic updates to ensure your appliances receive new features and security patches. Most manufacturers push updates quarterly.
