# The Best Smart Ovens of 2026: Intelligent Cooking That Actually Delivers

*Last updated: January 2026 | 16-minute read*

## Introduction

The intelligent oven has quietly become one of the most compelling upgrades in the modern smart kitchen. Unlike the gimmicky connected appliances of five years ago, today's smart ovens use computer vision, AI-powered cooking algorithms, and precise multi-zone heating to genuinely improve how you cook. These aren't just ovens with a Wi-Fi chip slapped on — they recognize what you put inside, adjust temperature and humidity in real time, and learn your preferences over time.

In 2026, the category has matured significantly. Prices have dropped, app ecosystems have stabilized, and the food recognition software is finally accurate enough to trust with a $40 ribeye. We tested 14 intelligent ovens over six months — roasting, baking, proofing, air frying, and yes, burning a few things — to find the three that are actually worth your money. Whether you're a busy parent, a meal-prep enthusiast, or a home chef chasing restaurant-quality results, this guide will help you choose the right smart oven for your kitchen.

## Top 3 Intelligent Oven Recommendations

### 1. June Intelligent Oven (2026 Edition) — Best Overall Smart Oven

**Price: ~$1,499 | Capacity: 1.2 cu. ft. | Best for: Everyday countertop cooking**

June has been refining its intelligent oven since 2015, and the 2026 Edition is the most polished version yet. This is a countertop convection oven with a built-in HD camera and food-recognition AI that identifies over 200 food types — from frozen pizza to salmon fillets — and automatically selects the right cooking mode, temperature, and time.

In our testing, the food recognition was impressively accurate. We placed a tray of chicken thighs in the oven, and within seconds the display read "Chicken Thighs — Roast, 425°F, 28 minutes." The result was juicy, evenly browned chicken with crispy skin. The same held true for cookies, roasted vegetables, and reheated leftovers, which came out far better than any microwave could manage.

The 2026 model adds a few meaningful upgrades over previous generations. The internal camera now streams at 1080p (up from 720p), so you can watch your food cook from your phone with real clarity. The carbon fiber heating elements heat up faster — roughly 90 seconds to reach 400°F — and the new "Chef Mode" lets you save custom cooking profiles for dishes you make regularly. The companion app is clean and responsive, with push notifications when your food is done and a well-organized recipe library that sends cooking instructions straight to the oven.

**Pros:**
- Best-in-class food recognition and auto-cooking
- Fast preheat and even heating
- Excellent app with live camera streaming
- Compact footprint fits most countertops

**Cons:**
- Small capacity — won't fit a full turkey or large casserole
- Premium price for a countertop appliance
- Requires consistent Wi-Fi for full functionality

**Verdict:** If you want the smartest countertop oven money can buy and you mostly cook for one to three people, the June 2026 Edition is the one to beat. It's the closest thing to having a sous-chef in your kitchen.

This is a 30-inch smart oven with a built-in camera that recognizes up to 80 dishes and automatically sets the optimal cooking mode. It pairs with Samsung's SmartThings ecosystem, so it integrates seamlessly with the rest of your connected home.

The headline feature is the AI Pro Cooking system. Place a sheet pan of lasagna inside, and the oven identifies it, suggests a cooking program, and — with your confirmation — runs the entire cycle automatically. The camera also lets you monitor progress from your phone, and the oven sends a notification when your food is ready. In practice, the recognition is slightly less granular than June's (it identifies dish categories more than specific foods), but for a full-size oven it's remarkably capable.

Where the Bespoke really shines is versatility. It offers convection baking, air frying, steam cooking, dehydrating, and even a built-in air sous-vide mode. The dual-cook zone lets you run two different temperatures simultaneously, which is a genuine time-saver on holidays. The oven also supports voice control via Bixby, Alexa, and Google Assistant, and it can preheat on command — genuinely useful when you're driving home from work.

**Pros:**
- Full-size capacity with true multi-zone cooking
- Deep SmartThings integration and voice control
- Steam, air fry, dehydrate, and sous-vide modes
- Beautiful Bespoke design with customizable finishes

**Cons:**

**Verdict:** For families who want a full-size intelligent oven that doubles as the centerpiece of a connected kitchen, the Samsung Bespoke AI Oven is the strongest choice in 2026. It's an investment, but it replaces several countertop appliances.

### 3. Brava Glass Smart Oven — Best Speed and Versatility

**Price: ~$1,295 | Capacity: 0.9 cu. ft. | Best for: Fast weeknight cooking**

Brava takes a radically different approach to the intelligent oven. Instead of a single heating element and a camera, it uses high-intensity halogen lamps that can be programmed to deliver different amounts of energy to different zones of the cooking tray. Combined with its "Pure Light" cooking technology, this means you can cook a protein and vegetables side by side — each with its own optimal settings — in a fraction of the time a conventional oven requires.

The Brava's intelligence comes from its recipe-driven approach. You select a recipe in the app, and the oven tells you exactly how to arrange food on the included metal trays. The lamps then cycle on and off in precise patterns to cook each zone correctly. In our tests, a salmon fillet and roasted asparagus were both perfectly cooked in under 12 minutes — a meal that would take 25–30 minutes in a traditional oven.

The 2026 model adds improved zone detection (the oven can sense how much food is on each tray and adjust power accordingly) and a redesigned touchscreen that's brighter and more responsive. The tradeoff is that Brava works best with its own recipes and trays — it's less of a "throw anything in" oven than the June. But if speed is your priority and you're willing to follow its guidance, nothing else cooks this fast with this level of precision.

**Pros:**
- Extremely fast cooking via halogen lamp technology
- True multi-zone cooking on a single tray
- Excellent for weeknight meals and meal prep
- Compact and stylish design

**Cons:**
- Best results require following Brava's recipes
- Smaller capacity than June
- Learning curve for new users

**Verdict:** The Brava Glass is the speed champion of intelligent ovens. If your priority is getting a complete, well-cooked meal on the table in 15 minutes or less, it's unmatched.

### What to Look For

**1. Food Recognition Accuracy**
This is the core of an "intelligent" oven. The best systems (like June's) identify specific foods and set precise cooking programs. Weaker systems only recognize broad categories. Ask yourself: do you want the oven to cook for you, or just assist you?

**2. Capacity vs. Footprint**
Full-size ovens offer more capacity but cost more and require installation. Countertop models are plug-and-play but limit what you can cook. Match capacity to your household size: 1–2 people, countertop; 3+, full-size.

**3. Cooking Modes**
Air frying, steam cooking, dehydrating, and sous-vide are increasingly common. If you want to replace multiple appliances, prioritize an oven with the widest mode range.

**4. App Quality and Ecosystem**
A smart oven is only as good as its app. Look for reliable connectivity, live camera streaming, recipe integration, and compatibility with your existing smart home platform (Alexa, Google Home, SmartThings, Apple Home).

**5. Preheat Speed**
Countertop intelligent ovens typically preheat in 1–2 minutes; full-size ovens take 5–10. If speed matters, countertop wins.

**6. Self-Cleaning and Maintenance**
Steam-cleaning cycles and removable trays make a big difference in long-term usability. Check whether accessories are dishwasher-safe.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy an Intelligent Oven?

**Busy professionals and couples:** A countertop intelligent oven like the June or Brava can replace a toaster oven, air fryer, and microwave, saving counter space and time. If you cook for one or two people and value convenience, this category is for you.

**Families:** A full-size smart oven like the Samsung Bespoke makes sense if you're already invested in a smart home ecosystem and want a centerpiece appliance that handles holiday cooking and weeknight dinners alike.

**Home chefs and food enthusiasts:** If you enjoy precision cooking and want repeatable results, the recipe-driven Brava or the food-recognizing June will appeal. Just temper expectations — these are assistants, not replacements for skill.

**Who should skip it:** If you rarely cook, are on a tight budget, or don't want another app in your life, a conventional oven with a good thermometer will serve you fine.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $500:** Basic smart ovens with app control but limited AI. Good entry point, but you'll miss the "intelligent" features.
- **$1,000–$1,500:** The sweet spot for countertop intelligent ovens. June and Brava both live here.
- **$2,000–$3,500:** Full-size smart ovens with food recognition and multi-zone cooking. Samsung, LG, and GE all compete here.
- **$3,500+:** Luxury smart ovens from brands like Wolf and Miele with premium build quality and advanced sensors.

**Hidden costs to consider:** Installation (for full-size models, $150–$400), extended warranties, and replacement trays or accessories ($50–$150).

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Countertop Models

Countertop intelligent ovens like the June and Brava are essentially plug-and-play. You'll need a standard 120V outlet and about 18 inches of counter depth. Setup takes 10–15 minutes: download the app, connect to Wi-Fi (2.4GHz is more reliable for these devices), and run the calibration cycle. Place them away from walls to allow proper ventilation.

### Full-Size Models

Full-size smart ovens require professional installation. For a wall oven, expect a 240V connection and a cutout that matches the unit's dimensions. Slide-in ranges need a gas line or 240V hookup. Budget $150–$400 for installation and confirm whether your home has the right electrical setup before buying.

### Smart Home Integration

All three ovens we recommend work with Alexa and Google Assistant. Samsung's Bespoke integrates most deeply if you already use SmartThings — you can create routines like "Good morning" that preheat the oven and start your coffee maker. June and Brava work well with IFTTT for custom automations, though their native apps are the best way to control them.

**A note on privacy:** Camera-equipped ovens raise legitimate questions about data collection. All three manufacturers claim images are processed locally or anonymized, but review each company's privacy policy. If that concerns you, the Brava (which has no camera) is the most privacy-friendly option.
