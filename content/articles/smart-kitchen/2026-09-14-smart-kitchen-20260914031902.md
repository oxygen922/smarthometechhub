# The Best Intelligent Ovens of 2026: A Comprehensive Review

## Introduction

The intelligent oven has quietly become one of the most transformative appliances in the modern American smart kitchen. Unlike the flashy air fryers and viral countertop gadgets that dominate social media, smart ovens work behind the scenes—learning your cooking patterns, recognizing what you place inside them, and delivering consistently perfect results with minimal human intervention. In 2026, these appliances have matured dramatically, thanks to advances in AI-powered cooking algorithms, integrated cameras, and seamless smart home connectivity.

Whether you're a busy professional who wants dinner ready the moment you walk through the door, a home chef chasing restaurant-quality roasting, or a tech enthusiast building a fully connected home, today's intelligent ovens offer something genuinely useful. But with prices ranging from $800 to over $6,000 and wildly varying feature sets, choosing the right model requires careful consideration. This review breaks down the top options, the features that actually matter, and how to integrate a smart oven into your connected home ecosystem.

## Top 3 Product Recommendations

**Price: $1,899 (estimated 2026)**

The June Oven has long been the poster child for AI-powered cooking, and the 2026 Pro model represents the most refined version yet. Place a salmon fillet inside, and the oven recognizes it, selects the optimal cooking profile, and notifies you through the June app when it's done.

What sets the 2026 Pro apart is its upgraded "Cook Assist" system. Using computer vision, the oven monitors browning in real time and adjusts temperature and time dynamically. In our testing, this eliminated the guesswork that plagues traditional roasting—chicken came out juicy with perfectly crisp skin, and cookies baked evenly without the dreaded burnt bottoms.

The June Pro also functions as an air fryer, dehydrator, broiler, and proofing oven, consolidating several countertop appliances into one. Its 12-inch touchscreen is responsive and intuitive, offering guided recipes from partners like Serious Eats and NYT Cooking.

**Pros:**

**Cons:**

**Verdict:** The best all-around intelligent oven for most households, especially smaller kitchens where versatility matters.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy an Intelligent Oven?

**Busy professionals and couples:** If your evenings are packed and you want dinner to cook itself with minimal oversight, a countertop intelligent oven like the June Pro or Brava is a game-changer. The ability to start cooking remotely and receive a notification when food is ready eliminates the "what's for dinner" stress.

**Families:** A full-size smart oven like the Samsung Bespoke AI is the better choice. It offers the capacity for sheet-pan dinners, holiday turkeys, and batch baking, while the AI features help prevent the common mistakes that ruin large meals.

**Tech enthusiasts and smart home builders:** Look for Matter support and broad voice assistant compatibility. Both the June Pro and Samsung Bespoke integrate well with platforms like Apple Home, Google Home, and Amazon Alexa, making them ideal for automated routines.

**Home cooks seeking precision:** If you care about perfectly cooked proteins and consistent results, the temperature probe and AI monitoring in these ovens provide a level of control that traditional ovens simply can't match.

### Budget Considerations

Intelligent ovens span a wide price range. At the entry level ($800–$1,200), you'll find basic smart ovens with app control and a few presets but limited AI. The sweet spot for genuine intelligence is $1,300–$2,000, where you get food recognition, cameras, and multi-function cooking. Premium full-size models exceed $3,000, but they replace a major kitchen appliance and offer the longest lifespan.

Consider total cost of ownership, too. Countertop models use less energy per meal but have smaller capacity. Full-size ovens cost more to run but handle larger volumes efficiently. Also factor in potential smart home hub requirements—though most modern ovens connect directly via Wi-Fi.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

**Countertop models (June, Brava):** Installation is essentially plug-and-play. Place on a heat-resistant counter with adequate clearance, plug into a standard 120V outlet, and connect to your Wi-Fi via the companion app. Setup takes under 15 minutes.

**Full-size models (Samsung Bespoke):** These require professional installation, especially wall ovens that need dedicated 240V wiring and cabinet modifications. Expect installation costs of $150–$400. Once installed, connect through SmartThings, then link to your preferred voice assistant.

**Smart home integration tips:**
- Enable **Matter** support where available for cross-platform compatibility.
- Create routines: "Hey Google, start preheating the oven" or "Alexa, ask June to cook salmon."
- Use IFTTT or SmartThings automations to trigger oven preheating when your smart lock detects you're arriving home.
- Keep firmware updated—AI cooking algorithms improve over time via OTA updates.
