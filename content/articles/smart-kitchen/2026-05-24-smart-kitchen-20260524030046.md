## Installation & Smart Home Integration

Installing a countertop intelligent oven is straightforward—just plug it into a standard 120V outlet and connect to your Wi-Fi network. However, for optimal smart home integration, follow these tips:

1. **Network Setup:** Ensure your 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi band is active, as many smart appliances only support this frequency. Place the oven within range of your router to avoid connectivity issues.
2. **App Configuration:** Download the manufacturer’s app and create an account. Follow the in-app instructions to pair the oven. This usually involves scanning a QR code or entering a PIN.
3. **Voice Assistant Linking:** In the app, enable Alexa or Google Assistant skills. You may need to link accounts (e.g., Amazon or Google) and discover devices. Once set up, you can create routines like “Start preheating when I arrive home” using geofencing.
4. **IFTTT Integration:** For advanced automation, use IFTTT (If This Then That) to connect your oven with other smart devices. For example, “If my smart smoke detector goes off, turn off the oven” or “When my morning alarm rings, preheat oven for breakfast.”
5. **Placement:** Leave at least 4 inches of clearance around the oven for ventilation. Avoid placing it near heat-sensitive surfaces.

Integration with platforms like Apple HomeKit is still limited in 2026, but Alexa and Google remain the standard. For a cohesive smart kitchen, pair your oven with a smart speaker, smart plugs, and a connected range hood.
