# The Ultimate Intelligent Oven Review: Smart Cooking in 2026

## Introduction

The kitchen has always been the heart of the home, but in 2026, it’s also the brain of your smart home ecosystem. Intelligent ovens have evolved far beyond simple programmable timers and Wi-Fi connectivity. Today’s smart ovens are AI-powered culinary assistants that can recognize your food, suggest recipes based on what’s in your fridge, and even learn your family’s preferences over time. Whether you’re a busy professional looking to streamline meal prep or a home chef eager to experiment with precision cooking, the latest generation of intelligent ovens offers something transformative. This review dives deep into the top three smart ovens of 2026, breaking down their features, performance, and value. After testing these units in real-world kitchens for over 100 hours, I’m ready to share which one deserves a spot on your countertop—and which might be overkill for your needs.

## Top 3 Product Recommendations

### 1. June Intelligent Oven 2026 Edition – Best Overall

The June Intelligent Oven has been a market leader since its inception, and the 2026 Edition solidifies its reputation. This countertop convection oven now features a 10-inch high-definition touchscreen powered by the new JuneOS 4.0, which includes a neural processing unit (NPU) dedicated to food recognition. The camera system has been upgraded to a 12-megapixel wide-angle lens, allowing it to identify over 200 different food items—from a whole chicken to a slice of frozen pizza—and automatically set the optimal cook time and temperature.

**Performance:** In testing, the June 2026 cooked a 4-pound roast chicken to perfection in 45 minutes, with the AI adjusting the convection fan speed mid-cycle to ensure even browning. The built-in food thermometer is wireless and charges via induction, so you never need to replace batteries. The oven also supports multi-stage cooking: you can program it to sear a steak, then lower the temperature to finish it medium-rare, all without opening the door.

**Smart Features:** The June app now includes “Meal Planning AI,” which scans your calendar and suggests recipes based on your schedule. If you have a late meeting, it will recommend a 20-minute meal. Integration with Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant is seamless, and the oven can even sync with smart refrigerators to know what ingredients you have on hand.

**Price:** $599 (2026 estimate)

**Verdict:** The June 2026 is the most complete intelligent oven for most households. Its balance of AI smarts, cooking performance, and user-friendly design makes it the clear winner for anyone serious about smart cooking.

### 2. Tovala Smart Oven Pro – Best for Meal Kit Lovers

Tovala has carved out a niche by combining a smart steam-and-bake oven with a meal delivery service. The Smart Oven Pro, released in late 2025, takes this concept further with a larger interior (0.8 cubic feet) and a new “Quick Steam” feature that injects moisture for crusty bread and juicy meats. The oven scans barcodes on Tovala meal kits to automatically set the perfect cooking profile, but it also works with non-Tovala foods via its AI scanner.

**Performance:** The Pro model excels at reheating leftovers. Its “Reheat+” mode uses a combination of steam and convection to restore pizza to crispy perfection and revive soggy fries. I tested it on day-old Chinese takeout, and the result was indistinguishable from fresh. For from-scratch cooking, the oven has 12 preset modes including broil, bake, air fry, and dehydrate. The steam function is particularly good for vegetables—broccoli came out bright green and tender-crisp in just 6 minutes.

**Smart Features:** Tovala’s app is intuitive, with a recipe library that grows weekly. The oven connects to Wi-Fi and can be controlled via voice assistants, but the standout feature is the “Scan & Cook” capability. You can scan any packaged food’s barcode, and the oven will look up the recommended settings in its database. For fresh foods, you can manually input weight and type.

**Price:** $349 (2026 estimate, with meal kit subscription discount)

**Verdict:** If you love the convenience of meal kits or frequently reheat leftovers, the Tovala Smart Oven Pro is a fantastic value. It’s less expensive than the June but sacrifices some advanced AI features.

### 3. Brava Glass Intelligent Oven – Best for Precision Cooking

Brava’s latest model, the Glass Intelligent Oven, uses a unique combination of visible light and infrared heating to cook food faster and more evenly than traditional ovens. The Glass model adds a transparent cooking chamber, so you can watch your food cook without opening the door. The oven uses six high-power lamps that can be individually controlled to create up to 20 different cooking zones.

**Performance:** The Brava Glass is astonishingly fast. It cooked a 12-inch pizza in 4 minutes and salmon fillets in 6 minutes. The precision is unmatched: you can set different temperatures for different parts of the cooking surface, so the left side can be 500°F while the right side stays at 350°F. This is ideal for cooking a steak and vegetables simultaneously. The oven also includes a built-in scale that weighs your food and adjusts cooking times automatically.

**Smart Features:** The Brava app offers guided cooking with step-by-step video instructions. The oven’s camera streams to your phone, so you can monitor progress from another room. It integrates with IFTTT and SmartThings, allowing for complex automations like preheating when your phone detects you’re 10 minutes from home.

**Price:** $1,295 (2026 estimate)

**Verdict:** The Brava Glass is for the enthusiast who demands restaurant-quality results and has the budget to match. It’s overkill for casual cooks but a dream for foodies.

## Buying Guide

**Who Should Buy What:**

- **Busy Families (June 2026):** If you’re juggling work, kids, and meal prep, the June’s AI meal planning and food recognition will save you 30 minutes a day. It’s the set-it-and-forget-it champion.
- **Meal Kit Enthusiasts (Tovala Pro):** If you subscribe to Tovala or similar services, the Pro is a no-brainer. It’s also great for singles or couples who cook simple meals and want to minimize waste.
- **Serious Home Chefs (Brava Glass):** If you love experimenting with sous-vide, reverse-searing, or multi-component dishes, the Brava’s precision is unmatched. It’s also ideal for small kitchens where speed matters.
- **Budget-Conscious Shoppers:** The Tovala Pro is the best value under $400. For under $200, consider the Breville Smart Oven Air Fryer Pro—it lacks AI but offers excellent manual control.

**Budget Considerations:**
- **Entry-Level ($200–$350):** Basic smart ovens with Wi-Fi and preset modes. Good for beginners.
- **Mid-Range ($350–$700):** AI features, food recognition, and better build quality. June and Tovala Pro fall here.
- **Premium ($700+):** Cutting-edge technology like light-based cooking, multiple zones, and app ecosystems. Brava Glass leads this segment.

**Installation & Setup:** All three models are countertop units requiring no installation beyond plugging into a standard 120V outlet. The June and Tovala need Wi-Fi (2.4 GHz recommended for stability). The Brava requires a dedicated circuit if you plan to use it at max power for extended periods.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

**Installation Steps (Common to All Models):**
1. **Unbox and Place:** Ensure at least 4 inches of clearance on all sides for ventilation. Avoid placing near a microwave or refrigerator.
2. **Connect to Wi-Fi:** Download the manufacturer’s app and follow the on-screen prompts. Most ovens use 2.4 GHz networks; if your router uses 5 GHz, you may need to enable a dual-band setup.
3. **Calibrate:** Some ovens (like the Brava) require a calibration cycle. This takes about 10 minutes and ensures even cooking.
4. **Update Firmware:** Check for updates in the app. The June 2026 had a critical update that improved food recognition accuracy by 15%.

**Smart Home Integration:**
- **Voice Assistants:** All three work with Alexa and Google Assistant. You can say, “Alexa, preheat the June to 375°F,” or “Hey Google, ask Brava to cook the salmon.”
- **Automations:** Use IFTTT or SmartThings to create routines. For example: “When I arrive home, preheat the Tovala to 400°F and start the air fryer.”
- **Cross-Device Sync:** The June can sync with Amazon’s Echo Hub to display cooking timers on your wall screen. The Brava integrates with Samsung SmartThings for energy monitoring.
- **Security:** All models require account creation. June and Brava offer two-factor authentication. Tovala’s app has basic password protection—consider using a password manager.

**Pro Tip:** For best results, place your smart oven on a stable, heat-resistant surface. Avoid using extension cords, as these ovens draw significant power (up to 1,800 watts on the Brava).

- **Best Overall: June Intelligent Oven 2026 Edition** – It’s the Goldilocks option: not too expensive, not too basic. The AI food recognition is genuinely useful, and the cooking results are consistently excellent. For $599, it’s the smartest investment you can make in your kitchen.

**Final Recommendation:** For the typical American household, buy the June 2026. It’s the oven that will actually change how you cook—not just because it’s smart, but because it makes you a better cook without extra effort. Pair it with an Echo Hub for the ultimate smart kitchen experience.
