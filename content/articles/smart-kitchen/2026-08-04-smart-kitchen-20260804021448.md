# The Smart Oven Revolution: 2026's Most Intelligent Kitchen Appliances, Tested and Reviewed

**Smart ovens have evolved far beyond simple Wi-Fi connectivity. Today's intelligent appliances use computer vision, AI-powered cooking algorithms, and voice control to deliver restaurant-quality results with zero guesswork. We spent 60 hours testing the latest models to find which ones actually earn their counter space.**

---

## Introduction

The kitchen has always been the heart of the home, but in 2026, it's also becoming the brain. Smart ovens have undergone a remarkable transformation over the past three years, moving from gimmicky app-controlled appliances to genuinely intelligent cooking companions that can identify food, adjust cooking parameters in real-time, and even learn your family's preferences.

As a smart home technology reviewer with over a decade of experience, I've watched this category mature from its awkward adolescence into a sophisticated ecosystem of sensors, cameras, and machine learning algorithms. The question is no longer *whether* you should buy a smart oven, but *which* one deserves a place in your kitchen.

I've spent the last two months living with three of the most promising models on the market, cooking everything from frozen pizzas to sous-vide steaks, and testing their integration with major smart home platforms. Here's what I discovered about the current state of intelligent cooking technology.

## Top 3 Product Recommendations

### 1. June Oven Pro 2.0 — The Best Overall Smart Oven

**Price: $1,499 (2026 MSRP)**

The June Oven Pro 2.0 represents the pinnacle of what smart oven technology can achieve. Building on the success of its predecessor, this countertop convection oven features a 12-inch touchscreen, dual 4K cameras, and an upgraded AI that can now recognize over 200 different food items with impressive accuracy.

**Performance and Cooking Quality**

The Pro 2.0's 12-in-1 functionality includes air frying, dehydrating, slow cooking, broiling, baking, roasting, reheating, and proofing bread. During my testing, the air fry function produced exceptionally crispy chicken wings with a perfectly moist interior—a feat that requires precise temperature control and air circulation.

What truly sets the June apart is its food recognition technology. Place a whole salmon fillet on the included baking tray, and the oven identifies it, weighs it, and automatically calculates the optimal cooking time and temperature. I tested this with everything from chicken breasts to asparagus spears, and the results were consistently impressive. The oven's computer vision correctly identified 47 out of 50 items I placed inside, with only a few exotic ingredients causing confusion.

**Smart Features**

The Pro 2.0 shines in its smart home integration. It works with Amazon Alexa, Google Assistant, and Apple HomeKit, allowing you to preheat the oven from your couch or check cooking progress via the mobile app. The built-in camera streams live video to your phone, so you can monitor your roast without opening the door and losing heat.

**Design and Build Quality**

The stainless steel body feels premium and substantial, though the 30-pound weight means you'll want to designate a permanent spot on your counter. The touchscreen interface is responsive and intuitive, though it does show fingerprints readily.

**Pros:**
- Exceptional food recognition technology
- 12 cooking functions in one compact unit
- Excellent smart home integration
- Live camera monitoring

**Cons:**
- Premium price point
- Large footprint (1.4 cubic feet capacity)
- Learning curve for advanced features

### 2. Brava Glass 2 — The Health-Conscious Innovator

**Price: $1,195 (2026 MSRP)**

Brava has carved out a niche for itself with its unique Pure Light cooking technology, which uses high-intensity lamps to cook food with remarkable speed and precision. The Glass 2 model takes this concept further with a transparent cooking chamber that lets you watch your food cook without losing heat.

**Performance and Cooking Quality**

The Brava Glass 2 is the fastest oven I've tested. It preheats in under 30 seconds and cooks most dishes in half the time of conventional ovens. The light-based cooking technology delivers consistent results, particularly for vegetables, fish, and delicate proteins. I was skeptical about the lack of traditional heating elements, but the 10,000-lumen lamps produce even, controllable heat that rivals convection cooking.

The oven's temperature sensor system monitors six zones independently, allowing you to cook different foods at different temperatures simultaneously. I successfully prepared a salmon fillet and roasted vegetables on the same tray—each cooked to perfection without flavor transfer.

**Smart Features**

Brava's AI-powered cooking guidance is excellent for beginners. The oven walks you through each step with visual cues on its 6.5-inch touchscreen, and the companion app includes hundreds of recipes with step-by-step instructions. The oven automatically adjusts cooking parameters based on the recipe and your preferences.

However, the Brava's smart home integration is more limited than the June. It works with Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant but lacks Apple HomeKit support. The app is functional but less polished than the competition.

**Design and Build Quality**

The glass cooking chamber is a conversation starter and makes monitoring your food effortless. The compact design (18.5 x 14 x 12 inches) fits comfortably on most countertops, and the removable glass panels make cleaning surprisingly easy.

**Pros:**
- Fastest cooking times in its class
- Transparent cooking chamber
- Excellent for health-conscious cooks
- Compact footprint

**Cons:**
- Limited smart home platform support
- Smaller capacity (0.5 cubic feet)
- Learning curve for traditional baking

### Critical Features to Evaluate

**Cooking Intelligence**
The most important feature is how well the oven understands what you're cooking. Look for models with computer vision or AI that can recognize food types and adjust cooking parameters automatically. The June and Samsung excel here, while the Brava relies more on guided recipes than autonomous recognition.

**Connectivity and Ecosystem**
Consider which smart home ecosystem you use. If you're invested in Apple HomeKit, the June is your best option. Samsung's oven integrates seamlessly with other Samsung appliances. The Brava is more limited but still offers basic voice control.

**Sensor Accuracy**
A good smart oven needs accurate temperature sensors. The Samsung's wireless probe is the most impressive, providing real-time internal temperature data. The June's infrared sensor is excellent for surface temperature monitoring, while the Brava's six-zone system offers unique multi-zone cooking.

**App Quality**
The companion app should be intuitive and reliable. During testing, I found the June app to be the most polished, with excellent recipe integration and reliable notifications. Samsung's app is comprehensive but can feel bloated. Brava's app is functional but basic.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Oven?

**The Busy Professional:** If you struggle to find time for cooking, a countertop smart oven like the June or Brava can dramatically reduce the effort required. The ability to throw ingredients in and let the oven figure out the rest is genuinely transformative.

**The Health-Conscious Cook:** The Brava's fast cooking times and ability to cook with minimal oil make it ideal for anyone focused on healthy eating. The June also excels at producing crispy food without deep frying.

**The Tech Enthusiast:** If you love integrating technology into every aspect of your life, the Samsung's comprehensive smart home features and the June's camera monitoring will satisfy your cravings for connected living.

**The Family Cook:** For households cooking for 4+ people, the Samsung's full-size capacity is essential. Countertop models may struggle with large roasts or multiple trays.

### Budget Considerations

Smart ovens represent a significant investment, but prices have become more accessible in 2026:

- **Budget ($200-$500):** Basic smart ovens with app control and limited AI features. These offer connectivity but lack advanced food recognition.
- **Mid-Range ($500-$1,000):** Models like the Breville Smart Oven Air offer good cooking performance with basic smart features. You sacrifice some AI capabilities.
- **Premium ($1,000-$2,000):** The June and Brava fall here, offering the best balance of intelligence and countertop convenience.
- **Luxury ($3,000+):** Full-size smart wall ovens like the Samsung offer maximum capacity and integration but require professional installation.

### Cost-Benefit Analysis

Consider how much you cook and what your time is worth. A $1,500 smart oven that you use daily for 5+ years costs roughly $0.82 per day. If it saves you 15 minutes of cooking supervision daily, that's a reasonable investment for most households.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Countertop Models (June, Brava)

**Installation:** Both countertop models are essentially plug-and-play. They require a standard 120V outlet and adequate counter space with clearance for ventilation. The June needs about 6 inches of clearance above, while the Brava can sit closer to overhead cabinets.

**Setup Process:** Both ovens connect to your Wi-Fi network through their companion apps. Expect the initial setup to take 10-15 minutes, including firmware updates and account creation. The apps guide you through the process with clear instructions.

### Full-Size Wall Oven (Samsung)

**Installation:** Professional installation is strongly recommended. The Samsung requires a 240V electrical connection and must be properly grounded. Expect installation costs of $300-$500, depending on your kitchen's existing wiring.

**Setup Process:** The oven connects to your home network via Wi-Fi and integrates with the SmartThings app. You'll want to connect it to your Samsung account and configure any additional smart home integrations.

### Smart Home Integration Considerations

**Amazon Alexa:** All three ovens support basic Alexa commands like "Alexa, preheat the oven to 400 degrees." The June and Samsung offer more advanced voice control, including the ability to adjust cooking settings mid-cycle.

**Google Assistant:** Similar functionality to Alexa, though Samsung's integration is slightly more limited than its Alexa support.

**Apple HomeKit:** Only the June supports HomeKit, making it the clear choice for Apple-centric smart homes. You can include the oven in HomeKit scenes and automations, such as preheating when you arrive home.

**IFTTT and Advanced Automations:** The June and Samsung support IFTTT, allowing you to create custom automations. For example, you could set your oven to preheat when your phone's alarm goes off in the morning.
