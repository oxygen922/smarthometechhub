## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Requirements

Most smart kitchen appliances require professional installation, especially gas ranges and built-in refrigerators. Expect costs of $150–$400 for installation, depending on complexity. Countertop appliances like the Thermomix are plug-and-play.

**Key considerations:**
- Ensure your kitchen has dedicated 220V outlets for ranges and ovens
- Wi-Fi coverage must reach your kitchen—consider a mesh network if needed
- Some appliances require a neutral wire for smart features (check before purchase)

### Smart Home Ecosystem Compatibility

**Matter Protocol:** In 2026, Matter support is crucial. It ensures your appliances work with Apple HomeKit, Google Home, Amazon Alexa, and Samsung SmartThings. All three recommended products support Matter.

**Integration Examples:**
- **Samsung Bespoke Oven:** Works with SmartThings routines—e.g., preheat oven when your smart lock detects you're 15 minutes away
- **LG Refrigerator:** Integrates with LG ThinQ to suggest recipes based on expiring ingredients
- **Thermomix TM7:** Syncs with Apple Health to suggest meals based on your dietary goals

**Pro Tip:** Use a smart plug for non-critical appliances (coffee makers, slow cookers) to add voice control without replacing the device.
