## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Basics

Most smart kitchen appliances require standard electrical connections (120V or 240V depending on the appliance) and a stable Wi-Fi network (2.4 GHz is recommended for reliability). For dishwashers and refrigerators, you'll also need a water line. Professional installation is recommended for ovens and dishwashers to ensure proper setup and avoid voiding warranties.

### Smart Home Integration Tips

1. **Start with a Hub:** If you don't already have one, invest in a smart home hub like Amazon Echo Show or Google Nest Hub. These serve as central control points for all your devices.
2. **Create Routines:** Use IFTTT (If This Then That) or your voice assistant's app to create automations. For example, "When I say 'Goodnight,' turn off the oven, lock the fridge, and set the dishwasher to run at 2 AM."
3. **Check Compatibility:** Before buying, verify that the appliance works with your existing ecosystem. Most modern devices support Alexa and Google Home, but Apple HomeKit compatibility is less common.
4. **Secure Your Network:** Smart appliances are vulnerable to hacking. Use strong passwords, enable two-factor authentication, and keep firmware updated.
5. **Test Connectivity:** Place your router centrally in your home. If your kitchen is far from the router, consider a mesh Wi-Fi system to ensure strong signals.
