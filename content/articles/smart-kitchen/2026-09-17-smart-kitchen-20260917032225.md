## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Basics

Most countertop smart appliances are plug-and-play. Larger appliances—refrigerators, ranges, dishwashers—may require professional installation, especially if they involve water lines or 240V outlets. Always confirm dimensions and clearance before ordering.

### Setting Up Your Smart Ecosystem

1. **Download the Manufacturer App:** Each brand has its own app (SmartThings, ThinQ, June, Anova).
2. **Connect to Wi-Fi:** Use the 2.4GHz band for best compatibility.
3. **Link to a Hub:** Add devices to Apple Home, Google Home, or Alexa for unified control.
4. **Create Routines:** Example—"Good Morning" triggers your smart coffee maker and preheats the oven.
5. **Enable Matter:** For cross-platform control, enable Matter pairing where supported.

### Integration Tips

- Use **IFTTT** or **Amazon Routines** to connect appliances that lack native integrations.
- Pair smart appliances with **smart plugs** for older devices.
- Keep firmware updated to maintain security and feature access.
