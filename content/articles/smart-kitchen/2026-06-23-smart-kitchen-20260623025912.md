## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Tips

1. **Wi-Fi Requirements:** All smart appliances need a stable 2.4GHz or 5GHz Wi-Fi network. Place your router within 30 feet of the appliance for best performance. Mesh networks (like Google Nest Wi-Fi or Eero) are recommended for larger homes.

2. **Electrical Considerations:** The Samsung fridge requires a dedicated 15-amp circuit. The June oven and GE smoker can share a circuit but should not be used simultaneously with high-draw appliances like microwaves.

3. **Ventilation:** The GE smoker needs at least 6 inches of clearance on all sides for airflow. Do not install it under cabinets unless they are at least 24 inches above the counter.

4. **Water Line:** For the Samsung fridge's AutoFill water pitcher and ice maker, you'll need a cold water line with a shut-off valve. Most homes have this behind the fridge, but older homes may require professional installation.

### Smart Home Integration

- **Samsung SmartThings:** The Samsung fridge acts as a SmartThings hub, connecting to Samsung appliances, lights, and sensors. You can create routines like "Good Morning" that turns on kitchen lights and starts the coffee maker.
- **Apple HomeKit:** All three appliances support HomeKit via Matter. You can ask Siri to preheat the June oven or check the fridge's inventory.
- **Google Home:** Create routines like "Movie Night" that dims lights and sets the GE smoker to hold mode.
- **Alexa Routines:** "Alexa, start smoking" will begin the GE smoker's preheat cycle.

**Pro Tip:** Use the IFTTT (If This Then That) platform to create advanced automations. For example, if your Samsung fridge detects you're low on milk, it can add it to your Amazon Fresh cart automatically.
