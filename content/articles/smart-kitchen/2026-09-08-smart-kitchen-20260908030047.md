## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Requirements

**Countertop Models (June & Breville):** Installation is straightforward—simply place the unit on a heat-resistant countertop surface with at least 4 inches of clearance on all sides. These ovens operate on standard 120V outlets, though we recommend a dedicated 15-amp circuit to prevent breaker trips during high-temperature cooking. No professional installation is required.

**Full-Size Range (Bosch):** Professional installation is strongly recommended for slide-in ranges. The process involves:
- Disconnecting and removing your existing range
- Verifying gas line pressure (if applicable)
- Connecting the 240V electrical supply (must be a dedicated circuit)
- Ensuring proper cabinet clearance (minimum 1-inch side clearance, 2-inch rear clearance)
- Leveling the unit and securing anti-tip brackets

**Ventilation:** Regardless of oven type, adequate ventilation is crucial. Countertop models should be positioned near a range hood or open window. Full-size ranges require either a downdraft ventilation system or overhead range hood rated for your cooking output.

### Smart Home Integration Strategies

**Voice Control Setup:**
- **Amazon Alexa:** Enable the manufacturer's skill in the Alexa app, then link your oven account. Common commands include: "Alexa, ask June to preheat to 400 degrees" or "Alexa, set Bosch oven to convection roast."
- **Google Assistant:** Similar setup process via the Google Home app. Google's integration often provides more granular control, including specific cooking modes.
- **Apple HomeKit (June only):** For Apple users, the June appears as a standard accessory in the Home app, enabling Scene automation (e.g., "Good Morning" scene triggers preheat).

**Automation Scenarios:**
- **Arrival Cooking:** Use geofencing to trigger preheating when you're 30 minutes from home.
- **Meal Prep Synchronization:** Coordinate your oven with other smart appliances (e.g., have your smart refrigerator send recipe data to the oven).
- **Safety Automation:** Set alerts when the oven has been left on for extended periods or when internal temperatures exceed safe thresholds.

**Troubleshooting Common Issues:**
- **Wi-Fi Connectivity:** Ensure your oven is within range of your router or use a mesh Wi-Fi system. Many ovens only support 2.4GHz networks, so check your router settings.
- **App Syncing:** If recipes aren't transferring, verify both the app and oven firmware are updated to the latest versions.
- **Sensor Calibration:** For probe-based systems, periodically test accuracy against a trusted instant-read thermometer.
