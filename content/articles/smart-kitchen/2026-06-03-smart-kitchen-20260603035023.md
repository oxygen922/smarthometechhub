## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Tips

1. **Refrigerators:** Ensure you have proper clearance for ventilation (at least 2 inches on sides and back). Most smart fridges require a dedicated 115V outlet. The Samsung Bespoke model may need professional installation for the water line if you opt for the ice maker and water dispenser.

2. **Countertop Ovens:** Place on a heat-resistant surface away from walls. The June Oven is heavy (35 lbs), so ensure your countertop can support it. No special electrical work is needed—it plugs into a standard outlet.

3. **All-in-One Machines:** The Thermomix TM7 requires a stable, flat surface. Its powerful motor can vibrate, so a non-slip mat is recommended. It’s corded and needs a standard outlet.

### Smart Home Integration Steps

- **Wi-Fi Setup:** All three devices require a 2.4GHz Wi-Fi network (5GHz may cause connectivity issues). Use your router settings to create a separate IoT network for stability.
- **App Installation:** Download the respective apps (SmartThings for Samsung, June App, or Cookidoo). Create accounts and follow on-screen pairing instructions.
- **Voice Assistant Linking:** In the app, enable skills for Alexa or Google Assistant. You can then say, “Alexa, ask June to preheat to 350°F.”
- **Automations:** Use platforms like IFTTT or Apple Shortcuts to create routines. For example, “When I leave work, turn on the June Oven and set my fridge to vacation mode.”

**Pro Tip:** For seamless integration, consider sticking with one ecosystem (e.g., Samsung SmartThings if you already have Samsung appliances). This reduces compatibility headaches.
