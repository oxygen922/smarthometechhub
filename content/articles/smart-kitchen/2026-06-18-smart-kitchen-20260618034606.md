## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Considerations

- **Refrigerators:** Ensure your kitchen has adequate space for a French-door model (typically 36 inches wide). Most smart refrigerators require a dedicated 20-amp circuit. Professional installation is recommended for water line connections (ice maker/water dispenser).
- **Countertop Ovens:** These are plug-and-play—just place on a heat-resistant surface and plug into a standard outlet. Ensure at least 4 inches of clearance on all sides for ventilation.
- **Induction Ranges:** Induction requires a 240-volt outlet (like electric ranges). If you’re switching from gas, you may need to hire an electrician to run a new circuit. Induction cooktops also require magnetic cookware (test with a magnet).

### Smart Home Integration

All three products work seamlessly with major smart home platforms:

- **Samsung Bespoke AI Family Hub:** Integrates with SmartThings, Google Assistant, and Amazon Alexa. You can view the fridge camera on a smart display or ask Alexa to add items to your shopping list.
- **June Intelligent Oven:** Works with Google Assistant and Amazon Alexa. You can say, “Hey Google, preheat the June oven to 375°F,” or check cooking progress via voice.
- **LG InstaView Induction Range:** Compatible with Google Assistant, Amazon Alexa, and Apple HomeKit (via ThinQ). The range can also sync with LG’s SmartThinQ sensors for additional automation.

**Pro Tip:** For a fully integrated smart kitchen, use a hub like Samsung SmartThings or Apple HomePod to create routines. For example, set a “Cooking Mode” that dims lights, turns on the range hood, and plays your favorite playlist.
