## Buying Guide: Who Should Buy What

Choosing the right intelligent oven depends on your lifestyle, cooking habits, and budget.

### Who Should Buy a Smart Countertop Oven (e.g., June, COSORI)?

- **The Tech Enthusiast:** If you love the latest gadgets and want the "wow" factor in your kitchen, a high-end countertop model like the June is a must-have.
- **The Busy Professional:** If you want to come home to a perfectly cooked meal without spending hours in the kitchen, the remote control and presets of a countertop smart oven are invaluable.
- **Small Families & Couples:** A countertop oven is often the perfect size for preparing meals for two to four people, saving energy compared to a full-size oven.
- **Apartment Dwellers:** For those with limited kitchen space, a countertop model is a practical and space-saving solution.

### Who Should Buy a Full-Size Smart Wall Oven (e.g., GE Profile)?

- **The Serious Home Chef:** If you regularly cook large meals, bake bread, or roast big cuts of meat, the capacity and power of a full-size oven are essential.
- **Families:** A 30-inch wall oven can accommodate multiple dishes and large roasting pans, making it ideal for feeding a family.
- **The Renovator:** If you're planning a kitchen remodel and have the budget, a built-in smart oven is a fantastic investment that adds value and functionality to your home.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $300:** You can find excellent smart countertop ovens like the COSORI, offering a great balance of features and value.
- **$500 - $1,000:** This is the sweet spot for premium countertop ovens like the June, with advanced AI, cameras, and superior build quality.
- **$2,500+:** This price range is for full-size built-in smart ovens from brands like GE, Samsung, and LG, which offer the most powerful features and largest capacities.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation

- **Countertop Ovens:** These are plug-and-play. Simply place them on a sturdy, heat-resistant countertop away from walls and ensure there’s adequate ventilation. No professional installation is required.
- **Built-in Ovens:** Professional installation is mandatory. A licensed electrician is needed to ensure the oven is correctly wired to your home's electrical system (typically a 240-volt circuit). The installation also involves precise cutting and fitting of the oven into your cabinetry.

### Smart Home Integration

The true power of an intelligent oven is realized when it’s part of a connected ecosystem. Here’s how the top models integrate:

- **Amazon Alexa & Google Assistant:** All three recommended ovens support voice commands. You can say, "Alexa, preheat the oven to 400 degrees," or "Hey Google, set a timer for 20 minutes."
- **Apple HomeKit:** The GE Profile is one of the few full-size ovens that integrates with Apple HomeKit, allowing you to control it through the Home app and use Siri.
- **IFTTT (If This Then That):** Some brands offer IFTTT support, allowing you to create custom automations. For example, you could have your oven turn on when your smart door lock detects you're on your way home.
- **Matter Protocol:** As the new universal smart home standard, Matter is slowly being adopted. This will ensure even greater cross-brand compatibility in the future. Check if the oven you're interested in is Matter-certified.
