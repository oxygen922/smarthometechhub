### Beyond the Basics: Critical Features to Look For

- **Cooking Probe:** A must-have. This allows the oven to cook to an exact internal temperature, not just a time, ensuring perfectly cooked meats and baked goods. Look for a probe with multiple sensors for the best results.
- **App Ecosystem:** The quality of the companion app can make or break the experience. Look for an app that is stable, offers a wide selection of guided recipes, and allows for intuitive control. The ability to send a recipe directly to the oven is a huge plus.
- **Camera and Computer Vision (AI):** This is the cutting edge of smart ovens. Cameras allow you to monitor your food remotely without opening the door (which loses heat). Computer vision takes it a step further by identifying the food and automatically setting the perfect cook cycle.
- **Convection Cooking:** Essential for even baking and fast air frying. A high-CFM fan will ensure crispy results and shorter cooking times.
- **Connectivity:** Ensure the oven has dual-band Wi-Fi (2.4GHz and 5GHz) for a stable connection. Bluetooth can be useful for initial setup but isn't necessary for daily use.
- **Safety Features:** Look for features like automatic shut-off, cool-touch exteriors, and internal cooling systems to protect your countertops.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy an Intelligent Oven?

- **The Busy Professional:** If you have little time to cook but want healthy, home-cooked meals, a smart oven is a lifesaver. You can prep in the morning, put the food in the oven, and use your phone to start it remotely so it's done when you walk in the door.
- **The Tech Enthusiast:** If you love the latest gadgets and integrating your home with smart technology, an intelligent oven is a must-have. Models like the June offer a "wow" factor that will impress your friends and elevate your cooking.
- **The Novice Cook:** If you lack confidence in the kitchen, these ovens are the ultimate safety net. Features like Food ID and guided recipes take the guesswork out of cooking, ensuring you don't undercook or burn your meals.
- **Families:** Full-size models like the GE Profile are perfect for families. The No-Preheat feature saves precious time on busy weeknights, and the large capacity allows you to cook multiple dishes at once.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $400:** This is the "entry-level" smart oven zone. You'll find excellent countertop models like the Instant Pot Smart Oven that offer app control and multiple functions but lack advanced AI features. These are excellent value and a great way to test the waters.
- **$400 - $1,000:** In this mid-range, you'll find high-end countertop ovens and some smaller built-in models. These often have superior build quality, more powerful convection fans, and better camera systems.
- **$1,000 and Above:** This is the premium tier, where you find the true "intelligent" ovens like the June. You are paying for cutting-edge technology like computer vision, high-resolution cameras, and a flawless, polished user experience. For built-in models, expect to pay $2,500+ for a full-size wall oven with smart features.

**Pro-Tip:** When budgeting, don't forget to factor in installation costs for built-in models. Professional installation for a wall oven can range from $150 to $300.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Considerations

- **Countertop Models:** These are plug-and-play. Simply place them on a sturdy, heat-resistant countertop, plug them into a standard 120V outlet, and you're ready to go. Ensure there is adequate clearance around the oven for ventilation.
- **Built-in Models:** These require professional installation. You'll need to ensure your kitchen's electrical system can handle the load (typically 240V for a wall oven). A licensed electrician may be needed to run a new circuit. The physical installation involves fitting the oven into a custom cabinet cutout, which should be done by a professional to ensure proper fit and ventilation.

### Smart Home Integration

The true power of an intelligent oven is unlocked through its integration with your smart home ecosystem. Here’s what to expect:

- **Voice Control:** Most smart ovens work with Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant. You can use voice commands to preheat the oven, set a timer, or ask for the oven's temperature. For example: "Hey Google, preheat the kitchen oven to 400 degrees."
- **Automations:** You can create routines that link your oven with other smart devices. For example, you can have your smart lights turn green and your smart speaker play a chime when your oven's cooking timer finishes.
- **Remote Monitoring:** The most practical feature is the ability to check on your food from anywhere. The app will show you the current oven temperature and a live video feed (if the oven has a camera), giving you total peace of mind.
- **Recipe Integration:** Many ovens partner with recipe apps like SideChef or Yummly. You can select a recipe, and the oven will automatically set the correct cooking mode, time, and temperature, eliminating the need to manually input settings.
