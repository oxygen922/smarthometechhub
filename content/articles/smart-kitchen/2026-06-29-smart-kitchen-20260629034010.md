### 2. **Breville PolyScience HydroPro Smart Oven**

**Overview:** Breville, a trusted name in kitchen appliances, partnered with PolyScience (known for sous-vide technology) to create the HydroPro Smart Oven. This countertop model uses a unique combination of convection, steam, and precision temperature control to deliver restaurant-quality results. It's particularly renowned for bread baking and roasting.

**Performance & Features:** The HydroPro's standout feature is its steam injection system, which creates a crispy crust on artisan breads and keeps meats moist. The "Smart Probe" with dual sensors monitors both internal meat temperature and ambient oven temperature, adjusting cooking parameters in real time. The oven connects to the Breville+ app, offering over 300 guided recipes with video instructions. It supports IFTTT for custom automation routines, such as preheating when you arrive home.

**Pros:**
- Superior steam cooking for breads and roasts
- Large capacity (fits a 14-pound turkey)
- Durable stainless steel construction
- Excellent temperature accuracy (±2°F)

**Cons:**
- Heavy (45 pounds) and requires dedicated counter space
- App interface can be sluggish
- No built-in camera (uses external probe only)

**Verdict:** Ideal for serious home bakers and cooks who demand professional-grade results. The HydroPro's steam capability sets it apart from competitors.

**Overview:** Samsung's entry into the intelligent oven market targets the built-in segment, offering a 30-inch wall oven that integrates seamlessly with SmartThings. **Pros:**

**Verdict:** Best for homeowners renovating their kitchen who want a built-in, future-proof appliance. The Samsung Smart Oven excels in a Samsung-centric smart home.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Considerations

- **Countertop Models (June, Breville):** Simply place on a heat-resistant surface, plug into a standard 120V outlet, and connect to Wi-Fi. Ensure at least 4 inches of clearance on all sides for ventilation.
- **Built-In Models (Samsung):** Hire a licensed electrician to install a 240V outlet (if not existing) and ensure proper cabinet dimensions (30-inch width, 27-inch depth). Ventilation may require a dedicated circuit.

### Smart Home Integration

All three ovens support major platforms, but here's how they excel:

- **June 2026 Edition:** Works flawlessly with Apple HomeKit for Siri voice control and automations (e.g., "Hey Siri, preheat the oven to 350°F"). Also compatible with Alexa and Google.
- **Breville HydroPro:** Supports IFTTT for custom routines, such as "When I leave work, preheat oven to 425°F." No native HomeKit support, but works via app.
- **Samsung Smart Oven:** Deep SmartThings integration allows you to monitor and control the oven from your Samsung refrigerator or TV. Voice control via Bixby is limited, but Alexa and Google work well.

**Pro Tip:** For a fully automated kitchen, pair your intelligent oven with a smart smoke detector (e.g., Nest Protect) and a smart plug for the exhaust fan. Set up routines like "When oven is on, turn on kitchen lights" for safety and convenience.
