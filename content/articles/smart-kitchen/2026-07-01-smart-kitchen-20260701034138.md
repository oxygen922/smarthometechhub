## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy What?

- **Tech Enthusiasts:** The Samsung Bespoke Refrigerator is a no-brainer. Its AI capabilities and large touchscreen make it a true smart home hub. Pair it with a compatible smart speaker for voice control.

- **Busy Professionals:** The June Oven is ideal. It takes the guesswork out of cooking, and its compact size fits apartment kitchens. If you frequently eat frozen meals or meal prep, this is your appliance.

- **Budget-Conscious Shoppers:** The Drop Smart Scale offers the most bang for your buck. It’s perfect for bakers, dieters, or anyone who wants to follow recipes precisely. Combine it with a basic smart plug for added automation.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $100:** Smart scales, smart plugs, and basic smart thermometers. These are great for testing the waters.
- **$100–$500:** Smart kettles, coffee makers, and air fryers with app connectivity. Look for devices with Wi-Fi and voice assistant support.
- **$500–$1,500:** Countertop ovens (like the June), smart slow cookers, and mid-range refrigerators with basic smart features.
- **$1,500+:** Premium refrigerators, full-size smart ovens, and integrated kitchen systems. Expect AI capabilities and high-end build quality.

### Pro Tip: Start Small

Don’t feel pressured to buy everything at once. Start with a smart scale or a Wi-Fi-enabled coffee maker. Use it for a month, then assess whether you want deeper integration. Many users find that a single smart appliance is enough to improve their daily routine.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Basics

- **Refrigerators:** Professional installation is recommended for built-in models. Ensure your home has a dedicated water line for ice makers. Most smart refrigerators require a strong Wi-Fi signal—consider a mesh network if your kitchen is far from the router.

- **Countertop Ovens:** The June Oven is plug-and-play. Place it on a heat-resistant surface, connect to Wi-Fi via the app, and you’re ready. No special wiring needed.

- **Scales and Small Appliances:** These are easy to set up. Download the companion app, pair via Bluetooth, and follow the on-screen instructions. Most take less than 10 minutes.

### Smart Home Integration

To get the most out of your smart kitchen, consider these integration strategies:

1. **Voice Control:** Use an Amazon Echo or Google Nest Hub to control appliances hands-free. For example, “Alexa, preheat the June oven to 350°F” or “Hey Google, add milk to my shopping list on the Samsung fridge.”

2. **Automations:** Create routines using IFTTT or Apple Shortcuts. Example: When you leave for work, the smart coffee maker starts brewing, and the refrigerator goes into energy-saving mode.

3. **Multi-Device Sync:** If you have a smart display (like the Echo Show 15), use it to view your refrigerator’s camera feed or follow a recipe from the Drop app. Some devices, like the Samsung Family Hub, can even control smart lights and thermostats.

4. **Security Considerations:** Always keep your appliances’ firmware updated. Use strong, unique passwords for each device. Avoid connecting smart kitchen appliances to public Wi-Fi networks.

### Common Pitfalls to Avoid

- **Over-reliance on Apps:** Some appliances require the app for basic functions. Ensure you’re comfortable with that trade-off.
- **Poor Wi-Fi Coverage:** If your kitchen has weak Wi-Fi, invest in a mesh system before buying smart appliances.
- **Ignoring Privacy:** Smart cameras and microphones in appliances can be privacy concerns. Read the privacy policy and disable features you don’t need.
