# The Ultimate Guide to Smart Ovens: Top 2026 Picks for a Connected Kitchen

## Introduction

The modern kitchen is no longer just a place for meal prep—it's the command center of the smart home. Among the most transformative appliances to emerge in recent years is the intelligent oven. These Wi-Fi-enabled, app-controlled marvels go far beyond simple baking and roasting. They learn your cooking habits, suggest recipes based on what's in your fridge, and even allow you to preheat on your commute home. As we settle into 2026, the market for smart ovens has matured dramatically, with models that combine precision cooking, voice assistant integration, and energy efficiency. Whether you're a busy professional, a tech enthusiast, or a home chef looking to elevate your culinary game, a smart oven can save time, reduce waste, and impress dinner guests. In this in-depth review, we'll explore the top three intelligent ovens available today, break down key features to consider, and help you decide which model fits your lifestyle and budget. Let's fire up the future of cooking.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation

**Countertop Models (June, Breville):**
- **Ease:** Very easy. Simply place on a countertop near a standard 120V outlet. Ensure at least 4 inches of clearance on all sides for ventilation.
- **Time:** 5 minutes (unbox, plug in, connect to Wi-Fi).
- **Requirements:** A flat, heat-resistant surface. Avoid placing near a sink or stove.

**Built-in Model (GE Profile):**
- **Ease:** Moderate to difficult. Requires professional installation due to electrical (240V) or gas connections. You'll also need a 30-inch cabinet cutout.
- **Time:** 1-2 hours for a professional installer.
- **Requirements:** A dedicated 240V circuit (for electric models) or a gas line (for gas models). Consult a licensed electrician or plumber.

### Smart Home Integration

- **June Intelligent Oven:** Seamlessly integrates with Alexa and Google Assistant. You can create routines like "Alexa, good morning" to preheat the oven for breakfast. Also works with IFTTT for advanced automation (e.g., turn off oven when you leave home).

- **GE Profile Smart Oven:** Works with Alexa, Google Assistant, and the GE Appliances app. Supports routines like "Alexa, dinner time" to preheat and set a timer. Also integrates with Samsung SmartThings for broader home automation.

- **Breville Smart Oven Air Fryer Pro:** No native smart features, but you can use a smart plug (e.g., Kasa or Wemo) for basic on/off control. Note: Smart plugs can't control cooking modes, only power. For full smart integration, consider the June or GE Profile.

**Tip:** If you're building a smart home ecosystem, choose an oven that matches your primary voice assistant. Alexa users will find the most compatibility with June and GE, while Google Home users will enjoy June's deeper integration.
