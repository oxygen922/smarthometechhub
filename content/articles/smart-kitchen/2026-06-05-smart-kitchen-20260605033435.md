### Other Key Considerations

- **Connectivity:** Ensure the oven supports your preferred smart home ecosystem. Most modern ovens work with Alexa and Google Assistant, but compatibility with Apple HomeKit is less common.
- **App Quality:** A well-designed app enhances the user experience. Look for apps with recipe libraries, remote monitoring, and push notifications.
- **Energy Efficiency:** Smart ovens often use less energy than traditional ovens due to faster preheating and more efficient cooking cycles. Check for Energy Star certification.
- **Safety Features:** Automatic shut-off, cool-touch exteriors, and child locks are important for households with children or pets.

### Installation & Smart Home Integration

**Installation:**

All three ovens are countertop models requiring no permanent installation. Simply place them on a heat-resistant surface with adequate ventilation. Ensure there's at least 6 inches of clearance above and behind the oven for airflow. The Breville, being larger, may need dedicated counter space. No electrical work is needed—they plug into standard 120V outlets. Initial setup involves connecting to Wi-Fi via the respective app, which takes about 10 minutes.

**Smart Home Integration:**

- **Voice Control:** All models support Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant. Common voice commands include "Alexa, preheat the oven to 350°F" or "Hey Google, set the oven to air fry mode."
- **Automation:** Use routines to trigger the oven based on other smart devices. For example, "When I arrive home, preheat the oven" using geofencing.
- **IFTTT Integration:** The June and Breville support IFTTT applets for advanced automation, such as sending a notification when the oven cycle is complete.
- **Third-Party Compatibility:** The Tovala app integrates with Samsung SmartThings, while Breville offers limited integration with Home Assistant for advanced users.
