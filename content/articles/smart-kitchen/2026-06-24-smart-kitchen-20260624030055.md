**Best for:** Tech-savvy home cooks who want versatility

The June Oven Pro 2026 is the Swiss Army knife of smart ovens. It combines a convection oven, air fryer, dehydrator, slow cooker, and toaster into one sleek countertop unit. During testing, the June Oven Pro nailed a perfectly roasted chicken with crispy skin and juicy meat—no manual adjustments needed. The 12-inch touchscreen display is intuitive, and the oven preheats in under 3 minutes. **Pros:**

Samsung’s 2026 flagship wall oven is a beast of precision engineering. **Pros:**

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Requirements

- **Countertop (June Oven Pro):** No installation needed. Just plug into a standard 120V outlet. Leave 4 inches of clearance on all sides for ventilation.
- **Wall Oven (Samsung AI Pro):** Requires a 27-inch or 30-inch cutout. Must be hardwired to a 240V circuit. Hire a licensed electrician. Expect 2-4 hours for installation.
- **Freestanding Range (GE Profile):** Standard 30-inch drop-in. Requires a 240V outlet (most homes have one). DIY-friendly if you’re comfortable with electrical work.

### Smart Home Integration

All three models support voice assistants, but the experience varies:

- **Alexa/Google Assistant:** Works with all three. Commands like “Alexa, preheat the oven to 350°F” are reliable. June also supports “Alexa, cook a frozen pizza.”
- **Samsung SmartThings:** The Samsung oven integrates deeply—you can see oven status on your TV or fridge, and create automations like “Turn off oven when I leave home.”
- **IFTTT (June only):** Create custom applets. Example: “If my Fitbit detects I’m home, preheat oven to 425°F.”
- **Apple HomeKit:** None of these models natively support HomeKit, but you can bridge via Homebridge or a smart plug (for countertop models).

**Pro Tip:** For the best experience, ensure your Wi-Fi router is within 30 feet of the oven. 5 GHz networks are faster but have shorter range; 2.4 GHz is more reliable for appliances.
