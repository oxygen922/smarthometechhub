## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Considerations

**Countertop Models (June, Breville):** These are plug-and-play. Simply place the oven on a heat-resistant surface, plug it into a standard 120V outlet, and download the app. Ensure there's adequate ventilation—at least 4 inches of clearance on all sides.

**Built-in Models (Samsung):** Professional installation is strongly recommended. You'll need a dedicated 240V circuit (typically 30-40 amps). The oven must be properly vented according to local building codes. Most manufacturers offer professional installation services for an additional fee, or you can hire a licensed electrician.

### Smart Home Integration Tips

1. **Wi-Fi Requirements:** All smart ovens require a stable 2.4GHz Wi-Fi network. 5GHz networks may cause connectivity issues. Consider a mesh network if your kitchen is far from your router.

2. **Voice Assistant Setup:** For seamless voice control, ensure your smart speaker (Echo, Nest, or HomePod) is in the same room as the oven. Group the oven with other kitchen devices in your smart home app for routines like "Good Morning" that preheat the oven.

3. **Automation Ideas:**
   - Create a routine that preheats the oven when you leave work (using location-based triggers).
   - Set a "Cooking Mode" that dims lights and plays music when the oven starts.
   - Use IFTTT (If This Then That) applets for advanced automation, like sending a notification when the oven reaches temperature.

4. **Ecosystem Lock-In:** Be aware that Samsung's full features require a Samsung account and SmartThings hub. Similarly, the June Pro works best within the Apple HomeKit ecosystem if you're an Apple user. The Breville is more platform-agnostic but offers fewer advanced features.

5. **Security:** Keep your oven's firmware updated to patch vulnerabilities. Use strong, unique passwords for your smart home accounts. Disable remote access when not needed.
