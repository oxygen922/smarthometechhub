# The Best Intelligent Ovens of 2026: A Complete Smart Kitchen Review

## Introduction

The smart kitchen revolution has arrived, and intelligent ovens are leading the charge. Gone are the days when an oven simply heated food—today's models use AI-powered cooking algorithms, built-in cameras, and app-based remote control to deliver restaurant-quality results from your own kitchen. Whether you're a busy professional who wants dinner ready when you walk through the door or a home chef eager to experiment with precision cooking, an intelligent oven can transform how you prepare meals.

In 2026, the market has matured dramatically. Major brands like June, Samsung, and GE Profile now offer ovens that recognize dishes automatically, suggest recipes based on what's inside, and integrate seamlessly with platforms like Alexa, Google Home, and Apple HomeKit. Prices range from around $600 for entry-level smart models to over $6,000 for professional-grade intelligent ranges.

In this comprehensive review, we'll break down the top three intelligent ovens worth your money this year, explain the features that actually matter, and help you decide which model fits your lifestyle and budget. Let's dive in.

## Top 3 Product Recommendations

### 1. June Intelligent Oven (2026 Edition) — Best Overall Smart Countertop Oven

**Price Estimate:** $1,799

The June Intelligent Oven has long been the benchmark for countertop smart ovens, and the 2026 Edition pushes the category even further. This third-generation model features a 12-inch touchscreen, a redesigned carbon-fiber heating system, and an upgraded AI camera that identifies over 200 food types with impressive accuracy.

**What We Loved:**

The standout feature is the Food ID system. Place a salmon fillet or a frozen pizza inside, and the oven automatically recognizes it, selects the ideal cooking profile, and starts the timer—no guesswork required. In our testing, the June nailed a medium-rare steak and a perfectly crisped Margherita pizza within minutes of each other, something traditional ovens simply can't match.

The new 2026 Edition also adds **Air Fry mode**, a welcome addition given how popular air frying has become. The oven circulates superheated air at up to 500°F, producing fries and wings with a satisfying crunch and minimal oil. The companion app (iOS and Android) lets you monitor cooking via live video feed, adjust temperature, and receive push notifications when your food is ready.

**What Could Be Better:**

At $1,799, it's a significant investment for a countertop appliance. The interior capacity (roughly 0.5 cubic feet) is also limited—it fits a 12-inch pizza or a small roast, but not a full turkey. And while the AI is impressive, it occasionally misidentifies leftovers, requiring manual correction.

**Verdict:** If you want the smartest, most intuitive countertop oven available in 2026, the June Intelligent Oven is the one to beat. It's ideal for small households, tech enthusiasts, and anyone who values convenience over sheer capacity.

### 2. Samsung Bespoke AI Oven with SmartThings — Best Full-Size Smart Range

**Price Estimate:** $3,299

For homeowners who want intelligence built into a full-size kitchen range, Samsung's Bespoke AI Oven is a compelling option. This 30-inch slide-in electric range combines a sleek, customizable design with Samsung's most advanced cooking AI to date.

**What We Loved:**

The centerpiece is the **AI Pro Cooking** system, which uses a built-in camera and sensors to recognize up to 80 different dishes and automatically set the optimal temperature and time. It even sends a notification to your phone when your food is nearly done—or when it detects burning. During our tests, the oven correctly identified a tray of roasted vegetables and a sheet-pan chicken dinner, adjusting cook times with surprising precision.

Integration with **SmartThings** is seamless. You can preheat the oven from your car, sync cooking modes with Samsung's Family Hub refrigerator, and receive voice alerts through Alexa or Bixby. The range also supports **SmartThings Energy**, which monitors power consumption and can shift cooking times to off-peak hours to save on electricity bills.

The Bespoke line's customizable color panels (available in white, navy, and stainless) let you match the range to your kitchen aesthetic—a nice touch that competitors often overlook.

**What Could Be Better:**

The AI works best with Samsung's own recipe library, and third-party recipe integration is still limited. The touchscreen interface, while bright, can be sluggish at times. And at $3,299, it's priced at a premium compared to non-smart ranges with similar cooking performance.

**Verdict:** The Samsung Bespoke AI Oven is the best choice for families who want a full-size range with genuine smart-home integration. If you're already invested in the Samsung ecosystem, it's a no-brainer.

### 3. GE Profile Smart Oven with Air Fry — Best Mid-Range Intelligent Wall Oven

**Price Estimate:** $2,499

GE Profile's Smart Oven with Air Fry hits a sweet spot between price and performance. This 27-inch wall oven offers many of the smart features found in higher-end models without the flagship price tag.

**What We Loved:**

The **Chef Connect** system uses a built-in temperature probe and app-based guidance to walk you through cooking proteins to your desired doneness. The oven also includes **No-Preheat Air Fry** mode, which uses a dedicated heating element and fan to crisp food without the wait. In our tests, frozen chicken nuggets came out golden and crunchy in under 15 minutes.

The **Smart HQ app** is one of the more polished smart-oven apps we've used. It supports remote preheat, temperature adjustment, and notifications, and it works reliably with both Alexa and Google Assistant. GE has also added **Scan-to-Cook** technology, which lets you scan the barcode of packaged foods and automatically sends the correct cooking instructions to the oven.

**What Could Be Better:**

The oven lacks the AI food-recognition camera found on the June and Samsung models, so you'll still need to select cooking modes manually. The app, while reliable, doesn't offer live video monitoring. And the wall-oven form factor requires professional installation, which adds to the total cost.

**Verdict:** The GE Profile Smart Oven is the best value for homeowners who want smart features and solid cooking performance without paying flagship prices. It's a great fit for remodelers and anyone upgrading from a basic oven.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy an Intelligent Oven?

**Tech enthusiasts and early adopters:** If you love automating your home and want the latest cooking tech, a smart oven is a natural addition. The June Intelligent Oven, in particular, feels like a glimpse into the future of cooking.

**Busy families:** A full-size smart range like the Samsung Bespoke can streamline meal prep, especially if you're already using SmartThings or Alexa. The ability to preheat remotely and get notified when dinner is ready is a genuine time-saver.

**Home cooks who value precision:** Features like temperature probes, AI-assisted cooking, and recipe integration help eliminate guesswork, making it easier to achieve consistent results.

**Who should skip it?** If you're happy with your current oven and don't care about app control or AI features, there's no urgent need to upgrade. A traditional oven with a good thermometer will still cook excellent food.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $1,000:** You'll find basic smart ovens with Wi-Fi and app control, but limited AI features. Brands like Cosori and Instant Pot offer affordable countertop options.
- **$1,500–$2,500:** This is the sweet spot for feature-rich countertop ovens and mid-range wall ovens. The June and GE Profile models fall here.
- **$3,000+:** Flagship full-size ranges with advanced AI, cameras, and premium finishes. Expect to pay for installation and possibly electrical upgrades.

**Pro Tip:** Check for utility rebates. Some states offer incentives for energy-efficient smart appliances, which can offset the cost by $50–$200.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Countertop Ovens

The June Intelligent Oven requires no installation—just plug it into a standard 120V outlet and connect it to your Wi-Fi network via the app. Setup takes about 10 minutes. It supports **Matter**, the universal smart home standard, which means it can be controlled through Apple Home, Google Home, and Alexa without extra hubs.

### Wall Ovens and Ranges

Full-size smart ovens like the Samsung Bespoke and GE Profile typically require professional installation. Most need a 240V outlet and may require cabinet modifications if you're replacing an older unit. Expect to pay $150–$400 for installation, depending on your location and the complexity of the job.

**Smart Home Integration Tips:**

- **Use routines:** Set up a "Good Morning" routine that preheats your oven while you make coffee.
- **Voice control:** Both Alexa and Google Assistant can handle commands like "Preheat the oven to 375 degrees."
- **Energy monitoring:** If your utility offers time-of-use rates, use your oven's scheduling features to cook during off-peak hours.
- **Firmware updates:** Keep your oven's software updated to access new recipes and features. Most brands push updates automatically.
