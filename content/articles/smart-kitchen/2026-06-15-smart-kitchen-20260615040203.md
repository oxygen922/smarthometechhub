## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy Smart Kitchen Appliances?

**Tech Enthusiasts:** If you love having the latest gadgets and enjoy controlling your home from your phone, smart kitchen appliances are a natural fit. The ability to preheat your oven on your commute home or check your fridge’s contents from the grocery store is genuinely useful.

**Busy Professionals:** Time is your most valuable commodity. Smart ovens that cook automatically and refrigerators that track inventory can save you hours each week. The June Oven, for example, can turn a frozen meal into a gourmet dinner with zero effort.

**Health-Conscious Cooks:** Appliances like the Anova Precision Oven make healthy cooking easier. Steam cooking preserves nutrients, air frying reduces oil, and sous vide ensures perfectly cooked proteins without added fats.

**Families:** A smart refrigerator with a family hub can be the center of your household’s organization. Shared calendars, meal planning, and grocery lists keep everyone on the same page.

### Budget Considerations

**Entry-Level ($100–$300):** Focus on smart kitchen scales, smart plugs for existing appliances, and basic connected slow cookers. These provide a taste of smart kitchen functionality without a major investment.

**Mid-Range ($300–$800):** This is the sweet spot for countertop appliances like the June Oven or Anova Precision Oven. You get genuine automation and excellent cooking performance. Also consider smart coffee makers and air fryers in this range.

**Premium ($800–$4,000+):** Full-size smart refrigerators, ranges, and dishwashers fall here. The Samsung Bespoke refrigerator is the standout, but brands like GE, LG, and Bosch also offer excellent options. Expect professional-grade performance and deep smart home integration.

**Hidden Costs:** Remember to factor in installation, potential electrical upgrades, and extended warranties. Some smart appliances require a dedicated 240V outlet or plumbing modifications.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Considerations

**Refrigerators:** Most smart refrigerators require standard 120V outlets and proper ventilation space. The Samsung Bespoke series offers modular panels that can be customized, but installation is best handled by professionals due to weight and leveling requirements.

**Countertop Ovens:** The June and Anova ovens are plug-and-play. Just place them on a heat-resistant surface with adequate clearance (at least 4 inches on all sides). They require a standard 120V outlet and should not be used on the same circuit as other high-wattage appliances.

**Network Requirements:** All smart appliances require a stable 2.4GHz Wi-Fi network. Some newer models support 5GHz, but 2.4GHz offers better range and penetration through walls. Ensure your router is within 30–50 feet of your kitchen.

### Smart Home Integration

**Voice Assistants:** The most seamless integration comes from choosing appliances that work with your existing ecosystem. Amazon Alexa users will find the widest compatibility, while Google Assistant offers excellent integration with Android devices. Apple HomeKit support is growing but remains less common.

**Automation Scenes:** Create routines that enhance your cooking experience. For example:
- “Good morning” routine: Coffee maker starts, oven preheats to 350°F, refrigerator displays weather and calendar.
- “Date night” scene: Oven sets to sous vide mode, smart lights dim, music playlist starts.

**IFTTT and Third-Party Integration:** Many smart appliances support IFTTT (If This Then That) for advanced automation. You can create triggers like “If refrigerator detects low milk, add to shopping list” or “If oven is preheated, send notification to phone.”

**Troubleshooting Common Issues:**
- **Wi-Fi drops:** Use a mesh network system for better coverage in larger kitchens.
- **App connectivity problems:** Ensure firmware is up to date and restart both the appliance and router.
- **Voice command failures:** Check that the appliance is properly linked to your voice assistant account and that you’re using correct wake words.
