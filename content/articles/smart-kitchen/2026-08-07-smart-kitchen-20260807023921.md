## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Considerations

**Refrigerators:** The Samsung Family Hub+ requires standard 36-inch width space and a water line for ice and water features. Professional installation is recommended due to weight and leveling requirements.

**Cooktops:** Induction cooktops typically require a 240-volt electrical circuit. Most homes built after 2000 have this available, but older homes may need an electrician to install a dedicated circuit — budget $300–$800 for this upgrade.

**Countertop Ovens:** These require zero installation beyond standard counter space and outlet access. The June Oven Pro needs approximately 18 inches of clearance above for ventilation.

### Smart Home Ecosystem Integration

**Amazon Alexa:** Most smart appliances offer native Alexa integration. You can use voice commands like "Alexa, preheat the June Oven to 400 degrees" or "Alexa, check what's in the Samsung fridge."

**Google Home:** Google Assistant integration is equally robust, with the advantage of better natural language processing for complex commands. The GE Cooktop works particularly well with Google Home's routines.

**Apple HomeKit:** Historically limited in the appliance space, HomeKit support is expanding. The 2026 June Oven Pro includes native HomeKit support, and Samsung has announced Matter protocol support for all 2026 appliances, ensuring cross-platform compatibility.

**Matter Protocol:** This emerging standard is the most significant development in smart home integration. All major manufacturers now support Matter, meaning your Samsung fridge can communicate with your Google Nest Hub and Apple TV seamlessly. Look for the Matter logo when shopping to ensure future-proof compatibility.

**Pro Tip:** Start with appliances that support Matter protocol to ensure maximum compatibility as your smart home ecosystem grows.
