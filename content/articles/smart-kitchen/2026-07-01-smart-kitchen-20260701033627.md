- **Busy Families:** The LG InstaView Refrigerator is ideal for parents who want to reduce food waste and streamline grocery shopping. The touchscreen can display family calendars and recipes.

- **Mid-Range ($500–$2,000):** Smart ovens, refrigerators, and dishwashers with Wi-Fi connectivity and basic AI features.
- **Premium ($2,000–$5,000+):** High-end appliances with advanced AI, computer vision, and full ecosystem integration.

**Pro Tip:** Smart appliances can save you money in the long run through energy efficiency and reduced food waste. The LG InstaView, for example, can extend produce life by up to two weeks, potentially saving $50–$100 per month on groceries.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Process

- **Ovens and Refrigerators:** Professional installation is recommended for built-in models. Most retailers offer installation services for $150–$300. Ensure your kitchen has a dedicated 20-amp circuit for ovens and proper ventilation for refrigerators.
- **Sous Vide and Countertop Appliances:** These are plug-and-play. Just download the app, connect to Wi-Fi, and follow the on-screen setup. Expect 10–15 minutes for initial configuration.

### Smart Home Integration

All three recommended products support the Matter protocol, ensuring compatibility with major smart home platforms. Here's how they integrate:

- **Samsung Bespoke AI Oven:** Works with Samsung SmartThings, Amazon Alexa, Google Assistant, and Apple HomeKit (via Matter). You can create routines like "Good Morning" that preheat the oven to 350°F while turning on kitchen lights.
- **LG InstaView Refrigerator:** Integrates with LG ThinQ, Amazon Alexa, Google Assistant, and IFTTT. You can set up an automation that sends a shopping list to your phone when the milk carton is empty.
- **Breville HydroPro Sous Vide:** Connects via Wi-Fi and works with Alexa, Google Assistant, and IFTTT. A useful routine: "Cook Dinner" starts the sous vide and sets a timer for 2 hours.

**Troubleshooting Tip:** If your appliance loses Wi-Fi connection, try rebooting your router and the appliance. For persistent issues, ensure your router is using a 2.4 GHz band (many smart appliances don't support 5 GHz). Most manufacturers offer 24/7 support via chat or phone.
