## Installation & Smart Home Integration

Installing smart kitchen appliances is generally straightforward, but there are a few considerations:

**Wi-Fi and connectivity:** Most smart appliances require a 2.4GHz Wi-Fi network. Ensure your router is positioned for strong coverage in the kitchen. For refrigerators with large screens, a 5GHz connection may be preferable for streaming.

**App setup:** Download the manufacturer's app (June, SmartThings, Instant Pot) and follow the in-app instructions. You'll typically create an account, connect to Wi-Fi, and pair the appliance.

**Voice assistant integration:** Link your appliance to Alexa or Google Assistant through the respective app. Once connected, you can use voice commands like "Alexa, preheat the June Oven to 400 degrees."

**Smart home ecosystem:** If you use SmartThings, Apple HomeKit, or Amazon Alexa as your central hub, check compatibility before purchasing. The Samsung Family Hub works best within SmartThings, while the June Oven supports Matter for broad compatibility.

**Automation ideas:**
- Set your smart lights to turn on when the oven preheats.
- Use IFTTT to send a notification when the fridge door is left open.
- Schedule your coffee maker to start brewing when your alarm goes off.

**Troubleshooting:** If your appliance won't connect, try restarting your router, ensuring the appliance is within range, and checking for firmware updates. Most manufacturers offer responsive customer support.
