# The Best Smart Ovens of 2026: A Complete Buyer's Review

## Introduction

The intelligent oven has quietly become one of the most useful upgrades in the modern American kitchen. Unlike the early "smart" appliances that simply added a Wi-Fi chip and a phone app, today's intelligent ovens combine thermal cameras, food probes, AI-driven cooking algorithms, and voice assistants to genuinely take the guesswork out of roasting, baking, and reheating. Whether you're a busy parent juggling dinner and homework or a home cook chasing restaurant-quality results, a smart oven can reduce burned meals, cut energy waste, and learn your preferences over time.

In 2026, the market has matured significantly. Prices have dropped, app ecosystems are more reliable, and features like air frying, steam assist, and automatic doneness detection are no longer luxury exclusives. In this in-depth smart oven review, we break down the top three intelligent ovens worth buying this year, walk through the key features to consider, and help you decide which model fits your kitchen, your budget, and your smart home setup. Let's dig in.

You place the food inside, confirm what the oven "sees" on its touchscreen, and press start.

What sets the 2026 June apart is its improved AI cooking engine. In our testing, it nailed roasted vegetables and chicken thighs with minimal input, and the new multi-zone temperature sensors reduced hot spots noticeably compared to prior generations. The integrated food probe is wireless, and the app now pushes real-time doneness notifications with a "carryover cooking" estimate so you can pull food at exactly the right moment.

**Performance highlights:**

- Air fry, dehydrate, broil, roast, bake, and reheat modes

- Responsive touchscreen plus full app and voice control

**Drawbacks:** The interior is small — it comfortably fits a 9x13 pan but not a full turkey — and the premium price puts it firmly in the luxury tier. Replacement accessories are also pricey.

**Estimated 2026 price:** $1,299–$1,499

**Verdict:** If you want the smartest countertop oven money can buy and mostly cook for one to four people, the June is the best intelligent oven on the market.

For buyers who want intelligence baked into a full-size range rather than a countertop appliance, the Samsung Bespoke AI Oven is the standout choice of 2026. During testing, the camera-based monitoring was genuinely useful for long bakes — we could check browning from the couch without opening the door and losing heat.

## Key Features to Consider

Before you buy an intelligent oven, it helps to understand what "smart" actually means across models. Here are the features that matter most in 2026.

**1. Computer vision and food recognition.** Cameras and AI can identify food and auto-select cooking modes. This is the most advanced tier of smart cooking, but accuracy varies and the feature is concentrated in premium models.

**2. Food probe and guided cooking.** A wireless or wired probe paired with app guidance is arguably the most consistently useful smart feature. It helps you hit perfect doneness on meats and large dishes.

**3. App ecosystem and voice assistants.** Check compatibility with Alexa, Google Assistant, Apple Home, and SmartThings. Ecosystem fit matters more than the number of features on a spec sheet.

**4. Cooking modes.** Air fry, steam, dehydrate, broil, and no-preheat modes add real versatility. Air fry in particular has become a near-universal expectation.

**5. Form factor.** Countertop intelligent ovens (like the June) suit small households and quick meals. Full-size ranges and wall ovens suit families and serious cooks.

**6. Energy monitoring and scheduling.** Smart energy features let you schedule cooking during off-peak hours and track consumption — a nice bonus for efficiency-minded buyers.

## Buying Guide

### Who should buy an intelligent oven?

**Busy professionals and small households** benefit most from countertop intelligent ovens. If you cook for one or two people and value speed, the June-style oven's quick preheat and auto-cooking modes are transformative.

**Families** are better served by full-size smart ranges like the Samsung Bespoke AI Oven. The larger capacity, camera monitoring, and recipe guidance help manage bigger meals, and SmartThings integration fits neatly into an existing connected home.

**Practical upgraders** who want connectivity without AI gimmicks should look at the GE Profile. It's the sensible middle ground: guided cooking, air frying, and voice control at a friendlier price.

### Budget considerations

Set your budget by form factor first. Countertop intelligent ovens generally run **$800–$1,500** in 2026, while full-size smart ranges and wall ovens span **$1,200–$3,000+** depending on finish and features. Remember to factor in:

- **Accessories:** Probes, air-fry baskets, and replacement parts can add $50–$200.
- **Installation:** Wall ovens may require professional installation ($150–$400).
- **Energy use:** Smart scheduling and energy monitoring can offset some operating costs over time.

A good rule of thumb: if you'll use AI cooking features weekly, the premium is worth it. If you mainly want app control and air frying, save your money and choose a mid-tier model.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

Installation complexity depends heavily on form factor. Countertop intelligent ovens like the June require nothing more than a standard 120V outlet and counter space — truly plug-and-play. Full-size ranges and wall ovens typically need a 240V connection and, for wall ovens, a cabinet cutout that matches the unit's dimensions. We strongly recommend professional installation for wall ovens to avoid fit and wiring issues.

On the smart home side, integration quality varies by ecosystem:

- **Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant** are the most broadly supported and work well across all three recommended models for voice commands like preheating or checking status.
- **Samsung SmartThings** is the deepest integration available, but it's largely limited to Samsung appliances.
- **Apple Home** support remains spotty in the oven category; check compatibility carefully if you're an Apple household.

For setup, plan for 15–30 minutes of app pairing and Wi-Fi configuration. Use a 2.4GHz network where possible for reliability, and enable notifications so you actually benefit from doneness alerts. Finally, keep firmware updated — smart ovens receive meaningful feature improvements over time, and staying current is the easiest way to protect your investment.
