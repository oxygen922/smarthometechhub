Child locks, auto-shutoff, and cool-touch exteriors are important safety considerations, especially for families.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Countertop Models
Countertop smart ovens like the June Pro X and Breville PolyScience require no professional installation. Simply place them on a sturdy countertop with adequate clearance on all sides for ventilation. Most models plug into standard 120-volt outlets, though it's wise to ensure the outlet is on a dedicated circuit to avoid tripping breakers during heavy use.

### Built-In Models
Built-in smart ovens like the GE Profile require professional installation. This typically costs between $200-$500, depending on your existing setup and whether any electrical work is needed. These ovens usually require a 240-volt connection, which may necessitate hiring a licensed electrician.

### Smart Home Setup Process

**Step 1: Connect to Wi-Fi**
Most smart ovens connect to your 2.4GHz Wi-Fi network through their companion app. Ensure your router is within range and that you have the network password handy.

**Step 2: Download the App**
Each manufacturer has its own companion app – June for June Ovens, SmartHQ for GE, and the Breville app for their models. Create an account and follow the in-app setup instructions.

**Step 3: Connect Voice Assistants**
For Alexa or Google Assistant integration, enable the appropriate skill or action in your voice assistant's app and link your oven account.

**Step 4: Configure Preferences**
Set your cooking preferences, dietary restrictions, and notification settings in the app. Some ovens allow you to create profiles for different family members.

**Step 5: Test and Learn**
Run a test cycle to ensure everything is working correctly. Many ovens offer guided tutorials for first-time users.

### Integration Tips
- Place your oven in a location with strong Wi-Fi signal for reliable connectivity
- Consider adding a smart plug to monitor energy usage
- Create automation routines like "preheat oven" when you start commuting home
- Use scene automation to coordinate oven, lighting, and music for entertaining
