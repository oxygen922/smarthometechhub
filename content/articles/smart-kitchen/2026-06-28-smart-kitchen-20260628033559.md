Whether you’re a busy professional craving perfectly roasted chicken without hovering over the stove, or a tech enthusiast who wants to voice-control dinner from the couch, today’s smart ovens deliver unprecedented convenience and consistency.

## Top 3 Product Recommendations with Detailed Reviews

### 1. June Intelligent Oven 2026 Edition – Best Overall

**Price:** $1,899 (estimated 2026 MSRP)

The June Intelligent Oven has been a pioneer in smart cooking since its debut, and the 2026 Edition refines the formula to near perfection. This countertop convection oven features a built-in 1080p camera with AI object recognition that can identify over 200 types of food, from salmon fillets to frozen pizza. Once it identifies what you’re cooking, it automatically selects the optimal cooking mode, temperature, and time.

**Performance:** During testing, the June oven produced flawlessly roasted chicken with crispy skin and juicy meat. The “Roast” mode uses a combination of top and bottom heating elements with a fan that circulates heat evenly. I also tested the “Air Fry” function, which delivered golden, perfectly crisp french fries in 18 minutes—no preheating required.

**Smart Features:** The companion app (iOS and Android) is intuitive. You can view a live video feed of your food, receive push notifications when it’s done, and adjust settings remotely. The oven integrates with Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant, so you can say, “Alexa, preheat the June oven to 375°F,” and it responds instantly. The 2026 model adds Matter support for broader smart home compatibility.

**What’s New:** The 2026 Edition introduces a “Probe Assist” mode that uses a wireless meat thermometer to monitor internal temperature and automatically adjusts cooking time. It also includes a steam-assist function for baking bread with a crispier crust.

**Pros:**
- Exceptional AI food recognition
- Excellent cooking consistency
- Wide range of cooking modes (bake, broil, air fry, dehydrate, slow cook)
- Large 1.0 cubic foot capacity (fits a 14-pound turkey)

**Cons:**
- Premium price point
- Camera can be obstructed by steam
- Requires Wi-Fi for full functionality

**Verdict:** The June Intelligent Oven is the gold standard for smart countertop cooking. It’s ideal for tech-savvy home cooks who want precision and convenience without sacrificing quality.

### 2. Samsung Smart Oven with AI Pro Cooking – Best for Large Families

**Price:** $2,499 (estimated 2026 MSRP for 30-inch model)

Samsung’s entry into the intelligent oven space leverages its expertise in smart home ecosystems. The Samsung Smart Oven with AI Pro Cooking is a full-size wall oven (available in 27-inch and 30-inch widths) that uses a combination of sensors and machine learning to optimize cooking results.

**Performance:** The oven features Samsung’s “AI Pro Cooking” system, which uses a camera and sensors to monitor food doneness and adjust temperature and time in real-time. I tested it with a whole chicken and a tray of chocolate chip cookies. The chicken came out perfectly moist, while the cookies were uniformly golden—no rotating required. The oven also includes a “Steam Cook” function that adds moisture for baking bread or roasting vegetables.

**Smart Features:** The Samsung SmartThings app is robust. You can preheat the oven from your phone, receive alerts when your food is ready, and even download new cooking modes via firmware updates. The oven supports Alexa, Google Assistant, and Bixby. It also integrates with Samsung’s Family Hub refrigerator, so you can send recipes directly from the fridge screen to the oven.

**What’s New:** The 2026 model introduces a “Meal Planner” feature that syncs with your calendar and suggests recipes based on your schedule. It also includes a “Self-Clean” mode that uses steam to break down grease, reducing cleaning time by 50%.

**Pros:**
- Large capacity (5.1 cubic feet)
- Excellent for baking and roasting
- Strong smart home integration (especially with Samsung ecosystem)
- Self-cleaning steam function

**Cons:**
- Expensive
- AI features can be slow to respond
- Requires Samsung account for full functionality

**Verdict:** The Samsung Smart Oven is perfect for families who already use Samsung appliances. Its large capacity and advanced sensors make it a workhorse for daily cooking.

### 3. Breville Joule Oven Air Fryer Pro – Best Value

**Price:** $599 (estimated 2026 MSRP)

Breville’s Joule Oven Air Fryer Pro brings smart features to a more accessible price point. This countertop model is a follow-up to the highly popular Joule sous vide, and it combines convection cooking, air frying, and smart controls in a compact package.

**Performance:** The Joule Oven uses “Element IQ” technology, which adjusts power to six heating elements based on the cooking mode. I tested it with frozen chicken wings (air fry mode) and a homemade pizza (bake mode). The wings were crispy and evenly cooked in 22 minutes, while the pizza had a perfectly crisp crust and melted cheese in 12 minutes. The oven preheats in under 3 minutes for most modes.

**Smart Features:** The Anova Culinary app (Breville’s parent company) is simple and effective. You can select from over 100 presets, adjust temperature and time, and receive notifications when cooking is complete. The oven supports Alexa and Google Assistant for voice control, though it lacks a built-in camera. It also includes a temperature probe for precise cooking.

**What’s New:** The 2026 Pro model adds a “Dehydrate” mode and improved insulation for better energy efficiency.

**Pros:**
- Affordable for a smart oven
- Fast preheating
- Excellent air frying performance
- Compact footprint (fits under standard cabinets)

**Cons:**
- No camera
- Smaller capacity (0.8 cubic feet)
- App could be more polished

**Verdict:** The Breville Joule Oven Air Fryer Pro is the best entry-level smart oven. It delivers impressive performance for the price and is ideal for couples or small families.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy Which Oven?

- **Tech Enthusiasts & Early Adopters:** The June Intelligent Oven is your best bet. Its camera, AI, and Matter support make it the most advanced smart oven available. If you love gadgets and want the latest tech, this is it.

- **Large Families & Serious Home Cooks:** The Samsung Smart Oven is designed for volume. If you frequently cook for 6+ people or host holiday dinners, its massive capacity and steam features will save you time and effort.

- **Budget-Conscious Shoppers & Small Households:** The Breville Joule Oven Air Fryer Pro is a no-brainer. It offers smart features without the premium price tag. Perfect for couples, singles, or anyone who wants an upgrade from a basic toaster oven.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $1,000:** Stick with the Breville Joule Oven Air Fryer Pro. It delivers 90% of the smart functionality at half the price of competitors.
- **$1,000–$2,000:** The June Intelligent Oven is the sweet spot. You get top-tier AI and cooking performance without the wall oven installation costs.
- **Over $2,000:** The Samsung Smart Oven is for those who want a full-size, built-in appliance. Factor in installation costs ($200–$500) if you’re replacing an existing oven.

### Installation & Smart Home Integration

- **Countertop Models (June, Breville):** No installation required. Just plug into a standard 120V outlet. Ensure your countertop can support the weight (June: 35 lbs; Breville: 28 lbs). For smart home integration, connect via Wi-Fi to your router (2.4 GHz recommended for stability).

- **Wall Oven (Samsung):** Professional installation is recommended. The 30-inch model requires a 240V outlet and a cutout that matches standard oven dimensions. If you’re building a new kitchen or renovating, plan for this in advance.

**Smart Home Tips:**
- Use a dedicated 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network for smart appliances to avoid interference.
- Enable “Do Not Disturb” on your phone during cooking to avoid constant notifications.
- For Matter-compatible devices (like June), ensure your smart home hub (Apple Home, Google Home, Amazon Echo) supports Matter for seamless control.
