# The Smart Kitchen Revolution: 2026’s Best Intelligent Ovens Reviewed

## Top 3 Product Recommendations with Detailed Reviews

### 1. June Oven 3rd Generation (2026 Edition)

**Overview:** The June Oven has been a trailblazer in the smart oven space since its inception, and the third-generation model cements its position as a premium choice. It’s a countertop convection oven with a 0.8 cubic foot capacity, designed for small to medium households. The standout feature is its built-in camera and AI that can recognize over 100 types of food, automatically adjusting cooking time and temperature.

**Performance:** During testing, the June Oven excelled at roasting vegetables, baking cookies, and air-frying chicken wings. The AI food recognition is remarkably accurate—place a raw chicken breast inside, and the oven identifies it, suggests a recipe, and sets the optimal cook cycle. The included temperature probe works flawlessly for meats, and the oven’s precision fan ensures even browning. The only downside is the capacity; it’s not suitable for large turkeys or multiple sheet pans.

**Smart Features:** The June app is intuitive, allowing you to monitor cooking via the camera, receive push notifications, and control the oven remotely. It integrates with Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant for voice commands like “Alexa, preheat June to 375 degrees.” The automatic shut-off and keep-warm functions add safety and convenience.

**Price (2026):** $899 (includes temperature probe and two baking sheets)

**Verdict:** Best for tech-savvy users who prioritize AI convenience and have limited counter space. Ideal for couples or small families.

### 2. GE Profile Smart Oven with Air Fry (Built-in, 30-Inch)

**Overview:** For those seeking a full-size built-in solution, the GE Profile Smart Oven is a powerhouse. This 30-inch electric wall oven features a 5.0 cubic foot capacity, true convection, and a dedicated air fry mode. Its smart capabilities are centered around the GE Kitchen Hub app, which offers recipe integration and remote control.

**Performance:** The oven heats up quickly, reaching 350°F in under 8 minutes. The air fry function produces crispy results without excess oil, and the true convection system circulates heat evenly across all racks. We tested roasting a whole chicken and baking a lasagna—both came out perfectly cooked. The self-cleaning steam cycle is a welcome upgrade, reducing manual scrubbing.

**Smart Features:** The oven supports Wi-Fi connectivity and works with Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant. You can preheat, set timers, and adjust temperature from your phone. The GE Profile app also includes a “Recipe Guided Cooking” feature that syncs with select recipes from the app, automatically adjusting settings. However, the camera feature is absent, which may disappoint some users.

**Price (2026):** $2,499 (installation not included)

**Verdict:** Best for homeowners remodeling their kitchen who want a professional-grade, full-size oven with smart functionality. Excellent for large families and frequent entertainers.

### 3. Brava Oven (2026 Model)

**Overview:** The Brava Oven takes a different approach by using light-based cooking technology (infrared and halogen) to cook food up to six times faster than conventional ovens. It’s a countertop model with a 0.6 cubic foot capacity, but it includes multiple cooking zones that can prepare different foods simultaneously without flavor transfer.

**Performance:** The Brava is a speed demon. A frozen pizza cooks in 8 minutes, and salmon fillets with asparagus are ready in 10 minutes. The touchscreen interface is responsive, and the oven comes with a library of pre-programmed recipes. The biggest limitation is the learning curve—you need to use specific metal pans (included) for optimal results, and the cooking zones require careful placement.

**Smart Features:** The Brava app is robust, offering over 1,000 guided recipes with step-by-step instructions. The oven connects to Wi-Fi and works with Amazon Alexa for basic commands. The built-in temperature sensor and automatic cook cycles reduce guesswork, but the lack of a camera means you can’t visually monitor progress remotely.

**Price (2026):** $1,295 (includes starter pack of pans and accessories)

**Verdict:** Best for busy individuals who value speed and convenience over capacity. Great for singles, couples, and small families who want restaurant-quality meals in minutes.

## Key Features to Consider (With Comparison Table)

When shopping for an intelligent oven, focus on these critical features:

- **Cooking Technology:** Convection vs. light-based vs. microwave combination. Convection is best for even baking, while light-based (like Brava) offers speed.
- **Capacity:** Countertop models typically range from 0.6 to 1.0 cubic feet, while built-ins offer 4.5 to 6.0 cubic feet.
- **AI and Camera:** Cameras allow remote monitoring and food recognition, while AI can suggest cooking settings.
- **App Integration:** Look for apps that offer recipe guidance, remote control, and notifications.
- **Voice Assistant Compatibility:** Most support Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant; some work with Apple HomeKit.
- **Additional Functions:** Air fry, dehydrate, proof, slow cook, and steam clean.
- **Energy Efficiency:** Look for Energy Star certification to save on electricity bills.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Requirements

- **Countertop Models (June, Brava):** Simply place on a heat-resistant surface within reach of a standard 120V outlet. Ensure at least 4 inches of clearance on all sides for ventilation. No professional installation needed.
- **Built-in Models (GE Profile):** Requires a 30-inch cutout in cabinetry, a dedicated 240V electrical circuit, and professional installation. Measure your existing space carefully—these ovens are not plug-and-play.

### Smart Home Integration

All three ovens support Wi-Fi (2.4 GHz and 5 GHz) and Bluetooth for initial setup. Here’s how they fit into smart home ecosystems:

- **Amazon Alexa:** Compatible with all three. Use voice commands like “Alexa, preheat the oven to 400 degrees” or “Alexa, start the air fry cycle.”
- **Google Assistant:** Works with June and GE Profile. Commands include “Hey Google, set oven timer for 20 minutes.”
- **Apple HomeKit:** Not natively supported by any of these models, but you can use third-party bridges like Homebridge for limited integration.
- **IFTTT (If This Then That):** June and GE Profile offer IFTTT applets for advanced automation, such as turning on the oven when you leave work.

Pro Tip: Ensure your Wi-Fi router is within range of the kitchen, as smart ovens require a stable connection for remote features.
