# The Ultimate Smart Oven Review: Revolutionizing Your Kitchen in 2026

## Introduction

The kitchen has long been the heart of the home, but in 2026, it's also becoming the brain of your smart living ecosystem. Among the most transformative appliances is the intelligent oven—a device that promises to combine culinary precision with AI-driven convenience. These ovens aren't just about baking and roasting; they're about learning your preferences, suggesting recipes based on available ingredients, and even adjusting cooking times based on real-time humidity and temperature sensors. As a smart home technology reviewer with over a decade of experience testing kitchen gadgets, I've spent the last month evaluating the top contenders in the smart oven market. From voice-controlled convection ovens to self-cleaning models with built-in cameras, the landscape has evolved dramatically. In this comprehensive review, I'll break down the top three products, key features to consider, and help you decide which intelligent oven deserves a spot on your countertop or in your wall. Whether you're a seasoned home chef or a busy parent looking for convenience, this guide will illuminate the best options for your smart kitchen in 2026.

## Top 3 Product Recommendations

### 1. **Breville Smart Oven Air Fryer Pro (Model BOV900BSS)**

**Price:** $399.95 (2026 estimate)
**Best for:** Versatile cooking with AI-assisted presets

The Breville Smart Oven Air Fryer Pro has long been a favorite among home cooks, and its 2026 iteration brings significant smart upgrades. This countertop oven uses Element IQ technology, which automatically adjusts power across six quartz heating elements to maintain precise temperatures. What sets it apart is the new "Smart Cook" feature: a built-in sensor detects the moisture content of your food and adjusts cooking time accordingly. For example, when reheating pizza, the oven recognizes if the crust is getting too crispy and reduces top heat. The 13 preset functions—including air fry, roast, bake, broil, dehydrate, and slow cook—are now voice-controllable via Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant. The 0.8 cubic foot capacity fits a 14-pound turkey or a 9x13 baking dish, making it suitable for families. I tested the air fry function with frozen french fries, and the result was perfectly golden and crispy in 18 minutes—without preheating. The included temperature probe allows for precise meat cooking, and the app provides real-time notifications when your dish is ready. The only downside is the lack of a built-in camera, which some competitors offer. However, for the price, this is the most versatile smart oven on the market.

### 2. **June Oven (2026 Edition)**

**Price:** $599.00 (2026 estimate)
**Best for:** AI-powered food recognition and guided cooking

The June Oven has been a pioneer in smart cooking since its inception, and the 2026 edition takes it to new heights. This countertop oven features a high-definition camera that automatically recognizes over 200 types of food—from chicken breasts to chocolate chip cookies—and selects the optimal cooking mode. The new "Adaptive Cook" algorithm learns from your preferences: if you like your steak medium-rare, it remembers and adjusts future cooks accordingly. The oven also includes a built-in scale, so you can weigh ingredients directly on the cooking surface. I was impressed by the "Meal Plan" feature: you input dietary restrictions (e.g., keto, vegan, gluten-free), and the oven suggests recipes and creates a shopping list. The 0.9 cubic foot capacity is slightly larger than the Breville, accommodating a 16-pound turkey. The June Oven also functions as an air fryer, dehydrator, and slow cooker. The companion app is intuitive, offering step-by-step video guides and push notifications when your food is ready. The only drawback is the price—at $599, it's a significant investment. But if you value convenience and precision, the June Oven is the smartest choice for tech-savvy cooks.

### 3. **GE Profile Smart Built-In Wall Oven (Model PT9800)**

**Price:** $2,499.00 (2026 estimate)
**Best for:** High-end built-in kitchens with advanced smart features

For those remodeling their kitchen, the GE Profile Smart Built-In Wall Oven is the gold standard. This 30-inch electric oven features a 7-inch touchscreen display that provides step-by-step cooking guidance, including video tutorials from professional chefs. The "Scan-to-Cook" feature allows you to scan the barcode of packaged foods, and the oven automatically sets the correct time and temperature. The oven also includes a temperature probe and a camera that lets you monitor your food remotely via the SmartHQ app. What makes this oven stand out is the "Chef Connect" feature: it syncs with compatible GE cooktops to automatically turn on the exhaust fan when the oven is in use. The True European Convection system ensures even baking across all three racks. I tested the self-cleaning function, which uses steam to loosen grime, and it worked flawlessly in under an hour. The oven also supports voice control via Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant. The price point is steep, but for a built-in appliance that integrates seamlessly with other smart home devices, the GE Profile is unmatched. It's best for homeowners who want a premium, integrated smart kitchen experience.

## Key Features to Consider

When shopping for a smart oven in 2026, consider these essential features:

- **AI Food Recognition:** Cameras and sensors that identify food and adjust cooking parameters automatically.
- **Voice Control:** Compatibility with Amazon Alexa, Google Assistant, or Apple HomeKit for hands-free operation.
- **App Connectivity:** Remote monitoring, recipe suggestions, and push notifications via smartphone.
- **Multi-Functionality:** Air frying, dehydrating, slow cooking, and proofing capabilities beyond standard baking.
- **Temperature Probe:** For precise meat cooking, especially for roasts and poultry.
- **Self-Cleaning:** Steam or pyrolytic cleaning systems that save time and effort.
- **Capacity:** Countertop models typically range from 0.5 to 1.0 cubic feet; built-in ovens offer larger capacities.
- **Energy Efficiency:** Look for Energy Star certification to reduce electricity costs.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy a Smart Oven?

- **Busy Professionals:** If you're short on time, the June Oven's AI food recognition and guided cooking can save you minutes per meal. The Breville's preset functions also simplify reheating and air frying.
- **Home Chefs:** For those who love cooking but want precision, the GE Profile's temperature probe and True European Convection ensure consistent results. The Breville's Element IQ is also excellent for baking enthusiasts.
- **Tech Enthusiasts:** If you enjoy integrating smart devices, the June Oven and GE Profile offer the most advanced connectivity, including recipe suggestions based on your dietary preferences.
- **Families:** The Breville's larger capacity (0.8 cu ft) can handle family-sized meals, while the GE Profile's built-in design is ideal for custom kitchens.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $500:** The Breville Smart Oven Air Fryer Pro offers the best value with versatile functions and voice control. It's a solid choice for most households.
- **$500–$800:** The June Oven is a premium countertop option with AI capabilities. If you cook frequently and want guided assistance, this is worth the investment.
- **Over $2,000:** The GE Profile Smart Wall Oven is for those building or renovating a kitchen. It offers the highest level of integration and professional-grade features.

### Installation & Smart Home Integration

**Countertop Models (Breville, June):** Installation is straightforward—simply place the oven on a heat-resistant surface, plug it into a standard 120V outlet, and connect to Wi-Fi via the companion app. Ensure at least 4 inches of clearance on all sides for proper ventilation. Both models support Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant for voice commands, such as "Alexa, preheat the oven to 350 degrees." The June Oven also integrates with IFTTT for custom automations (e.g., turning on the oven when you leave work).

**Built-In Model (GE Profile):** Professional installation is recommended. The oven requires a 240V outlet and a cutout that matches standard 30-inch wall oven dimensions. The SmartHQ app guides you through Wi-Fi setup and allows you to link the oven to other GE appliances, such as cooktops and refrigerators. For smart home integration, the GE Profile works with Amazon Alexa, Google Assistant, and Apple HomeKit (via a hub). You can create routines like "Good morning" to preheat the oven while you brew coffee.

**General Integration Tips:** Use a dedicated 2.4GHz Wi-Fi network for stable connectivity. Enable firmware updates to access new features. For advanced users, consider a smart home hub like Samsung SmartThings to automate cooking schedules.
