## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy an Intelligent Oven?

**Busy Professionals:** If your schedule leaves little time for meal planning and cooking, an intelligent oven's automation features can be transformative. The ability to place food in the oven and have it automatically cook to perfection eliminates guesswork and reduces meal preparation stress.

**Tech Enthusiasts:** If you've embraced smart home technology throughout your house, an intelligent oven represents the logical next step. Integration with existing smart home systems creates a cohesive experience, and the novelty of AI-powered cooking appeals to those who enjoy cutting-edge technology.

**Home Chefs:** For those who love cooking but want to expand their skills, intelligent ovens offer guided experiences that can teach new techniques. The precision control and consistent results help develop confidence with challenging recipes.

**Families:** The larger capacity of wall oven configurations makes intelligent ovens practical for families. Features like remote monitoring let you keep an eye on dinner while helping with homework or managing other tasks.

### Who Should Wait?

**Budget-Conscious Buyers:** Intelligent ovens carry a significant premium over traditional models. If budget is a primary concern, a quality conventional oven with a separate smart thermometer may deliver 80% of the benefits at 40% of the cost.

**Minimalist Kitchens:** Countertop intelligent ovens require dedicated counter space, which may not be available in smaller kitchens. Wall oven configurations require professional installation and significant renovation.

**Cooking Purists:** If you prefer the tactile experience of traditional cooking and find technology distracting in the kitchen, an intelligent oven's features may feel like unnecessary complexity.

### Budget Considerations

Intelligent ovens range from approximately $1,000 for basic models to over $4,000 for premium wall ovens with all features. Consider these factors when budgeting:

- **Countertop vs. Wall Oven:** Countertop models are generally more affordable and require no installation. Wall ovens cost more but offer larger capacity and a built-in look.

- **Feature Trade-offs:** AI recognition, steam cooking, and camera systems add cost. Determine which features you'll actually use regularly.

- **Long-term Value:** Consider energy efficiency ratings and build quality. A well-made intelligent oven should last 10-15 years with proper care.

- **Smart Home Ecosystem:** If you're already invested in a particular ecosystem, choosing a compatible oven avoids the need for additional hubs or compromises.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Countertop Models

Countertop intelligent ovens like the June Pro and Breville HydroPro require minimal installation. Simply place them on a heat-resistant surface near an electrical outlet. Ensure adequate ventilation—these appliances generate significant heat during operation.

For optimal performance, position your countertop oven away from walls and cabinets to allow proper airflow. Most manufacturers recommend at least 4 inches of clearance on all sides.

### Wall Oven Installation

Built-in wall ovens like the Samsung Bespoke require professional installation. Factors to consider:

- **Electrical Requirements:** Most intelligent wall ovens require a dedicated 240V circuit. Verify your kitchen's electrical capacity before purchasing.

- **Cabinet Modifications:** Standard cabinet openings may need modification to accommodate specific oven dimensions. Measure carefully and consult with an installer.

- **Ventilation:** Proper ventilation is essential for wall ovens. Ensure your kitchen has adequate exhaust capacity.

### Smart Home Integration Tips

**Network Requirements:** Intelligent ovens require a stable 2.4GHz Wi-Fi connection. If your router is far from the kitchen, consider a mesh network system to ensure reliable connectivity.

**Voice Assistant Setup:** Link your oven to your preferred voice assistant during initial setup. Create routines that combine oven functions with other smart devices—for example, dimming lights when the oven timer completes.

**App Configuration:** Take time to explore your oven's companion app fully. Many features, including recipe libraries and cooking history, are only accessible through the app.

**Security Considerations:** As with any connected device, secure your oven's network connection with a strong password. Enable automatic updates to receive security patches and feature improvements.

**Integration Examples:**
- Trigger kitchen exhaust fan when oven activates
- Receive phone notifications when cooking completes
- Adjust smart lighting for optimal visibility during cooking
- Sync with smart speakers for timer announcements
