### For the Budget-Conscious Homeowner
**Recommendation:** Wyze Bulb Color (2nd Gen)
If you're outfitting a whole house with smart bulbs, Wyze is unbeatable for value. At under $15 per bulb, you can cover a 2,000 sq ft home for under $200. ### Who Should Skip Smart Bulbs?
If you have a home with many three-way switches (two switches controlling one light) or use dimmer switches extensively, consider smart switches instead of bulbs. Smart bulbs require constant power, and traditional dimmer switches can cause flickering or damage.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Step-by-Step Installation Guide

1. **Screw in the bulb** like a standard light bulb. Ensure the fixture is compatible (most E26 base bulbs fit US fixtures).
2. **Download the app** (Philips Hue, LIFX, or Wyze) and create an account.
3. **Power on the bulb** and wait for it to pulse or flash, indicating pairing mode.
4. **Follow in-app instructions** to connect the bulb to your Wi-Fi or hub. For Philips Hue, you'll need to connect the Bridge to your router via Ethernet first.
5. **Name your bulbs** and group them into rooms (e.g., "Living Room Ceiling").
6. **Set up voice control** by linking the bulb's skill to your preferred assistant (Alexa, Google, or HomeKit).

**Pro Tip:** For outdoor fixtures, ensure the bulb is rated for damp or wet locations. Philips Hue offers specific outdoor bulbs, while LIFX and Wyze's standard bulbs are for indoor use only.

### Smart Home Integration

- **Voice Assistants:** All three bulbs work with Alexa and Google Assistant. Philips Hue and LIFX also support Apple HomeKit, allowing Siri control and automation in the Home app.
- **Automation Platforms:** Philips Hue is compatible with SmartThings, Hubitat, and Home Assistant via its Bridge. LIFX and Wyze work with IFTTT for basic automations.
- **Scene Creation:** Create custom scenes like "Movie Night" (dim, warm light) or "Wake Up" (gradual brightening) using the app. Philips Hue's "Hue Labs" offers experimental automations.
- **Geofencing:** All three bulbs support geofencing, automatically turning lights on when you arrive home and off when you leave. This feature works best with the Philips Hue Bridge for reliable performance.

### Common Integration Issues and Solutions

- **Wi-Fi Interference:** If your LIFX or Wyze bulb disconnects frequently, try moving your router closer or using a mesh Wi-Fi system. For Zigbee bulbs, ensure the Bridge is centrally located.
- **Slow Response Times:** This is often due to network congestion. Dedicate a 2.4 GHz network for smart devices, or upgrade to a Wi-Fi 6 router for better bandwidth management.
- **No Apple HomeKit:** If you're an Apple user, stick with Philips Hue or LIFX. Wyze's lack of HomeKit support is a significant drawback for iOS-centric smart homes.
