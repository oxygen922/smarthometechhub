**Verdict:** For budget-conscious users or those outfitting an entire home, the Wyze Bulb Color is the best bang for your buck. It’s simple to set up and delivers reliable performance for everyday use.

## Key Features to Consider (With Comparison Table)

When shopping for a smart light bulb, these features matter most. Here’s what to look for.

### 1. Connectivity Protocol
- **Wi-Fi:** No hub needed, but can congest your router. Best for small setups (e.g., Wyze, LIFX).
- **Zigbee/Z-Wave:** Requires a hub but offers mesh networking for reliability and low power. Best for large ecosystems (e.g., Philips Hue).
- **Bluetooth:** Limited range and no remote access. Only for basic control.

### 2. Brightness and Color
- **Lumens:** 800 lumens is standard for a 60W equivalent. 1,100+ lumens for larger rooms.
- **Color Temperature:** Tunable white (2,700K-6,500K) lets you switch from warm to cool light.
- **Color Gamut:** Look for bulbs with 16 million+ colors and a CRI of 90+ for accurate color reproduction.

### 3. Smart Home Integration
- **Voice Assistants:** Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant are standard. Apple HomeKit is a premium feature.
- **Automations:** Schedules, scenes, and geofencing (lights turn on/off when you arrive/leave).

### 4. Power and Energy
- **Wattage:** Most bulbs use 8-12W, saving 80%+ compared to incandescent bulbs.
- **Lifespan:** 25,000-50,000 hours (15-25 years of typical use).

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Steps (All Models)
1. **Screw in the bulb** into a standard E26 (medium) socket. Ensure the fixture is compatible (no enclosed fixtures for most models).
2. **Download the app** (Philips Hue, Wyze, or LIFX).
3. **Follow in-app instructions** to connect to your Wi-Fi or Zigbee network. For Wi-Fi bulbs, ensure your router is on the 2.4 GHz band.
4. **Name your bulb** (e.g., “Living Room Lamp”) and group it into rooms.
5. **Set up automations:** Schedules, scenes, and voice control.

### Smart Home Integration Tips
- **Voice Assistants:** Say, “Alexa, turn on the living room lights to 50%” or “Hey Google, set the bedroom lights to cool white.”
- **Alexa Routines:** Create a “Good Morning” routine that gradually brightens lights and turns on your coffee maker.
- **Apple HomeKit:** Use the Home app to create scenes like “Movie Night” that dim lights and close smart blinds.
- **Geofencing:** Set lights to turn on automatically when you arrive home (works with all three brands).

### Troubleshooting Common Issues
- **Bulb won’t connect:** Move the bulb closer to the router or hub. For Wi-Fi bulbs, check for 2.4 GHz band compatibility.
- **Flickering:** Ensure the fixture is not on a dimmer switch (smart bulbs require constant power). Use a smart dimmer instead.
- **Color inaccuracies:** Some bulbs have a lower CRI. Calibrate via the app if available.
