### Understanding CADR

CADR, or Clean Air Delivery Rate, is the single most important spec for comparing purifiers. It measures how many cubic feet of clean air the unit delivers per minute. As a rule of thumb, you want a CADR that's at least two-thirds of your room's square footage for effective cleaning. So for a 300-square-foot room, you want a CADR of at least 200.

### True HEPA vs. HEPA-Like

Many budget purifiers advertise "HEPA-type" or "HEPA-like" filters, which are not the same as true HEPA. True HEPA filters capture 99.97% of particles at 0.3 microns. If a product doesn't explicitly say "True HEPA" or "HEPA H13," be skeptical.

### Smart Home Integration

If you're building a smart home, look for purifiers that support your existing ecosystem. HomeKit users should prioritize Coway. Alexa and Google users have more options. Consider whether you want voice control, scheduling, or air quality triggers for other devices.

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy an Air Purifier?

- **Allergy sufferers:** If you wake up congested or sneeze constantly during pollen season, a purifier with true HEPA filtration is a game-changer.
- **Pet owners:** Dander and odors are no match for a purifier with strong carbon filtration.
- **Urban dwellers:** If you live near busy roads or in areas with poor outdoor air quality, a purifier keeps your indoor air safe.
- **Wildfire-prone regions:** During smoke events, a purifier with high CADR and robust carbon filtration is essential.
- **Parents of young children:** Cleaner air supports respiratory health, especially for kids with developing lungs.

### Who Doesn't Need One?

If you live in a rural area with excellent outdoor air quality, don't have pets or allergies, and rarely open your windows, a purifier may offer marginal benefits. That said, indoor air is often 2-5 times more polluted than outdoor air, so most households benefit from some level of purification.

### Budget Considerations

- **Under $250:** Expect solid performance for small rooms. Levoit and Winix are strong brands here.
- **$250–$600:** Mid-range units offer better coverage, quieter operation, and improved smart features.
- **$600+:** Premium purifiers deliver maximum coverage, best-in-class filtration, and premium build quality.

Don't forget to factor in filter replacement costs. A $200 purifier with $80 annual filters costs more over five years than a $400 purifier with $40 filters.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Setting Up Your Purifier

Installation is straightforward for all three recommended units. Unbox, remove the plastic wrap from the filters, plug in, and connect to your Wi-Fi network via the companion app. Most setups take under 15 minutes. Place the unit at least 6 inches away from walls to ensure proper airflow.

### Smart Home Automation Ideas

- **Air quality triggers:** Use IFTTT or your smart home hub to turn on the purifier when outdoor air quality drops below a certain threshold.
- **Sleep schedules:** Set the purifier to low mode automatically at bedtime.
- **Alexa routines:** Create a "Good Morning" routine that turns off the purifier and reads your air quality report.
- **HomeKit scenes:** Coway users can integrate the purifier into HomeKit scenes for seamless automation.

### Maintenance Tips

- Vacuum the pre-filter every two weeks.
- Replace HEPA filters every 6–12 months depending on usage.
- Replace carbon filters every 3–6 months if you have pets or smoke.
- Keep the unit away from curtains and furniture that could block airflow.
