## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy an Air Purifier?

- **Allergy sufferers:** HEPA filters capture pollen, dust mites, and pet dander, reducing allergy symptoms.
- **Pet owners:** Air purifiers help control pet odors and dander.
- **Urban dwellers:** If you live near highways or industrial areas, an air purifier can reduce exposure to pollutants.
- **Wildfire-prone regions:** During wildfire season, a purifier with activated carbon can filter out smoke particles and VOCs.
- **Parents of young children:** Cleaner air supports respiratory health and reduces illness frequency.
- **Remote workers:** If you spend all day at home, investing in air quality is investing in your health.

### Budget Considerations

- **Budget ($80–$200):** Expect basic HEPA filtration, limited coverage, and no smart features. Best for small rooms.
- **Mid-Range ($200–$500):** Better CADR, larger coverage, and often some smart features. Good for most households.
- **Premium ($500–$1,000+):** Top-tier filtration, large coverage, advanced sensors, and full smart home integration. Ideal for large homes or health-conscious buyers.

**Long-Term Costs:** Don't forget filter replacements. A $200 purifier with $80 annual filters costs more over five years than a $500 purifier with $40 filters. Always factor in ongoing expenses.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Setting Up Your Air Purifier

1. **Unbox and remove all packaging** — including plastic wrap around filters.
2. **Choose the right location** — place the unit at least 6 inches from walls and furniture to ensure proper airflow.
3. **Install the filter** — most models require you to remove the filter from its plastic bag before first use.
4. **Power on and test** — run the unit on high for 10 minutes to check functionality.
5. **Connect to Wi-Fi (if applicable)** — download the manufacturer's app and follow the pairing instructions.

### Smart Home Integration

Modern air purifiers integrate with:

- **Amazon Alexa:** "Alexa, set the air purifier to auto mode."
- **Google Assistant:** "Hey Google, what's the air quality in the living room?"
- **Apple HomeKit:** Control via the Home app and create automations.
- **IFTTT:** Create custom routines, like turning on the purifier when your smart door sensor detects someone entering.

**Pro Tip:** Use smart plugs to schedule your purifier to run during off-peak electricity hours, saving money while maintaining clean air.
