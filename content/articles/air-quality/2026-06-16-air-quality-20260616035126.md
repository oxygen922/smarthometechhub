**Smart Features:** This is where the A5 shines. It supports Matter 1.3 protocol, making it universally compatible with Alexa, Google Home, and Apple HomeKit. You can set up "Good Night" routines that automatically dim the display and drop to silent mode when your smart lights turn off.

**Understanding CADR:** This is the single most important number. Look for a CADR (Smoke) of at least 200 for a standard living room. A higher number means the machine can pull smoke (and allergens) out of the air faster.

**Smart vs. App-Controlled:** True "smart" purifiers have onboard sensors that adjust the fan based on air quality. "App-controlled" purifiers just let you turn the fan on/off from your phone. Always look for "Auto Mode" with a laser particle sensor.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

Setting up a modern smart purifier is easier than setting up a smart bulb.

**Step 1: Physical Setup**
Unbox the unit and remove the plastic wrapping from the filter (a common mistake!). Plug it in. Most units will auto-detect the filter and run a calibration cycle for 30 seconds.

**Step 2: App Connection**
Download the manufacturer's app (VeSync for Levoit, AeraMax for the A5, Dyson Link for Dyson). Turn on Bluetooth on your phone. The app will usually scan a QR code on the unit to pair it instantly.

**Step 3: Voice Assistant Integration**
- **For Alexa:** Open the Alexa app > Skills & Games > Search for "AeraMax" or "VeSync" > Enable Skill > Link your account. You can now say, "Alexa, set the air purifier to auto mode."
- **For Google Home:** Open Google Home > Add Device > Works with Google > Select your brand.
- **For Apple HomeKit (AeraMax only):** Open the Home app > Add Accessory > Scan the Matter code on the unit. No bridge required.

**Pro Tip:** Create a "Home Arrival" routine. When your phone connects to Wi-Fi, set the purifier to "Boost Mode" for 10 minutes to clear out any stale air that accumulated while you were gone.
