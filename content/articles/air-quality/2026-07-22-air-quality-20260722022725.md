### What to Look For

- **CADR Ratings:** Higher numbers mean faster cleaning. For a 500 sq ft room, aim for a CADR of at least 200 for smoke.
- **Filter Type:** HEPA H13 is the gold standard for particle removal. Activated carbon is essential for VOCs and odors. Some units (like Blueair) use hybrid technologies—check independent lab tests.
- **Smart Home Compatibility:** Matter is the new universal standard. If you're building a smart home, choose a Matter-compatible purifier for easy integration with Alexa, Google Home, and Apple HomeKit.
- **Noise Levels:** Sleep mode should be under 25 dB. Standard operation at medium speed should stay under 40 dB.
- **Energy Efficiency:** Look for Energy Star certification. Purifiers run 24/7, so energy costs add up.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Setup Process

All three purifiers are designed for out-of-the-box simplicity. The Levoit and Blueair require only plugging in, removing the filter from its plastic wrapping, and connecting to Wi-Fi via their respective apps. The Dyson is slightly more complex due to its multi-function design, but the app walks you through calibration of the air quality sensors and temperature settings. Expect setup to take 10–20 minutes.

### Smart Home Integration

- **Matter Compatibility:** The Levoit 600S Pro and Dyson Gen2 both support Matter, meaning they work seamlessly with Apple Home, Google Home, and Alexa. You can create automations like "If outdoor AQI exceeds 100, turn on living room purifier."
- **Voice Control:** All three work with Alexa and Google Assistant. The Dyson also supports Siri shortcuts.
- **Automation:** The Blueair app allows IFTTT integrations, while Dyson and Levoit offer native scheduling. I set my Levoit to run at medium speed during cooking hours (5–7 PM) and switch to sleep mode at 10 PM.
- **Multi-Unit Management:** If you have multiple units, the Dyson app excels with its "Home" view showing all devices and air quality across rooms. Blueair's app is simpler but effective.

### Placement Tips

- Place purifiers in the room where you spend the most time (bedroom or living room).
- Keep them at least 12 inches from walls and furniture for optimal airflow.
- For pet owners, position the unit near pet sleeping areas.
- Avoid placing near windows or doors where outside air can interfere with sensors.
