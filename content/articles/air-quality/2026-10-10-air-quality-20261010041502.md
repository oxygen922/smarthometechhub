### Understanding CADR Ratings

Clean Air Delivery Rate (CADR) measures how quickly an air purifier removes pollutants from a room. Higher numbers mean faster cleaning. For reference:
- **Small rooms (up to 200 sq ft):** 100-150 CADR
- **Medium rooms (200-400 sq ft):** 150-250 CADR
- **Large rooms (400+ sq ft):** 250+ CADR

### Filter Types Explained

**True HEPA Filters:** Capture 99.97% of particles down to 0.3 microns. This includes dust, pollen, pet dander, and most smoke particles.

**Activated Carbon Filters:** Absorb odors, VOCs, and gases. Quality varies significantly—look for units with substantial carbon content.

**Catalytic Filters:** Break down formaldehyde and other chemicals at a molecular level. Found primarily in premium units like the Dyson.

**Pre-Filters:** Capture large particles and extend the life of primary filters. Washable versions save money long-term.

### Smart Features That Matter

- **Auto Mode:** Uses sensors to adjust fan speed automatically
- **Air Quality Display:** Real-time feedback on your air quality
- **App Control:** Remote operation and scheduling
- **Voice Assistant Integration:** Hands-free control
- **Filter Life Indicators:** Know when to replace filters

### Energy Efficiency

All three recommended units are Energy Star certified. At maximum speed, they consume between 45-65 watts—comparable to a standard light bulb. Running 24/7 costs approximately $50-70 annually, depending on your electricity rates.

## Buying Guide: Who Should Buy an Air Purifier?

### You Need an Air Purifier If You:

- **Live in wildfire-prone areas:** The western US increasingly faces wildfire smoke events. An air purifier is essential for maintaining healthy indoor air during these periods.

- **Have allergies or asthma:** Air purifiers dramatically reduce airborne allergens, providing relief for sufferers.

- **Own pets:** Pet dander is a major indoor pollutant. Air purifiers help control dander, odors, and associated allergens.

- **Live in urban areas:** City air contains higher levels of traffic-related pollutants. Indoor air purifiers create a clean air sanctuary.

- **Have a new home or furniture:** New building materials and furniture off-gas VOCs and formaldehyde. Purifiers with carbon and catalytic filters help.

- **Cook frequently:** Cooking generates significant indoor air pollution. An air purifier in or near the kitchen helps.

### Budget Considerations

**Under $300:** You can find capable air purifiers for small to medium rooms. Expect fewer smart features and higher filter replacement costs relative to initial price.

**$300-$700:** This sweet spot offers excellent performance, comprehensive smart features, and reasonable filter costs. Our top picks from Coway and Levoit fall here.

**$700+:** Premium units offer specialized capabilities like formaldehyde removal, superior build quality, and advanced sensors. Justified for those with specific needs.

**Don't Forget Filter Costs:** A $200 purifier with $100 annual filters costs more over 5 years than a $500 purifier with $50 annual filters. Calculate total cost of ownership.

### Room Size Matters

Always choose a purifier rated for a larger room than you actually have. This ensures it can run at lower, quieter speeds while still providing excellent purification. Running a small purifier at maximum speed in a large room is inefficient and noisy.

## Installation and Smart Home Integration

### Initial Setup

Setting up modern air purifiers is straightforward:

1. **Unbox and remove all packaging:** Don't forget to remove plastic wrap from filters.
2. **Install filters:** Most units come with filters pre-installed but check the manual.
3. **Choose placement:** Position at least 6 inches from walls and away from furniture that might block airflow.
4. **Power on and connect:** Follow app instructions for Wi-Fi setup.

### Optimal Placement

- **Bedrooms:** Place near the bed but not so close that airflow hits you directly.
- **Living rooms:** Central placement provides even coverage.
- **Kitchens:** Position away from cooking surfaces to avoid grease clogging filters.
- **Multiple units:** For whole-home coverage, consider one unit per major living area.

### Smart Home Integration Tips

**Amazon Alexa:**
- "Alexa, set air purifier to auto mode"
- "Alexa, what's the air quality in the living room?"
- Create routines: "Alexa, good night" triggers sleep mode

**Google Assistant:**
- "Hey Google, turn on the air purifier"
- "Hey Google, set fan speed to 50%"

**Apple HomeKit:**
- Add purifier to scenes: "Movie Night" dims lights and sets purifier to low
- Check air quality via Siri: "Hey Siri, what's the air quality?"

### Automation Ideas

- **Wildfire Alert Automation:** Use IFTTT to trigger maximum fan speed when local AQI exceeds 100
- **Sleep Schedule:** Automatically switch to quiet mode at bedtime
- **Away Mode:** Reduce fan speed when everyone leaves home
- **Morning Routine:** Boost purification before waking up
