## Key Features to Consider (with Comparison Table)

When shopping for a smart air purifier in 2026, focus on these critical factors:

- **CADR (Clean Air Delivery Rate):** Measures how quickly the purifier cleans smoke, dust, and pollen. Higher is better for larger rooms.
- **Filter Type:** HEPA H13 or H14 is standard for particle removal. Activated carbon handles odors and VOCs.
- **Smart Integration:** Look for Matter compatibility for future-proofing, plus support for your preferred voice assistant.
- **Noise Level:** Check dB ratings at different speeds. Sleep mode should be under 25 dB.
- **Energy Efficiency:** Look for Energy Star certification to keep electricity costs low.
- **Sensor Quality:** Laser-based sensors are more accurate than infrared for detecting fine particles.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Setup Process

All three units are relatively easy to set up:
1. Unbox and remove plastic wrap from filters.
2. Plug in and power on.
3. Download the companion app and create an account.
4. Connect to your Wi-Fi (2.4 GHz required for most models).
5. Follow in-app instructions for calibration (some units require a 10-minute sensor warm-up).

The Dyson and Blueair take about 10 minutes total. The Coway may take slightly longer due to its learning mode setup.

### Smart Home Integration

- **Alexa:** All three support “Alexa, turn on the [purifier name]” and “Alexa, set air quality mode to auto.” The Dyson also allows temperature control via voice.
- **Google Assistant:** Similar command set. The Blueair and Coway support routines like “Good night” to set sleep mode.
- **Apple HomeKit:** Only the Dyson supports HomeKit natively. The Blueair can be added via Matter if you have a Matter-compatible hub.
- **Matter:** The Blueair 411i Max is the only one with native Matter support in 2026, making it the most future-proof.
- **IFTTT:** Dyson has the best IFTTT integration, allowing custom triggers like “If outdoor AQI is poor, turn on purifier.”

### Placement Tips

- Place purifiers in the room where you spend the most time (bedroom or living room).
- Keep at least 1–2 feet away from walls and furniture for optimal airflow.
- Avoid placing near heat sources or direct sunlight.
- For whole-home coverage, consider one unit per 400–500 square feet.
