## Key Features to Consider

When choosing a smart air purifier, understanding the technical specifications is crucial. Here are the most important features to evaluate:

### Filtration Type
- **True HEPA H13/H14:** The gold standard, capturing 99.97% of particles down to 0.3 microns. Essential for allergies.
- **Activated Carbon:** Traps odors, VOCs (volatile organic compounds), and smoke.
- **Electrostatic (like Blueair):** Uses an electric charge to attract particles, often more energy-efficient.
- **Pre-Filter:** Captures large particles (pet hair, dust) to extend the main filter's life.

### CADR (Clean Air Delivery Rate)
This measures how quickly a purifier cleans the air. Higher is better for larger rooms. Look for a CADR rating at least 2/3 of your room's square footage.

### Smart Features
- **Real-time Monitoring:** Sensors for PM2.5, PM10, VOCs, and humidity.
- **Auto Mode:** Automatically adjusts fan speed based on air quality.
- **Voice Control:** Alexa, Google Assistant, or Apple HomeKit.
- **App Control:** Scheduling, remote operation, and historical data.
- **Integration:** Does it work with your existing smart home hub?

### Noise Level
Measured in decibels (dB). Sleep mode should be under 30 dB (whisper-quiet). Turbo mode can reach 50-60 dB (conversation level).

### Energy Consumption
Look for Energy Star certification. Many smart purifiers use less than 50W on low settings.

### Filter Replacement Cost
This is a recurring expense. Typical filters last 6-12 months and cost $30-$80. Some models have washable pre-filters to reduce waste.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Unboxing and Setup

All three models are relatively straightforward to set up. The Levoit and Blueair require you to remove the filter from its plastic wrap and snap or slide it into place. The Dyson involves a few more steps: attaching the base, sliding in the filter, and plugging it in. Expect setup to take 5-10 minutes.

### App Installation

1. **Dyson Link App:** Download from the App Store or Google Play. Create an account, then press the "Pair" button on the unit. The app will guide you through Wi-Fi setup (2.4 GHz required). Once connected, you can name the device and set your preferred mode.

2. **VeSync App (Levoit):** Similar process. The app is clean and offers scheduling, timers, and air quality history. You can also create "Groups" if you have multiple Levoit devices.

3. **Blueair App:** Simple pairing via Bluetooth and Wi-Fi. The app shows real-time PM2.5 levels and filter life. It lacks scheduling but offers remote on/off and mode selection.

### Smart Home Integration

- **Amazon Alexa:** All three work well. You can say, "Alexa, turn on the living room purifier" or "Alexa, set the Dyson to Auto mode."
- **Google Assistant:** Similar voice commands. The Levoit and Blueair respond reliably; the Dyson has the most advanced voice control options (e.g., "Set fan speed to 5").
- **Apple HomeKit:** Only the Dyson supports HomeKit. This is a significant advantage for Apple users who want to automate the purifier with other HomeKit devices (e.g., "When the outdoor air quality drops, turn on the purifier").

### Automation Tips

- **Create a "Sleep" Routine:** Set the purifier to Sleep Mode at 10 PM and turn it off at 7 AM. This saves energy and ensures quiet operation.
- **Use Geofencing:** With the Dyson, you can set the purifier to turn on when you leave work and off when you leave home.
- **Trigger from Sensors:** If you have a smart air quality sensor (like an Awair or Airthings), you can use IFTTT or a hub to trigger the purifier when PM2.5 levels rise.
