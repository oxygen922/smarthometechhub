## Key Features to Consider

Before buying any air purifier, understand these core specifications. They determine whether a unit will actually work for your space.

| Feature | Why It Matters | What to Look For |
|---|---|---|
| **CADR (Clean Air Delivery Rate)** | Measures how quickly the purifier cleans air | Match CADR to room size; higher is better |
| **Room Coverage (sq ft)** | Manufacturer's max recommended area | Choose 1.2x your actual room size |
| **Filter Type** | Determines what particles are captured | True HEPA H13 or H14 minimum |
| **Activated Carbon** | Removes odors, smoke, VOCs | Look for 2+ lbs for serious odor control |
| **Noise Level (dB)** | Affects sleep and daily comfort | Under 25 dB for bedrooms |
| **Energy Use (Watts)** | Impacts running costs | 20–60W typical; check 24/7 cost |
| **Smart Features** | Automation and monitoring | Wi-Fi, app, Alexa/Google/HomeKit |
| **Filter Replacement Cost** | Long-term ownership expense | Budget $60–$150/year |
| **Sensor Accuracy** | Auto mode reliability | Laser particle sensors are best |
| **Warranty** | Protects your investment | 2–5 years standard |

## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy an Air Purifier?

Air purifiers aren't just for people with allergies. Consider one if you:

- **Live in wildfire-prone areas** — smoke particles are among the most harmful to lungs
- **Have pets** — dander and odor are constant concerns
- **Suffer from allergies or asthma** — HEPA filtration is clinically proven to help
- **Live in older homes** — dust, mold, and lead particles are common
- **Cook frequently** — especially with gas stoves, which release nitrogen dioxide
- **Live in urban areas** — traffic pollution seeps indoors
- **Have infants or elderly family members** — more vulnerable to poor air quality

### Budget Considerations

**Under $200:** Expect smaller coverage (300–500 sq ft), basic HEPA filtration, and fewer smart features. Great for bedrooms and renters.

**$200–$500:** Mid-range units with better CADR, larger carbon filters, and solid app support. Best value zone for most households.

**$500–$1,000+:** Large-room coverage, medical-grade filtration, premium sensors, and longer warranties. Worth it for serious health needs or big open spaces.

**Don't forget ongoing costs.** A $200 purifier with $120/year filters costs more over five years than a $500 unit with $70/year filters. Always calculate total cost of ownership.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Setup Is Easier Than You Think

Most modern air purifiers are essentially plug-and-play:

1. **Unbox and remove plastic wrap** from filters (a common mistake — the unit won't work if you skip this).
2. **Place the unit** at least 6–12 inches from walls and furniture for proper airflow.
3. **Download the companion app** and connect via Wi-Fi (2.4 GHz networks are most reliable).
4. **Link to your smart home hub** — Alexa, Google Home, or Apple HomeKit.
5. **Set up automations** based on air quality triggers or schedules.

### Smart Home Integration Tips

- **Voice routines:** "Alexa, turn on the bedroom purifier" works with all three recommended models.
- **IFTTT and Matter:** The AeroShield supports Matter, making it future-proof for smart home ecosystems.
- **Air quality triggers:** Pair your purifier with a smart thermostat or standalone air quality monitor to auto-activate when PM2.5 spikes.
- **Energy scheduling:** Run purifiers on higher speeds during off-peak electricity hours to save money.

### Placement Matters

- **Bedrooms:** Place near the bed but not directly in airflow path.
- **Living rooms:** Central location maximizes coverage.
- **Kitchens:** Position away from cooking surfaces to avoid grease clogging filters.
- **Basements:** Elevate on a small platform to avoid moisture issues.
