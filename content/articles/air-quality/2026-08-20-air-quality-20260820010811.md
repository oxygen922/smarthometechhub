## Key Features to Consider

When shopping for a smart air purifier, it’s easy to get distracted by flashy marketing. Here’s what actually matters:

### CADR (Clean Air Delivery Rate)
This is the industry standard for measuring purification speed. It tells you how many cubic feet of air per minute the unit cleans. Higher CADR means faster coverage for larger rooms. For a 400 sq ft room, aim for a CADR of at least 250 CFM.

### Filter Type
- **True HEPA:** Captures 99.97% of particles down to 0.3 microns. Essential for allergy sufferers.
- **Activated Carbon:** Removes odors, smoke, and VOCs. Look for a thick carbon layer if you cook frequently or live near traffic.
- **Washable Pre-Filters:** Extend the life of your main filter by catching large particles like pet hair and dust bunnies.

### Smart Integration
- **App Control:** Look for apps that offer real-time air quality graphs, filter life tracking, and scheduling.
- **Voice Assistants:** Alexa and Google Assistant compatibility is standard; Matter support is the emerging gold standard.
- **Automation:** IFTTT support allows you to trigger actions based on sensor data (e.g., turn on the purifier if PM2.5 exceeds a threshold).

### Noise Levels
Check the decibel rating on low and high settings. A bedroom unit should stay under 30 dB on low; living room units can tolerate up to 50 dB.

### Energy Consumption
Continuous operation is common, so look for energy-efficient models. Most smart purifiers use between 30W and 60W on max. ENERGY STAR certification is a good indicator.

### Filter Replacement Costs
Factor in the long-term expense. Some units have filters costing $79 every 6 months; others use $39 filters that last a year. Calculate the annual cost before committing.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Setting Up Your Smart Air Purifier

Installation is refreshingly simple for all three units:

1. **Unbox and remove plastic wrapping** from the filters.
2. **Insert the filter** according to the manual (each unit has a specific orientation).
3. **Plug in and power on** the device.
4. **Download the companion app** and create an account.
5. **Connect to Wi-Fi** (2.4 GHz is typically required for all three brands).
6. **Pair with voice assistants** via the app or by saying "Alexa, discover devices."

### Smart Home Integration Tips

**Alexa & Google Assistant:**
Once paired, you can use voice commands like "Alexa, set the air purifier to auto mode" or "Hey Google, turn on the purifier at 7 PM."

**Routines & Automation:**
- **With Alexa:** Create a routine that triggers the purifier when your smart thermostat detects high humidity or when you arrive home.
- **With Google Home:** Use the "Home & Away" feature to automatically turn off the purifier when you leave.
- **With IFTTT (Coway only):** Set up applets like "If outdoor PM2.5 exceeds 100, turn on the purifier to max."

**Matter (Blueair only):**
If you’re building a future-proof smart home, Matter support allows the Blue Pure 311i+ Max to work with Apple Home, SmartThings, and any Matter-certified platform without additional bridges.

**Pro Tip:** Place your purifier at least 3 feet away from walls and furniture for optimal airflow. Avoid placing it near doors or windows where drafts can interfere with sensor readings.
