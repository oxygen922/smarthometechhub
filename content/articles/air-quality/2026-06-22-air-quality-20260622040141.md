### CADR (Clean Air Delivery Rate)
This is the industry standard for measuring how quickly an air purifier can clean a room. Higher CADR numbers mean faster filtration. For most rooms, look for a CADR of at least 200 for smoke, dust, and pollen.

### Filter Type
True HEPA filters are the gold standard, capturing 99.97% of particles. However, HEPASilent technology (used by Blueair) offers similar performance with lower energy consumption. Activated carbon filters are essential for removing odors and VOCs.

### Noise Levels
Consider where you'll place the unit. Bedroom units should operate below 30 dB on low settings. Living room units can be louder, but anything above 55 dB may be disruptive during conversation or TV watching.
