## Key Features to Consider

When evaluating air purifiers, you'll encounter a dizzying array of specifications. Here's what actually matters:

### CADR (Clean Air Delivery Rate)
This is the industry standard for measuring an air purifier's effectiveness. It tells you how many cubic feet of clean air the device can deliver per minute. As a rule of thumb, the CADR should be at least two-thirds of your room's square footage. For a 300-square-foot room, you want a CADR of at least 200.

### Filter Types
- **True HEPA:** Captures 99.97% of particles at 0.3 microns. This is the gold standard.
- **HEPA H13:** A higher grade that captures particles as small as 0.1 microns. Found in the Dyson.
- **Activated Carbon:** Essential for removing odors, VOCs, and gases. Not all purifiers include this.
- **Washable Pre-filters:** Extend the life of your main filter and save money long-term.

### Smart Features
- **Auto Mode:** Uses a built-in sensor to adjust fan speed based on real-time air quality.
- **App Connectivity:** Allows remote control and monitoring.
- **Voice Assistant Compatibility:** Alexa, Google Assistant, or Apple HomeKit.
- **Air Quality Display:** Shows real-time PM2.5, VOC, and temperature readings.

### Noise Levels
Most purifiers produce between 20-50 decibels. For bedroom use, look for a "sleep mode" that operates below 30 dB.

### Filter Replacement Costs
This is the hidden cost of ownership. Check the price of replacement filters and how often they need changing (typically every 6-12 months). Some brands charge $50-$80 per filter, which adds up over time.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

One of the greatest advantages of modern air purifiers is their plug-and-play nature. All three models I tested can be unboxed and running within five minutes. Here's what to expect:

### Step-by-Step Setup
1. **Unbox and remove plastic wrap:** The filters are pre-installed in all three models.
2. **Place the unit:** Position it at least 6 inches away from walls and furniture for optimal airflow.
3. **Plug in and power on:** Each unit has a physical power button.
4. **Connect to Wi-Fi:** Use the respective app to connect to your 2.4GHz network.
5. **Pair with voice assistants:** Enable the skill in Alexa/Google Home/HomeKit and link your account.

### Automation Ideas
- **Morning Routine:** Set your purifier to run at high speed for 30 minutes before you wake up to clear overnight pollutants.
- **Cooking Mode:** Create an automation that turns the purifier to max when your smart smoke detector detects elevated particles.
- **Geofencing:** Use your phone's location to automatically turn off the purifier when you leave home and turn it on when you return.

### Maintenance
Filter replacement is the only ongoing maintenance. Most apps will notify you when it's time to change the filter, typically every 6-12 months depending on usage and air quality. The Dyson and Levoit use a "reset" button on the device after replacing the filter; the Blueair requires a reset through the app.
