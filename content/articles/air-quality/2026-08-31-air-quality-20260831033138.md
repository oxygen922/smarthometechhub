## Key Features to Consider

When shopping for a smart air purifier in 2026, here are the most important features to evaluate:

| Feature | Why It Matters | What to Look For |
|---------|---------------|------------------|
| **CADR (Clean Air Delivery Rate)** | Measures how quickly the purifier cleans a room | Higher is better; match to room size (minimum 100 CFM per 100 sq ft) |
| **Filter Type** | Determines what particles are captured | True HEPA (H13) for allergens; activated carbon for odors |
| **Smart Connectivity** | Enables remote control and automation | Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and compatibility with Alexa/Google Assistant |
| **Air Quality Sensor** | Allows automatic adjustments | Look for laser sensors that detect PM2.5, VOCs, and humidity |
| **Noise Level** | Affects comfort, especially in bedrooms | Aim for ≤35 dB in sleep mode |
| **Coverage Area** | Ensures the device matches your room size | Check square footage rating (buy for the largest room you'll use it in) |
| **Filter Replacement Cost** | Long-term maintenance expense | Budget $40–$80 per filter, typically replaced every 6–12 months |
| **Energy Consumption** | Affects operating costs | Look for Energy Star certification; most use 30–60 watts |
| **Display and Controls** | Ease of daily use | Touch controls, clear indicators, and app-based monitoring preferred |

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Setup Process

All three purifiers are remarkably easy to set up. Here's what to expect:

1. **Unboxing and Placement:** Choose a location with at least 12 inches of clearance on all sides. For best results, place the purifier in the center of the room or near the primary source of pollutants (e.g., near your pet's bed or by a window).

2. **Filter Installation:** Remove the filter from its packaging, insert it into the unit, and close the compartment. Most models have a filter reset button that you'll press after installation.

3. **Wi-Fi Connection:** Download the companion app (Dyson Link, VeSync, or Coway's app), create an account, and follow the on-screen instructions to connect the device to your 2.4GHz Wi-Fi network.

4. **Voice Assistant Setup:** In the app, link your Amazon Alexa or Google Assistant account. You'll then be able to control the purifier with voice commands like "Alexa, set the air purifier to auto mode."

### Smart Home Integration Tips

- **Routines:** Set up an automation that turns on the purifier 30 minutes before you wake up, ensuring you breathe clean air from the moment your alarm goes off.
- **Sensors:** If you have smart sensors (like a door sensor), you can trigger the purifier to run at high speed when a door or window opens, helping to clear incoming outdoor pollution.
- **Energy Monitoring:** Through the apps, you can track energy usage and set the purifier to run only during off-peak electricity hours.
- **Remote Diagnostics:** All three apps provide filter health indicators, so you'll know exactly when to order replacements—no more guessing.
