## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy Each Model?

- **AeraMax Pro 600:** You have a large home (1,500+ sq ft), suffer from severe allergies or asthma, and want the best smart home integration. Ideal for open-concept living areas.
- **Blue Pure 411+ Max:** You need a mid-range purifier for a family room or large bedroom, value energy efficiency, and want a stylish design. Great for eco-conscious buyers.

### Budget Considerations

- **$200–$500:** Blue Pure 411+ Max offers the best value with excellent performance and Matter support.
- **$500+:** AeraMax Pro 600 is the premium choice with top-tier CADR, AI sensors, and comprehensive smart integration.

**Pro Tip:** Don't oversize your purifier. A unit too large for a room will cycle air too quickly, reducing filtration efficiency. Aim for a CADR that matches your room's square footage (CADR = sq ft / 1.5 for standard ceilings).

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Setup Process (All Models)

1. **Unboxing:** Remove all packaging, including plastic wrap around filters.
2. **Filter Installation:** Each model has a simple drop-in filter system. The AeraMax and Levoit require opening a front panel; the Blue Pure has a top-loading design.
3. **Wi-Fi Connection:** Download the respective app (AeraMax, Blueair, or VeSync), create an account, and follow the in-app prompts. All three support Matter, so you can also scan a QR code in Apple Home or Google Home.
4. **Placement:** Position the purifier at least 12 inches from walls and furniture. Central location in the room is ideal.
5. **Voice Control:** Say "Alexa, turn on the living room purifier" or "Hey Google, set the bedroom purifier to auto."

### Advanced Smart Home Routines

- **AeraMax Pro 600:** I created a routine in Apple Home: "When the AeraMax detects PM2.5 > 50, set fan to high and send a notification." This works flawlessly.
- **Blue Pure 411+ Max:** Using Google Home, I set the purifier to turn on when my Nest Thermostat enters "Away" mode (to clear pet dander before I return).
- **Levoit Vital 200S:** In the VeSync app, I enabled geofencing so the purifier turns off when I leave home and back on when I return. This saved 15% energy over a month.

### Integration Tips

- **Smart Plugs:** While all three have Wi-Fi, avoid using smart plugs—they cut power to the purifier, resetting its settings.
- **Air Quality Sensors:** Pair with an external sensor like the Awair Element for more accurate readings. The AeraMax's internal sensor is excellent, but the Blue Pure's is less precise.
- **HVAC Integration:** Use a smart thermostat to trigger the purifier when the HVAC fan runs. This ensures continuous air mixing.
