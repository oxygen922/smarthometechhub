## Buying Guide

### Who Should Buy an Air Purifier?

Air purifiers are beneficial for nearly everyone, but they're especially important for:

- **Allergy sufferers:** If you're constantly sneezing, coughing, or dealing with itchy eyes, a HEPA air purifier can significantly reduce airborne allergens like pollen, dust mites, and pet dander.
- **Pet owners:** Pets shed dander and fur, which can trigger allergies and asthma. An air purifier with a pre-filter and HEPA filter can help.
- **Urban dwellers:** If you live in a city with high pollution levels, an air purifier can reduce your exposure to PM2.5 and other harmful particles.
- **Wildfire-prone areas:** During wildfire season, air purifiers are essential for keeping indoor air safe.
- **Smokers:** If you smoke indoors (or live with someone who does), an air purifier with activated carbon can help reduce odors and harmful chemicals.
- **Health-conscious individuals:** Even if you don't have specific concerns, cleaner air can improve sleep, reduce headaches, and boost overall well-being.

### Budget Considerations

Air purifiers range from under $100 to over $1,000. Here's a rough breakdown:

- **Budget ($100–$200):** Expect basic models with HEPA filters, manual controls, and limited room coverage. The Coway Mighty is a standout in this range.
- **Mid-Range ($200–$400):** Better build quality, larger room coverage, and sometimes smart features. The Blueair 211+ Auto fits here.
- **Premium ($400+):** Top-tier filtration, real-time air quality monitoring, smart home integration, and additional features like heating/cooling. The Dyson Gen2 is the gold standard.

**Don't forget replacement filters.** A cheap purifier with expensive filters can cost more over time than a premium model with affordable filters.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation

Most air purifiers are plug-and-play. Here's what to expect:

1. **Unbox and remove all packaging** (including plastic wrap around filters).
2. **Install the filters** if they're not pre-installed. This usually involves removing a back panel and sliding the filter into place.
3. **Choose a location.** Place the purifier in a spot with good airflow—avoid corners or behind furniture. For best results, keep it at least a few feet away from walls.
4. **Plug in and power on.** Most units have a simple power button and fan speed controls.

### Smart Home Integration

If you want to control your air purifier with your voice or automate it based on air quality, look for models with Wi-Fi and compatibility with:

- **Amazon Alexa**
- **Google Assistant**
- **Apple HomeKit**

The Dyson Gen2 excels here, allowing you to say, "Alexa, set the air purifier to auto mode," or create routines like "If PM2.5 > 10, turn on the purifier." The Coway and Blueair models lack smart features, so they're better for users who prefer manual control.

**Pro Tip:** Even without built-in smart features, you can use a smart plug to turn your air purifier on and off remotely or schedule it to run at specific times.
