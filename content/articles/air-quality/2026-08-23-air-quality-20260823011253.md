## Key Features to Consider

When shopping for a smart air purifier, don't get distracted by marketing jargon. Here's what actually matters:

### 1. CADR Rating
CADR (Clean Air Delivery Rate) tells you how quickly a purifier can clean a room of a specific size. Look for a CADR of at least 200 CFM for a 300-square-foot room. Higher is always better, but remember that higher CADR also means more noise and energy consumption.

### 2. Filter Type
True HEPA is the gold standard, capturing 99.97% of particles down to 0.3 microns. Some units use electrostatic technology (like Blueair) which can be more efficient but may produce trace amounts of ozone—look for CARB certification to ensure safety.

### 3. Smart Features
Real-time air quality monitoring, auto mode, app control, and voice assistant integration are the features that separate a "smart" purifier from a dumb one. Consider which features you'll actually use—if you're never going to open the app, save your money.

### 4. Noise Levels
Air purifiers run 24/7, so noise matters. Look for a sleep mode under 30 dB (roughly a whisper) and a maximum noise level under 55 dB for high-speed operation.

### 5. Filter Replacement Cost
This is the hidden cost of ownership. A $150 purifier with $40 filters every 3 months costs more per year than a $650 purifier with $90 filters every 12 months. Always calculate the total cost of ownership.

### 6. Energy Consumption
Most modern purifiers draw between 20-60 watts on high, costing $15-$45 per year in electricity. Look for Energy Star certification to ensure efficiency.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Setting Up Your Smart Air Purifier

The installation process for all three units is refreshingly simple. Here's what to expect:

1. **Unboxing and Placement:** Find a spot with at least 12 inches of clearance on all sides. Avoid placing purifiers directly against walls or furniture, as this restricts airflow and reduces efficiency.
2. **Filter Installation:** All three units ship with filters that need to be removed from plastic packaging and inserted into the unit. This takes less than 2 minutes.
3. **App Setup:** Download the respective app (Dyson Link, VeSync, or Blueair), create an account, and follow the Wi-Fi pairing instructions. This typically takes 3-5 minutes.

### Smart Home Integration

All three purifiers support Alexa and Google Assistant, but the depth of integration varies:

- **Dyson:** Full integration. You can ask Alexa to set specific fan speeds, check air quality, and even turn on auto mode. Dyson also supports IFTTT, allowing for complex automation chains.
- **Levoit:** Solid integration via the VeSync app. You can enable "smart scenes" that trigger the purifier based on other device states. For example, turn on the purifier when your smart thermostat detects you're home.
- **Blueair:** Basic integration. You can turn the unit on/off and switch modes via voice, but there's no advanced automation capability.

**Expert Tip:** For the best smart home experience, pair your purifier with a smart air quality monitor (like the uHoo or Awair) that can trigger the purifier when pollution levels exceed a threshold. This gives you a more responsive system than relying on the purifier's built-in sensors alone.
