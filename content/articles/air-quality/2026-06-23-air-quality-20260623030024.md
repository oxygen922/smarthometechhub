**Pros:** Exceptional smart home compatibility, accurate sensors, low noise levels, energy-efficient (45W on high).

## Key Features to Consider (With Comparison Table)

When choosing a smart air purifier, focus on these critical features:

- **Filtration Type:** True HEPA (H13 or H14) is essential for capturing 99.97% of particles. Activated carbon helps with odors and VOCs.
- **CADR (Clean Air Delivery Rate):** Higher CADR means faster cleaning. Match it to your room size (CADR should be at least 2/3 of room area in square feet).
- **Smart Integration:** Native support for Alexa, Google Home, and Apple HomeKit ensures seamless automation.
- **Sensor Accuracy:** Look for laser-based PM2.5 sensors and VOC sensors for comprehensive monitoring.
- **Noise Levels:** Consider dB ratings for bedrooms (under 30 dB on low is ideal).
- **Filter Replacement Cost:** Factor in annual costs; some units have expensive proprietary filters.
- **Energy Efficiency:** Energy Star certification saves money long-term.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation

All three purifiers are designed for easy setup. Unbox, remove plastic wrap, insert filters (pre-installed in some models), and plug in. The AeroShield Pro 5000 takes about 10 minutes to set up, including app pairing. The PureBreeze Smart 3000 is even simpler, with a QR code for quick Wi-Fi connection. The AirGuard Elite X requires a bit more effort due to its Zigbee pairing, but the app guides you step-by-step.

### Smart Home Integration

- **AeroShield Pro 5000:** Seamless. Works with all major platforms. I created an automation that turns the purifier to high when my Nest Protect detects smoke.
- **PureBreeze Smart 3000:** Good but limited. Works with Alexa and Google for basic commands like “turn on” and “set fan speed.” No HomeKit or advanced triggers.
- **AirGuard Elite X:** Excellent. Its Zigbee support allows direct connection to SmartThings hubs without relying on cloud services. The Wildfire Mode is a standout automation.

For best results, place the purifier in a central location, away from walls and furniture. Use the app to calibrate sensors and set schedules (e.g., run on low during the day, high at night).
