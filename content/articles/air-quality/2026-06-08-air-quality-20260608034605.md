## Key Features to Consider

When shopping for a smart air purifier in 2026, understanding the technology behind the specs is crucial. Here's a breakdown of the most important features:

### Filtration Technology
- **True HEPA**: The gold standard, capturing 99.97% of particles down to 0.3 microns. Essential for allergy sufferers.
- **Activated Carbon**: Absorbs odors, VOCs, and gases. Important if you live near highways or have pets.
- **Pre-Filters**: Capture large particles like pet hair and dust, extending the life of the HEPA filter.
- **UV-C / Ionizers**: Some models include UV light or ionizers to kill bacteria, but may produce trace ozone. Look for units with CARB certification.

### Smart Home Integration
- **Voice Control**: Compatibility with Amazon Alexa, Google Assistant, or Apple HomeKit allows hands-free operation.
- **App Features**: Real-time air quality monitoring, scheduling, filter life tracking, and remote control.
- **Automation**: IFTTT (If This Then That) support enables triggers like "turn on purifier when air quality drops below 50."

### Room Coverage & CADR
- **CADR (Clean Air Delivery Rate)**: Measures how quickly the purifier cleans the air. Aim for a CADR at least 2/3 of your room's square footage.
- **Coverage Area**: Most units list a maximum square footage. For best results, choose a unit rated for 1.5x your room size.

### Noise Levels
- **Low Setting**: 15-25 dB (whisper-quiet, ideal for bedrooms)
- **High Setting**: 50-60 dB (comparable to a conversation)

### Filter Replacement Costs
- **Annual Cost**: Typically $50-$150 per year. Some models offer washable pre-filters to reduce costs.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Unboxing and Setup

All three recommended models are designed for plug-and-play installation. Here's a general guide:

1. **Unpack the unit** and remove all packaging materials, including plastic wraps around filters.
2. **Install filters** (if not pre-installed). Most units have a simple latch or magnetic door.
3. **Connect to power** and place the unit at least 6 inches from walls for proper airflow.
4. **Download the companion app** (Dyson Link, Coway iAware, or Blueair Friend).
5. **Follow in-app instructions** to connect via Wi-Fi (2.4 GHz band is typically required).
6. **Set up voice control** by enabling the skill in Alexa or Google Home app.

### Smart Home Integration Tips

- **Routines**: Create routines like "Goodnight" that turn off the purifier and set the thermostat.
- **Geofencing**: Some apps allow the purifier to turn on when you're within a certain distance from home.
- **IFTTT**: Use triggers like "If outdoor air quality drops below 50, turn on purifier at medium speed."
- **Zigbee/Z-Wave**: For advanced automation, consider a hub like SmartThings or Hubitat. Only a few high-end models support these protocols.

### Common Troubleshooting

- **Wi-Fi Connectivity**: Ensure your router is on the 2.4 GHz band. 5 GHz networks may cause issues.
- **Filter Replacement Alerts**: Reset the timer after changing filters (usually a button on the unit or in the app).
- **Noise Issues**: Check for loose components or debris in the fan. Most units have a self-diagnostic mode.
