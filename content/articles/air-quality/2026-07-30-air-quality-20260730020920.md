## Key Features to Consider

When shopping for a smart air purifier in 2026, keep these factors in mind:

- **CADR (Clean Air Delivery Rate):** Higher numbers mean faster filtration. For a 500 sq. ft. room, aim for a CADR of at least 250 CFM.
- **Filter Type:** True HEPA H13 is the gold standard. Some models add activated carbon for odors or electrostatic tech for extra efficiency.
- **Smart Home Compatibility:** Look for Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and support for Alexa, Google Home, or Apple HomeKit. Matter support is a bonus for future-proofing.
- **Noise Levels:** Sleep modes should be under 30 dB. Check decibel ratings for high-speed operation.
- **Energy Use:** Most purifiers use 30-60 watts on high. Look for Energy Star certification.
- **Filter Replacement Cost:** Budget for filters every 6-12 months. Costs range from $30 to $80.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Steps

1. **Unbox and Set Up:** Remove all packaging, including plastic wraps on filters. Plug in the unit.
2. **Placement:** Put the purifier in a central location, away from walls and furniture. Ensure at least 1-2 feet of clearance on all sides for optimal airflow.
3. **Connect to Wi-Fi:** Download the manufacturer’s app (e.g., Dyson Link, VeSync, or Blueair app). Follow the in-app instructions to connect to your 2.4 GHz network.
4. **Voice Assistant Setup:** For Alexa, enable the skill in the Alexa app. For Google Home, link the account. For HomeKit (Dyson only), scan the QR code or enter the setup code.

### Smart Home Integration Tips

- **Routines:** Set up automations like “When I leave home, turn off the purifier” using Alexa Routines or Google Home routines.
- **Sensors:** Pair with a smart air quality monitor (e.g., Airthings Wave Plus) for more accurate automation triggers.
- **Matter Support:** If you have a Matter-compatible hub (like Apple HomePod or Amazon Echo 4th Gen), the Blueair 5440i will work seamlessly across ecosystems.
- **Energy Savings:** Use the app to schedule the purifier to run during off-peak hours or when you’re home.
