## Key Features to Consider

When shopping for an air purifier in 2026, don't get distracted by flashy specs. Here’s what really matters:

**CADR (Clean Air Delivery Rate):** This measures how fast the purifier cleans a room of smoke, dust, and pollen. A higher CADR means faster cleaning. For a 1,000 sq. ft. room, aim for a CADR of at least 300 CFM.

**Filter Type:** HEPA H13 or H14 is standard. H14 captures 99.995% of particles down to 0.1 microns—worth the premium if you have asthma. Activated carbon is essential for VOCs and odors.

**Smart Features:** Matter certification ensures future-proof integration. Outdoor air data integration is a game-changer for wildfire season. Look for real-time PM2.5, VOC, and NO2 sensors.

**Noise Levels:** A purifier that’s too loud will be turned off. Check dB ratings: under 30 dB on low is library-quiet; 50+ dB is noticeable.

**Filter Replacement Cost:** Annual cost ranges from $50 to $150. Factor this into your budget.
