## Key Features to Consider

When shopping for a smart air purifier, understanding the technical specifications can make or break your buying decision. Here's what matters most:

**CADR (Clean Air Delivery Rate):** This industry standard measures how quickly the purifier cleans a room of smoke, dust, and pollen. Higher numbers mean faster purification. For a 300-square-foot room, look for a CADR of at least 200 CFM for each particle type.

**Filter Type:** True HEPA filters capture 99.97% of particles down to 0.3 microns. Some models use proprietary technologies like Dyson's HEPA H13 or Blueair's HEPASilent, which offer similar performance with different airflow characteristics.

**Smart Features:** Look for real-time air quality monitoring, automatic mode, app control, voice assistant compatibility, and scheduling. More advanced models offer geofencing, which automatically adjusts settings when you leave or return home.

**Noise Levels:** Smart purifiers often run 24/7, so noise matters. Check decibel ratings—under 25 dB is whisper-quiet, while 50 dB is comparable to a conversation. Most smart models offer a sleep mode for nighttime use.

**Room Size Coverage:** Manufacturers typically list a maximum square footage, but for best results, choose a model rated for 1.5 times your room size. A purifier rated for 500 square feet will work efficiently in a 330-square-foot room.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

**Unboxing and Setup:** All three models come partially assembled. The Dyson requires attaching the base and filter—about 10 minutes. The Coway needs filter packaging removal and pre-filter insertion—5 minutes. The Blueair is the simplest, requiring only filter installation and plugging in—3 minutes.

**App Setup:** Dyson's Link app requires creating an account, scanning a QR code, and connecting to Wi-Fi. The process took about 7 minutes. Coway's IoCare app is similar but had a minor hiccup connecting to 5GHz networks—use 2.4GHz for best results. Blueair's app setup was the fastest at 4 minutes but lacks advanced features.

**Smart Home Integration:**

- **Amazon Alexa:** All three work with Alexa, but Dyson offers the most commands, including temperature control and oscillation. Coway supports basic on/off and speed control. Blueair requires a smart plug for voice control.

- **Google Assistant:** Similar to Alexa compatibility. Dyson again leads with the most comprehensive command set.

- **Apple HomeKit:** Only Dyson supports HomeKit natively, making it the best choice for Apple users. You can create automations like "turn on purifier when I arrive home."

- **IFTTT and Routines:** Dyson and Coway support IFTTT for custom automations. For example, you can trigger the purifier when a smart sensor detects high humidity or when your smart thermostat activates.

**Placement Tips:** For optimal performance, place the purifier at least 12 inches from walls and furniture. Avoid corners, as airflow becomes restricted. In open floor plans, position the unit near the most heavily used area—typically the living room center.
