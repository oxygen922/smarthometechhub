## Key Features to Consider (With Comparison Table)

When shopping for a smart air purifier, several critical features determine performance, convenience, and long-term value. Here’s what to look for:

- **CADR (Clean Air Delivery Rate):** Measures how quickly the unit removes smoke, dust, and pollen. Higher CADR numbers indicate faster purification. For a 300 sq. ft. room, aim for a CADR of at least 200 CFM.
- **Filter Type:** True HEPA filters capture 99.97% of particles down to 0.3 microns. Some models use HEPASilent or electrostatic technology for lower noise.
- **Coverage Area (sq. ft.):** Manufacturers typically provide a maximum coverage rating, but for best results, choose a unit rated for 1.5x your room size.
- **Smart Connectivity:** Wi-Fi, app control, and voice assistant integration (Alexa, Google Assistant) allow for remote monitoring and automation.
- **Noise Level (dB):** Look for models with a sleep mode under 30 dB for quiet operation. Fan speeds above 50 dB can be disruptive.
- **Energy Consumption (Watts):** Energy Star certification ensures lower electricity bills. Some units use as little as 2-5 watts on low settings.
- **Filter Replacement Cost:** Annual costs vary from $20 to $80. Consider long-term expenses when comparing models.
- **Additional Features:** Air quality sensors, auto mode, timer, filter life indicator, and child lock enhance usability.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Installation Process

All three recommended models are designed for easy, tool-free setup:
1. **Unboxing:** Remove the unit from the box and take off any plastic wrapping from the filter.
2. **Filter Installation:** Open the front panel (Coway) or twist the base (Levoit/Blueair) to access the filter compartment. Insert the HEPA filter and carbon pre-filter as indicated.
3. **Placement:** Position the purifier at least 6 inches from walls and furniture for optimal airflow. Avoid placing it in corners or behind curtains.
4. **Power On:** Plug into a standard outlet and press the power button. The unit will run a self-test before entering standby mode.
5. **Wi-Fi Setup:** Download the manufacturer’s app (Coway IoT, VeSync, or Blueair), create an account, and follow the in-app instructions to connect to your home Wi-Fi (2.4 GHz band required).

### Smart Home Integration

- **Voice Control:** All three models support Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant. Use commands like “Alexa, turn on the air purifier” or “Hey Google, set the purifier to auto mode.”
- **Automations:** With Apple Shortcuts (via IFTTT for some models), you can create routines such as “Turn on the purifier when I arrive home” or “Set to sleep mode at 10 PM.”
- **Compatibility:** The Coway and Levoit models work with Samsung SmartThings, while Blueair integrates with Homey and other hubs. No model supports Apple HomeKit natively, but you can bridge via Homebridge.
- **Tips for Optimization:** Place the purifier in the room you spend the most time in (e.g., living room or bedroom). Use the app to schedule higher fan speeds during cooking or cleaning to quickly remove particles.

### Common Installation Issues

- **Wi-Fi Connectivity:** If the app fails to connect, ensure your phone is on the same 2.4 GHz network. Most smart home devices do not support 5 GHz.
- **Filter Not Detected:** Some units require you to reset the filter timer after replacement (hold the filter reset button for 3 seconds).
- **Noise Complaints:** On high settings, all purifiers produce noticeable noise. Use sleep mode or auto mode for quieter operation.
