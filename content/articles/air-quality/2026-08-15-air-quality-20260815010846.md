## Key Features to Consider

When evaluating smart air purifiers, understanding the technical specifications is crucial. Here's a breakdown of the essential features that separate excellent purifiers from mediocre ones:

### Filtration Technology
- **True HEPA (H13/H14):** Captures 99.97% of particles at 0.3 microns. Essential for allergy sufferers.
- **Activated Carbon:** Absorbs gases, odors, and VOCs. Critical if you live in urban areas or near highways.
- **Pre-filters:** Extend main filter life by capturing larger particles like pet hair and dust bunnies.
- **Electrostatic Technology:** Some purifiers (like Blueair) use charged particles to enhance capture efficiency.

### CADR Rating (Clean Air Delivery Rate)
This industry-standard metric measures how quickly a purifier cleans a specific room size. Higher CADR means faster purification. For a 300 sq ft room, look for a CADR of at least 200 CFM.

### Smart Features
- **Real-time Air Quality Monitoring:** Sensors for PM2.5, PM10, VOCs, and humidity.
- **App Connectivity:** Remote control, scheduling, and historical data tracking.
- **Voice Assistant Integration:** Alexa, Google Assistant, and Apple HomeKit support.
- **Automation:** IFTTT compatibility and integration with other smart home devices.

### Noise Levels
Measured in decibels (dB), noise output varies by fan speed. Look for purifiers with a sleep mode below 30 dB for bedroom use.

### Energy Efficiency
Continuous operation means energy consumption matters. Energy Star certification ensures reasonable power usage.

### Filter Replacement Costs
Factor in long-term costs. HEPA filters typically need replacement every 6-12 months, with costs ranging from $30 to $100.

## Installation & Smart Home Integration

### Getting Started

**Unboxing and Placement:** All three purifiers require minimal setup—simply remove packaging, insert the filter (if not pre-installed), and plug in. For optimal performance, place the unit at least 12 inches from walls and furniture. Central placement in the room ensures even air distribution.

**App Setup:** Download the respective app (Dyson Link, VeSync, or Blueair Friend) and follow the Bluetooth pairing instructions. All three apps guide you through Wi-Fi connection and account creation. Expect this process to take 5-10 minutes.

### Smart Home Integration Deep Dive

**Voice Assistant Commands:**
- **Alexa:** "Alexa, turn on the living room purifier" or "Alexa, ask Dyson to set the purifier to auto mode."
- **Google Assistant:** "Hey Google, set the Levoit to turbo mode."
- **Siri (Dyson only):** "Hey Siri, set the air quality to night mode."

**Automation Examples:**
- **Geofencing:** Set the purifier to activate when you're within 5 miles of home via app-based location services.
- **Weather Triggers:** Connect to weather services to boost purification when outdoor air quality deteriorates.
- **Device Synchronization:** Pair with smart thermostats to run purification when HVAC fans are active, or with smart displays to show air quality readings.

**IFTTT Compatibility:** The Dyson and Levoit support IFTTT applets, enabling creative automation like receiving a notification when air quality drops below a certain threshold or turning on the purifier when your smart doorbell detects you're arriving home.

**Troubleshooting Tips:** If the app loses connection, ensure the purifier is on a 2.4GHz Wi-Fi network (many smart devices don't support 5GHz). Reset the device by unplugging for 30 seconds, then re-pair through the app. Firmware updates are delivered automatically through the apps, so keep them updated for optimal performance.
